正文草稿：
副业第一次收款时，人很容易把兴奋当成答案：有人肯付钱，是不是就该辞职？关键不是赚过多少，而是钱从哪里来。订单可能属于你的专业，也可能借用了原单位的客户、设备或信用。

判断副业能否转主业，不是给一个“适合创业”的标签，而是拆开财帛、官禄、迁移和身宫。先找可以带走的收费能力，再确认离开熟悉平台后还有客户，最后才谈扩大时机。

### 先问副业赚的是谁的资源
官禄宫说明组织内的位置，化权或财权常表现为能管项目、预算和人员，却不代表客户与订单归你。若副业主要靠单位品牌、同事转单或办公资源完成，收入仍和原平台绑在一起。

把最近二十笔订单列出来，标注客户来源、购买理由和离职后是否还会购买。专业能带走、渠道不能带走时，应继续测试，不要急着辞职。

### 财帛化科要落成可重复的收费
财帛宫见化科，重点不是“聪明”，而是专业、作品或方法能被市场辨认。文曲化科再会天钺，适合用专业服务收费；真正的承接是范围清楚、报价稳定、交付后有人复购。

例子一：身宫在财帛，财帛又见文曲化科与天钺，副业靠设计、咨询或教学收费；若迁移宫还能带来陌生客户，连续半年有复购和正常回款，才接近可独立经营。星曜给出收费方向，成交记录才证明方向已经跑通。

### 迁移宫要验证离开原圈子还能成交
后天做出来的财，常要从官禄或迁移寻找。迁移宫不只问去不去外地，也看陌生渠道和新平台能否接住你。若客户只来自亲友或原公司，关系一变，流水可能很快归零。

例子二：官禄宫化权，平时能调团队、拿内部资源，副业订单却大多来自原公司的客户与同事；财帛承接普通、迁移也弱时，一辞职就可能同时失去渠道和信用。先用公开内容、独立转介绍或陌生客户测试三个月，更能看清收入是不是自己的。

### 用现金和时间决定什么时候转
转主业前核对连续六个月净利润、回款时间、单一客户占比和十二个月生活储备。流水接近工资，若仍要垫资或依赖一名客户，也不能承担全职波动。身宫在财帛不会替你补现金缺口。

大限流年适合用来选择扩大、签约或离职的窗口，但要连同三方四正看。若现实指标尚未达标，就把好时段用来增加独立客户、标准化交付和建立储备；指标已经稳定，再借时机完成转换，风险会小得多。

### 排盘使用顺序
读盘先看身宫是否把后天重心推向财帛，再查财帛的化科、禄与收费能否持续；随后用官禄分清单位资源，用迁移核对陌生客户，最后结合大限流年安排扩大或离职。半年净利润、复购、回款、客户集中度和十二个月储备没跑稳，就先不转主业。

Read the Wealth Palace for repeatable paid expertise, then separate employer-owned authority in the Career Palace from independently earned income. ## 3. 紫微斗数订单不少却现金流紧，怎么看贪狼带来的生意量有没有被财帛化忌吃掉

搜索意图：判断订单增长但回款、成本和现金留存恶化时，贪狼带来的业务量是否被财帛化忌、劳耗或合作问题抵消。

正文草稿：
订单多、账户没钱，是经营中最容易制造错觉的状态。消息不断、客户很多、每天都在交付，看起来生意兴旺；等到发工资、补库存或缴税时，却发现现金仍然不够。

贪狼可以把业务量和交易机会带起来，财帛化忌则提醒钱在某个环节受阻。读盘不能用一个吉凶结论盖过经营过程，而要追踪钱从成交、开票、回款到留存究竟在哪一步漏掉。

### 贪狼先看生意量，不先算净利润
贪狼在事业线上主财，也带有机会多、接触面广和应酬增加的特点。它能让客户数量、询盘或项目活跃起来，但活动越多，获客费、招待费和临时承诺也可能一起增长。

经营者要先把“签了多少”改成“收回多少”。尚未回款的订单只是应收，毛利过低的订单甚至会在交付阶段继续吃现金。贪狼的优势是找到生意，不负责替你控制成本。

### 财帛化忌要定位钱卡在哪一段
化忌不宜笼统解释成没财，它更适合用来寻找破财、纠纷和反复卡住的位置。落到财帛线时，可依次查折扣、退货、坏账、赔付、垫资和冲动扩张，哪一项正在持续吞掉可用现金。

例子一：财帛宫见贪狼又逢化忌，订单来自促销和应酬，表面成交很快，实际被折扣、退单、垫资与延迟付款切走，最先要查单笔毛利和账期。停止亏损单，比继续冲销量更重要。

### 经手很多钱，不代表公司真的有钱
财星与权星相会，可能让一个人掌预算、管账户或调度大额资金。放在经营场景里，营业额、客户预付款和代收款都可能很大，但其中还包含成本、税费、供应商款与合伙人权益。

每天只看收款总额，会把财权误认成净财富。至少同时记录已收现金、待交付成本、应付账款和可自由使用余额，才知道这笔钱是否真正属于企业。

### 朋友宫有问题，先把合作现金隔开
朋友宫有吉星、财星时，合作可以共同获利；见巨门、化忌或空耗煞曜，则容易在承诺、分账和资金去向上产生争议。感情再好的伙伴，也不能用聊天记录代替完整规则。

真正需要保留的不是官名，而是官名背后的工作结构：谁承担正式责任，谁管理资源，谁靠专业流程，谁在高压环境做决断，谁必须借外部平台发展。现代职业只是这些结构的新载体。

### 先把古代职位拆成五种工作功能
官代表组织授予的责任与位置，财代表收入、交易和资源控制，文职强调规划、记录、教育与制度流程，武职强调执行、纪律、风险和临场决断，迁移则补充跨地区、对外与平台条件。

一份现代工作可能同时包含多种功能。产品负责人既要文职式规划，也要权责与跨部门执行；银行管理者掌握的是财权，不等于所管资金都是个人财富。

### 宫位决定功能落在哪个现实领域
星曜不能脱离落宫。官星进官禄，较直接对应正式岗位与责任；财星进财帛，较直接对应收入渠道；财星落官禄，也可能表现为管理预算、资金或交易规则，而非本人暴富。

命宫还要连看官禄、财帛和迁移。一个人擅长决断，仍要确认组织是否给位置、收入如何形成，以及外部市场是否比本地平台更能承接。

### 星曜提供工作方式，不直接发职位名称
例子一：天机落官禄宫，传统文官性质可转换为行政规划、教学、银行作业或分析岗位，重点是信息处理与制度流程，不是固定等于公务员。具体行业仍要看财帛与迁移的配合。

太阳、武曲、廉贞或七杀带出的武职感，可以理解为公开责任、资源执行、纪律和攻坚。只有官禄与化权等条件把责任坐实，才进一步讨论管理、执法或高压运营。

### 先分清格局，再按落宫换译
例子二：紫微天府或七杀朝斗落官禄宫，都可从大型组织中的职位、权责与决断范围核对，但两组不能混成同一种星性。财帛宫要另开一条：只有紫微天府落财帛时，才讨论企业资源、财权或职业经理人路线；不能把七杀在官禄的判断一起搬来。落命宫时，则先回到本人承担角色的方式。

最后用岗位说明验证：是否有正式授权、预算规模、决策边界、专业门槛和外部范围。传统结构若能对应这些事实，转换才成立；只有职位名字相像，并不足以证明方向合适。

### 排盘使用顺序
排盘时先从传统称谓提取权责、资源、专业流程、执行压力和流动范围，再看星曜究竟落在命宫、官禄、财帛还是迁移；连看命财官迁后，把结构对应到具体岗位职责，最后用授权、预算、收入方式、专业门槛与外部平台逐项核验，不按古代官名直接定职业。

英文标题：What Do Ancient Zi Wei Dou Shu Career Labels Mean Today?
英文描述：Translate old official, wealth, civil, and military labels into modern duties, then check the relevant palace, authority, income model, and day-to-day work before naming a career.
英文正文：
Traditional Zi Wei Dou Shu descriptions often use jobs from an older society: general, minister, civil official, military officer, or keeper of wealth. Reading those labels literally would exclude most people working in modern companies, schools, hospitals, banks, public agencies, and independent businesses.

The useful part is not the old job title. It is the work being performed. Ask whether the role depends on formal authority, managing money or other resources, applying knowledge through a process, making hard decisions under pressure, or working across external markets and institutions. Only after that should a modern occupation be considered.

### Translate the Duty, Not the Title
An official label points to recognized responsibility inside a group. A wealth label points to earning, transactions, budgets, or control of resources. A civil label emphasizes planning, records, teaching, analysis, and procedure. A military label emphasizes execution, discipline, risk, and decisions that cannot be postponed.

A modern job may combine several duties. A product manager plans and documents, but also owns decisions across teams. A bank executive may control a large budget without personally owning that money. The common function matters more than the industry name.

### Use the Palace to Identify the Real-Life Area
In this system, a palace is a chart area tied to one part of life. The Career Palace concerns formal duties and status. The Wealth Palace concerns income and resource handling. The Life Palace concerns the person's usual way of acting, while the Travel Palace can show how external markets, institutions, or opportunities affect development.

A star associated with money in Career may describe budget responsibility, financial administration, or oversight of transactions rather than personal wealth. A forceful star in Life may describe decisiveness, but a management interpretation still needs a real role and authority in Career.

### Tian Ji Usually Points to Structured Information Work
Example 1: Tian Ji in the Career Palace can fit planning, teaching, operations, accounting support, or analysis because all of these roles organize information through repeatable processes.

The shared function is the reason for the match; Tian Ji does not automatically mean government employment.

To narrow the choice, inspect the income route and the external environment. Stable salary, client fees, institutional promotion, and independent service are different work models even when all use planning and information skills.

### Authority Patterns Must Be Matched to Actual Responsibility
Example 2: In the Career Palace, Zi Wei with Tian Fu points to authority exercised through coordination and stewardship of established resources. A Qi Sha-centered pattern points to authority tested through difficult decisions and pressure. Compare each reading with the person's actual approval rights, team scope, and accountability.

Example 3: Zi Wei with Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace can describe responsibility for company resources, financial authority, or a professional-manager path. Check whether the person actually controls budgets, allocates resources, or carries responsibility for business performance.

Check the job itself: Who can approve a decision? What budget or team is the person responsible for? How is income earned? What qualifications are required? Does the work depend on external clients or institutions? If the daily duties do not match, a familiar-sounding title is not enough.

### Practical Reading Order
Turn each old career label into a plain work function: formal authority, resource control, professional process, high-pressure execution, or access to external markets and institutions. Identify whether the pattern is centered in Life, Career, Wealth, or Travel. Then compare it with actual decision rights, budget or team responsibility, income model, qualifications, clients, and daily tasks before naming a modern career.
