## 21. 紫微空宫怎么看？借对宫的星为什么要减力
slug：`ziwei-konggong-jiexing-duigong-jianli`
来源：2026-11-16 https://oracleeast.com/empty-life-palace-zi-wei-dou-shu/
角度及与旧文区别：聚焦空宫借星的减力读法与常见错误，不重复满盘星曜词典和三方四正的泛入门。
正文草稿：
打开命盘看到某一格里没有十四主星，很多人会以为这一格是空的、什么都没有。其实空宫只是没有主星坐镇，不等于没有信息；这一格仍有辅星、煞星和四化，并且要借对宫的主星来读。关键在于借过来的星要减力，不能照本宫坐星那样直接套用。

### 空宫到底空了什么
空宫缺的是十四主星这一层，辅星、佐曜、煞星和宫干四化照样落在这一格，所以议题范围仍然清楚。借星是古典读法：跨过对宫，把对宫的主星当作这一格的参考，但力量是从对面照过来的，表现更间接、更受环境牵动。把空宫直接读成“命不好”或“这方面没着落”，是最常见的两个误读。

### 借星为什么要减力
主星坐在本宫，能量是自己发出来的；借对宫的星，能量要穿过一格才到，稳定性和主导性都弱一些。同样的星，坐本宫时表现得主动、持续，借过来时往往只在特定场合或被环境触发时才明显。读空宫还要同时看它自己的辅煞和三合，不能只盯对宫一颗星就下结论。

### 空宫借星分步判断

1. 先确认这一格确实没有十四主星，再看里面有哪些辅星、煞星和四化。
2. 找到正对的那一宫，记下它的主星组合与庙旺落陷状态。
3. 把对宫主星借作参考，按减力、间接的方式理解，不照本宫断语硬套。
4. 回到这一格的三合宫位，核对议题方向有没有被加强或削弱。
5. 把借星结论和这一格自己的辅煞并排列出，再决定整体判断。

### 空宫阅读的边界
借星只解决主星缺位，不改变这一格的宫位含义，也不能反复换一种借法去凑想要的结果。若对宫本身也偏弱或带煞，借过来的力量更不稳，要如实标注。盘面给的是倾向，现实中的选择和积累仍要单独评估。

### 排盘使用顺序

1. 先固定出生时间和盘面，确认空宫不是排盘误差造成的。
2. 标出空宫、对宫和两个三合宫，先连成一组再读。
3. 先读空宫自己的辅煞四化，再借对宫主星。
4. 借星一律按减力处理，写清是间接表现。
5. 结论保留条件，不把空宫包装成确定的吉凶断言。

英文标题：How to Read an Empty Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Borrowing Opposite Stars at Reduced Weight
英文描述：Why an empty palace still carries meaning, how to borrow the opposite palace's stars, and why the reading carries less weight.
英文正文：
When a palace has none of the fourteen major stars, it is not a blank box. The palace still holds helper stars, challenging stars, and transformations, and the classical rule is to borrow the major stars from the palace directly opposite. The key point is that borrowed stars carry less weight than stars sitting in the palace itself.

### What "empty" really means
Empty only means no major star sits there. The palace still describes its normal life topic, and the smaller stars still apply. Borrowing means you look across the chart for a reference rather than reading the palace as weak or unlucky. Treating an empty palace as a missing life area is one of the most common beginner mistakes.

### Why borrowed stars are weaker
A star sitting in a palace sends its energy from within. A borrowed star has to shine across the chart, so its expression is less stable and more dependent on circumstances. The same star may look steady when it sits at home but only show up in specific situations when it is borrowed. The local helper and challenging stars still matter, so the reading should not rely on one opposite star alone.

### Step-by-step empty-palace reading

1. Confirm the palace truly has no major star, then list its local helper stars, challenging stars, and transformations.
2. Find the palace directly opposite and note its major stars and their strength.
3. Borrow those stars as a reference, treating them as indirect and reduced rather than applying home-palace wording.
4. Check the two supporting palaces to see whether the topic is strengthened or weakened.
5. Present the borrowed reading and the local stars side by side before drawing a conclusion.

### Reading limits
Borrowing only fills the missing major-star layer; it does not change what the palace represents. If the opposite palace is itself weak or tense, the borrowed reading is even less stable and should be labelled that way. The chart offers tendencies, while real choices and effort still need their own review.

### Chart reading order

1. Fix the birth time and chart first, making sure the empty palace is not a charting error.
2. Mark the empty palace, its opposite, and its two supporting palaces as one group.
3. Read the local helper and challenging stars before borrowing opposite stars.
4. Treat every borrowed star as reduced and indirect in your notes.
5. Keep the conclusion conditional rather than turning an empty palace into a fixed prediction.

正文草稿：
小限是用来看一年荣枯的另一套方法，和按当年地支定位的流年太岁法不是一回事。它的起点由出生年的三合局决定，再按性别顺行或逆行，一年走一格。很多人把小限和流年混在一起读，结果两套起点叠在一起，越看越乱。

### 小限的起点在哪
小限以虚岁计算，一岁的起点按出生年的三合局分四组：寅午戌年从辰宫起一岁，申子辰年从戌宫起一岁，亥卯未年从丑宫起一岁，巳酉丑年从未宫起一岁。先把出生年归到对应的三合局，才能找到第一格。

### 顺行和逆行怎样走
定好起点后，小限一律男命顺行、女命逆行，不看出生年的阴阳。顺行就是顺着十二宫的排列一格一格推进，逆行则反向走，每过一个虚岁移动一格，十二年绕一圈。读某一年的小限，要从起点按岁数逐格数到该年所在的宫。

### 小限起法分步

1. 先确认出生年的地支，把它归到寅午戌、申子辰、亥卯未或巳酉丑其中一组。
2. 按三合局找到一岁小限的起点宫，四个组分别对应辰、戌、丑、未。
3. 确认性别，男命顺行、女命逆行，规则不按出生年阴阳变化。
4. 从起点开始，按虚岁逐格数到要判断的那一年。
5. 记下该年小限所在宫，再结合这一格的星曜和四化阅读。

### 小限和流年的区别
小限按出生年定起点、按年龄推进，每个人的落点随岁数变化；流年太岁法按当年地支，同一年人人都从同一个地支起算。两套方法可以互相参照，但不能把小限的宫当成流年命宫，也不要混用起点。

### 排盘使用顺序

1. 先固定出生年地支和性别，再确认所用的是虚岁。
2. 按三合局定一岁起点，先不急于往后数。
3. 男顺女逆，从起点逐格数到目标虚岁。
4. 小限宫定好后，再读这一格的主星、辅煞和四化。
5. 与流年结论并列时标明各自口径，不混成一种读法。

英文描述：How the minor annual limit starts from the birth-year trio group, moves by nominal age, and follows forward for men and backward for women.
英文正文：
The minor annual limit is a separate one-year method from the annual Tai Sui reading. Its starting palace depends on the birth-year trio group, and it moves one palace per nominal age, forward for men and backward for women. Mixing it with the annual Tai Sui method is a common source of confusion.

### Where the minor limit starts
The count uses nominal age. The first palace follows four trio groups: Yin-Wu-Xu starts at Chen, Shen-Zi-Chen at Xu, Hai-Mao-Wei at Chou, and Si-You-Chou at Wei. The birth year must be placed in the right trio group before the first palace can be found.

### How direction works
After the start, the minor limit always moves forward for a male chart and backward for a female chart, regardless of year polarity. Forward follows the order of the twelve palaces, while backward moves the other way, one palace per nominal age on a twelve-year loop. To find a given year, count palace by palace from the starting point.

### Step-by-step setup

1. Confirm the birth-year branch and place it in one of the four trio groups.
2. Use the trio group to find the age-one starting palace among Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei.
3. Confirm gender: forward for men and backward for women, with no year-polarity adjustment.
4. Count palace by palace from the start up to the target nominal age.
5. Note the resulting palace and read its stars and transformations.

### Minor limit versus annual luck
The minor limit starts from the birth year and moves by age, so each person's palace changes with age. The annual Tai Sui method uses the current year branch, so everyone begins from the same branch in a given year. They can be compared, but the minor-limit palace should not be treated as the annual Life Palace.

### Chart reading order

1. Fix the birth-year branch and gender, and confirm the count uses nominal age.
2. Set the age-one starting palace by trio group before counting ahead.
3. Move forward or backward one palace per nominal age.
4. Once the palace is set, read its major stars, helper stars, and transformations.
5. When comparing with annual luck, label each method clearly instead of merging them.

## 23. 身宫怎么用？它为什么寄在十二宫之一而不是第十三宫
slug：`ziwei-shengong-jiyong-shiergong-feidishisan`
来源：2026-11-16 https://www.xinyj.com/wen/ziwei-chart-reading-order/
角度及与旧文区别：聚焦身宫的寄居位置与后天作用，不重复命宫性格和大限流年的算法。
正文草稿：
初学紫微常把身宫当成命盘上额外的一格，想在十二宫之外再找一个第十三宫。实际上身宫没有独立的格子，它寄在命宫、夫妻、财帛、迁移、官禄、福德这六个宫位之一，和所在宫共用同一格。身宫代表后天的发力方向，理解它落在哪一宫，比把它当成新宫位更重要。

### 身宫寄在哪些宫
身宫只寄在六个宫位：命宫、夫妻宫、财帛宫、迁移宫、官禄宫、福德宫。它落在哪个宫，就和那个宫的星曜同读，不会单独占一格。排盘时先看身宫标注指向哪一宫，再把这一宫当作后天投入精力的重点。

### 命宫和身宫怎样分工
命宫偏向先天的基本特质和起点，身宫偏向后天主动把精力放在哪里。随着年龄增长，身宫的比重通常会越来越明显；身宫落在事业相关宫位的人，往往在工作上持续加码，落在福德或财帛，则分别偏向内在满足与资源经营。这不是前后割裂，而是同一盘里先天与后天的体用关系。

### 身宫判断分步

1. 先在盘上找到身宫标记，确认它指向六个宫位中的哪一个。
2. 不要把身宫当独立格子，而是和所在宫的主星、辅煞一起读。
3. 先读命宫的先天特质，再读身宫所在宫代表的后天方向。
4. 结合年龄阶段，判断身宫的比重是在增强还是尚未明显。
5. 把命宫和身宫的结论并排，看两者是互相加强还是需要平衡。

### 使用身宫的边界
身宫不改变所在宫的基本含义，也不会增加新的宫位议题；它只是提示后天发力的重心。不要因为身宫落在某个宫，就把那一宫的好坏放大成绝对结论。现实中的选择和长期积累，才是身宫方向能否落地的关键。

### 排盘使用顺序

1. 先固定命盘，再定位身宫标记，确认它寄在哪一宫。
2. 身宫与所在宫同读，不另起一个第十三宫。
3. 先命宫后身宫，分清先天特质和后天重心。
4. 结合所在宫的星曜四化，判断精力投入的领域。
5. 结论写清是后天倾向，不把身宫读成固定命运。

四正就是中心宫加上正对的那一宫，两者在一条线上互相照会；三方是中心宫和它的两个三合宫，三个格子构成等边关系。把三方再加上对宫，合称三方四正，实际阅读时是四个宫位一起看。

1. 先确定要回答的具体问题，再选出作为中心的宫位。

3. 找出中心宫的两个三合宫，和中心宫构成三方。
4. 把四个宫位的主星、辅煞和四化并排，看是加强还是互相牵制。

三方四正是阅读结构，不会把不同宫位的议题混为一谈；对宫和三合只是提供侧面信息。不要因为某一宫见吉星，就忽略其他三宫的煞星或四化冲突。盘面给的是一组倾向，现实条件仍要单独核对。

### 排盘使用顺序

1. 先固定盘面和问题，避免一上来就逐颗星读。
2. 定中心宫，再标出对宫和两个三合宫。
3. 先读四个宫位各自的星曜，再看彼此的呼应。
4. 四化和辅煞按四个宫位一起核对，不孤立判断。
5. 围绕单一问题下结论，不扩散成整盘断言。

When a chart looks full and hard to start, the supporting-palace method links the relevant boxes into one group. Around one center, you add the opposite palace and the two three-harmony palaces, giving four palaces that answer one concrete question. Linking first and reading stars second keeps you from judging from one star alone.

### What the terms mean
The opposite axis is the center plus the palace directly facing it. The three-harmony side is the center plus two palaces that form an equal-sided relationship. Adding the opposite palace to that trio gives the full four-palace group used in practice.

### Why they are read as one group
The same major star changes meaning in different palaces, so one box alone loses context. Four palaces describe the same topic from the center, the opposite side, and two supporting angles. For a work question, the Career, Life, Wealth, and Spouse palaces might be linked together. Decide the question first, then choose the center palace.

### Step-by-step reading

1. Define the concrete question and choose the center palace.
2. Find the opposite palace and note the stars on that axis.
3. Identify the two three-harmony palaces that form the trio with the center.
4. Place the major stars, helper stars, and transformations of all four palaces side by side.
5. Use the center as the main reading and the other three as support before concluding.

### Reading limits
The structure does not merge different palace topics; the opposite and supporting palaces only add side information. One auspicious palace should not make you ignore tension in the other three. The chart gives a set of tendencies, while real conditions still need separate checking.

### Chart reading order

1. Fix the chart and the question instead of reading star by star.
2. Set the center, then mark the opposite and two supporting palaces.
3. Read the stars in each palace before looking at how they respond to one another.
4. Check transformations and helper stars across all four palaces rather than in isolation.
5. Conclude on the single question without expanding into a whole-chart verdict.
