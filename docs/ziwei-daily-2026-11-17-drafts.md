## 01 人没出国却接海外客户，迁移宫要怎么看？
参考研究：2026-11-16 https://xingge.tw/zh-hant/notes/qianyi-chuguo。区别：聚焦远程接触外部市场，不重写外派、移居或出差期限。
中文初稿：迁移宫的“外部”不一定等于搬家。若设计师留在本地，却每天与海外客户开会，先问官禄宫承担的交付责任，再看迁移宫如何接触陌生市场与沟通规则。财帛宫用于核回款，不把跨境机会直接说成收入增加。读盘时把工作地点、客户所在地和结算方式分开记录。
English draft: In Zi Wei Dou Shu, an overseas client does not automatically mean relocation. A designer may stay home while working across time zones. Check the Career Palace for deliverables, the Travel Palace for outside contacts, and the Wealth Palace for actual payment. Record where the worker lives, where the client is, and how invoices are settled.

中文初稿：同城搬家可能让住房更稳，却把上班路程拉长。田宅宫对应居住安排，迁移宫对应每日外出环境，官禄宫则要核班次与到岗要求。别因为田宅宫有利就忽略两小时通勤。比较租约、路程和实际工作时间，再决定是否值得搬。
English draft: A move within the same city can improve housing and worsen the commute. Use the Property Palace to frame the home, the Travel Palace for the daily route, and the Career Palace for schedule demands. Compare rent, travel time, and work hours before calling the move beneficial.

English draft: Signing a contract and receiving payment are separate events, even when they are only days apart. Record the signature, delivery, and deposit dates. Then use one consistent monthly cycle method. The Career Palace frames the work obligation; the Wealth Palace frames cash received.

## 04 一个项目跨两个流月，怎样拆开观察工作变化？

中文初稿：项目从立项到验收跨了两个月，不宜拿其中一个月的盘概括全程。先列出启动、交付、验收的日期与责任人，再看官禄宫所问的工作变化，必要时才比较两张流月盘。若第二个月只是收尾，盘面问题应是验收风险，而非重新预测职业方向。
English draft: A project can start in one monthly cycle and close in another. Separate kickoff, delivery, and acceptance dates before comparing charts. Ask the Career Palace about the responsibility at each stage. A closeout month calls for a closeout question, not a new career forecast.

中文初稿：出生时间属于出生地当时的钟表记录，不应直接套用现在居住城市的时区。先核出生证明的地点、日期、时刻，再确认排盘工具对时区与真太阳时的处理。若家人只记得“当地晚上”，要标注不确定性；不能为了得到满意的宫位随意改时辰。
English draft: Use the time and place of birth, not the time zone of the city where someone lives now. Check the birth record and the chart tool's time zone and solar-time settings. If the family remembers only “late evening,” keep that uncertainty visible instead of changing the hour to fit a preferred reading.

English draft: When a leap lunar month produces different charts, first confirm whether the original record is lunar or Gregorian and whether the leap month was marked. Keep the input and tool version. Comparing life events before checking the date conversion can hide a simple data error.

English draft: An annual transformation can highlight a theme without making the same event last all year. State the annual cycle method, note the palace involved, and record the dates of the real decisions. A project discussed in June and signed in September has at least two stages.

## 20 加班和约会撞期，官禄与夫妻宫哪个先看？
参考研究：2026-11-16 https://spyll.id/en/blog/zi-wei-dou-shu-spouse-palace。区别：聚焦单次时间冲突与补救，不重复高压项目长期相处。
中文初稿：一场临时加班撞上约会，先确认工作的不可推迟程度，再谈关系中的告知与补约。官禄宫核岗位责任，夫妻宫核双方对承诺的理解，迁移宫仅在出差或跨城时加入。读盘时分清一次意外和长期模式，并把实际通知时间与补救安排写下来。
English draft: When overtime conflicts with a date, first identify the work obligation and whether it can move. The Career Palace frames that obligation; the Spouse Palace frames expectations and repair. Add the Travel Palace only if distance is involved. Separate a one-off conflict from a repeated pattern.
