## 双语短初稿

01 中文：押金争议先看租约条款、入住照片和扣费明细。田宅宫用于讨论居住安排，财帛宫再核押金的实际返还；不能凭一个宫位推断房东会不会退钱。English: In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Property Palace frames the living arrangement, while the Wealth Palace helps frame cash tied up in a deposit. Compare the lease, move-in photos, and itemized deductions before drawing a conclusion.

02 中文：看中房子却要先交定金时，先写清退款条件和付款时点。田宅宫帮助澄清居住需求，财帛宫核紧急预备金，不能把喜欢房子当成现金已够。English: The Property Palace can frame why a home fits, but a deposit is a cash decision. Check refund terms, payment dates, and emergency reserves with the Wealth Palace question kept separate.

03 中文：学徒反复返工，先核任务说明、示范和反馈周期。子女宫可以观察带教关系，官禄宫再看工作质量标准，不因某颗星就给晚辈贴能力标签。English: A trainee who revises work repeatedly may need clearer examples or faster feedback. Use the Children Palace for the mentoring relationship and the Career Palace for the work standard, then test the pattern against actual review cycles.

04 中文：产品上线只是开始，售后维护要明确负责人。子女宫看培养出来的成果，交友宫看协作网络，官禄宫看长期职责；先列缺陷单和响应时限。English: A launched product still needs maintenance. The Children Palace frames the created work, the Friends Palace the team, and the Career Palace ownership. Start with the issue log and response times.

05 中文：每周往返两地，交通费、住宿费和恢复时间都是真成本。迁移宫看外部环境，财帛宫看费用，福德宫看是否有休息空间。English: Repeated travel is more than a commute. Read the Travel Palace for outside conditions, Wealth for expenses, and Blessing for recovery time. Compare the chart question with a real weekly schedule.

06 中文：临时办公点频繁变化，先核网络、设备和客户保密要求。迁移宫指向外部条件，官禄宫指向交付责任；地点变化不能自动解释为事业机会。English: A temporary office can disrupt equipment, privacy, and delivery. The Travel Palace describes outside conditions; the Career Palace frames the job obligation. Check the actual workspace before calling movement an opportunity.

07 中文：合伙人即使握有经营权，也未必清楚退出后的客户、设备和债务归属。官禄化权可作为责任问题的线索，退出条款仍要独立写明。English: Decision authority does not define an exit. In a Zi Wei Dou Shu reading, a Career Palace authority theme can prompt questions about responsibility. Put clients, equipment, and liabilities in separate exit clauses.

08 中文：预算通过和款项入账是两道关。官禄宫核项目批准与交付，财帛宫核到账日期和使用限制；不得把批复当成可支配现金。English: An approved budget is not cash in the account. Use the Career Palace for authorization and delivery, then the Wealth Palace for receipt dates and restrictions on use.

09 中文：还债和攒首付都占用结余。财帛宫先核每月可用现金，田宅宫再核居住目标，把利息成本和预备金写成两列比较。English: Debt payments and a home down payment compete for the same surplus. Read Wealth for usable monthly cash and Property for the housing goal; compare interest cost with emergency reserves.

10 中文：在家接单添设备，先分开工作投入与居住改造。官禄宫看接单职责，田宅宫看空间条件，财帛宫核折旧和付款。English: A home office purchase may serve work, housing, or both. Separate the Career Palace question about delivery from the Property Palace question about space, then track the cost in Wealth.

11 中文：父母照护排班不是一句流年吉凶能解决。先列可到场的人、时间和备用方案，再看流年落宫与父母宫是否对应当前任务。English: A yearly palace trigger cannot arrange care by itself. List available relatives, hours, and backup coverage first. Then see whether the annual cycle and Parents Palace describe the current responsibility.

12 中文：同事交接起争议时，把口头承诺、文件版本和验收人分开。巨门化忌只提示沟通可能成为议题，结论仍取决于记录与实际职责。English: When a handoff is disputed, separate spoken promises, document versions, and sign-off ownership. Ju Men Hua Ji may frame a communication issue; records determine what happened.

13 中文：分期退款要区分承诺、审批和到账。财帛宫同见科忌时，不必择一解释；把书面处理质量和实际现金回收分成两条线。English: A refund promise, approval, and deposit are different events. If the Wealth Palace shows both Hua Ke and Hua Ji, track documentation quality separately from actual cash received.

14 中文：验收通过仍未收尾款，先确认付款条件与到期日。财帛双四化可分别提示说明和卡点，不把验收文件直接算作收入。English: Acceptance is not the final payment. Read different transformations in the Wealth Palace as separate questions about proof and delay, then check the contract due date.

15 中文：高压故障现场需要快速决定，也需要明确升级权限。七杀独坐申可作为决断风格的讨论起点，但风险上限、授权人和回滚条件要写实。English: In an incident, quick decisions need clear escalation authority. Qisha in the Shen palace may frame a decisive style, but the practical checklist is an owner, a risk limit, and a rollback path.

16 中文：总替伴侣处理问题，可能让关心变成管理。夫妻宫看互动，七杀的决断风格还要与对方意愿合读；先问对方需要建议还是陪伴。English: Solving every problem for a partner can feel like management. Read the Spouse Palace alongside a decisive Qisha style, and ask whether the partner wants advice, help, or simply company.

17 中文：两人轮班错开，家务责任不能只靠临时提醒。夫妻宫看相处，官禄宫看班次约束，田宅宫看家务流程；次日任务写在共同表里。English: Opposite work shifts can make chores invisible. Use the Spouse Palace for interaction, Career for schedules, and Property for home routines. Put the next day's tasks in a shared plan.

18 中文：伴侣副业租工作室，先核租约签字人和共同支出上限。夫妻宫说明协商关系，田宅宫说明空间安排，财帛宫说明现金边界。English: A partner's studio lease creates a household decision. Read Spouse for negotiation, Property for the space, and Wealth for the cash cap. Check who signs and who pays.

19 中文：排盘工具的换限日期不同，先核出生时辰、农历换算、虚岁和五行局。大限宫位不能在输入口径尚不一致时拿来比较。English: If two charts switch major cycles on different dates, compare birth time, calendar conversion, Chinese age, and elemental bureau settings first. Do not interpret a decade palace before inputs match.

20 中文：换限同时接新岗位，旧项目收尾与新职责启动可能并存。先列交接日期和实际职责，再按本命、大限、流年三层定位，不把新周期当作即刻翻篇。English: A new role can begin while old work is still closing. Record handoff dates and responsibilities, then compare natal pattern, major cycle, and annual timing. A cycle change is context, not an instant reset.
