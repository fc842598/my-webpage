# Zi Wei English SEO Workflow

## Audience

English readers usually arrive through broad searches such as "Zi Wei Dou Shu chart", "Chinese astrology chart", "life palace meaning", "wealth palace", "career palace", "annual luck", or "Chinese birth chart". Many are familiar with Western astrology language, but not Chinese metaphysics terms.

Write for people first:

- Use "chart", "life area", "money path", "career role", "outside environment", "timing cycle".
- Keep "Zi Wei Dou Shu" for search recognition, but explain the idea immediately.
- Avoid long lists of star names unless the article is specifically about stars.
- Use practical examples: job role, cash flow, clients, relocation, partner dynamics.
- Do not promise certainty. Position the chart as a structured thinking map.

## SEO Shape

Each English article should include:

- one search-focused H1;
- a title with "Zi Wei Dou Shu" or a plain English palace name;
- meta description for English search intent;
- canonical to `/articles/en/<slug>.html`;
- `hreflang` links to the Chinese page and English page;
- Article JSON-LD with `inLanguage: "en"`;
- internal links to the chart tool and related English articles;
- entry in `/articles/en/`, `/articles/en/feed.xml`, and `sitemap-en.xml`.

## Translation Rule

Do not translate sentence by sentence. Rewrite from the Chinese logic:

1. Identify the Chinese article's core question.
2. Convert Chinese terms into a plain English frame.
3. Keep one or two searchable terms.
4. Replace old-style examples with modern examples.
5. Keep the article calm, useful, and readable.

## Current Implementation

English article pages are generated by:

```powershell
node scripts/generate-en-articles.mjs
```

Generated output:

- `articles/en/index.html`
- `articles/en/*.html`
- `articles/en/feed.xml`
- `sitemap-en.xml`

The generator also adds `hreflang` and an English link back into the Chinese article pages.
