## 1. 六吉星我都有，为什么还不算六吉会命？
slug: `ziwei-liuji-quanpan-douyou-bushi-liuji-shouming`

正文草稿：
左辅、右弼、文昌、文曲、天魁、天钺，一个没少，是否说明自己特别有贵人运？完整显示这些辅星的本命盘，本来就会安出这六颗星。要判断“六吉会命”，需要检查的是位置关系。

### 全盘有，和命宫会到，差在哪里
把命宫选作讨论对象，它的三方四正包括命宫本宫、财帛宫、官禄宫和迁移宫。通常说吉曜会命，指这些位置与命宫的联系，不是把十二宫中所有吉星相加。

假设文昌在命宫、文曲在官禄宫，可以记录为命宫三方四正见昌曲。如果另一张盘的昌曲都落在这四宫之外，便不能只因全盘找得到它们，写出相同的会照结论。这是假设的位置比较，不是两个人的成绩比较。

### 夹宫要另外写
左右邻宫的辅星，可能构成夹宫关系。夹宫与三合会照的判断依据不同，不能为了凑齐六颗，把所有关系都改叫“同宫”。传统格局名称也各有条件，星数相同不保证格局相同。

实际看事业时，可以问哪颗星在官禄、哪颗在迁移，它们与主星怎样配合。记清位置，才知道解读所说的帮助与哪一宫有关；“我有六吉”本身不能证明会有六位贵人。

英文标题：Every Zi Wei Dou Shu chart has six auspicious stars. What makes a meeting?
英文描述：Finding all six auspicious stars somewhere in a chart differs from having them meet the Life Palace. Learn which positions actually count.
英文正文：
A full natal chart normally includes Zuo Fu, You Bi, Wen Chang, Wen Qu, Tian Kui and Tian Yue. Finding all six somewhere on the screen does not make your chart an unusual six-star configuration.

### Location is the missing condition
When the Life Palace is the reference, its principal network consists of the Life, Wealth, Career and Travel Palaces. A traditional statement about auspicious stars meeting the Life Palace concerns their placement in that network.

For a hypothetical comparison, Wen Chang in Life and Wen Qu in Career belong to that network. Placing both outside it is a different arrangement, even though both charts contain the same named stars.

Stars in the two neighboring palaces may instead form a flanking arrangement. Record that separately. Do not rename flanking or distant placements as co-location to reach a desired count.

Once the geometry is clear, interpretation can examine the main stars and the life area involved. Six symbols are not evidence that six helpful people will appear. This is a distinction within traditional chart reading, not a validated prediction.

## 2. 辰戌丑未宫里看到禄，是禄存还是化禄？
slug: `ziwei-lucun-bu-ru-chen-xu-chou-wei`

正文草稿：
命宫在辰，旁边写着一个“禄”，不能立即读成“禄存坐命”。按常见安星法，禄存不安在辰、戌、丑、未这四个地支位置；化禄却可以随相关星曜落在这些位置。

### 一个是星名，一个是变化标记
禄存是独立安置的辅星。甲年在寅，乙年在卯，丙戊在巳，丁己在午，庚在申，辛在酉，壬在亥，癸在子。这张位置表中没有辰戌丑未。

化禄则要先找是哪颗星化禄，再找该星在哪里。假设辰位的主星旁标“禄”，完整说明写的是该星的生年化禄，就应记录星名和四化来源，不把它改写成禄存。

也可能是查看流年时出现的标记。流禄有时指流年禄存，有时被读者简称为流年化禄，单看简称不足以判断。

### 没有禄存坐守，不等于这一宫没有财
排法只限制禄存的位置，没有宣布四个宫位不能讨论收入或积累。财帛宫落辰的人，仍要看该宫主星、四化及相关宫位。不能拿一条安星规则，推成对一类人的财富结论。

遇到“辰宫禄存”的文字，先检查它说的是坐守、会照，还是误把化禄简写了。提问时带上完整星名与地支，别只留下一个“禄”字。

英文标题：Lu in an earth palace: Lu Cun or Hua Lu in Zi Wei Dou Shu?
英文描述：Lu Cun does not occupy Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei under the common placement rule. A Lu transformation can appear there, so check the full label.
英文正文：
If your Life Palace is at Chen and displays a Lu label, that does not automatically mean Lu Cun sits there. The common placement table never assigns Lu Cun to Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei. Hua Lu, a transformation attached to another star, can appear at those positions.

### Read the complete name
Lu Cun is a separately placed star. Hua Lu describes a transformation of a named star. In a hypothetical Chen palace, a main star carrying natal Hua Lu should be recorded with that star's name and the natal timing layer.

An annual display adds another ambiguity: people sometimes shorten both annual Lu Cun and annual Hua Lu to “annual Lu.” The abbreviation cannot tell you which one the chart means.

### A placement restriction is not a wealth verdict
Someone whose Wealth Palace occupies one of those four positions still has a full palace to interpret. Main stars, transformations and related palaces remain relevant within the tradition.

When a description says “Lu Cun at Chen,” check whether it means residence, an aspect from elsewhere or a mislabeled transformation. Keep the full label when asking about it.

## 3. 天魁天钺能在本命同宫吗？贵人星的两种标记别混读
slug: `ziwei-kuiyue-tonggong-xian-hedui-cengci`

正文草稿：
按常见的生年天干安魁钺表，天魁与天钺分居两处，并没有本命两星同宫的一组。看到“魁钺同宫”的解读，应先核对原盘，而不是立刻解释成贵人加倍。

### 先区分本宫文字和会照摘要
有的解读把本宫及三方四正的星曜列在同一段。两颗名字挨着，不等于它们在同一个格子。比如一颗坐命，另一颗在相关宫位，只能按实际关系记录。

安魁钺有表格差异，尤其查不同资料时，要先确认所用版本。但不能因为存在派别差异，就随意编出一组同宫位置。若软件真的这样显示，需要查它的具体安星规则。

### 流曜叠入又是另一回事
本命天魁与某年的流年天钺，可能在显示时挤进同一格。这是不同时间层的叠合，不能删掉“流年”两个字，改成出生时就有的组合。

假设你问去年入职时的推荐人，原盘只有本命天魁，当年又显示流钺，就把年份、星名前缀和宫名一起记下。讨论的重点是那一年的运限联系，不是替本命增加一颗星。

贵人帮助也要回到发生过的事：谁提供了推荐，是否真的获得面试或合作机会。两颗吉星的名字不能替那位推荐人承诺后续帮助。

英文标题：Can Tian Kui and Tian Yue share a natal palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu?
英文描述：The common natal table places Tian Kui and Tian Yue in distinct positions. Check annual overlays and aspect summaries before calling them co-located.
英文正文：
In the common birth-stem table, Tian Kui and Tian Yue occupy different positions. A claim that they share one natal palace deserves a closer look at the chart.

### A list is not a location
Some readings list resident stars together with stars aspecting the palace. Two names beside each other in a paragraph may therefore describe separate chart cells. Record the actual relationship.

Tables vary between traditions, so retain the version used. Variation is a reason to check a rule, not permission to invent a co-location.

### An annual overlay changes the question
A natal Tian Kui and an annual Tian Yue may be displayed together. Keep “annual” in the description; this is not a pair of natal stars.

For a hypothetical question about a job referral last year, record the year, palace and each star's prefix. Then compare the reading with the referral that actually happened. These traditional symbols cannot establish that someone will solve every obstacle for you.

## 4. 同月出生都有辅弼，三台八座为什么不在同一处？
slug: `ziwei-santai-bazuo-bushizhi-kan-shengyue`

正文草稿：
两个人按同一个农历月份排盘，左辅右弼在相同地支，三台八座却不同，这通常不矛盾。常见排法中，辅弼看生月，三台八座还要接着数生日。

### 起点相同，走的步数可以不同
三台从左辅所在宫起初一，顺数到农历生日；八座从右弼所在宫起初一，逆数到生日。初一就算起点，不先多走一格。

用一个只为说明算法的例子：同为农历正月，左辅在辰、右弼在戌。初一出生，三台在辰、八座在戌；初二则三台到巳、八座到酉。两人辅弼没动，三台八座已经分开。

这里比较的是地支位置。若出生时辰不同，命宫等宫名也可能不同，不能把“同在巳”顺手写成“同在事业宫”。闰月则需先统一所采用的月份处理规则。

### 不能把附带关系重复算成多份证据
辅弼与三台八座的排法有联系，读这些星时，要记得它们的起点来自辅弼。不能数到四个名字，就说命盘提供了四份独立证明。

讨论任职或被认可的经历，仍要回到官禄、主星和当时运限。生日决定一个符号落在哪格，不会直接决定一个人会被授予什么职位。

英文标题：Why San Tai and Ba Zuo differ for people born in the same lunar month
英文描述：Zuo Fu and You Bi provide starting positions, while the lunar day changes San Tai and Ba Zuo. See why matching a birth month is not enough.
英文正文：
Two people using the same lunar birth month can have Zuo Fu and You Bi at matching branch positions but different San Tai and Ba Zuo placements. The latter pair also depends on the lunar day.

### Count the starting cell as day one
In the common rule, San Tai starts at Zuo Fu and moves forward through the branch sequence. Ba Zuo starts at You Bi and moves backward.

For an illustrative first-month calculation, Zuo Fu is at Chen and You Bi at Xu. On day one, San Tai and Ba Zuo remain at those starting cells. On day two, they move to Si and You respectively.

These are branch positions, not necessarily matching palace names. Different birth hours can change which life area each cell represents. Leap-month treatment must also be consistent.

Because the placement rules are connected, four labels do not amount to four independent pieces of evidence for a promotion. Traditional interpretation still needs the Career Palace, main stars and relevant timing layer. The arithmetic does not determine a person's job title.

## 5. 命盘为什么有两个大耗？先辨认博士十二神和岁前十二神
slug: `ziwei-liangge-dahao-xitong-bu-tong`

正文草稿：
一个“大耗”在本命盘下面，另一个在流年栏里，不必先担心会破两次财。斗数的不同十二神序列可能使用同名标记；大耗同时见于博士十二神和岁前十二神。

### 名字一样，起法并不一样
博士十二神以禄存为起点，顺逆依所用规则排列。岁前十二神则围绕年支排布。判断你看到哪一种，要看栏目和整组星名，不能只截出“大耗”两个字。

博士那一组有博士、力士、奏书等名称；岁前一组有岁建、晦气、龙德等名称。如果盘面又叠加了流年层，还应记录这个标记依据的是生年还是所选年份。

### 两个标签不能直接数成两笔损失
假设你回顾某年，既搬了家又换了设备，这两笔支出是否必要、是否超出预算，要看账单与当时安排。不能因为盘上有两个大耗，便指定它们各自对应一笔，更不能把正常花费自动叫作灾难。

传统上把大耗与较明显的耗用联系起来，这类辅助标记需要放回宫位与主星结构中讨论，没有把金额、原因或次数写在星名里。

提问时可以写：“我看到博士栏的大耗在某宫，流年岁前栏的大耗在另一宫，两者该怎么区分？”先弄清是哪两项，再讨论解读是否符合那年的经历。

英文标题：Two Da Hao labels in a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart: check their cycles
英文描述：Da Hao appears in more than one auxiliary cycle. Identify the series and timing layer before interpreting two labels as two separate losses.
英文正文：
Da Hao can appear in both the Boshi cycle and the Suiqian, or year-associated, cycle. Two labels on one screen need not mean two predicted financial losses.

### Identify the series first
The Boshi series begins from Lu Cun. The Suiqian series is arranged from a year's earthly branch. Neighboring labels help identify them: Boshi, Li Shi and Zou Shu belong to the former; Sui Jian, Hui Qi and Long De to the latter.

Also check whether the display uses birth-year placements or overlays for the selected year. Series and timing layer are separate pieces of information.

Suppose a person moved home and replaced equipment in one year. Those expenses have actual reasons and amounts. Assigning one to each Da Hao label after the fact does not demonstrate a prediction.

Traditional readings associate the symbol with expenditure or depletion, but it specifies neither an amount nor an event count. Ask about the full series, palace and year before interpreting the repeated name.

## 6. 水二局的人命宫是属金的武曲，五行是不是冲突了？
slug: `ziwei-xingyao-wuxing-bu-dengyu-wuxingju`

正文草稿：
排盘上写水二局，查武曲却说属金，再看命宫地支还有另一种五行。这几项可以同时成立，因为它们描述的对象不同。

### 先把“谁的五行”补全
五行局来自命宫干支的纳音归类，用于安紫微、起大限岁数等排盘环节。武曲属金，是传统星曜性质的一种分类。宫位地支的五行又是位置属性。

假设一张盘属于水二局，命宫由武曲坐守，能记录的是“水二局，武曲坐命”。不能把它改成“你一半是水命，一半是金命”，更不能单凭这两个标签算出性格中有百分之多少冲突。

### 生克不是一道自动打分题
有些读法会继续讨论星宫五行的生克，需要先说明比较哪两项、按什么规则使用。把局、主星、地支和八字日主全塞进同一张加减表，却不说明方法，便无法解释每一分是怎么算出来的。

同样，水二局不能推出“缺火”，武曲属金也不能推出应该换到某个行业。缺什么与适合做什么，不能靠这里出现的一个五行字决定。

阅读解盘时，遇到“五行不合”先问它具体指什么。若对方只重复金木水火土，却说不出在比较局、星还是宫，问题还没有被解释清楚。

英文标题：Water bureau, Metal star: conflicting elements in a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart?
英文描述：The bureau and a star’s elemental classification belong to different parts of the chart system. They are not competing labels for your entire identity.
英文正文：
A chart may say Water Two Bureau while its Life Palace contains Wu Qu, traditionally classified as Metal. A palace branch can have another elemental classification. These labels describe different things.

### Name the object being classified
The bureau is derived from the Life Palace's stem-branch classification and serves calculation purposes, including main-star placement and the first decade's starting age. Wu Qu's element describes a star in traditional symbolism. A branch's element describes a position.

“Water bureau, Wu Qu in Life” is a coherent chart description. It does not make someone half Water and half Metal or provide a percentage of inner conflict.

Some traditions examine elemental relationships between stars and palaces. Such a reading must state exactly what it compares. Mixing the bureau, stars, branches and a Ba Zi day master into an unexplained score obscures the method.

Neither label alone establishes a missing element or an appropriate career. If a reading claims an elemental conflict, ask which two chart components it means.

## 7. 农历生日不同，十四主星却一样，是排盘没更新吗？
slug: `ziwei-gaishengri-zhuxing-weibian-yuanyin`

正文草稿：
不一定。安紫微的算法不是每天沿命盘固定走一格；同一个五行局，不同生日可能算到同一处紫微。紫微位置相同，依它排列的十四主星就可能保持原位。

### 一个可以手算的例子
按常见“补足局数、奇退偶进”的算法，水二局初一先补一得到二，商为一；从寅起一，再退一格，紫微在丑。农历二十五日补一得到二十六，商为十三；绕十二宫后仍从寅退一格，也落丑。

这两个生日相差很远，主星仍可同位，已经说明“生日不同就必须主星不同”并不成立。具体相邻两天是否重合，则要按该局实际计算，不能把这个例子泛化到每两天。

### 主星没变，全盘未必没变
三台八座、恩光天贵等还涉及农历生日，可能发生变化。比较时应检查出生日期确实更新，再逐项看主星和日系辅曜，不只盯着命宫最大的两个字。

这个计算例子假设五行局、月份处理和时辰等其他条件不变。若改日期跨了农历月份或年界，还可能牵动更多条件，需要重新比较。

相同主星布局也不能用来证明两个生日“命运一样”。它只表示这一层计算出现相同结果，既不排除其他层差异，也不能替代出生记录。

英文标题：Why changing a lunar birthday may leave Zi Wei Dou Shu main stars unchanged
英文描述：More than one lunar day can produce the same Zi Wei position within a bureau. Check day-based auxiliary stars before judging two charts identical.
英文正文：
Changing the lunar day does not guarantee a different main-star layout. The traditional Zi Wei calculation can map different days to the same position within a bureau.

### A calculation example
Under the common remainder method for Water Two Bureau, day one is increased to two. The quotient is one; counting from Yin and retreating one position places Zi Wei at Chou. Day twenty-five is increased to twenty-six. Its quotient of thirteen returns to Yin around the twelve positions, and the same retreat again reaches Chou.

This demonstrates a repeated result, not a rule that every adjacent pair of days matches. Each proposed date must be calculated.

### Other stars can still change
Day-dependent auxiliary stars may differ even when all fourteen main stars remain in place. Check those as well as the displayed birth date.

The example holds other inputs constant. Crossing a lunar month or year boundary introduces additional changes. Matching one layer cannot establish identical lives or settle an uncertain birth date.

## 8. 紫微斗数的文曲，是出生那晚天空里的那颗星吗？
slug: `ziwei-xingming-bushi-tianwen-xingtu`

正文草稿：
斗数借用了不少传统星名，但命盘中的“文曲在某宫”，通常不是说出生那晚肉眼看到的文曲正好在那里。斗数星曜按历法与安星规则排布，不能直接当作天文观测图。

### 同一个名字，可能处在不同知识系统里
古代天文、民间信仰与斗数都使用星名，各自的分类和用途并不完全相同。把某颗星的天文学距离、亮度搬进斗数解释，不能因此证明对应宫位会发生什么。

斗数常说的庙旺落陷，也不是根据你出生当天的天气、云量或城市光污染测出来的。夜里看不见星，不会让命盘里的文昌突然变成落陷。

### 十二宫不是望远镜里的十二片天空
命宫、夫妻宫、官禄宫是斗数解释人生议题的分类。假设某人的文曲落官禄宫，这句话讨论的是传统盘内关系，不能翻成“文曲实体星位于他的事业上空”。

它与使用实际天体位置的其他占星体系，也不能只凭几个相似词直接对照。先了解各自如何排图，才能比较它们提出的问题。

学习斗数可以研究安星算法及传统解释，但不要把借用星名写成天文学已经证实了命理效应。解读若援引天文观测，应该说明那项观测究竟验证了什么。

英文标题：Is Wen Qu a physical star in a Zi Wei Dou Shu birth chart?
英文描述：Zi Wei Dou Shu uses inherited star names in a symbolic placement system. Its chart cells are not a map of the sky at the moment of birth.
英文正文：
Wen Qu in a Zi Wei Dou Shu palace is a symbol placed by chart rules. It is not ordinarily a statement about a visible star's position above your birthplace that night.

### Shared names do not make identical systems
Traditional astronomy, popular religious traditions and Zi Wei Dou Shu reuse some names for different purposes. A physical star's distance or brightness does not establish the meaning assigned to a birth-chart palace.

Likewise, the chart's strength classifications are not measurements of cloud cover or light pollution. A cloudy birth night does not weaken a chart symbol.

The Life, Spouse and Career Palaces organize topics within the tradition; they are not twelve telescope views. A hypothetical Wen Qu in Career therefore describes an internal chart relationship, not a celestial object above someone's career.

Study the placement method on its own terms. Borrowed astronomical names do not demonstrate scientifically verified effects on a person's life.

## 9. 禄存旁边总有博士，是否算两颗吉星同时帮忙？
slug: `ziwei-lucun-boshi-tonggong-bu-shi-liangci-jiyu`

正文草稿：
如果看的是同一层博士十二神，博士与禄存同宫是排法规定的起点关系。它们经常一起出现，不是每张盘碰巧多得到一项罕见组合。

### 起点规则带来的重合
这组十二神从禄存位置起博士，再依顺逆布下一颗。无论之后顺行还是逆行，第一颗都先放在起点，因此不能用博士是否同宫判断顺逆排错。

假设某盘禄存在午，对应这一层的博士也应在午。若另一个博士显示在别处，先查是不是本命与流年混在同一屏，或正在比较不同体系的标记。

### 解释时要知道哪些条件有关联
传统解读可分别讨论禄存与博士的象意，但不宜说“禄存证明有资源，博士又独立证明一次，所以可信度翻倍”。后一个位置由前一个决定，它们不是两次互不相关的观察。

这种区分也有助于读格局文章。有的说法把同一组安星关系拆成许多“同时应验的条件”，可能只是把同一个前提数了好几遍。

若要核对某次学习或任职机会，把实际年份、宫位和主星组合放在一起。两个星名可以帮助组织传统解释，但不能把固定同宫关系算成两次机会，更不能直接换算成学历。

英文标题：Why Boshi sits beside Lu Cun in a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart
英文描述：The Boshi cycle starts at Lu Cun. Their shared location follows a placement rule and should not be counted as two independent confirmations.
英文正文：
Within the same Boshi cycle, Boshi shares Lu Cun's location because the cycle begins there. Their co-location follows a rule rather than an unusual coincidence.

### The starting point stays put
The sequence proceeds forward or backward according to the applicable rule, but its first label remains at the starting cell. If Lu Cun is at Wu in a hypothetical chart, the corresponding Boshi begins at Wu too.

If another Boshi appears elsewhere, check timing layers before declaring an error. A natal placement and an annual overlay are different items.

### Related labels are not independent evidence
An interpreter may discuss their traditional meanings separately. Yet Boshi's derived position cannot independently confirm Lu Cun's position and double confidence in a prediction.

This matters when a reading counts numerous connected placement rules as separate confirmations. For an actual education or appointment question, retain the year, main stars and palace context. A fixed pair of labels neither counts opportunities nor establishes academic qualifications.

## 10. 三方四正的“四正”，就是子午卯酉四个宫吗？
slug: `ziwei-gongwei-sizheng-bushi-ziwu-maoyou`

正文草稿：
不是同一套范围。子午卯酉是一组固定地支，常被称为四正地；“某宫的三方四正”则由你选中的宫位决定，不会永远固定在这四格。

### 选中辰宫，就能看出区别
以辰位为参照，三合位置是申、子，对宫是戌，加上辰位本宫，合起来看辰、申、子、戌。这组显然不是子午卯酉。

如果参照位改到巳，三合是酉、丑，对宫是亥，于是看巳、酉、丑、亥。两个例子只说明盘上位置关系，与哪一组比较富贵没有关系。

### 先定参照，再读宫名
本命命宫的三方四正对应命、财帛、官禄、迁移。换成夫妻宫作参照，就不能继续不加区别地叫“命财官迁”。讨论大限或流年时，还要注明用的是哪一层宫名。

有人按方形命盘的上、下、左、右四条边找“四正”，也会找错。画面可以旋转或换版式，地支之间的相对关系不会因此变动。

排盘后，不妨先做一次位置核对：写下参照宫的地支，再列两处三合与一处对宫，最后补上宫名。如果四格找错了，后面读到的星曜组合也会跟着出错。

英文标题：Does “four positions” mean Zi, Wu, Mao and You in Zi Wei Dou Shu?
英文描述：The four-position reading network depends on a reference palace. It differs from the fixed Zi-Wu-Mao-You grouping of earthly branches.
英文正文：
Zi, Wu, Mao and You are a fixed group of earthly-branch positions. The “three directions and four positions” reading network is different: it depends on the palace chosen as the reference.

### Try a reference at Chen
Chen connects by the triangular relationship to Shen and Zi, and faces Xu. Including Chen itself gives Chen, Shen, Zi and Xu.

Move the reference to Si and the network becomes Si, You, Chou and Hai. Neither example describes the fixed Zi-Wu-Mao-You group.

### Add palace names after locating the cells
For the natal Life Palace, the network corresponds to Life, Wealth, Career and Travel. A different reference palace requires different palace names. Decade and annual readings must also specify their timing layer.

Do not substitute the top, bottom, left and right edges of a square screen for these relationships. Display orientation can change while the branch relationships remain the same. Locate the four cells first, then interpret their stars.
