Why the Children Palace Should Not Be Used to Count Children

Why Zi Wei Dou Shu is better at describing parenting style, creative output, and relationship timing than promising a fixed number of children.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:09

The Better Question

A chart is more useful for relationship style, care pressure, teaching habits, and timing than for a guaranteed child count.

Ask What the Person Needs

A fertility question, a parenting question, and a creative-project question can all point to the Children Palace but require different checks.

Examples

Tian Tong with a pressured Fu De Palace may show worry while caring. Po Jun with a strong Career Palace may describe a trial-and-adjust approach to parenting or making.

Use Real Information

Medical, legal, and family-planning decisions need real-world evidence. Keep the palace as a reflection tool for relationships and responsibility.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
