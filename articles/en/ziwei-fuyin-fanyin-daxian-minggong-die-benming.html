Fu Yin and Fan Yin: When the Decade Life Overlaps Natal Life or Sits Opposite

When the decade Life reaches natal Life it is Fu Yin (same-palace overlap): the decade's focus returns to self, old lessons replay and success hinges on one's own character. Reaching the opposite palace is Fan Yin: external impact and reverse pull, change from environment and others. Reading these two special overlaps grasps the decade's inner rhythm.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Fu Yin is an inward decade facing repeated patterns; Fan Yin an outward-pulled decade where people and environment rush from opposite—one cultivates within, the other responds without.

What This Question Is Really Asking

In a Fu Yin decade people feel old problems repeating and think luck is bad; it is actually the period reserved for solving one's own patterns—changing the pattern beats changing tracks.

The Underlying Logic

Fu Yin: decade Life shares the natal Life palace (same branch), amplifying its stars—return to self, old matters replay, introspection and self-struggle.

In that decade success mostly stems from one's own character and choices with limited outside help; auspicious means deep breakthrough, malefics mean repeated drain—conserve or fully change the pattern.

Fan Yin: decade Life sits opposite natal Life (the clash position)—environment, others and external relations pull in reverse, change and impact arriving from outside.

That decade is about responding externally: Travel, partnership, marriage and rivals are activated; meet them with flexibility and boundaries, not head-on clash.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find the natal Life branch first
  2. Locate decade Life and judge same-palace versus opposite to natal Life
  3. Fu Yin: list recurring old patterns and cultivate within
  4. In auspicious Fu Yin deepen core ability; in malefic conserve rather than change tracks
  5. Fan Yin: focus on managing external relations, partnership and movement
  6. In Fan Yin overcome softly, hold boundaries and slow major decisions a beat

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Fu Yin's repeating problems as bad luck

Mistake 2: Fleeing outward instead of changing within during Fu Yin

Mistake 3: Fighting external impact head-on in Fan Yin, losing both

Back to Your Own Chart

Fu Yin and Fan Yin are two special switches of a decade's rhythm. In Fu Yin fix yourself and end repeating lessons; in Fan Yin receive the external and trade clash for flexibility—then you hold the decade's initiative.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Fu Yin and Fan Yin overlaps before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Fu Yin and Fan Yin overlaps line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
