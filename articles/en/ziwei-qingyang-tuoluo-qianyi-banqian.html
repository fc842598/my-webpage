Qing Yang or Tuo Luo in the Travel Palace During a Move

Separate sudden conflict from slow delays in a relocation plan.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30 09:08

Relocation problems have different rhythms. Qing Yang may frame an abrupt conflict, while Tuo Luo may frame slow approvals or repeated waiting; neither predicts an accident. A landlord changing terms on signing day requires quick documentation and a contract check. A delayed key handover needs a timeline and backup housing. Read the Travel Palace with the Property Palace and the real budget.

A sudden move and a delayed move are different problems. One person needs to check a lease and temporary housing; another needs mortgage documents. Travel frames the outside change, Property the home decision, and Wealth the cost. A star cannot replace a contract review.
