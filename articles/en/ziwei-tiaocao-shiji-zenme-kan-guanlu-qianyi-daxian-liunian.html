How to Read Job-Change Timing in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Signals from Career, Travel and Decade/Year

Job change reads Career (the work itself), Travel (outward chance and movement), and Life/Happiness (inner urge). When Career or Travel is activated by Lu/Quan and the decade/year enters a movement structure it is a good window; moving amid Career Ji or malefic Travel is worse. The core is separating should-leave from want-to-escape and moving when energy rises.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Don't leave at every grievance: Career shows current and next job quality, Travel whether outside chances open, decade/year when to move—all three aligned means jumping higher.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some quit in anger amid Career Ji and malefic Travel, each move worse; others freeze when a decade activates Travel Lu with excellent outside chances and miss the window.

The Underlying Logic

Career: work state and form; Lu/Quan mean new posts and leading chances, Ji means a current bottleneck or problems in the next job too.

Travel: outside chance, going out and change; Travel Lu/Quan with Kui/Yue means mentors and good platforms outside, malefics/Ji mean blocked movement.

Life/Happiness: inner motive—leaving for growth and chance (auspicious) versus escaping emotion (often inauspicious); the latter repeats old problems elsewhere.

Timing: negotiate offers and move when decade Career/Travel is auspicious and the year activates Lu/Quan; amid Career Ji or a heavy-malefic year, lie low and job-hunt while employed.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Career to tell a temporary from structural bottleneck
  2. Read Travel to assess real outside chances and mentors
  3. Check inner motive: growth versus escape
  4. Use decade Career/Travel to judge whether to move these ten years
  5. Use year Lu/Quan activation to set the concrete window
  6. In malefic/Ji years don't quit bare; job-hunt while employed and line up the next role

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Quitting bare at every grievance, ignoring Career/Travel fortune

Mistake 2: Leaving to escape, repeating problems elsewhere

Mistake 3: Freezing when a decade opens a good chance, fearing change

Back to Your Own Chart

A job change is timed movement. Tell moving-toward-chance from fleeing-trouble, read Career/Travel and await the decade/year Lu/Quan window, and every jump takes you higher rather than repeating drain elsewhere.

  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
