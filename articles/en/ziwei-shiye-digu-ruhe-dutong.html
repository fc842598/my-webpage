How to Get Through a Career Low: Observe, Rebuild Skills and Adjust Direction

In a career low, first steady your emotions and observe like an outsider which old pattern has failed and what ability you are being pushed to build, rather than complaining or enduring. List transferable skills and resources, rebuild a sense of control through small actions, and adjust direction or seek help when needed; the ups and downs of the decade and year are cyclical signals, and a low is often a preparation for turning.
