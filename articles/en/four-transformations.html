Hua Ke, Hua Quan, Hua Lu, and Hua Ji describe four kinds of movement. Their meaning depends on the star that transforms, the palace it occupies, and the surrounding chart.

Hua Ke emphasizes recognition and professional trust. Hua Quan emphasizes authority and pressure. Hua Lu emphasizes resources and flow. Hua Ji emphasizes friction, attachment, or delay. None of them should be read as a guaranteed event by itself.

Hua Ke: Recognition

Hua Ke in the Career Palace can support advancement through credentials, documented work, or reputation. In the Wealth Palace, it may support income from teaching, analysis, or professional service. The host star and palace strength show whether recognition becomes a usable result.

Hua Quan: Authority

Hua Quan in Career can bring project ownership or management responsibility. In the Life Palace, it may make the person more directive. In the Spouse Palace, it can make power-sharing a visible relationship issue rather than proving that one partner will dominate.

Hua Lu: Resources

Hua Lu in Wealth may open an income or client path, while Hua Lu in Parents may point to support from family, mentors, or institutions. Whether the resource lasts depends on the receiving palaces and the active timing cycle.

Hua Ji: Friction

Hua Ji in Wealth can highlight late payments, debt, or cash-flow pressure. In Career, it may show blocked processes or responsibility without authority. Read the opposite palace, triad, and timing before deciding how serious or long-lasting the friction is.

Combination one: Wen Qu with Hua Ke in the Wealth Palace can make writing, design, research, or consulting expertise easier to verify. If the Career Palace provides a stable delivery role, recognition may support paid work. Without a clear offer or buyer, Hua Ke may bring praise and credentials before it brings dependable income.

Combination two: Hua Quan in the Career Palace together with Hua Ji in Wealth can mean greater authority, heavier targets, and slower collection at the same time. A promotion may be real while the financial return lags. Read the host stars, opposite palaces, and active cycle before calling the year simply fortunate or difficult.

Chart-Reading Order

First identify the transforming star and the palace receiving Hua Ke, Hua Quan, Hua Lu, or Hua Ji. Then read the opposite palace and triad to see what supports or complicates the movement. Compare Career, Wealth, Life, and Travel where relevant, and add the ten-year and annual layers last. Translate each transformation into a practical condition rather than a guaranteed event.
