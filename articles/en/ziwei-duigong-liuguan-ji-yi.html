The Six Opposite-Palace Pairs in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Memorize Them in One Pass

The twelve palaces pair into six opposite pairs: Life-Outward, Sibling-Subordinate, Spouse-Career, Child-Property, Wealth-Karma, Parent-Health. The opposite palace is the mirror and complement of the home palace; read home and opposite together, never one side alone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

New readers find the twelve palaces a jumble, but remembering they pair off cuts the mess in half. Once you ask who sits opposite the Life Palace and who opposite Wealth, the twelve become six easy pairs.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The opposite palace is not an unrelated palace but the mirror and complement of the home palace. The home palace states the matter itself; the opposite states its external echo; the two together make one complete thing.

The Underlying Logic

Life-Outward: Life and Migration oppose, showing self against outer circumstance.

Spouse-Career: Spouse and Career oppose, showing how marriage and work pull on each other.

Child-Property: Children and Property oppose, showing children linked to the home estate.

Wealth-Karma and Parent-Health: Wealth against Karma shows money versus ease, Parents against Health shows upbringing versus body.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Fix the Life Palace and find Migration directly across
  2. Count clockwise to Spouse; its opposite is Career
  3. Continue to Children; its opposite is Property
  4. Then Wealth; its opposite is Karma
  5. Finally Parents opposite Health, completing the six pairs
  6. Whenever reading any palace, pull out its opposite and read them together

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the home palace and missing half the picture

Mistake 2: Mixing up the six pairs and matching the wrong opposites

Mistake 3: Treating the opposite as optional once the home palace is settled

Back to Your Own Chart

Twelve palaces remembered as six pairs makes the chart half as hard. Life-Outward, Spouse-Career, Child-Property, Wealth-Karma, Parent-Health; each pair is a mirror, home states and opposite reflects, and reading both completes the matter.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to memorizing the six opposite-palace pairs before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the memorizing the six opposite-palace pairs line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
