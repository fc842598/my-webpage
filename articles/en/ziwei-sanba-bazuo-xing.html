What Are San Tai and Ba Zuo: Status, Nobility and Weight in Public Settings

San Tai and Ba Zuo are a paired pair of assistant stars governing nobility, status, steadiness and weight in public, favoring public office, management and roles needing seniority and reputation. They are supporting stars that rely on a strong core star and auspicious meetings, while malefics leave an empty name.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

San Tai and Ba Zuo are like the regalia that lend dignity to the core star; the stronger the star and the more proper the setting, the more they show status, while alone they carry no weight.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see these stars and assume high office, then find the name unsupported; the issue is taking assistant stars that set off status as status itself.

The Underlying Logic

San Tai and Ba Zuo arrange by birth-year stem in pairs, symbolizing steadiness, nobility and status and favoring coordination and management.

They neither govern fortune nor succeed alone, needing an exalted core star and auspicious meetings to make status real.

With Kui Yue, Hua Ke and the flank stars, reputation and support are steadier; with gathered malefics and emptiness they become empty show.

Still return to the home core star, triple-direction and decade activation rather than judging from assistants alone.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First recognize where the paired stars appear and their basic symbol
  2. See whether the home core star is exalted and self-standing
  3. Count Kui Yue, Hua Ke, the flank stars and malefics for the net effect
  4. With a strong core star, use steadiness and reputation for management or public office
  5. With a weak core star, build ability first rather than taking show as status
  6. When the decade activates, make seniority and standing real

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking assistant stars as status itself without substance

Mistake 2: Reading these stars without the core brightness and triple-direction

Mistake 3: Valuing show over ability until malefics leave an empty name

Back to Your Own Chart

San Tai and Ba Zuo teach matching substance to position. Steady the core star and make seniority real, and the regalia genuinely serve you rather than leaving empty show.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the San Tai and Ba Zuo stars before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the San Tai and Ba Zuo stars line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
