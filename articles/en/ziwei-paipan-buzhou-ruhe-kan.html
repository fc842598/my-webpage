Steps to Build a Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart

Building a chart has five steps: set birth time and Life/Body Palaces, place the fourteen major stars, place assistant and malefic stars, set natal transformations, and check empty palaces. Modern software does this, but knowing the steps lets you understand where the chart comes from.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Building a chart is like erecting a house from a blueprint: Life Palace is the foundation, major stars the beams, assistants and malefics the doors and windows, transformations the wiring; order matters.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some rely on one-click software without knowing the steps, and when the chart errs or time sits on a boundary they are lost; knowing the steps lets you verify and understand.

The Underlying Logic

Step one set Life and Body Palaces from birth date and time.

Step two place the fourteen major stars including Zi Wei and Tian Fu.

Step three place Zuo Fu You Bi, Wen Chang Wen Qu, Kui Yue, and Yang Tuo Huo Ling plus emptiness.

Step four set natal transformations from the birth-year stem, five check empty palaces.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First verify birth information: solar or lunar date, gender, birth place
  2. Set the Life Palace from birth time, then the Body Palace
  3. Place the fourteen major stars by the Zi Wei positioning method
  4. Place assistant stars and six malefics
  5. Set transformations from the birth-year stem
  6. Overall check: empty palaces, brightness, omissions

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Relying only on software without steps, unable to verify

Mistake 2: Ignoring time boundaries, the Life Palace may be wrong

Mistake 3: Skipping steps, omitting stars or transformations

Back to Your Own Chart

Chart-building steps ask how a chart is erected step by step. Know the steps and verify, and the chart software gives you is truly understood and trustworthy.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the steps to build a chart before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the steps to build a chart line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
