What Is the Lai Yin Palace: The Source of Qin-Tian Four Transformations, Explained

Lai Yin (cause palace) is key in the Qin-Tian flying-transformations school: the palace holding the birth-year stem, the source from which birth-year Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji energy arises, determining which life theme the four transformations revolve around. The San-He school does not use it; it is an advanced flying view. Beginners need only grasp its role without confusing it with the natal palace system.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Birth-year transformations are energy; Lai Yin is which palace emits it. The same Hua Lu differs greatly depending on whether it originates from Life or Spouse.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Beginners force Lai Yin in and tangle it with San-He palaces; first master trines and birth-year transformations, then use Lai Yin as the advanced key to why transformations fall along a certain line.

The Underlying Logic

Lai Yin = the natal palace holding the birth-year stem, the origin of birth-year Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji and the total source of this life's causal themes.

It sets the transformations' thematic direction: from Career, lifelong change revolves around work; from Spouse, relationships; from Wealth, resources.

It belongs to the Qin-Tian/flying school emphasizing where qi comes and goes; the San-He school stresses star combinations—two languages, neither invalidating the other.

Beginner method: fix Lai Yin, then trace where each birth-year transformation flies, understanding what brings gain (Lu) and what binds (Ji) this life.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Cast the natal chart and find the birth-year stem
  2. Locate the palace holding that stem—Lai Yin
  3. Read its theme (Career=work, Spouse=relationship, etc.)
  4. Trace where birth-year Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji each fly
  5. Connect: from which theme this life starts and where transformations land
  6. Treat it as advanced; keep building natal palaces and trines first

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Confusing Lai Yin with San-He palaces as a beginner

Mistake 2: Noting only where transformations land, not their source

Mistake 3: Pitting Qin-Tian and San-He languages against each other

Back to Your Own Chart

Lai Yin answers where this life's themes begin. Beginners need not predict with it at once; remember birth-year transformations flow from this source to each palace—understand the origin and flying transformations stop being isolated symbols.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Lai Yin palace introduction before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Lai Yin palace introduction line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
