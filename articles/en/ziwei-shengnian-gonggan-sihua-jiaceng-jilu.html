Birth-Year vs. Palace-Stem Four Transformations: Recording Both Layers

How to separate the fixed birth-year transformations from palace-stem transformations triggered by a specific event.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

One event is often moved by two layers at once. Birth-year transformations are fixed at birth and describe a long-term pattern. Palace-stem transformations fly out from a specific palace during an event and show what moves this time.

Why the sources differ

Birth-year transformations come from the year's heavenly stem and land in fixed palaces. Palace-stem transformations start from a palace, follow its stem, and send a change to another palace. The first is the background; the second is the action in the event.

Recording steps

  1. Write the birth-year transformations: stem, each transformed star, and its palace.
  2. Identify the event and the palace that triggers the change, with its stem.
  3. Record the starting palace, transformed star, direction, and landing palace.
  4. Place both layers side by side and mark overlaps or links.
  5. Separate the long-term pattern from the new trigger before concluding.

Reading order

  1. Fix the birth-year stem and the palaces of its transformations.
  2. Check the palace and stem involved in the event as a separate layer.
  3. Compare the two layers, then read the main stars and supporting palaces.
  4. Add the major-cycle and annual transformations to see what is triggered now.
  5. Write the answer as a long-term pattern plus a current trigger, not one fortune label.
