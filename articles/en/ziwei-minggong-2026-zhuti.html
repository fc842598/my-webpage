How to Read the Life Palace in Your 2026 Zi Wei Dou Shu Cycle

A practical guide to reading the 2026 Life Palace by separating personal choice, outside pressure, and the responsibility that follows.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:51

The Life Palace Is a Focus

In an annual cycle, the Life Palace shows the issue that puts the person in the foreground. It is not a single luck score.

Add Three Contexts

Career shows the role, Wealth the resources, and Outside the stage. Their direction tells you whether to commit or keep options open.

Two Patterns

Life Hua Ke with Career Hua Quan can turn proof into responsibility. Life Hua Ji with Outside Hua Lu may favor a small external experiment before a major decision.

Practical Use

Name the responsibility first. Then decide what evidence, budget, and time window would make the 2026 choice safer.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
