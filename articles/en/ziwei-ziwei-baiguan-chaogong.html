The Hundred Officials Court the Ruler Pattern in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Emperor Star Needs Its Court

Hundred Officials Court the Ruler means Zi Wei in the Life Palace with auspicious stars such as Zuo Fu You Bi, Tian Kui Tian Yue, Wen Chang Wen Qu and Lu Cun Tian Ma meeting its triple-direction, indicating support, skill in using people and real accomplishment. Without them it falls to a lone ruler; the pattern’s truth rests on actual meetings.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-30

Zi Wei is the emperor star and needs support however strong. People with Zi Wei in the Life Palace ringed by auspicious stars are often not solo fighters but skilled at building teams and accomplishing through others; with only a lone Zi Wei on the chart, the top can feel cold.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what forms the Hundred Officials pattern. The key is counting group by group whether supporting stars truly meet, rather than claiming the pattern at the sight of Zi Wei; a lone ruler and a courted ruler are two different charts.

The Underlying Logic

Zi Wei governs leadership, self-respect and coordination; in the Life Palace one has views and the wish to accomplish, and also needs a stage and a court.

The officials fall into four groups: Zuo Fu You Bi for support and execution, Tian Kui Tian Yue for patronage, Wen Chang Wen Qu for documents and reputation, and Lu Cun Tian Ma for resources and movement.

The more meetings and the fewer malefics or Ji breaking them, the truer the pattern: skill in using and gathering people, with resources and connections steadying the position.

If auspicious stars are few and malefics with Ji surround it, this is not the pattern but a lone ruler, appearing as high aims with limited means, doing everything personally with little help.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Zi Wei is in the Life Palace and check its brightness
  2. Group by group, count whether Zuo You, Kui Yue, Chang Qu and Lu Ma meet
  3. Count malefics and Ji to assess whether the support is broken
  4. If meetings are ample, proceed by building teams and accomplishing through others
  5. If meetings are sparse, drop the expectation of support and first learn to handle matters alone
  6. With the decade, expand teams and platforms in the limit where auspicious stars gather

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Claiming the Hundred Officials pattern at the sight of Zi Wei without counting actual support

Mistake 2: Taking support as needing no personal involvement and ignoring one’s own ability

Mistake 3: Ignoring malefics and Ji and mistaking a lone ruler for an auspicious pattern

Back to Your Own Chart
