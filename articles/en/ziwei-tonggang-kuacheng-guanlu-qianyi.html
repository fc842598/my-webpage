Same Job, New City: Career and Travel Palaces

A job title can stay the same while clients, costs and support change.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-07 09:08

Moving to another city for the same role changes more than geography. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Career Palace frames duties and work style; the Travel Palace frames how the person meets a new outside environment. The difference matters even when the title stays identical.

Compare two transfers

A product manager gains direct client contact. An engineer changes office but keeps remote approvals from the old team. Their outside-world conditions differ. Compare reporting lines, local support and living costs before interpreting a favorable Travel Palace sign.

Reading order

Review Career, the Life and Travel axis, and Wealth for the budget. Add timing last. Use the written transfer terms to decide.
