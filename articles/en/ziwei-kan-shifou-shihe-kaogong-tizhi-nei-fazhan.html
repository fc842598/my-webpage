Institutional fit is never one star: Life sets temperament, Career the work form, Parents the document/exam luck, the decade the timing—four aligned layers make civil service a natural move.

What This Question Is Really Asking

A Sha-Po-Lang chart forcing civil service is miserable even after passing; a clear Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang stable chart can be led astray by outside money. Fit matters far more than following the crowd.

The Underlying Logic

Temperament (Life/Travel): stable, orderly, coordinating galaxies—Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang, Tian Fu/Tian Xiang, Sun/Tian Liang, Tong/Tai Yin—best fit institutions.

Career (Career palace): a steady major star with Lu/Quan/Ke and no malefic/Ji break suits clear-hierarchy, seniority-based organizations.

Exam layer (Parents as documents/repute palace plus Chang/Qu/Hua Ke/Kui/Yue): many scholarly stars and activated Ke smooth exams, interviews, vetting and title evaluation.

Timing (decade/year): apply when decade Career is auspicious and the year carries scholarly activation (Ke, Chang/Qu, Kui/Yue); heavy malefic/Ji years are for preparation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Life/Travel for fit with a stable, orderly environment
  2. Read Career star and transformations for institutional work form
  3. Check Parents and Chang/Qu/Ke/Kui/Yue for exam and document luck
  4. Use decade Career and year scholarly activation to pick the best window
  5. Stable charts prepare systematically and take the long seniority route
  6. Pioneering charts that still enter should choose competitive, pioneering institutional roles

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging exam success from one star

Mistake 2: Sha-Po-Lang forcing stability, stable charts blindly diving into business

Mistake 3: Reading only the natal chart and missing the exam window
