How to Handle Hua Ji in the Zi Wei Friends Palace

Hua Ji in the Friends Palace often points to unclear promises, payment, authority, or information. Repair the mechanism before blaming a person.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 11:40

Hua Ji in the Friends Palace is often labeled ‘bad friends,’ but real partnerships more often show unclear promises, payment, authority, or information. Fix the mechanism before deciding it is a personality problem.

Hua Ji is a checkpoint, not a verdict.

Find the destination

Track where Hua Ji goes. From Friends to Wealth can show split or collection disputes; to Career, unclear duties and approval; to Travel, repeated changes from outside clients or distance.

The flying path carries more information than the label alone.

Make four lists

Write the resource source, delivery standard, payment milestones, and exit terms. If Hua Quan is involved, define authority; if Hua Ke is involved, define credit and professional output.

The lists also test where the chart is actually expressing the friction.

Examples

Hua Ji into Wealth asks about advances and long terms; into Career asks who can change scope and approve; into Travel asks about client changes and travel cost.

Practical reading order

Confirm the birth-year transformations, follow Hua Ji to its destination, add Career, Wealth, and Travel, then use timing to upgrade the agreement.

Start with the question palace, compare its opposite and trine partners, then add timing and transformations before making a practical judgment.
