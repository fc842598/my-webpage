Hua Quan

A plain-English guide to Hua Quan, with a practical reading order, simple examples, and clear boundaries for Zi Wei Dou Shu learners.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-01

Hua Quan is a practical Zi Wei Dou Shu topic. Start with the relevant palace, then read the opposite palace, supporting structure, and current timing.

What This Means

For English readers, Hua Quan should be read as a practical chart question, not as a fixed label. Start with the palace, star, or transformation named in the topic, then check whether the opposite palace and timing cycle can support it in real life.

How To Read It

Start from the main palace, star, or transformation named in the question. Then compare the opposite palace, the triad, and the current ten-year or annual trigger. This keeps the reading grounded in roles, money, relationships, and real choices.

Simple Examples

  • Map the topic to the exact palace or transformation named in the question instead of reading one star in isolation.
  • Check the opposite palace and the triad to see whether the promise has support, pressure, or leakage.
  • Use the ten-year or annual trigger to decide whether the pattern is active now or only background potential.

Practical Order

First define the real-life question. Then read the main palace, its opposite palace, the supporting palaces, and the active cycle. Finish by asking what the chart says to check, protect, or act on next.
