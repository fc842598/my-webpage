How to Use Electric Bikes Safely: Registration and Charging Rules

For electric bikes, register as required, wear a helmet, and do not carry passengers or modify illegally; buy qualified products. Charge batteries in a ventilated, supervised safe place, not indoors, in corridors or with improvised wires, and avoid overcharging to prevent fire; obey traffic rules and watch battery and wiring safety.
