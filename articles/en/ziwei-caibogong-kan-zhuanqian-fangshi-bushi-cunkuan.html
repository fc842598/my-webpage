The Wealth Palace Shows How You Earn, Not How Much You Have Saved

Wealth governs cash flow, earning style and feel for money. Its major star shows whether you fit a steady salary, professional skill, business or investment; total savings also need Property and Happiness palaces.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Wealth answers how money comes in and how you use it, not final net worth—that is a joint ledger of Wealth, Property, Happiness and Career.

What This Question Is Really Asking

An ordinary Wealth Palace is read as cannot-earn, yet wealth may come through Property assets or the Career platform; cash flow alone misses it.

The Underlying Logic

Wealth governs income style and money behavior: Wu Qu/Tian Fu lean business and stewardship; Ji/Tong/Tong/Liang steady and professional; Sha-Po-Lang pioneering and volatile.

Hua Lu means smooth inflow and chances, Hua Quan resource control, Hua Ji tight cash flow and money worry—check whether it is a phase.

Wealth faces Career across the axis; the work model sets the income model, so money luck cannot be read apart from vocation.

Wealth and Happiness are two sides: Happiness sets the mindset of spending and keeping—earning does not guarantee keeping.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Wealth major star to set earning style: salary, skill, business or investment
  2. Read transformations: Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji for inflow, control, reputation, blockage
  3. Check opposite Career to confirm the work model behind income
  4. Use Property for asset accumulation and Happiness for spending/keeping mindset
  5. Read malefics for the cash-flow volatility rhythm
  6. Expand when the decade Wealth activates Lu/Quan; contract when it activates Ji or empty stars

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Wealth as a savings number instead of earning style

Mistake 2: Discussing money luck apart from the Career vocation

Mistake 3: Ignoring Happiness, so earned money is never kept

Back to Your Own Chart

Wealth is your earning manual. Understand the way money comes to you most smoothly before effort and investing, rather than forcing someone else's path.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Wealth Palace as earning style before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Wealth Palace as earning style line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
