Hua Quan in the Friends Palace: Who Sets the Agenda?

A strong Friends Palace can describe capable partners or your role in a group. Identify actual decision rights before labeling anyone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-27 09:07

Hua Quan in the Friends Palace may raise two different questions: do you meet forceful partners, or do you hold an organizing role? Decide which relationship is under discussion first. A friend group, a work team, and a business partnership do not share the same decision structure.

Record whether the transformation belongs to the natal, decade, or annual chart. Then compare Life, Career, and Wealth for your response, formal role, and money responsibility. In a small studio, one partner might manage clients while another approves delivery. Ask who sets agendas, signs spending, and handles failure. These facts are more useful than calling either person “strong.” Put decision rules in writing and confirm them with everyone involved.
