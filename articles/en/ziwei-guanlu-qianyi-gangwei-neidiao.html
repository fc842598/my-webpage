An Internal Transfer in the Career and Travel Palaces

Separate a new role from a change in outside exposure.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30 09:07

An internal transfer may change responsibilities without changing the city or office. Begin with the Career Palace for duties, reporting, and evaluation. Check the Travel Palace if the role adds clients or external contact. A move from support to customer-facing work differs from a move into internal analysis. Compare chart timing with the written role, pay, and trial period.

A transfer inside one company can change the work environment without moving cities. Compare new duties, team boundaries, client contact, and compensation. Career addresses the role, Travel the surrounding platform, and Wealth the actual pay terms. The transfer letter matters more than a broad chart label.
