How to Handle a Traffic Accident and Claim: Safety, Liability and Evidence

After a traffic accident, first ensure personal safety and promptly call the police and seek medical care; do not settle privately and flee. Photograph the scene, and keep the liability determination, medical records, receipts and repair bills, then report and claim through the insurance process. For disputes over compensation, use mediation or litigation; injury and compensation standards follow the police determination, insurance terms and a qualified lawyer.
