Zi Wei Dou Shu: When an Annual Career Signal Meets a Cautious Decade

Compare a job opportunity with the longer career cycle and verifiable role details.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-09 09:13

An attractive job offer can coincide with an active annual Career Palace while the ten-year cycle still favors skills built in the current field. These layers do not cast votes. A Zi Wei Dou Shu natal chart frames recurring strengths; a decade sets the longer context; the year highlights a current decision.

What kind of move is this?

Changing employer while keeping the same expertise differs from entering a new profession. A finance manager may carry team experience to a larger company. A designer entering sales may need months of training despite a higher advertised salary. Check authority, probation terms and reliable pay before reading a favorable transformation as a promise.

Compare the evidence

List pay, commute, manager, learning time and a cash buffer for each role. Read the natal Life and Career Palaces, then the decade, then the year. A verbal promise to lead a team remains uncertain until the role is defined in writing.
