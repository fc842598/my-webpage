How to Read Remarriage: First See the Lessons of the Earlier Relationship, Then the Present Foundation

Reading remarriage is not comparing who is better but whether the lessons of the earlier relationship are complete, whether present values and real conditions are solid and whether children and old matters can be handled. Read the Spouse Palace and decade, but in reality watch honesty and steady effort.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Remarriage is like walking a new road with old luggage; set down what should be set down and close what should be closed, and a lighter, honest step steadies the new road.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some either compare the new partner to the former or hold back from fear of being hurt again; the issue is unfinished lessons and an unclear view of the present foundation.

The Underlying Logic

First read the natal Spouse Palace and the earlier relationship: what was the structural problem and what did you learn.

Then see whether the current decade relationship line activates and whether this stage offers conditions to start again.

The real foundation of remarriage: values, finances, children and old matters must be laid out honestly.

Judge from the present effort of both rather than being led by past shadows or momentary emotion.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First review the earlier relationship and see the unfinished lessons
  2. Close old matters, debts and emotional knots that should be handled
  3. Then read the current decade relationship line to judge when to start again
  4. Talk honestly with the present partner about values, finances and children
  5. Neither compare present to former nor hold back from fear of hurt
  6. Verify the relationship through long, real effort

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Rushing into a new relationship before the earlier lessons are complete

Mistake 2: Comparing present to former or guarding everywhere from fear

Mistake 3: Seeing only emotion without the real foundation while old matters remain

Back to Your Own Chart

Remarriage teaches closing the past and tending the present. Complete the lessons, clarify reality and invest honestly, and this relationship can move more mature and steady.

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to second marriage and remarrying before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the second marriage and remarrying line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
