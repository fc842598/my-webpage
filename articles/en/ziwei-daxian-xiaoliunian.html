Ten-Year Cycles and Annual Cycles

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-01 08:43

A ten-year cycle describes the stage on which a period unfolds; an annual cycle shows which part of that stage becomes active now. Mixing them leads to exaggerated readings: one difficult year does not erase a strong decade, and one fortunate year may not repair a weak long-term structure.

The decade sets direction

Start by identifying the ten-year palace and its triad. A Career-centered decade emphasizes role and responsibility; a Wealth-centered decade emphasizes earning and allocation; a Travel-centered decade emphasizes movement, markets, and external platforms. Transformations show whether resources, authority, reputation, or bottlenecks dominate the background.

The year selects the event

Example one: the decade has Hua Quan in Career, while the annual cycle enters Travel. The larger story is increased responsibility; that year may bring relocation, an external project, or a new market. Example two: the decade is financially supportive, but the annual Wealth Palace receives Hua Ji. The year may require tighter cash-flow control without making the whole decade financially unsuccessful.

Short opportunity needs long support

A weak decade can still contain a useful year. Hua Lu in an annual cycle may bring a contract or payment, but if Career and Wealth lack long-term support, it may remain a one-off gain. Conversely, Di Kong or Di Jie in one year may cause delay or leakage inside an otherwise productive decade. Strategy belongs to the decade; budgeting and scheduling belong to the year.

Chart-reading order

Read the natal structure first. Next, define the ten-year palace, its opposite, triad, and transformations. Then place the annual palace inside that context and identify the specific trigger. Compare repeating or conflicting signals. Finish with a layered conclusion: what the decade is building, what this year activates, and which career, money, or relationship decision needs attention now in practical terms.
