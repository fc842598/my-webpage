Wu Qu and Tian Xiang in the Siblings Palace: Practical, Principled Peers and How to Keep the Bond Steady

The Siblings Palace covers siblings and peers, along with peer-level cooperation and money matters. Wu Qu is practical and rule-oriented while Tian Xiang is upright and tactful, so the people around you tend to be steady and principled and the bond rational and stable, though neither is sentimental, so one must guard against principles crowding out warmth.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-01

People with Wu Qu and Tian Xiang in the Siblings Palace often find a peer who quietly takes responsibility and does things by the book; the bond rests not on sweet words but on reliability.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how siblings and peers relate with Wu Qu and Tian Xiang in the Siblings Palace. Understand it covers both family and peer cooperation, then see how the two stars set the tone.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Siblings Palace makes peers practical, rule-oriented and responsible, with clear lines around money and tasks.

Tian Xiang there makes peers upright, tactful and able to mediate, valuing both rules and dignity in the relationship.

Together they bring practicality and integrity: peers are people you can get things done with, and cooperation or money matters stay relatively clear.

With Lu Cun, Hua Lu and auspicious stars, peers become steady support; with malefics and Ji, distance grows over tasks, money or principles.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Tian Xiang share the Siblings Palace and check their brightness
  2. Sort the people around you into siblings, friends and partners, with the boundary for each
  3. When money or tasks are involved, be clear and keep records in Wu Qu’s way
  4. Use Tian Xiang’s tact to mediate early so small misunderstandings do not harden
  5. Beyond principles, actively show care to supply the warmth both stars lack
  6. With the decade and year, deepen cooperation when the peer relationship is activated

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Tian Xiang in the Siblings Palace only as reliable siblings while ignoring boundaries in peer cooperation

Mistake 2: Stressing rules without warmth until the bond cools

Mistake 3: Leaving money matters undocumented so each side remembers it differently
