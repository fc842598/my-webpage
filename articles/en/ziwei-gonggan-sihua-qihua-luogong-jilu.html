Zi Wei Dou Shu Palace Transformations: Record the Origin as Well as the Destination

A practical record for the source palace, stem, transformed star, destination, and timing layer.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:15

“Hua Ji enters the Wealth Palace” is incomplete when discussing palace-stem transformations. Record the palace that initiates the change, its stem, the affected star, and the destination. A work-related source points to different evidence from a housing-related source.

Follow one traceable path

If the Career Palace initiates a transformation toward Wealth, inspect duties, acceptance, and payment terms. If the Property Palace is the source, inspect deposit, rent, or housing costs. The same destination does not prove the same cause. Also label whether the path belongs to the natal chart, decade, or annual layer.

Verify before concluding

Use contracts, invoices, and cash records to test the real bottleneck. Then read the triad and opposite palace. Hua Ji is a prompt to inspect a constraint, not a standalone forecast of loss.
