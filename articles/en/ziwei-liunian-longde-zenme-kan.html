A Long De Year: When Trouble Finds Rescue, Using Turnaround to Repair and Restart

Long De governs turning trouble into safety, benefactors, returns and room to resolve. In a Long De year, when deadlocks or trouble appear, someone often mediates, a second chance arises or space to ease opens, suiting repairing relationships, resolving disputes and restarting stalled matters. The key is offering a graceful way out, keeping dignity and not making words final. It is an annual auspicious star but not all-powerful, and the major stars, transformations and real action still matter.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-03

A Long De year often feels like someone lending a hand at every hurdle with room to turn: deadlocks loosen and relationships warm, and offering a graceful way out brings a turnaround.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to make good use of a Long De year and enable turnaround. Understand it as rescue and easing, then see which palace it lands in and whether auspicious stars accompany it.

The Underlying Logic

Long De, one of the twelve pre-year stars, governs rescue in trouble, benefactors and turnaround, is the auspicious one in the set and always sits opposite Hui Qi.

Its fortune shows as room to resolve: disputes can be mediated, relationships repaired, stalled matters restarted, but someone must first offer a graceful way out.

With Kui and Yue, Hua Ke or Chang and Qu, benefactor and reputation help is clearer; if tightly framed by malefics and Ji, the rescue is discounted.

The minor star is only an annual layer and Long De gives a chance rather than a result, still requiring active communication and dignity, with the natal and decade as the main verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate which annual palace Long De sits in to identify where turnaround may appear this year
  2. In a deadlock, avoid final words and actively offer a graceful way out for both sides
  3. Find the right person to mediate, or ease matters through written, formal channels
  4. Reassess stalled relationships, partnerships and plans and advance what can restart
  5. Keep dignity when repairing, not fixating on winning a moment, and take the longer view
  6. If malefics and Ji also appear, do not grow careless relying on Long De; still do the work solidly by the rules

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Long De as all-powerful and waiting for improvement without acting

Mistake 2: Having the upper hand and making words final, wasting the chance to resolve

Mistake 3: Promising good outcomes from one auspicious star while ignoring the major stars, transformations and malefics

Back to Your Own Chart

A Long De year offers a year when trouble easily finds rescue. Offering a graceful way out, keeping dignity and repairing relationships lets the turnaround actually land rather than remaining a possibility.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the year when Long De, the dragon-virtue star, is active before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the year when Long De, the dragon-virtue star, is active line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
