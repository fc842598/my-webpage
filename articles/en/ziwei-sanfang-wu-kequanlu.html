When Hua Ke, Hua Quan, and Hua Lu Are Absent: Salary or Entrepreneurship?

Hua Ke is the transformation of recognition, Hua Quan is the transformation of authority, and Hua Lu is the transformation of opportunity and flow. If none.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-17 17:45

Hua Ke is the transformation of recognition, Hua Quan is the transformation of authority, and Hua Lu is the transformation of opportunity and flow. If none supports the main Life-centered structure, advancement may be less visibly driven by credentials, formal power, or easy opportunity. That absence does not prove that salary is best or that entrepreneurship will fail. Main stars, Lu Cun, palace strength, timing, skills, and market conditions still decide how work can function.

An Absence Is a Missing Support, Not a Career Verdict

For a Life-centered reading, Wealth and Career are the two trinal palaces and Travel is opposite Life. First judge the main stars across that four-palace structure. Stable stars may favor gradual accumulation even without the three positive transformations. Change-oriented stars may still support independent work if the person has a market, capital, and an active period. Hua Ji and malefic stars show costs, but they also must be tied to a specific palace.

Two Conditional Work Paths

If the natal structure lacks Hua Ke, Hua Quan, and Hua Lu, but Tian Fu stabilizes Career, Wu Qu supports Wealth, and Lu Cun helps retention, a salaried management or specialist path may build dependable income through experience. If the same three transformations are absent natally but Po Jun or Tan Lang supports Career, clients are visible through Travel, and a ten-year cycle brings Hua Lu and Hua Quan, a staged move into consulting or business may become workable. The timing layer changes the choice.

Use Real-World Readiness as the Tie Breaker

A founder role requires a product or service, customer demand, enough runway, collection discipline, and tolerance for uncertainty. A salaried role requires a usable institution, fair compensation, and room to develop expertise. The chart can compare these demands with the person's structure; it cannot replace financial planning. A hybrid path, such as keeping salary while testing a paid specialty, may be more appropriate than forcing an all-or-nothing identity.

Chart-Reading Order

First confirm whether Hua Ke, Hua Quan, and Hua Lu are truly absent from the relevant Life-centered four-palace structure. Second, judge the main stars in Life, Wealth, Career, and the opposite Travel Palace. Third, add Lu Cun, Hua Ji, assistants, and malefic stars. Fourth, compare natal capacity with the current ten-year and annual cycles. Finish by testing salary, hybrid work, and entrepreneurship against actual savings, demand, contracts, and support.
