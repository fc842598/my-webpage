Punishment-and-Ji Clasping the Seal: Tian Xiang Constrained by Hua Ji and Qing Yang, Distinct from Prisoner-Seal

When Tian Xiang is clasped by Hua Ji (often Tian Liang Hua Ji) plus Qing Yang or Tian Xing, it signals constraint, obstruction, contract/document pressure and disputes. It differs from Prisoner-Seal (Lian Zhen-Tian Xiang with Qing Yang) and must be read separately.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

Tian Xiang clasped by wealth and shade is backed; clasped by punishment and Ji is blocked. Knowing the two forces matters far more than memorizing the pattern name.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People lump every "Tian Xiang plus malefic" into Prisoner-Seal and get muddled. Distinguish Hua Ji plus Qing Yang clasping (Punishment-Ji) from Lian Zhen the prisoner sharing a palace with Qing Yang (Prisoner-Seal); the pressure sources differ.

The Underlying Logic

Punishment-Ji means Tian Xiang is clasped by Hua Ji (often Tian Liang Hua Ji) on one side and Qing Yang/Tian Xing on the other, leaving the seal unable to move.

Hua Ji brings debt, blockage and document/emotional knots; Qing Yang/Tian Xing bring hard collision, rules and liability; together they grind.

It shows as unable to advance or retreat, constrained at work, and prone to flaws in contracts, procedures or compliance that others can exploit.

Prisoner-Seal is Lian Zhen (the prisoner) sharing Tian Xiang with Qing Yang joining, leaning toward personal/friction and direct litigation; one is "clasped," the other "sharing a palace with a malefic"—different structures.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate Tian Xiang, list both neighbors and confirm Hua Ji on one side and Qing Yang/Tian Xing on the other
  2. Identify which star Hua Ji transforms to tell whether the block is money, documents or relationships
  3. Gauge Qing Yang/Tian Xing strength and exaltation to judge whether conflict is open or hidden
  4. Check for Lu Cun or assistants in the same palace that buffer the constraint
  5. Read by palace: clasped Career means obstruction, Wealth means frozen finances, Travel means trouble away from home
  6. Translate "constraint" into verifiable contract terms, processes and boundaries, and dismantle them rather than being intimidated

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Punishment-Ji and Prisoner-Seal as one pattern and mixing verdicts

Mistake 2: Predicting certain disaster from any clasp while ignoring Lu Cun and assistants as buffers

Mistake 3: Citing an ominous name without remedies, when fixing paperwork, clarifying boundaries and changing pace all ease the constraint

Back to Your Own Chart

Punishment-Ji is not a verdict but a map of where things jam. Find the clasped palace on your chart, translate the Ji knot and the Qing Yang clash into concrete matters, and fix gaps early; constraint becomes manageable risk.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Punishment-and-Ji Clasping the Seal pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Punishment-and-Ji Clasping the Seal pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
