How Zi Wei Dou Shu Judges Whether to Change Jobs: Career, Life, Travel and the Decade Together

A job change is not just whether the Career Palace looks good; combine Life for fit, Travel for outside opportunity, and the current decade and annual transformations to tell whether you need a new platform or stronger skills.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

For a job-change chart, the key is not current comfort but whether opportunity sits outside or inside and whether timing is now or later; the wrong direction makes each move worse.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People want to leave at the first frustration, mistaking lagging skills for a bad platform, or jump during a contracting decade and land worse—reading mood instead of structure.

The Underlying Logic

The Career Palace describes work style and role status—the core, but not the only basis.

Life Palace decides whether the current track fits your temperament and way of working.

Travel holds outside opportunity and helpful people; when activated, moving out helps, while activated Career favors internal adjustment.

The decade sets the ten-year career direction, the year the timing of a move; a double-Ji year favors staying over jumping.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Career status and transformations to locate the problem in role, industry or self
  2. Compare Life to see whether the track fits your temperament and ability
  3. Check whether Travel is activated to gauge outside opportunity maturity
  4. Read the current decade career direction—upward, plateau or contraction
  5. Use the year to time the move, avoiding double-Ji or Ji clashing Career
  6. Conclude among new platform, new track, internal transfer or building skills first, then act

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Blaming every frustration on the platform while ignoring skill gaps

Mistake 2: Reading Career without Travel, unable to tell whether outside roles exist

Mistake 3: Ignoring decade and year timing and jumping in a contraction year

Back to Your Own Chart

Changing jobs is structural, not emotional. Laying Career, Life, Travel and the cycles side by side tells you whether to go, stay, or build yourself first.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to changing jobs before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the changing jobs line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
