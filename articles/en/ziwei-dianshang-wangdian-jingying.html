Running an E-Commerce Store in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Matching Star Nature to Each Link

E-commerce is a system war of product selection, traffic, conversion and supply chain at once. Read Wealth for transaction monetization, Career for selection and operations, Travel for platform traffic and outside customers, Property for warehousing, supply chain and home base. Tan Lang has a nose for winning products, Wu Qu masters cost and cash flow, Tian Ji data operations, Tai Yin visuals and detail-page presentation. Match star nature to each link and use decade/year Lu/Quan to scale or consolidate.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-15

Running the same kind of store, one picks products precisely, another controls costs brilliantly, another scales through visuals and content; each e-commerce link maps to a different star strength.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether you suit e-commerce, why the store will not grow or when to scale, first see which link is your strength and where the weak spot is, then set strategy and rhythm.

The Underlying Logic

Monetization reads Wealth: Wealth with Wu Qu/Tian Fu/Lu keeps clear accounts, steady cash flow and retained profit; Tan Lang in Wealth has many chances but also over-diversifies and ties money in inventory. Wealth decides whether the business ultimately earns and keeps money.

Selection and operations read Career: Tan Lang senses demand and trends with a hit-product nose; Tian Ji excels at data analysis, testing styles and optimizing conversion; Tian Xiang standardizes process. Stressed Career with Kong/Jie/Ji switches categories often without accumulation, better suited to small tests than heavy stock bets.

Traffic and customers read Travel: platforms, public traffic and strangers all belong to Travel. Auspicious Travel with Lu/Ke smooths paid traffic, acquisition and store weight; stressed Travel sees paid traffic wasted and should start with repeat customers and organic content traffic.

Supply chain and rhythm read Property and the decade: Property governs warehousing, inventory, premises and the stable rear, and a steady Property keeps the supply chain reliable; decade/year Lu/Quan activating Wealth/Travel is the window to scale and expand categories, while Ji into Wealth/Property calls for inventory control and cash preservation, not blind expansion in a contraction.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Wealth to judge whether the business truly earns and keeps profit
  2. Read Career to locate your strong link among selection, data and process
  3. Read Travel to assess acquisition and paid traffic; if stressed, start with repeat customers and content
  4. Read Property to confirm the warehousing, inventory and supply-chain rear
  5. Divide labor or fill gaps by star nature so one link does not sink the whole business
  6. Set rhythm by decade/year: scale when Lu/Quan activate Wealth/Travel, control inventory when Ji enters Wealth/Property

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Ignoring weak links and stocking heavily because others sell out, leaving inventory stranded in a Ji year

Mistake 2: Burning on paid traffic while Travel is stressed, with high acquisition cost and no retention

Mistake 3: Scaling or contracting without the decade rhythm, expanding blindly when cash should be preserved
