Zi Wei Dou Shu on Post-Retirement Re-Employment: The Late Career Reactivates

Being re-employed after retirement often shows the late-limit Career Palace reactivating, marking someone who retires but does not stop. At this stage it is usually a service relationship rather than employment, so pay, duties and injury handling should be written into an agreement, not relying on the protections of active employment.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-29

Post-retirement re-employment is for purpose and income; the key is putting pay and risk in black and white.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some re-employment relies only on verbal terms, and when pay is delayed or an injury occurs, they find there is no work-injury insurance and claims are difficult.

The Underlying Logic

Post-retirement re-employment often appears when the late-limit Career Palace is activated, marking continued professional value after retirement.

Re-employment after drawing a pension is usually treated as a service relationship, with pay, hours and termination set by agreement.

As work-injury insurance is no longer paid, liability for injury and accident should be agreed in the contract, with commercial accident insurance if needed.

The Karma Palace shows the state of mind after retirement; if re-employment only fills emptiness, weigh health and energy.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the pension is drawn and understand re-employment is usually a service relationship
  2. Sign a written agreement on pay, hours, duties and termination terms
  3. Agree liability for injury and accident in the contract and consider commercial accident insurance
  4. Read the late-limit Career and Karma Palaces to judge whether re-employment suits
  5. Arrange work within your capacity without overloading
  6. Rely on the agreement to claim when pay or rights are harmed

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating re-employment as active employment with assumed work-injury insurance

Mistake 2: Relying only on verbal terms with unclear pay and liability

Mistake 3: Pushing through to fill emptiness and ignoring health
