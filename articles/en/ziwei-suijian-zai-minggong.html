Sui Jian in the Life Palace: Setting a Steady Tone for the Year

Sui Jian in the Life Palace suggests a year suited to consolidation and steady, rule-abiding conduct. Set a sound tone early, taking major matters slowly and avoiding force.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-21

A steady tone at the start smooths the year; slow and steady wins.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some rush and force at the start; stars are tendency, while steady progress prevails.

The Underlying Logic

Sui Jian in Life means the start of the year.

Consolidate, stay steady.

Take major matters slowly.

This is tendency, not an absolute verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Plan and set the pace at the start
  2. Delay major decisions a few days
  3. Follow rules
  4. No impulse or force
  5. Review regularly
  6. Rest on steadiness

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Rushing at the start

Mistake 2: Big moves, overreach

Mistake 3: Force
