How to Read a Hot-and-Cold Partner: Consistency of Actions Over Words

With a hot-and-cold, non-proactive partner, first see whether their long-term actions are consistent and whether they have clear priorities and willingness to invest, separating a passive personality or genuine busyness from simply not caring enough. Express your needs steadily, give room for action, and hold your own boundaries rather than being drained by occasional warmth.
