How to Divide the Fourteen Major Stars into the Zi-Wei and Tian-Fu Systems

The fourteen major stars can be understood through the Zi-Wei and Tian-Fu systems, moving from memorizing personalities to reading structure. Yet the grouping cannot replace palaces, brightness and meetings.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Beginners are overwhelmed by personality lists for fourteen stars and forget them quickly. Change approach: first split them into two systems and see who handles power, resources, execution and change, giving memory a skeleton.

What This Question Is Really Asking

What must be guarded is thinking that grouping means understanding. The systems only provide an organizing frame for relationships; in real reading, the same star behaves differently in the Life, Career and Wealth palaces, so you must return to palaces.

The Underlying Logic

Grouping the fourteen stars into the Zi-Wei and Tian-Fu systems helps understand their functional relationships.

The Zi-Wei system leans toward power and creation, and the Tian-Fu system toward resources and reception, complementing each other.

Change stars such as Qi-Sha and Po-Jun must be observed within the systems rather than in isolation.

The systems are only an entry index; star personality is the baseline and the palace decides the applied question.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First list the names of the fourteen major stars
  2. Split them into the Zi-Wei and Tian-Fu systems
  3. Understand that Zi-Wei leans toward power and creation and Tian-Fu toward resources and reception
  4. Place change stars such as Qi-Sha, Po-Jun and Tan-Lang back within the systems
  5. Use the same star's differences in the Life, Career and Wealth palaces to test that the palace decides expression
  6. Remember the systems are only a skeleton, followed by brightness, meetings and transformations

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Memorizing personalities one by one without building relationships through systems

Mistake 2: Thinking grouping equals understanding and skipping palaces and brightness

Mistake 3: Treating Qi-Sha and Po-Jun as isolated bad stars instead of observing them in the systems

Back to Your Own Chart

The two systems are a rack to hang the fourteen stars on, not the finish. Build the skeleton first, then return to palaces and brightness, and the major stars move from labels into living structure.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to dividing the fourteen major stars into two systems before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the dividing the fourteen major stars into two systems line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
