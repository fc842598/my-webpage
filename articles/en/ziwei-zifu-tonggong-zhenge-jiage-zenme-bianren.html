Is Zi-Fu Tong Gong a Real Pattern in Zi Wei Dou Shu? The Emperor and the Treasury Still Need Support

Zi Wei and Tian Fu sharing Yin or Shen is only the ticket to the pattern; it becomes real when the trine brings Zuo Fu, You Bi and other auspicious stars and a decade activates it. A lone emperor with no aides tends to guard without strength.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

Zi-Fu Tong Gong is a fine foundation of nobility and treasury in one palace, but whether it achieves much depends on support and decade activation.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many find Zi-Fu Tong Gong and claim an emperor pattern without checking the trine for aides or whether a decade activates it, overestimating the pattern.

The Underlying Logic

Zi Wei rules nobility and command; Tian Fu rules wealth and safekeeping. Together in Yin or Shen, they innately combine leadership with resource stewardship.

The pattern hinges on Zuo Fu, You Bi, Chang, Qu, Kui and Yue in the triple-direction; a lone emperor struggles to make nobility real.

Tian Fu guards while Zi Wei needs executors; with unchecked malefics this turns conservative, image-conscious and low on action.

A pattern is innate structure; it pays off only when a decade brings Hua Lu/Quan or auspicious activation—otherwise it is an unlit foundation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the Life Palace is in Yin or Shen and Zi Wei truly shares it with Tian Fu
  2. Check the triple-direction for aides like Zuo Fu and You Bi to tell a lone emperor from a supported one
  3. Count malefics such as Yang, Huo and Ling and their checks to assess execution
  4. Read natal transformations: whether Lu, Quan and Ke bless and where Ji lands
  5. Find the current and coming decades that activate the pattern
  6. Conclude in three tiers—real, half or nominal—and tailor advice rather than over-praising

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging Zi-Fu Tong Gong as top-tier without checking support

Mistake 2: Reading only the Life Palace and ignoring the supporting cast in the triple-direction

Mistake 3: Assuming a pattern guarantees wealth and rank without decade activation or action

Back to Your Own Chart

Zi-Fu Tong Gong gives the makings of a strong hand; playing it well depends on support, the right decade, and your willingness to move from guarding to using.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Zi-Fu Tong Gong pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Zi-Fu Tong Gong pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
