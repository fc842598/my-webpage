Wu-Qu in the Companions Palace: Pragmatic, Deal-Minded Friends; Good Business Partners, Yet Keep Money from Hurting Feelings

With Wu-Qu in Companions, friends are pragmatic, practical and capable, often the doers with whom you can talk business and trade resources; ties rest on ability and mutual benefit, so guard against overly transactional relationships where money hurts feelings.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-27

Your friends tend to be direct doers who get things done and are especially reliable when execution and money are involved, but when benefits are reckoned too clearly, friendships can grow distant.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Mutual benefit needs warmth: use capable business partners for resource exchange, yet put partnerships, loans and splits clearly in writing; do not reduce every tie to benefits, or once money is settled, people drift away.

The Underlying Logic

Wu-Qu is the finance star, hard and practical; in Companions, friends are pragmatic and capable.

It suits business partners, resource exchange and circles of doers.

Ties rest on exchange, and over-transactional dealings make money hurt feelings.

Be mutual yet warm, put partnership money in writing, and rely on professional lawyers and formal channels.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Wu-Qu in Companions and its star combination
  2. See whether friends are pragmatic, capable and practical
  3. Use doer partners for resource and business collaboration
  4. Watch for overly transactional, benefit-first ties
  5. Put partnership money and splits clearly in writing
  6. Keep friendship apart from interest so money stays in proportion

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Praising capable friends without noting that money hurts feelings

Mistake 2: Doing deals on word of mouth without written terms

Mistake 3: Reducing every tie to interest and losing human warmth

Back to Your Own Chart

Wu-Qu in Companions gives you doers who get things done and trade resources, a real asset for work. Put benefits on the table and keep warmth in heart, so collaboration both settles accounts clearly and keeps people.

  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Wu-Qu in the Companions Palace line is activated in this stage

  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
