Can You Marry With Different Values: Differences vs Bottom-Line Conflicts

Value differences may be workable lifestyle preferences or bottom-line conflicts over money, children, loyalty and family responsibility. Differences you can respect and hold in common need not be magnified, while conflicts touching the bottom line that neither will compromise call for caution. Talk through money, children and living with elders clearly before marriage.
