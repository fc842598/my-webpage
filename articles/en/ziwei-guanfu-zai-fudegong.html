Guan Fu in the Blessings Palace: Resolving Disputes and Letting Go

Guan Fu in the Blessings Palace suggests a mind that fixates on a dispute or a wounded pride, losing sleep. Handle what should be handled by law, then learn to let go rather than draining yourself on fixation.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-17

A case may close, but the mind debt lingers; after doing your best lawfully, forgive yourself.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some litigate small matters for years and break down; stars are tendency, while the mind must be eased and severe anxiety needs help.

The Underlying Logic

Guan Fu in Blessings means fixating on disputes.

Handle by law, then let go.

Do not drain on fixation.

This is tendency not diagnosis; rely on formal medical care.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Assess disputes and handle by law
  2. Delegate to professionals when fit
  3. Stop replaying after closure
  4. Exercise, relax, shift attention
  5. Seek care for persistent anxiety
  6. Rest on letting go

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Litigating small matters for years

Mistake 2: Replaying and draining

Mistake 3: Enduring distress alone
