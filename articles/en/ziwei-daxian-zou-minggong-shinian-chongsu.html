Decade Through Life Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Ten-Year Self-Reinvention

The Decade is a ten-year fortune cycle in Zi Wei Dou Shu. When the Decade moves through the natal Life Palace, the theme of this decade is "self"—re-understanding yourself, reshaping life direction, changing from inside out. This is one of the most important transition periods in life; grasping it well can achieve leapfrog growth.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24

Characteristics of Decade through Life Palace: first, "self-awareness awakening"—this decade you will focus more than ever on "who am I" "what do I want," may doubt previous life choices. Second, "major changes"—easily make major decisions this decade (changing jobs, career switching, moving, marriage/divorce, lifestyle changes). Third, "internal-external mismatch"—external environment and inner needs may conflict, e.g., doing a disliked job but inner desire for change. Fourth, "body and energy changes"—Decade Life Palace also affects overall health, pay special attention to body signals this decade. Themes by star in Decade Life Palace: Decade Life Palace Zi Wei—this decade pursues status and recognition, suitable for career sprint, but watch not to be too self-centered. Decade Life Palace Ji Ji—this decade many changes, many ideas, suitable for learning and transition, but avoid being half-hearted. Decade Life Palace Tai Yang—this decade outgoing, active, social, suitable for expanding network and influence, but watch energy distribution. Decade Life Palace Wu Qu—this decade pragmatic, results-oriented, suitable for accumulating wealth and professional ability, but do not be too utilitarian. Decade Life Palace Tian Tong—this decade enjoyment, slow pace, suitable for rest and cultivating hobbies, but do not be too lazy. Decade Life Palace Lian Zhen—this decade emotionally rich, creative, suitable for art and expression, but watch emotional management. Decade Life Palace Tian Fu—this decade stable, accumulating, suitable for buying house, saving, building security, but do not be too conservative. Decade Life Palace Tai Yin—this decade introverted, sensitive, suitable for deep thinking and inner growth, but watch emotional health. Decade Life Palace Tan Lang—this decade strong desires, many opportunities, suitable for expansion and trying, but avoid biting off more than can chew. Decade Life Palace Ju Men—this decade much talk and disputes, suitable for living by eloquence and expertise, but watch communication style. Decade Life Palace Tian Xiang—this decade steady, coordinating, suitable for management and intermediary roles, but do not rely too much on others. Decade Life Palace Tian Liang—this decade mature, has benefactors, suitable for helping others and accumulating seniority, but do not be preachy. Decade Life Palace Qi Sha—this decade strong drive, big changes, suitable for entrepreneurship and breakthrough, but watch risk control. Decade Life Palace Po Jun—this decade break old build new, suitable for radical change, but do not destroy too much. Growth strategies for self-reinvention period: first, "self-exploration"—this decade suitable for psychological tests, reflection, counseling, deeply understand yourself. Second, "skill upgrading"—use this decade to learn new skills, get certificates, prepare for the next decade. Third, "relationship梳理"—re-examine relationships, leave people who drain you, get close to people who nourish you. Fourth, "health investment"—establish exercise and diet habits, this decades body state affects subsequent decades. Fifth, "do not fear change"—Decade Life Palace is for changing, changes made this decade are often more likely to succeed than at other times.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Practical case: The decade when the Decade moves through Life Palace is a self-reinvention period. If this Decade simultaneously has Prosperity entering Wealth Palace, it means that during the process of reinventing yourself, wealth luck also improves—because you find the direction truly suited to you, income naturally increases.

The Underlying Logic

Absolutizing the "good" and "bad" of Decade/Annual, ignoring that every stage has opportunities and challenges.

Only looking at Decade without Annual, or only Annual without Decade, lacking overall perspective.

Doing nothing during a "bad" Decade, wasting time for accumulation and preparation.

Treating chart fortune predictions as "fate," ignoring the role of personal choice and effort.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Chart which palace the current Decade moves through, determine the ten-year theme.
  2. See the Decade palaces major stars and transformations, understand the decades strengths and challenges.
  3. See which palace the Annual moves through, judge the specific event direction for the year.
  4. Take initiative in the Decades "advantage areas," hold steady and build in "challenge areas."
  5. Combine long-term goals with annual plans, progressing step by step.
  6. Review at year-end, adjust next years plan based on actual conditions.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Decade/Annual only indicates trends; specific results depend on personal choices and actions.

Mistake 2: A "good" Decade can be wasted by wrong decisions; a "bad" Decade can accumulate through caution.

Mistake 3: Do not do nothing because the chart says "a certain year is bad"; preparation and accumulation always have value.

Back to Your Own Chart
