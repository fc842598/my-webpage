Can Tian Kui and Tian Yue share a natal palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu?

The common natal table places Tian Kui and Tian Yue in distinct positions. Check annual overlays and aspect summaries before calling them co-located.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09 18:22

In the common birth-stem table, Tian Kui and Tian Yue occupy different positions. A claim that they share one natal palace deserves a closer look at the chart.

A list is not a location

Some readings list resident stars together with stars aspecting the palace. Two names beside each other in a paragraph may therefore describe separate chart cells. Record the actual relationship.

Tables vary between traditions, so retain the version used. Variation is a reason to check a rule, not permission to invent a co-location.

An annual overlay changes the question

A natal Tian Kui and an annual Tian Yue may be displayed together. Keep “annual” in the description; this is not a pair of natal stars.

For a hypothetical question about a job referral last year, record the year, palace and each star's prefix. Then compare the reading with the referral that actually happened. These traditional symbols cannot establish that someone will solve every obstacle for you.
