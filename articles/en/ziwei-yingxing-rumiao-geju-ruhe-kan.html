The Ying Xing Ru Miao Pattern: Po Jun Exalted at Zi or Wu, Building Through Crisis

Po Jun exalted in the Life Palace at Zi or Wu forms Ying Xing Ru Miao, showing someone who dares to break a stale order and builds through difficulty; it still needs Lu and auspicious stars, and a fallen Po Jun with malefics does not form the pattern.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

Ying Xing Ru Miao describes Po Jun at full strength with the nerve to act in disorder; the breaking exists to build, not for its own sake.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some apply the pattern to any Po Jun in Life while ignoring brightness and Lu meetings, then find it inconsistent; the issue is skipping the conditions that form it.

The Underlying Logic

The first condition is Po Jun exalted at Zi or Wu with full strength; in other palaces or fallen it does not hold.

The second is Lu: Po Jun carries drain and most needs Hua Lu or Lu Cun to restrain it, so breaking with support raises the level.

Left/Right Assistants, Kui/Yue and Chang/Qu in the trine support it, while gathered malefics and obstruction break the pattern.

It governs building after breaking, often showing difficulty before achievement, with timing still set by the decades.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm whether the Life Palace holds Po Jun at Zi or Wu and exalted
  2. Then check whether Hua Lu or Lu Cun meets it to restrain the drain
  3. Combine the trine to count assistant and malefic stars for net support
  4. If malefics and obstruction gather, be cautious and do not force the pattern
  5. Read the decade arrangement to see when build-after-break is realized
  6. Point the breaking toward what should be built rather than only breaking

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Applying the pattern to any Po Jun without brightness and Lu

Mistake 2: Speaking only of breaking and not building, turning the pattern into constant upheaval

Mistake 3: Ignoring decades and unable to judge when it is realized

Back to Your Own Chart

Ying Xing Ru Miao teaches breaking with direction and building with capital. Check all conditions and leave timing to the decades, and the nerve to break genuinely constructs something.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Ying Xing Ru Miao pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Ying Xing Ru Miao pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
