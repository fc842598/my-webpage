Which Major Stars Belong to the Tian-Fu System: From Receiving Resources to Executing Change

The Tian-Fu system includes Tian-Fu, Tai-Yin, Tan-Lang, Ju-Men, Tian-Xiang, Tian-Liang, Qi-Sha and Po-Jun, understood through resource management, expression and judgment, and action and change, still returning to palaces and brightness.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Mention the Tian-Fu system and people assume it is all conservative stars, but its lineup is broad, from Tian-Fu managing resources to Ju-Men judging and Qi-Sha and Po-Jun driving change, covering the full line from receiving to transforming.

What This Question Is Really Asking

What must be broken is the stereotype that the system equals conservatism. It contains stars for receiving resources, expressing and judging, and executing change; understanding their roles shows how a team or person operates.

The Underlying Logic

The Tian-Fu system includes Tian-Fu, Tai-Yin, Tan-Lang, Ju-Men, Tian-Xiang, Tian-Liang, Qi-Sha and Po-Jun.

Tian-Fu and Tai-Yin lean toward receiving resources, and Tian-Xiang and Tian-Liang toward coordination and oversight.

Ju-Men leans toward expression and judgment, Tan-Lang toward desire and opportunity, and Qi-Sha and Po-Jun toward action and change.

The system only provides an organizing frame; return to palaces and brightness and understand stars through job tasks rather than personality labels.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First list the eight major stars of the Tian-Fu system
  2. Group them into receiving resources, coordination and oversight, expression and judgment, and action and change
  3. Understand the full chain from receiving resources to driving change
  4. Use team roles as an analogy to see what task each star carries
  5. Place the stars back in the Life, Career and Wealth palaces to compare real tasks
  6. Remember the system is a frame and the final reading still depends on palaces and brightness

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the whole system as conservative and missing the change stars Qi-Sha and Po-Jun

Mistake 2: Memorizing names without grouping and unable to grasp the functional chain

Mistake 3: Stopping at the frame and not returning to palaces and brightness for concrete judgment

Back to Your Own Chart

The Tian-Fu system is a complete chain from managing resources to driving change, not a basket of conservative stars. See the roles of the eight stars clearly and you gain a fresh understanding of how a chart operates.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to resources and change within the Tian-Fu system before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the resources and change within the Tian-Fu system line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
