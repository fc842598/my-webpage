How to Read Struggling to Lead: Goals, Roles or Capability

When a team will not move, first separate unclear goals, unclear roles, weak incentives, and capability or attitude problems. Break goals into executable tasks, clarify ownership and feedback, coach capability issues and set boundaries for attitude issues. Managing also means communicating upward to get resources rather than carrying everything alone.
