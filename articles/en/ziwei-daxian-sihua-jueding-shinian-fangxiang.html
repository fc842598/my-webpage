How Decade-Limit Transformations Set a Ten-Year Direction

Each decade limit has its own stem and transformations, concentrating resources, control, reputation and blockage on one matter for ten years before the theme shifts.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

The natal chart sets a lifelong tone; the decade limit is the stage for ten years, and its transformations are that decade's main plot—not a life sentence.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People who sail through or struggle in one decade assume their fate is fixed, when that decade's transformations simply pushed one kind of matter to the front.

The Underlying Logic

The decade limit moves one palace every ten years and raises a fresh set of transformations from that palace's stem.

Decade Lu points to where resources gather; decade Ji points to the most grinding lesson of these years.

Decade transformations layer over natal ones, magnifying, easing or redirecting the innate theme.

To read a decade, start with its Life Palace and transformations, then relate them to the natal chart.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Determine which natal palace holds the current decade Life Palace
  2. Derive the decade Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji from that palace's stem
  3. See where decade Lu lands—that field holds the decade's opening
  4. See where decade Ji lands—that field is the lesson due
  5. Layer decade over natal transformations to judge tailwind or turn
  6. Keep the conclusion to this stage rather than defining a whole life

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking a decade's luck as lifelong fate and ignoring the stage change in ten years

Mistake 2: Reading only the decade Life Palace star while missing its transformation storyline

Mistake 3: Assuming a decade Ji ruins ten years without checking whether Lu, Quan and Ke compensate

Back to Your Own Chart

Decade transformations tell you where to invest ten years. Ride the tailwind when up, and when blocked treat it as a stage lesson rather than a life verdict.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the decade-limit transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the decade-limit transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
