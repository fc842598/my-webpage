Tian Shi in the Migration Palace: Travel Bringing Transmission, Reception and Connection, Making Itineraries and Information Solid

Tian Shi is an assistant star governing transmission, reception, messages, connection and having a mission away from home. What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to read and use Tian Shi in the Migration Palace. First understand it as an assistant star of transmission, reception and connection, then read the Migration major star for the travel pattern and the trine for external support.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Shi is an assistant star in Zi Wei Dou Shu, governing transmission, reception, messages, connection and a mission away from home, paired with Tian Cai and leaning toward transmission in motion.

In the Migration Palace, travel, long trips and changing environments easily bring encounters of information transmission, network coordination and reception, and one often feels there is a role to play and things to accomplish away from home.

The strengths are many travel opportunities, good information flow and easy connection-building; the risk is little support away from home, itineraries easily going wrong, miscommunication and over-reliance on others for reception.

The approach isproactively connecting plus solid preparation: before travel, make a detailed itinerary, confirm information and contacts, keep written records of important matters, and while awayproactively build networks and information channels without placing all hope on others.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Tian Shi is in the Migration Palace and read the Migration major star and trine for the travel and external environment main line
  2. Review travel experience to see whether encounters of information transmission, coordination and connection are common, verifying Tian Shi’s effect
  3. Before travel, make a detailed itinerary: transportation, accommodation, contacts and time nodes, confirming item by item
  4. Confirm important information such as addresses, times, contacts and agreements in writing, not by memory or word of mouth
  5. While away,proactively build connections: ask more, note more, exchange contact information, but do not rely on a single contact
  6. Read the decade Migration for the travel stage; Tian Shi is only connecting power, not a guarantee that everything goes smoothly away from home

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming Tian Shi means someone will definitely receive you away from home, making no preparations and not confirming information

Mistake 2: Relying only on others for reception while traveling withoutproactively building connections and information channels

Mistake 3: Ignoring the Migration major star and trine, judging travel fortune from Tian Cai alone
