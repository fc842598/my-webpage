Classifying Assistant and Malefic Stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Start by Knowing Who Helps and Who Drains

Assistant stars add support or detail to the main star, such as Zuo Fu You Bi and Tian Kui Tian Yue; malefic stars add pressure or friction, such as Qing Yang Tuo Luo and Huo Xing Ling Xing. Neither is absolutely good or bad; which palace they land in and which main star they meet decides whether they help or drain.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

New readers tense at malefics and relax at assistants, sorting them into good stars and bad stars. Assistants and malefics are more like seasoning; main stars are the dish, and where the seasoning lands changes everything.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Assistants and malefics are not standalone fortune cards but modifiers of the main stars. First recognize who is assistant and who is malefic, then see which main star and palace they sit on, and judge whether the modifier amplifies the main star's strength or weakness.

The Underlying Logic

Assistants such as Zuo Fu You Bi, Tian Kui Tian Yue and Wen Chang Wen Qu stand for support, patrons, paperwork and fine detail.

Malefics such as Qing Yang Tuo Luo, Huo Xing Ling Xing and Di Kong Di Jie stand for pressure, friction, drain and a flip side of drive.

Neither is absolutely good or bad: a harsh star meeting a firm main star can become drive, while an assistant fallen may help the wrong way.

Read them against the main star and palace: together with a strong main star they smooth, together with a pressured one they roughen.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First recognize the twelve main stars and treat them as the leads
  2. Pick out assistants such as Zuo Fu You Bi, Kui Yue and Chang Qu
  3. Pick out malefics such as Qing Yang Tuo Luo, Huo Ling and Kong Jie
  4. Palace by palace, see which main star each assistant or malefic sits on
  5. Judge whether the modifier amplifies the main star's strength or flaw
  6. Treat assistants and malefics only as modifiers, never judging them alone

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading assistants as always lucky and malefics as always harsh regardless of main star and palace

Mistake 2: Judging great misfortune from one malefic apart from its main star

Mistake 3: Assuming more assistants means a better chart without checking which palace they sit in

Back to Your Own Chart

  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the classifying assistant and malefic stars line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
