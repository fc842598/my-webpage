How to Read the Friends Palace: Friends, Colleagues and Networks, Plus Partnership Boundaries

The Friends Palace governs friends, colleagues, subordinates and peer networks, and also reflects how you move in groups and whom to trust or partner with. Read the major star for tone, then combine triple-direction and transformations to see which relationships help and which need boundaries.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

The Friends Palace is like a report on relationships; the major star shows whom you attract and the triple-direction shows whether they help or drain you, and that decides closeness or distance.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some take the palace as merely how many friends they have, or at a malefic assume no one is trustworthy; the issue is not friends but failing to clarify conduct, partnership boundaries and accounts.

The Underlying Logic

The Friends Palace describes peer and group relationships: the major star sets tone, many auspicious stars bring helpful networks and gathered malefics bring dragging and disputes.

Partnerships, loans and guarantees are read together with the Siblings Palace, naming rules, accounts and exit first in writing.

The triple-direction and transformations decide the net effect, with Hua Lu bringing mutual help and Hua Ji bringing rifts or fixation over loyalty.

It complements the complete per-star set for this palace: one reads star by star, this reads the whole palace.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the Friends Palace major star to set your tone in groups
  2. Then combine the triple-direction to count auspicious stars, malefics and transformations
  3. Sort networks into close, cooperative and casual, and conduct each differently
  4. Before partnerships, loans and guarantees, clarify rules and accounts in writing
  5. Settle terms first rather than agreeing to vague matters to spare face
  6. Review periodically which relationships deserve long-term investment

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking the palace only as friend count and ignoring quality and boundaries

Mistake 2: Partnerships and loans without writing, then falling out over loyalty

Mistake 3: Closing off at a malefic and trusting no one

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading the Friends Palace well is not more friends but knowing whom to trust deeply and where to hold boundaries. Put rules first, and the network genuinely becomes support.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to how to read the Friends Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the how to read the Friends Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
