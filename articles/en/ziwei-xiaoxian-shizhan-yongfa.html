How to Use the Minor Cycle: It's Not a "Second Annual Forecast"

The minor cycle starts from Life at age one, going clockwise for yang-male/yin-female and counterclockwise for yin-male/yang-female, reflecting personal-level yearly themes. It complements the annual cycle (stem transformations) — one sees external environment, the other internal state.

Methods2026-08-20 10:15

Many people learning Zi Wei only read the annual cycle and ignore the minor cycle, thinking it's redundant. But they answer different questions: annual cycle sees "what the external environment gives you this year"; minor cycle sees "what state you're in personally this year." Annual stem transformations are "heaven"; the minor cycle palace is "person" — right timing but wrong person still fails.

Minor cycle arrangement: start from Life at age one, clockwise for yang-male/yin-female, counterclockwise for yin-male/yang-female, one palace per year. E.g., yang-male with Life in Zi: age 1 in Zi, age 2 in Hai, age 3 in Xu... Which palace the minor cycle reaches is that year's personal theme. Difference from annual: annual sees stem transformations' effect on the whole chart; minor sees which palace you've walked into.

Minor vs Annual

Annual — stem transformations, the "external environment" theme.

Minor — which palace you reach, the "personal state" theme.

Good annual transformations but minor cycle in malefic palace — good environment but poor personal state, can't seize opportunities.

Bad annual transformations but minor cycle in auspicious palace — poor environment but good personal state, can rise against odds.

Read both for completeness — timing (annual) + personal condition (minor).

How to Read Minor Cycle

Which palace does it reach — determines the year's personal theme (Wealth=money, Career=work, Spouse=love).

What stars are in that palace — temple/prosperous/auspicious = good state; fallen/malefic = poor state.

Check that palace's triple direction — the year's "ecosystem."

Check annual stem transformations — if they land in or aspect the minor palace, that field activates.

The minor palace's own stem transformations can also be referenced — where you direct energy this year.

Practical Use

Minor cycle in Life — year to "be yourself," reposition, change image.

Minor in Wealth — focus on money, suited to financial planning, salary negotiation, entrepreneurship.

Minor in Career — focus on work, suited to promotion, job change, exams.

Minor in Spouse — relationships are the theme; may marry, break up, meet someone important.

Minor in Travel — suited to going out, travel, changing environment.

Reading Order

For a yearly forecast:

  1. Annual stem transformations — the year's big environment.
  2. Minor cycle palace — the year's personal theme.
  3. Stars and triple of minor palace — is personal state good.
  4. Do annual transformations aspect the minor palace — do timing and personal condition align.
  5. Major cycle — it's the background; annual and minor are foreground.
  6. Don't read only one.
