An Empty Property Palace and a Home Office

Borrow the opposite palace while testing real space needs.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-02 10:15

An empty Property Palace does not mean there is no stable home. Borrow the opposite Children Palace carefully, while keeping transformations and minor stars in the original palace. Career tells you what work requires; Wealth constrains the renovation budget. Draw the room, log meeting and study hours, test lighting and noise for two weeks, and ask every user what space they need.
