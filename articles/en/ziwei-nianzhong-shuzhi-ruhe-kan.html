How to Read a Year-End Review: Account for Contributions

For a year-end review, account for contributions, data and next year’s plans with facts, prepared and without exaggeration or complaint.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-10

Clear records through the year make the review easy; speak of contributions and plans.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some improvise or complain; stars are tendency, while records matter.

The Underlying Logic

Read year-end review, account clearly.

Speak of contributions and plans.

No exaggeration or complaint.

This is tendency, not an absolute verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  3. Use data

  5. State next year’s plans
  6. Rest on facts
