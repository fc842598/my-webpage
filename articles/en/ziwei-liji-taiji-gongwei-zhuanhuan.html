Shifting the Taiji Pole in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Treat One Palace as the New Life Palace and Rebuild the Twelve

Shifting the taiji pole means taking one palace as a new Life origin and renaming the other eleven by their relative positions: the Spouse Palace for spouse matters, the Children Palace for child matters. It changes the subject, not creating a new fate; the original pattern always bounds the borrowed line.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

Wondering how a child will turn out while staring at your own Life Palace points the wrong way. In Zi Wei, a new question needs a new origin: set the relevant palace as the new Life and rebuild the twelve around it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Shifting taiji answers who is the subject. With the Life Palace as origin it is your own life; with the Spouse Palace as origin it is the spouse's projection within your chart. After shifting, all twelve palaces must be renamed, not just the Life cell.

The Underlying Logic

Shifting works by translating relative positions: once the new origin is set, rename the rest by the fixed twelve-palace relationships.

Set the Spouse Palace for a spouse, the Children Palace for a child, the Parents Palace for parents, each as its own origin.

The borrowed twelve sit on top of the original chart, bound by its pattern; they do not become a separate fate.

Once the origin shifts, the triple-direction relationships are recalculated, not the ones from the Life Palace.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First clarify whether the question is about spouse, child, parent or career
  2. Pick the corresponding palace as the new taiji origin
  3. Rename the other eleven by their original relative positions
  4. Recalculate the triple-direction under the new origin
  5. Read the new line's stars, assistants, malefics and transformations
  6. Remember this is a borrowed view of one chart, bound by the original pattern

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading your own Life or career palace when asking about another party

Mistake 2: Shifting the origin without renaming the other eleven and the triple-direction

Mistake 3: Treating the borrowed chart as the other party's independent fate

Back to Your Own Chart

Shifting taiji changes the subject on one chart. Move the origin and the twelve relationships become the new line's story, yet the base remains the original chart; with the right subject, the spouse or child line reads accurately.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to shifting the taiji pole between palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the shifting the taiji pole between palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
