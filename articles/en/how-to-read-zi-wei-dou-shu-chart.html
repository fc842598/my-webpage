A generated chart is a map of relationships among palaces, stars, transformations, and timing. The first task is to choose a reading order, not to hunt for one dramatic prediction.

Begin with the Life Palace, then identify the palace that matches your question. Compare its opposite palace and triad before adding the ten-year and annual cycles.

Start With the Life Palace

The Life Palace describes baseline temperament and response style. If it has no main star, borrow the main stars from the opposite palace for interpretation and pay extra attention to the surrounding structure. An empty palace does not mean the environment completely defines the person.

Choose the Question Palace

Use Wealth for income and cash flow, Career for role and responsibility, Spouse for relationship dynamics, and Travel for outside environments and platforms. Keeping one question in focus prevents unrelated symbols from taking over the reading.

Read the Supporting Structure

For a career question, combine Life, Career, Wealth, and Travel. A strong Career Palace with weak Wealth may bring responsibility before compensation; strong Travel support may indicate that a different market or platform improves the role.

Combination one: an empty Life Palace borrowing Tian Fu from the opposite palace should not be read as a person with no identity. Compare Career and Wealth to see where Tian Fu can actually manage resources. Stable Career support may turn the borrowed pattern into administration or planning, while weak support may leave responsibility without enough authority.

Combination two: an annual Hua Ji in Wealth during a decade with Hua Quan in Career can describe more work alongside delayed payment or rising costs. The correct reading separates the natal income model, the decade-long responsibility, and the annual cash-flow trigger instead of letting one difficult symbol define the entire chart.

Chart-Reading Order

Use the birth chart as the baseline, the ten-year cycle as the current chapter, and the annual cycle as the immediate trigger. This order preserves context and turns the chart into practical questions about role, money, movement, and relationships.
