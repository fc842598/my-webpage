Tian Fu Xing in the Career Palace: A Steady, Blessed Career Built Into Lasting Security

Tian Fu Xing in the Career Palace adds steadiness and good fortune to work: one is reliable and treated well with support, suiting stable professional roles, education, welfare-oriented and institutional fields, with firmer position and rewards after midlife. The key is continuing to improve within security and deepening reputation.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-01

The Underlying Logic

Tian Fu Xing in the Career Palace gives steady work and reliable treatment, with firm position and rewards.
