2027 Zi Wei Dou Shu Wealth Palace: Pet Boarding Deposits and Real Cash Flow

Use the 2027 annual Wealth Palace as a question map while separating pet boarding deposits, earned revenue, staffing, and facility costs.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-26 11:03

A pet boarding shop takes thirty holiday reservations. Deposits arrive before most pets do, and the bank balance makes a new row of kennels look affordable. Yet some bookings may be cancelled, extra staff may be needed, and rent is still due. A 2027 Zi Wei Dou Shu reading can organize these questions; it cannot turn an advance payment into earned profit.

Locate the annual palace before reading the money

First confirm the convention used for the start of the 2027 annual cycle. Locate the annual Wealth Palace in Mao, then identify which natal palace occupies that same branch in this particular chart. If it corresponds to Career, staffing and delivery capacity may be central. If it corresponds to Property, examine the lease and equipment. Check the stars, transformations, opposite palace, and the Life-Career-Wealth triangle. A Hua Lu marker is not the same item as Lu Cun or money already earned.

Put deposits beside the work they promise

Suppose customers pay 15,000 yuan in deposits, while 6,000 relates to stays that have not begun and 4,000 may be refundable. Wealth frames the movement of cash. Career asks whether the shop can actually fulfill the bookings: who handles night checks, sick pets, and a last-minute staffing gap? If the annual Wealth placement touches natal Career and Career has a Hua Ji concern, check capacity and cancellation terms before spending. Even a favorable pattern does not recognize revenue before the service is delivered.

Keep the facility decision separate

Property points to kennels, the lease, and the shop's physical constraints. More bookings may coexist with higher fixed costs. Record completed service revenue, refundable deposits, wages, cleaning, rent, and equipment in separate lines. Run the holiday season with current capacity and observe actual occupancy, cancellations, and complaints before expanding.

Read the chart, then read the ledger

Confirm the birth time and annual boundary. Mark the natal palace at Mao, then compare Wealth, Career, and Property with their stars and supporting relationships. Pair every chart question with evidence: booking terms, completed stays, refunds, shifts, and the lease. Contract duties and cash flow decide what the owner can safely spend.
