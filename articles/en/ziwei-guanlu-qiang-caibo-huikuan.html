Zi Wei Dou Shu Career Responsibility and Delayed Commission

A stronger role can precede actual cash receipts.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-09 09:14

A promotion can increase responsibility before a commission is paid. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Career Palace asks what the role requires; the Wealth Palace asks how personal income arrives. Company budget authority is not personal cash.

Read the pay terms

Separate base salary, quarterly bonus and payment-based commission. List the trigger and expected date for each. A signed deal may not pay until acceptance. Do not fund fixed living costs with an unconfirmed bonus.

Compare chart with records

Read natal Career and Wealth, add the client or platform context, then the timing layers. Ask for the written compensation policy and calculate three months of certain cash flow. A title change and an improved bank balance may happen at different times.
