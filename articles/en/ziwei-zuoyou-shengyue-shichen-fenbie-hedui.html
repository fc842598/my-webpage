Zuo Fu and You Bi in a Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart: Check Birth Month Before Birth Hour

Learn why month based supporting stars and hour based writing stars need separate input checks when two Chinese astrology charts differ.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-04 21:11

Two Zi Wei Dou Shu charts can look different when a birth record says only “around one in the morning.” Before choosing the chart that sounds more familiar, separate the inputs. In a common placement method, Zuo Fu and You Bi use the lunar birth month, while Wen Chang and Wen Qu use the birth hour.

Hold the month steady

Copy the date, time range, and birthplace from the best available record. Use the same calendar conversion and chart settings in both runs. Change only the uncertain hour. A date close to a lunar month boundary deserves an extra check: a different calendar setting can move the month based stars even if the recorded civil date is identical.

Compare the palace placements

Suppose a family is unsure whether a birth fell just before or after an hour boundary. With the lunar month fixed, note where Wen Chang and Wen Qu land in each chart. Zuo Fu and You Bi should stay put under the same month rule. If they move too, review the calendar, time zone, and software method before reading the result. In another case, two apps disagree even with a well recorded hour. Compare their lunar dates and placement methods rather than guessing a new birth time.

Interpret only after the calculation

Wen Chang in the Career Palace raises questions about writing and procedure at work; a Travel Palace placement raises questions about communication with people outside the usual team. Neither placement proves talent. Check the main stars, transformations, and actual work samples. Keep both readings open if the hour cannot be established. A personality description alone cannot validate the input.
