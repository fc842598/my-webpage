A Product After Launch: Children and Career Palaces

Use the chart to separate long-term creation from operating duties.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30 09:07

Launching a product starts a longer cycle of care. The Children Palace can frame a work that grows over time; the Career Palace asks who handles fixes and support. A tool with many users and frequent crashes needs stability work. A stable tool with little adoption needs clearer positioning. Compare the chart with retention, support tickets, and actual team capacity before drawing a conclusion.

A product can be treated as an ongoing output in some chart traditions, but the analogy has limits. A bug fix and a new feature request demand different decisions. Check Career for team duties, Friends for collaboration, and Wealth for maintenance cost. Let user reports and work hours guide the release plan.
