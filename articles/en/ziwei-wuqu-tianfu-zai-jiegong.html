Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Health Palace: A Solid, Resilient Body and Turning Endurance and Storage Into Health Without Overdraft

The Health Palace reads the constitution, health tendencies and mishaps. Wu Qu is the finance star governing firm action and metal nature, while Tian Fu is the treasury star governing steadiness and holding, so together in Zi or Wu the constitution tends to be solid, resilient and quick to recover with few routine ailments. The risk is Wu Qu metal showing in bones, breathing and teeth and Tian Fu in the spleen and stomach, plus a character that endures silently and hides stress, which can drag into chronic issues, so the key is regular routine, checkups and early care. This covers only metaphysical tendencies and cannot replace diagnosis; seek proper medical care for discomfort.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Health Palace gives a firm, resilient, active constitution with strong metal tending to affect bones, breathing and teeth, yet also endurance and an acute temper.

Tian Fu there gives a steady constitution with good recovery and reserves, requiring care for the spleen, yet also conservatism and ignoring small signals.

Together they mean firmness plus storage: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars and regular routine the base is thick and healthy; with malefics and Ji and long overdraft, chronic issues from endurance, stress, bones or spleen follow.

The lesson is enduring with rhythm and holding with checks: turn endurance into regular exercise, take checkups and seek early care rather than delaying.
