Debt Management in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Jealousy and Tuo Lo Entanglement in Wealth Palace

Debt is a financial困境 many face, but from the chart perspective, people prone to debt often have specific star structures—Wealth Palace Jealousy (lessons in the money domain), Tuo Lo (entanglement, delay, snowballing), Di Kong/Di Jie (impractical spending or investment). Understanding these signals helps adjust before debt becomes severe.

Chart signals prone to debt: first, Wealth Palace Jealousy—attitude toward money has lessons, may be overspending, investment mistakes, or unstable income. Second, Wealth Palace Tuo Lo—debt easily "entangled," repays then borrows again, more and more, lacks decisive action to彻底 solve. Third, Wealth Palace Di Kong/Di Jie—impractical spending or investment, easily borrows due to "wanting it all at once." Fourth, Life Palace Tan Lang plus malefics—strong desire but lack moderation, easily overspends. Fifth, Decade Jealousy enters Wealth—this decade has high financial pressure, easily incurs debt during this period. Priority for handling different debt types: first priority is "high-interest debt" (credit card installments, online loans, consumer loans), debts with annualized rates over 15% should be repaid first because interest growth exceeds any investment return. Second priority is "medium-interest debt" (car loans, consumer loans), can gradually repay after high-interest debt is cleared. Third priority is "low-interest debt" (mortgage, provident fund loans), can repay normally per contract, no need to prepay. Fourth is "good debt" (business loans, investment loans), if return rate exceeds interest rate, can keep. Methods to escape the debt cycle: first, "stop adding new debt"—this is the most critical step, put credit cards and borrowing tools aside, use cash or debit cards. Second, "inventory all debts"—list amount, interest rate, monthly payment for all debts, create repayment plan (recommend avalanche method: repay highest interest first). Third, "increase income"—for those with Wealth Palace Jealousy, the fundamental solution to debt is increasing income not just saving, consider side jobs, part-time, skill upgrading. Fourth, "build emergency fund"—after debt is cleared, first save 3-6 months of living expenses as emergency fund, avoid borrowing again when unexpected situations arise. What This Question Is Really Asking

Practical case: Someone with Prosperity in Wealth but Jealousy in Tian Zhai has good income but cannot save—earns much but spends much, or loses money through property or family expenses. The key is "income looks at Wealth, preservation looks at Tian Zhai"—both palaces must be read.

The Underlying Logic

Only looking at Wealth Palace without Tian Zhai, earning much but not preserving.

Equating Prosperity with "will definitely get rich," ignoring that Prosperity can also mean "more money more problems."

Not watching Decade/Annual wealth changes, making wrong investments at wrong times.

Treating chart financial tendencies as investment advice, without own research and risk assessment.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Chart the Wealth, Tian Zhai, and Fortune palaces major stars and transformations.
  2. See the Decade Wealth Palace state to judge the ten-year income trend.
  3. Distinguish between "regular income" (salary) and "windfall" (investment, side business) sources.
  4. Check Tian Zhai wealth-preservation ability, set savings and asset allocation plans.
  5. Take initiative in good wealth years, hold steady in bad wealth years.
  6. Regularly review financial status and adjust strategy based on actual conditions.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: The chart only indicates financial tendencies; it does not constitute investment advice.

Mistake 2: Good wealth years can still lose money from wrong decisions; bad wealth years can still preserve capital through caution.

Mistake 3: Financial content is based on licensed institutions and official product descriptions; investments carry risk.
