How to Read Ke Quan Lu Flanking the Life Palace

When Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke sit in the two palaces flanking the Life Palace, outside support surrounds you with reputation and resources on both sides. Its geometry differs from the Three Wonders meeting in the trine; it is rarer and must be checked for malefics or Ji breaking it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

Flanking means the two adjacent palaces hold the Life Palace, which is not the same as meeting from the trine; a tight, clean flank brings support close at hand.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some confuse this pattern with the Three Wonders meeting, while others call any three nearby auspicious transformations a flank; the real test is geometric position and whether anything breaks it.

The Underlying Logic

The flanking palaces are the two adjacent to the Life Palace (Parents and Siblings); three auspicious transformations there form the pattern.

It differs from the Three Wonders: those meet in the trine, while a flank is adjacent, tighter and rarer.

Lu is resources, Quan is control and Ke is reputation; together they bring close support and opportunity.

Check whether the flanking palaces and the Life Palace have malefics, Ji or emptiness; a break discounts or voids the support.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the Life Palace and identify its two flanking palaces
  2. Check each flank for Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke, gathering all three
  3. Confirm they flank rather than meet in the trine, distinguishing the structure
  4. Check whether the Life Palace and flanks have malefics, Ji or emptiness breaking it
  5. Read which major star each transformation attaches to for the concrete form of support
  6. Layer the decade and year to see whether outside support is activated now

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Mistaking the Three Wonders meeting in the trine for a flank

Mistake 2: Calling it a flank when the three transformations are not in the adjacent palaces

Mistake 3: Treating a flank as an automatic win without checking for malefics or Ji

Back to Your Own Chart

This pattern asks whether outside support is truly close at hand. Distinguish flank from meeting and check for breaks, and you know whether the good hand is truly reliable or merely looks busy.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Ke Quan Lu flanking the Life Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Ke Quan Lu flanking the Life Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
