Lian-Zhen in the Companions Palace: Talented, Strong-Willed Friends; Exciting Circles with Emotional Tangles

With Lian-Zhen in Companions, friends are talented, strong-willed and black-and-white; ties are exciting yet complex, suiting professional, interest and creative circles; guard against emotional entanglements, gossip and internal friction.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-27

Your friends tend to be capable and individual; being with them is exciting, but moods swing and feelings run strong, with circles that are tight as brothers one day and at odds the next.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Excitement needs boundaries: enjoy the spark of talent and personality, yet keep out of friends' romances and feuds with a little distance; stay out of gossip and do not take sides, however lively the circle.

The Underlying Logic

Lian-Zhen governs talent, career, emotion and complexity; in Companions, friends are talented and strong-willed.

It suits professional, interest and creative circles, and ties are exciting.

The risk is emotional tangles, gossip and internal friction.

Keep distance, stay out of feuds and avoid taking sides.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Lian-Zhen in Companions and its stars
  2. See whether friends are talented, strong-willed and moody
  3. Use exchange in professional and interest circles
  4. Watch for being drawn into friends' romances and feuds
  5. Stay out of gossip and avoid taking sides
  6. Keep a comfortable distance from exciting friends

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Praising talent without noting emotional entanglements

Mistake 2: Being drawn into friends' feuds and dragged in yourself

Mistake 3: Blindly taking sides as circles heat up and courting trouble

Back to Your Own Chart

Lian-Zhen in Companions surrounds you with talented, story-rich people, and an exciting circle is a real asset. Hold boundaries and stay out of trouble, and exciting ties inspire you rather than drain you.
