Money Arguments in a Zi Wei Dou Shu Relationship Reading

Separate a real budget gap from fear about future income.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28 09:11

When partners say there is not enough money, they may mean two different things. Bills may exceed current income, or the accounts may be sound while one partner fears future unemployment. The Wealth Palace frames cash flow, the Wellbeing Palace insecurity, and the Spouse Palace decision rules.

Start with three months of income, bills, debt, and savings. A delayed client payment differs from anxiety about possible job loss. Agree on a reserve target and who can approve a large purchase. Compare natal palaces and annual transformations, then use bank records and a scheduled conversation to decide what changes. The chart cannot establish whether the household has a deficit.
