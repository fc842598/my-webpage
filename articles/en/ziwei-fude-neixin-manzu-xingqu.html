The Wellbeing Palace: Inner Satisfaction, Interests, and How You Rest

The Wellbeing Palace covers inner life, hobbies, rest, and mental satisfaction, separate from the Wealth Palace's money reading.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 10:35

Having money does not always mean feeling happy, and that is exactly what the Wellbeing Palace covers. It represents the inner world, hobbies, rest, and mental satisfaction. The Wealth Palace handles moving money, while the Wellbeing Palace handles whether the mind feels settled, and the two should be read separately.

What it includes

This palace describes where you naturally find joy: reading, music, exercise, time alone, creating, or being with people, with different stars pointing to different kinds of mental nourishment. It also shows whether you can switch off after work or keep thinking instead. Mental enjoyment and taste appear here.

Inner satisfaction versus money

A strong Wealth Palace describes how resources and income are handled, while a strong Wellbeing Palace points to contentment and emotional calm, and they do not move together. A weak or tense Wellbeing Palace may show up as restlessness, anxiety, or difficulty relaxing, but this is not fixed; routines and interests can adjust it.

Step-by-step reading

  1. Confirm whether the topic is interest, rest, emotion, or mental satisfaction.
  2. Keep the Wealth Palace's money reading separate from the Wellbeing Palace.
  3. Read the major stars to see what attracts the person and how they relax.
  4. Check helper stars and transformations to distinguish calm from anxiety.
  5. Include the opposite and supporting palaces before concluding on the inner state.

Reading limits

The palace describes mental tendencies; it does not directly decide happiness or guarantee a particular hobby. Tense stars should not be read as permanent anxiety. Real routines, exercise, and mental adjustment can change the state, while the chart flags preferences and habits decide stability.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and locate the Wellbeing Palace with its supporting palaces.
  2. Separate mental topics from material topics first.
  3. Read the palace stars for interests and ways of relaxing.
  4. Then check helper stars and transformations for emotional stability.
  5. Match the conclusion to real routines without absolute claims about happiness.
