Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Wealth Palace: Boldly Breaking Earning Patterns and Turning the Finance Star and Change Into Grounded Income

The Wealth Palace reads the earning style and cash state. Wu Qu is the finance star governing firm action and execution while Po Jun governs change, depletion and rebuilding, so together in Si or Hai one is bold in earning, daring to break and rebuild and making money through transformation and pioneering, suiting business, entrepreneurship, finance and restructuring posts. The risk is heavy depletion, frequent change and unstable cash flow, so the key is making change planned with cash flow and a way back. This is only a metaphysical reference and not investment advice.

People with Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Wealth Palace earn boldly and find chances in transformation and troubled situations, often succeeding after breaking; yet when change is fierce and no base is kept, depletion is heavy and cash flow easily breaks.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the wealth-seeking pattern with Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Wealth Palace. Understand the palace reads cash, then see how the finance star and change combine so one dares to pioneer while keeping cash flow.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Wealth Palace gives practical, decisive earning that dares to discuss money, yet also rigidity and seeking quick gains.

Po Jun there gives earning meeting change, daring to break and rebuild and succeeding after breaking, yet also change, depletion and starting over.

Together they mean finance star plus change: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars under planning transformation becomes great wealth; with malefics and Ji and no restraint, frequent forcing and losses from hard pushing and depletion follow.

The lesson is breaking with planning and earning with a way back: advance change in nodes, keep cash flow, and hear opinions before large decisions and act within one’s means.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Po Jun share the Wealth Palace and check their brightness
  2. Advance change in nodes rather than overturning all at once
  3. Keep cash flow and a way back to avoid runaway depletion
  4. Hear opinions before large decisions to avoid stubborn forcing
  5. Build through skill and long-term reputation rather than betting on shortcuts
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of earning activation and harvest

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Po Jun in the Wealth Palace only as bold and change-ready while ignoring depletion

Mistake 2: Frequently starting over without cash flow, letting depletion run away

Mistake 3: Stubborn forcing, with large decisions beyond one’s means
