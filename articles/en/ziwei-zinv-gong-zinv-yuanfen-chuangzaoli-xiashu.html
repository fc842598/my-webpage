The Children Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Beyond Offspring—Creativity, Students and Subordinates

The Children Palace governs bonds with children and by extension creativity, output and works, students/juniors and subordinates, plus some romance and partnership. Auspicious transformations mean capable juniors and rich creativity; malefics/Ji mean more worry over what you create or lead.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Its broad core is what you give birth to: children, works, ideas and people you lead; read it to know whether creating and leading flow or drain you.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People without children assume this palace is irrelevant, yet it governs creative output and juniors—a key palace for career and self-expression.

The Underlying Logic

Its core meaning is offspring bonds, number tendency, children's character and parent-child relations; modern reading favors quality over number.

Broadly it covers creativity and works—writing, design, R&D, content—the ability to produce something from nothing.

In relations it covers students, apprentices and subordinates you lead, distinct from the Friends palace of peers and colleagues.

It also informs romance, partnership and outward spending, read with Spouse, Happiness and Wealth.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Children major star and transformations for the overall produce-and-lead tone
  2. With children: read the relating mode and bond lessons
  3. Without: treat it as creativity, checking whether output and expression flow
  4. At work use it for whether subordinates, students and juniors are capable and easy to lead
  5. With malefics/Ji, anticipate creative blocks or leadership worry and adjust early
  6. When the decade Children activates, expect a child, a launch, team growth or partnership change

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming the palace is useless without children

Mistake 2: Blending subordinates and friends, missing that it covers juniors

Mistake 3: Only reading children's gender/number, ignoring the modern creativity layer

Back to Your Own Chart

Children is about what you can create and raise—children, works or teams. Beyond the literal, it describes your ability to turn ideas into results.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Children Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Children Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
