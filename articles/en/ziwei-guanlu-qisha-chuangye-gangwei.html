Qi Sha in the Career Palace may indicate decisive work under pressure, but it does not prove that starting a company is the right choice.

Read the chart in layers

Qi Sha in the Career Palace can suggest a forceful way of handling responsibility. It does not name a job or prove that a person should start a business. An emergency coordinator can make rapid calls inside an institution; a solo consultant must also sell, invoice, and carry losses. Read Life, Wealth, and Friends with Career, then test the business assumptions in a small project.
