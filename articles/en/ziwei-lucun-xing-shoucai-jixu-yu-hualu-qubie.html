Lu Cun in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Savings Star Is Not the Same as Hua Lu

Lu Cun is set by the birth-year stem, yin earth, governing steady accumulation, retained wealth and existing blessing—the stock. Hua Lu is flowing opportunity and gain—the flow. One keeps, one brings; they read differently.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Lu Cun answers whether you can hold wealth and have a base; Hua Lu where chances come from. Real security needs both: earn and keep.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People see Hua Lu and predict wealth without Lu Cun to hold it, so money comes and goes; others have Lu Cun without Hua Lu, guarding stock with no new inflow—both are one-sided.

The Underlying Logic

Lu Cun is a northern assistant of yin earth, always flanked by Yang and Tuo, governing the treasury, salary, steady income and conservatism.

It is stock: savings, assets, fixed returns and existing blessing; Hua Lu is flow: chances, encounters and newly arriving resources.

Sharing or meeting a major star gives that palace a stable base, but flanked by Yang/Tuo it also brings caution and a guarded, hard-to-release side.

It loves meeting Tian Ma (Lu-Ma galloping) or Hua Lu (double Lu); when keeping and flowing work together, wealth is both steady and growing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate Lu Cun to fix where you accumulate best
  2. Separate Lu Cun (stock) from Hua Lu (flow), never blending them
  3. Check the Yang/Tuo flank for caution and guardedness
  4. Find the major star it meets to see how you accumulate
  5. Expand when it meets Hua Lu or Tian Ma; with Lu Cun alone, favor conservation and saving
  6. In years the decade activates Lu Cun, buy property, lock deposits and secure long-term returns

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Lu Cun and Hua Lu as the same wealth

Mistake 2: Assuming Lu Cun alone means wealth while it only keeps, not brings

Mistake 3: Ignoring the Yang/Tuo flank and missing its guarded caution

Back to Your Own Chart

Lu Cun is your financial ballast. Grasp its division of labor with Hua Lu—one keeps, one brings—and you neither spend what you earn nor merely hoard without growth.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Lu Cun the retained-wealth star before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Lu Cun the retained-wealth star line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
