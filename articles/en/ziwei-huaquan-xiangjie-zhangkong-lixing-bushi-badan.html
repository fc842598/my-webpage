Hua Quan Explained in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Control, Drive and Pressure as Two Sides of One Coin

Hua Quan is fire, governing control, expansion, action and say-so, strengthening and activating its palace. Positively boldness, responsibility, leadership and decisiveness; negatively dominance, control, pressure and going it alone. Unlike Hua Lu's smooth gain, Quan is contested and shouldered—leadership when well used, oppression when overdone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

Lu is things coming to you, Quan your taking and carrying; it gives power and initiative, but greater force means greater pressure on yourself and those nearby.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Hua Quan is read as promotion while ignoring toil, dominance and strained relations; mature Quan holds control without controlling others, able to carry and to share power.

The Underlying Logic

Hua Quan is fire governing authority, control, expansion, action and competition, making its palace proactive, stronger and faster.

Positive: bold, decisive, strong executor who carries responsibility and leads—suited to management, frontier work and decision seats.

Negative: dominant, controlling, stubborn, pressuring, prone to overwork; in family palaces it brings power struggles in relationships.

Harmony: with Lu it pairs resources and control (best); with Ke it is firm yet gentle with repute; with Ji authority meets obstruction—avoid white-knuckling.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate Hua Quan to see where control concentrates
  2. At work: take initiative, practice decision and team leadership
  3. Guard dominance: deliberately delegate and listen, avoid doing all
  4. In family palaces: win less, consult more in relationships
  5. Lead boldly with Lu/Ke; with Ji reduce pressure instead of forcing
  6. Direct control at self-management, not controlling others

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading Hua Quan only as promotion

Mistake 2: Ignoring dominance and overwork

Mistake 3: Using control to dominate those nearby

Back to Your Own Chart
