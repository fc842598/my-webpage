How the Lai-Yin Palace Splits Self-Reliant from Other-Reliant Patterns: First, This Is an Internal Frame of the Qin-Tian School

The palace holding the birth stem is the Lai-Yin palace; in Life, Health or Virtue it is a self-reliant pattern, in relative palaces an other-reliant one, and in Wealth, Career, Property or Travel a wealth-and-career pattern. Note this is an internal frame of the Qin-Tian school, not a consensus across schools.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-27

The term Lai-Yin gets tossed around many charts, but it is really one school's internal frame. State the boundary before using it, so a single-school view is not treated as a universal rule.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The Lai-Yin logic is: the palace where the birth stem arises hints which domain pulls your life. In your own houses you stand on your own, in relatives' houses you are pulled by relationships, and in wealth-career-property-travel you are led by worldly matters.

The Underlying Logic

Lai-Yin is the palace holding the birth stem, taken as where this life's cause and lesson begin.

In Life, Health or Virtue it is self-reliant, standing mainly on one's own character and will.

In sibling, spouse, children or servant relative palaces it is other-reliant, led by relationships and people.

In Wealth, Career, Property or Travel it is a wealth-career pattern, pulled by worldly matters and achievement.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First note the birth stem and find which palace it occupies
  2. Classify it as Life-Health-Virtue, relative, or wealth-career-property-travel
  3. For Life-Health-Virtue read self-reliant and focus on inner will
  4. For relative palaces read other-reliant and focus on relations and boundaries
  5. For wealth-career-property-travel read the worldly pattern and focus on worldly affairs
  6. Always state this is a Qin-Tian frame and leave room when comparing with other schools

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Lai-Yin as a universal rule across schools without naming its origin

Mistake 2: Seeing Lai-Yin in relatives and concluding lifelong dependence while ignoring later choice

Mistake 3: Deciding a life by Lai-Yin alone and ignoring the actual stars and transformations

Back to Your Own Chart

Lai-Yin reads like a hint about which domain tends to pull your life. It is one school's doorway, not a verdict: self-reliance is built inward, other-reliance through relations, and the worldly pattern through doing things; tell them apart to use it steadily.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Lai-Yin palace: self-reliant and other-reliant patterns before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Lai-Yin palace: self-reliant and other-reliant patterns line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
