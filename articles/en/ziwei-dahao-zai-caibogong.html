Da Hao in the Wealth Palace: When Cash Flow Swings Wide and How to Hold the Opening

Da Hao in the Wealth Palace makes the relationship with money loose, prone to large inflows and outflows, low sensitivity to cost and thin reserves, with sizable spending possible in investment, business or social obligations. It does not mean no money; the key is disciplining cash flow with a budget, savings and risk control.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-01

People with Da Hao in the Wealth Palace often see busy accounts and quiet balances, with sizable turnover but little retained; watching the outlets is what lets money stay.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what to guard with Da Hao in the Wealth Palace. First separate its cash-flow scatter, then see whether auspicious stars aid inflows or malefics add outflows.

The Underlying Logic

Da Hao in the Wealth Palace brings large inflows and outflows, weak cost awareness and unexpected major expenses.

In business or sales with Lu and Quan, large turnover may be a working need and can be profitable.

With malefics or void stars, loss, deception and failed investment follow, so risk control and due diligence must be strict.

Budgets, automatic savings and limits on credit and debt are the key tools that hold the opening shut.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Da Hao is in the Wealth Palace and see the sharing major star
  2. Automatically save a fixed share of income first
  3. Set approval and cooling-off rules for large spending, investments and obligations
  4. Track expenses for a month or two to find the biggest leak
  5. Control debt and guarantees and avoid carrying uncertain money for others
  6. With the decade and year, leave room ahead of loss-signaling years

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Da Hao in the Wealth Palace only as being unable to keep money

Mistake 2: Enjoying the turnover without budgets or risk control

Mistake 3: Giving guarantees and lending from obligation and carrying others’ losses

Back to Your Own Chart

Da Hao in the Wealth Palace signals loose cash flow rather than a poor fate. Automating savings and managing large expenses and debt let money accumulate despite lively turnover. This is not investment advice; for any product, rely on licensed institutions and official disclosures.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Da Hao in the Wealth Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Da Hao in the Wealth Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
