How to Read Lu and Ji Clashing Across the Axis in Zi Wei Dou Shu

When Lu and Ji land in opposite palaces, resources and concern pull across the axis, often showing gain on one side and outflow on the other. Read the net flow rather than fixating on only Lu or only Ji.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

Some see Lu yet cannot keep money, often because the opposite Ji pulls: gain here and concern or spending there. Both ends must be read.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The Lu-Ji clash is not simply gain then loss but a structural in-and-out. The real question is whether what Lu brings covers the ongoing investment Ji demands and whether the net is positive.

The Underlying Logic

Lu-Ji clash means Lu and Ji sit in opposite palaces and pull along the axis.

The Lu palace gives resources, opportunity or gain, while the Ji palace brings concern, spending or repeated investment.

It shows one kind of matter flowing in through one area and out through another, so read the net flow.

If Lu outweighs Ji with auspicious support the net can be positive; if Ji is heavier, much comes in but much also goes.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find which two palaces Lu and Ji land in
  2. Confirm whether they form an opposite pair
  3. Read what resources the Lu palace gives and what investment the Ji palace demands
  4. Understand the in-and-out pull along the axis
  5. Compare the weight of Lu and Ji and any auspicious support for the net flow
  6. Give conditional advice from the net effect rather than declaring gain then loss

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring good fortune at Lu and ignoring the opposite Ji

Mistake 2: Applying the gain-then-loss cliche without detailed calculation

Mistake 3: Reading one palace and failing to combine the in-and-out into a net flow

Back to Your Own Chart

The Lu-Ji clash is a balance sheet, not black and white. Spread out the resources and concern on both sides, total the net, and you see whether the matter holds.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Lu and Ji clashing across the axis before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Lu and Ji clashing across the axis line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
