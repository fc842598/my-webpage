Zi Wei Dou Shu Brightness Labels Differ: Do You Need a New Chart?

When star placements match but brightness labels differ, compare the rule tables before reading career claims.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:15

Two Zi Wei Dou Shu apps place the same main star in the Career Palace but assign different brightness labels. Keep star placement and brightness as separate facts. The whole chart does not become wrong because one label changed.

Isolate the difference

Compare birth input, Life Palace, major stars, and natal transformations first. If these match, look for different brightness tables or app versions. If a star's palace changes, inspect the calculation rules instead. Note which career claims actually depend on the brightness label.

Return to work evidence

The same Career Palace can describe a planner or a technician with very different duties. Supporting stars, transformations, resources, and real job tasks matter more than a single adjective. Read the placement, then the brightness convention, then the full palace combination. Treat opposite conclusions based solely on a label as unresolved.
