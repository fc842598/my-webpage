The Ji-Yue Tong-Liang Pattern: Even After Forming, Check the Career Scenario and Star Conditions

Ji-Yue Tong-Liang describes the planning and clerical tendency of Tian-Ji, Tai-Yin, Tong-Tian and Tian-Liang, but it is not a fixed job list. Brightness and transformations decide which kind of stable work it becomes.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Ji-Yue Tong-Liang is often summed up as suited to government work, which is too coarse. It does lean toward thinking, planning and institutional paths, but civil service, professional services and product planning are three different kinds of stability.

What This Question Is Really Asking

What must be broken is the reading that a pattern locks in a career. Forming only shows the tendency; which job it becomes depends on the four stars' palaces and brightness, and on which potential abilities the transformations turn into tasks.

The Underlying Logic

Ji-Yue Tong-Liang is a structural tendency, not a fixed career list.

Tian-Ji handles thinking and planning, Tai-Yin detailed clerical work, Tong-Tian coordination, and Tian-Liang oversight, each with a role.

The palaces and brightness of the four stars change expression, and weak brightness discounts the advantage.

Transformations turn potential abilities into concrete tasks or pressure, the key to the pattern landing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Tian-Ji, Tai-Yin, Tong-Tian and Tian-Liang form the pattern in the triple-direction
  2. Check each star's palace and brightness to see which is strongest
  3. Read the Career Palace to translate the tendency into concrete work tasks
  4. Compare civil service, professional services and product planning to see which stable path fits the chart
  5. Read the transformations to confirm which abilities become promotion tasks or pressure
  6. Use the decade to judge when this stable path is smoothest before choosing

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reducing Ji-Yue Tong-Liang to government work in one sentence

Mistake 2: Looking only at the name and not the four stars' brightness and palaces

Mistake 3: Ignoring transformations and unable to explain why the same pattern leads some to management and others to execution

Back to Your Own Chart

Ji-Yue Tong-Liang gives a tendency to work through intellect and institutions, not a job assignment table. See the four stars and transformations clearly to find the kind of stability that belongs to you.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the career scenarios of the Ji-Yue Tong-Liang pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the career scenarios of the Ji-Yue Tong-Liang pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
