Changing Jobs: Read the Career Palace Before the Travel Palace

A practical Zi Wei Dou Shu guide for separating a role change from a platform change when you are thinking about switching jobs.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-30 00:17

When job-change questions show up in Zi Wei Dou Shu, the first split is between the Career Palace and the Travel Palace. One is about the role itself, the other is about the platform, market, or outside world that may need to change.

What This Means

The travel palace also means platforms, markets, and life outside the familiar setting. That is why some charts grow only after movement or relocation.

How To Read It

Do not read movement as travel only. Compare the outside-world palace with the Life Palace and Career Palace to see whether a bigger stage strengthens the chart or simply adds stress.

Simple Examples

  • When the career palace is activated, read responsibility, role, and income together.
  • A move, a bigger platform, or a new market can activate the chart more strongly than staying in the original environment.
  • A chart may stay average at home but become far more visible after a move or platform change.

Practical Order

For a topic like "Changing Jobs: Read the Career Palace Before the Travel Palace," start with the main palace named in the topic, then compare the opposite palace and the supporting structure. Finish by testing role, responsibility, and how the career line cashes out so the reading stays concrete instead of drifting into a fixed label.
