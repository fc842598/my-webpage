How to Read Friends Growing Apart: Accepting the Life Cycle of Relationships

Friends often grow apart as stages, cities and life priorities change; this is a natural part of relationships and does not mean anyone is at fault. Cherish the shared memories, actively maintain those you still care about, and accept that some people only walk with you for a while, directing energy to comfortable, mutual relationships rather than blaming yourself or forcing bonds.
