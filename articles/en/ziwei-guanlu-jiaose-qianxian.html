How to Read Job Boundaries in the Zi Wei Career Palace

Promotion can add responsibility before authority or pay. Read the Career Palace with Life, Travel, and Friends to define the real role.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 11:00

A promotion can feel heavier when responsibility rises before authority, support, or pay. The Career Palace describes work roles and obligations, not only a title.

Define the boundary first to see whether the change is growth, compensation, or a temporary cover.

What is the role asking?

Hua Quan pushes decisions and accountability, Hua Ke emphasizes expertise and standards, Hua Lu brings resources and orders, and Hua Ji shows process or responsibility friction.

The Life Palace tells you whether the person can carry the role.

Add Travel and Friends

Travel shows the outside platform; Friends shows team allocation. A strong Career with weak Friends can carry work without support. A strong Travel Palace can bring opportunity without an immediate title.

Translate the chart into who funds, signs, and carries the result.

Examples

Hua Quan in Career with Hua Lu in Travel can expand external projects, but authority and revenue share need definition. Hua Ke in Career with Hua Ji in Friends needs written workflow and records.

Practical reading order

Read Career for role, Life for capacity, Travel for platform, Friends for collaboration, and timing for the promotion window.

Practical Reading Order

Start with the question palace, compare its opposite and trine partners, then add timing and transformations before making a practical judgment.
