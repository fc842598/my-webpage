Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Wealth Palace: Bold for Big Deals While Guarding Against Easy Come, Easy Go

Wu Qu is the wealth star and Qi Sha the general star; together in the Wealth Palace, the earning style is hard-edged, bold for big deals and able to take money through competition, but money arrives and leaves fast. The key is whether storehouse stars and planning retain it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-30

Some earn money like fighting a campaign, choosing challenging, large deals and able to win them; but this financial path most fears money entering one hand and leaving the other—earning much yet retaining little.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to read Wu Qu and Qi Sha guarding Wealth together. Separate bold earning from retention: the first rests on star nature, the second on storehouse stars, malefics, Ji and spending habits.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu governs wealth and rules, Qi Sha governs decisiveness and execution; together the wealth star meets the general star, earning with boldness and willingness to compete.

It suits large deals, project-based, pioneering and highly competitive income, with large elasticity and a high ceiling.

The risk is breaking: Qi Sha carries solitary firmness and Wu Qu favors hard edges, so money comes and goes with big swings, especially with heavy malefics and Ji.

Whether it is retained depends on storehouse stars such as Tian Fu or Lu Cun in the triple-direction and on your own savings plan; otherwise it is merely passing money.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Qi Sha guard Wealth together and check brightness
  2. Separate bold earning from retention
  3. Check whether the triple-direction has storehouse stars such as Tian Fu or Lu Cun
  4. Count malefics and Ji to weigh big swings and loss risk
  5. Preset a savings ratio for large-deal income, moving it aside on arrival before spending
  6. With the decade and year, take bold deals when the wealth stars activate and guard cash in heavy-malefic years

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging great wealth from Wu Qu and Qi Sha while ignoring retention conditions

Mistake 2: Treating large-deal income as steady income when planning expenses, leading to tight off-seasons

Mistake 3: Only chasing earnings without planning retention, earning much yet never saving

Back to Your Own Chart

Wu Qu and Qi Sha guarding Wealth gift boldness for big deals, and the lesson is retaining money. Pair it with storehouse stars and savings discipline; then the pair’s high ceiling truly becomes the balance in your account.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Wealth Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Wealth Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
