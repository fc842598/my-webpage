Zi Wei Dou Shu on Domestic Violence: Personal Safety Comes First

What to do when facing domestic violence often maps to the Spouse Palace for the relationship and the Health Palace for safety. Put safety first, leave danger promptly, call the police and seek care, keep evidence, turn to support services and lawfully apply for a personal safety protection order, rather than staying silent.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30

The key is personal safety first and prompt police reports with evidence, as silence only lets risk accumulate.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some forgive repeatedly out of shame or worry, without police reports or injury evidence, and find no records and little help when violence escalates.

The Underlying Logic

Domestic violence is often triggered when the Spouse Palace covers the relationship and the Health Palace safety, marking physical or mental harm within the family.

Leave danger and call the police immediately, seek care and keep medical records, injury photos and evidence.

Turn to community committees, women’s federations and the police, and apply for a personal safety protection order where conditions are met.

The Health Palace shows tendencies rather than diagnosis, so seek formal care for injuries; with Hua Ji and malefics, prioritize safety and keep evidence, with professional legal and support-service advice as the basis.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First ensure personal safety and leave the dangerous environment if needed
  2. Call the police immediately and keep the report receipt
  3. Seek care promptly and save medical records, injury photos and evidence
  4. Read the Spouse and Health Palaces to judge the relationship and safety risk
  5. Turn to women’s federations, community committees or support services
  6. Apply for a personal safety protection order where conditions are met, with professional legal advice as the basis

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Staying silent and forgiving without police reports or evidence

Mistake 2: Not seeking care for injuries, leaving them unproven

Mistake 3: Not knowing about protection orders and missing legal protection
