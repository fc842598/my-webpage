Monthly and Daily Fortune in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How to Derive Them and When You Actually Need Them

Monthly fortune starts the first lunar month at the yearly Dou-Jun palace and proceeds in order; daily fortune starts day one at the monthly palace. They govern short-term fluctuations and are needed only for timing precise decisions—signings, interviews, trips; otherwise decade and year suffice.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Monthly/daily fortune is a magnifier, not the main plot: decade and year decide whether an event exists; month/day only help pick the smoother time to act.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People read daily fortune and let it steer mood, but detached from decade and year a single day's blip never decides the result—self-imposed drama.

The Underlying Logic

First set the yearly Dou-Jun: from the year branch and birth month, as the starting palace for that year's first month.

Monthly: the first month begins at Dou-Jun and proceeds around the twelve palaces, one per month, for monthly focus and transformations.

Daily: day one begins at that month's palace, one palace per day; hourly then begins zi-hour at the daily palace.

Hierarchy rule: a month cannot overturn a year, a day cannot overturn a month; short charts only time moves within what the long chart allows.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Use decade and year first to decide whether the matter exists this year and is worth doing
  2. Once committed, set the yearly Dou-Jun palace
  3. Proceed from Dou-Jun to the target month's palace and transformations
  4. Within a favorable month, use daily fortune to pick a good specific day
  5. Time signings, interviews and trips to the day; everyday decisions need not
  6. Any bad month/day is only a timing note, never overturning the yearly read

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading daily fortune constantly, led by short blips

Mistake 2: Letting month/day overturn decade/year trends

Mistake 3: Misordering the derivation, arranging months before fixing Dou-Jun

Back to Your Own Chart

Monthly and daily fortune are tools, not anxiety. Read the main plot with decade and year, then use month/day to time important moves—that is the correct use of short-term charts.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to monthly and daily fortune before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the monthly and daily fortune line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
