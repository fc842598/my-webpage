The Siblings Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu Is Not Just About Siblings: It Also Rules Mother and Your Cash Reserves

In practice the Siblings Palace maps onto three things: relationships with siblings and peers, the bond with your mother, and the liquid cash you can draw on. It is not only for counting brothers; while Wealth Palace reads income, the Siblings Palace reads how thick your ready cash is.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

Many hear Siblings Palace and think it counts brothers and sisters. In Zi Wei this palace holds three jobs: siblings, mother, and the liquid cash you can move day to day.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Separate the three real lines of the Siblings Palace: siblings are human bonds, the mother line is the tie with and care for elders, and the wealth line is cash reserves. All three share one palace, so a star's quality touches all three at once.

The Underlying Logic

The Siblings Palace originally reads sibling ties, and in practice extends to peers such as colleagues, classmates and partners.

Among kin it corresponds to the mother; its quality often reflects the bond, care and interaction with one's mother.

Financially it corresponds to liquid reserves, the ready cash after salary, distinct from the fixed assets ruled by the Property Palace.

When it holds a malefic or Hua Ji, watch three things at once: peer disputes, mother's health and care, and cash flow.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate your Siblings Palace and the stars in it
  2. Read the sibling-peer line: how you get along with brothers, colleagues and classmates
  3. Read the mother line: closeness to your mother and whether care has been heavy lately
  4. Read the wealth line: whether ready cash covers three to six months of turnover
  5. When a malefic or Hua Ji sits there, prioritize the three lines together
  6. Mother's health is only a tendency reminder; persistent discomfort follows formal medical care

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Using the Siblings Palace only to count siblings while missing mother and cash flow

Mistake 2: Reading a malefic there as only sibling conflict while missing tight cash turnover

Mistake 3: Declaring certain illness for the mother from a bad Siblings Palace; state it only as a tendency
