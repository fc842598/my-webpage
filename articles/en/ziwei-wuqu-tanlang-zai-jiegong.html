Wu Qu and Tan Lang in the Health Palace: A Body Vulnerable to Overuse and Entertaining, Turning Desire and Drive Into Moderate Health

The Health Palace reads the bodily foundation and health tendencies. Wu Qu is metal, governing firmness and the metal-related breathing, teeth, bones and injuries, while Tan Lang is wood with water, governing desire, entertaining, the reproductive and urinary systems and the liver and gallbladder, so together in Chou or Wei the body is often not weak by birth but vulnerable to depletion from overuse, entertaining, alcohol and unchecked desire. The stars show tendencies rather than a diagnosis, so the key is regular routine and checkups and prompt care at a proper hospital when unwell.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what to watch with Wu Qu and Tan Lang in the Health Palace. Understand it reads health tendencies, then see the metal and wood natures, practice moderation early, and do not treat the stars as a medical diagnosis.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Health Palace, metal governs breathing, the nose, teeth, bones and metal injuries, and a firm character can endure, yet also tends to force through, bump into things and ignore small wounds.

Tan Lang there governs desire, entertaining, alcohol, the reproductive and urinary systems and the liver, with strong energy, yet also depletion from overuse, entertaining and lack of restraint.

Together they mean metal plus wood: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars and a disciplined life recovery is strong; with malefics and Ji and unchecked Tan Lang, chronic metabolic, reproductive, urinary or breathing problems can follow.

The lesson is desire with restraint and endurance with rest: manage entertaining, late nights and desire, take regular checkups, seek prompt proper care for lasting symptoms, and treat the stars only as tendencies.

  2. Manage entertaining, late nights, alcohol and desire with restraint

Mistake 2: Lack of restraint in entertaining and desire, slowly depleting the body

Mistake 3: Self-diagnosing from the stars or forcing through discomfort without care
