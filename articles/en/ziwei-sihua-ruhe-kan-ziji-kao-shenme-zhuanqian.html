Using the Four Transformations to See What You Earn From: Where Lu, Quan and Ke Land

To see what someone earns from, do not fixate on the Wealth Palace alone; read where the natal Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke land together with the Life, Wealth and Career palaces: Hua Lu often points to resources and opportunity, Hua Quan to control and professional authority, Hua Ke to reputation and credentials. The key is locating your main income model and then making it real through action. This is a tendency, not a fixed result; money matters follow licensed institutions and official product terms and this is not investment advice.

Many people want to know what they are best suited to earn from, and the answer is usually not one star but which energy the transformations place on the Life, Wealth and Career line.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to use the transformations to locate an income model. First distinguish what Lu, Quan and Ke each point to, then read their palaces rather than treating Hua Lu as guaranteed wealth.

The Underlying Logic

Hua Lu governs resources, opportunity, connection and smooth flow, and whichever field it lands in tends to bring income that is offered and can be received.

Hua Quan governs control, execution and professional authority, and its palace often points to income earned through ability, competition and leadership.

Hua Ke governs reputation, credentials and dignity, and its palace points to income from recognized word of mouth, qualifications and work.

Read the three together with Life, Wealth and Career, and use the decade and year to see when they activate; the transformations give an innate tendency, while making it succeed still depends on accumulation and action.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Cast the natal chart and find which palaces the natal Lu, Quan and Ke land in
  2. Note the real-life fields of the three palaces: work, networks, reputation, home and so on
  3. With Life, Wealth and Career, judge whether the main income model leans to resources, control or credentials
  4. Read the decade and year transformations to see when this model is most easily activated
  5. Choose platforms, roles or business directions accordingly and make the tendency real through action
  6. Keep room to test and adjust, not treating one reading as a lifelong fixed label

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fixating on the Wealth Palace and declaring wealth at Hua Lu while ignoring Quan, Ke and the palaces

Mistake 2: Taking the tendency as a guarantee that success comes without effort

Mistake 3: Forcing the answer you want rather than reading the palaces and triple-direction honestly

Back to Your Own Chart

Using the transformations to see what you earn from gives an income-aptitude map. Reading where Lu, Quan and Ke land and making them real through stages and action makes it easier to turn innate strengths into steady income.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to using the transformations to see what you earn from before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the using the transformations to see what you earn from line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
