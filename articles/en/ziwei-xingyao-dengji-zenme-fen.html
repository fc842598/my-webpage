Zi Wei Dou Shu Star Hierarchy: What Major, Assistant, Malefic and Minor Stars Each Do

The stars fall into four tiers: the fourteen major stars set the main themes, the six auspicious assistants add support, the four malefics plus emptiness stars add pressure, and many minor stars fill in detail. Read major stars first and minor stars last, or you may judge big matters from a tiny star.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Beginners are overwhelmed by dozens of stars, but they are not equal; they have ranks and roles, and once you learn the layers it stops being confusing.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is which stars to read first and which are only modifiers. Sorting stars into four tiers with different weights gives the reading an order.

The Underlying Logic

The fourteen major stars are the leads in each palace and set its main theme.

Assistant stars such as Zuo Fu You Bi, Tian Kui Tian Yue, Wen Chang Wen Qu and Lu Cun Tian Ma stand for support, patrons, documents and stability, adding to the major star.

Malefics such as Qing Yang Tuo Luo, Huo Xing Ling Xing and Di Kong Di Jie stand for pressure, friction, drive and drain, posing the lessons.

Minor stars such as Hong Luan Tian Xi, Tian Xing Tian Yao, Hua Gai Xian Chi and Long Chi Feng Ge are many but light; they add detail and color rather than deciding fortune alone.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find the major stars in each palace to set the theme
  2. Mark where assistant stars fall to see support and patrons
  3. Count the malefics and emptiness stars to see pressure and friction
  4. Mark the four transformations on the major stars to set energy flow
  5. Only then scan minor stars for detail
  6. Conclude mainly from major stars and transformations, never letting minor stars dominate

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating all stars as equal and judging big matters from one tiny minor star

Mistake 2: Memorizing names without ranks, so the reading has no order

Mistake 3: Assuming malefics are all bad and assistants all good, forgetting they only modify the major star

Back to Your Own Chart

Star tiers give a reading its priorities: major stars set the tone, assistants add, malefics pose lessons and minor stars color it. Read large before small and the chart will not pull you around.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the hierarchy and roles of stars before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the hierarchy and roles of stars line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
