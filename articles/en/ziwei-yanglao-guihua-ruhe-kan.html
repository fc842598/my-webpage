How to Read Retirement Planning

Retirement reads Property (housing), Karma (spirit) and Wealth (pension) together with decade timing. But planning ultimately relies on proper financial products, social security, contracts and licensed institutions; astrology only gives direction, not investment advice.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Retirement planning is like digging a well for later years in advance; Property, Wealth and Karma show where and whether there is water; but the well is dug with proper tools.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some rely only on timing without real planning, others feel retirement is far off and do not prepare; the right way plans early, uses proper products and keeps contracts.

The Underlying Logic

Property reads retirement housing.

Wealth reads pension accumulation.

Karma reads later spiritual state.

Products, social security and contracts rely on licensed institutions and writing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read Property, Wealth and Karma timing
  2. Estimate retirement funds and housing needs
  3. Understand social security and proper retirement products
  4. Allocate conservatively with clear contracts
  5. Keep enough medical and emergency arrangements
  6. Review regularly, not investment advice

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Relying only on timing without real planning

Mistake 2: Feeling retirement is far and not preparing

Mistake 3: Trusting non-formal retirement products
