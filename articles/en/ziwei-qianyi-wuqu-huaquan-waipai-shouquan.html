Wu Qu Hua Quan in the Travel Palace: Check the Mandate

Compare an overseas or regional assignment with written authority.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-02 10:14

Wu Qu with Hua Quan in the Travel Palace can frame a question about responsibility outside the home office. It does not create legal authority. Read Career for the role and Wealth for budget control. List who can approve spending, sign contracts, assign staff, and make client promises. Compare each authority with the targets in the assignment letter before accepting the post.
