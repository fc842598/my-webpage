How the Fourteen Major Stars Communicate: A Comparison

For the same matter, Qi Sha speaks directly, Tian Ji circles, Zi Wei minds face, Tian Tong seeks harmony and Tai Yin withdraws. Understanding your own and the other star script helps you connect and reduce misunderstanding, without ranking who is better.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

There is no single right way to communicate, only whether the channels match; first recognize which script the other uses, then choose your words.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some feel others communicate wrongly, when it is only a different star script; treating difference as right or wrong lets misunderstanding pile up.

The Underlying Logic

Pioneering stars (Zi Wei, Tian Fu, Wu Qu, Tian Xiang, Qi Sha, Po Jun, Lian Zhen, Tan Lang) lean direct and result-oriented, fast but easily blunt.

Supporting stars (Tian Ji, Tai Yin, Tian Tong, Tian Liang) lean indirect and feeling-focused, detailed but easily circular.

Zi Wei wants respect, Tian Ji wants logic, Tian Tong wants harmony, Tai Yin wants understanding, Qi Sha wants frankness.

Recognize the other star nature first, then adjust pace and entry point; this works better than arguing right or wrong.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read your own Life Palace major star to know your communication script
  2. Read the other Life Palace or relevant palace star to know their script
  3. Judge whether the difference is pace (fast/slow) or focus (task/people)
  4. Give conclusions first to fast communicators, lay background first for slow ones
  5. Use logic with task-focused people, feelings with people-focused people
  6. Review afterward what connected and what did not, and adjust gradually

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating communication differences as right or wrong and demanding the other adapt

Mistake 2: Only using your own comfortable style without reading the other script

Mistake 3: Thinking you must change your star nature to communicate, when adjusting expression is enough

Back to Your Own Chart
