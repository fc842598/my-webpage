Zi Wei Dou Shu Travel Palace for Remote International Clients

The Travel Palace can describe external rules even when work stays at home.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-09 09:13

Working with an international client from the same desk can still expose a person to a different market. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Travel Palace concerns the outside setting as well as physical movement. That does not make every overseas call a major life change.

Follow the work and the payment

A studio serving European clients may face a new time zone, contract standard and revision cycle. The Career Palace asks whether the service can be delivered; the Wealth Palace asks how and when the invoice is paid. A webinar with foreign viewers may increase visibility without producing revenue. Both are external exposure, but their business results differ.

Keep a three-month record

Track meeting hours, revisions, net receipts and sleep. Read the natal Life and opposite Travel Palaces before adding career, wealth and timing layers. Use signed terms and actual payments to judge whether the arrangement can continue.
