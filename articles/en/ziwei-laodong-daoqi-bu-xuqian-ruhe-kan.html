Zi Wei Dou Shu on a Fixed-Term Contract Not Renewed: Signs in the Career Palace

A fixed-term contract not renewed often corresponds to the annual Career Palace being activated together with the Travel Palace, marking one stage ending before the next begins. It differs from mid-term dismissal, and compensation and the job-search timeline should be handled separately.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-29

When a contract is not renewed, the focus is not on what you did wrong, but on arranging compensation, handover and the job-search window ahead of time.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many take non-renewal as personal rejection and rush into any job, without checking whether severance is owed and which stage they are in.

The Underlying Logic

Non-renewal often appears when the annual Career Palace is activated together with movement in the Travel Palace, marking an employment relationship reaching its natural end.

If the employer keeps or improves terms and you decline, severance is usually not owed; if the employer does not renew or lowers terms, you may claim severance based on years of service.

When the Career Palace stars are strong and the year only triggers a handover, it tends to be a smooth transition; with Hua Ji and malefics, the process can stall or dispute over compensation.

The Travel Palace represents the next environment; activated well, it is often a chance to change platforms; if blocked, you should reserve a longer buffer for job search and cash flow.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm whether the contract expired without renewal or was terminated early, as the two differ in compensation
  2. Check whether the employer kept or improved the original terms to decide whether you can claim severance
  3. Estimate severance months by years of service and keep the contract, pay records and expiry notice
  4. Read the annual Career and Travel Palaces to judge a smooth transition or a chance to change platforms
  5. Reserve three to six months of living and job-search buffer rather than taking the first offer
  6. Complete handover, the separation certificate and social-insurance transfer at once before moving on

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking non-renewal as personal failure and rushing into any job

Mistake 2: Not checking compensation terms and missing owed severance

Mistake 3: Ignoring the Travel Palace and cash flow and underestimating the job-search window
