Two Wealth Palace Stars Each Carrying a Transformation: Why Keep Them Separate

How to read two stars and their transformations as distinct signals before combining them into one cash-flow judgment.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-17 10:12

If the Wealth Palace holds two stars and each carries a different transformation, the easiest mistake is to blend them into one vague good or bad. The two stars actually cover separate parts and the two transformations have separate meanings. Keeping them apart lets you see the two forces that coexist in the same money.

Why they cannot be merged

Two stars in one palace often represent different sides of cash flow, such as one leaning steady and one leaning changing. The transformations on the two stars then bring resources, responsibility, reputation, or blockage separately. Judging only from "this palace has a transformation" compresses different signals into one and misses key details.

Step-by-step dual-transformation check

  1. Confirm which two stars are in the Wealth Palace and their basic natures.
  2. Then confirm separately which transformation each star carries.
  3. By star nature, understand which part of cash flow each star represents.
  4. By the transformation's meaning, judge the resources or blockages each part brings.
  5. Record the two lines side by side and see how they act together without merging early.

Combine only after separate reading

Keeping them apart does not mean they are unrelated, and the reading finally returns to the same palace to see how the two forces interact. But combining must be built on separate reading rather than blurring everything at the start. This makes the cash-flow judgment detailed.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and confirm the two Wealth Palace stars and their transformations.
  2. Separate stars and transformations first, then read their meanings.
  3. Record the two lines side by side without merging first.
  4. Finally return to the palace as a whole to combine.
  5. Keep the conclusion conditional rather than reading two stars as one verdict.
