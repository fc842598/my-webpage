Minor Cycle vs. Annual Cycle: When to Use the Minor Cycle for Details

How to separate the external events of the annual cycle from the inner state shown by the minor cycle.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Besides the major and annual cycles, Zi Wei Dou Shu has a minor cycle method. When the minor cycle and annual cycle point to different themes, they are not truly conflicting. They describe different sides of the same year.

Different sources

The annual cycle uses the year's stem and branch and its Life Palace, focusing on external events. The minor cycle follows the birth-year branch and focuses more on the person's inner state. One is outward and event-based, while the other is inward and feeling-based.

Judgment steps

  1. Under one fixed convention, lay out both the annual Life Palace and the minor-cycle palace.
  2. Record external events from the annual cycle and inner state from the minor cycle separately.
  3. Compare their host stars and transformations for agreement or difference.
  4. Use the annual cycle for events and the minor cycle for feelings and pressure.
  5. When they differ, let them supplement each other and note the convention.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs, software version, and rule convention, then lay out both cycles.
  2. Record external events and inner state separately.
  3. Use the major-cycle line to confirm the broader stage.
  4. Choose the main layer by question type and use the other for details.
  5. Write the answer as events plus inner state, not as two competing methods.
