Reading Assets and Living Foundation Through the Property Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu

The Property Palace shows fixed assets, living environment and family roots; linked with Karma it shows whether you feel at home, and with Wealth the liquidity of assets. It is a tendency reference and not investment advice; home buying, wealth products and asset allocation rest on licensed institutions and official product documentation.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-27

Some feel settled after moving, others hold many assets yet live uneasily; the difference often lies in the home palace, which is not only about the house but whether you can put down roots.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Read in pairs: Property with Karma judges whether your living environment settles you; Property with Wealth judges whether assets are locked in real estate or can move. For major purchases and allocation, rely on licensed institutions and official product docs; do not use the chart as an investment basis.

The Underlying Logic

The Property Palace shows fixed assets, living environment and family roots.

With Karma it shows whether you feel at home.

With Wealth it shows asset liquidity and convertibility.

It is not investment advice; licensed institutions and official product documentation prevail.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Property main star and transformations
  2. Assess fixed assets and living environment tendency
  3. Link with Karma to judge whether you feel settled
  4. Link with Wealth to see asset liquidity
  5. Layer decades and years to find purchase and adjustment stages
  6. Base major decisions on licensed institutions and official product docs

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Property as a direct basis for home investment

Mistake 2: Looking only at whether there is a house and ignoring peace of mind

Mistake 3: Skipping the Wealth link and locking all assets in real estate

Back to Your Own Chart

The Property Palace is the root where you stand. It reminds you what kind of home settles you and how much to sink or keep liquid, but when buying or allocating, take it as reference and leave the decision to licensed institutions and official product docs.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Property Palace: assets and living foundation before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Property Palace: assets and living foundation line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
