How to Read the Parents Palace: Elders, Documents and Roots, Not Debt

The Parents Palace describes your bond and relating with parents, elders and teachers, and it also governs documents, education, contracts and protection. Do not read friction as a destined debt; combine the opposite Health Palace and trines.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

The Parents Palace first holds your bond with those who brought you into the world and raised you, and it also traces your lifelong line of documents and agreements.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Seeing a malefic here, some conclude the relationship with parents is doomed or that they are repaying a debt, without noticing how relating works and the side of documents and protection.

The Underlying Logic

The Parents Palace holds the strength and pattern of your bond with parents, elders and teachers.

It also governs documents, education, contracts, reputation and protection from elders.

It sits opposite the Health Palace, so innate roots, inheritance and health tendencies are related and read together.

The elder bond needs the triple-direction plus Kui Yue, Chang Qu and transformations; one palace alone is half the story.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the Parents Palace and read its star for the elder bond and relating pattern
  2. Open the triple-direction to see whether support or adjustment dominates with elders
  3. Read the opposite Health Palace to understand innate roots and health tendencies
  4. Count Kui Yue, Chang Qu, assistants and malefics to separate protection, documents and friction
  5. Read transformations here: Lu is care, Ke documents and achievement, Ji concern
  6. Layer the decade and year to see the current theme in elder relationships or contracts

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading a malefic as a doomed relationship or debt and turning the bond adversarial

Mistake 2: Reading only the Parents Palace while ignoring your own relating and the document side

Mistake 3: Reading only this palace without Health and the trines, missing the full roots

Back to Your Own Chart

The Parents Palace shows your bond with elders and documents. Read it within Health and the trines to understand how to receive care, relate well and recognize your own roots.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Parents Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Parents Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
