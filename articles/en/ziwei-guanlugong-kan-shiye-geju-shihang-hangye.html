The Career Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Reading Your Vocation and the Right Industry

The Career Palace governs direction, work mode, the relationship with bosses and academic achievement. Its stars show whether you fit management, technical expertise, pioneering sales or a stable institution—read it before choosing an industry.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Career answers what you stand on and where you perform best; together with Life, Wealth and Travel it completes the vocational choice.

What This Question Is Really Asking

When lost, people ask their element but ignore the work mode in Career, repeatedly draining inside an organizational form that does not fit.

The Underlying Logic

The major star sets work temperament: Zi Wei/Tian Fu coordinate and manage; Ji/Liang research and plan; Sha-Po-Lang pioneer and assault; Tong/Xiang coordinate and serve.

Career trines Life and Wealth; a work model lasts only when it fits both personality (Life) and cash flow (Wealth).

It also covers bosses and study: Hua Ke aids exams and credentials, Hua Quan authority and teams, Hua Ji bottlenecks or friction with superiors.

Its opposite axis is Travel: whether work expands outward or across regions needs the Travel Palace.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Career major star and combination for work mode and organization type
  2. Trine Life and Wealth to confirm it fits personality and brings cash flow
  3. Read transformations: Ke for professional reputation, Quan for management, Ji to clear bottlenecks
  4. Check opposite Travel for local depth versus outward expansion
  5. Read assistants/malefics: team support versus high-pressure volatility
  6. Change jobs or start up when the decade Career activates, checked against the natal direction

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Choosing industry from Life alone, ignoring Career work mode

Mistake 2: Reading Career Hua Ji as career ruin when it is often a phase bottleneck

Mistake 3: Ignoring Travel and misjudging outward or regional expansion

Back to Your Own Chart

Career is the vocational compass. It does not guarantee success, but it helps you avoid the wrong track where effort always feels wrong, putting energy into your natural mode.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Career Palace and vocation before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Career Palace and vocation line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
