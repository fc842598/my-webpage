Why Good Fortune Does Not Feel Like It: It May Land in Areas You Ignore, Be Discounted by Malefics, or Need Action to Activate

Many feel that these years are said to be good fortune, so why do I not feel it, but good fortune does not mean everything goes smoothly or happens automatically. There are three common reasons: first, good fortune lands in an area you do not focus on, for example Hua Lu entering the Siblings Palace means good rapport and cooperation, but you only watch wealth; second, good fortune is discounted by malefics or fallen stars, the auspicious star arrives but shares the palace with malefics and Hua Ji, partially offsetting its force; third, good fortune needs action to activate, opportunity arrives but you do not move, so it remains only possibility rather than result. The key is first seeing which palace the good fortune lands in and what field it corresponds to, then seeing whether it is discounted, and finally using action to receive it. Astrology gives tendency and rhythm, not automatically fulfilled results.

Many expect good fortune to mean good things happen while lying down, but true good fortune is more like a door opening. You have to walk through it yourself, and walk through the right door.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is why the chart says good fortune but it is not felt. First understand that good fortune is field-specific, discountable and action-requiring, then check item by item which situation applies.

The Underlying Logic

Reason one: good fortune lands in the wrong focus area. Hua Lu entering Siblings means good rapport and cooperation, entering Karma means good spiritual enjoyment, entering Property means good home and assets, but if you only watch Career and Wealth, you will feel there is no good fortune.

Reason two: good fortune is discounted by malefics. The auspicious star and Hua Lu arrive, but the same palace or trine has malefics, Hua Ji or voids, partially offsetting the force, resulting in good things but not big ones, or good things taking effort.

Reason three: good fortune needs action to receive. Opportunity arrives and the window opens, but you do not move, prepare or seize it, so good fortune remains only possibility and does not automatically become result.

There is also good fortune in the shadows: for example Hua Ke entering Parents may mean improved elder health or family relationships, which are unremarkable but truly good fortune. Learn to feel from multiple fields rather than only money and career.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First see which palace the current decade and year’s auspicious stars, Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke land in
  2. Map this palace to its real-life field, Siblings equals rapport and cooperation, Karma equals spiritual enjoyment, Property equals home and assets, confirming whether good fortune lands in an area you do not focus on
  3. See whether this palace and its trine have malefics, Hua Ji or voids, judging whether good fortune is discounted
  4. If good fortune lands in your focus area and is not discounted, ask yourself whether you haveproactively acted to receive this opportunity
  5. If good fortune lands in an unfocused area, try to feel changes in that field; perhaps good things have already happened
  6. Remember that astrology gives tendency and windows, and results need action and choices to fulfill, so do not sit and wait

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming good fortune means everything goes smoothly and happens automatically, ignoring its field-specific and action-requiring nature

Mistake 2: Watching only money and career, ignoring good fortune landing in rapport, health, family and other fields

Mistake 3: Not acting when opportunity arrives, then blaming bad fortune, attributing possibility not becoming result to astrology

Back to Your Own Chart

Good fortune not being felt is mostly because it lands where you are not looking, is discounted by malefics, or needs you to walk over and receive it. Seeing clearly where good fortune lands and its field, and receiving it with action, you will find that much unfelt good fortune has been there all along.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to why good fortune does not feel like it before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the why good fortune does not feel like it line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
