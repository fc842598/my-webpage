How the Annual Palace Sets This Year's Theme in Zi Wei Dou Shu

The annual Life Palace lands on a natal palace by the year's earthly branch and combines with that palace's stars to set the year's main scene; annual transformations and its trine then pin down which matter rises or falls.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

To read a year, first find which natal palace this year's self stands in; whatever that palace governs is the theme most likely pushed to the front.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People read the year only through its zodiac or one annual Ji and stay vague, because they never locate the annual Life Palace within the natal twelve palaces.

The Underlying Logic

The annual Life Palace is set by the year's branch, with the annual twelve palaces spread from it as a temporary self for the year.

It overlays a natal palace's stars: that palace's long-term meaning becomes the year's main scene.

Annual transformations set its tone—Lu the opening, Ji the snag—activating whichever field they land in.

The year is read under natal and decade; when all three align, that year's matter is clearest.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the natal base and current decade direction
  2. Use the year's branch to find which natal palace the annual Life Palace lands on
  3. Read that palace's natal meaning to set the year's main scene
  4. Spread annual transformations to see where Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji land
  5. Read the annual Life Palace triple-direction for this year's help and friction
  6. Plan heavily where all three layers align; treat a lone annual signal as reference only

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only zodiac fortune without casting the annual Life Palace

Mistake 2: Detaching the year from decade and natal and inflating it into a full-year verdict

Mistake 3: Fixating on annual Ji while ignoring the opening annual Lu provides

Back to Your Own Chart

The year is the closest layer on the timeline. Standing it back in its natal palace and under the decade's direction makes this year's theme concrete: which matter, and how it moves.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the annual palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the annual palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
