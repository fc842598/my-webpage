How to Read an Annual Property Palace for Home Decisions

An annual Property Palace can describe a move, renovation, family duty, or asset decision. Separate living, money, and family roles before buying.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 20:00

An annual Property activation can mean a purchase, renovation, move, or a change in family responsibility. It is not automatically a promise of owning a home.

Separate living, assets, and family roles before interpreting the year.

Name the type of event

Use Property and Inner-Life for comfort, Property and Wealth for buying and selling, and Parents, Siblings, and Travel for family roles and movement. Hua Lu can bring resources, Hua Quan a decision, and Hua Ji budget or title disputes.

The combination defines the event.

The decade shows capacity

A strong decade Property Palace makes home and assets a larger theme; the year brings a signing, renovation, or move forward. If Wealth and Career are pressured, test cash flow first.

Do not buy solely because a favorable star appears.

Examples

Annual Hua Lu in Property with steady Wealth suits staged improvements. Annual Hua Quan in Property with Hua Ji in Parents can require written ownership and care responsibilities.

Practical reading order

Read Property for home and assets, Wealth for budget, family palaces for roles, Travel for movement, and decade plus annual timing for execution.

Practical Reading Order

Start with the question palace, compare its opposite and trine partners, then add timing and transformations before making a practical judgment.
