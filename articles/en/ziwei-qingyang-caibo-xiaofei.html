Qing Yang in the Wealth Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Impulse Spending, but Also a Whetstone That Forces You to Earn

Qing Yang is a blade-bearing malefic. In the Wealth Palace it often shows as decisive spending, splurging for face or on impulse, and as a tough, head-to-head way of pursuing money. It is not a poverty sign; it makes both the entry and exit of money loud. The key is whether Lu Cun, Hua Ke or a strong ruling star sheathes the blade.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

Many panic at the sight of Qing Yang in the Wealth Palace, assuming money will never stay. In fact Qing Yang there is like holding a sharp knife: it chops fast, but it also nicks the fingers. It forces you to act on money rather than leave you penniless.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Two things must be answered: how Qing Yang in the Wealth Palace spends and earns, and whether that force can be put to good use. It governs hard-fought money and brake-less spending; the fix is to give the blade a sheath, not to throw it away.

The Underlying Logic

Qing Yang belongs to metal, firm in nature, governing decisiveness and drive; in the Wealth Palace it pursues money the hard way, unafraid of bargaining, bidding or competing.

On the spending side it shows as impulse, instant buys, buying when irritated or for face, with regret coming later.

A malefic is a whetstone, not a guillotine: Qing Yang forces action and pressure on money and can push someone into becoming a high-capacity earner.

Remedy depends on Lu Cun in the same palace, Hua Ke in aspect, or strong rulers like Zi Wei, Tian Fu or Wu Qu taming the blade into discipline and expertise.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First see whether Qing Yang sits alone in Wealth or shares it with Lu Cun or Hua Ke
  2. If Lu Cun is present, note that the blade has a sheath and money can be earned and kept
  3. Check the triple direction for a strong ruler that channels firmness into expertise
  4. Set a hard spending rule, such as waiting three days on any large purchase
  5. Channel the drive into competition, negotiation and expansion rather than temper tantrums
  6. If the malefic feels heavy, rely on budgets and automatic savings rather than willpower

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading Qing Yang in Wealth as a definite ruin and lifelong inability to save

Mistake 2: Assuming a malefic can only be avoided instead of tamed by Lu Cun, Ke or a ruler

Mistake 3: Blaming all impulse spending on the star while skipping budgets and cooling-off rules

Back to Your Own Chart

Qing Yang in the Wealth Palace is a sharp knife, not a demand note. Put its drive into earning and negotiating, add a cooling-off period to spending, and the knife chops vegetables instead of fingers.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Qing Yang in the Wealth Palace and spending before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Qing Yang in the Wealth Palace and spending line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
