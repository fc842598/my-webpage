Natal vs. Decade Transformations in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Baseline and Temporary Adjustments

Natal transformations come from the birth year stem and set a lifelong baseline; decade transformations come from the active decade's stem and make a ten-year adjustment. Mark the two layers separately, then check whether they resonate in one palace.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

The same Hua Lu from the birth year and from a decade differ in weight and duration; mixing them mistakes temporary luck for lifelong resources.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Keep separate ledgers: the natal set is the long-term base and the decade set changes every ten years. Only when both layers land in one palace and point the same way does the force stack up.

The Underlying Logic

Natal transformations follow the birth year stem, stay for life, and set the chart's baseline tone and direction.

Decade transformations follow the active decade's stem, last about ten years, and make a stage adjustment.

The two layers run on different time scales; decade transformations never replace natal ones.

When natal and decade transformations share a palace and direction, they resonate and that area is especially strong for the ten years.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Mark the birth-year natal transformations and palaces as the baseline
  2. Determine the active decade and its stem
  3. Mark the decade transformations and palaces as the ten-year adjustment
  4. List the two layers separately without replacing one another
  5. Check palace by palace for same-palace, same-direction resonance
  6. Focus on resonance and separate long-term from stage effects elsewhere before concluding

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Mixing natal and decade transformations into one layer

Mistake 2: Taking decade transformations, which change every ten years, as lifelong

Mistake 3: Missing same-palace resonance and over- or under-estimating the force

Back to Your Own Chart

Separating the two layers divides your lifelong cards from this decade's luck. The baseline lasts and adjustments are temporary; stack them right to read accurately.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to natal versus decade transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the natal versus decade transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
