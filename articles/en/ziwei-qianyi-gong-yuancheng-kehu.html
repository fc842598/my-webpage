The Outside Palace and Remote Work: Clients, Platforms, and Distance

How to read the Outside Palace for remote work, cross-city clients, and online platforms without reducing it to a yes-or-no relocation answer.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:18

Distance Has New Forms

The Outside Palace can describe overseas life, but also remote clients, online platforms, travel, and cross-city teamwork.

Three Checks

Career shows the role you can perform outside. Wealth shows how the opportunity becomes cash. Life shows whether you can maintain visibility and boundaries.

Two Scenarios

Outside Hua Lu with Career Hua Ke can support expert content and remote clients. Outside Hua Ji with Wealth Hua Quan calls for pricing and scope control.

Reading Order

Define the outside opportunity, inspect the work and money link, then price the lifestyle cost before treating movement as a solution.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
