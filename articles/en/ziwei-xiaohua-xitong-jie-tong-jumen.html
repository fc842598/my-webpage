Digestive Health in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Tian Tong and Ju Men in Health Palace

In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Health Palace reflects a persons constitutional tendencies and body systems prone to issues. Tian Tong governs spleen-stomach and dampness, Ju Men governs mouth, esophagus, and intestines; people with these stars in Health Palace often need to pay special attention to the digestive system. But the chart only indicates "tendency," it cannot replace medical diagnosis.
