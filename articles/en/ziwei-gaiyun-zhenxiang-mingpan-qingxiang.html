Can Zi Wei Dou Shu Change Fortune? Chart Tendencies and Personal Choices

"Changing fortune" is the hottest and most easily abused concept in the fortune-telling industry. Many people think spending money on rituals, buying lucky charms, or changing names can change destiny. But from the Zi Wei Dou Shu perspective, the chart reveals "tendencies and probabilities," not "predestined results." True fortune change does not rely on external forces, but on correct choices after understanding yourself.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Practical case: Many people who consult fortune tellers most often ask "when will I get rich" "when will I get married." But what the chart can tell you is "in which areas you have advantages" "in which periods you have opportunities," not "you will definitely have a certain event on a certain date."

The Underlying Logic

Treating the chart as a "prediction machine," demanding precise year-month-day forecasts.

Only listening to the "good" parts, ignoring the "need attention" parts.

Treating chart advice as "commands," not combining with own actual situation.

Changing major life decisions because of one fortune-telling result, without independent thinking.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Clarify what you want to ask—trends, choices, or specific events.
  2. Find a reputable, capable fortune teller, avoid charlatans.
  3. Treat chart interpretation as "reference," not "decision."
  4. Combine with own actual situation (ability, resources, environment) to judge.
  5. For major decisions, listen to multiple opinions, including non-astrology professionals.
  6. Stay rational—the chart is a tool, not a religion.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: The chart cannot replace professional advice (medical, legal, financial, etc.).

Mistake 2: Fortune teller ability varies; interpretation results may differ.

Mistake 3: Do not give up effort because of chart results—"destiny" is the hand you are dealt, "luck" is how you play it; playing your hand well is what matters.
