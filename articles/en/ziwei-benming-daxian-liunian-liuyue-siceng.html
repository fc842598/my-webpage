How to Read the Four Layers in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Constitution, Climate, Trigger and Flashpoint

Reading time uses four layers: the natal is the innate constitution and foundation, the decade is the ten-year climate, the year is the trigger, and the month or day is the flashpoint where the matter concentrates. In inference the flowing stars take priority, layered natal, decade, year, month, so you both see the big trend and pin down the specific month.

The four layers are like medicine: the natal is constitution, the decade the environment, the year the trigger, and the month or day the days it actually flares.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some judge a whole life from the natal alone or chase small matters in the year and month, and both lose balance, because each layer governs one time scale and missing one leaves the picture incomplete.

The Underlying Logic

The natal chart is the constitution, setting innate character, pattern and resource foundation, the base of all judgment and unchanging over time.

The decade is the ten-year climate, setting the stage's theme and environment, and whether one follows or resists the trend is mainly read here.

The year is the trigger, setting this year's point and rhythm, and many matters are ignited in this year.

The month and day are the flashpoint, where concrete matters such as signing, breaking up, starting work, moving and exams concentrate in a certain month or days, and the flowing stars take priority in inference.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the natal chart clearly to set the innate constitution and foundation
  2. Then read the current decade to set the ten-year climate and theme
  3. Then read the year to set this year's trigger
  4. Finally read the month and day to set the concrete window for the matter
  5. In yearly and monthly inference, flowing stars take priority and original stars do not act without clash
  6. Layer all four, seeing the trend first and then pinning the month

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging a whole life from the natal alone and ignoring time layers

Mistake 2: Chasing only small yearly-monthly matters without the trend

Mistake 3: Not prioritizing flowing stars and muddling timing

Back to Your Own Chart

The four layers are a large-to-small positioning: natal sets constitution, decade climate, year trigger, and month-day flashpoint. Layer in order with flowing stars as priority, and you both see the ten-year trend and pin a matter to a specific month, so judgment is not vague.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the natal, decade, year and month layers before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the natal, decade, year and month layers line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
