How to Use Borrowed Palaces in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Treat the Spouse Palace as a New Life Palace and Rebuild the Twelve

When reading a spouse's career, treat the Spouse Palace as a new Life Palace and rebuild the twelve positions; the spouse's career palace then corresponds to your native Wealth Palace. Borrowing palaces is shifting perspective on one chart, not starting a separate life.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-27

Wondering about a spouse's career while staring at your own career palace points the wrong way. The right move is to borrow palaces: set the Spouse Palace as the new origin and rebuild the twelve around it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Borrowed palaces solve a perspective problem: with one palace as the new Life Palace, the same twelve relationships that were yours become your spouse's. It does not create a second life; it changes the subject.

The Underlying Logic

The core of borrowing is a shift: take one palace as the new Life Palace and rename the twelve by their original relative positions.

For a spouse's career, with the Spouse Palace as the new Life, its career palace maps to your native Wealth Palace.

A spouse's wealth or family is read the same way, rebuilding from the Spouse Palace and then reading that line.

A borrowed result is the spouse's projection within your chart, still bound by the original pattern, not a separate life.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First clarify whether the question concerns a spouse, child or parent
  2. Pick the corresponding palace as the new Life origin
  3. Rename the twelve by their original relative positions from that origin
  4. Locate the target palace under the new origin, such as spouse career as the new career
  5. Read the new line's stars, assistants, malefics and transformations
  6. Always remember this is a borrowed view of one chart, not the other person's independent fate

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading your own career or Wealth palace when asking about a spouse

Mistake 2: Treating the borrowed chart as the spouse's independent fate after shifting

Mistake 3: Changing only the Life origin without rebuilding the other eleven palaces

Back to Your Own Chart

Borrowing palaces changes the subject on one chart. Shift the origin and the twelve relationships become the spouse's story, yet the base remains your chart; with the right angle, the spouse's line reads accurately.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to how to use borrowed palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the how to use borrowed palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
