How to Judge the Strength of the Twelve Palaces: Not One Star, but Net Combination, Transformations and Framing

Judging a palace’s strength is not just whether it has auspicious stars or a bright major star; combine the net stars of its triple-direction, whether the natal transformations land there, and whether the two flanking palaces are auspicious or harsh: auspicious meetings, transformation activation and clean framing make true force. Strength is not fixed and shifts as the decade and year activate it. It describes grounding in that field rather than directly equaling a good or bad result.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-03

Many people judge palace strength by one star in the home palace, but the grounding hides in its trine, transformations and flanking palaces, and only combining them reads accurately.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the standard for judging palace strength. Understand that the home palace is only the center and force comes from the surrounding network, then read item by item.

The Underlying Logic

The home major star and brightness are the first layer, but even a strong single star is hollow if the triple-direction offers no support.

The net combination of the triple-direction is the main body: auspicious meetings and few, controlled malefics make the palace steady.

Whether natal Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji land in the home and its trine decides whether innate energy strengthens or ties it.

The two flanking palaces also take part: auspicious framing helps and harsh framing harms; strength also shifts with decade and year activation and is not fixed for life.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First lock the palace to judge and read its major star and brightness
  2. Pull the triple-direction, list stars palace by palace and compute the net effect of auspicious and malefic stars
  3. See whether the natal transformations land in the home or its trine
  4. Check whether the flanking palaces frame it auspiciously or harshly
  5. Combine home, trine, transformations and framing to set the palace’s strength
  6. Then layer the decade and year to see when this field is strengthened or weakened

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging strength from one home star or brightness alone

Mistake 2: Ignoring transformations and framing and taking a surface-auspicious palace as steady

Mistake 3: Treating strength as fixed for life without seeing shifts from cycle activation

Back to Your Own Chart

Judging palace strength is network work: the home, triple-direction, transformations and framing all matter. Combining these layers tells you in which field you truly have grounding.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to judging palace strength before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the judging palace strength line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
