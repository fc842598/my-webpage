Lian Zhen and Tan Lang in the Career Palace: A Bold, Versatile Career and Turning Two Peach Blossoms Into Achievement That Stays On Course

The Career Palace reads the work state and style. Lian Zhen governs firmness, the secondary peach blossom and rules while Tan Lang governs romance, desire, versatility and socializing, so together in Si or Hai one is bold and versatile at work, good at socializing and suiting public relations, sales, entertainment, management and posts needing connections. Tan Lang there gives versatile, sociable work with many chances, yet also distraction, taking on too much and excessive socializing.

Together they mean secondary peach blossom plus peach blossom: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars and focus, talent and connections become career; with malefics and Ji and indulgence, biting off too much and setbacks from socializing and romance follow.

The lesson is striking with focus and broad with roots: narrow chances to one or two main lines, turn connections into reputation, keep socializing measured and goals measurable.
