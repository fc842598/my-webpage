How to Read the Life and Body Palaces Together in Zi Wei Dou Shu

The Body Palace never stands alone. It attaches to one of six palaces, so read it only after confirming where it lands and opening the full triple-direction.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Many people see a Body Palace beside the Life Palace and assume a thirteenth position. It is actually an attached marker showing where your focus shifts later in life.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Beginners ask which palace takes priority, but the question itself is off. The two do not compete; one is the innate baseline and the other is acquired direction, layered on one structure.

The Underlying Logic

The Life Palace is set by birth month and hour and shows innate temperament; the Body Palace shows where accumulated experience pulls you over time.

It attaches only to Life, Spouse, Wealth, Travel, Career or Karma, and whichever it joins draws your later years.

When the two share a palace you stay consistent; when they differ, you often seem like two people at different ages because life is filling a gap.

Neither palace can be read apart from the triple-direction and transformations; the attached position only signals focus, never a fixed career or marriage outcome.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Cast the Life Palace from the lunar birth month and hour, then check the natal major star and its brightness
  2. Locate the Body Palace and confirm which of the six positions it attaches to
  3. Connect the two positions to see whether innate and acquired align or diverge
  4. Alignment means you grow into your nature; divergence shows which gap life is training you to fill
  5. List each position's triple-direction, assistant and malefic stars, and transformations for the net effect
  6. Use the decade limit to see when the Body Palace activates before concluding, rather than reading two separate fates

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the Body Palace as an independent thirteenth position with its own reading

Mistake 2: Reading only the Life Palace and being unable to explain change with age

Mistake 3: Assuming attachment to the Career Palace guarantees high office while ignoring the triple-direction and real conditions

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading the two together shows that no one is fixed at birth. Innate temperament sets the baseline, while later choices gradually rewrite where your center of gravity sits.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Life and Body palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Life and Body palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
