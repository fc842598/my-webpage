How to Decide Whether to Pay Off a Loan Early

Paying off a loan early is not the earlier the better; one must weigh the loan interest rate, investment return, cash-flow safety and emergency reserve. The Wealth and Property Palaces can hint at financial rhythm, but the final decision follows the formal loan contract and licensed institution guidance; this is not investment advice.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

Paying off early is like filling a pit with cash in hand; the pit filled brings peace of mind, but you lose your surplus. The calculation is whether peace of mind or flexibility is worth more.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some pay off early as soon as they have money, others refuse to pay and invest instead; what to calculate is the interest gap, cash flow and emergency reserve, not an emotional decision.

The Underlying Logic

The core trade-off of early repayment: loan interest rate versus the return rate of other uses of the funds.

You must keep 3-6 months of emergency funds, cannot pour all cash into the loan.

Check the loan contract for early-repayment penalties, frequency limits and minimum amounts.

The Wealth, Property and decade charts can hint at financial rhythm, but cannot replace actual calculation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First calculate the loan interest rate, remaining principal and early-repayment rules
  2. Assess whether your emergency reserve is sufficient (at least 3-6 months of expenses)
  3. Compare the loan rate with the return on conservative investments
  4. If the loan rate is far above risk-free return, early repayment is more worthwhile
  5. If cash flow is tight or there are better uses, delay or partially repay
  6. Decide based on the loan contract and licensed institution guidance; this is not investment advice

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Pouring all cash into early repayment without keeping emergency funds

Mistake 2: Looking only at interest rate without cash flow, leading to tight liquidity

Mistake 3: Not calculating at all, deciding to repay or not based on emotion

Back to Your Own Chart

Early repayment is about balancing interest rate, cash flow and emergency funds. Calculate clearly, keep enough surplus and follow the contract, then you can decide whether paying early brings more peace or keeping cash brings more flexibility.
