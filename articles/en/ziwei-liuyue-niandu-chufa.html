How to Use the Monthly Layer: Read the Yearly Background First, Then Find the Month Trigger

The monthly layer is not an independent chart but subdivides the yearly theme into monthly rhythm. Confirm the decade and year, then locate the monthly Life Palace and transformations, translating trends into observable actions for that month.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Many read months like a brand-new chart with fresh fortune each month, which is misuse. The month is a magnifier; it does not create a theme but distributes this year's play across twelve scenes.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The key is not reversing order: with the decade and yearly background first, the month has something to magnify. Locate the monthly Life Palace and transformations, then translate abstract trends into concrete actions that month you can do and verify.

The Underlying Logic

The month handles refining rhythm and does not replace the natal, year or decade.

The solar terms and month-setting rules must be declared first, since different methods change the starting point.

The monthly Life Palace and transformations bring the yearly theme into the current month.

Monthly judgment should land on observable tasks and feedback, with one focus per month.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm the background themes of the decade and year
  2. Declare the solar terms and month-setting rules used
  3. Locate where the current monthly Life Palace lands
  4. Read that month's transformations to find triggers
  5. Translate the yearly theme into one focus and observable task for the month
  6. Arrange the monthly rhythm around action windows such as signing, exams and moving, then review feedback

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the month as an independent chart with fresh fortune each month

Mistake 2: Reversing order and chasing the month without the yearly background

Mistake 3: Leaving monthly judgment in abstract fortune without observable tasks

Back to Your Own Chart

The month is a rhythm tool, not a switch of fate. Read the yearly trend first, find the month trigger, and keep one focus each month for a much clearer sense of when to act.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the yearly background and triggers of the monthly layer before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the yearly background and triggers of the monthly layer line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
