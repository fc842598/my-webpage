Si in the Life Palace: Small Actions to Lift Low Drive

Si in the Life Palace suggests weak vitality and drive with passive, stubborn tendencies. Do not force big contests; accumulate small actions to recover.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-23

When drive is weak, do small things; completed wins revive the heart.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some stay still and obsess; stars are tendency, while small actions restore.

The Underlying Logic

Si in Life means weak drive.

Do not force, do small things.

Recover slowly.

This is tendency, not an absolute verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. List one or two small tasks
  2. Mark them done
  3. No obsession
  4. Move a little
  5. Keep routine
  6. Rest on small wins

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Stillness

Mistake 2: Obsession

Mistake 3: Passivity
