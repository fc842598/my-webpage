A question like "问要不要回前公司，先看紫微还是六爻" is easy to turn into "which system is more accurate." The better first move is to ask whether you are judging 长期职业位置 or 这次回流窗口. Without that split, the tools only appear to conflict.

紫微 and 六爻 are not strongest on the same layer. Once you separate long structure from the one-event window, the division of labor becomes much cleaner.

Separate which layer this question belongs to first

Separate the layers first: 紫微 is stronger on 长期职业位置, while 六爻 is stronger on 这次回流窗口.

When the layers collapse into one question, people often mistake a short event window for full long-term fit, or the other way around.

The center of gravity changes with the tool

If the first concern is whether your long career structure still fits the old platform, Zi Wei usually separates that longer position more clearly.

If the long line already allows a return and the real doubt is whether this one timing window is worth taking, Liu Yao is usually better for the event itself.

Do not use the wrong tool for the wrong layer

Do not turn 紫微 and 六爻 into a slogan-level rivalry. They often answer different parts of the same decision.

And do not let one event window replace the long-term reading, or let the long-term reading replace the event window.

A steadier starting order

  1. Decide whether 长期职业位置 or 这次回流窗口 is the urgent layer.
  2. Match 紫微 and 六爻 to the right layer.
  3. Then bring both readings back into one decision.
