Hua Lu and Hua Ji in Opposition: Palaces, Clashes and Layers

Hua Lu is resources, opportunity and ease, while Hua Ji is contraction, blockage and a lesson. When they land in opposite palaces or clash in the same three-ways, see which sits in the Life core and which is activated in time, and note which layer—natal, decade or year—each falls on. Do not declare success or failure simply because Lu and Ji appear together.
