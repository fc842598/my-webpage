The Twelve Palaces Aren't Isolated: Palaces Have Family Relationships

The twelve palaces aren't twelve independent boxes — they have fixed relationships: Life/Wealth/Career are trine, Life/Spouse are opposite, Property/Spouse are trine. Reading one matter requires cross-palace reading.

Methods2026-08-20 15:30

Beginners tend to read only Wealth for money, only Spouse for love, only Career for work. But in reality, no one matter is determined by one palace. Your wealth isn't just Wealth palace — Life determines earning ability, Career determines where money comes from, Property determines savings, Fortune determines whether money makes you happy. The twelve palaces are a web, not boxes.

Palace relationships: first, triple direction — this palace plus trine and opposite palaces form a whole; second, adjacent palaces influence each other, e.g., Life/Siblings, Spouse/Children; third, palace overlap — cycle palaces overlay natal palaces; fourth, flying relationships — one palace's stem flying to another connects them. Reading one matter requires stringing relevant palaces together.

Which Palaces for Wealth

Life — do you have earning ability and drive.

Wealth — income style and cash flow.

Career — what work does money come from.

Property — can you save, fixed assets.

Fortune — your attitude toward money, happiness from it.

Travel — external or out-of-town income sources.

Read five palaces together for the full wealth picture.

Which Palaces for Love

Spouse — relationship pattern and partner traits.

Life — how you behave in relationships.

Fortune — your inner feelings in the relationship.

Travel — how you meet partners (out of town, social settings).

Property — cohabitation, marriage, home life.

Children — romance, intimacy, children's impact on the relationship.

Love isn't decided by Spouse palace alone.

Which Palaces for Career

Career — career type and development.

Life — your professional personality and ability.

Wealth — income from career.

Travel — out-of-town development, business trips, external opportunities.

Friends — colleagues, subordinates, partners.

Spouse — partner's impact on your career (Spouse is Career's travel palace).

Reading Order

For any matter:

  1. Find the "main palace" — Wealth for money, Spouse for love.
  2. Find its triple direction — support system.
  3. Find related palaces — cross-read based on the matter.
  4. Check flying — energy flow between palaces.
  5. Check cycles/annuals — which palaces are activated.
  6. One matter is determined by multiple palaces; don't fixate on one.
