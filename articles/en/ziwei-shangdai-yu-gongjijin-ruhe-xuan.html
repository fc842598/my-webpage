What This Question Is Really Asking

Some choose a pure commercial loan for the higher amount without checking provident-fund eligibility, later finding a combined loan saves interest, or overestimating the fund limit.

The Underlying Logic

Loans are often triggered when the Wealth Palace covers monthly payments and the Property Palace the purchase, marking long-term debt against real estate.

Provident-fund loans usually have lower rates but are limited by contribution time, account balance and a maximum amount.

Commercial loans offer flexible amounts and repayment with usually higher rates and can be combined with the fund.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First check provident-fund contribution time, account balance and loanable amount
  2. Confirm the purchase price, down payment and total loan needed
  3. Work out rates and monthly payments for commercial, fund and combined loans
  4. Read the Wealth and Property Palaces to judge cash flow and the purchase
  5. Compare total interest and the term and assess ability to pay

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Looking only at monthly payments without comparing total interest

Mistake 2: Not checking fund eligibility and limits, leaving expectations unmet

Mistake 3: Overestimating ability to repay and taking on too much pressure
