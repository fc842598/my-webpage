Zi Wei Dou Shu: Wealth Palace Income: Salary Versus Performance Bonus

A fixed salary and a conditional bonus are different cash-flow items.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-06 09:13

A fixed salary and a conditional bonus are different cash-flow items. The Wealth Palace may frame income questions, while Career points to performance duties. Separate contracted pay, targets, and payment dates; use payroll records for actual amounts.

Read the chart in layers

A favorable Wealth symbol is not a payroll entry. Separate contracted salary, conditional performance pay, reimbursements, and one-time awards. A sales bonus may depend on client payment; a signing award may carry a repayment clause. Read Wealth with Career and the annual layer, then use payroll documents to decide what is actually available.
