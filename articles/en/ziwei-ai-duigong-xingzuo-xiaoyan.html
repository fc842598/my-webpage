When AI Moves a Star Across a Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart

Check the opposite palace without treating its stars as resident stars.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-31 09:04

An empty Property Palace may borrow context from its opposite palace. That does not move the opposite palace's stars into the Property Palace. Copy the chart's palace names, resident stars, opposite stars, and transformations into separate columns. If an AI answer invents Hua Lu or calls an opposite star resident, discard that claim and return to the chart. Real housing costs and debt still need separate records.

A second error occurs when an annual transformation is described as natal. Keep the timing label with every claim. For housing, compare the chart question with lease cost, title records, debt, and work location.
