Annual vs. Major-Cycle Transformations: Which Layer Sets This Year's Action

How to resolve conflicts between annual transformations and the major-cycle transformations that set the decade's direction.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

In a given year, major-cycle transformations and annual transformations both appear. The major cycle sets the ten-year tone, while the annual cycle sets the short-term trigger. When they seem to conflict, decide which layer leads instead of choosing the preferred one.

Different time ranges

The major-cycle transformations describe resources and blockage across the decade. The annual transformations last only for the year and trigger a specific point. They are not equal: the major cycle sets direction, and the annual cycle shows what moves now.

Judgment steps

  1. Fix the major-cycle transformations and the palaces of its resources and blockage.
  2. Write the annual transformations and the palaces they activate.
  3. Compare the two layers for agreement, opposition, or different palaces.
  4. Add birth-year transformations to check the deeper long-term limit.
  5. Use the major cycle for direction and the annual cycle for this year's trigger.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then confirm the major-cycle and annual palaces.
  2. Record transformations in birth-year, major-cycle, and annual layers.
  3. Mark agreement and conflict, then check the host stars and supporting palaces.
  4. Use the major cycle for direction and the annual cycle for the trigger.
  5. Write the answer as decade tone plus annual trigger, not one fortune label.
