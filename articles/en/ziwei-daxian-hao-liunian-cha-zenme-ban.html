What to Do When the Decade Is Good but the Year Is Difficult in Zi Wei Dou Shu

During an upward decade, a year triggered by Ji or malefics is a short-term correction within a larger trend, not the end of a good ten years; the strategy is defend the core, cut leverage and wait for rhythm to return.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

Distinguish wind from a single wave: the decade is the wind, the year a wave; when the wind has not turned, one headwind wave is no reason to abandon ship.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People hit one hard year in a good decade, deny the whole picture and pivot hastily, turning a digestible correction in a ten-year uptrend into a real loss.

The Underlying Logic

The decade governs the ten-year trend, the year a short trigger—different levels, different weight.

A hard year in a good decade usually disrupts rhythm or snags one matter rather than shaking the foundation.

The key test is whether the year's Ji clashes with the decade main line or natal core; if not, it is a local correction.

In a correction year, shrink risk, fix weak links and store energy for the next smooth year instead of overturning direction.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the decade main line is truly up, so you do not mistake a turning decade for a yearly correction
  2. Locate the hard year's Ji and malefics to judge local versus global impact
  3. Hold the core assets, relationships and position tied to the ten-year line
  4. Cut leverage and aggressive decisions; prioritize stability in a correction year
  5. Use the slower rhythm to build skill, process and weak links
  6. Re-accelerate when the next annual transformations improve rather than quitting at the low point

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Overturning a ten-year direction over one hard year

Mistake 2: Confusing a turning decade with a yearly correction and acting the wrong way

Mistake 3: Adding leverage in a correction year to bet a rebound, magnifying loss

Back to Your Own Chart

A good decade is not smooth every year. Read a hard year as a breathing point on the way up—hold the core, pull the fist back, and the next strike lands harder.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to a good decade with a difficult year before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the a good decade with a difficult year line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
