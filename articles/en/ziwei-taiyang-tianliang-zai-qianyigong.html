Tai Yang and Tian Liang in the Travel Palace: Noble Help and Principles Away From Home, Turning Dignity and the Shelter Into an Upright Path Elsewhere

The Travel Palace reads the external environment, development elsewhere and the state away from home. Tai Yang governs brightness, warmth and generosity while Tian Liang governs principles, oversight and steadiness, so together in Mao or You one has noble help and principles away from home, gaining a foothold through reputation and skill, suiting education, public welfare, oversight and consulting elsewhere. The Underlying Logic

Tai Yang in the Travel Palace gives warmth, noble help and reputation away from home with good rapport, yet can bring concern about appearances, much giving and generosity.

Tian Liang there makes one principle-minded, reputation-focused and able to oversee and consult away from home, yet can be detached, cautious and critical.

Together they mean noble plus shelter: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars reputation and principles become good support; with malefics and Ji, pressure from detachment, face or travel follows.

The lesson is upright while able to act and loyal with calculation: turn principles into professional value and build reputation actively.
