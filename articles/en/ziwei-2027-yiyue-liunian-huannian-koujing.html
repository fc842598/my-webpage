January 2027 Zi Wei Dou Shu Forecast: Check the Year Boundary First

Why a January 2027 chart can show different annual layers under different calendar conventions.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:15

One Zi Wei Dou Shu app shows the Ding Wei year in January 2027; another still shows the prior year. Check the exact query date and whether each app changes years at Lunar New Year or at the solar term Lichun. A Gregorian year label is not enough.

Compare the same date

Save each app's version and year-boundary setting. Look at dates just before and after the stated boundary within one app, then note changes to the annual Life Palace and four transformations. Even after both apps enter the same annual year, Ju Men or Tai Yin will sit in different palaces for different people.

Read the personal chart

Locate natal stars, then the annual transformation and decade context. Use real contracts, budgets, and schedules for decisions. Keep the two calendar conventions visible if they cannot be reconciled; the display difference is a rules question rather than proof that a life event changed overnight.
