Tai Yang in Health Palace: Watch the Heart, Eyes, and "Internal Heat"

Tai Yang in Health calls for attention to the heart, eyes, blood circulation, and internal heat. Tai Yang is fire with warm tendencies; excessive worry also harms health.

Main Stars2026-08-21 10:15

Tai Yang in Health means issues related to "fire" — prone to internal heat, high blood pressure, eye strain. Tai Yang is yang fire, corresponding to the heart and eyes, your key organs. Additionally, Tai Yang in Health types tend to worry. You want to manage everything and can't let go; this "mental exhaustion" harms you more than physical issues.

Health palace shows constitution and risks. Tai Yang here, being fire, primarily affects heart, eyes, and blood circulation. But Tai Yang's psychological traits — passion, impatience, worry — also affect health. Temple/prosperous Tai Yang means a hot constitution but strong recovery; fallen means watch chronic heart and eye issues. With malefics, pay special attention to acute inflammation and accidents.

Health Tendencies of Tai Yang in Health

Heart and circulation — palpitations, high blood pressure, arrhythmia are common.

Eyes — myopia, dry eyes, eye strain; Tai Yang corresponds to eyes.

Internal heat — mouth ulcers, sore throat, skin issues.

Head — headaches, migraines, especially when overthinking.

Regular checkups — Tai Yang in Health types ignore body signals; check heart and blood pressure regularly.

Health Risks by Combination

Tai Yang with Tian Liang — Tian Liang is longevity star, mitigating some risks, but watch digestion.

Tai Yang with Tai Yin — fire and water balance; relatively balanced constitution, but watch mood swings.

Tai Yang with Qing Yang — watch heart surgery, injuries, acute inflammation; the "blade" with fire needs extra care.

Tai Yang with Huo Xing — two fires together; inflammation, heat, blood pressure worse.

Tai Yang with Hua Ji — chronic heart or eye issues; get regular checkups.

Health Advice for Tai Yang in Health

Worry less — you're not everyone's sun; give others' problems back to them.

Light diet — less spicy/fried; more yin-nourishing, heat-clearing foods (mung beans, snow fungus, pear).

Regular routine — Wu hour (11-13) is heart meridian time; rest then; don't stay up late.

Eye care — after prolonged screen use, look far, warm compress, reduce screen time.

Manage emotions — Tai Yang types are impatient; learn deep breathing and slowing down.

Reading Order

For Tai Yang in Health:

  1. Tai Yang is fire — focus on heart, eyes, circulation.
  2. Check brightness — temple/prosperous: strong recovery; fallen: chronic risk.
  3. Check malefics — Qing Yang: surgery/injury; Huo Xing: double fire.
  4. Check Life and Fortune — are your worry and impatience consuming your body.
  5. Check cycles — when a cycle reaches Health or malefics activate, increase checkups.
  6. How long since you rested with "nothing to manage"?
