How to Read Stacked Palaces and Layered Charts

Read the same palace stacked across natal, decade and year: when an area is activated on all three layers, the stage theme concentrates there. Stacked palaces read the accumulation of force, not simple repetition.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

When a matter points to the same palace on natal, decade and year, like three signals lit in one place, hard to miss.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some read in layers without stacking, and cannot see why a stage concentrates; stacking shows the theme weight.

The Underlying Logic

Natal sets innate, decade ten years, year the current year.

An area activated on all three layers has the most concentrated theme.

Stacked palaces need reading whether layer transformations reinforce or pull.

Only one layer activated means a light, short theme.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read natal for area tones
  2. Find which palaces the current decade activates
  3. Then find which the year activates
  4. Stack all three, find jointly activated areas
  5. Read stacked transformations for reinforcement or pull
  6. Judge where the stage theme concentrates and how heavily

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading in layers without stacking, missing theme weight

Mistake 2: Taking stacked palaces as simple repetition

Mistake 3: Not reading layer transformation directions

Back to Your Own Chart

Stacked palaces read how force accumulates layer by layer. Three layers pointing to one palace and transformations aligned, the stage theme is especially concentrated and heavy.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to stacked palaces and layered charts before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the stacked palaces and layered charts line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
