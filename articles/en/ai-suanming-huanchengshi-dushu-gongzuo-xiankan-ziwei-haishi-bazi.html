A move-to-another-city question usually carries two layers at once: where the platform fit is, and whether your current stage can carry the cost.

Zi Wei is often stronger for the position and movement structure. Ba Zi is often stronger for stage strength and bearing capacity.

Separate the kind of question first

Platform fit and stage condition are different tables.

A good relocation judgment often needs both, not only one.

Questions like "For a Move-to-Another-City Study-or-Work Question in AI Fortune Telling, Should You Start With Zi Wei or Ba Zi" become cleaner when you split the immediate event, the stage condition, and the long-term structure before choosing a tool.

The emphasis changes when the tool changes

A bigger city opportunity can still hit a weak cash-flow stage badly.

A strong personal stage can still be wasted if the old platform is already capped.

Once those levels are separated, the different systems stop competing and start covering different parts of the same decision.

Do not use the wrong tool for the wrong question

Do not reduce movement questions to a flat good-or-bad answer.

You also do not always need to choose one tool forever; one can set the axis and the other can fill the blind spot.

So this should not become a brand-war question about which method is always best. It should become a level-matching question first.

A steadier starting order

  1. Decide whether platform fit or stage condition matters first.
  2. Use Zi Wei for the position layer and Ba Zi for the stage layer.
  3. Only then decide whether to move.
