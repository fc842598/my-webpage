How to Read the Timing of Romance

To see when romance arrives, do not only check whether the year has Hong Luan or Tian Xi; read whether the Children Palace (romance palace) flights activate romance stars or romance-bearing main stars. When activation forms groups with strong force, the chance of a bond is high.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

Romance stars are like seeds and Children Palace flights like spring wind; with seeds but no wind, blossoms do not open.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Hong Luan in the year and assume romance, but it does not happen; they missed whether flights activate it.

The Underlying Logic

The Children Palace is the romance palace; read its flights first.

Flying Lu Quan Ke activating romance stars brings bond chances.

Activation forming groups (Lu-Ji, Quan-Ke) doubles force.

Romance stars only appearing in the year without activation do not necessarily respond.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read natal Children Palace and romance stars
  2. Read annual and decade Children Palace flights
  3. See whether flights activate romance stars
  4. Judge whether activation forms groups and strength
  5. Romance stars in the year and activated, bond chance is high
  6. Combine real social life, do not wait passively

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing only annual romance stars without flight activation

Mistake 2: Seeing Hong Luan and assuming certain romance

Mistake 3: Not reading Children Palace, romance incomplete

Back to Your Own Chart

Romance timing asks whether romance stars are stirred by spring wind. Children Palace flights activate in groups with force, and meeting romance stars, a bond easily responds.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the timing of romance before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the timing of romance line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
