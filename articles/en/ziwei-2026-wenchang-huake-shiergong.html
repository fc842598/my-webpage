What This Question Is Really Asking

The Underlying Logic

Wen Chang is the literary star governing writing, exams, certificates, contracts, order and expression, and Hua Ke makes these more visible and fairly treated.

Hua Ke leans to name rather than money: it smooths reputation, credentials and titles but does not directly pay or make a thing succeed on its own.

The palace sets the domain: Life or Body gives personal scholarly repute, Career gives professional standing, Wealth lets one charge for expertise and reputation, and Spouse points to communication and proper standing.

Hua Ke soothes and eases urgency, but with malefics or Ji it can become empty fame, face concerns or small paperwork errors, so details must be checked.
