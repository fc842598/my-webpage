Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Parents Palace: Practical, Reliable Elders and a Bond Built on Mutual Care

Wu Qu is the wealth star and Tian Fu the storehouse star; together guarding the Parents Palace, the elders are often practical, steady, home-minded and able to carry matters, and may help with material and practical problems; but expression is reserved, with few sweet words and love shown mainly through action.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-30

Some parents are not expressive yet spend a lifetime holding the family up and arranging money and matters well; this is the tone of Wu Qu and Tian Fu in Parents: the bond with elders is read through action and mutual care.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to read Wu Qu and Tian Fu in Parents. Separate the strength of reliable, practically helpful elders from the risk of reserved expression and overly polite distance.

The Underlying Logic

The Parents Palace governs parents, mentors and superiors; Wu Qu governs pragmatism and Tian Fu steady guarding, so together the elders are often steady and reliable.

The parents have strong responsibility and manage the household well, often giving concrete support with money, work and practical difficulties.

The risk is distance: not good at expressing emotion, with more politeness than closeness and a tendency to report only good news.

With Lu or assistant stars, the elders’ support is steady and the family base solid; with malefics and Ji, distance easily arises over stubbornness, money or duties, and the key is active communication and more presence.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Tian Fu guard Parents together
  2. List both sides clearly: reliable practical elders versus reserved, polite expression
  3. Read the care parents express through action
  4. Take the initiative on emotional expression and talk more about feelings, not only matters
  5. Accept the elders’ support in practical difficulty and repay it with presence
  6. With malefics, Ji or distance, communicate early to untie knots

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking parents’ reserve as lack of closeness or love

Mistake 2: Only contacting when there is a problem and ignoring daily presence and emotion

Mistake 3: Handling money and duties only by unspoken understanding and letting knots accumulate
