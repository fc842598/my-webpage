Which Palaces to Read for a Business Partnership: Friends and Children

Partnership is read mainly through the Friends (Servants) palace and the Children palace. When Friends sends Hua Lu, Quan or Ke to meet the Life three-ways, partners tend to help; when Friends Hua Ji clashes the Life palace, partnering is unwise. The Children palace covers shareholders, equity and subordinates, and Children Hua Ji clashing Life also argues against taking a share. The chart only shows tendencies; in practice, use a written agreement, define equity and spell out the exit.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some partner on a verbal promise; under Friends or Children Hua Ji, the closer the friend, the more the accounts and exit must be written.

The Underlying Logic

Friends Lu, Quan or Ke meeting Life three-ways favors helpful partners.

Friends Hua Ji clashing Life argues against partnership.

Children Hua Ji clashing Life argues against taking a share.

Rely on a lawyer and a written contract with equity and exit terms.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Friends palace stars
  2. See Friends transformations into the three-ways
  3. Check Friends Hua Ji clashing Life
  4. Check Children Hua Ji
  5. Decide whether to partner
  6. Sign a written agreement

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Partner on a verbal promise

Mistake 2: See only Friends good stars

Mistake 3: Skip the exit clause
