How to Read the Killing-Surrounding-Lian-Zhen Pattern: Achieving Amid Change

Killing-Surrounding-Lian-Zhen places Lian Zhen and Tan Lang guarding the life in Si or Hai, with Wu Qu and Qi Sha meeting from You or Mao, formed by four stars. It signals a life of many changes that can achieve in specialized, unconventional or highly competitive fields, depending on star strength and the mix of auspicious and malefic.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28

The keyword is change; the path is rarely straight, yet amid change and competition it can carve a position others lack.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Seeing Lian Zhen and Tan Lang, some think only of romance; seeing Wu Qu and Qi Sha, only danger, and miss that this group is about achieving amid turbulence.

The Underlying Logic

There are two basic structures: Lian Zhen and Tan Lang guard the life in Si with Wu Qu and Qi Sha meeting from You, or Lian-Tan in Hai with Wu-Sha meeting from Mao.

The ancients saw Lian-Tan as romance and Wu-Sha as stern force; the meeting puts human feeling, desire, decision and competition together, so the life has many turns and choices.

When the stars are strong and Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars balance them, change becomes professional breakthrough, suiting technical, sales, military-police and entrepreneurial fields.

When malefics and Ji are too heavy or the stars are fallen, turbulence becomes repeated upheaval and relationships fluctuate; changing direction even more is then unwise.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm one of the two structures with Lian-Tan guarding life and Wu-Sha meeting in the trine
  2. Check the strength of each of the four stars to see whether force expresses or is awkward
  3. Look for Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars to balance change into breakthrough
  4. Count malefics and Hua Ji; if too heavy, guard against repeated upheaval
  5. Match your field to whether it suits high-change, high-competition work
  6. Use the limits to see which stages are turning points and which should be held steady

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing Lian-Tan only as romance and Wu-Sha only as danger and missing the pattern

Mistake 2: Ignoring star strength and balance and treating every change as opportunity

Mistake 3: Changing direction repeatedly when malefics and Ji are already heavy

Back to Your Own Chart

Killing-Surrounding-Lian-Zhen is not a steady pattern; its strength lies in change and competition. Read the star strength and balance, be bold when momentum favors breakthrough and avoid upheaval when it is time to hold, and turbulence becomes your home ground.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Killing-Surrounding-Lian-Zhen pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Killing-Surrounding-Lian-Zhen pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
