The Spouse Palace in Depth: Beyond the Partner's Traits, Your Own Pattern in Intimacy

The Spouse palace shows the partner's character, appearance and the relationship, while mirroring your own needs, interactions and lessons in intimacy. Strong/auspicious means smooth; malefics/Ji do not mean no love but a relationship needing work; the opposite Career palace governs how love and career trade energy.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

It is a two-way mirror: outward the partner, inward how you love and are loved. Miss your own half and choosing by partner traits alone repeats the same pitfall.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Malefics in Spouse are read as divorce; they usually mean interaction needs磨合 and a winding path, not absence of love. Others fixate on partner conditions and ignore their own recurring pattern.

The Underlying Logic

It governs the partner's traits (stars), relationship state (strength and auspiciousness), marriage timing, and your own intimacy pattern.

Stars set partner type: Sun outgoing and responsible, Ji clever and thoughtful, Tan Lang versatile and romantic; malefics add friction.

Malefics/Ji mean a winding path with communication or emotional lessons, not no marriage; late marriage and the right interaction style often stabilize it.

Its opposite is Career, so work and love pull on each other; for women, Property and Happiness also deeply shape stability—read as a whole.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read its major star for the partner's traits and your attraction type
  2. Check strength and auspiciousness for a smooth versus grinding tone
  3. Reverse to yourself: the star also maps your needs and lessons
  4. Read opposite Career to assess how work pulls on love
  5. With heavy malefics, favor late marriage and communication skills before binding
  6. In years it activates, expect romance, marriage or a relationship turning point

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading malefics as divorce when they mean a winding path

Mistake 2: Choosing only by partner conditions, ignoring your own pattern

Mistake 3: Reading Spouse in isolation from Career/Happiness/Property

Back to Your Own Chart

The Spouse palace writes a two-person script and one lead is you. Understanding your own recurring pattern in intimacy leads to stability more than repeatedly grading a partner's conditions.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Spouse palace in depth before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Spouse palace in depth line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
