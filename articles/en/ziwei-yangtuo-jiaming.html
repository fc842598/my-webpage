Qing Yang and Tuo Luo Flanking a Palace: The Role of Lu Cun

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-02 17:20

Qing Yang is the Blade star, associated with direct pressure; Tuo Luo is the Entangling star, associated with delay and repetition. In the standard natal arrangement, they occupy the two branches beside Lu Cun, the Stored Prosperity star. Therefore, when Qing Yang and Tuo Luo flank a focal palace, the reader must first verify that Lu Cun occupies that middle palace. Otherwise the chart may show two difficult neighbors, but it should not automatically receive the standard Yang-Tuo flanking label.

The Center Contains Both Resource and Pressure

Lu Cun gives the focal palace something worth preserving: money, position, capacity, or a stable resource. Qing Yang presses directly from one side, while Tuo Luo drags or complicates matters from the other. The result is not simply bad luck. It can describe a useful resource that attracts demands, deadlines, competition, or slow obligations. The focal palace identifies the subject, and its main stars determine resilience.

Two Conditional Combinations

If Life contains Lu Cun and is flanked by Qing Yang and Tuo Luo while Hua Ji enters Wealth, an informal loan, guarantee, or shared investment may put personal resources under both immediate and lingering pressure. Written terms become important. If Career contains Lu Cun and is flanked by the two stars while Hua Quan and Hua Ke support the wider structure, the same pressure may be channeled into auditing, engineering, compliance, restoration, or risk management where direct action and patient follow-through are both required.

Do Not Reduce the Pattern to Enemies

Pressure may come from deadlines, procedures, family obligations, close colleagues, or the person's own difficulty setting limits. It does not prove malicious intent. Hua Lu, Hua Quan, or Hua Ke may provide opportunity, mandate, or expertise, but none removes the need for scope, payment terms, evidence, and checkpoints. If health concerns arise in real life, medical evaluation should guide decisions rather than symbolic prediction.

Chart-Reading Order

First locate Lu Cun and verify Qing Yang and Tuo Luo in the two adjacent palaces. Second, identify the focal palace containing Lu Cun and judge its main stars. Third, read its exact opposite palace and two trinal supports. Fourth, add the Four Transformations and current malefic activations. Apply decade and annual timing last, then translate the pattern into the specific resource under pressure, the two kinds of demand, and the practical boundary needed.
