Can a Chart Be Changed? The Chart Cannot, Choices Can

The chart structure cannot change, but it writes tendencies not verdicts; environment, effort and key choices can change how and how much materializes. What cannot change is the tone, what is held is how to live.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

The chart is like a map; routes are fixed, but which to take, how and how far are yours.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some feel the chart fixed and give up; others seek to change the chart through dubious ways; what truly changes is materialization.

The Underlying Logic

Chart structure cannot change.

Tendencies are not verdicts.

Choices and effort change materialization.

Do not seek dubious chart-changing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First accept the chart tone
  2. Read the chart as tendencies
  3. Find optimizable directions
  4. Use choices and effort to change materialization
  5. Do not take the chart as verdict
  6. Do not waste effort changing the chart

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Giving up once the chart is fixed

Mistake 2: Seeking dubious chart-changing

Mistake 3: Taking tendencies as verdicts

Back to Your Own Chart

The chart cannot change, but how to live can. Accept the tone, take tendencies as reference and use choices and effort to change materialization; this is the right way.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to can a chart be changed before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the can a chart be changed line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
