Reading the Transformations: How Money Flows From Wealth Into Property, Passing Cash vs Stored Assets

The Wealth Palace governs how money moves in and out, while the Property Palace governs whether it settles into assets, home and security. Whether Hua Lu or Hua Quan lands in Wealth or Property means quite different things: Lu in Wealth often means smooth earning and lively cash flow, while Lu in Property means money is retained and tends toward real estate and accumulation. The key is first giving Wealth a source, then guiding part of it to settle in Property rather than only passing through. This is a structural tendency and not investment advice, and property and finance follow licensed institutions and official product terms.

Many people wonder why they earn well yet cannot save, and the issue is often lively Wealth with Property not catching it: money passes through without settling.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how the transformations show money flowing and settling. First distinguish the roles of Wealth and Property, then see where Lu and Quan land and whether a line connects the two.

The Underlying Logic

The Wealth Palace is where cash flows through, governing the rhythm of income and spending; the Property Palace is where money settles, governing assets, home and security.

Hua Lu or Hua Quan in Wealth often means smooth earning, bold claiming and lively cash flow, but money is not necessarily retained.

Hua Lu or Hua Ke in Property is what makes money settle, home steady and gradual buying favorable; Hua Ji in Property brings worry over property and money tied up in the home.

The key is whether a line from Wealth to Property exists, through Lu Cun, linked Hua Lu or flying transformations, and actively turning live money into savings through forced saving and regular investing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate the Wealth and Property palaces and their major stars, reading the strength of each
  2. Find whether the natal Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji land in Wealth or Property
  3. Judge whether the current pattern is earning smoothly but not retaining, or settling and enabling buying
  4. Look for a Lu Cun or flying-transformation line connecting Wealth and Property
  5. Use forced saving, regular investing and separate accounts to guide live money toward settling
  6. Read the decade Property and Wealth to judge the right stage for buying and large accumulation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming strong Wealth means savings while ignoring that money must be caught by Property

Mistake 2: Taking Hua Ji in Property as never being able to buy when it often means worry needing steadier planning

Mistake 3: Relying on willpower alone with no system, so live money naturally passes through

Back to Your Own Chart

In reading how money flows, the key is giving Wealth a source and letting Property catch it. Setting systems to settle passing money into assets means earning more also equals keeping more.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to how money flows from the Wealth Palace into the Property Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the how money flows from the Wealth Palace into the Property Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
