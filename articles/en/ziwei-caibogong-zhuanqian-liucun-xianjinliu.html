The Wealth Palace Is Not an Income Statement: Separate Earning, Keeping and Cash Flow

The Wealth Palace writes how money is used and managed; high income and keeping it are not the same. Separate earning, retention and risk tolerance, then read it with the Life, Career, Property and Travel palaces.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

I earn well, why can I not save is the classic Wealth Palace question. Earning is one ability and keeping money another, with cash-flow rhythm in between; the three cannot be conflated.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Living paycheck to paycheck, cash-flow strain and high income with low retention are three different financial states with different stars. Breaking the equation Wealth Palace equals money shows where money leaks.

The Underlying Logic

The Wealth Palace is more how money is used and managed than an income figure.

Earning ability, retention ability and risk tolerance must be observed separately.

The Life, Career, Property and Travel palaces change how Wealth expresses.

Financial reading cannot replace real data; rely on licensed institutions and official product terms, and this is not investment advice.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First set the Wealth Palace major star and brightness
  2. Translate the star into how you earn rather than how much
  3. See how money is kept and check the retention step
  4. Read the cash-flow rhythm to spot the strained months
  5. Combine the Career for income sources, Property for asset accumulation and Travel for outside chances
  6. Budget separately for paycheck-to-paycheck, strain and low retention, using real accounts

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the Wealth Palace directly as income level

Mistake 2: Looking only at earning and not retention and cash flow

Mistake 3: Using fortune numbers instead of real financial data for decisions

Back to Your Own Chart

The Wealth Palace teaches your way of relating to money. Separate earning, retention and cash flow, then rely on real budgets and official terms, and finances gradually stabilize.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to earning, keeping and cash flow in the Wealth Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the earning, keeping and cash flow in the Wealth Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
