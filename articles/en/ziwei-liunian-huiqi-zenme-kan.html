A Hui Qi Year: When Misunderstandings and Low Moods Mount, Easing Off and Speaking Clearly

Hui Qi governs gloom, stagnation, misunderstandings and low spirits. In a Hui Qi year it is easy to feel unmotivated without a clear cause and to be misunderstood, with higher communication costs. The key is not forcing clashes while emotional, saying what needs saying early and plainly, and allowing enough rest; Long De and auspicious stars ease it. It is only an annual layer and does not set long-term results.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-03

A Hui Qi year often feels a bit dull and vaguely stuck: small things stall and good intentions are misread, and forcing the issue only blocks it more.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to ease a Hui Qi year and avoid misunderstandings. Understand it as gloom and blocked communication, then see whether the opposite Long De carries auspicious stars to resolve it.

The Underlying Logic

Hui Qi, one of the twelve pre-year stars, always sits opposite Long De and governs gloom, stagnation, misunderstandings and low mood.

When Long De meets annual Kui and Yue or Chang and Qu with auspicious transformations, Hui Qi’s harshness eases; if Long De lacks support and Hui Qi meets malefics, poor mood and disputes follow.

Hui Qi is mostly a communication and state-of-mind issue: words left unclear, expectations unaligned, rest insufficient, while the matter itself may be fine.

It is an annual minor layer that does not change the natal and decade main line, with the focus on easing clashes, explaining more and restoring energy.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate which annual palace Hui Qi sits in to identify where things may stall
  2. Check whether the opposite Long De meets auspicious stars, judging whether someone or something can mediate
  3. Say what is needed early and plainly rather than leaving others to guess
  4. Do not force clashes while emotional; pause and sleep before a dispute
  5. Allow enough rest and exercise to steady routines and spirits
  6. If Hui Qi meets malefics, avoid major decisions in those months

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Hui Qi as a year of major misfortune when it is mostly communication and mood

Mistake 2: Holding words back and expecting others to understand, letting misunderstandings grow

Mistake 3: Forcing clashes in a low period and turning small matters big

Back to Your Own Chart

A Hui Qi year’s lesson is that communication and mood easily knot; speaking plainly, easing clashes and restoring energy let many apparent setbacks loosen on their own.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the year when Hui Qi, the gloom star, is active before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the year when Hui Qi, the gloom star, is active line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
