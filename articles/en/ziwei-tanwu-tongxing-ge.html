The Tan Wu Tong Xing Pattern in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Why Wu Qu and Tan Lang Together Tend to Mature Late

Tan Wu Tong Xing means Wu Qu and Tan Lang guarding together, often in Chou or Wei; the wealth star meets appetite, bringing early socializing, swings and restlessness. After midlife, if fire stars or Lu turn that appetite into enterprise, it steadies—a classic late-blooming pattern.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-30

People with Wu Qu and Tan Lang together have many opportunities, connections and ideas when young and often spend years in restless trying; only when appetite is focused into a business or specialty does the staying power emerge, which is why the tradition says this pair does not favor early success.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what forms the Tan Wu Tong Xing pattern and why it matures late. The key is whether Wu Qu’s pragmatism can focus Tan Lang’s appetite, and whether fire stars or Lu channel that appetite into enterprise.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu gives pragmatism and execution, Tan Lang gives appetite and sociability; together the wealth star meets desire, with no lack of resources and social skill.

In early limits Tan Lang dominates: much entertaining, social obligation and trying, energy scattered and swings large, so early success often does not hold.

The pattern requires fire or Lu: fire stars channel appetite into enterprise, while Hua Lu or Lu Cun give resources an outlet, so after midlife the career opens further.

If malefics and Ji are heavy without control, boom-and-bust easily follows from overreaching, social obligations or entertaining, and late blooming never arrives; judging the pattern must return to these chart evidences.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Tan Lang guard together in the Life Palace or its triple-direction
  2. See whether fire stars meet them to judge if appetite becomes enterprise
  3. Then look for Hua Lu or Lu Cun to confirm resources have an outlet
  4. Count malefics and Ji to weigh boom-and-bust and entertainment loss
  5. When young, deliberately narrow the track and turn appetite into a specialty or business
  6. With the decade, expand mainly in the middle-age limit carrying fire or Lu

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging sudden wealth from Wu Qu and Tan Lang while ignoring the fire and Lu conditions

Mistake 2: Overreaching for speed and spreading everywhere when young, so early success does not hold

Mistake 3: Trusting the pattern name without checking malefics, Ji and the decade, so late blooming may not arrive

Back to Your Own Chart

The Tan Wu Tong Xing pattern wins on resources and social skill and is valued for blooming late. First focus appetite with pragmatism and wait for fire or Lu to trigger expansion; this pair’s staying power is often more reliable than its early bustle.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Tan Wu Tong Xing pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Tan Wu Tong Xing pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
