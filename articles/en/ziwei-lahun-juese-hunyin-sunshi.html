The Divorce Decision in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Phase Hurdle or Structural Depletion

Whether to divorce is never read from one malefic but from whether Spouse is repeatedly activated by decade/year malefics without resolution, whether Karma is chronically drained in the relationship, how the opposite Career line is pulled, and the realistic Wealth/Property split. An occasional Ji clash is a hurdle; continuous malefic/Ji across phases with no Karma recovery is closer to a structural problem. Consult a professional lawyer for major decisions; this covers chart reasoning only.

No chart is free of marital conflict; what truly warrants caution is not one year's Ji but a relationship repeatedly activated with chronic depletion and no improvement, and these two states differ greatly in the chart.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether a marriage is worth continuing, whether to divorce, or why you are repeatedly hurt, first distinguish a phase hurdle from structural depletion before deciding.

The Underlying Logic

Marital state reads Spouse and its opposite: Spouse and Career are two sides and read as a pair. An occasional annual Ji or Qing Yang clash usually means conflicts concentrate that year with a specific trigger; if decade after decade Spouse is occupied by malefics/Ji with Ji bouncing back, the problem is not one event but a long-term structural mismatch.

Mental depletion reads Karma: in deciding whether to continue, Karma is more honest than Spouse. Long-term Ji/Kong-Jie/Ling Xing in Karma within the relationship—anxiety, insomnia, falling self-worth that recovers after time apart—shows the relationship drains your mental foundation; if Karma is only phase-activated and usually steady, it is more likely repairable fluctuation.

Repair potential reads transformations and assistants: a Spouse with malefics but met by Lu/Ke, Tian Liang/Tian Xiang or Zuo Fu/You Bi usually still has handles for communication and repair; stacked malefics/Ji with no rescue and the opposite Career also damaged (career and health collapsing together) calls for facing reality.

Realistic split reads Wealth/Property and the decade: divorce involves assets, home and children, and a decade with stressed Wealth/Property makes the split more drawn-out, so plan early, keep evidence and use formal legal channels. Phases activating Spouse while Karma is stressed are high periods for decisions, yet exactly then decide slowly and handle steadily, not letting emotion sign a lifelong document.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Spouse and its Career opposite to separate a single-year conflict from a cross-decade structural problem
  2. Focus on Karma to judge phase fluctuation versus long-term mental depletion
  3. Check whether Lu/Ke and assistants rescue the malefics, assessing remaining repair handles
  4. Lay out the realistic Wealth/Property/Children matters rather than deciding unprepared
  5. Slow major decisions; when needed, separate to cool down or seek professional counseling
  6. Handle legal matters like asset division through formal channels and a lawyer, keeping evidence

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging divorce from a single-year Ji clash, mistaking a phase hurdle for the end

Mistake 2: Reading only Spouse without Karma and missing long-term depletion in the relationship

Mistake 3: Deciding hastily at the emotional peak, signing before assets and children are arranged
