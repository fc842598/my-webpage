Bing Fu in the Career Palace: Balance Work and Rest

Bing Fu in the Career Palace suggests hard work where strain affects health and in turn career. Balance work and rest, keep regular hours and do not overdraw the body, the foundation of work.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some overwork and overdraw, losing both; stars are tendency, while sustainability beats short bursts.

The Underlying Logic

Bing Fu in Career means strain affecting health.

Balance work and rest, do not overdraw.

Health is the foundation of work.

This is tendency, not an absolute verdict.
