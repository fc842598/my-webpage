True Romance vs Fleeting Attraction in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Hong Luan/Tian Xi Versus Tian Yao/Xian Chi

Hong Luan and Tian Xi are upright-romance stars of marriage and committed bonds; Tian Yao and Xian Chi are side-romance stars of desire, ambiguity and brief attraction. Separate the nature first, then palace and cycle, or fleeting chemistry is mistaken for the one.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Many romance stars do not mean good marriage; the key is commitment-bound attraction versus attraction that stops at chemistry—they land and activate differently.

What This Question Is Really Asking

When a romance star activates, people assume marriage but get brief affairs—they mistook Tian Yao/Xian Chi side-romance for Hong Luan/Tian Xi commitment.

The Underlying Logic

Hong Luan and Tian Xi are a pair of upright-romance stars of joy, marriage and commitment, often settling a relationship when the year brings them to Life, Spouse or Happiness.

Tian Yao is charm, desire and ambiguity; Xian Chi is physical romance and fleeting affairs—strong attraction, weak stability.

Committed-bond galaxies also involve Tai Yang/Tai Yin and Lian Zhen/Tan Lang, which must be bright and free of Hua Ji, then paired with Hong Luan/Tian Xi to be stable.

Romance nature is read with the natal Spouse Palace: a solid base lets romance turn committed; a weak base makes any number of romances passers-by.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Identify whether Hong Luan/Tian Xi or Tian Yao/Xian Chi is activated
  2. Upright romance: see if it enters Life, Spouse or Happiness to push commitment
  3. Side romance: note whether it brings only attraction and ambiguity with no landing
  4. Combine the natal Spouse Palace to judge a stable foundation
  5. Check whether the decade is a marriage cycle; the yearly star is only the trigger
  6. In side-romance years, hold boundaries and confirm slowly; chemistry is not commitment

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking any romance star as the one without separating the two pairs

Mistake 2: Reading yearly romance while ignoring the natal Spouse base

Mistake 3: Mistaking intensity of attraction for stability of relationship

Back to Your Own Chart

Romance stars are the entrance to connection, not the result. Tell who leads to commitment and who only entertains, and your romantic judgment grows far clearer.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to true romance versus fleeting attraction before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the true romance versus fleeting attraction line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
