Annual Luck, Minor Limit, and the Tai Sui Twelve Spirits: Moving Palaces and Fixed Circles

Three different yearly methods: the moving annual palace, the birth-year minor limit, and the fixed circle of twelve spirits.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 10:35

When reading a year, people often hear about annual luck, the minor limit, and the Tai Sui twelve spirits, but these are three different things and are easily mixed together. A simple split is that annual luck is a moving Life Palace, the minor limit is another palace set from the birth year, and the twelve spirits form a fixed circle of supporting information. Separating the frames first prevents double counting.

Annual luck versus minor limit

Annual luck uses the current year branch to rotate the whole palace system and is the most common yearly framework. The minor limit starts from the birth-year trio and moves by nominal age, so each person lands differently. Both settle on a palace, but their start and method differ, and they can be compared without replacing one another.

What the twelve spirits are

The Tai Sui twelve spirits are a fixed circle arranged around the current year branch, describing supporting references for different sides of the year. It is not itself a moving Life Palace and does not re-arrange the stars. When placed beside the other two, it should be understood as a fixed circle rather than a third yearly Life Palace.

Step-by-step reading

  1. Confirm which year is being read, then set up annual luck and the minor limit separately.
  2. Annual luck rotates by the current year branch, while the minor limit is set by birth year and nominal age; label them apart.
  3. Arrange the fixed twelve-spirit circle by the current year branch as a supporting reference.
  4. Place the three results side by side to see whether they agree or conflict on the same topic.
  5. Use annual luck as the main frame and the other two as supplements rather than weighting them equally.

Reading limits

The three methods are only different reading frames; using more than one does not make the result more accurate. When conclusions conflict, return to real conditions rather than choosing the one you prefer. The chart gives tendencies, while reality decides what to take.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and the target year.
  2. Set up annual luck first, then the minor limit by birth year, labeling each method.
  3. Then arrange the fixed twelve-spirit circle as support.
  4. Check the three side by side on the same topic without double counting.
  5. Match the conclusion to reality without absolute yearly claims.
