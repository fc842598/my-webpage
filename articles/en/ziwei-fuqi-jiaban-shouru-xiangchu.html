Overtime, Income, and Time Together in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Read extra earnings, relationship time, and recovery as separate questions.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28 09:11

Working longer hours can improve income and still strain a relationship. The Wealth Palace frames money, the Spouse Palace agreements, and the Wellbeing Palace recovery. A favorable money sign does not erase the time cost.

Calculate net income after commuting, meals, and childcare. List which plans are repeatedly canceled and who takes over home tasks. A second project may pay well but require two evenings of paid care. Another arrangement may increase earnings while leaving every day off for recovery. Compare the natal chart and annual layer. Agree on an end date for the busy period, notice for schedule changes, and protected time together.
