How to Read Moving and Relocating: Travel Palace Sets External Opportunity, Property Palace Sets Rootedness

Whether to leave home, which direction, and how smoothly moving goes combine Travel (outer world/away fortune), Property (living/rooting), Tian Ma (motion) and decade activation. Travel stronger than Life often means developing away from home; steady Property favors putting roots nearby.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

Relocation is essentially changing environments so chart energy expresses differently; the chart shows whether movement enlivens you or leaves you adrift, and when and in which direction moving flows better.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People agonize over leaving home for a big city. Wanting to go is not enough; compare Life vs. Travel strength, whether Property is steady or moving, whether Tian Ma activates, and whether the decade triggers Travel/Property.

The Underlying Logic

Travel is "you after leaving the native environment": strong Travel with Lu/Quan/Ke/Kui-Yue means meeting benefactors and a bigger stage away from home—the classical "pattern forms away from home."

Property is rooting and belonging: steady Property with Lu/auspicious stars gives a solid home/family rear suited to local depth; Property Ji/void/Tian Ma clash means frequent moves or weaker ties to the origin, with movement flowing better.

Tian Ma and transformations set motion and direction: Tian Ma in Life/Travel with Lu means earning in motion; a decade/year activating Travel or Property (especially Lu into Travel, Ji into Property) often marks the actual move.

Direction and pace reference star nature plus reality: Tai Yang/Tian Ji/Tan Lang/Lian Zhen lean outward, far and changing; Tai Yin/Tian Fu/Tian Xiang lean steady and near. The chart gives tendency; choosing a city also weighs industry, family and cost—use a trial stay to lower risk.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Compare Life and Travel strength: local-rooting vs. away-developing type
  2. Read Property stability for the rear, housing and family ties after moving
  3. Find Tian Ma and Lu/Ji to tell whether motion brings income/opportunity or drain
  4. Cast decade/year for stages activating Travel/Property to set the move window
  5. Combine star nature with real industries to shortlist matching cities, then trial-live
  6. After moving, verify over one to two years whether career, connections and mindset truly improve before putting down long roots

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Moving on impulse without comparing Life/Travel or reading Property

Mistake 2: Taking strong Travel as a mandate to leave while the decade is absent and movement drifts

Mistake 3: Ignoring real industries and family, substituting chart direction for actual city research

Back to Your Own Chart

Relocation trades environment for expression, not escape. See whether movement enlivens or steadies you, which decade to move and which direction fits, then combine real research and a trial stay so each move genuinely takes you somewhere better.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to moving, relocation and choosing a city before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the moving, relocation and choosing a city line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
