Mentoring a New Colleague Through the Children Palace

Separate training gaps from team handoff problems in Zi Wei Dou Shu.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30 09:07

Repeated rework by a new colleague may reflect unclear training or a broken handoff. The Children Palace can frame the work of developing someone; the Friends Palace frames peer cooperation. If examples and acceptance criteria were never given, repair the training. If those exist but review ownership is unclear, repair the team process. Track the errors and feedback dates before attaching a star interpretation.

Mentoring a new colleague is a work process, not a fixed prediction from the Children Palace. Compare training instructions, team support, and who approves the final result. One repeated error can come from missing guidance; another can come from ignored written steps. Use the record to change the process.
