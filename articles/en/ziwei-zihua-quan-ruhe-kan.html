How to Read the Self-Transforming Hua Quan

When a palace stem makes its own star transform to Quan, it is the energy of actively wanting control and decisions, favoring leadership, entrepreneurship and responsibility. Unlike a natal Quan that is innate, guard being too rigid, dictatorial and deaf to others.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Self-transforming Quan is like taking up a command burden yourself, not a position given by others; carrying it well is responsibility, overdoing it is dictatorship.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some take self-Quan as guaranteed authority, but it is the energy of actively wanting control; without ability and team behind it, the more you control the more isolated.

The Underlying Logic

Self-Quan = the palace stem activates its own star to Quan, active control.

It differs from natal Quan: natal is innate, self-Quan is later actively seeking control.

It favors leadership, entrepreneurship, technical responsibility and decision roles.

Guard rigidity, dictatorship, competitiveness and deafness to dissent leading to isolation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find the self-Quan palace, the area of active control
  2. Understand it as later seeking control, not an innate position
  3. Read the home palace star ability for the backing to control
  4. Check trine team support, not relying on the individual
  5. Count malefics to guard rigidity, fights and disputes
  6. Deliberately share control and hear dissent, not turning control into dictatorship

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking self-Quan as innate authority and waiting

Mistake 2: Being rigid and dictatorial, deaf to others and isolated

Mistake 3: Reading only the home palace without team ability

Back to Your Own Chart

Self-Quan asks how active control becomes responsibility. With ability, willingness to share and hearing others, this energy is leadership rather than dictatorship.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the self-transforming Hua Quan before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the self-transforming Hua Quan line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
