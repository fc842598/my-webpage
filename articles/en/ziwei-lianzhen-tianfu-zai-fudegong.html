Lian Zhen and Tian Fu in the Karma Palace: A Spirit That Values Both Principles and Storage, Living Tact Without Tension

The Karma Palace reads the inner world, ways of enjoyment and inner satisfaction. Lian Zhen governs principles and tact while Tian Fu governs solid storage, so inwardly one values both rules and boundaries and accumulation and steadiness, suiting satisfaction through order, expertise and assets. The risk is being too principled, holding too tightly and mental tension, so the key is leaving room for ease and enjoyment beyond rules, not letting the spirit become a tightly shut storehouse.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-02

People with Lian Zhen and Tian Fu in the Karma Palace inwardly value both rules and tact and want to live steadily and thickly, prizing both principles and accumulation; yet when holding too tightly, they can become mentally tense, lacking enjoyment and ease.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the inner pattern with Lian Zhen and Tian Fu in the Karma Palace. Understand it reads inner satisfaction, then see how principles and storage become freedom from tension.

The Underlying Logic

Lian Zhen in the Karma Palace gives a spirit that values principles, tact and boundaries, with rules inwardly, and also a tendency toward sensitivity and tension.

Tian Fu there gives a spirit that values accumulation, storage and steadiness, seeking stability inwardly, and also a tendency toward conservatism and pettiness.

Together they mean principles plus storage: the inner self is steady and tactful, and with Lu Cun, Hua Lu and auspicious stars satisfaction is steady and thick; with malefics and Ji, tension, conservatism and pressure from overly tight principles or money-holding follow.

The lesson is letting the storehouse breathe: hold principles at the boundary, plan accumulation for the long term, and actively leave room for enjoyment, socializing and ease, not shutting the spirit too tightly.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Lian Zhen and Tian Fu share the Karma Palace and check their brightness
  2. List the principles and accumulation you value inwardly, sorting what matters
  3. Hold principles at the boundary, not using rules to manage daily details
  4. Protect fixed time and budget for enjoyment, socializing and ease
  5. Cultivate interests that relax without spending, reducing dependence on accumulation
  6. Regularly review the inner state and promptly adjust tension and conservatism

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Lian Zhen-Tian Fu in the Karma Palace only as principled and accumulative while ignoring ease

Mistake 2: Being too principled and storage-focused, with mental tension and less enjoyment

Mistake 3: Turning holding money into pettiness, affecting relationships and quality of life
