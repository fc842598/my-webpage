Zuo Fu, You Bi, Tian Kui and Tian Yue: Support and Opportunity Stars

Zuo Fu and You Bi indicate steady support and assistance, like reliable people and resources around you. Tian Kui and Tian Yue indicate timely opportunities and sponsorship, often turning a corner in difficulty. Meeting the Life palace or its three-ways they strengthen a pattern and soften harsh-star impact, but they are help rather than replacement; if absent or under harsh stars, your own action still decides.
