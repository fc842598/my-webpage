Tai Yang in Wealth Palace: Earn Openly, Spend Openly

Tai Yang in Wealth means income from legitimate, visible sources; suited to earning through reputation, expertise, and social standing. Generous spender, but watch savings.

Main Stars2026-08-21 10:15

Tai Yang in Wealth means earning openly and honorably — you don't suit or stoop to "dark money"; your income ties to reputation, expertise, and social standing. You may gain opportunities through helping others and business through word of mouth. But one issue: spending too generously — generous to friends, family, and yourself, with weak savings habits.

Wealth palace shows income style and financial management. Tai Yang here means the "front door" route — suited to socially visible work like management, education, government, PR. Tai Yang Hua Lu brings good wealth tied to reputation; Hua Ji may mean money worries or unstable income. People with Tai Yang in Wealth need to learn to "increase income" while "reducing outflow."

Earning Traits of Tai Yang in Wealth

Open, honest income — no dirty money; the legitimate route.

Earn through reputation — when word of mouth and visibility are good, income follows naturally.

Generous spender — liberal with friends and family; willing to spend for face.

Benefactors bring wealth — rewarded for helping others.

Poor at saving — money comes and goes fast; build savings habits.

Wealth by Combination

Tai Yang with Tian Liang — earns through professional reputation; steady rising income.

Tai Yang with Tai Yin — sun and moon both bright; good wealth with both status and savings.

Tai Yang with Hua Lu — best wealth luck; high income from legitimate sources.

Tai Yang with Qing Yang — hustles for money, high competition, but strong drive earns hard money.

Tai Yang with Hua Ji — financial setbacks; may lose through guaranteeing others' loans or unstable income.

Financial Advice for Tai Yang in Wealth

Save first, spend later — auto-transfer 20% to savings on payday, then spend the rest.

Don't spend for face — you don't need to prove generosity through spending.

Be cautious with guarantees — Tai Yang types are loyal and easily guarantee friends' loans; this is a major money pit.

Favor stable investments — high-risk speculation doesn't suit you; steady wealth management does.

Target your generosity — helping others is fine, but don't be a "scattercash kid."

Reading Order

For Tai Yang in Wealth:

  1. Check brightness — temple/prosperous: high income; fallen: more worry.
  2. Check transformations — Lu: good wealth; Ji: prevent losses.
  3. Check co-stars — Tian Liang: steady; Tai Yin: saving; Qing Yang: competitive.
  4. Check Life and Career — does your work bring reputation and income.
  5. Check Property — can you keep money.
  6. Is your generosity from kindness or from "wanting to be praised"?
