Why November Is Not One Zi Wei Dou Shu Monthly Cycle

A Gregorian month can cross a lunar month boundary. Check the chart setting and event date before reading November's monthly cycle.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-27 09:05

A forecast labeled “November” can hide two different monthly charts. Zi Wei Dou Shu software may change its monthly palace and transformations at a lunar month boundary rather than on November 1. Before interpreting a job interview or a contract, check which chart actually covers that date.

Fix the calendar rule first

Write down the event date, the software's lunar month start, and its method for locating the monthly Life Palace. Some systems use a different boundary convention. Keep the birth data, time zone, and school setting the same while comparing screenshots.

An early November client meeting and a late November signature may belong to different monthly periods. The meeting raises questions about contacts and roles; the signature also calls for contract and payment checks. A good meeting does not prove that collection will be easy.

Read the shorter period within the larger chart

The natal chart describes longer patterns, the decade cycle supplies background, and the annual cycle narrows this year's themes. The monthly chart changes the immediate emphasis. Start with the exact event and its date, then connect that period to the longer layers and to the real calendar of meetings, obligations, and payments.
