How to Tell a Real Zi Wei Pattern: A Four-Element Check

A named pattern is not real just because the stars line up. Check four elements: whether the major stars are bright, whether helpful stars like Zuo Fu, You Bi, Tian Kui and Tian Yue meet, how heavily harsh stars break it, and whether the four transformations activate it. All four make a real pattern; fallen stars with harsh stars and no activation are usually only a name.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-27

A real pattern needs bright major stars, helpful stars meeting, light harsh-star breaks and transformation activation; check each.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some match themselves to a famous name on sight; the same pattern can be real or broken, and a broken one is ordinary.

The Underlying Logic

First check whether the major stars are bright and the energy is clean.

Second check helpful stars such as Zuo Fu, You Bi, Kui and Yue.

Third check how much Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Tian Xing and Kong Jie break it.

Fourth check whether Lu, Quan and Ke activate it before deciding.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Name the pattern
  2. Check major-star brightness
  3. Check helpful stars meeting
  4. Check harsh-star breaks
  5. Check transformation activation
  6. Judge all four together

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Trust the name

Mistake 2: See only major stars

Mistake 3: Ignore transformation activation

Back to Your Own Chart

Check brightness, helpful stars, harsh stars and transformations one by one; only with all four is the pattern real, otherwise it is ordinary.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to How to Tell a Real Pattern From a False One before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the How to Tell a Real Pattern From a False One line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
