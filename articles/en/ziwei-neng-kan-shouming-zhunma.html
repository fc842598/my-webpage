Can Zi Wei Dou Shu Read Longevity: It Shows Health Tendencies, Not a Fixed Lifespan

A Zi Wei chart can read constitutional leanings and resilience through the Health Palace, Karma Palace and decades to flag health directions, but it cannot and should not assert a fixed lifespan; all health matters rely on formal medical care.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

Longevity is not a fixed number the chart can determine; what it can read is where you are softer, how to care for yourself and when to pay more attention during certain decades.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some ask how long they will live and panic at a malefic; the issue is not a fierce star but mistaking a health tendency for a lifespan verdict.

The Underlying Logic

Old texts occasionally mention long and short life, an extreme figure of speech in their context and never a concrete verdict on an individual.

What the chart reads is the Health Palace constitutional leaning and the Karma Palace mental state and resilience.

Decades and years passing through Health only flag a stage to watch rest and checkups, not a lifespan.

For discomfort always rely on formal medical care; the chart serves only as a reminder for daily self-care.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First separate whether you ask about lifespan or health care; the former cannot be asserted
  2. Use the Health and Karma palaces to read constitutional leaning and resilience
  3. Turn tendencies into sleep, exercise and regular checkup routines
  4. When decades and years pass through Health, pay attention early and do not delay checkups
  5. Seek formal medical care promptly for discomfort, neither frightened nor falsely reassured by the chart
  6. Read old-text wording rationally without absolute interpretation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking health tendencies as a lifespan verdict and panicking

Mistake 2: Asserting longevity from malefics and creating mental burden

Mistake 3: Replacing medical care with the chart and delaying checkups and treatment

Back to Your Own Chart

This question teaches taking the chart as a care reminder and leaving health to doctors; what is readable is tendency, not lifespan, and regular living plus formal care are the true responsibility to the body.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to whether longevity can be read before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the whether longevity can be read line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
