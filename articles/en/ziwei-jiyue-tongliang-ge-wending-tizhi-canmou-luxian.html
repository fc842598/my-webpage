Ji Yue Tong Liang: Planning and Coordination in the Wider Chart

Identify Tian Ji, Tai Yin, Tian Tong, and Tian Liang before interpreting Ji Yue Tong Liang. The pattern does not prescribe public service or rule out entrepreneurship.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06Updated 2026-11-06 15:07 (UTC+8)

Ji Yue Tong Liang brings together Tian Ji, Tai Yin, Tian Tong, and Tian Liang in the related palace structure around the Life Palace. Read the actual arrangement and the convention being used before applying the name. A traditional association with institutional work is not a career requirement.

Name All Four Stars

Tian Ji contributes a theme of planning and adjustment, Tai Yin of detail and resources, Tian Tong of ease and relationships, and Tian Liang of judgment and responsibility. Explain how these themes are connected in this chart rather than presenting a generic description of an office worker.

Compare Support and Constraints

Read the conditions of the stars, assisting stars such as Wen Chang and Wen Qu, and the transformations actually present. Challenging stars may change the interpretation, but they do not establish that the person is incapable of stable work or must avoid a particular field.

Use the Reading to Ask Better Work Questions

Compare the person's experience in planning, communication, service, or coordination with the demands of a real role. Separate skill, preference, and opportunity. Then read the ten-year and annual layers without treating a pattern name as proof of a promotion or a suitable employer.
