Tai Yin in the Health Palace: Yin Deficiency, Hormones, Sleep

Tai Yin belongs to water, and in the Health Palace it often points to kidney yin, hormones, gynecology, sleep and the eyes. Yin deficiency with heat, menstrual or sleep disturbance are common; avoid staying up which consumes yin, keep emotions steady and seek specialist care for gynecological or hormonal abnormalities.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-11

The moon waxes and wanes; staying up is like keeping lights on all night; with the lights never off, yin is drained bit by bit.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some treat menstrual disorder and long insomnia as stress and endure; stars are tendency, while gynecology and hormones need specialists and tests.

The Underlying Logic

Tai Yin is water, focus on kidney yin, hormones, gynecology and sleep.

Prone to yin-deficiency heat, menstrual or sleep disturbance.

Do not stay up consuming yin, keep emotions steady.

This is tendency not diagnosis; rely on formal medical care.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Do not stay up, nourish yin and protect sleep
  2. Keep emotions steady, reduce anxiety
  3. See specialists for gynecological or hormonal ills
  4. Manage dry eyes and heat appropriately
  5. Watch hormonal and gynecological checks
  6. Follow the doctor judgment

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Long-term staying up consumes yin

Mistake 2: Enduring gynecological or hormonal ills without care

Mistake 3: Treating all insomnia and menstrual issues as stress
