Turn sharpness into a professional edge

In an undefined role, that quality may look confrontational; in a technical role, it can become diagnosis, editing, engineering, enforcement, or disciplined instruction. Career fit depends on whether the organization gives this sharpness a legitimate task and a clear method.

During pressured cycles, credentials or a shift toward specialist work may be more productive than fighting only for rank.
