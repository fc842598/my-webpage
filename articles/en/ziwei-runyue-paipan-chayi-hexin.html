Leap Month Births in a Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart: Find the Source of a One-Palace Difference

Check birth records and leap-month rules before comparing two Chinese astrology charts.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:14

Two chart apps place the Life Palace one position apart for a leap-month birthday. Start with the input record: the leap-month marker, day, birth time, and location. A form that silently drops the leap marker has already changed the question.

Compare the calculation settings

Keep the same clock time and birthplace in both apps. Record each app's lunar conversion, Life Palace, and Body Palace. If the converted month differs, inspect the leap-month rule first. If the month matches, inspect day-boundary and star-placement rules. Different AI wording alone does not establish a calculation error.

Use the chart carefully

Preserve the original civil date and each app's version. Read natal palaces and timing layers only after the input convention is known. If a tool does not reveal its leap-month setting, label the result uncertain rather than selecting a chart by a familiar-sounding personality paragraph.
