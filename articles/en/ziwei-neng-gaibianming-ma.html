Can Zi Wei Dou Shu Change Fate? The Chart Gives Tendencies and Behavior Changes Results

The chart cannot change the baseline you were born with, but once you see the structure, choices, habits and actions gradually change results, closer to changing luck and yourself than changing fate. Rather than pursuing a changed fate, turn insight into long-term action.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Fate is like a hand of cards you cannot change, yet how you play and sharpen the skill is yours, and played well an ordinary hand can still make a decent game.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some think knowing fate changes it, or hearing it is fixed they stop trying; the issue is mistaking seeing tendencies for either no action needed or a guaranteed turnaround.

The Underlying Logic

The natal structure gives tendencies and probability and cannot change the starting point or baseline.

What can change are choices, habits and responses: working with structure to favor strengths changes results.

So-called changing fate is mostly changing luck and oneself, and insight without action changes nothing.

Stay rational, neither trusting outside forces to change fate nor giving up in fatalism.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First accept the baseline and tendencies rather than dwelling on what cannot change
  2. See the chart clearly: which strengths to use and which gaps to mend
  3. Turn insight into concrete habits and actions and keep at them
  4. Adjust choices with the structure rather than forcing against it
  5. Neither trust paid rites to change fate nor use fate as an excuse
  6. Verify the effect of action through real changes over time

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming seeing fate changes it while insight never becomes action

Mistake 2: Giving up at hearing it is fixed and taking tendency as ending

Mistake 3: Trusting outside forces or paid rites instead of starting with yourself

Back to Your Own Chart

Zi Wei offers no magic to change fate but a chance to see yourself clearly. Turn insight into action and keep the habits long, and luck improves in your own hands bit by bit.

  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the whether Zi Wei Dou Shu can change fate line is activated in this stage
