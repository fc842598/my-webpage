Zi Wei Dou Shu Travel Palace for a Six-Month Overseas Contract

Compare a short assignment with the decade career path and exit costs.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-09 09:13

A six-month contract abroad can be valuable without becoming a permanent relocation. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the Travel Palace concerns adaptation outside familiar settings; the ten-year cycle frames the longer career direction. A busy annual signal does not settle the contract decision.

Calculate the real return

Compare after-tax pay, housing, insurance, flights and what happens if the contract ends early. An engineer paying these costs personally faces a different offer from a researcher whose institution covers them. Ask whether the assignment builds a skill that remains useful afterward.

Read and decide

Start with natal Life and Travel Palaces, add Career and Wealth, then the decade and year. Confirm work authorization, coverage and return travel in writing. Put net income, transferable skills and exit costs side by side.
