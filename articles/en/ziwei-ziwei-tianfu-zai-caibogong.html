Zi Wei and Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace: Earning from a Leading Place and Keeping It in the Treasury

The Wealth Palace reads cash flow and how one manages money. Zi Wei leads and coordinates while Tian Fu stores and steadies, so together one earns with breadth and manages with order, suiting fund management, large operations and steady finance. The risk is conservatism, face-related spending and proud isolation, so the key is letting money move and grow alongside keeping it, and sharing appropriately.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-01

People with Zi Wei and Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace handle money with a clear center, able both to earn with breadth and keep the books steady; when the treasury is guarded too tightly, money only sits instead of moving and gets spent on face.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the financial style with Zi Wei and Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace. Understand it reads cash flow, then see how leading and storing combine.

The Underlying Logic

Zi Wei in the Wealth Palace gives leadership and breadth in earning, often initiating with funds and resources while caring about dignity.

Tian Fu there gives skill at keeping money and managing, with solid reserves and the ability to store wealth.

Together they both earn and keep: with Lu Cun, Hua Lu and supporting stars the finances are large and steady, suiting managing big money and operations.

With malefics, void stars or Great Expenditure, loss follows from face, one-sided decisions or over-tight guarding, so movement and allocation need care.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Zi Wei and Tian Fu share the Wealth Palace and check their brightness
  2. Read cash flow and assets, separating earning, keeping and growing
  3. Use coordination to plan income, savings and investment as a whole
  4. Avoid spending on face and obligations, with rules for large expenses
  5. Let money beyond the savings move and grow sensibly, and share appropriately
  6. With the decade and year, decide whether to advance or hold as finance is activated

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Zi Wei-Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace only as becoming wealthy while ignoring management

Mistake 2: Only storing without movement until the treasury stagnates

Mistake 3: Spending on face, obligations and dignity until breadth becomes loss
