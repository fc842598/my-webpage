Is Zi Wei Dou Shu Accurate? It Shows Tendencies, Not Fixed Fate

Zi Wei Dou Shu describes the temperament tendencies and event probabilities shown by star combinations. It helps you understand your patterns and plan ahead, but it does not decide outcomes. Accuracy depends on a correct chart and birth time, and on whether the reading is layered and verifiable rather than absolute or promising to change fate.
