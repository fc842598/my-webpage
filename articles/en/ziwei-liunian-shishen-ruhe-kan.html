How to Read the Annual Body Palace

The annual Body Palace sets the later effort direction and focus of the year, read with the annual Life Palace (the year tone). Whichever area it lands in is where the year effort goes; it only adjusts one year.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

The annual Life Palace writes the year tone, the annual Body Palace where effort actually goes; reading both shows the year main line.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see only annual Life without Body, missing actual effort; others take the annual Body as long direction, but it only governs one year.

The Underlying Logic

The annual Body Palace attaches to one of six palaces on the annual chart.

Annual Life sets tone, Body sets later focus.

Body in Career: year focus on work; in Wealth: on money.

It governs one year; the long term still depends on natal Body and decade.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First build the annual chart and set annual Life Palace
  2. Find which palace the annual Body attaches to
  3. Read annual Life and Body together for tone and focus
  4. Read the Body star for effort style
  5. Read that palace transformations for energy activation
  6. Combine the decade to place the year focus in ten years

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing only annual Life without Body

Mistake 2: Taking annual Body as long direction

Mistake 3: Reading annual Body without star and transformations

Back to Your Own Chart

The annual Body reads where the year effort actually goes. Read Life and Body together for one year, then combine the decade, and the year main line is accurate.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the annual Body Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the annual Body Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
