Body Palace in the Wealth Palace: A Life Organized Around Earning and Financial Security

The Life Palace describes the chart's basic orientation, while the Body Palace shows where experience, effort, and responsibility become more concentrated.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-09 06:54

The Life Palace describes the chart's basic orientation, while the Body Palace shows where experience, effort, and responsibility become more concentrated over time. When the Body Palace is in Wealth, income structure and resource decisions often become recurring practical themes.

Money as a Life Organizing Problem

This placement does not mean greed or guaranteed wealth. It suggests that choices about work, independence, household costs, clients, and financial security repeatedly shape the person's later development. A stable salary, professional practice, business, or project-based income can all fit. The actual route comes from the main stars and transformations in the Wealth Palace, together with the Career and Travel Palaces.

Combinations That Change the Income Route

Body Palace in Wealth with Hua Ke in the Wealth Palace favors income built through recognized knowledge, credentials, craft, or a reputation for reliable work. It may suit consulting, design, analysis, licensed services, or specialized content, especially when the Career Palace supports continuity. Body Palace in Wealth with Po Jun and Wen Qu transforming into Hua Ke is more change-oriented: technical repair, product redesign, systems migration, and project improvement can become billable expertise. If the Career Palace is also strong, leaving work for an undefined period may feel more destabilizing than expected. If the Travel Palace is strong instead, clients, platforms, or markets outside the original environment may provide the better outlet. Hua Quan in Wealth adds control and commercial responsibility, while Hua Ji calls for tighter cash-flow rules rather than panic.

Chart-Reading Order

Confirm that the Body Palace falls in Wealth, then identify the Wealth Palace's main stars and Four Transformations. Read the Career Palace for the work vehicle, the Travel Palace for outside markets, and the opposite palace for pressure or support. Finally add the ten-year and annual periods. The practical goal is to build one sustainable income structure before chasing multiple disconnected opportunities.
