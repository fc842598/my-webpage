Cross-City Commute, Same Salary: Career and Wealth Palaces in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Compare work location, travel time, and net cash when commuting costs rise without a pay change.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:16

A company moves its office to another city. Pay stays the same, but train fares and meals become more expensive. The Career Palace frames work location and duties; the Wealth Palace frames cash left after recurring costs. An unchanged salary is not an unchanged budget.

Compare real options

List required office days, journey hours, remote-work terms, fares, and reimbursements. One employee travels daily; another rents a room for two nights a week. A one-time moving allowance should not be counted as permanent monthly income. The Travel Palace can frame the external change, but tickets and payroll establish the numbers.

Decide from facts

Read Career duties, Wealth cash flow, and Travel conditions before the timing layer. Use the employment contract and several months of actual costs to discuss hybrid work or compensation. The chart organizes questions; it does not decide whether to leave.
