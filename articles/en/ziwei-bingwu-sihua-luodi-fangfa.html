Putting the 2026 Bing-Wu Four Transformations into Practice

The 2026 Bing stem gives Tong-Tian Lu, Tian-Ji Quan, Wen-Chang Ke and Lian-Zhen Ji, but locate the landing palace and natal structure first; the yearly table is not a universal fortune for everyone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Anyone can look up the yearly table, but the hard part is applying it. The same Tong-Tian Lu in the Wealth Palace and in the Karma Palace are two different matters; the star is the baseline and the transformation is the current adjustment.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many people force the Bing-Wu transformations onto themselves without the landing palace. The issue is not the transformations but where they act on your natal chart; without locating that, the yearly fortune is empty.

The Underlying Logic

The Bing stem fixes Tong-Tian Lu, Tian-Ji Quan, Wen-Chang Ke and Lian-Zhen Ji, but the landing palace differs by person.

Tong-Tian Lu leans toward resources of comfort, Tian-Ji Quan toward planning drive, Wen-Chang Ke toward writing and reputation, and Lian-Zhen Ji toward friction with rules and relationships.

Quan often brings responsibility and drive, not only promotion; Ke's exposure needs real content to back it.

The year stem alone cannot judge an individual; it must layer over the natal chart and decade to see whether the structure can carry it.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm which four stars the 2026 Bing stem transforms
  2. Locate each of the four stars in your natal chart
  3. Read the landing palace's major star and triple-direction to set the structural baseline
  4. For Tong-Tian Lu see what resources land on; for Tian-Ji Quan identify where responsibility sits
  5. For Wen-Chang Ke examine what backs the reputation; for Lian-Zhen Ji see what the friction requires you to restrain
  6. Layer the decade and year to place this year within the ten-year background before acting

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the yearly table as a universal fortune for everyone

Mistake 2: Looking only at the star without the landing palace

Mistake 3: Assuming Quan guarantees promotion and Ji guarantees trouble without checking what carries them

Back to Your Own Chart

The value of the Bing-Wu transformations appears only through the landing palace and natal structure. Locate first, examine capacity next, and only then plan the year.

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to putting the 2026 Bing-Wu transformations into practice before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the putting the 2026 Bing-Wu transformations into practice line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
