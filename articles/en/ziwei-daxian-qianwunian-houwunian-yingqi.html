Dividing the First and Second Five Years of a Decade in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Within a ten-year decade, effects do not land evenly: Northern Dipper stars tend to manifest in the first five years and Southern Dipper stars in the last five, likewise for the first and second half of a year. It helps place major events in a more specific stage, but still combine the transformations rather than applying it mechanically.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some only ask whether the decade is good, yet Northern fortune arrives early and Southern fortune late; dividing the halves refines the timing.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to place a decade matter in the first or second half. First see whether the governing star is Northern or Southern, then see which years the decade transformations activate, combining both rather than relying on one rhyme.

The Underlying Logic

Northern stars tend to initiate and their effects land in the first five years.

Southern stars tend to gather and guard and their effects land in the last five.

Within a year, Northern effects lean to the first half and Southern to the second, the same rule refined.

It only indicates the likely stage; the exact year still follows the decade and year transformations.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First identify the governing stars of the decade
  2. Tell whether they are Northern or Southern
  3. If Northern, focus the first five years; if Southern, the last five
  4. Then place the decade transformations to find activated years
  5. Align the dipper tendency with the year activation
  6. Conclude with stage and rhythm, not treating the rhyme as fixed years

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating decade fortune as even across the halves

Mistake 2: Mechanically fixing North to early and South to late without transformations

Mistake 3: Dividing the halves but never pinning specific years

Back to Your Own Chart

Decade timing divides into halves: Northern-led matters show early and Southern-led late. It gives a stage tendency, then the year transformations pin the years, making the ten-year rhythm both detailed and sound.
