How Zi Wei Separates Wealth, Career, and Property

Income, job role, and stored assets are different questions in Zi Wei Dou Shu. Read the Wealth, Career, and Property Palaces as a chain.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 10:20

Higher income without assets, or a senior title with tight cash flow, is not necessarily a contradiction. It often means Wealth, Career, and Property were treated as one topic.

Wealth shows money flow, Career shows the role that earns it, and Property shows what can settle into a base.

Name the question

Ask Wealth for how money moves, Career for the work role, and Property for what remains. The Life Palace shows whether the person can carry the line.

Transformations land in different links and should not be blended.

Check the triad

Strong Wealth with weak Property can earn without retaining. Strong Career with pressured Wealth can carry responsibility while collections lag. Strong Property with ordinary Career can preserve a base while income grows slowly.

Use the full chain rather than one strength label.

Examples

Hua Lu in Wealth with Hua Ke in Career can monetize expertise but still needs a Property budget. Hua Quan in Career with a neutral Wealth Palace often asks for clearer pay and authority before accepting more responsibility.

Practical reading order

Name income, role, or assets, read the matching palace, add Life and the triad, then use timing to see which link moves first.

Practical Reading Order

Start with the question palace, compare its opposite and trine partners, then add timing and transformations before making a practical judgment.
