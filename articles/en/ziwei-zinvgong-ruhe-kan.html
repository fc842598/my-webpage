How to Read the Children Palace: Children, Bonds and Creativity, Not Debt

The Children Palace describes your bond and relating with children and juniors, and it also governs creativity and the fruits of cooperation. Do not use it only to count children or read friction as someone coming to collect a debt.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

The Children Palace first holds how you relate to the next generation, then your creativity in turning ideas into results; the two are often the same energy.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Seeing a malefic in the Children Palace, some fear a child has come to collect a debt. The real issue is reading parenthood as opposition; the palace describes how to relate, not a grudge script.

The Underlying Logic

The Children Palace holds the strength and pattern of your bond with children and juniors, and reflects your attitude toward the next generation.

It also governs creativity, output and the fruits of cooperation; children and creations share one source here.

It sits opposite the Property Palace, so children and home are two sides and are read together.

A child traits need the Children Palace triple-direction; one palace alone is half the story.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the Children Palace and read its star for the parenting pattern and the child likely traits
  2. Open the triple-direction to see whether support or friction dominates the relating
  3. Read the opposite Property Palace to understand the child within the family setting
  4. Count romance, malefic and assistant stars to separate bond, testing and disputes
  5. Read transformations here: Lu is affection and Ji is concern or worry
  6. Layer the decade and year to see the current theme in trying to conceive, raising or relating

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Using the palace only to count children and their gender while ignoring relating and creativity

Mistake 2: Reading a malefic as debt and turning parenthood into opposition

Mistake 3: Reading only the home palace without the Property Palace and trines, missing the full child

Back to Your Own Chart

The Children Palace shows how you relate to the next generation and create things. Read it within the home and triple-direction, and the bond becomes clearer while worry turns into method.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Children Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Children Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
