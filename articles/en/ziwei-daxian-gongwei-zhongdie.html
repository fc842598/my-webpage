Overlap Between Natal and Decade Palaces: How to Read the Native Chart Laid Over the Ten-Year Chart

The decade chart takes one palace as its new Life Palace, rebuilds the twelve, and lays it over the native chart. Where they overlap is the life area magnified over the decade. The natal chart is the base; the decade is the lesson; read the overlap in two layers—whether the foundation is sound is natal, whether it holds up for these ten years is the decade.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

Entering a certain decade, someone suddenly feels one area magnified: it was unremarkable, yet these ten years revolve around it. This usually happens when the decade Life Palace overlaps a natal palace, making that palace the lead for the decade.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Palace overlap answers two things: which area is pushed to center stage for the decade, and whether the natal foundation in that area can carry it. Overlap is not simply adding fortune and misfortune; it is the decade's lesson pressing on a specific natal foundation.

The Underlying Logic

The decade takes one palace as its new Life, rebuilds the twelve into a ten-year mini chart, and lays it over the native chart.

The overlap is the life area magnified and forced to be faced over the decade, where the ten-year lesson lands.

The stars and pattern of that natal palace decide the thickness and carrying capacity of the foundation itself.

Read two layers: the natal for the foundation, the decade for that ten-year load and trigger; only a weak foundation under a heavy decade reads as strain, never from one layer alone.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find where the decade Life Palace falls
  2. Treat that palace as the new Life and rebuild the decade's twelve palaces
  3. Layer each decade palace back onto the native chart and mark where it presses
  4. Find the deepest overlap, activated by decade transformations, as the ten-year lesson
  5. Read that natal palace's foundation: its stars, assistants, malefics and natal transformations
  6. Then layer the year: only when a yearly limit triggers the overlap is it a timing

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the decade chart as a separate fate, ignoring that it lies on the natal foundation

Mistake 2: Reading only the overlap's fortune without the thickness of that natal foundation

Mistake 3: Compressing a ten-year lesson into a guaranteed good or bad year, collapsing the two layers

Back to Your Own Chart

The decade chart does not start a new fate; it presses ten years of weight onto one natal foundation. Thick ground and a fitting lesson mean a steady decade; thin ground repeatedly triggered by yearly limits means shoring up early. Read the two layers together and the overlap makes sense.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to overlap between natal and decade palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the overlap between natal and decade palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
