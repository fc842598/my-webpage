Zuo Fu and You Bi: How Same-Palace, Opposite, Flanking, and Supporting Help Differ

The two helper stars bring support in different positions, and the placement decides whether help is direct or indirect.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 10:35

Zuo Fu and You Bi are a pair of helpful stars that both lean optimistic and generous, but their help differs in strength and style depending on position. First separate same-palace, opposite-axis, flanking, and three-harmony placements, then judge whether the help is direct or indirect.

Their character

Zuo Fu leans honest and steady, while You Bi leans resourceful and flexible. Together they often soften a sharp or pessimistic major star and bring noble helpers or good friends, a kind of support that does not need to be forced. They are mainly supportive and cannot replace the major stars.

How the four positions differ

Same palace, especially in the Career Palace, means people help both directly and indirectly. An opposite axis on the Life-Travel line suggests support wherever you go. Flanking help is more indirect and less obvious, while the three-harmony position is strongest in the Life Palace's trio or when a major limit reaches it. The position should guide the reading rather than treating all help as equal.

Step-by-step reading

  1. Find the two stars on the chart and confirm their position relative to the target palace.
  2. Separate same-palace, opposite, flanking, and three-harmony cases to judge direct versus indirect help.
  3. Read Zuo Fu's steadiness and You Bi's resourcefulness as different styles of help.
  4. Combine with the major stars to see whether they strengthen an advantage or ease a weakness.
  5. Check whether the major limit or annual cycle triggers them and at which stage the help is stronger.

Reading limits

The stars point to outside support; they do not guarantee a helper appears or mean effort is unnecessary. In real life, maintaining relationships and doing your own work well make the support usable. The chart flags opportunity, while preparation decides whether it can be used.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and mark the two stars and the target palace.
  2. Judge the four positional relationships first, then set the strength of help.
  3. Combine with the major stars for the direction of support rather than reading the helpers alone.
  4. Check limit triggers to see the stage when support appears.
  5. Match the conclusion to real cooperation and networks without absolute claims.
