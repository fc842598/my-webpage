Lu Cun and Hua Lu Together in the Wealth Palace: Double Lu Looks Great, But It Is Not Free Money

Lu Cun is fixed stock and Hua Lu is flowing increment. When both share or aspect the Wealth Palace, savings and inflow strengthen together; whether money actually stays depends on same-palace Ji, Yang Tuo and whether decade or yearly limits trigger outflow.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

Seeing Lu Cun and Hua Lu together in the Wealth Palace, many immediately feel they have hit it big. Hold the celebration: double Lu is a strong base, but it governs earning and saving ability, not an automatic deposit.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Double Lu should be split: Lu Cun is inventory in the warehouse, Hua Lu is fresh inflow. Full warehouse plus inflow is structurally good, but whether the warehouse leaks and whether the flow gets siphoned depend on malefics and Ji.

The Underlying Logic

Lu Cun is celestial salary, standing for fixed stock, steady income and stable assets; in Wealth it means solid saving ability.

Hua Lu is flowing salary, standing for opportunity and new income; in Wealth it means fresh current in recent years.

Double Lu in one palace or by triple aspect strengthens saving and open-source at once, a structural advantage.

Yet Lu Cun is often flanked by Yang Tuo, and if Ji clashes or a decade or yearly limit triggers, guarantees, bad investments or favors can still leak money.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm whether Lu Cun and Hua Lu share the Wealth Palace
  2. Separate which is stock and which is increment
  3. Check the same and flanking palaces for Yang, Tuo, Huo and Ling
  4. Check whether Ji clashes or a decade limit triggers outflow
  5. Judge whether double Lu means earn-and-keep or earn-more-keep-less
  6. In practice, manage cash flow and diversify rather than concentrating on one asset

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring free money from double Lu and blindly leveraging

Mistake 2: Treating Lu Cun as spendable cash, ignoring it is stock

Mistake 3: Reading double Lu without malefics and Ji, missing the leak channels

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Lu Cun and Hua Lu together in the Wealth Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Lu Cun and Hua Lu together in the Wealth Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
