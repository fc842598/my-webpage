Decade and Birth-Year Transformations Coexisting: Why Neither Replaces the Other

How the lifelong birth-year tone and the ten-year decade trigger work on different time scales and must be read as separate layers.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-17 10:51

In a decade phase, the chart holds both birth-year transformations and the decade transformations, with two layers of signals overlapping. Some ask whether the decade transformations make the birth-year ones unnecessary. They actually work on different time scales and cover separate parts, so neither can replace or merge into the other.

The roles of the two transformation layers

Birth-year transformations are the lifelong tone describing long-term recurring themes, while decade transformations govern only these ten years as the environment and trigger of the stage. If both layers are on the same star, first separate which is long term and which is for the decade, then see how they overlap rather than covering one with the other.

Step-by-step coexistence reading

  1. Confirm which transformations are birth-year and which are the current decade's.
  2. Mark birth-year transformations separately and understand them as a long-term pattern.
  3. Mark decade transformations separately and understand them as the ten-year trigger.
  4. Read the long-term tone first, then see how this stage acts on it.
  5. Record the two layers apart before overlaying, rather than treating the decade as a replacement.

The short term overlays the long term

The decade transformations make one part of these ten years especially smooth or stuck, but they do not directly rewrite the birth-year tendency. Keeping the time scale steady prevents one good or bad phase from being misread as the lifelong pattern.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and separate birth-year from decade transformations.
  2. Read the long-term birth-year layer first, then the ten-year layer.
  3. Keep the two layers apart, then see the overlap.
  4. Check against the reality of this phase.
  5. Keep the conclusion conditional and do not let the two layers replace each other.
