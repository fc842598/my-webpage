Bing Fu in the Health Palace: Prevention and Early Care

Bing Fu in the Health Palace is a fairly direct health sign, suggesting a sensitive, illness-prone constitution. Put prevention first, live regularly, seek early care for symptoms without delay and follow doctor orders.
