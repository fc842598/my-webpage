How to Verify Your Chart Readings

Verification is the practical way to improve: write down predictions beforehand (which stage, what matter, what tendency), then compare afterward; record matches and find reasons for misses, and skill grows fast.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

Reading without verification leaves right and wrong muddled; write and review to know what you read accurately.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some fear wrong notes and face loss, not writing, staying at the same level; verification is for yourself, not others grading.

The Underlying Logic

Beforehand write clear predictions: time, area, tendency.

Afterward compare one by one.

Matches: note the signals read right.

Misses: find whether signals were misread or layers confused.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Form the habit of writing predictions first
  2. Write clear time and area, not vague terms
  3. At the point, return and compare
  4. Summarize patterns in matches
  5. Review which step failed in misses
  6. Accumulate over time into your own reading feel

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading without verification, right-wrong muddled

Mistake 2: Fearing face loss and not writing

Mistake 3: Not finding reasons for misses, skill stagnant

Back to Your Own Chart

Chart verification asks how reading skill genuinely improves. Write, return to compare and review right-wrong, and you know where you are accurate and where you fall short.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to verifying chart readings before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the verifying chart readings line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
