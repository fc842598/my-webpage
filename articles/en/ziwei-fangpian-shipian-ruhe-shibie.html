How to Spot Wealth-Loss Signals in a Chart and Prevent Fraud

In a chart, Hua Ji, emptiness, robbery or waste stars aspecting the Wealth or Karma Palace signal a tendency toward wealth loss. But fraud prevention ultimately relies on real actions: do not click unknown links, do not transfer money, verify official channels, and any paid luck-changing, treasury-replenishing or consecrated wealth-blessing is likely a scam.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

A chart can hint at what may cause wealth loss this stage, but it cannot block scammers for you; the real firewall is your own habits and verification.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see wealth-loss signals and panic into buying luck-changing services, only to be scammed; others ignore the signals entirely and set no defenses, and both lose out.

The Underlying Logic

Chart wealth-loss signals: Hua Ji in the Wealth Palace, emptiness or robbery in aspect, waste stars conjunct, hinting money comes and goes this stage.

Common loss scenarios: high-interest bait, insider tips, rebate scams, impersonating authorities, paid luck-changing.

Three real prevention principles: do not click unknown links, do not transfer to unknown accounts, always verify official channels.

Any promise of paid luck-changing, treasury replenishing, consecrated wealth or guaranteed profit can essentially be judged a scam.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the Wealth and Karma Palaces for wealth-loss signals to know the risk direction
  2. Translate the signal into real scenarios (investment impulse or gullibility)
  3. Build prevention habits: no unknown links, verify before transferring
  4. When encountering paid luck-changing promises, directly judge as scam
  5. Pause large decisions for 24 hours and discuss with someone trusted
  6. Regularly check accounts and subscriptions, clean up unnecessary authorizations

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing wealth-loss signals and buying luck-changing services, getting scammed instead

Mistake 2: Ignoring signals entirely and setting no defense, trusting high-interest promises

Mistake 3: Treating the chart as a shield, assuming having a signal means you cannot be scammed

Back to Your Own Chart

Recognizing scams combines chart hints with a real-life firewall. Know where you tend to stumble, then establish the habits of not clicking, not transferring and not superstition, and you can avoid the wealth-loss traps. For all money matters, rely on licensed institutions and official channels.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to recognizing scams and fraud before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the recognizing scams and fraud line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
