Your Spending Style in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Wealth, Karma and Property Explain Why Money Never Stays

How someone spends, why they cannot resist buying and whether they save read Wealth (spending manner), Karma (whether money buys joy or reassurance) and Property (whether money settles into assets). Combinations map to reward, status, anxiety and hoarding spending types; naming yours lets you design the right saving structure.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-10

Trouble saving is often not income but spending pattern—some buy joy, some soothe anxiety, some pay for face; these drivers are clear in Wealth and Karma.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why a decent salary still vanishes, why stress triggers orders and why saving fails, first know your spending type, then design saving that routes around human weakness.

The Underlying Logic

Wealth sets the spending action: Wu Qu/Tian Fu/Tian Xiang are careful, value-minded planners; Tan Lang has diverse desires and spends on experience and taste; Po Jun/Qi Sha swing big, daring large one-off purchases and drastic decluttering; Tai Yin is gradual but hoards for family and security; Tian Ji is comparison-shopping, researching before buying.

Karma sets the psychological driver: Karma with Tan Lang/Lian Zhen or Ji/malefics is often emotion-driven—reward when happy, compensate when sad, order to regain control when anxious, the most month-end-empty type; steady Karma spends closer to real needs and is less pushed by livestream selling and limited offers.

Property sets retention: steady Property with Lu turns earnings into savings, real estate and long-term assets; Property with Kong/Jie/Great Loss leaks through impulse buys, ineffective hoarding and paying for others—auto-transfer income to savings first, lower spendable checking, and plug the leak with structure.

Targeted saving design: reward types use save-first plus a fixed joy budget, planning reward rather than banning it; status types reduce high-spend social scenes and separate "I want" from "others think I should have"; anxiety types handle emotion first with a 24-hour cooling period; hoarding types use lists and one-in-one-out. Designing around the type beats simply vowing to spend less.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Wealth stars to classify planner, experience, big-swing or hoarder
  2. Read Karma to find real need versus emotion behind spending
  3. Read Property to judge retention and locate the leak
  4. Match yourself: reward, status, anxiety or hoarding type
  5. Plug leaks with save-first, auto-transfer, cooling periods and lists
  6. Separate want from need, spending limited money where it truly raises quality of life

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Blaming being careless with money without the Wealth/Karma psychological driver

Mistake 2: Quitting buying by willpower without auto-saving and cooling-period structures

Mistake 3: Ignoring Property retention, earning more yet still ending the month empty

Back to Your Own Chart

Before blaming income for not saving: Wealth writes how you spend, Karma why, Property whether it stays. Know your type and route around impulse with save-first structure, so saving does not restart from scratch every year.
