Fu-Xiang Facing the Wall Pattern: A Stable Treasury and Management Destiny

When Tian Fu and Tian Xiang meet across the Life trine without malefic break, it is Fu-Xiang facing the wall—steady, reliable, good at coordination, valuing security and dignity, suited to finance, administration, operations and leadership. Wealth accumulates through steady management and resource integration; auspicious transformations bring plenty, malefics reduce it and warn against over-conservatism.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

It is the steward pattern: Zi Wei the ruler, Tian Fu the treasury, Tian Xiang the seal; this chart best arranges resources, people and process, steadying mature organizations.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some find it slow and unexciting, yet its strength is steadiness and endurance; watch over-caution that refuses chances and turns management talent into stagnation.

The Underlying Logic

Formation: Tian Fu and Tian Xiang arch toward Life across the trine without malefic/Ji break, major stars strong.

Tian Fu is the storing treasury, Tian Xiang the seal of assistance and coordination—together steady, trustworthy, planning, security-and-dignity oriented.

Favors accounting, banking, administration, HR, operations, supply chain, secretarial staff and management, rising through long-term trust and resource integration.

With Lu/Quan/Ke and assistants, wealth and rank are steady and ample; malefics break it toward missed chances or security anxiety—delegate and experiment in moderation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Tian Fu/Tian Xiang face Life in trine without malefic break
  2. Assess strength and auspicious transformations for steadying power
  3. Favor finance, administration, operations and coordination routes
  4. Build trust on a mature platform through reliability and integration
  5. Deliberately raise decisiveness and risk tolerance to avoid over-caution
  6. In good decades rise and expand authority; in malefic ones hold the base

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Mistaking steadiness for lack of ability and chasing thrills

Mistake 2: Over-valuing safety and watching chances pass

Mistake 3: Judging wealth from Fu-Xiang without checking malefics

Back to Your Own Chart

Fu-Xiang's gift is making everything run reliably. Be a coordinator on a platform for long-term growth, but do not let caution bind you; your reliability is itself scarce leadership.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Fu-Xiang facing the wall pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Fu-Xiang facing the wall pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
