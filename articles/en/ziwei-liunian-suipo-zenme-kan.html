A Sui Po Year: A Clash-Year of Change, Seeking Stability and Avoiding Impulsive Decisions

Sui Po always sits opposite the year’s Tai Sui and clashes with it, governing major change, breaks, overturned plans and turbulence. In a Sui Po year, sudden shifts in work, housing, relationships or partnerships are common. The key is postponing big matters, avoiding irreversible decisions under emotion or pressure, leaving room and preparing alternatives. A clash can also open a deadlock, and the point is adjusting with the current rather than forcing it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-03

A Sui Po year often feels unusually changeable, with plans repeatedly overturned: what seemed steady may shift suddenly, forcing it is exhausting, while leaving a way out makes it smoother.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to handle a Sui Po year and prevent major breaks. Understand it as clashing with Tai Sui and bringing turbulence, then see which palace it lands in and which line it clashes.

The Underlying Logic

Sui Po, one of the twelve pre-year stars, sits opposite Tai Sui and governs major change, breaks and overturned plans, with one of the stronger forces in the set.

It leans harsh but is not a dead end: a clash can open a long deadlock, yet impulsive decisions and forcing it can turn a postponable matter into a major break.

Whichever palace it lands in is what it clashes: Career for work, Spouse for relationships, Property for housing, read with the opposite and trine palaces.

The minor star is only an annual layer; respond by postponing big matters, keeping alternatives and avoiding irreversible emotional decisions, and once it passes the natal and decade line resumes.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate which annual palace Sui Po sits in to identify what may be broken open this year
  2. List the year’s major decisions and postpone what can wait until after the clash
  3. Prepare alternatives and a fallback for key plans rather than relying on one path
  4. Do not take irreversible steps such as quitting, separating or signing under emotion and pressure
  5. If change has already happened, adjust with it and treat it as a chance to clear a deadlock
  6. Read the decade to see whether this clash is a long-term turn or a one-year disturbance

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring a year of major ruin at the sight of Sui Po while ignoring that a clash can free a deadlock

Mistake 2: Forcing matters in change and emotion and making irreversible decisions impulsively

Mistake 3: Keeping no fallback or alternatives, becoming passive once things shift

Back to Your Own Chart

A Sui Po year’s lesson is that change is unavoidable; slowing big matters, keeping ample fallback and adjusting with the current means the clash often clears what should have been let go long ago.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the year when Sui Po, the year-break star, is active before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the year when Sui Po, the year-break star, is active line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
