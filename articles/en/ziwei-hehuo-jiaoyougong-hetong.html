Reading Partnership in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Separate the Friends Palace, Contracts and Equity First

Partnership first reads the Friends Palace for cooperation and subordinates, then contracts. Good relations do not mean clear terms; equity, roles and exits must be in writing, with a professional lawyer's advice as the standard.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Many partner because of good relations and later split for the same reason: the rules were never made clear. The Friends Palace writes how you and partners get along, but goodwill cannot replace contracts and equity.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The key is separating relationship from contract. A good Friends Palace only shows you get along; what decides whether the partnership lasts is whether equity, roles and exits are clear, both indispensable.

The Underlying Logic

The Friends Palace reflects partners, subordinates and the pattern of relating.

Contracts are read through document stars such as Wen-Chang and Wen-Qu and related palaces.

Good relations do not mean clear terms; equity, roles and exits must be in writing.

Legal matters follow a professional lawyer and proper channels; fortune reading only gives relationship tendencies.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the Friends Palace to set the pattern with partners
  2. Check for malefics and Ji to identify friction risk in cooperation
  3. Read document stars to judge contract fortune
  4. List equity, roles and decision mechanisms item by item
  5. Design exit and dissolution terms, settling rules before feelings
  6. Have a professional lawyer review all terms before signing and investing

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Partnering on goodwill without a clear contract

Mistake 2: Equating a good Friends Palace directly with partnership success

Mistake 3: Ignoring exit mechanisms and becoming entangled at the split

Back to Your Own Chart

Partnership combines relationship and contract, and missing either is dangerous. Read the relating pattern on the chart, write equity and exits on paper, and use lawyer review so cooperation lasts.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to partnership through the Friends Palace and contracts before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the partnership through the Friends Palace and contracts line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
