When the Six Auspicious Stars Truly Form a Pattern: Assistants, Nobles and Literary Stars Are Not "the More the Better"

The six auspicious stars are three pairs—Zuo Fu/You Bi, Tian Kui/Tian Yue, Wen Chang/Wen Qu. To form a pattern they must share the major star's palace or trine it without being broken by malefics/voids; scattered stars are decoration; aligned, supporting and protecting the major star is real help.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

Auspicious stars are not a counting game. What matters is whether they gather around the major star, push the same way, and whether malefics or voids intercept that help midway.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People call many assistants "good fortune." Ask whether they sit in the triple-direction, which major star they support, whether they pair up, and whether malefics/voids break them—scattered luck and aligned support differ.

The Underlying Logic

The six form three pairs with distinct roles: assistants for long-term peer support/execution, nobles for key-moment sponsorship, literary stars for learning/expression/reputation—none substitutes for another.

First condition is position: share the major star's palace or trine it; stars in unrelated palaces cannot help the main line however numerous.

Second is pairing and momentum: one star is thin, a pair is steadier, and three pairs aligned with Lu/Quan/Ke form strong "ruler with full court" support.

Third is not being broken: assistants dislike sharing or being clasped by Yang/Tuo/Fire/Bell/voids, which discount help or turn it into well-meaning interference.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. List the major star of Life (or the asked palace), then place all six auspicious stars
  2. For each, judge whether it sits in that star's triple-direction; drop those that do not
  3. See whether they pair and whether all three align toward the same major star to gauge concentration
  4. Check each for sharing/clasping malefics or voids to compute net help
  5. Read by function: assistants=helpers, nobles=sponsors, literary=study/expression—do not blend
  6. Finally add transformations; Lu/Quan/Ke meeting auspicious stars magnifies help into real opportunity

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating assistant count as a fortune score, "more is better"

Mistake 2: Ignoring position and counting unrelated-palace stars as Life support

Mistake 3: Overlooking how malefics/voids break auspicious stars, treating clasped/clashed ones as full help

Back to Your Own Chart

Auspicious patterns are about surrounding the right star, pairing up and staying protected. Stop counting stars on your chart; see whether they align cleanly around your major star without malefic damage—aligned, one beats ten scattered.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the conditions for the six auspicious stars to form a pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the conditions for the six auspicious stars to form a pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
