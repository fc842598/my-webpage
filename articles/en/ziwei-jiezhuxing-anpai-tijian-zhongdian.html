Health Check Priorities by the Health Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu

The Health Palace does not destine disease but flags which system alarms first under stress; tailoring check-up priorities to its stars beats indiscriminate test bundles. Sun leans cardiovascular, eyes and blood pressure; Tian Ji nerves, sleep and thyroid; Zi Wei digestion and metabolism; Tan Lang liver/gallbladder and reproductive hormones; Ju Men digestion and oral health. Add checks when a decade/year activates Health. This covers tendencies and screening direction only, not diagnosis; for any symptom follow a formal hospital.

Health checks suffer two extremes: never checking, or piling on tests that miss your weak point; the value of Health Palace is focusing limited check-up budget on the system most worth watching.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking what to check, which parts need more attention, or how to choose tests, first read the priority system the Health stars mark, then combine age and symptoms.

The Underlying Logic

Cardiovascular/eyes lean Sun: Health with Sun alarms first through heart, blood pressure and eyes under stress; focus on blood pressure, lipids, glucose, ECG and fundus, with regular rechecks under chronic high pressure and late nights.

Nerves/sleep lean Tian Ji: Tian Ji in Health tends to neurasthenia, insomnia and anxiety with somatic symptoms; thyroid, sleep and stress markers deserve attention, half addressed by tests and half by routine and emotional regulation.

Digestion/metabolism lean Zi Wei, digestion Ju Men: Zi Wei watches spleen-stomach, weight and glucose metabolism; Ju Men watches gastrointestinal, oral and throat and absorption, where regular eating beats supplements.

Liver/gallbladder/reproduction lean Tan Lang, chronic accumulation Tuo Luo: Tan Lang watches liver-gallbladder, reproductive and endocrine markers and controls alcohol and routine; Tuo Luo in Health governs accumulated chronic issues, so three-high markers need scheduled rechecks and never stop medication by feel. In years when decade/year or malefics activate Health, proactively screen that system; even Tian Liang-Hua-Ke, which often means meeting a good doctor and turning a corner, still means check first, then rest easy.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Health stars to mark the system that alarms first under stress
  2. Choose check priorities by star rather than piling on tests indiscriminately
  3. Sun checks cardiovascular/eyes, Tian Ji nerves/sleep/thyroid, Zi Wei metabolism, Tan Lang liver-gallbladder/endocrine
  4. Tuo-Luo chronic types recheck on schedule and never stop medication by feel; add screening in malefic-activated years
  5. Combine age, family history and current symptoms and let a formal-hospital doctor set the final plan
  6. A check-up is only screening; for any persistent discomfort follow formal medical diagnosis

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Health stars as a diagnosis, frightening yourself or self-medicating

Mistake 2: Doing generic checks yearly without adding targeted screening and missing the real risk

Mistake 3: A Tuo-Luo chronic type stopping medication by feel and skipping rechecks until an accumulated issue turns acute
