How to Read the Fire-Tan Pattern in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Sudden Gain vs. Keeping It

The Fire-Tan pattern pairs Tan Lang with Huo Xing, supported by Lu Cun or Hua Lu, and signals sudden opportunity and self-made gains. Whether you keep that windfall depends on the Wealth and Karma palaces and later limits, not a guarantee of riches.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28

Fire-Tan describes sudden momentum; opportunity arrives faster, yet arriving fast is not the same as keeping it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many see Fire-Tan and ask when they will get rich, mistaking a pattern about timing for a guarantee of wealth.

The Underlying Logic

Fire-Tan forms when Tan Lang is strong in Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei, with Huo Xing in the same palace or close in the triple-direction, supported by Lu Cun, Hua Lu, Hua Quan, Hua Ke and the assistant stars.

If Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Di Kong, Di Jie appear together, or Hua Ji strikes Tan Lang and Huo Xing directly, the pattern breaks and sudden opportunity comes with sharp rises and falls.

The fire is the trigger that ignites Tan Lang's desire and connections in a short time, so it often shows as a sudden rise in one stage rather than a smooth life.

After a windfall, the Wealth palace shows how money moves, the Karma palace shows whether you have the mindset to keep it, and the next limits show whether momentum continues; missing any one, it comes and goes quickly.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm whether Tan Lang is strong in its palace; a fallen star weakens the pattern sharply
  2. See whether Huo Xing shares the palace or meets in the triple-direction; the same palace triggers most directly
  3. Check for Lu Cun, Hua Lu, Hua Quan, Hua Ke and the assistant stars; without them there is drive but few resources
  4. Count Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, the emptiness stars and Hua Ji; the more breakers, the more you must guard against swings
  5. After a windfall, focus on Wealth and Karma to see whether you will keep it or reinvest it all
  6. Layer the current and next two limits to confirm whether the momentum is a phase or can continue

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Fire-Tan as a guarantee of wealth and ignoring its forming and breaking conditions

Mistake 2: Seeing only Tan Lang and Huo Xing without the lu and auspicious support, so opportunity arrives without resources

Mistake 3: Assuming the rise will continue after a windfall and ignoring Wealth, Karma and later limits

Back to Your Own Chart

Fire-Tan gives a sudden one-step-ahead opportunity, and the real divide is whether you keep it afterward. Read it as timing, then plan with Wealth, Karma and the limits so good luck does not vanish.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Fire-Tan pattern and sudden gains before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Fire-Tan pattern and sudden gains line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
