When Zhu Que Appears: Disputes and Rumor; Clear Words Remove Half the Trouble

Zhu Que governs disputes, rumor, paperwork arguments and careless speech, bringing more misunderstandings than usual; clarify first, keep written records and use proper channels to avoid most trouble.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

Zhu Que governs words; this year much of the trouble is not a bad matter but a misspoken line or a twisted meaning.

What This Question Is Really Asking

When talked about, some rush to argue back and inflate a small matter; the issue is not whether gossip exists but being led by emotion and failing to manage speech and evidence.

The Underlying Logic

Zhu Que is fiery and governs speech and documents, showing arguments, misunderstandings, rumor and disagreements over contract terms.

It tests expression: a matter explained clearly and recorded stays small; careless or vague words are easily twisted.

When it meets auspicious stars and Hua Ke, proper documents and open statements resolve things; when malefics gather, disputes linger.

Important agreements this year especially need to be in writing, relying on a qualified lawyer, proper channels and a written contract.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate which palace Zhu Que falls in to see which relationships or affairs draw disputes
  2. Put important communication in writing this year and rely less on spoken or emotional words
  3. Clarify facts first when misunderstood rather than arguing back
  4. Keep contracts, receipts and messages organized so evidence is solid
  5. Handle legal and contract matters through proper channels, not private feuds
  6. Practice saying things briefly and precisely to reduce room for twisting

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Answering gossip with emotion and inflating small matters

Mistake 2: Relying on spoken agreements with nothing written and no evidence

Mistake 3: Blaming every setback on bad actors without examining how things were said

Back to Your Own Chart

A Zhu Que year teaches making words bear repeating. Keep speech short, keep records complete and hand disputes to proper channels, and rumor struggles to take hold.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the year when Zhu Que appears before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the year when Zhu Que appears line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
