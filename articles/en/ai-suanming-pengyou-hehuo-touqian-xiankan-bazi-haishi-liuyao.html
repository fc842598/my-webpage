A question like "问朋友合伙投钱做项目，先看八字还是六爻" is easy to turn into "which system is more accurate." The better first move is to ask whether you are judging 长期财务承受 or 这次资金动作. Without that split, the tools only appear to conflict.

八字 and 六爻 are not strongest on the same layer. Once you separate long structure from the one-event window, the division of labor becomes much cleaner.

Separate which layer this question belongs to first

Separate the layers first: 八字 is stronger on 长期财务承受, while 六爻 is stronger on 这次资金动作.

When the layers collapse into one question, people often mistake a short event window for full long-term fit, or the other way around.

The center of gravity changes with the tool

When investing with friends, if the urgent question is whether your current stage can actually carry volatility, loss, and pressure, Ba Zi usually serves the long endurance layer better first.

If the long endurance line is already acceptable and the real question is whether this one project deserves the money now, or whether payback timing will damage the friendship, Liu Yao is usually more direct for the move itself.

Do not use the wrong tool for the wrong layer

Do not turn 八字 and 六爻 into a slogan-level rivalry. They often answer different parts of the same decision.

And do not let one event window replace the long-term reading, or let the long-term reading replace the event window.

A steadier starting order

  1. Decide whether 长期财务承受 or 这次资金动作 is the urgent layer.
  2. Match 八字 and 六爻 to the right layer.
  3. Then bring both readings back into one decision.
