Qi Sha and Po Jun in Aspect: Ordering the Pace of Change

How to separate the pioneering force of Qi Sha from the rebuilding force of Po Jun during a period of change.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Qi Sha and Po Jun are both strong change stars, often linked with Tan Lang in an pioneering structure. Yet they work differently. Qi Sha decides and charges forward, while Po Jun breaks the old structure and rebuilds it.

How the two forces work

Qi Sha is like the vanguard that opens the situation and takes risk. Po Jun is the reformer that removes the old arrangement and redistributes resources. One moves first, the other rebuilds after.

Judgment steps

  1. Confirm whether Qi Sha and Po Jun share a palace or meet through the three-way positions.
  2. Mark Qi Sha's decisions and Po Jun's rebuilding separately.
  3. Look for steady forces such as Lu Cun or Tian Fu to judge whether the change has backing.
  4. Check transformations and difficult stars for resources, responsibility, and blockage.
  5. Match the decide-first, rebuild-later order to the real plan before concluding.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then confirm the star relationship.
  2. Record the two roles separately rather than using one label.
  3. Check steady forces, transformations, and difficult stars for resilience.
  4. Add the current cycle to see whether it triggers pioneering or rebuilding.
  5. Write the answer as decisions plus rebuilding pace, not one volatility label.
