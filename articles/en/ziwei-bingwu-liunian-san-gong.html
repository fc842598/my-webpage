Which Three Palaces to Read First in a Bing Wu Year

A practical way to read a Bing Wu annual cycle by separating personal direction, career responsibility, and money flow.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:30

Three Jobs, Not One Forecast

Start with Life for personal direction, Career for duties and position, and Wealth for the actual money path.

Annual Transformations Are Entry Points

The Bing transformations need the ten-year background, the three supporting palaces, and the annual landing palace before they become a conclusion.

Two Patterns

Life Hua Lu, Career Hua Quan, and Wealth Hua Ke can describe visibility, responsibility, and credential-based income. Life Hua Ji with Wealth Hua Lu calls for narrower choices and cash reserves.

Practical Order

Read the person, the role, and the cash flow. Then decide whether to expand, renegotiate, or protect resources.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
