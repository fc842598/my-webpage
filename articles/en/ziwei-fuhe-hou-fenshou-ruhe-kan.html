What to Do About On-Off Cycles: Whether the Core Problem Was Solved

Breaking up again after reuniting usually means the core problem that caused the split was never truly solved. Calmly review the recurring conflict and whether both sides are willing to change, rather than relying on nostalgia; if the problem cannot be solved and the cycle keeps draining you, seriously consider letting go and save energy for a relationship that can move forward steadily.
