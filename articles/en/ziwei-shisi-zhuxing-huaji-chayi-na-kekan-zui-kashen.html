How Each Major Star's Hua Ji Differs in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Same Hook, Different Sticking Point

Hua Ji governs attachment, blockage and inward gathering, but the star decides where and how one is stuck: Wu Qu Ji is cash-flow and decisional trouble, Tai Yin Ji emotion and female-relative worry, Tian Ji Ji overthinking, Ju Men Ji verbal dispute, Wen Chang Ji document error. Hua Ji must be read with star nature to reveal the real lesson.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

Hua Ji is the action of sticking; the star sets the matter—Wu Qu stuck on money, Tian Ji on thoughts, Ju Men on words—so solutions differ entirely.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Every Ji gets a vague take-care; a Wu Qu Ji person regulating emotion or Tian Ji Ji fixing cash flow addresses the wrong thing. Distinguish star nature to solve the real lesson.

The Underlying Logic

Hua Ji governs gathering, attachment, sense of lack and sticking; the stuck point is set by the transformed star.

Wealth-star Ji: Wu Qu Ji cash-flow, decision and loss worry; Tai Yin Ji emotional drain, home/female-relative attachment.

Ingenuity-star Ji: Tian Ji Ji overthinking and plan loops; Tan Lang Ji frustrated desire and scattered breadth; Lian Zhen Ji emotional, relational or institutional trouble.

Speech/document-star Ji: Ju Men Ji disputes and blocked communication; Chang/Qu Ji document, exam and contract errors—each needs different prevention by star nature.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find which major star Hua Ji lands on
  2. Classify the sticking point: money, emotion, thought, speech or documents
  3. Wu Qu Ji: secure cash flow and contracts, don't white-knuckle turnover
  4. Tian Ji Ji: stop rumination, set decision deadlines
  5. Ju Men/Chang-Qu Ji: document communication, double-check paperwork
  6. Combine the palace to target the lesson, not vague worry

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Dismissing every Ji with vague caution

Mistake 2: Ignoring star nature, fixing the wrong thing

Mistake 3: Treating different stars' Ji as one misfortune

Back to Your Own Chart

Hua Ji is not scary; not seeing where it sticks is. Wu Qu's Ji is money, Tian Ji's thoughts, Ju Men's words—recognize your transformed star's lesson and the block becomes a concrete, solvable problem.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to major stars Hua Ji differences before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the major stars Hua Ji differences line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
