Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Career Palace: Daring Hard Battles and Turning Drive Into a Career

Wu Qu is pragmatic and execution-oriented while Qi Sha is courageous and tackles hard problems. Together in the Career Palace they bring strong drive, resolve and the ability to carry difficult work, suiting high-pressure sales, engineering, law, enforcement or entrepreneurship. The risk is head-on clashes, risk and solitude, so the key is courage with a plan, the right direction and a fallback.

People with Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Career Palace rise to difficult work, willing to decide and answer for it, often leading from the front; constant head-on pressure can pile up both risk and physical cost.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the career style with Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Career Palace. Understand it reads how one works, then see whether execution is dragged by risk and malefics.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Career Palace gives pragmatism, results and strong execution, with rules and persistence.

Qi Sha there gives courage, independence and the ability to tackle hard problems, suiting pioneering and high-challenge roles.

Together they are doubly firm: career drive is strong, and with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars one builds a position through hard work.

With malefics and Ji, risk, head-on clashes and little room to maneuver follow, so cooperation and health need active care.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Qi Sha share the Career Palace and check their brightness
  2. List the hard tasks and opportunities, sorting what matters
  3. Weigh risk and fallback before acting rather than charging on impulse
  4. Focus drive on one or two main directions rather than opening fire everywhere
  5. Actively build cooperation and team to offset the stars’ solitude
  6. With the decade and year, find the years to pioneer and the years to hold

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Qi Sha in the Career Palace only as daring enough to win while ignoring risk

Mistake 2: Meeting every hard fight head-on and mistaking brute force for ability

Mistake 3: Pushing career alone while ignoring cooperation, fallback and health
