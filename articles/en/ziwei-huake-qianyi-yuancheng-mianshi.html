Hua Ke in the Travel Palace During a Remote Job Interview

Separate public reputation from tested job skills in a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30 09:05

Praise for a portfolio does not prove readiness for a specific job. Hua Ke in the Travel Palace can frame how other people recognize your work, while the Career Palace asks what the role requires. Compare public feedback with a practical test and the job description. A widely shared essay and a completed timed assignment are different evidence. Use the chart to organize the questions, then act on actual interview feedback.

For a remote role, separate reputation from delivery. A public article may earn praise while a timed task exposes a missing skill. A quieter candidate may have stronger project evidence. The Travel Palace frames external visibility, the Career Palace the work itself, and the Wealth Palace the proposed pay.
