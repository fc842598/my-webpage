Sub-Health and Chronic Fatigue in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Energy Management Starts With Health and Karma

Energy management is really energy, not time. Read Health's physical capital, Karma's mental drain, Life's energy type and Property's recovery environment: Tian Ji/Tai Yin overthink and drain the nervous system, Huo/Ling/Qing Yang push through into overdraft, Tian Tong/Tian Liang recover slowly but steadily. When a decade/year repeatedly activates Health/Karma, fatigue shifts from cured-by-sleep to chronic depletion requiring type-specific replenishment rather than grit. This covers tendencies and routine only, not medical diagnosis.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-15

Some thrive on six hours, some sleep nine and stay tired, some fatigue is physical and some mental; the factory energy setting differs, so management should differ too.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why you are always tired, why rest does not restore you, or how to manage energy, first separate physical baseline, mental drain and recovery environment, then adjust accordingly, and seek medical care if it persists.

The Underlying Logic

Physical capital reads Health: its star flags the system that alarms first—Sun leans cardiovascular and eyes, Tian Ji nerves and sleep, Zi Wei digestion, Tan Lang liver/gallbladder and metabolism. A malefic in Health does not destine illness but shows which system reports stress first, and energy management should protect that weak point first.

Energy type reads Life: Sha-Po-Lang/Wu Qu/Qing Yang are sprinters who burst at high intensity but must stop deliberately; Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang/Tai Yin are steady-stream types who cannot take long high-pressure noisy stretches; Tian Tong/Tian Liang recover slowly and collapse first if forced onto others' nonstop schedule. Aligning routine to type is more realistic than one uniform early-to-bed rule.

Mental drain reads Karma: much bodily tiredness is really Karma drain—Tian Ji-Hua Ji whose mind never stops, Ju Men ruminating, Lian Zhen self-pressuring; such fatigue is not recovered by sleep but by cutting internal friction, meditation, solitude and genuine relaxation. Only when Karma is not empty can the body refill.

Recovery environment reads Property and the decade: Property governs home and rest quality; noisy, interrupted rest sharply cuts recovery. Phases activating Health/Karma are depletion-prone, so deliberately lower load, keep regular cardio and treat sleep as a hard metric. If fatigue comes with persistent symptoms, follow formal medical care; the chart is only a lifestyle reference.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Health stars to find the system that alarms first under stress and protect it
  2. Read Life energy type to tell sprinter, steady-stream or slow-recovery
  3. Read Karma to separate bodily fatigue from mental drain; the latter is fixed by cutting internal friction
  4. Read Property rest environment to rule out noise, interruption and sleep debt
  5. When decade/year activate Health/Karma, lower load and make sleep plus regular cardio hard metrics
  6. If it persists after adjustment or carries clear symptoms, seek formal medical care rather than diagnosing from the chart

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Copying others' routines while ignoring different Life energy types

Mistake 2: Treating Karma-drain fatigue as physical, which sleep alone cannot fix

Mistake 3: Pushing through a decade-activated depletion phase without cutting load until the body forces a shutdown
