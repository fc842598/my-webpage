Lu and Ji in the Property Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Timing Buying and Selling

Lu or Quan in the Property Palace means steady home luck, smooth purchases and holding good assets; Ji means property movement, possibly selling or moving, but also tangles in procedures, contracts or loans. Layer whether the decade reaches Property with Lu-Quan activation. This is not investment advice, and purchase contracts and loans rely on a qualified lawyer, proper channels and a written agreement.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some buy only by market price, but the Property Lu-Ji indicates whether this stage fits moving real estate; right timing and smooth procedures are steadier than forcing it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is when to buy or sell. Read Property transformations, see whether the decade activates it, and verify contracts, loans and title; Lu-Ji only gives tendency rather than deciding for you.

The Underlying Logic

Property Lu: steady home luck, smoother purchases and fitting listings, favoring holding.

Property Quan: strong control and active decisions, best with professional checks.

Property Ji: property movement or tangles in procedures, loans and contracts, favoring slowness and verification.

A decade reaching Property with Lu-Quan activation is often a buying window; contracts and loans rely on lawyers and writing, and this is not investment advice.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji in Property
  2. Then place decades to see whether Property is activated
  3. Lu-Quan with decade activation is often a buy-and-hold window
  4. With Ji, guard against movement and procedure-loan tangles
  5. Leave contracts, loans and title to qualified lawyers and writing
  6. Do not make property investment decisions from the chart; use proper institutions

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Property Lu as guaranteed gain and buying blindly

Mistake 2: Taking Ji as never buy, ignoring it may only mean slower procedures

Mistake 3: Buying without checking contracts and loans or consulting a lawyer, relying only on the year
