The Parents–Children–Friends Trine: Keep Each Person in the Right Role

Use the relationship trine in Zi Wei Dou Shu without swapping parents, children, students, and business partners to fit a story.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07 17:22

Parents, Children, and Friends belong to one natal trine. They can be examined together when several relationships participate in the same situation, but the people do not become interchangeable.

A hypothetical family business

A parent contributes experience and established customers, an adult child manages online sales, and a partner runs storage. If the question concerns parental help, the Parents Palace remains the starting subject. A favorable marker in Friends cannot simply be translated into a promise that the parent will provide cash.

If the question changes to the partner's reliability, the reading must change its starting point and reasoning. Replacing the name in an old conclusion is not enough.

Extensions need an explanation

Some traditional interpretations extend Children to students or other younger people in particular contexts. State the context rather than switching between a child and an employee whenever the story requires it. Family and employment relationships have different responsibilities.

Write down whose relationship and whose action you are discussing. Then compare the interpretation with a past event and the roles people actually performed.

The trine can help organize participants. It should not become a device for moving identities between boxes until every outcome appears to fit. Clear subjects make both chart study and ordinary conversations about family cooperation easier to follow.
