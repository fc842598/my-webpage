Wu Qu in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Hard-Minded Wealth Star and How to Read Its Income and Decisiveness

Wu Qu, a metal star and the primary wealth star, governs practical work, execution and money decisions. Those with it in the Life Palace act crisply and take on hard jobs; income tends to be earned through craft, skill, finance or industry rather than speculation. Its hardness reads as execution when used well, and as head-on friction when not.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

With Wu Qu in the Life Palace, talk of sentiment does not move him; mention that something can be delivered and billed and he perks up. He is not afraid of hard work; he is afraid of a matter dragged on without a clear word.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Wu Qu reads as two things: where the income comes from, and whether one dares to decide on money. Unlike Hua Lu, which is about opportunity, it is about earning by hand and deciding on the spot; follow the line of practical work, payment and decisiveness.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu is the primary wealth star, firm in nature, governing decisiveness and execution; those with it in the Life Palace often excel in practical, financial, uniformed or engineering fields.

Its wealth is earned through toil and craft, built by skill, seniority and execution, and is a different line from windfall or speculative money.

The bright side is acting on the spot without dragging; the dark side is refusing to bend and clashing head-on with relationships.

Its income path depends on co-palace and triple-direction stars: Lu Cun or Hua Lu means solid sources, while malefics and Hua Ji mean a bumpy pursuit; Wu Qu alone does not fix wealth.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm whether Wu Qu is in the Life Palace or a triple-direction, telling core temperament from a local trait
  2. See whether Lu Cun or Hua Lu sits with it to judge whether income sources are solid
  3. Then see whether fire, bell or blade malefics sit nearby to judge a bumpy pursuit
  4. Apply its firmness to decisions: on money, contracts and job moves, can it commit
  5. See whether decade or yearly transformations activate Wu Qu, and whether the period opens income or defends it
  6. Use its execution to close gaps: once decided, land it rather than circling on feelings

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring wealth at the sight of Wu Qu without checking solid sources or malefics and Hua Ji

Mistake 2: Treating its hardness as pure virtue while ignoring head-on costs in relationships

Mistake 3: Mistaking Wu Qu for a windfall or speculative star and chasing trends

Back to Your Own Chart

Wu Qu is a hard-minded, practical wealth star: income through craft and execution, decisiveness through daring to commit. Point that hardness at work and it is a blade; point it at arguing with people and it becomes head-on friction.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Wu Qu star, income sources and decisiveness before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Wu Qu star, income sources and decisiveness line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
