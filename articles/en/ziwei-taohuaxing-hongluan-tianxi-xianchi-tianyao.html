How to Read the Romance Stars: Hong Luan, Tian Xi, Xian Chi, Tian Yao, Healthy Romance vs. Trouble

The romance stars in Zi Wei include Hong Luan, Tian Xi, Xian Chi, Tian Yao and Mu Yu: Hong Luan and Tian Xi mostly indicate proper romance, marriage and happy occasions, while Xian Chi and Tian Yao lean toward charm, desire and social attraction. The romance stars are not inherently bad; the key is whether malefics share the palace to create romance trouble, and whether emptiness stars or Tian De resolve it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28

Romance stars describe attraction and affinity; handled well they give good relations and healthy romance, and only with uncontrolled malefics become trouble.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see romance stars and assume promiscuity or bad romance, while others fix a wedding date from Hong Luan and Tian Xi alone, neither reading the full nature and mix.

The Underlying Logic

Hong Luan and Tian Xi are proper romance, mostly indicating affinity, marriage, happy occasions and new children, and when activated by the year often make a good time for love and marriage.

Xian Chi, Tian Yao and Mu Yu lean toward charm, desire and social attraction, with a lively nature, and with malefics easily bring disputes and unhealthy romance.

Romance stars are dangerous only with malefics in the same palace; for example Qing Yang with Hong Luan is warned in old texts for injury and emotional conflict, the source of triangles and entanglement.

If Xian Chi and similar stars share the palace with emptiness stars such as Di Kong, Jie Kong, Xun Kong or Tian De, the negative effects of romance can be resolved, and the mix is the key.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First tell proper romance (Hong Luan, Tian Xi) from lively romance (Xian Chi, Tian Yao)
  2. See when the year and limits activate it to judge the window of affinity
  3. Check for malefics in the same palace to guard against trouble and triangles
  4. See whether emptiness stars or Tian De resolve the negative effects
  5. Use attraction for proper romance and good relations rather than promiscuity
  6. Treat marriage on real relating and commitment, not the stars alone

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking romance stars as promiscuity or bad romance at first sight

Mistake 2: Fixing a wedding date from Hong Luan and Tian Xi alone

Mistake 3: Ignoring the danger of romance with malefics and falling into entanglement
