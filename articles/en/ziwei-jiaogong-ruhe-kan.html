How to Read the Flanking Palaces in a Zi Wei Chart

The flanking palaces are the two adjacent palaces, such as Parents and Siblings around the Life Palace. Auspicious flanks bring close support, malefic flanks bring close pressure; flanks read the nearest external forces.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Flanking palaces are like two people standing on either side; whether they help or trip you depends on what those palaces hold.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some read the trines but ignore the flanks, missing close forces; though not in the trine, flanks are the nearest external influence.

The Underlying Logic

Flanks = the two adjacent palaces; around the Life Palace they are Parents and Siblings.

Auspicious flanks (Lu Quan Ke, Zuo You Kui Yue) bring close support.

Malefic flanks (Yang Tuo Huo Ling, emptiness) bring close pressure.

Read flanks with the triple-direction; close force plus external supply is complete.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate the home palace and find the two adjacent palaces
  2. Read whether the flanks hold auspicious or malefic stars
  3. Auspicious flank: judge which area the close support comes from
  4. Malefic flank: judge which area the close pressure comes from
  5. Read flank transformations to set the energy nature of the close force
  6. Read with the triple-direction to set the overall situation of the area

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the trines without flanks, ignoring close force

Mistake 2: Confusing flanks with trines, unable to distinguish geometry

Mistake 3: Seeing auspicious flanks and relaxing, ignoring malefic flanks

Back to Your Own Chart

Flanking palaces read the nearest external forces. Distinguish auspicious from malefic flanks and combine with the trines, and you know whether what is close is support or pressure.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the flanking palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the flanking palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
