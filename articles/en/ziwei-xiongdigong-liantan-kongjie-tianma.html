Why the Peer Line Feels Unstable

The Siblings Palace includes siblings, peers, classmates, equal partners, and people involved in shared benefits. Weakened Lian Zhen and Tan Lang can make this circle lively but inconsistent. People may connect quickly through shared interests, yet calculate their own advantage when responsibility or money must be divided.

This does not mean every friendship fails. It means charm and enthusiasm should not be mistaken for long-term reliability. Observe what happens after deadlines, costs, and accountability enter the relationship.

Practical Cooperation Rules

Keep ownership, payment dates, decision rights, and withdrawal terms explicit. Test a new partnership with a limited project before combining major resources. For family money, distinguish a gift from a loan and document whichever arrangement is chosen.

A strong Friends and Servants Palace can provide a more stable professional network, while a strong Career Palace favors clearly ranked organizations. A supportive Travel Palace may also show that opportunities improve when the person builds connections beyond the original social circle.
