Hua Ji in the Spouse Palace During a Stable Relationship

Look for a specific communication burden before drawing conclusions.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-31 09:14

Hua Ji in the Spouse Palace is not a breakup forecast. A stable couple may repeatedly negotiate caregiving schedules, while another struggles with shared expenses. Identify whether the transformation is natal, decade-based, or annual. Record the actual issue and any agreement reached; do not let one symbol erase years of observed behavior.

A stable couple can have a difficult budget discussion without heading toward separation. Locate the exact palace and time layer, then identify the real issue under discussion.
