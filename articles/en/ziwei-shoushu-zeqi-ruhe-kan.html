How to Approach Scheduling Surgery

A chart can hint at the timing and tendency of health thresholds, but surgery scheduling must follow the judgment of proper hospitals and professional doctors. Do not delay necessary treatment for astrology; scheduling only means choosing a relatively stable time within what the doctor allows.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

In scheduling surgery, astrology is a nice-to-have reference, while the doctor judgment is the unshakable backbone; the order cannot be reversed.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some delay surgery waiting for a good day, while others schedule arbitrarily with no regard for timing; the correct approach is to listen to the doctor first, then reference astrological rhythm within what is allowed.

The Underlying Logic

The Health Palace and decade/year charts can hint at health threshold tendencies and timing.

But astrological tendency is not a diagnosis and cannot replace medical examination or doctor judgment.

For elective surgery, within what the doctor allows, one may avoid years or months heavy with Hua Ji or malefics.

Emergency and time-sensitive surgery must follow doctor orders, never delayed for any astrological reason.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm the surgery type: emergency means immediate care, no scheduling consideration
  2. For elective surgery, first hear the doctor recommended time window
  3. Within what the doctor allows, reference health tendencies in the year and month
  4. Avoid time periods heavy with Hua Ji, malefics or clashing the Health Palace
  5. After choosing, confirm with the doctor, medical arrangement is the final decision
  6. Follow doctor orders for post-surgery recovery, astrology is only a supporting reference

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Delaying necessary surgery waiting for a good day, endangering health

Mistake 2: Treating astrological tendency as diagnosis and skipping proper examination

Mistake 3: Ignoring doctor orders entirely and scheduling only by astrological timing
