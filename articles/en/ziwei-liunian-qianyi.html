An Annual Cycle in the Travel Palace

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-01 08:43

When the annual Life Palace enters the Travel Palace, the outside world becomes the year's main stage. Movement may mean relocation or travel, but it can also mean new clients, a different platform, a public-facing assignment, or a change in the environment around work and study.

Travel is broader than changing address

For an employee, the year may bring external projects, a transfer, or more customer contact. For a business owner, it may activate a new market or platform. For a student, it may involve graduation, exchange, or leaving a familiar institution. The stars in Travel show whether movement feels supported, pressured, profitable, or mainly exhausting.

Money and career decide what movement produces

  • An annual Travel Palace with Hua Lu, supported by a sound Wealth Palace, may bring outside clients or a new market that produces collectible income. Tian Ma can intensify the movement but does not by itself prove profit.
  • Annual Travel with Hua Quan in the Career Palace may describe assignment abroad, cross-team leadership, or representing an organization externally. If Hua Ji strikes Wealth, travel and operating costs may rise faster than the return.

Move with a defined purpose

Ask what the change is meant to improve: role, customer access, education, or quality of life. Price the move, clarify authority, and test the external platform before abandoning the current one. Malefic pressure does not always mean staying still; it can mean allowing more time, insurance, documentation, and contingency budget.

A clear chart-reading order

First confirm the annual Life Palace in Travel and assess its stars. Second read the opposite Life Palace for personal capacity. Third inspect Wealth for financial return and Career for the source of movement, including Hua Lu, Hua Quan, Hua Ji, and Tian Ma. Finally compare the natal chart and ten-year cycle before deciding whether to travel, relocate, expand, or simply change platforms.
