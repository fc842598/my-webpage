What Is the Qi-Number Position in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Career-of-Career and Why It Shows How Long Something Lasts

A palace's qi-number position is nine steps from it (its own Career position) and reads that matter's staying power and resilience. The home palace shows the present; the qi-number shows staying power—a strong home with a hollow qi-number often shines briefly but cannot last.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09

To judge whether something lasts, the home palace alone misleads with surface state; finding its qi-number shows how long the fire burns and whether it collapses at the first wave.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether a job, relationship or business lasts and has staying power means reading not only its palace but also the position nine steps away, its qi-number.

The Underlying Logic

The palaces have fixed rotation: from any palace, the ninth is its Career position, the qi-number; Life's is Career, Career's is Wealth, and so on.

The home palace shows current state and performance; the qi-number shows structure, foundation and staying power—steady stars with Lu/Quan/Ke mean endurance and anti-cycle strength, while Emptiness/Harvest/malefics/Ji mean a thin base that cannot bear消耗.

For career, a strong Life or Career only shows present ability; if Career's qi-number (Wealth) is stressed, the work is busy but unprofitable, expanding fast on cash flow that cannot hold—unsustainable.

It applies to any palace: marriage longevity reads Spouse's ninth (Wealth, the shared material base), whether money is kept reads Wealth's qi-number—the method is constant: home for now, qi-number for lifespan.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Lock the palace whose staying power you are judging as home
  2. Count nine palaces along the sequence from it to find the qi-number
  3. Read the home stars to set the matter's present state and strength
  4. Read the qi-number stars and transformations to set foundation, structure and endurance
  5. Compare: strong home with steady qi-number lasts; strong home with hollow qi-number shines but fades
  6. Layer the decade: when the qi-number is activated or stressed in a phase, staying power is being tested

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring longevity from the home palace alone, fooled by a temporary high

Mistake 2: Not knowing how to count the qi-number and staying trapped inside each palace

Mistake 3: Confusing the qi-number with the triple-direction; it is a ninth-position staying-power view, not a trine

Back to Your Own Chart

The qi-number gives a lifespan lens: even the brightest present must pass the staying-power test. Count to the ninth for what matters to you, and you see earlier whether it is a marathon or a sprint.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the qi-number position for a palace's staying power before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the qi-number position for a palace's staying power line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
