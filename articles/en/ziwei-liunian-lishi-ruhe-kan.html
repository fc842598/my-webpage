When Li Shi Appears: Execution and Support That Carry Your Plans Through

Li Shi rises with Tai Sui, governing execution, support and carrying plans through; your ability to get things done is strong this year. Direct that energy at a settled direction and guard against overexertion and doing everything yourself.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

Li Shi is like the executor beside Tai Sui; this year there is no shortage of strength, only the question of where and at what pace to use it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some work exceptionally hard this year but spread effort everywhere and do every task themselves, ending exhausted and unappreciated; the issue is not low effort but execution not aimed at the main line.

The Underlying Logic

Li Shi governs execution and support, showing strong hands-on ability, fast progress and someone who carries out concrete tasks beside you.

It rises with Tai Sui, so the direction must be set first; with a clear direction it guarantees follow-through, while a scattered one means effort pulls you off course.

Guard against overexertion, including doing everything yourself, an overfull pace and refusing to share work.

It governs only one year of execution; the long-term pattern still rests on the natal chart and decade.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate which palace Li Shi falls in to see which affairs can be completed this year
  2. Confirm direction and goals before acting to avoid spreading effort
  3. Sequence tasks and put energy first into the key steps on the main line
  4. Delegate what can be shared rather than doing everything yourself
  5. Leave room in the pace to avoid starting at full tilt and losing momentum
  6. After each stage, review whether effort drifted and recalibrate

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Starting everywhere just because you can work hard and scattering energy

Mistake 2: Doing everything at a full pace and exhausting yourself and those around you

Mistake 3: Taking one strong execution year as a long-term pattern without the natal chart and decade

Back to Your Own Chart

A Li Shi year teaches turning strength into results. Set the direction, rank priorities and be willing to delegate, and the year execution truly lands where you want it.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the year when Li Shi appears before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the year when Li Shi appears line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
