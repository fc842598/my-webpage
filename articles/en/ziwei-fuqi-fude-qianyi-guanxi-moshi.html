How to Read the Spouse, Karma and Travel Palaces Together for a Relationship Pattern

The Spouse Palace writes how you relate, Karma your inner emotional needs, and Travel the external scenes of relating. Reading the three shows how a relationship actually operates.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Looking only at the Spouse Palace is like seeing two people stand together. What each expects inside and in which scenes things go wrong lie in Karma and Travel; the three connected form the full relationship.

What This Question Is Really Asking

After a fight, which palace do you check, the most practical question. The conflict itself is in Spouse, but why you fight, whether expectation gap or scene pressure, returns to Karma and Travel, separated to be clear.

The Underlying Logic

The Spouse Palace writes the surface pattern of relating and attraction.

The Karma Palace writes inner emotional needs and how they are met.

The Travel Palace writes the external environment, such as distance, social life and public relating.

The three linked explain how the relationship operates; one palace gives only a fragment.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First set the Spouse Palace star for the relating pattern
  2. Then set the Karma Palace star for your inner emotional needs
  3. Then set the Travel Palace star for external relating scenes
  4. Compare against the three cases of cohabiting, long distance and public relationship
  5. Find the gaps among the three to identify whether conflict is expectation or scene
  6. Use the decade to see what topic the relationship faces in each stage

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fixating on Spouse and ignoring inner needs and external scenes

Mistake 2: Generalizing all conflict without separating expectation from scene pressure

Mistake 3: Not connecting the three and missing how the relationship operates

Back to Your Own Chart

A relationship is two people interacting across inner expectations and real scenes. Learn to read the three palaces together and you understand why you get along and fight rather than fixing on a compatible label.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the relationship pattern across Spouse, Karma and Travel before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the relationship pattern across Spouse, Karma and Travel line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
