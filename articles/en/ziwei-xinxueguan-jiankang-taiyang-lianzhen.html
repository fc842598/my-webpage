Tai Yang is fire element, governs heart, blood circulation, and eyes; Lian Zhen is fire element, governs blood, inflammation, and cardiovascular system. Especially after middle age, need to pay special attention to blood pressure, blood lipids, and heart health. The chart indicates tendency; specifics follow medical examinations.
