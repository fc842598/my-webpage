How to Root Decade Transformations in the Natal Chart in Zi Wei Dou Shu

When you see a decade or year transformation, look back to where the same kind of transformation sits in the natal chart; this is rooting in the natal. The natal is the root and the limit the branch; find the root line first to know where the decade opportunity or lesson comes from rather than judging the surface.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some rejoice at a decade Lu and tense at a decade Ji without looking where the force is rooted, reading only the phenomenon and not the full line.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is why limit transformations point backward. The limit activates and the natal sets the baseline; matching the same kind finds the root, while a different kind shows which line it pulls.

The Underlying Logic

Rooting: a decade Lu seeks the natal Lu and a decade Ji seeks the natal Ji; that palace line is the root.

The natal is the substance, the decade and year are the function; the function never leaves the substance.

If a limit transformation has no natal match, see which palace it flies into and which domain it pulls.

Once the root is found, judge whether the decade strengthens along the line or opens a lesson on another line.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First place the decade transformations
  2. For each, find where the same natal transformation sits
  3. Connect the root palace and limit palace into a line
  4. Judge whether the decade strengthens along the line or opens another lesson
  5. If no match, see which palace the flight pulls
  6. Combine major stars and palaces before concluding

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only limit transformations without rooting them

Mistake 2: Separating natal and limit and missing the root

Mistake 3: Forcing the rooting and drawing random lines when no match exists

Back to Your Own Chart

Limit transformations are branches and natal transformations the root. Root them first to find the full line, then see how the decade activates, so your reading has a source rather than stopping at surface fortune.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to rooting decade transformations in the natal transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the rooting decade transformations in the natal transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
