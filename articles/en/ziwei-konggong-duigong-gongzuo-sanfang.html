Empty Life Palace and Work: Why the Opposite Palace Is Only One Part of the Chart

Use the opposite palace and supporting palaces to frame career questions in Zi Wei Dou Shu.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:15

An empty Life Palace does not mean a person has no career pattern. The opposite Travel Palace can show how outside contacts respond, but its stars should not simply be copied into the Life Palace as a complete verdict.

Follow the palace relationships

Check supporting stars and transformations in the Life Palace, then the opposite palace and its triad. If Tian Ji in Travel corresponds to changing client requests, the Career Palace still asks whether the person can deliver, and the Wealth Palace asks when money arrives. A remote consultant may receive many inquiries yet lack the resources to complete the work.

Test the reading

Track inquiries, signed work, delivery time, and net receipts separately. Read the natal pattern before decade and annual timing. Neither an empty palace nor a favorable opposite star guarantees a promotion.
