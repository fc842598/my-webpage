Does the Life Palace Change Every Year in Zi Wei Dou Shu?

Separate the fixed natal Life Palace from the moving annual Life Palace and keep transformation labels in the correct layer.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07 17:21

Two boxes labeled Life Palace do not necessarily mean a chart is wrong. One label may belong to the natal chart and another to the selected annual chart.

A fixed chart with another set of labels

The natal Life Palace stays in place when the birth details and calculation method remain unchanged. The twelve earthly-branch positions also stay fixed. Annual palace names are arranged separately according to the selected year.

Under the commonly used annual-branch method, a Horse year places the annual Life Palace at the Horse position. That position might be the Wealth Palace in your natal chart. Both names can therefore describe the same box at different levels.

Transformations also need a time label

A star displaying an annual difficulty transformation does not necessarily carry that transformation in the birth chart. A natal recognition marker and an annual authority marker can appear beside the same star without one deleting the other.

When reading, use a complete sentence: the annual Life Palace for this year overlaps this natal palace. Then examine the relevant annual subject palace and its relationship to natal and ten-year conditions. An annual Life Palace overlapping natal Wealth does not establish that every event that year concerns money. On YuetianAI, identify the layer before interpreting the label.
