Getting Along With Coworkers in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Siblings Palace Holds Peer Relations

Coworker relations are read mainly through Siblings, the palace of peers, together with Friends for team atmosphere, Career for the work environment and Travel for cross-team collaboration. Siblings stars set the baseline with peers; clustered malefics/Ji bring office friction; Travel decides how smoothly cross-team work moves. Reading this is not about pleasing everyone but knowing whom you click with, where to hold a boundary, and how to push across departments.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-15

Some befriend coworkers in any department, some are drained by peers everywhere, some are warm internally yet stuck the moment work crosses teams; these sit in different palaces.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why coworker relations go badly, cross-team work stalls or office politics tire you, first separate peer relations, team atmosphere and external collaboration.

The Underlying Logic

Peer relations read Siblings: its stars set the baseline with peers—Tian Tong/Tian Xiang/Tai Yin are gentle and collaborative, Zi Wei/Wu Qu/Qi Sha hold firm positions and compete for dominance among peers; Siblings with Lu Cun/Lu brings peer help and shared information; with Qing Yang/Tuo Luo/Ji it brings hidden rivalry, buck-passing and disputes, so set collaboration rules and division of labor in advance.

Team atmosphere reads Friends, the wider team and network; clustered auspicious stars mean a lifting team with support, heavy malefics/Ji mean credit-grabbing, cliques and frequent turnover. When Friends is stressed, stay out of factions and private gossip; keeping relations professional is safest.

Work environment reads Career: beyond career it describes your daily workplace ecology; smooth Career usually means a team with clear rules, Career with malefics/Ji an internally draining environment—not poor social skills but a draining setting, where changing platform may be the real fix.

Cross-team collaboration reads Travel: working across departments, outward, with unfamiliar people is Travel in essence. Auspicious Travel naturally borrows outside resources and advances collaboration; stressed Travel hits walls cross-team and should push through written process, shared-upward goals and formal meetings rather than personal ties alone.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Siblings to confirm your peer-relations baseline and friction points
  2. Read Friends for team atmosphere; with heavy malefics, avoid factions and keep professional distance
  3. Read Career to tell whether relational fatigue is personal or a draining environment
  4. Focus on Travel for cross-team work and assess outward momentum
  5. If Travel is stressed, push via written process and shared goals, not personal ties alone
  6. Layer coworkers into close, collaborate-only and boundary-needed groups and treat each differently

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Attributing all workplace relations to Friends while missing that Siblings governs peers

Mistake 2: Forcing closeness in a malefic-heavy team and getting hurt by factions

Mistake 3: Relying only on personal ties cross-team while Travel is stressed and skipping formal process, so work stalls
