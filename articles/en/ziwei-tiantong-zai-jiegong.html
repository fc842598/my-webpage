Tian Tong in the Health Palace: Water Metabolism, Urinary and Sugar

Tian Tong belongs to water, and in the Health Palace it often points to the urinary system, kidneys and bladder, water-dampness and sugar metabolism. The constitution tends to be deficient, prone to swelling and fatigue; exercise moderately, cut sweets and cold, avoid long sitting and check metabolic markers regularly.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-11

Water stays clear only when moving; blocked, it breeds dampness and swelling; people too must move for water to flow.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some treat swelling and fatigue as deficiency and tonify hard, worsening dampness; stars point direction, while urinary and metabolic ills rely on tests.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Tong is water, focus on urinary, kidneys and water metabolism.

The constitution tends deficient, prone to swelling and fatigue.

Exercise moderately, cut sweets and cold, avoid long sitting.

This is tendency not diagnosis; rely on formal medical care.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Exercise moderately to promote fluid metabolism
  2. Cut sweets and cold drinks, eat lightly
  3. Avoid long sitting, keep regular hours
  4. Seek care for swelling or abnormal urine
  5. Watch blood sugar and kidney function
  6. Follow the doctor assessment

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating all swelling and fatigue as deficiency to tonify

Mistake 2: Long sitting with no restraint in sweets and cold

Mistake 3: Not checking urinary abnormalities

Back to Your Own Chart
