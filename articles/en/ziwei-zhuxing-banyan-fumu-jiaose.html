Learn Zi Wei/Major Stars

How Each Major Star Parents: Different Teaching Styles, Keeping Personality Shortcomings Out of the Parent-Child Bond

Once a person becomes a parent, the Life Palace star’s traits naturally enter parenting: Zi Wei easily values authority and rules, the Sun easily values performance and public recognition, Tai Yin easily values care and attentiveness, Qi Sha easily values results and efficiency. Every style has benefits, but each also easily projects its own shortcomings onto the child: too strict, too doting, too urgent, too controlling. The key is first recognizing your own parenting style, then distinguishing whether this is for the child’s good or your own anxiety and habit, giving the child space suited to their nature rather than shaping them into another version of yourself.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-03

Many parents love their children, but the way they love carries their own personality habits: the urgent rush the child, the meticulous do everything for the child, the driven push the child to excel. Seeing this layer separates love from control.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to read your own parenting style and common pitfalls from the major star. First read the Life Palace star for the core style, then the Children Palace for expectations and interaction patterns.

The Underlying Logic

Zi Wei and Tian Fu parents value rules, resources and order, giving the child a stable framework, but risk being too authoritative and not listening to the child, needing to practice stepping down.

Sun and Lian Zhen parents value performance, sociability and public image, encouraging the child outward, but risk caring too much about outside judgment and pressuring the child, needing to practice accepting the child’s ordinariness.

Tai Yin and Tian Tong parents value care, feelings and daily details, offering warmth and attentiveness, but risk doing everything and spoiling, leaving the child unindependent, needing to practice letting go.

Qi Sha, Po Jun and Wu Qu parents value results, efficiency and toughness, building the child’s resilience, but risk being too urgent and hard and ignoring feelings, needing patience and softness; Tian Liang parents value principles and lecturing, needing to practice doing more and saying less.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read your Life Palace star to confirm your core parenting style and common mistakes
  2. Then read the Children Palace to understand your expectations and interaction patterns with the child
  3. Recall how your own parents raised you, distinguishing what you agree with from what you are repeating from the previous generation
  4. In concrete matters, ask yourself whether this is for the child’s good or satisfying your own anxiety and face
  5. Give the child space according to their nature and rhythm, not shaping them into what you want
  6. Regularly review your parenting with your partner or someone trusted, as outside reminders come easier than self-detection

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming that doing it for the child’s good means no mistakes, treating personality shortcomings as love

Mistake 2: Reading only your own parenting habits without the child’s nature and feedback

Mistake 3: Unconsciously repeating the previous generation’s problems on the next and calling it normal

Back to Your Own Chart

Parenting is a practice of re-recognizing yourself. Seeing the parenting style your major star brings, separating love from anxiety and giving the child space suited to them means what you offer is support rather than shaping.
