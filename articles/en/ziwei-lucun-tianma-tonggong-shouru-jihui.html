Lu Cun and Tian Ma Together: Stable Income Meets Movement

How to combine the stability of Lu Cun with the moving opportunities of Tian Ma in a career or income reading.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Lu Cun represents stability and retained wealth, while Tian Ma represents movement and outward development. Together they are often called the Lu-Ma interaction, where resources are linked with travel and change.

Opposite but complementary

Lu Cun is a steady treasury that keeps resources and values certainty. Tian Ma is a running horse that favors outside movement and cross-regional growth. Instead of cancelling each other, they let stable resources arise through movement.

Judgment steps

  1. Confirm the palace of Lu Cun and Tian Ma and whether the question is income or opportunity.
  2. Mark Lu Cun's steady accumulation separately from Tian Ma's movement.
  3. Check for Lu and a strong host star to see whether resources can come through movement.
  4. Read difficult stars to judge whether travel brings gain or just busy effort.
  5. Compare with real travel, relocation, or mobile-work arrangements before concluding.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then confirm the shared palace.
  2. Record the two roles separately rather than using one label.
  3. Check Lu, the host star, and difficult stars for balance.
  4. Add the current cycle to see whether it is a stable or moving period.
  5. Write the answer as stability plus movement, not one pattern name.
