Unknown Birth Hour in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Build an Event Timeline for Candidate Charts

Use dated life records to compare candidate Chinese astrology charts without forcing a birth hour.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:15

If a family remembers only “some time in the morning,” several Zi Wei Dou Shu charts may be possible. Write down the uncertainty first. Collect dated records of schooling, moving, and career changes independently of any chart reading.

Compare candidates fairly

Keep the same birth date and place, then list the Life, Body, Travel, and Career Palaces for each possible hour. Compare natal structure, decade cycle, and annual events separately. A lease dated in a moving year can be useful evidence; a vague personality description is much weaker. If two charts fit the same event, the event has not selected one of them.

Preserve uncertainty

Use the original documents before interpretations. Mark conclusions that change with birth hour as low confidence. A single retrospective match does not prove the exact minute, and it should not turn a candidate chart into a certainty.
