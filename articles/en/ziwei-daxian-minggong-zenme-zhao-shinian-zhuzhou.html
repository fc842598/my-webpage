How to Find the Decade Life Palace and Set a Ten-Year Theme in Zi Wei Dou Shu

The decade starts at an age set by the Life palace's five-element phase, advancing clockwise for yang-male/yin-female and counterclockwise otherwise, ten years per palace; the palace reached is the decade Life palace. To set the ten-year theme, treat it as a new taiji, re-lay the twelve palaces, and read its major star, transformations and trine to judge the decade's theme, chances and lessons.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

The natal chart is the whole script, a decade one act: finding the decade Life palace shows which domain the stage is set on and what theme the spotlight hits for ten years.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People confuse decade with year and read ten years from one-year stars; others read only the arrived star without re-laying the decade twelve, missing the decade's overall work, relationship and wealth direction.

The Underlying Logic

Start age comes from the Life palace's five-element phase (water-2, wood-3, metal-4, earth-5, fire-6); yang-male/yin-female advance clockwise, others counterclockwise, ten years per palace.

The decade Life palace is the natal palace reached; use it as the new Life and re-lay the decade twelve (Siblings, Spouse, Wealth, etc.) in fixed order.

Set the theme: its major star and brilliance set tone; the decade stem's transformations (esp. Lu and Ji flying into natal palaces) set ten-year gain/loss direction.

Combine with natal: an auspicious decade over a strong natal rises; a malefic/Ji decade clashing a weak natal palace calls for conservation and lessons—never read one layer alone.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Determine the Life five-element phase, start age and direction
  2. By age find the natal palace the current decade reaches
  3. Use it as new taiji and re-lay the decade twelve palaces
  4. Read its major star and brilliance for the decade tone
  5. Derive the decade stem transformations, seeing where Lu/Ji fly in the natal chart
  6. Combine natal and decade to state the ten-year theme, chances and lessons

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading ten years from yearly stars

Mistake 2: Reading only the arrived star without re-laying the twelve

Mistake 3: Reading the decade alone without the natal base

Back to Your Own Chart

A decade slices life into ten-year themes. Find the decade Life palace, re-lay the twelve around it, then read flying transformations—and you know clearly what to pursue, protect and learn these ten years.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to decade Life palace and ten-year theme before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the decade Life palace and ten-year theme line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
