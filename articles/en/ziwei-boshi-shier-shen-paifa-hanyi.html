The Twelve Doctor Spirits in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Twelve Annual Helpers Starting From Lu Cun

The twelve Doctor spirits start at Lu Cun and proceed clockwise for yang-male/yin-female or counterclockwise for yin-male/yang-female: Doctor, Strength, Green Dragon, Small Loss, General, Memorial, Flying, Joy, Illness, Great Loss, Ambush and Official. They never lead a verdict; they add or subtract on the major-star structure and hint at timing.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09

Many people panic at the swarm of mysterious minor spirits, but the twelve Doctors are light-weight helpers for detail. Remember they start at Lu Cun and their direction depends on gender and year stem, and you have the placement.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether Doctor, Green Dragon or Ambush matter and whether to heed them means first learning how they are placed and what each governs, then putting them back into the larger structure of major stars and transformations.

The Underlying Logic

Placement is fixed: find natal Lu Cun, then for yang-male/yin-female go clockwise and for yin-male/yang-female counterclockwise, one spirit per palace through all twelve; move Lu Cun and the whole set rotates.

Auspicious ones: Doctor governs wit, reputation and mood; Strength governs power (stronger with Hua Quan); Green Dragon brings money through happy events; General brings authority and satisfaction; Memorial governs documents, exams and winning disputes; Joy governs additions and celebrations.

Loss-and-friction ones: Small Loss governs minor waste and lost items; Great Loss bigger financial loss; Illness minor ailments and delay; Flying gossip, petty people and isolation; Ambush hidden restraint and worry; Official legal-administrative hassle and paperwork.

They act mainly in the same palace (weaker by aspect) and only ever add or subtract: on an auspicious base, Doctor/Memorial/Joy embellish; on a stressed base, Great Loss/Ambush/Official amplify the friction.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find where natal Lu Cun sits as the starting point
  2. Set direction: clockwise for yang-male/yin-female, counterclockwise for yin-male/yang-female
  3. Place Doctor through Official one per palace and mark them on your chart
  4. Memorize the six auspicious and six loss/friction meanings
  5. Only read those sharing a palace with major stars and transformations; judge the main structure first
  6. Auspicious base plus good spirit is a plus, bad base plus loss spirit a minus; never judge from a minor spirit alone

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Using the Doctors as the main verdict and fearing Ambush or Official on sight

Mistake 2: Not knowing they start at Lu Cun with gender/stem-based direction, then reading a misplaced set

Mistake 3: Memorizing isolated meanings without adding/subtracting within the major-star structure

Back to Your Own Chart

The twelve Doctors are fine-grained annotations, not the protagonists of fate. Master the placement and their basic nature, then let them serve the major stars and transformations; your reading gets finer without being led by a crowd of minor spirits.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the placement and meaning of the twelve Doctor spirits before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the placement and meaning of the twelve Doctor spirits line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
