Wu-Qu and Tian-Fu in Zi or Wu as a Wealth Vault: Having Hands and a Vault Does Not Guarantee Riches

Wu-Qu makes money and Tian-Fu guards it; together in Zi or Wu it is a hands-plus-vault structure. Whether it truly makes wealth depends on the福德宫 Tanlang, malefics, Hua Lu and Empty stars, and on splitting the Zi-Wu difference by birth-stem transformations. This is cultural discussion of destiny studies, not investment advice; follow licensed institutions and official product terms.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-27

Wu-Qu is the wealth star and Tian-Fu the vault star; sharing a Zi or Wu Life Palace sounds like being born with a money vault. But hands and a vault are only structure; whether the vault holds goods depends on other palaces.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Wu-Qu earns and Tian-Fu guards; without either the loop stalls. Real wealth also depends on whether福德宫 Tanlang supplies desire and drive, whether malefics pierce the vault, whether Hua Lu pours money in, and whether Empty stars drain it out.

The Underlying Logic

Wu-Qu is the proper wealth star, governing practical earning and execution; Tian-Fu is the vault, governing savings and guarding.

Together in Zi or Wu they form an earn-and-save vault structure with a steadier base than average.

But福德宫 Tanlang governs enjoyment and desire; without drive or with excess spending, the vault stays thin.

Malefics piercing the vault, Empty stars draining wealth, and no Hua Lu to pour in mean a vault without goods; downgrade the reading.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu-Qu and Tian-Fu share the Life Palace in Zi or Wu
  2. Check their brightness to gauge earning and vault-holding strength
  3. Read福德宫 Tanlang for desire drive and spending habits
  4. Check one by one whether Yang, Tuo, Fire, Ling or Empty pierce or drain the vault
  5. See whether natal or decade Hua Lu or Quan pours wealth into the vault
  6. Split the Zi-Wu difference by birth stem to judge whether the vault is real or hollow

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring wealth at Wu-Qu Tian-Fu in Life while ignoring Empty and piercing stars

Mistake 2: Seeing earn-and-save without examining福德宫 Tanlang's spending and desire

Mistake 3: Ignoring the Zi-Wu difference and transformation activation and overrating it as rich

Back to Your Own Chart

The Wu-Qu Tian-Fu vault is a good base, but it needs goods put in, a lock held and no draining. For financial decisions, follow licensed institutions and official product terms; do not treat the pattern as an insurance policy.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Wu-Qu Tian-Fu in Zi-Wu as wealth vault before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Wu-Qu Tian-Fu in Zi-Wu as wealth vault line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
