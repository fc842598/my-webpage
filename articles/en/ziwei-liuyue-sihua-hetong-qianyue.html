How to Use Monthly Transformations to Time a Contract Signing

For signing, first read the yearly background, then use monthly transformations to find that month's window. Lu and Ke favor documents and exposure, while Ji and Quan call for repeated checks; the contract and lawyer review remain the standard.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Which month is better to sign is one of the most practical uses of the monthly layer. It is not picking an auspicious date but, after understanding the yearly trend, solidifying terms in the right month.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Avoid two misuses: reading the month detached from the yearly background and taking a good month as reason not to review terms. Fortune reading only suggests rhythm; the written contract and professional review truly protect you.

The Underlying Logic

The month subdivides the yearly theme into monthly rhythm.

Lu and Ke favor documents, cooperation and exposure.

Quan and Ji indicate terms need repeated checks and attention to boundaries.

Legal matters follow the contract text and professional lawyer review; fortune reading does not decide outcomes.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the year and decade to set the signing background
  2. Then locate the relevant month's Life Palace and transformations
  3. Months with Lu and Ke favor advancing documents and cooperation
  4. Months with Quan and Ji call for checking terms and responsibilities
  5. Clarify signing, breach and exit clauses item by item
  6. Have a professional lawyer review before signing rather than deciding by the month

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Picking a date by the month alone, detached from the yearly background

Mistake 2: Skipping contract review when the month looks good

Mistake 3: Taking fortune reading as legal basis and ignoring professional review

Back to Your Own Chart

Monthly transformations are a tool to arrange rhythm, not a shield for a contract. Read the year, choose the month, solidify terms and use lawyer review so signing is genuinely secure.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to monthly transformations and contract signing before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the monthly transformations and contract signing line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
