How to Verify a Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart Using Past Events Before Reading

Do not judge a freshly cast chart at once. Use personality and the timing of past events such as schooling, moves, job changes and marriage to reverse-check the hour and pattern before interpreting.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

A small error in the birth hour shifts the whole chart. The most reliable check is not recalculating but matching major events that already happened against the chart.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many ask about the future from a chart that may itself be wrong. Verify the past first; once the chart stands, looking forward is trustworthy.

The Underlying Logic

Chart verification is the mandatory check between casting and reading, confirming the hour and palaces.

Match personality to the Life star, then match years of schooling, moves, job changes, marriage and illness to the decade and year.

If events do not match, first suspect the hour, leap month or midnight handling, adjust, and recheck.

Several events matching in a row means the chart is set and later interpretation has a foundation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Cast the full chart from the given birth data
  2. Match personality and temperament to the Life star for an initial direction
  3. List the major events the person remembers and their years
  4. Match each to the decade and yearly palaces and transformations
  5. If they fail, check the hour, leap month and midnight, then adjust and recheck
  6. Once several events match, confirm the chart and begin the formal reading

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading the future from an unverified, possibly wrong chart

Mistake 2: Trusting the chart after one small match instead of repeated checks

Mistake 3: Ignoring hour error when events do not match and reading on anyway

Back to Your Own Chart

Verification takes patience but decides everything that follows. Once the chart is checked and set, every forward statement holds.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to chart verification against past events before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the chart verification against past events line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
