Natal and Annual Hua Ji in the Spouse Palace: Which Layer Comes First?

Separate natal and annual Hua Ji before reading relationship pressure in a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-26 22:11

Seeing Hua Ji twice on a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart can feel alarming. The marks may belong to different chart layers, stars, and palaces. Their meaning is clearer when each is written as a full statement instead of a warning label.

Identify the layer first

Record the star and palace of the natal Hua Ji. Then record the star and palace of the annual Hua Ji. Some software overlays annual transformations on natal palaces; other displays reposition the annual palaces. Check the legend, birth time, and the year boundary before comparing them.

A natal relationship pattern may describe a recurring difficulty with trust or boundaries. An annual signal may bring a concrete scheduling problem this year. Together they suggest a topic to address, not a guaranteed breakup. If the annual blockage actually falls in the Wealth Palace, shared bills may be the trigger.

Test the reading against life

Look at the star carrying Hua Ji, the supporting palaces, and the decade backdrop. A couple considering a move can list career costs, travel, and contracts. A couple short on time because of a work project can change their calendar before assigning a lifelong meaning to one difficult month.

Use the natal layer for the recurring pattern and the annual layer for this year's task. Then ask both people what is happening. Without a real issue, treat the chart as a prompt to observe rather than a forecast of disaster.
