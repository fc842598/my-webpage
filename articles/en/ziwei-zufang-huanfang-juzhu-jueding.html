Rent or Buy in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Property and Travel Shape How You Live

Rent or buy, move or stay is not only about money. Read Property's living state, Travel's mobility, Life's need for stability and Karma's sensitivity to environment. Tian Ma in the four horse places or active Travel naturally suits renting and mobility; Tian Fu guarding Property needs ownership and stability. A decade activating Property is often the moving window. Housing choice is matching lifestyle to star nature, not blindly chasing ownership.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-15

Some thrive on moving often, others only settle in an owned home; with the same budget, the two choices reflect different stability needs set by Property and Travel.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether to rent or buy, whether now suits moving, or why your home feels wrong, first see your living nature and current decade before deciding.

The Underlying Logic

Living state reads Property: steady Property with Tian Fu/Tian Xiang needs a stable container, and the security of ownership feeds overall state; Property with Tian Ma/Po Jun or pulled by active Travel carries mobility in living itself and feels cramped locked into one home. Property describes your relationship with your nest.

Mobility needs read Travel and Tian Ma: active Travel, Tian Ma in Life/Travel or Life in the four horse places (Yin/Shen/Si/Hai) change work city and life radius quickly, where the flexibility of renting is an advantage; stable Travel that dislikes change finds frequent moves steadily draining security.

Environmental sensitivity reads Karma: Karma with Tai Yin/Tian Tong/Tian Ji is especially sensitive to light, noise and atmosphere, and a wrong home directly hits mood and sleep—such people prioritize living quality when moving; less sensitive Karma tolerates hardware more and weighs value and convenience.

Moving window reads decade/year: decade Lu/Quan activating Property often brings smooth upgrade, move or purchase; Ji into Property brings lease disputes, renovation overruns and housing problems—slow rather than rush. Combine life stage, work mobility and Property rhythm; renting and owning have no absolute rank, only fit.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Property to judge whether you need stable ownership or carry living mobility
  2. Read Travel and Tian Ma to estimate the odds of work and life-radius change in coming years
  3. Read Karma to confirm sensitivity to light/noise/atmosphere and the weight of living quality
  4. Combine finances to separate must-buy-now from rent-first
  5. Move in decade Lu/Quan windows activating Property; go slow when Ji enters Property
  6. Treat rent-vs-buy as lifestyle fit rather than a one-dimensional asset anxiety

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Ignoring strong Travel mobility and taking on a mortgage that binds you to one city with the trend

Mistake 2: A highly environment-sensitive Karma considering only price, drained long-term by a wrong home

Mistake 3: Rushing to sign or renovate in a Ji-into-Property phase, stacking problems and overruns

Back to Your Own Chart

There is no standard housing answer. Property reads your bond with home, Travel mobility, Karma environmental sensitivity; follow the decade Property rhythm and both renting freely and owning securely are good choices—what matters is fitting you.
