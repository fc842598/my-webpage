How to Read the Double-Lu Flanking Pattern: Resources Around You, and Where They Come From

Double-Lu Flanking places Lu Cun and Hua Lu in the two palaces on either side of the Life Palace, signaling that resources, opportunity and help with money are often close. It does not mean gain without work; see which palaces the two lu fly from and whether Hua Ji breaks them.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28

Double-Lu Flanking is like having resources offered from both sides, with extra help in money and matters, but the source of the lu decides the nature of that help.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Hearing double-lu flanking, some assume an easy life with resources handed to them, without checking which palaces the lu come from or whether they are stable.

The Underlying Logic

The structure places Lu Cun in one adjacent palace and Hua Lu in the other, flanking the Life Palace; classical texts see steady help in seeking money throughout life.

Lu Cun is stable resource and foundation, while Hua Lu is opportunity activated in a stage; one still, one moving, so both resources and opportunity sit beside the person.

Check the palaces holding the two lu: flanking from wealth, career or property-related palaces tends to give real resources; if those palaces are struck by Ji or hold empty stars, the help is discounted.

Flanking is good external support, but the Life Palace's own main star must still be strong; a weak life relying on flanking often cannot seize the opportunity in front of it.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the Life Palace, then check whether the two adjacent palaces hold Lu Cun and Hua Lu
  2. Tell which is Lu Cun (stable) and which is Hua Lu (active) to understand the two kinds of help
  3. Read the meaning of the palaces holding the two lu to judge which line resources come from
  4. Check whether the flanking palaces are struck by Hua Ji or hold empty, fallen stars
  5. Return to the Life Palace itself and confirm the main star is strong enough to take the support
  6. Layer the limits and years to see which stage most easily realizes the help

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Double-Lu Flanking as gain without work and ignoring the Life Palace's own strength

Mistake 2: Not seeing where the two lu come from and misjudging the nature of the resources

Mistake 3: Ignoring that the flanking palaces are struck by Ji, so external help is unstable

Back to Your Own Chart

Double-Lu Flanking does keep resources and opportunity close, but it is external support, not someone doing the work for you. See the source of the two lu, keep the Life Palace strong, and actively take the help in activated stages to make it truly yours.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Double-Lu Flanking pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Double-Lu Flanking pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
