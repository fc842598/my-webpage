Using the Twelve Doctor Spirits in Annual Reading: Green Dragon's Income, Memorial's Documents, Ambush's Hidden Drain

The Doctors earn their keep mainly in the annual and minor-limit reading: as the year's palace reaches a spirit, combined with that palace's stars and transformations it hints where the year brings joy, document-based opportunity, or hidden drain and paperwork—a timing aid.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09

On the natal chart the Doctors are light; they shine in the annual reading. The same Green Dragon only becomes a clear "income through a happy event or project this year" when it lands in the annual Wealth palace with Hua Lu.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking which months bring chance and where to guard against loss or friction means overlaying the annual Doctors on the annual palaces, focusing on what the good spirits aid and where the loss spirits drain.

The Underlying Logic

For annual use, first set the year Life and twelve palaces, then read each palace's co-resident spirit; the spirit creates no event itself, it only magnifies or labels the trend already set by that palace's stars and transformations.

Income and opportunity: annual Wealth/Career with Green Dragon or Doctor plus Hua Lu/Hua Ke brings income or promotion through a project, happy event or reputation; Memorial favors all document matters—exams, contracts, bids, approvals, filing lawsuits.

Loss and friction: annual Wealth with Small/Great Loss points to scattered or larger outlay, and with Hua Ji guards against investment mistakes; Life/Career with Ambush means hidden obstruction and stalled process, while Official guards against fines, compliance and administrative issues.

Timing can reach the month: when a month palace reaches the same spirit, that is often when the matter lands; yet everything presupposes the stars' and transformations' verdict—the spirit only answers when and in what form.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Set the year Life and twelve annual palaces for the year in question
  2. Place that year's Doctors on the annual chart and compare palace by palace
  3. Locate Green Dragon, Doctor, Memorial and Joy, and with Hua Lu/Hua Ke set this year's opportunities
  4. Locate Small/Great Loss, Ambush, Official and Flying, and with Hua Ji set the drains and frictions to guard
  5. Map opportunities to concrete events (income, exams, contracts, celebrations) and pre-plan the drains
  6. For months, overlay the monthly chart and find which month re-activates the same spirit

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Over-reading natal Doctors while ignoring that they mainly work in the annual chart

Mistake 2: Declaring a ruined year from Great Loss or Ambush without checking whether the stars and transformations are actually bad

Mistake 3: Judging good/bad without timing, wasting these spirits' real strength—when things happen

Back to Your Own Chart

In the annual chart the Doctors are a timing magnifier: they do not change the year's baseline but locate opportunity and hidden drain in concrete events and months. Always let the stars and transformations speak first; the spirits fill the detail.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to practical annual use of the twelve Doctor spirits before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the practical annual use of the twelve Doctor spirits line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
