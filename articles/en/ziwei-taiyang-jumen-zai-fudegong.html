Tai Yang and Ju Men in the Virtue Palace: A Bright, Expressive Mind and Turning Devotion and Deep Study Into Ease Without Inner Friction

The Virtue Palace reads the mental state, inner enjoyment and values. Tai Yang governs brightness, benevolence and devotion while Ju Men governs speech, deep study and worry, so together in Yin or Shen the mind is bright, warm and expressive, pursuing meaningful things that help others and holding opinions. The risk is Ju Men deep thinking, worry and argument and Tai Yang caring for others, so the key is using expression for constructive matters, separating one’s own tasks from others’ and learning to calm the mind.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-05

People with Tai Yang and Ju Men in the Virtue Palace have ideals, warmth and expression, willing to give for others; yet when thinking too deep and arguing, the mind easily fluctuates and often worries over others’ matters.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the inner pattern with Tai Yang and Ju Men in the Virtue Palace. Understand the palace reads the mental state, then see how brightness and speech combine so one keeps ideals while living at ease.

The Underlying Logic

Tai Yang in the Virtue Palace gives a bright, benevolent, idealistic mind, yet also worrying for others and giving much.

Ju Men there gives a thoughtful, deep, expressive mind, yet also worry, argument and fluctuation.

Together they mean brightness plus speech: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars, ideals and expression become calm; with malefics and Ji, pressure from worry, disputes or caring follows.

The lesson is bright with calm and persuasive with tolerance: use expression for constructive matters, separate one’s own tasks from others’, and practice calming and accepting.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Tai Yang and Ju Men share the Virtue Palace and check their brightness
  2. Use expression for constructive matters to avoid argument and friction
  3. Separate one’s own tasks from others’ to reduce worry
  4. Practice calming the mind and leave an outlet for emotion
  5. Use steady inner interests and a sustainable helping rhythm
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of mood and inner cultivation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Tai Yang-Ju Men in the Virtue Palace only as idealistic and expressive while ignoring inner friction

Mistake 2: Thinking too deep and arguing, with large mental fluctuation

Mistake 3: Worrying for others too much, unable to separate tasks
