Many negotiate a raise only by feeling that they deserve one, without looking at when the company sets budgets or when their performance is seen. With the right timing, the same words have very different effects.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to judge the right timing for a raise negotiation from the chart. First read Career and Wealth for the foundation, then the decade and year transformations for activation, and finally align with real cycles.

The Underlying Logic

The Career Palace sets work performance and platform: an auspicious Career Palace with a well-placed major star and Lu, Quan and Ke in the trine means work is easily recognized and the platform has room for raises; malefics and Ji in Career mean high work pressure and low recognition.

The Wealth Palace sets income and bargaining: Hua Lu and Lu Cun in Wealth mean income has growth room; Hua Ji in Wealth brings anxiety about money and easily blocked bargaining.

The decade and year transformations set timing: years when Hua Lu or Hua Quan enters Career or Wealth are usually windows when performance is seen and budget has room; years with Hua Ji entering favor holding steady. The year Life Palace star also matters: auspicious-star years such as Zi Wei, the Sun, Tian Fu and Tai Yin are more favorable for negotiation, while malefic-heavy years favor waiting.

Chart timing must align with reality: company budget cycles, usually at the start of the year or after the fiscal year ends, performance review cycles, and just after completing a major project are all good real-world windows; when the chart and reality resonate, negotiation success is highest.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the strength, major star and transformations of your Career and Wealth palaces to set the raise foundation
  2. See whether the current decade and year transformations activate Career or Wealth, judging whether it is a window period
  3. See whether the year Life Palace star is auspicious or malefic-heavy, tohelp judge whether the year favors negotiation or waiting
  4. Align chart timing with reality: company budget cycles, performance reviews and recently completed projects
  5. Prepare in advance: organize performance data, market salary rates and your core contributions
  6. After choosing the window,proactively schedule the talk, speak with data and contributions, not emotionally or threatening to leave

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Negotiating only because you feel you deserve a raise, without considering company budget and performance cycles, pushing at the wrong time

Mistake 2: Looking only at auspicious transformations on the chart without preparing performance data and market rates, asking with empty hands

Mistake 3: Pushing a raise in a Hua Ji or malefic-heavy year, leaving a bad impression on leadership instead
