How Zi Wei Dou Shu Judges Entrepreneurship Fit: Sha-Po-Lang, Career/Wealth Palaces and Decade Timing

Entrepreneurship fit depends on Life's pioneering resilience (Sha-Po-Lang, Zi Wei/Wu Qu/Tan Lang lean pioneering), whether Career suits independence over employment, whether Wealth/Property hold a fund-receiving vault, and whether Travel favors outward expansion. Crucially, whether the decade reaches a launch phase: natal fit plus decade activation is the window to act.

Entrepreneurship fit is temperament + work form + treasury + timing: the natal chart decides if you are the material, the decade whether now is the time; missing either means paying tuition.

What This Question Is Really Asking

A natal Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang employee chart starting a business in a malefic decade fails repeatedly; a Sha-Po-Lang type launching in a low decade before activation struggles just as much.

The Underlying Logic

Temperament: pioneering Life stars—Sha-Po-Lang, Zi Wei, Wu Qu, Tan Lang—dare risk, bear pressure and break ground, suiting startups; Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang and Fu-Xiang favor organizations.

Career: Quan/Lu with a strong star means steering independently; heavy malefics or Ji in Career means cash-flow, team or direction problems—don't dive in rashly.

Wealth/Property: startups need a vault for cash flow; stable Wealth/Property with Lu Cun/Hua Lu resists risk, empty stars flanking wealth mean broken cash chains.

Timing (key): launch when the decade Life takes a pioneering pattern and decade Career/Wealth activate Lu/Quan; malefic/Ji decades are for employment and accumulation, not against-trend launches.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Life star for pioneering resilience
  2. See whether Career favors independence with activated Quan/Lu
  3. Check Wealth/Property for the fund vault and risk resistance
  4. Read Travel for outward market expansion
  5. Use the decade to find the real launch window
  6. When fit is partial, choose side business, intrapreneurship or partnership

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming entrepreneurship suits everyone

Mistake 2: Reading natal only and launching in a low decade

Mistake 3: Starting leveraged with an empty-star treasury
