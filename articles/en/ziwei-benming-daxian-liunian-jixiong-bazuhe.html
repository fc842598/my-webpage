How Natal, Decade and Year Luck Stack: The Eight Good-Bad Combinations of Three Layers Explained

The natal chart is the baseline, the decade the ten-year stage, the year the trigger; each leans good or bad, giving exactly eight combinations. Sorting your matter into one of the eight tells whether this year is for pushing, holding, lying low or defending hard.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09

Expecting every year to be good from a strong natal chart, or panicking at one bad year, is single-layer reading. Real luck is stacked across three; the same good year is heavily discounted inside a weak decade.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why a good natal chart feels hard lately, or why a good-looking year yields nothing, means assigning each of natal, current decade and this year a lean, then matching one of the eight combinations.

The Underlying Logic

First set each layer's tone: natal strength of the matter's palace and transformations, decade whether these ten years activate it well or badly, year whether annual transformations help or block—mark each good or bad.

Three-good (natal good, decade good, year good) is tailwind with aligned support across layers, suited to initiative, expansion and big decisions with the highest兑现 rate, while guarding over-optimism and keeping a safety margin.

Two-good-one-bad such as good-good-bad or good-bad-good has an upward main line with a local hurdle: the first is a correction year in a good decade—keep small steps and do not change course on one year's swing; the second is a rebound window in a weak stage—take short chances but do not over-commit or mistake rebound for reversal.

Other combinations each have a playbook: one good layer is usually a staged or local chance; two leaning bad call for defense, risk control and building inner strength; three-bad concentrates the most pressure—preserve capital, avoid battle and big decisions, and push again when decade and year turn.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. For your chosen matter, set the natal tone of its palace and transformations
  2. Set whether the current decade activates that matter well or badly
  3. Set whether this year's transformations help or block it, obtaining a three-layer good/bad sequence
  4. Match the eight: three-good, two-good-one-bad, one-good-two-bad, three-bad
  5. Three-good pushes; two-good-one-bad advances in small steps or catches rebound; one-good-two-bad defends with selective offense; three-bad preserves and avoids battle
  6. Re-lay with each new year (decade changes about every ten years, year every one) and adjust the playbook

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging lifelong luck from the natal chart alone, ignoring decade stage and year trigger

Mistake 2: Using a vague good/bad impression across layers instead of the specific palace of the matter asked

Mistake 3: Forcing in a three-bad year and hesitating in a three-good one, with playbook mismatched to combination

Back to Your Own Chart

The eight three-layer combinations turn "should I advance or retreat this year" into something decidable. Tune natal, decade and year, match the pattern, and you neither stir when you should lie low nor waste a tailwind window.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the eight good-bad combinations across natal, decade and year before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the eight good-bad combinations across natal, decade and year line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
