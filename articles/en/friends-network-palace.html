The Friends and Network Palace helps you evaluate partners, colleagues, clients, and communities. It asks which relationships can carry responsibility, not simply whether you have friends.

Supportive stars can make introductions and teamwork easier. Malefics or Hua Ji can increase competition, unclear roles, or communication costs. The result depends on contracts, the Wealth Palace, and the active cycle.

Test Cooperation Structure

Mars or Ling Xing here can make a partnership start quickly and become reactive, so decision rights and exit terms matter. Tian Ma can describe partners or clients who are remote, mobile, or frequently changing. These combinations call for structure rather than a blanket judgment about loyalty.

Compare People and Money

A strong Friends Palace with a weak Wealth Palace can mean capable people but an unstable revenue model. A pressured Friends Palace with a strong Career Palace may favor formal organizational roles over informal partnerships.

Use It for Real Decisions

Before hiring a friend or joining a partnership, define ownership, payment, responsibilities, and an exit process. Then compare the opposite palace and wider triad to see whether the network has resources and a workable role.

Combination one: Tian Ji and Tian Ma in the Friends Palace with Hua Lu supporting Wealth can bring referrals, remote clients, or cross-regional project partners. The opportunity becomes useful only when ownership, delivery, and payment dates are defined. Movement in the network is not the same as instability when contracts and responsibilities are clear.

Combination two: Kong Jie or Hua Ji in Friends with a strong Career Palace may favor formal vendors and documented organizational roles over an informal partnership with acquaintances. The network can still provide information and introductions, but shared accounts, verbal promises, and undefined exit terms deserve extra caution. When Friends is active only in an annual cycle, test one bounded project before making a long-term partnership commitment.

Chart-Reading Order

Read the main stars and transformations in the Friends Palace, then check Wealth for money, Career for authority, and the opposite palace for the other side of the agreement. Add decade and annual timing only after the partnership structure is clear.
