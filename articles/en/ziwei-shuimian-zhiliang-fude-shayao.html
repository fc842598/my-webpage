Sleep Quality in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Fortune Palace Malefics and Improvement

Sleep problems are a common modern complaint. From the chart perspective, the star combinations in the Fortune Palace (mental state, inner world) greatly affect sleep quality—people with heavy malefics in Fortune Palace easily overthink, feel tense, and have difficulty falling asleep. Understanding your sleep type helps find corresponding improvement methods.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24

If Fortune Palace has many malefics, the mind does not relax easily, and sleep is naturally affected.
