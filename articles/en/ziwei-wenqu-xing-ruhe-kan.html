The Wen Qu Star Overview: Speech, Art and Feeling

Wen Qu governs speech, art, feeling and distinction by an unconventional path, opposite Wen Chang (reason). With auspicious transformations it favors communication, art and verbal fame; with malefics, guard disputes, moodiness and relationship entanglement. It gives expression and sensitivity.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

Wen Qu is like a feeling artist, eloquent and perceptive; but feeling needs reason to avoid emotional trouble.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some take Wen Qu only as romance, ignoring speech and art; others see it and assume eloquence, not guarding disputes.
