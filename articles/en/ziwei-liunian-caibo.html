An Annual Cycle in the Wealth Palace

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-01 08:43

When the annual Life Palace moves into the Wealth Palace, money becomes a central management topic for the year. This does not guarantee either profit or loss. It brings income, spending, pricing, debt, collection, investment, and shared finances closer to the front of daily decisions.

Activation reveals the existing money structure

A strong natal Wealth Palace may use the year to negotiate compensation, add a client channel, or organize several income sources. A strained Wealth Palace may expose late payment, rising costs, or unsuitable leverage. The annual layer activates what the chart can already support; it does not create durable wealth without a career engine or market.

Two combinations show different kinds of money

  • An annual Wealth Palace with Hua Lu, supported by Hua Quan in Career, may bring higher income because a promotion, project, or responsibility expands. This is earned responsibility income, not necessarily easy money.
  • An annual Wealth Palace with Hua Ji or Kong Jie, while the Property Palace is activated, may bring delayed collection alongside housing, family, or renovation costs. A larger revenue figure can coexist with weaker cash reserves.

Trace both the source and the exit

Career indicates salary, role, and project income; Travel may show external customers or platforms. Inner Life can reveal emotional or status spending, while Property can carry major household expenses. A useful reading therefore asks not only how much comes in, but when it arrives, what must be paid first, and what remains after obligations.

A clear chart-reading order

First judge the natal Wealth Palace and then the annual stars and transformations activating it. Second read Career and Travel to identify the income path. Third inspect Hua Ji, Kong Jie, Inner Life, and Property for leakage or major spending. Finally compare the ten-year cycle and build a practical plan for pricing, collection, reserves, and risk.
