The General Star Well-Placed Pattern: Wu Qu at Brightness

When Wu Qu (general and wealth star) guards life at a bright palace, it brings resolve, decisiveness, finance and execution, favoring finance, police-military, engineering and technology. Guard excess rigidity and bluntness; auspicious stars temper it to use the general-wealth star well.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

This pattern is Wu Qu the general-wealth star in a strong position, able to carry matters and manage wealth; but firmness needs softness.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Wu Qu bright and assume wealth and authority, but excess firmness breaks and needs tempering; read combinations.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu governs wealth, generalship and firm execution.

At brightness guarding life, the general-wealth star is well-used.

It favors finance, police-military, engineering and technology.

Guard excess rigidity and bluntness; malefics break it.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu bright guarding life
  2. Read Wu Qu brightness
  3. Count auspicious stars for tempering
  4. Count malefics to guard excess firmness
  5. Read trine wealth-career for direction
  6. Judge valid or blunt isolation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing Wu Qu bright and assuming wealth without tempering

Mistake 2: Excess firmness and bluntness, not guarding loss

Mistake 3: Seeing only wealth, not generalship execution

Back to Your Own Chart

This pattern asks whether the general-wealth star well-placed has firmness matched with softness. Brightness gives force, auspicious stars temper, and resolve becomes wealth and distinction. This cultural-tendency reference is not investment advice; defer to licensed institutions.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the General Star Well-Placed pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the General Star Well-Placed pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
