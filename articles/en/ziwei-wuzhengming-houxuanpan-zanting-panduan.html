Missing Birth Certificate: Which Zi Wei Dou Shu Claims Should Wait?

How to label uncertain palace readings when the birth hour cannot be confirmed.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:15

An approximate afternoon birth time can produce neighboring candidate charts. Do not pick one to announce a definite marriage or career year. Compare the changing palaces first and state which claims rely on the missing evidence.

Mark the unstable parts

Enter plausible times with the same birthplace and calendar rule. Compare the Life, Spouse, and Career Palaces and the timing layers. If an hour boundary changes those placements, pause precise predictions based on them. A job decision can still use verified duties, savings, and travel costs; a chart remains a question to investigate.

Read in order

Seek the original record, narrow the time range through independent family evidence, and keep multiple charts if the uncertainty remains. Explain which conclusions are stable across candidates and which must wait for better data.
