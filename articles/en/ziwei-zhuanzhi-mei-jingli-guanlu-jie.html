Too Exhausted to Apply for Jobs: A Zi Wei Dou Shu Checklist

Separate career options from energy, health, and cash constraints.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-31 09:25

Before treating an annual Career or Travel Palace signal as an instruction to resign, review real capacity. One worker has interviews but no time after long shifts; another can apply but lacks cash for a job gap. Compare duties, outside offers, health needs, and savings before setting a timeline.

An active annual Travel Palace can justify exploring options, not an immediate resignation. Check energy, medical needs, written offers, and savings first.
