Many users are not afraid of paying itself. They are afraid of paying before seeing whether the core experience is even there.

On YuetianAI's current public pages, the base chart comes first, then follow-up use is tiered into 3 guest questions, 8 free logged-in questions, and 80 for members each day.

What the free layer is best for

It is best for first-pass confirmation: can you see the chart clearly, is the question flow usable, and does the answer return to real chart structure?

The free layer is most valuable when it helps you decide whether the system fits you at all.

How to use the free part well

Use one past event to test whether the answer really reads your chart.

Use one live issue to see whether the answer gives an actionable order instead of emotional comfort alone.

Who the member layer fits better

Membership mainly suits people who will keep separating career, relationships, and timing into repeated follow-up questions.

If you are a one-time user, the free layer is often enough for screening. The 19.90-yuan plan matters more for frequent use.

A cleaner order

  1. Use the free layer to confirm the chart and the question flow.
  2. Decide whether you are really a frequent user.
  3. Buy only if repeated follow-up is part of how you use it.
