Can AI Fortune Telling Help With Major Decisions? Put It in the Support Seat First

AI Fortune-Telling Q&A2026-07-25 23:33

AI fortune telling can help with major decisions, but it should usually sit in the support seat, not the driver's seat.

Career moves, marriage, business, and relocation still require real contracts, health limits, financial reality, and outside information.

Which layer it actually helps with

It is strongest at separating direction: is this mainly a role question, a platform question, a relationship-carrying question, or a timing question?

It is not the right layer for legal judgment, medical diagnosis, or high-risk financial execution.

How to keep it in the support seat

Before a business move, use the chart to check direction and timing, then do market and finance work separately.

Before marriage, use the chart to check pattern and timing, then handle family boundaries and practical arrangements in real life.

Why support-seat use is stronger

Support-seat use sharpens real-world research instead of replacing it.

Driver-seat use tempts you to compress complex reality into one mystical sentence.

A better major-decision order

  1. Use the chart for direction and timing first.
  2. Do real-world and professional checking second.
  3. Make the final decision only after both layers are on the table.
