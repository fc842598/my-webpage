Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Life Palace: Strong, Decisive and Able to Storm Hard Targets, Turning Wealth and the General Into a Life That Is Not Alone

The Life Palace reads the underlying character and life direction. Wu Qu transforms into wealth, governing firm execution, while Qi Sha transforms into authority, governing decisiveness, independence and pioneering, so together in Mao or You authority and wealth support each other, giving a strong, hardy, highly driven character who dares to fight, suiting military, police, technical, storming and entrepreneurial fields that need hard skill. The risk is over-firmness, friction, risk and loneliness, so the key is turning edge into tact and learning to collaborate.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the character of Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Life Palace. Understand the palace reads the foundation, then see how wealth and authority combine so one dares to storm without losing rapport.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Life Palace gives firmness, execution, practicality and hardiness with a wealth sense, yet also bluntness and hardness without softness.

Qi Sha there gives decisiveness, independence, pioneering and dignity like a general, yet also over-firmness, risk and no way back.

Together they mean wealth plus authority: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars hard skill becomes big enterprise; with malefics and Ji and no restraint, friction, conflict and setbacks from forcing through and lone decisions follow.

The lesson is firmness with softness and storming through collaboration: keep edge within tact, build partnerships actively, weigh the way back before risk and use decisions on battles worth fighting.

  2. Match storming ability to hard targets worth it rather than risking for no reason
  3. Build partnerships and learn collaboration rather than fighting alone
  4. Keep edge within tact and leave a step for discussion
  5. Weigh the way back in major decisions to avoid forcing through
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of pioneering and turning

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Qi Sha in the Life Palace only as strong and able to fight hard while ignoring loneliness

Mistake 2: Over-firm and risk-loving with no way back, causing friction and bumps

Mistake 3: Fighting alone and unwilling to collaborate, narrowing the path
