How to Read the Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu Pattern: A Strength for Study and Exams

Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu places a strong Tai Yang and Tian Liang with Wen Chang and Lu Cun in the triple-direction, favoring study, exams, research and public-service work. Whether Wen Chang joins naturally in the trine or is merely pulled from the opposite palace changes the strength, and many malefics break it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28

Its strength is staying focused, explaining clearly and performing steadily in exams, suiting paths built on expertise and seniority.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some call it a top-scholar destiny without checking whether Wen Chang and Lu Cun truly meet in the trine or counting the malefics, and overrate it.

The Underlying Logic

The core is a strong Tai Yang and Tian Liang guarding the life (often in Mao), with Wen Chang and Lu Cun meeting in the triple-direction; the old saying ranks it first for examinations.

Tai Yang resolves Tian Liang's aloofness and turns its principle into academic and institutional principle, so today it shows in research, education, judiciary, public service and technical professions.

When Wen Chang joins naturally in the trine, scholarly strength flows; when it is only pulled from the opposite or propped by Lu Cun, the refined quality drops clearly.

If Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Huo Xing, Ling Xing, the emptiness stars or natal Hua Ji meet the trine, study and exams are interrupted or performance is unstable, breaking the pattern.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Tai Yang and Tian Liang guard the life together and are strong, not fallen
  2. Check whether Wen Chang and Lu Cun truly meet in the triple-direction
  3. Tell whether Wen Chang joins naturally in the trine or is pulled from the opposite to gauge strength
  4. Look for auspicious stars such as Kui Yue, Zuo You and Chang Qu as bonus points
  5. Count Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, fire-bell, emptiness and natal Hua Ji; the more breakers, the more you guard against interruption
  6. Layer the limits and years to see whether key years for school, certificates or public exams are activated

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Calling it a top-scholar destiny at the sight of Tai Yang and Tian Liang without checking Wen Chang and Lu Cun

Mistake 2: Treating opposite-palace Wen Chang as a true trine meeting and overrating the pattern

Mistake 3: Ignoring malefics and natal Hua Ji, leading to unstable exam performance

Back to Your Own Chart

Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu genuinely favors study, exams and research, but it forms on strict conditions. Verify how the stars meet and how many malefics there are, then focus effort on key years, rather than relying on a scholar label.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu pattern and study before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu pattern and study line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
