The Annual Health Palace and Real Screening Priorities

Choose medical screening from clinical risk and advice, not chart symbolism.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30 09:06

An activated annual Health Palace cannot determine which medical test you need. Someone with no symptoms but chart-related worry has a different decision from someone with persistent symptoms. Use age, history, symptoms, and professional advice to choose screening or care. The Zi Wei Dou Shu chart may prompt a question about wellbeing; it cannot diagnose disease or replace a clinician.

Before interpreting an annual Health Palace, confirm that the birth time and chart settings match across tools. A worried person without symptoms and someone with persistent symptoms need different real-world responses. A chart symbol cannot select a screening test or diagnose a condition.
