Layoffs in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Using the Decade and Year to Tell Crisis From a Pivot

Being laid off or optimized off reads on three layers: the decade for whether the career line is in overall decline, the year for whether this is only a phase hit, and Career/Parents/Wealth/Property together for re-employment difficulty and runway. The chart cannot save your job but helps separate an industry pivot from a temporary setback, deciding whether to find similar work fast or use the moment to switch tracks.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-10

After a layoff people panic and scatter resumes; first see whether it is a ten-year direction problem or a single-year shock—the response pace differs entirely.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why now, how long to re-employment, or whether to change fields, open the decade/year plus Career/Parents/Wealth/Property rather than fixating on the lost job.

The Underlying Logic

The decade sets whether the line still works: a strong decade Career with Lu/Quan/Ke means the layoff is a local company/role adjustment with an intact ability line and easy re-employment; if decade Career walks malefics/Ji with Kong/Jie, the whole industry or role type is contracting, and chasing the same work hits walls—move to adjacent tracks or new directions.

The year sets the shock's nature: annual Ji clashing Career/Parents plus malefics brings role change, non-renewal or leadership turnover, a timing response; on a sound decade it warms once the year passes, so avoid permanent decisions at the trough (total self-negation, under-selling); a year activating Travel or sending Lu into Career may even be a forced but upward move.

Re-employment reads Career/Parents/Friends: Career for fit, Parents for interviews/offers/institutional recognition, Friends for referrals and industry network; when the job-search period's year activates Parents/Friends with Lu/Ke, applications, referrals and interviews convert better; when all three are stressed, lower expectations, widen the funnel and compensate with skills.

Runway reads Wealth/Property: steady Property with savings and family backing lets you choose calmly or pivot in a gap; Wealth/Property with Kong/Jie/Great Loss and tight cash flow needs a job covering expenses first, then search while employed and split the pivot into two steps. Any labor rights (severance, non-compete, social insurance) follow formal agreements and professional legal advice; the chart sets pace, not real decisions.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the current-decade Career to tell industry contraction from local role adjustment
  2. Read the year to separate a timing response from long-term decline
  3. Read Career/Parents/Friends to assess re-employment difficulty and referral/interview windows
  4. Read Wealth/Property to compute cash runway and whether calm pivoting is possible
  5. On a sound base avoid permanent decisions at the trough; on a tight base stabilize first then search while employed
  6. Let formal agreements and legal advice govern severance, non-compete and social insurance; never sign from emotion

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fixating on the lost job without the decade view of the whole industry line

Mistake 2: Taking a one-year phase shock as total failure, under-selling at the trough

Mistake 3: Gap-pivoting without cash-flow math, or clinging to shrinking same-type roles when pivot is due

Back to Your Own Chart

A layoff is an external shock, not a verdict on ability. The decade shows industry direction, the year the shock's nature, three palaces re-employment, Wealth/Property your footing; once clear, move fast or pivot deliberately—far more agency than panicked scattering.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to layoff, being optimized off and the employment gap before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the layoff, being optimized off and the employment gap line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
