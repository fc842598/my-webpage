Four Transformations in the Parents Palace: Caring, Strict, Smooth, or Stuck with Authority

How the four transformations in the Parents Palace describe relationships with elders and bosses, from caring to strict, smooth, or stuck.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 10:35

The Parents Palace covers both documents and relationships with elders, bosses, and teachers. Its transformations directly describe whether you tend to be supported, held to strict standards, able to communicate smoothly, or easily stuck with authority. Remember the basic meaning of each transformation first, then map it to a specific relationship.

How the four types show up

Prosperity often means elders care for you and resources come smoothly, with extra照顾 in the relationship. Power means the elder or boss is strict and controlling, which can also bring pressure. Reputation means exams, documents, and communication tend to go smoothly with a steady name. The challenging type signals communication that easily stalls, distance from authority, or paperwork trouble.

Relationships versus documents

The same transformation may affect both the boss relationship and topics such as certificates or approvals, so the reading should separate the two layers. Birth-year transformations are a steadier base, while limit and annual transformations are stage-specific triggers, and the two should not be treated as one time frame.

Step-by-step reading

  1. Confirm whether the transformation in the Parents Palace is prosperity, power, reputation, or challenge.
  2. Separate the elder-and-boss relationship from certificates, documents, and approvals.
  3. Read prosperity as care and resources, power as strictness and pressure, reputation as smoothness, and challenge as a block.
  4. Distinguish birth-year from limit transformations, labeling long-term base versus stage trigger.
  5. Include the opposite and supporting palaces before concluding on relationships and documents.

Reading limits

The transformations describe relationship tendencies; they do not decide how one boss will act or mean the relationship cannot improve. In real life, adjusting how you report and preparing materials thoroughly can ease power pressure or a stuck challenge. The chart flags patterns, while communication decides the relationship.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and locate the Parents Palace with its transformations.
  2. Separate relationship topics from document topics first.
  3. Map the four types to care, pressure, smoothness, and block.
  4. Label the different time frames of birth-year and limit transformations.
  5. Match the conclusion to real reporting relationships without absolute claims.
