How to Read Qi Sha in the Zi Wei Career Palace

Qi Sha can handle fast decisions and hard problems, but career success also needs authority, resources, and a clear cost of failure.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 14:20

Qi Sha in the Career Palace can show courage and crisis capacity. The practical career question is whether authority is clear and who carries the cost when plans fail.

Read its force through Career, Life, and Travel rather than praising toughness alone.

Name the task

Qi Sha suits fast-changing roles and decisions, not every high-pressure job. Hua Quan requires authority to match responsibility; Hua Ji can show blame or changing demands.

The environment decides the price of the star's style.

Add Life and Travel

A steady Life Palace can turn force into execution. A strong Travel Palace can provide a stage through external competition; a weak one asks for resources and process first.

Do not finish with the slogan that Qi Sha always means entrepreneurship.

Examples

Qi Sha in Career with Hua Quan and Hua Lu in Travel can expand with budget and mandate. Qi Sha with Hua Ji and Tian Tong in Life needs risk plans and clearer refusal skills before a sprint.

Practical reading order

Read the task in Career, capacity in Life, battlefield in Travel, return in Wealth, and timing for when to push or protect.

Practical Reading Order

Start with the question palace, compare its opposite and trine partners, then add timing and transformations before making a practical judgment.
