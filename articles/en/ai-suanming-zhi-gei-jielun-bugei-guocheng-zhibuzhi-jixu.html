What to Ask When an AI Chart Reading Gives Only a Conclusion

Request a concise explanation of the chart factors, assumptions, and uncertainty. A confident conclusion alone is not evidence of a reliable reading.

AI Fortune-Telling Q&A2026-08-01 14:29 +08:00Updated 2026-11-06 14:39 (UTC+8)

An answer such as 'you can make the change' is hard to assess without an explanation. Ask for the relevant factors and limits, not an increasingly confident version of the same conclusion.

Request an Explainable Summary

Ask which palaces, combinations, and time period are relevant, and how they connect to the question. A concise explanation is enough; a long account of internal model reasoning is neither necessary nor proof of accuracy.

Check What Came From You

Separate facts you supplied from interpretations drawn from the chart. If you already mentioned workplace pressure, an answer repeating that pressure is not independent confirmation. Ask what additional distinction the chart interpretation is making.

Ask About Alternatives and Limits

What other explanation could fit, and what missing information would matter? If the answer cannot address those questions, do not treat more fluent wording as greater reliability. Keep the reading as a tentative reflection and use verifiable information for the actual decision.
