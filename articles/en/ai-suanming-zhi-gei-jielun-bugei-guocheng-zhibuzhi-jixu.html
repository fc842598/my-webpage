When people see a question like "只给结论不给判断过程，值不值得继续," they often focus on whether the answer sounds clear and confident. The better test is whether it returns to 盘面结构 and explains 现实条件 instead of floating above them.

If the answer cannot reconnect the conclusion to chart structure and real conditions, more follow-up usually turns into emotional confirmation. If both layers stay connected, even a plain answer is often more useful than a polished but empty one.

Check whether it is actually making a judgment

The first check is not tone. It is whether the answer can move back into 盘面结构 and then explain how 现实条件 changes the call.

Without that return path, you cannot tell whether it is judging the question or simply completing a comforting script.

Test it against a real-life situation

On a job-change question, a weak answer says only 'you can move' without showing whether the driver is career structure, relocation pressure, or cash flow.

On a reunion question, 'there is still a chance' is not enough unless it explains whether the chance comes from emotion, practical repair, or timing.

Do not confuse comfort with reliability

Do not confuse confidence with reliability. When 盘面结构 and 现实条件 are still disconnected, confidence only makes vagueness sound stronger.

And do not reject an answer just because it names risk. Stronger answers usually name both the working conditions and the failure conditions.

A steadier order

  1. Check whether it returns to 盘面结构.
  2. Check whether it explains how 现实条件 changes the answer.
  3. Continue only when both lines are visible.
