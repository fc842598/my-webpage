A Spouse-Palace Decade and a Business Partner: Define the Shop Agreement

Use Zi Wei Dou Shu to frame long-term partnership duties while checking a real contract.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:16

Two people open a shop: one buys inventory and the other handles sales. A decade cycle through the Spouse Palace can frame a lasting one-to-one commitment. It does not require marriage and cannot promise a profitable business.

Define the roles

Check the natal Spouse Palace and the active decade, then the opposite Career Palace for operations and the Wealth Palace for cash. If decision authority is a theme, write down who may approve large purchases or hire temporary staff. Include stock counts, profit sharing, and exit terms.

Test the agreement

“Half each” is too vague when inventory is lost. Use contracts and records to settle responsibility. Read the chart as a prompt for questions, then let the written agreement and actual cash flow determine the decision.
