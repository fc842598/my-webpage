Decade, Minor Limit and Yearly Fortune in Zi Wei Dou Shu: A Clear Breakdown

The decade governs a ten-year stage, arranged forward or backward from Life; the yearly fortune covers a calendar year with the same branch for everyone; the minor limit follows nominal age as a second yearly reference. Modern reading treats the decade as background and the year as trigger, with the minor limit as backup.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

The decade is your life season, the year is that year's weather, and the minor limit is another yearly reference—different levels, not one thing.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Beginners treat them as either-or, using yearly signs to deny a ten-year trend (or reverse), so readings contradict themselves.

The Underlying Logic

Decade: starts at Life, forward for yang-male/yin-female and backward for yin-male/yang-female, moving one palace per ten years, setting the decade's tone and stage.

Yearly: the lunar-year branch fixes the yearly Life Palace, identical in branch for everyone that year, governing concrete event triggers within the year.

Minor limit: follows nominal age, one palace per year, a traditional second yearly reference of inner state at that age.

Correct hierarchy: natal sets lifelong pattern (body), decade the ten-year stage, year the timing, minor limit as backup; four layers complete the read.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the natal chart for lifelong pattern before any cycles
  2. Lay out the decade: which ten years, forward or backward, and its tone
  3. Within that decade, lay out the year to find trigger palaces and transformations
  4. Use the minor limit (nominal age) as backup for inner yearly state when needed
  5. Always read decade for trend and year for timing, never reversed
  6. Interpret yearly signs inside the decade: a good year fires only in a good decade

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Mixing decade, year and minor limit as one thing

Mistake 2: Using yearly signs to deny the ten-year trend

Mistake 3: Discussing cycles without the natal baseline

Back to Your Own Chart

Cycles nest like dolls: natal is you, decade the ten years you walk, the year this year, minor limit a side note. Read them nested and fortune stops being confusing.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to decade, minor limit and year differences before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the decade, minor limit and year differences line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
