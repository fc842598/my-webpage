Relocation, Emigration and Cross-City Development Through the Zi Wei Dou Shu Travel Palace

Travel governs going out, the external environment and outward development; strong, auspiciously transformed Travel with good Life-Travel interplay means leaving home, cross-city or abroad goes smoother; Travel self-Ji or heavy malefics mean obstacles and strain elsewhere. Emigration especially weighs Travel, with the first three years as adaptation; use decade Travel to time when going out is best.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

Life is your local state, Travel your state outside the comfort zone; when Travel beats Life, moving away broadens and lifts you.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Staying local on a good Life alone, or emigrating blindly with Travel self-Ji, brings strain and little gain; before leaving, read Travel and decade Travel—timing beats impulse.

The Underlying Logic

Travel governs away-from-home encounters, external environment, outward relations and room to grow, paired with Life (Life-Travel axis).

Favoring going out: strong Travel with Lu/Quan/Ke, Lu Cun/Zuo-You/Kui-Yue means mentors and a bigger stage away from home.

Caution signs: fallen Travel, self-Ji or four malefics/emptiness mean obstacles, petty people and low return elsewhere; major moves need more preparation.

Emigration: focus on decades with auspicious decade Travel; the first three years are usually adaptation (language, status, rebuilding network), with Wealth/Property for post-move finances and housing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Compare Life and Travel for local versus outward strength
  2. Check Travel brilliance/auspiciousness versus fallen/malefics
  3. If outward-favored, pursue cross-city, secondment and abroad chances
  4. Time emigration to a decade with auspicious decade Travel
  5. Reserve funds and mindset for the three-year adaptation
  6. With Travel Ji/malefics, pilot with short stints, don't bet all

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading Life only, ignoring the axis

Mistake 2: Emigrating blindly with Travel self-Ji

Mistake 3: Ignoring decade timing, quitting and leaving on impulse

Back to Your Own Chart

Travel answers whether to stay or go. With strong Travel and an auspicious decade, leaving broadens your world; with Ji/malefics, pilot and prepare. Right direction and timing make relocation a springboard, not a drain.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to migration and relocation via Travel palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the migration and relocation via Travel palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
