Whether to Lend to a Friend: Purpose, Ability to Repay and What You Can Afford

Before lending to a friend, see whether the purpose is sound, whether they can repay, and their credit history, and ask whether you could afford not to be repaid. For large sums, prefer a written IOU with repayment terms and, where needed, a lawyer and a written contract. Help genuine emergencies, not endless dependency; if it exceeds what you can afford, decline gently.
