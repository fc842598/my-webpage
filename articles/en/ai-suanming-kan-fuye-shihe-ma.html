AI astrology says you should start a side hustle. Did it get your past right?

Before trusting an AI astrology reading about a side hustle, compare its claims with past freelance work or a small business. Keep the misses as well as the apparent hits.

AI Fortune-Telling Q&A2026-07-26 20:43Updated 2026-09-08 19:58 (UTC+8)

You have tried freelancing after work. Perhaps you opened an online shop and eventually closed it. Now an AI chart reading tells you that you were never meant to rely on one paycheck. That sounds encouraging. But what has it actually identified?

Your next venture has not happened yet. Your previous one has. Whether you found customers, received payment or stopped taking orders is something you can check.

Write down what happened before you ask

Keep a short private record of one past attempt: when you started, what you offered, whether anyone paid, and why you continued or stopped. You do not need to reveal every outcome in your opening question. If the AI already has your story, an explanation built around it is not an independent match.

Suppose you earned money from design commissions but gave them up because revisions consumed your evenings. A reading that says you are creative and sometimes tired is broad. One that mentions selling an existing skill and spending too much time on client changes gives you something more specific to compare. Even then, ask whether it is simply describing a common experience among freelance designers.

Now imagine a shop that never attracted regular orders. A claim that you earned plenty but could not hold on to the money does not match. Failure to make sales and spending money after earning it are different events.

Keep the mistakes in the record

Save the first answer. An incorrect year or an invented dispute with a business partner matters, even if another sentence feels accurate. An explanation produced after you correct the AI should be recorded separately from its original claim.

Being told you suit a side hustle is appealing when you are already considering one. A few apparent matches with your past do not establish predictive accuracy. That is especially important if earlier messages in the conversation already supplied the details.

Check that the chart evidence exists

For a Zi Wei Dou Shu reading, compare any named star and palace with the chart on screen. A fluent explanation does not make an absent placement real. The answer should also distinguish your natal chart from a ten-year or annual cycle. A general personality description is not, by itself, evidence about a particular year.

If the reading uses terms such as direct wealth or indirect wealth, ask which tradition it is using and what those labels mean. Calling all wages one category and all side income another does not explain why a specific activity would suit you.

After generating a chart on YuetianAI, you can start with a question about a past side project and compare the untouched answer with your own record. Look at what was actually claimed, not how neatly the AI can explain things once you supply the outcome. A chart reading should not substitute for evaluating the practical conditions of a new project.
