How to Read Tian Ji Hua Quan Across the Twelve Palaces in 2026

In the 2026 Bing Wu year, Tian Ji carries Hua Quan, governing taking charge within change, setting the pace, planning and adapting. Whichever palace it lands in calls on you to decide actively there, but the Quan also carries responsibility, and rushing it breeds anxiety.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-30

Tian Ji is already the wisdom star, adept at adapting, and with Hua Quan it is the year’s most planning-and-deciding energy: where change is greatest, someone must make the call, and that person may be you.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to read Tian Ji Hua Quan in each palace. First understand that this Quan is leadership within change, then see palace by palace where it turns you from a follower into the decision-maker.

The Underlying Logic

The tone of Tian Ji Hua Quan is taking charge within change: it governs planning, adjustment, decisions and technical leadership, so the landing palace is won through intellect and pacing.

In Life, Travel or Career: in Life it gives strategic decisiveness suited to advising; in Travel it gives adaptability and initiative away from home; in Career it means leading through expertise and planning, suiting technical management.

In Wealth, Property or Karma: in Wealth, earnings come through ideas and flexible allocation; in Property, home and assets are often adjusted on your own terms; in Karma the inner resolve is firm but prone to over-tight thinking.

In Spouse, Siblings, Parents, Children, Friends or Health: in relationship palaces you like to decide there and must guard being forceful; in Friends you lead teams through plans; in Health it indicates a wound-tight, thinking-driven body pattern, relying on proper medical care.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find on your chart which palace Tian Ji Hua Quan lands in
  2. According to that palace’s affairs, judge what leading within change concretely makes you responsible for
  3. Check the sharing major star and triple-direction to see whether the Quan has professional support
  4. Count malefics and Ji to weigh anxiety, wound-tight nerves and forceful leadership
  5. Use the Quan for planning first, then deciding, rather than interfering in everything
  6. With the month stream, take the lead in months that call for decisions and adjustment

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading Hua Quan only as promotion and ignoring responsibility and decisions within change

Mistake 2: Assuming authority means the final word without professional and team support

Mistake 3: Using the Quan forcefully, becoming domineering in relationships and over-tight in the body
