Why the San Qi Jia Hui Pattern Is Rare in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How Hard It Is for Lu, Quan and Ke to Converge

When Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke meet across the Life triple-direction it is San Qi Jia Hui—a complete structure of name and gain. Because the three come from different sources and Hua Ji must not break it, the bar is high.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

San Qi Jia Hui keeps resources, execution and reputation online at once—complete precisely because it is rare; most people hold only one or two.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People with one or two transformations claim San Qi, but all three must meet without Ji breaking them, and converging is genuinely uncommon.

The Underlying Logic

Lu supplies resources, Quan execution and Ke reputation; meeting across the Life trine closes the loop of obtaining, driving and being recognized.

The three may come from natal transformations or flying transformations of palace stems—different sources, but they must converge on one line.

The pattern also needs Ji kept from breaking a key position; otherwise San Qi has a gap and name-and-gain cannot both hold.

Even when formed, a decade must activate it; San Qi means completeness, not size—scale still depends on major and wealth stars.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate Lu, Quan and Ke: which stem each comes from and where it lands
  2. Confirm the three converge on one line across the Life triple-direction
  3. Check specifically whether Ji breaks any link
  4. Read major and wealth stars for scale, not just completeness
  5. Find the decade or year that activates San Qi, the window for name and gain together
  6. If one is missing, do not force it; strategize around the one or two actually present

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Claiming San Qi with only one or two transformations

Mistake 2: Seeing the three meet but not checking whether Ji breaks it

Mistake 3: Assuming the pattern guarantees great wealth while scale needs major and wealth stars

Back to Your Own Chart

San Qi Jia Hui is rare because it demands resources, execution and reputation together. Falling short is normal; seeing which pieces you have and strengthening the rest is more practical than envying the pattern.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the San Qi Jia Hui pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the San Qi Jia Hui pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
