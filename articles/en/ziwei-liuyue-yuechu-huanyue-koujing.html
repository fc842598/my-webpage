Zi Wei Dou Shu Monthly Charts: Why November Can Start on Different Dates

Compare month boundaries, chart inputs, and calculation rules before reading two monthly Zi Wei Dou Shu reports.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 09:00

Two Zi Wei Dou Shu tools can both label a report “November” while covering different date ranges. The calendar label is not enough to compare their monthly palaces. Start with the date of the real event and the boundary each tool uses.

Fix the date first

For a job interview early in the month, record the local date and time. One tool may still assign the event to its preceding monthly period. Check the displayed lunar date, birthplace settings, and time zone before treating the charts as a disagreement.

Separate calculation layers

If the monthly palace differs, compare the month progression rule. If palace placement matches but transformations differ, examine the transformation table and chart version. Keep natal, ten-year, annual, and monthly signals in separate notes. A Career Palace signal concerns the role; a Wealth Palace signal may concern compensation.

Use one stated boundary consistently when following events. If a tool does not disclose its date rule, mark that monthly reading as unverified rather than filling the gap with a generic prediction.
