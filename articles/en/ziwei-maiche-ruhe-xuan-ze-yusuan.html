How to Choose a Car and Set a Budget: Use and Total Cost of Ownership

For a car, first define the real use (commute, family, long trips), then calculate the total cost of ownership: price, purchase tax, insurance, fuel or electricity, maintenance, parking and depreciation, and keep the budget from squeezing emergency money and essential spending. Choose a reliable, value-holding car that meets the need within your means, not one driven by face or features. This is not investment advice.
