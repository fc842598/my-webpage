Malefics Aren't Scary: What's Scary Is Not Knowing What They Sharpen

Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Huo Xing, Ling Xing, Kong Jie are called malefics, but they're whetstones, not disaster stars. Used well they're drive and breakthrough; used poorly they're harm.

Methods2026-08-20 10:15

Tensing at Qing Yang/Tuo Luo, fearing Huo Xing/Ling Xing/Kong Jie — common beginner habit. But think: a knife is a cooking tool in a chef's hands, a weapon in a murderer's. Malefics are the same — not inherently good or bad; what matters is how you use them. Qing Yang is "drive," Tuo Luo is "resilience," Huo Xing is "explosiveness," Ling Xing is "delayed eruption," Kong Jie is "inspiration."

Malefics' essence is "extreme energy." Auspicious stars are gentle and balanced; malefics are intense and extreme. Extreme energy in the right place is talent — Qing Yang's decisiveness suits surgeons, Tuo Luo's resilience suits long-term research, Huo Xing's explosiveness suits entrepreneurship, Kong Jie's inspiration suits art. In the wrong place it's disaster.

The Positive Side of the Six Malefics

Qing Yang — decisive, brave, fast/precise/ruthless; suits military, police, surgeons.

Tuo Luo — resilient, patient, relentless; suits research, long-distance running, slow craftsmanship.

Huo Xing — explosive, action-oriented, jumps right in; suits entrepreneurship, sales, competition.

Ling Xing —隐忍, strategic, builds power in secret; suits strategy, negotiation, undercover work.

Di Kong — inspiration, intuition, out-of-the-box; suits art, philosophy, innovation.

Di Jie — risk-taking, break-and-build, fearless of loss; suits investment, pioneering, reform.

How to Use Malefics Well

Malefics in Life — tough personality with drive, suited to competitive industries.

Malefics in Career — hard work but breakthroughs, suited to trailblazing.

Malefics in Wealth — hard-earned but big money, suited to high-risk-high-reward.

Malefics plus auspicious stars — auspicious stars channel malefic energy, giving it direction.

Malefics plus Hua Lu — hard work pays off, "killing" a path to wealth.

When Malefics Are Scary

Uncontrolled malefics — no auspicious stars to channel, extreme energy runs wild, impulsive trouble.

Too many malefics — 3+ in one palace, energy too extreme, life with huge ups and downs.

Malefics in relationship palaces — in Spouse: frequent arguments; in Parents: conflict with elders.

Annual cycles activating malefics — be extra cautious when cycles pass through malefic positions.

Reading Order

When you see malefics:

  1. Which malefic — each has a different "extreme" direction.
  2. Which palace — what field does the extreme energy act on.
  3. Check auspicious stars — they guide malefic energy.
  4. Check Hua Lu/Hua Quan — they give malefic drive a payoff.
  5. Don't fear malefics — they're "whetstones," not "curses."
  6. Is your "malefic" driving you or harming you?
