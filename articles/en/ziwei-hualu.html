Hua Lu

A plain-English guide to Hua Lu, with a practical reading order, simple examples, and clear boundaries for Zi Wei Dou Shu learners.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-01 08:43

Hua Lu is a practical Zi Wei Dou Shu topic. Start with the relevant palace, then read the opposite palace, supporting structure, and current timing.

What This Means

Hua Lu shows where resources, help, comfort, or income can flow in. It is not only a money sign; the key is to ask where the resource comes from, how it enters real life, and whether it can be kept.

How To Read It

Read the palace and star receiving Hua Lu, then check the opposite palace and the career or wealth structure. A good Hua Lu pattern should show both a source of support and a practical channel for receiving it.

Simple Examples

  • If Hua Lu supports wealth, check the income channel, payment timing, and whether cash flow can be retained.
  • If Hua Lu supports career, resources may come through role, organization, client access, or a better work platform.
  • If Hua Lu supports relationships or travel, help may arrive through networks, referrals, or outside environments.

Practical Order

First define the resource being asked about. Then read the Hua Lu palace, its source, the opposite palace, and the current cycle. The reading should explain where support enters and what must be done to hold it.
