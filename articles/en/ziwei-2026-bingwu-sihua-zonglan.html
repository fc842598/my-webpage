What This Question Is Really Asking

The year stem Bing gives the set Bing Tong Ji Chang Lian: Tian-Tong Lu, Tian-Ji Quan, Wen-Chang Ke and Lian-Zhen Ji. Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji each govern a slice; Ji marks the year's lesson, not a verdict on the whole year.

The Underlying Logic

Tian-Tong Lu lands on the blessing star, so the year favors comfort, networks, mood and goodwill, easing relations and cooperation.

Tian-Ji Quan governs planning and change with control; those who move, revise and set the pace deliver more this year.

Wen-Chang Ke favors documents, exams, credentials and professional expression; reputation and formal papers are a plus.

Lian-Zhen Ji is the year's lesson in relations, ethics, compliance and minor physical or electronic wear; a focus point, not a full-year curse.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate in your natal chart which palaces hold the four Bing-year stars
  2. Where Tian-Tong Lu lands, see which area has the smoothest resources and goodwill
  3. Where Tian-Ji Quan lands, see which area needs you to drive and set pace
  4. Where Wen-Chang Ke lands, see where documents, exams and reputation pay
  5. Then where Lian-Zhen Ji lands, see which area needs careful handling
  6. Read all four together to find where to push and where to hold; do not fixate on Ji

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Searching only Lian-Zhen Ji and bracing for disaster while ignoring Lu-Quan-Ke opportunity

Mistake 2: Reading the four layers in isolation without seeing how their palaces echo

Mistake 3: Applying the year transformations alone without the natal and decade sets
