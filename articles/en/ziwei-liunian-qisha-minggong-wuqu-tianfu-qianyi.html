Qi Sha in the Annual Life Palace, Wu Qu and Tian Fu Outside

How to read an annual Qi Sha Life Palace with Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Outside Palace by separating initiative from resource support.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:57

Initiative and Support

Qi Sha in the annual Life Palace can push a person to act quickly. Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Outside Palace describe the rules and resources that may support the move.

Avoid Simple Good or Bad

Qi Sha is not automatically destructive, and Wu Qu Tian Fu is not an automatic wealth promise. The relationship between the palaces matters.

Check Career and Wealth

Career shows whether the change becomes a role or project. Wealth shows whether the reward actually arrives and can be kept.

Examples

With Outside Wu Qu Hua Quan, ask for budget and authority before taking over a difficult project. With Outside Tian Fu Hua Ke, use credentials and process to enter a platform.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
