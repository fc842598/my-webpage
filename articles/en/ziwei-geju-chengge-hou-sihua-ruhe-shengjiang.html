After a Pattern Forms in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How Transformations Raise or Lower It

A pattern is not a fixed grade. Meeting Lu, Quan or Ke raises it and brings it to fruition, while Ji or Empty lowers and discounts it. The same pattern rises and falls with transformations across decades and years, so read it dynamically.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

Many treat a pattern as a fixed grade that, once formed, stays secure for life. It is more like a base that transformations raise or lower as they move.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Transformations are the raise-lower switch: Lu, Quan and Ke activating the relevant palaces realize potential, while Ji or Empty clashing discounts what formed. Check where the current decade and year transformations land.

The Underlying Logic

A pattern gives innate structural potential, and transformations decide whether it pays out in a given stage.

Lu, Quan or Ke activating relevant palaces raises the pattern and realizes its benefit.

Ji, Empty or malefics clashing lower the formed area and discount its breakout.

The same pattern rises and falls across decades and years and must be judged dynamically with the timing layers.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the pattern itself forms and where its potential lies
  2. Read the active decade transformations and palaces
  3. If Lu, Quan or Ke activate relevant palaces, look for raised, realized benefit
  4. If Ji or Empty clashes, guard against a lowered, discounted result
  5. Read the yearly transformations for the current year's raise-lower trigger
  6. Assess the current grade dynamically rather than treating the pattern as fixed

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating a pattern as a fixed grade that stays secure once formed

Mistake 2: Ignoring how transformations raise and lower it

Mistake 3: Looking only at the innate chart and missing the payout period or risk

Back to Your Own Chart

The pattern is the base and transformations the switch. A good base still needs timing; see which way the current transformations turn to know when the strong hand pays out.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to how transformations raise or lower a formed pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the how transformations raise or lower a formed pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
