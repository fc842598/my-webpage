How to Read the Yearly Trine and Transformations in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Do not read a year by staring at the yearly Life Palace alone; take the yearly Wealth, Career and Travel palaces with the yearly transformations. The Life Palace alone exaggerates the year, while reading the trine shows the full direction.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

Many check a year by only its Life star, overjoyed or crushed, but the year's events spread across the trine and one palace cannot show them all.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The right way: the yearly Life sets the tone, then open the trine to see how Wealth, Career and Travel are each activated by stars and transformations; together they make the full year.

The Underlying Logic

The yearly Life sets the tone and axis.

The yearly trine governs the year's specific areas of wealth, career and travel.

The yearly transformations mark where resources, control, reputation and lessons land.

Reading them together keeps one palace's fortune from being exaggerated.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Set the yearly Life Palace and star as the tone
  2. Cast the yearly trine palaces
  3. Mark where each yearly transformation lands
  4. Read yearly Wealth activation: income and spending
  5. Read yearly Career and Travel activation: work and movement
  6. Combine the trine and transformations for a balanced yearly reading

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the yearly Life and judging the year from one palace

Mistake 2: Being overjoyed or crushed by the amplified single palace

Mistake 3: Ignoring the yearly trine and transformations so the full direction is missed

Back to Your Own Chart

A year is a complete small chart, not one star's solo. The Life sets the tone, the trine unfolds and transformations mark the theme; read together and the year is seen accurately and steadily.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to reading the yearly trine and transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the reading the yearly trine and transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
