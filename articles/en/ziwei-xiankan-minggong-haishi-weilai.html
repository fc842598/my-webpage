In Zi Wei Dou Shu, Do You Read the Life Palace or the Future First?

However eager you are about the future, first fix the Life Palace and its triple-direction. The yearly answer is only a timing layer on the natal foundation, and skipping it invites misreading.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

I only want to know whether I will be promoted this year is the most common opening. But this year you are still the natal you; without the foundation, yearly keywords have nowhere to sit.

What This Question Is Really Asking

On the surface this asks about order, but really it asks whether a shortcut exists. It does not. The future does not replace the natal chart; it layers on top, and a weak foundation makes a busy upper layer hollow.

The Underlying Logic

The Life Palace and triple-direction are the static foundation, answering who you are and what cards you hold.

The decade and year are dynamic timing layers, answering when those cards are pushed forward.

The layers add rather than replace; the year never erases the natal structure.

Asking only about the future without the foundation is like decorating a floor before checking the foundation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Cast the natal chart and set the Life Palace star and brightness
  2. Open the triple-direction to confirm the Wealth, Career and Travel structure
  3. Read the natal transformations to set the baseline
  4. Once the foundation is solid, layer the decade for the ten-year background
  5. Then read the year to set this year's trigger
  6. Finally answer in conditional terms rather than an absolute yes or no

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the year as an independent chart detached from the natal

Mistake 2: Assuming a yearly Hua Lu guarantees wealth without checking natal capacity

Mistake 3: Wanting a shortcut to the result instead of first reading the foundation

Back to Your Own Chart

Foundation first and future later is not a detour but the only viable path. Without understanding the base, any number of future claims are rootless guesses.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to whether to read the Life Palace or the future first before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the whether to read the Life Palace or the future first line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
