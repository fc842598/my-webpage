Which City and Direction to Develop in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Travel Palace, Tian Ma and Direction

Whether to leave home, which direction, big city or small, mainly reads Travel (going out and the social stage), the Life-Travel axis (whether you flourish locally or away) and Tian Ma (the drive to move), plus star five-element correspondence with direction. It gives a tendency of smoothness and environment type; concrete city choice still weighs jobs, family and real costs.

Some thrive the moment they leave home, others flourish locally; the Life-Travel axis and Travel palace directly show the soil in which you unfold.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether to go to a top-tier city, return home or stay away, and which direction favors you, Travel, Tian Ma and Life-Travel strength give a clear tendency.

The Underlying Logic

The Life-Travel axis sets local versus away: strong Life with weak Travel is steadier in familiar settings and native networks, struggling away; an exalted Travel with Lu/Quan/Ke and grounded stars meets patrons and a bigger stage away, flourishing the farther out—the classic "a moved tree dies, a moved person thrives"; strong in both works anywhere opportunities are greatest.

Tian Ma sets the drive to move: Tian Ma in Travel/Career/Wealth when activated suits the naturally mobile—cross-region work, business travel, expatriate and cross-border roles; Tian Ma meeting Lu Cun/Hua Lu is "Lu-Ma exchange," gaining through movement; Tian Ma with Kong/Jie/malefics makes movement tiring with little result, so change cities carefully with housing and work secured first.

Star five elements set direction and environment type: fire stars favor the south, bright, lively, fast-paced settings; water stars the north, fluid, water-adjacent trade/media places; wood stars the east, growth/education/culture zones; metal stars the west, rule-based finance/machinery belts; earth stars the center, stable real-estate/platform cities—a tendency reference, not a compass that dictates the city.

Decisions combine tendency with reality: years when Travel is strong, Tian Ma active and decade/year activate Travel are favorable windows to relocate, expatriate or develop away; strong Travel with steady Property and heavy family ties weighs opportunity cost; whichever direction, secure a concrete offer or steady income before moving—the chart gives direction, landing still takes planning and cost accounting.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Life versus Travel strength to tell local from away-from-home flourishing
  2. Read Tian Ma's palace and whether Lu-Ma exchange occurs to assess gain versus奔波 from moving
  3. Use star five elements to fix the smoother direction and city-environment type
  4. Read Property and family ties to weigh the opportunity cost of leaving
  5. Pick decade/year windows that activate Travel for relocation or expatriation
  6. Secure an offer or steady income before moving; count real costs alongside tendency

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Blindly chasing big cities by opportunity count without checking Travel fit for leaving home

Mistake 2: Treating five-element direction as a fixed verdict and using a compass to pick a city, ignoring real jobs

Mistake 3: Repeatedly quitting and moving with Tian Ma-Kong/Jie, ending in tiring movement with little result

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to which city and directional region to develop in before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the which city and directional region to develop in line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
