Many Malefic Stars in Your Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart? Do Not Just Count Them

Check display settings and relevant palace relationships before treating the number of unfavorable star names as a judgment of your life.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07 17:21

A full chart may display Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Huo Xing, Ling Xing, Di Kong, and Di Jie across different palaces. Another screenshot may show only the Life Palace. Counting the visible names in those two images tells you little.

Concentration matters to the traditional reading

Readers examine which stars connect to the palace under discussion and how they interact with its major stars. A group concentrated around one subject is not equivalent to the same count scattered elsewhere.

The names also carry different traditional meanings. Friction, delay, and loss of an expected result should not become identical deductions from a fictional life score.

A combination still needs conditions

Huo Xing and Tan Lang have a recognized traditional combination, but merely spotting both names does not promise a windfall. Likewise, Qing Yang does not establish an illness or an inevitable accident. Specific serious claims require real-world evidence, not a symbol alone.

Before accepting a frightening reading, identify the palace, the natal or annual layer, and the companion stars. If the analysis consists only of many bad stars means a bad life, it has skipped the relevant distinctions. Unify the display settings, take a break from alarming interpretations, and assess the actual circumstances you are dealing with.
