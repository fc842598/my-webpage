Reading Distant and Foreign Development Through the Zi Wei Dou Shu Travel Palace

The Travel Palace, opposite the Life Palace, governs going out, distant and foreign places, the public stage and how others see you. Auspicious, strong stars there mean smooth travel, ties to far places and a fit for moving outward, while heavy malefics bring friction. To judge whether to leave home, relocate or go abroad, read Travel first, then combine Life and the decade.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some wonder whether to leave home for another region while staring at the Life Palace; the outer circumstance is written in Travel, which answers the question.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is whether you fit moving outward and whether opportunity waits afar. Read the Travel stars and transformations for smoothness, then see whether the decade activates Travel and whether Life is willing to move.

The Underlying Logic

Travel governs movement and the outer: trips, relocation, far places and public evaluation.

Exalted Travel stars with auspicious meetings mean help outside, a larger stage and a fit for outward development.

Malefics or Ji in Travel mean friction, blocks or disputes away from home, eased by choosing place and timing.

Travel opposes Life: the home is inner and Travel outer; read both to know whether home or away is better.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate Travel and its major stars, borrowing Life stars if empty
  2. Read the Travel brightness and triple-direction fortune
  3. Count its assistants, malefics and transformations
  4. Auspicious and strong means smooth travel and far-place ties
  5. Heavy malefics indicate friction, requiring care with place and timing
  6. Then combine Life and the current decade to decide whether to move outward

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging whether to leave from Life alone without Travel

Mistake 2: Seeing a Travel malefic and declaring no travel, without resolution and place

Mistake 3: Treating Travel as only trips and ignoring the public stage and far development

Back to Your Own Chart

Whether opportunity waits afar is read in Travel: auspicious strong stars mean smooth outer paths and far-place ties, while heavy malefics bring friction. Combine Life and the decade to judge steadily whether to stay home or move outward.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Travel Palace and development in distant or foreign places before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Travel Palace and development in distant or foreign places line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
