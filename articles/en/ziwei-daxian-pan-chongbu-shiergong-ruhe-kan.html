How to Rebuild the Twelve Palaces on the Decade Chart

When you enter a decade, use its Life Palace as the new origin and rebuild the twelve palaces in direction for those ten years, then add the decade transformations. Palaces are relationships, not place names; this is the ten-year lens, layered separately from the annual chart.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

Rebuilding the decade chart shifts the lens from the natal me to the me of these ten years; once the coordinate system changes, the roles of the twelve palaces are re-arranged.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some keep using natal palace names when reading a decade and everything misaligns; others merge the decade and annual charts into one layer and lose the hierarchy.

The Underlying Logic

The decade Life Palace is the new Tai Ji of these ten years; use it as origin to rebuild the twelve palaces in direction.

After rebuilding, drop the natal palace names; palaces express relative relationships, not fixed place names.

Add the decade transformations to see which palace governs work, money and relationships in these years.

The decade chart is the ten-year lens; layering the annual chart narrows events to a specific year.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First identify the current decade and the position of its Life Palace
  2. Use the decade Life Palace as Tai Ji and rebuild the twelve palaces in the yin-yang gender direction
  3. Read each palace star combination meaning across these ten years
  4. Add the decade transformations to set the energy switches for the period
  5. Identify which palace governs key areas such as career, money and relationships
  6. Then rebuild the annual chart to narrow the ten-year reading to a year

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Keeping natal palace names when reading the decade so nothing aligns

Mistake 2: Merging decade and annual charts into one layer and losing the hierarchy

Mistake 3: Reading only the decade Life Palace without rebuilding the other eleven palaces

Back to Your Own Chart

Rebuilding the decade chart is learning to see yourself through the coordinate system of these ten years. Rebuild the palaces, add transformations, then layer the year, and you read the stage main line accurately.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to rebuilding the twelve palaces on the decade chart before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the rebuilding the twelve palaces on the decade chart line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
