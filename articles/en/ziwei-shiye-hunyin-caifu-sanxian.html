How to Read Career, Marriage and Wealth as Three Linked Lines Instead of One Palace

The Career, Spouse and Wealth palaces affect one another: marriage shapes energy allocation, career shapes income, and wealth shapes family choices. Reading the three lines explains real trade-offs in life.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

A person is not split into independent boxes of career, relationship and money. Choosing to push career squeezes the relationship; marrying changes the logic of spending and property, and these trade-offs are written in the linkage.

What This Question Is Really Asking

One-palace reading cannot explain why a busy career strains marriage or why buying a home tightens cash. The answer sits where three lines cross, and the palaces must be read connected.

The Underlying Logic

The Career Palace writes where energy goes and income comes from.

The Spouse Palace writes how the relationship pulls energy and decisions.

The Wealth Palace writes how money supports or limits the first two.

The three-line linkage forms real trade-offs, and one-palace reading is necessarily one-sided.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First set the major stars of the Career, Spouse and Wealth palaces
  2. Read the career line: energy direction and income source
  3. Read the marriage line: how the relationship pulls decisions
  4. Read the wealth line: whether money supports or limits choices
  5. Find where the three palaces cross, identifying squeeze or support
  6. Use the decade to judge which line is central in the current stage

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating career, marriage and money as unrelated matters

Mistake 2: Reading one palace and unable to explain chain reactions in life

Mistake 3: Not ordering the three lines and missing the current stage's focus

Back to Your Own Chart

A life is a linked network, not three single-choice questions. Learn to read the three lines together and you understand where your win-some-lose-some moments really come from.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the three-line linkage of career, marriage and wealth before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the three-line linkage of career, marriage and wealth line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
