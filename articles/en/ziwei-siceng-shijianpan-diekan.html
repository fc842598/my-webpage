Natal, Decade, Year and Month: How to Stack the Four Timing Layers

The four-layer model runs from the natal foundation through the decade stage, the year and the month. The year cannot be explained apart from the natal and decade; each layer answers one question and uses trend language.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Skilled readers hold four transparent charts and stack them. The natal says what you have, the decade what these years perform, the year where things move now, and the month the current rhythm, each finer than the last.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Avoid two extremes: judging from one yearly palace alone, and piling all four layers until the point is lost. The trick is having each layer answer one question and expressing conditions and trends rather than saying something must happen.

The Underlying Logic

The natal decides the structure that can be carried, the foundation of all four.

The decade decides the ten-year theme, and the year handles that year's progress.

The month subdivides the yearly theme into the current rhythm and triggers.

Trend language fits the verifiable boundary better than certainty; no guarantee is given without a concrete chart.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the natal, answering only what structure I have
  2. Second read the decade, answering only what this ten-year theme is
  3. Third read the year, answering only where the theme moves this year
  4. Fourth read the month, answering only the current rhythm and triggers
  5. Check whether any layer oversteps into another layer's question
  6. Use conditional trend language throughout and organize it into a flow from structure to timing

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading one yearly palace detached from the natal and decade

Mistake 2: Piling all four layers and missing that each answers one question

Mistake 3: Using it must happen language beyond the verifiable boundary

Back to Your Own Chart

Stacking four layers does not complicate information but places structure and timing correctly. Foundation, decade, year and month progress in order, giving the full story of any matter.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to stacking the four layers of timing charts before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the stacking the four layers of timing charts line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
