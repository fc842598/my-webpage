Monthly Relationship Palace: Why One Month Cannot Define a Partnership

Read natal, long term, annual, and monthly relationship layers separately, then examine the actual decision and shared responsibilities.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-04 21:23

A monthly focus on the Relationship Palace cannot tell you whether a partnership is ending. It may point to a current decision, while a repeated conflict may belong to a longer pattern. Read the natal chart, longer cycle, annual context, and monthly trigger in that order, then check what actually happened between the people involved.

Separate a schedule problem from a pattern

Two partners caring for an older relative may argue because both have overtime this month. List hospital visits, transport, costs, and backup help. If one person has carried the work for years, discuss that longer imbalance separately. The monthly schedule alone will not settle it.

Define the housing decision

A couple planning to rent together may need to decide the deposit and commute now, while shared accounts and exit terms require a longer conversation. Property and Wealth add useful questions. If an argument centers on wording, compare the actual messages and draft agreement. A transformation does not prove that one person is inherently difficult.

Keep the decision with the people

Hua Lu does not guarantee reconciliation, and Hua Ji does not guarantee separation. Locate the transformation and its time layer. Record the facts, each person's needs, the next action, and a review date. Use the chart as a prompt for a focused conversation, not as a verdict on the relationship.
