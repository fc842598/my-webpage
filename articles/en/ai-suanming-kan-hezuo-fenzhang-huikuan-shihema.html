Questions about cooperation splits and repayment should not stay at 'can we work together?' The real risk often lives in how money moves.

Once you put the money line on the table, the question becomes much more concrete.

What matters first

Start with wealth and cash-flow lines first.

Then read partner, role, and rule structure.

Zi Wei style questions work better when life pattern, wealth, career, outside platform, and timing are read together instead of being flattened into one slogan.

The more useful answer usually explains why it is reading the chart that way, what to verify next, and where not to over-claim.

A practical scenario

People can get along well and still have weak repayment structure.

A year with active money issues makes split and repayment questions even more important.

When a reading lands on chart structure, a real-life scenario, and a next verification step at the same time, it becomes much easier to judge whether it is actually useful.

The boundary to remember

AI can expose risk structure, but it cannot replace contracts or accounting.

Relationship comfort alone is a poor screen for cooperation money risk.

Whether the layer is free or paid, a safer product lets you verify in a small scope before asking for deeper commitment.

A safer order

  1. Reframe the issue as a split-and-repayment question.
  2. Check rule and exit structure next.
  3. Only then decide whether to proceed.
