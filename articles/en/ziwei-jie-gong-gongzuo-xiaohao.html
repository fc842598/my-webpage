What the Health Palace Can Show About Work Drain in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Learn how to read the Health Palace as a work-drain signal by separating body load, emotional load, and financial leakage.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:03

Three Kinds of Drain

The Health Palace can point to a recurring load, but you still need to separate physical fatigue, emotional fatigue, and money leakage.

Use Career and Wealth as Checks

Career shows the duty being carried. Wealth shows what the duty returns. Fu De shows the mental cost of carrying it.

A Safer Boundary

Chart language can suggest a routine to inspect. It cannot diagnose an illness. Use checkups, records, and professional support for the actual decision.

Practical Reading

Name the duty, measure the cost, and then see whether the annual trigger asks for rest, renegotiation, or a different income structure.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
