Hong Luan vs Tian Xi: Romance and Celebration Stars

Hong Luan governs romance, marriage movement and relationship joy; Tian Xi governs celebration, children and relationships. The two always sit six positions apart in opposition. When activated, they often correspond to marriage or birth, but still read natal Spouse and Children Palaces.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Hong Luan is like blossoms opening, stirring feelings; Tian Xi is like joy arriving with a new child; one leans romance, one celebration.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Hong Luan and assume marriage, Tian Xi and assume birth, but they are only joy signals; whether it happens depends on natal and reality.

The Underlying Logic

Hong Luan governs romance and marriage movement, Tian Xi celebration and children.

The two always sit in opposition six apart.

When met in a year or decade, they often correspond to relationship or birth joy.

Combine natal Spouse and Children Palaces; romance stars alone are insufficient.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find Hong Luan and Tian Xi palaces
  2. Distinguish Hong Luan (romance) from Tian Xi (celebration)
  3. See whether the year or decade activates them
  4. Combine natal Spouse Palace for romance
  5. Combine natal Children Palace for birth
  6. Take signals as reference; real effort and bonds decide

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming Hong Luan means marriage and Tian Xi means birth

Mistake 2: Not distinguishing the two

Mistake 3: Seeing only romance stars without natal Spouse and Children

Back to Your Own Chart

Hong Luan vs Tian Xi read how romance and celebration signals compare. Distinguish them and combine natal palaces, and joy signals are read accurately, not exaggerated.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the difference between Hong Luan and Tian Xi before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the difference between Hong Luan and Tian Xi line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
