How to Read Tian Tong Hua Lu Across the Twelve Palaces in 2026

In the 2026 Bing Wu year, Tian Tong carries Hua Lu, governing blessings, harmony and resources that come through goodwill. Whichever palace it lands in finds goodwill and cooperation more readily, but the Lu is an opportunity, not a free ride; you still have to receive it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-30

Tian Tong is already the blessing star, and with Hua Lu it is the gentlest energy of the year: help arrives readily and relationships come together. The key is first finding on your chart which palace it lands in, then judging which matter this blessing covers.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what Tian Tong Hua Lu means in each palace. First understand that this Lu is blessings and harmony, then read palace by palace; it cannot be read as a simple wealth star.

The Underlying Logic

The tone of Tian Tong Hua Lu is blessing: it governs enjoyment, care, human warmth and goodwill, so matters in the landing palace advance in a relaxed, congenial atmosphere.

In Life, Wealth or Career: in Life it gives lifelong congeniality and an open mood; in Wealth, earnings come through harmony and reputation; in Career, colleagues and superiors are supportive, suiting service and coordination roles.

In Spouse, Siblings, Parents or Children: in relationship palaces, help comes through family warmth, with partners, siblings, elders and juniors as supports, while guarding against warmth turning into dependence.

In Travel, Friends, Property, Karma or Health: Travel and Friends bring helpful strangers and kind companions; Property gives a stable home; Karma gives contentment; Health gives care during illness and support in recovery, still relying on proper medical treatment.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find on your chart which palace Tian Tong Hua Lu lands in
  2. According to that palace’s affairs, judge how the blessings and harmony concretely appear
  3. Check the sharing major star and triple-direction to see whether the Lu can be received steadily
  4. Count malefics and Ji to assess whether warmth may become dependence or mere promises
  5. Actively take the opportunity the Lu gives—speak up, act and confirm—rather than waiting
  6. With the month stream, advance key cooperation and requests in the months when harmony is strongest

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading Tian Tong Hua Lu only as wealth and ignoring blessings and harmony

Mistake 2: Assuming the Lu means no action is needed and failing to receive the opportunity

Mistake 3: Treating goodwill as permanent support and ignoring that relationships need care and reciprocity

Back to Your Own Chart

Tian Tong Hua Lu in a palace gives goodwill, cooperation and chances to be cared for. Locate the palace and actively receive it; this year you more readily meet people willing to help in that very matter.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Tian Tong Hua Lu across the twelve palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Tian Tong Hua Lu across the twelve palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
