Annual Friends Palace Activation: Team Changes, Client Renewals, and Handoffs

When the annual Life Palace reaches the natal Friends Palace, team turnover, client renewal terms, and handoff responsibility often become the practical questions to verify.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-08-05 20:13

When the annual Life Palace reaches the natal Friends Palace, the year does not have to begin with making new friends. The first visible change may be a teammate leaving, a client replacing its contact, or an established account returning with different renewal terms.

The useful question is operational rather than social: who now owns the work, who can approve it, who accepts delivery, and which promises still exist in writing? Those records show whether the collaboration has actually continued after the people around it changed.

Start with the working relationship

This placement directs attention toward coworkers, project partners, vendors, channels, and client contacts. Read the natal stars and transformations already in the Friends Palace, then identify which relationship carries a real task. The placement sets the field of attention; it does not promise a breakup, a new contract, or a fixed outcome.

Build one timeline from staffing notices, customer emails, project schedules, and contract dates. The first change that continues to affect delivery is usually more useful than a broad judgment that relationships are simply good or difficult.

A staffing change requires a new handoff

A departure matters less than the work left behind. Tian Ji and Tian Ma can support a reading focused on movement, role changes, and shifting coordination, but the actual notice and schedule determine whether a replacement has occurred. Check account access, decision rights, unresolved tasks, and the new deadline owner.

Example 1: The annual Life Palace reaches the natal Friends Palace with Tian Ji and Tian Ma; the client replaces its project lead and the work becomes cross-city. Rewrite the handoff with named contacts, approvers, deliverables, and deadlines instead of assuming the old arrangement still applies.

A renewal must restate the working terms

A client saying yes does not automatically preserve the old arrangement. Hua Lu supported by Zuo Fu and You Bi can be read as available resources and helpful coordination, while the contract still has to define scope, price, payment milestones, acceptance authority, and duration.

Example 2: The annual Life Palace reaches the natal Friends Palace, where Hua Lu represents partnership resources and Zuo Fu with You Bi represents team assistance and role coverage. Treat the renewal as confirmed only when the contract states the scope, term, and person authorized to accept the work.

Shorten commitments when responsibility is disputed

Ju Men Hua Ji with Qing Yang can direct attention to disputed language and sharper boundary conflicts. It does not prove that the partnership must fail. Reduce the commitment period, limit access, name the final approver, and retain meeting notes before increasing scope or authority.

Example 3: The annual Life Palace reaches the natal Friends Palace with Ju Men Hua Ji and Qing Yang; after a staffing change, pricing and responsibility boundaries become disputed. Use a limited-term renewal, keep meeting records, and preserve an exit clause before expanding authority.

Practical Reading Order

First confirm that the annual Life Palace reaches the natal Friends Palace, then read the natal stars, transformations, and connected palaces. List team members, partners, vendors, and client contacts separately. Compare the dates of staffing notices, handoff records, renewal drafts, and signed agreements. Treat the renewal as confirmed only when the contract states the scope, term, and person authorized to accept the work. Finish by checking account access, approval authority, who has authority to accept the deliverables, payment milestones, and exit terms before deciding whether the collaboration is continuing, being renegotiated, or temporarily reduced.
