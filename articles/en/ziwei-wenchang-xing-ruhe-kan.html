The Wen Chang Star Overview: Documents, Exams and Reason

Wen Chang governs documents, exams, reason and contracts, the star of scholarly distinction. With Hua Ke its literary name shows and favors exams and signing; fallen or with malefics, guard document errors and contract disputes. It gives order and writing, not ready distinction.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

Wen Chang is like a rigorous scribe with clear order and precise writing; but documents need correct content to avoid trouble.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Wen Chang and assume exam passage, but it needs Ke activation and effort; the star gives state, not answers.

The Underlying Logic

Mistake 2: Not guarding document errors
