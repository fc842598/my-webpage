Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Travel Palace: Bold and Change-Ready Away From Home, Turning Execution and Breaking-Rebuilding Into a Grounded Path Elsewhere

The Travel Palace reads the external environment, development elsewhere and the state away from home. Wu Qu governs firm execution and skill while Po Jun governs change, depletion and rebuilding, so together in Si or Hai one is bold and pioneering away from home, gaining a foothold through skill and change, suiting pioneering, transformation and storming elsewhere. The risk is travel, large change, depletion and conflict, so the key is making change planned with a way back and cash flow.

People with Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Travel Palace are bold away from home, daring to take hard work and often winning credit in pioneering elsewhere; yet when change is fierce and no base is kept, they place themselves in repeated travel and start-over situations.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the away-from-home style with Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Travel Palace. Understand the palace reads the external environment, then see how execution and change combine so one dares to pioneer while keeping the bottom line.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Travel Palace makes one rely on skill, execution and real ability away from home, willing to work hard, yet also blunt and slow to adapt.

Po Jun there gives change away from home, daring to break and rebuild and succeeding after breaking with strong pioneering force, yet also travel, depletion and change.

Together they mean wealth plus change: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars under planning pioneering elsewhere becomes real results; with malefics and Ji, conflict, depletion and setbacks from forcing through follow.

The lesson is breaking with planning and rebuilding with a way back: advance change in nodes, keep cash flow and a way back and build networks elsewhere actively.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Po Jun share the Travel Palace and check their brightness
  2. Advance change and pioneering in nodes rather than overturning all at once
  3. Keep cash flow and a way back to avoid runaway travel and depletion
  4. Build networks away from home rather than fighting alone
  5. Weigh risk in major decisions to avoid forcing through
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of travel activation and pioneering

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Po Jun in the Travel Palace only as bold and pioneering while ignoring travel and depletion

Mistake 2: Change too fierce with no way back or cash flow, repeatedly starting over

Mistake 3: Fighting alone and forcing through, narrowing the path elsewhere
