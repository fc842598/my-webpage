Tian Guan in the Health Palace: Steady Care and Follow-Up

Tian Guan in the Health Palace suggests a fairly steady constitution and slower illness, yet one cares about face and may avoid doctors or tough it out. Set hesitation aside, care properly and keep chronic follow-up on time, not delaying for face.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-11

Illness spares no one and ignores dignity; standing on ceremony before the doctor only harms your own body.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some delay checks out of trouble-aversion and face and worsen; stars are tendency, while health ills need proper care and regular follow-up.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Guan in Health means a steady constitution, slower illness.

Prone to face, avoiding care or toughing it out.

This is tendency not diagnosis; rely on formal medical care.

  1. Set face aside, seek timely care
  2. Care properly, follow orders
  3. Follow up chronic ills, do not delay
  4. Keep regular routines and diet
  5. Do not avoid doctors
  6. Follow the doctor plan

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 2: Toughing out checks, worsening

Mistake 3: Skipping chronic follow-up
