How to Upgrade from One Palace to Reading the Four Palaces Together

The triple-direction joins the home palace, its opposite and two trine palaces. One palace gives only partial meaning; the four together form a complete topic, with transformations as the driving force.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Judging from one palace and one star is the classic beginner error. The home palace is center stage, but the opposite writes the pull and the trines write resources and execution; miss one and the play is incomplete.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People ask whether Zi Wei in the Life Palace is enough, but even the strongest star depends on whether the four palaces support it or send malefics. The verdict lies in the net effect of four positions, not one star.

The Underlying Logic

The home palace writes the core, the opposite writes the pull, and the two trine palaces write resources and execution; together they are the triple-direction.

Auspicious meetings mean support and malefic clashes mean breaks; strength is the net effect rather than one star.

The triple-direction is also the basic unit for judging whether a pattern forms.

Transformations layer onto the four-palace structure to show where force moves, never flying free of it.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Lock the palace you ask about as the home palace
  2. Find the opposite palace and see how it pulls the home palace
  3. Follow the trine to the other two palaces for resources and execution
  4. List the major stars in all four, then count assistant and malefic stars
  5. See where transformations land among the four to set the force direction
  6. Combine the four for the net effect before judging the palace solid or hollow

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fixating on one star and ignoring the opposite and trines

Mistake 2: Judging one malefic as bad without checking whether auspicious stars resolve it

Mistake 3: Knowing the rhyme but never actually drawing the lines on a real chart

Back to Your Own Chart

Mastering the four-palace reading moves you from one star to a relationship network, the first threshold between memorizing stars and truly reading a chart.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to reading the four palaces of the triple-direction together before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the reading the four palaces of the triple-direction together line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
