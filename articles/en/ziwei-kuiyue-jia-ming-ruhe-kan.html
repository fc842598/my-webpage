Kui Yue Flanking the Life Palace: Close Mentors

Tian Kui (yang mentor) and Tian Yue (yin mentor) guard the two flanking palaces around Life, bringing close mentors and help in need. The pattern needs no malefic break; mentors help the prepared, and one own ability remains the root.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

This pattern is like having mentors on either side, help when in need; but mentors show the way, you still walk it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Kui Yue flanking and assume help everywhere without effort, and even mentors cannot lift them; mentors are support, not replacement.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Kui yang mentor and Tian Yue yin mentor flanking Life bring close mentors.

They bring help in need, relief in trouble and introductions to opportunity.

The flanking palaces must have no malefic or Ji break.

Mentors help the prepared and able; one own effort is the root.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Kui Yue flank the Life Palace
  2. Read their brightness
  3. Count malefics and Ji for breaks
  4. Read the stars they attach to for mentor type
  5. Combine your own ability to judge whether mentors can help
  6. Layer decade and year for when mentors appear

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing Kui Yue flanking and assuming help without effort

Mistake 2: Not checking malefic or Ji breaks

Mistake 3: Taking mentors as replacement, ignoring own ability

Back to Your Own Chart

This pattern asks whether close mentors find you worth helping. No malefic break, ability and effort, and mentor support truly lands.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Kui Yue flanking the Life Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Kui Yue flanking the Life Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
