What to Do if Your Partner Hid Debt: Verify and Discuss Honesty

If you find your partner hid debt before marriage, calmly verify the amount and nature (such as gambling or high-interest loans) and whether it could involve you, then communicate honestly and see whether the person is willing to disclose fully and handle it together. Marriage also involves financial trust; take significant concealment or bad debt seriously, consult a lawyer when needed and consider a prenuptial agreement.
