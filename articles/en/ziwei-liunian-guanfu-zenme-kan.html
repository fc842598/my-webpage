A Guan Fu Year: When Contracts and Rules Multiply, Holding to Paperwork and Compliance

Guan Fu governs rules, constraints, contracts, systems and official pressure. In a Guan Fu year, signing, approvals, compliance and duty-related matters increase; handled well they bring responsibility and standing, handled poorly they bring fines, disputes or friction. The key is putting important matters in writing, reading the terms, staying compliant on time and seeking professional legal counsel for major contracts rather than relying on words or assumptions.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-03

A Guan Fu year often feels like a year with unusually much to sign and many rules to follow: processes, approvals and contracts come one after another, and steady steps bring standing while a misstep brings trouble.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to handle a Guan Fu year and avoid legal friction. Understand it as rules and constraints, then see which palace it lands in and which stars accompany it.

The Underlying Logic

Guan Fu, one of the twelve pre-year stars, governs systems, rules, contracts and official pressure, and those areas are easily triggered this year.

It is neutral leaning harsh: signing, standing and responsibility landing is good, but unclear terms, violations or verbal promises easily bring disputes and fines.

With Ju Men, Qing Yang, Tian Xing, Lian Zhen or Hua Ji, legal risk rises; with Long De, Hua Ke or Kui and Yue, room to resolve appears.

The minor star is only an annual layer, and the natal and decade major stars and transformations still set the verdict; Guan Fu reminds you to do compliance and paperwork early.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate which annual palace Guan Fu sits in to identify the matter: work, partnership, property and so on
  2. List the contracts, approvals and promises to handle this year
  3. Read the terms of important documents line by line, relying on professional legal counsel where unclear
  4. Follow processes and deadlines compliantly, keeping paperwork and payment records
  5. Avoid verbal promises and vague agreements, not cutting corners through connections
  6. If malefics and Ji also appear, avoid litigation in those months and postpone big matters

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Guan Fu only as a lawsuit omen when it also brings standing and responsibility

Mistake 2: Working on words and connections without reading contract terms

Mistake 3: Judging a year of disaster from one minor star while ignoring the major stars, transformations and rescue

Back to Your Own Chart
