Why Patterns Are Set by the Life Palace Three-Ways, Not Other Palaces

A pattern describes the overall structure and level of a person, and the Life palace is the hub, with Wealth, Career and Travel as its trine and opposite forming the main axis. Spouse, Children and Friends are topic palaces; they can form a small pattern within that domain, but they cannot define the lifelong major pattern.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-27

Major patterns follow the Life palace three-ways axis; other palaces only form topic-level small patterns in their domain.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see stars in the Spouse palace and declare a wealth pattern; a topic-palace combination is not the life axis.

The Underlying Logic

The Life palace is the hub and tone of the whole chart.

Life, Wealth, Career and Travel form the main axis.

The pattern level is set by the axis structure and transformations.

Other palaces are judged only within their own domain.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Separate major pattern from topic
  2. Set the Life axis
  3. Read Life, Wealth, Career and Travel
  4. Return the topic to its palace
  5. Do not set the whole chart from a topic palace
  6. Judge axis and topic separately

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Take a topic palace as the major pattern

Mistake 2: Set wealth from the Spouse palace

Mistake 3: Mix axis and topic

Back to Your Own Chart

Read the major pattern only from the Life three-ways axis; combinations in Spouse, Children and other palaces stay within their domain and do not define the lifelong pattern.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Why Patterns Are Judged From the Life Palace Three-Ways before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Why Patterns Are Judged From the Life Palace Three-Ways line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
