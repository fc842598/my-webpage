How to Protect a Child s Teeth: Brushing, Diet and Regular Dental Visits

Dental care starts with the first tooth: brush morning and night with fluoride toothpaste, with parents helping until after school age, limit sugary snacks and drinks and avoid a bottle in bed, and get regular fluoride, fissure sealants and checkups. If cavities, an underbite or crooked teeth appear, seek a proper dental clinic for treatment or an orthodontic assessment early; baby-tooth problems should not be delayed.
