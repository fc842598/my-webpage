Tian-Fu in the Companions Palace: Steady, Resourceful Friends in a Stable Relationship Setup

With Tian-Fu in Companions, friends are steady, reliable and hold resources, with noble help and real support to borrow; relationships value reciprocity and the long term, making a reassuring setup.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

Your friends tend to be grounded and trustworthy, some with savings and connections who give real help rather than mere banquet goodwill.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Trade steadiness for steadiness: Tian-Fu values reciprocity, so be the reliable, measured person yourself for ties to last; also guard against overly conservative circles that miss new chances.

The Underlying Logic

Tian-Fu is the storehouse, a steady star; in Companions, friends are reliable and hold resources.

There is noble help and real, steady support to borrow.

Relationships value reciprocity, trust and the long term and reassure.

The risk is conservative circles; stay open on a stable base.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Tian-Fu in Companions and its stars
  2. See whether friends are steady, reliable and resourceful
  3. Use real noble help and practical support
  4. Be the reliable, trustworthy, measured person yourself
  5. Maintain ties through reciprocity and the long view
  6. Guard against conservative circles and stay open to new chances

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Praising resourceful friends without noting reciprocity

Mistake 2: Only taking without being reliable, so ties do not last

Mistake 3: Overly steady circles that miss new people and chances

Back to Your Own Chart

Tian-Fu in Companions gives dependable friends and real support, a steady blessing. Return reliability with reliability and stay open within steadiness, and this network grows richer.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Tian-Fu in the Companions Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Tian-Fu in the Companions Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
