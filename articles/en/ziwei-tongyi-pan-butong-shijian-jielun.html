Does the Same Chart Change Over Time? Separating Natal and Timing Layers

Why the natal chart stays fixed while major-cycle, annual, and minor-cycle layers activate different parts over time.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

People often ask why the same Zi Wei Dou Shu chart seems to produce different statements at different times. The chart itself does not change. The natal chart and the timing layers are separate levels.

The natal chart is fixed

The natal chart from the birth time keeps the same palaces, main stars, and birth-year transformations. It describes long-term tendencies and recurring themes, and these facts remain the same in every year.

Judgment steps

  1. Confirm the fixed natal structure: Life Palace, main stars, and birth-year transformations.
  2. Write the long-term natal tendency separately from any specific year.
  3. Read the current major cycle to see which palaces are activated in this stage.
  4. Add the annual and minor cycles to explain this year's events and feelings.
  5. Let the natal chart define structure and the timing layers define timing.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then confirm the natal chart.
  2. Record the long-term structure, then add major, annual, and minor cycles.
  3. Separate the time range of each layer and mark long-term versus short-term.
  4. Verify timing activations against real events rather than one broad claim.
  5. Write the answer as fixed structure plus current timing, not one fortune label.
