Zi Wei Dou Shu: Migration Palace for a Three-Month Branch Transfer

A three-month transfer to a branch office is a temporary assignment.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-06 09:12

A three-month transfer to a branch office is a temporary assignment. Read Migration for the unfamiliar setting, Career for duties, and Property for living arrangements. Confirm expenses, handover, and the return position in writing.

Read the chart in layers

A three-month internal transfer is a temporary change of setting, not automatically a permanent move. Read Migration for unfamiliar conditions, Career for duties, Property for housing, and Wealth for added costs. A short hotel stay and a six-month lease create different commitments. Confirm return duties and expenses in the assignment letter.
