Flying vs Natal Transformations: Two Layers

Natal transformations are set by the birth-year stem, the innate static energy tone; palace-stem flights are activated by each palace stem, the later dynamic cause-effect actions. Read the two layers separately, not confusing innate with self-made.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Natal transformations are like terrain at birth, flights like the paths you walk on that terrain; terrain cannot change, how you walk is yours.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some merge flights and natal transformations, and cause-effect blurs with tone; first read natal for tone, then trace flights for actions, and layers are clear.

The Underlying Logic

Natal transformations: activated by birth-year stem, one set per chart, static.

Flights: each of twelve palaces has a stem and can fly multiple sets, dynamic.

Natal sets innate resource and sticking-point distribution, flights set later active cause-effect.

Reading order: natal first, then flights, not mixed.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find the birth-year stem and natal transformation landings
  2. Use natal transformations to set the innate tone
  3. Then read each palace stem for flights
  4. Read flights for active actions and cause-effect direction
  5. Compare layers: later flights follow or oppose innate
  6. Combine decade and year to see which layer is activated

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Merging flights and natal into one layer

Mistake 2: Seeing only natal without flights, missing later actions

Mistake 3: Tracing only flights without natal, no tone

Back to Your Own Chart

Flying vs natal transformations read two layers: innate tone and later actions. Natal first then flights, read separately, and you know what is innate and what you walk yourself.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the difference between flying and natal transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the difference between flying and natal transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
