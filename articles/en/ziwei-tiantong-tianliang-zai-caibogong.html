Tian Tong and Tian Liang in the Wealth Palace: Earning Through Service Rather Than Strain

Tian Tong is harmonious while Tian Liang is protective and principled. Together in the Wealth Palace earning leans to ease, reputation and service, suiting education, public-good work, consulting, stable institutions and caring fields, with income that arrives gently but stays relatively clean and supported. The risk is passive detachment from money, so the key is actively managing income alongside letting things come.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-01

People with Tian Tong and Tian Liang in the Wealth Palace are reluctant to bend for money, earning with both feeling and principle, with good reputation and helpful people; when too detached, they fail to pursue what is due and income tightens.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the earning style with Tian Tong and Tian Liang in the Wealth Palace. Understand it reads cash flow, then see how service and protection pair with active management.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Tong in the Wealth Palace gives harmonious, service-oriented and easygoing income that is steady but not aggressive.

Tian Liang there gives sheltered and patron-supported wealth, principled and able to turn difficulty around.

Together they mean service plus protection: with Lu Cun, Hua Lu and auspicious stars the income is clean and supported.

With malefics and Ji, passivity and detachment make one miss chances, so quoting prices and pursuing must be practiced.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Tian Tong and Tian Liang share the Wealth Palace and check their brightness
  2. Connect reputation, service and helpful people to income
  3. Quote prices, bill and negotiate terms actively, overcoming awkwardness
  4. Build steady income through contracts, renewals and regular clients
  5. Pursue actively within your principles rather than waiting for things to come
  6. With the decade and year, grasp the wealth stage actively

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Tian Tong-Tian Liang in the Wealth Palace only as clean, supported money while ignoring active management

Mistake 2: Staying too detached to quote or pursue, tightening income

Mistake 3: Mistaking ease for inaction and not taking chances when they arrive
