How to Use Celebrity Zi Wei Dou Shu Cases Without Treating Stories as Proof

A careful way to learn from public Zi Wei Dou Shu cases: use them to study method, while separating verified facts from interpretation.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:54

Cases Are Method Practice

Celebrity examples can help you study combinations and palace logic, but public birth times and event records are often incomplete.

Separate Fact and Inference

Mark what is verified, what is reported, and what you infer. Do not fill an unknown birth time just to make the story fit.

A Repeatable Process

Choose one public event, map the relevant palace and its opposite and trines, then test the timeline. If evidence is thin, lower the claim.

Why This Matters

A careful case teaches readers how to reason. A dramatic but unverified story only teaches them to trust a narrative.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
