How Zi Wei Dou Shu Shows Relationship Repair

A difficult Spouse Palace describes recurring friction and repair work, not an automatic breakup. Learn how to separate emotion, duty, and old accounts.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 09:20

Malefic stars or Hua Ji in the Spouse Palace do not mean a relationship must end. They act more like a checklist showing where friction repeats and what repair should address first.

Calling every conflict bad fate can hide the useful clues: emotional expression, money roles, or family boundaries.

Name the conflict

The main star gives the relationship habit; malefic stars and Hua Ji show the snag. Huo Xing and Ling Xing can erupt suddenly, Tuo Luo can delay and cool the conversation, and Qing Yang can make words too sharp.

Always return the sign to the actual problem being asked about.

Use the Inner-Life Palace as a buffer

A steady Inner-Life Palace can give a pressured Spouse Palace room to repair. A strained Inner-Life Palace can magnify a small disagreement.

When timing activates it, rest and private space may work better than forcing an immediate conversation.

Examples

Tuo Luo in Spouse with Tian Tong in the Inner-Life Palace often needs a clear deadline for the postponed talk. Hua Ji in Spouse with Tian Ma in Travel can require a fixed contact rhythm and shared account during distance.

Practical reading order

Read Spouse for the pattern, Inner-Life for recovery, Career for duties, and timing for when an old issue needs a formal repair.

Practical Reading Order

Start with the question palace, compare its opposite and trine partners, then add timing and transformations before making a practical judgment.
