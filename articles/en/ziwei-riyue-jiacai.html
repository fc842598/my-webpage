Ri Yue Jia Cai: Reading the Sun and Moon Flanking the Wealth Palace

Ri Yue Jia Cai means the Sun and Moon flanking the Wealth Palace: Tai Yang, the Sun, and Tai Yin, the Moon, occupy the two palaces immediately beside.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-02 17:20

Ri Yue Jia Cai means the Sun and Moon flanking the Wealth Palace: Tai Yang, the Sun, and Tai Yin, the Moon, occupy the two palaces immediately beside Wealth. In the classic bright arrangement, Tai Yang at Yin, the Tiger branch, and Tai Yin at Zi, the Rat branch, flank the Wealth Palace at Chou, the Ox branch. The formation can make financial opportunities more visible, but it is only one part of the financial judgment. Brightness, the main stars inside Wealth, transformations, and the wider palace structure determine whether income can be earned and retained.

Flanking describes the financial environment

Tai Yang tends to describe public activity, leadership, clients, and income that depends on visibility. Tai Yin tends to describe continuity, administration, savings, recurring contracts, and assets built over time. When both lights flank Wealth, the person may have access to more than one money channel. The Wealth Palace itself still answers the central questions: who owns the money, how reliably it arrives, what obligations come with it, and how much remains after costs.

Two combinations show income and retention

  • If a bright Sun and Moon flank the Wealth Palace, Hua Lu enters Wealth, and the supporting Career Palace is steady, visible assignments and repeat customers may become dependable income. A clear fee structure and controlled expenses can help turn favorable access into retained profit rather than money that merely passes through the business.
  • If the same flank surrounds a Wealth Palace affected by Hua Ji, Di Kong (Earth Void), or Di Jie (Earth Robbery), demand may coexist with delayed collection, unclear ownership, family obligations, or weak savings. The chart then supports a cash-flow review, not a claim that gain or loss is certain.

Use the formation as a financial audit

Wealth directly opposes the Fortune Palace and is supported by the Life and Career Palaces. Together they show the relationship among financial method, emotional habits, personal capacity, and professional activity. The Property Palace can add useful context about real estate or accumulated assets, although it is not part of the Wealth Palace triad. Separate business revenue from personal savings, family resources from owned assets, and professional reputation from cash already collected.

Chart-reading order

First confirm that Tai Yang and Tai Yin occupy the two palaces adjacent to Wealth. Judge the brightness and condition of both stars before naming Ri Yue Jia Cai. Next read the main stars and transformations inside the Wealth Palace, followed by the opposite Fortune Palace and the supporting Life and Career Palaces. Check Hua Lu and Hua Ke for access or recognized skill, and Hua Ji, Di Kong, and Di Jie for collection or retention problems. Use the ten-year and annual cycles last to identify when a specific income channel or financial bottleneck becomes relevant.
