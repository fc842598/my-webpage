How to Place Palace-Stem Flying Transformations Accurately in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Re-casting transformations with each palace's stem shows where that palace's transformations fly and how they echo the natal set. Confirm the palace-stem method and self-transformations first, or the flying stars become chaos.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

Palace-stem flying is an advanced tool that, used well, shows where a matter is actively pushed, but it depends on placing the palace stems correctly or the whole reading gets muddled.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Hold two premises: each palace's stem must be derived by rule, and self-transformations in the home palace must be identified. With the premises set, seeing where the transformations fly becomes meaningful.

The Underlying Logic

Palace-stem flying re-casts transformations with each palace's stem to trace where its force actively flows.

First place each palace's stem accurately, or all flying transformations are wrong.

Then identify self-transformations, where a palace's transformed star lands back in itself, which carry special meaning.

Lu, Quan, Ke or Ji flying into another palace shows support or concern between palaces and is read with the natal set.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Cast the twelve palaces and derive each stem accurately
  2. Check and separately mark any self-transformations
  3. Take the asked palace's stem and cast its four transformations
  4. Trace which palaces the four transformed stars fly into
  5. See whether their direction echoes or conflicts with the natal transformations
  6. Read together to judge where that palace's affairs are pushed and what concerns them

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Flying transformations before the palace stems are correct

Mistake 2: Overlooking self-transformations and missing key information

Mistake 3: Reading flying transformations without the natal set, giving a one-sided verdict

Back to Your Own Chart

Palace-stem flying traces an active current from each palace, provided the stems and self-transformations are clear first. With the foundation right, the flying stars have direction.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to placing palace-stem flying transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the placing palace-stem flying transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
