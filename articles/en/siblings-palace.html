Siblings Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Peers, Sharing, and Boundaries

The Siblings Palace covers siblings and peer-level relationships, including classmates, colleagues, and people with whom resources are shared. It asks how equality and boundaries work in practice.

Supportive stars may make peers more reliable. Malefics or Hua Ji can increase competition, unclear obligations, or stress around shared money. These patterns call for clearer agreements, not automatic distrust.

Separate Support From Shared Resources

A peer can be emotionally supportive but unsuitable as a business partner. When wealth-related stars or Hua Ji touch this palace, define contributions, ownership, repayment, and exit terms before combining money.

Compare Peers With Networks

The Siblings Palace emphasizes equals, while the Friends Palace emphasizes broader networks, staff, clients, and partners. If peer support is weak but the Friends Palace is strong, professional networks may provide more reliable structure.

Use a Practical Combination

If Siblings is pressured and Wealth is also weak, informal lending or a side business with friends deserves extra caution. If Wealth is stable and contracts are clear, the same relationship may still work.

Combination one: Wu Qu in the Siblings Palace with Hua Ji touching Wealth can bring shared-money questions into a peer relationship. A sibling or colleague may be capable and well intentioned while a loan or side business remains poorly structured. Written repayment dates, ownership percentages, and account access protect both the money and the relationship.

Combination two: Tian Tong in Siblings with a strong Friends Palace can mean peers offer emotional ease while a broader professional network supplies more reliable work opportunities. This is not a judgment that siblings are unhelpful. It distinguishes personal closeness from the formal structure needed for clients, staffing, and commercial responsibility. If Wealth is also stable, cooperation may work once payment, authority, and exit terms are documented; if Wealth is pressured, keep personal support separate from shared accounts. An annual trigger may make one loan or collaboration visible without defining every peer relationship in the natal chart.

Chart-Reading Order

Read the main stars and transformations in Siblings, then compare Wealth, Friends, and the opposite palace. Add decade and annual timing to see when sharing, competition, or cooperation becomes active.
