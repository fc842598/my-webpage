Authority and Resources Meeting in the Wealth Palace

The Wealth Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu describes how money is earned, handled, and retained. Hua Quan, the transformation of authority, adds control and responsibility; Hua Lu, the transformation of resources, adds access and opportunity. When they meet here, money is often connected to managing people, budgets, clients, or commercial systems rather than passive luck.

Hua Lu Opens the Door and Hua Quan Runs It

Hua Lu can provide customers, useful relationships, or a profitable opening. Hua Quan determines whether the person can set prices, allocate resources, and take responsibility for results. This combination may suit business operations, revenue leadership, portfolio management, procurement, or any role where decisions directly affect money.

Career Support Determines Whether Money Becomes Sustainable

Hua Lu in the Wealth Palace with Hua Quan in a strong Career Palace can show income rising through promotion, leadership, or a larger commercial mandate. The gain comes with accountability: a sales director may earn more because the team target and budget are now theirs to manage.

Hua Quan in the Wealth Palace with a weak Career Palace can produce the opposite imbalance. A person may control payments, negotiate contracts, or carry a large workload without having a secure title or dependable margin. Authority over transactions is not automatically personal wealth.

Revenue and Retained Cash Are Different

If Hua Ji, the transformation of obstruction, joins the Wealth Palace while Quan and Lu are active, turnover may grow faster than retained cash. Expansion, payroll, inventory, or slow collection can absorb the gain. A strong Travel Palace may bring a larger market, but contracts, payment schedules, and operating reserves still decide whether growth remains healthy.

Chart-Reading Order

Start with the Wealth Palace and identify which stars produce Hua Quan and Hua Lu. Next read the Career Palace for role and operating structure, the Travel Palace for clients and market reach, and the opposite palace for pressure. Then check Hua Ji, Void, or Robbery stars for leakage. Use the ten-year and annual cycles last to decide whether the current period favors expansion, consolidation, or stronger cash controls.
