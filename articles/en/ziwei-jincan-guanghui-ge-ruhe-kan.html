How to Read the Golden Radiance Pattern

When the Sun star guards the Life Palace in the Chen or Si palace, its light grows stronger, bringing outward expression, achievement and many mentors. Guard being too rigid, overworking and showing too much edge; brightness gives visibility, but results still need transformations and real accumulation.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

This is the late-morning sun, growing brighter, suited to stepping forward and building reputation; but too strong a light can burn others and exhaust oneself.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some assume the Sun in Chen or Si guarantees fame and success, while others read outwardness as flightiness; the real test is whether the light turns into results rather than mere brightness.

The Underlying Logic

The Sun in Chen or Si is the growing late-morning light at brightness, governing outward expression and achievement.

The pattern favors public stages, sales, management and roles needing visibility, with many mentors.

With Hua Lu, Hua Quan or Hua Ke in the trine, achievement and resources materialize more easily.

With malefics, guard being too rigid, disputes, overwork and showing too much edge and provoking envy.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the Life Palace is in Chen or Si with the Sun, the basis of the pattern
  2. Check the Sun brightness to confirm the growing light is well-placed
  3. Read the triple-direction for Lu Quan Ke and mentor support
  4. Count malefics to guard rigidity, overwork and disputes
  5. Read transformation activation to see how achievement and resources materialize
  6. Layer the decade and year to see whether outward opportunities arrive now

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming the Sun in Chen or Si guarantees fame and waiting

Mistake 2: Reading outwardness as flightiness and underestimating stage ability

Mistake 3: Ignoring malefics and Ji, failing to guard rigidity and overwork

Back to Your Own Chart

This pattern asks how the growing light illuminates real results. Pair outward energy with restraint and steadiness, so achievement is not a gust of wind but light that lasts.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Golden Radiance pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Golden Radiance pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
