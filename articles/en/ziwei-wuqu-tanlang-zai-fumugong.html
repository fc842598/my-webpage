Wu Qu and Tan Lang in the Parents Palace: Capable, Practical Parents and Turning High Expectations and Socializing Into a Close Bond With Elders

The Parents Palace reads the bond with parents, relationships with elders and academic support. Wu Qu governs firm execution and practicality while Tan Lang governs desire, versatility and socializing, so together in Chou or Wei the parents tend to be capable, practical, sociable and strong, with high expectations for the child and able to give real resources. The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Parents Palace gives firm, practical, execution-minded parents who carry matters, yet can be blunt and poor at showing warmth.

Tan Lang there gives versatile, sociable parents with many desires who understand socializing, yet can be too busy and social to be present.

Together they mean wealth plus romance: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars the parents have strength and willingly help; with malefics and Ji, distance over high demands, the generation gap or little communication follows.

The lesson is strict with understanding and close with communication: express thoughts and speak openly, turn high demands into concrete support and discuss more.
