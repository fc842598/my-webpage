Birth Hour Boundary in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Compare Four Stars, Not Only Wen Chang

Compare Wen Chang, Wen Qu, Di Kong, and Di Jie across adjacent birth hour charts before interpreting a changed Chinese astrology placement.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-04 21:15

If a birth record sits on an hour boundary, checking only Wen Chang can miss a wider change. In a common Zi Wei Dou Shu placement method, Wen Chang, Wen Qu, Di Kong, and Di Jie all use the birth hour, though their starting points and directions differ. Their relationship across palaces can change along with their individual positions.

Make a four star table

Keep the date, place, calendar conversion, and chart method fixed. Change only the uncertain hour. List all four stars by palace for both charts, then compare shared palaces, oppositions, and supporting triangles. If major stars also change unexpectedly, review the calendar settings before reading the result.

Ask different practical questions

A student preparing for a qualification exam may need to check study materials and the actual syllabus; a designer may need to compare portfolio presentation with delivery costs. A changed Wen Qu placement cannot by itself prove talent, and a changed Di Kong placement cannot predict failure. The useful question depends on the palace and on the real task.

Keep uncertainty visible

Check birth certificates or other contemporary records where possible. Personal traits are weak evidence for selecting a birth hour because both charts may produce plausible descriptions. Keep two interpretations if the time remains uncertain. Use the chart to frame a review of documents, work samples, and plans, not to replace them.
