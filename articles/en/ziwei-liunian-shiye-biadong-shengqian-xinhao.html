How to Read Annual Career Change and Promotion: Layer the Year Career Palace over Decade and Natal

Whether the year brings promotion, change or stability comes from layering the annual Career palace and annual transformations over the decade and natal Career: annual Quan/Lu into Career with an upward decade means promotion or a good move; annual Ji clashing Career warns of change and communication costs.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

The annual chart is not a new chart but the current-year slice of natal and decade; the decade sets career direction, and the year only says whether to accelerate or brake on that line.

What This Question Is Really Asking

An annual Ji in Career sparks layoff fears. Read three layers: does the natal hold this career, is the decade climbing or declining—the year only triggers on that background; one annual star neither scares nor decides.

The Underlying Logic

First set natal Career: the fitting role and long-term structure—whether a career exists and what it develops through, fixed for life.

Then decade Career: whether the ten years climb (Lu/Quan, auspicious), consolidate or drain (Ji, malefics); an upward decade lets you catch good annual signals.

Finally the year: where annual Life lands, annual Career stars, and where the year stem flies Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji—Lu brings projects/raises, Quan real authority/promotion, Ke reputation/certification, Ji in or clashing brings blocks, change, interpersonal or paperwork trouble.

Layer the verdict: upward decade + annual Lu/Quan into Career = promotion/good-move year to push actively; flat decade + annual Ji = minor wave, defend; a decade already on Ji plus a year clashing Career is the year to guard against layoff/transfer/contraction—stabilize, document, prepare a fallback.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read natal Career for long-term direction and role type
  2. Cast decade Career and transformations to classify the ten years as climb, consolidate or drain
  3. Cast the year: annual Life, annual Career and annual transformation landings
  4. Focus on whether annual Lu/Quan/Ke enter Career or annual Ji clashes it
  5. Layer: an upward decade catches annual good; a weak decade guards annual bad
  6. Push projects/promotion in good years; stabilize, document and prepare plan B in risk years

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Asserting promotion/layoff from annual Career alone without natal/decade

Mistake 2: Reading every annual Ji as unemployment when an upward decade makes it a small block

Mistake 3: Staying passive in good years and charging in risk years, reversing the rhythm

Back to Your Own Chart

Annual career is the current-year tempo on a larger direction. Layer natal, decade and year and you will know whether to push, consolidate or defend this year; with the right rhythm, the same effort takes far fewer detours.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to annual career change and promotion signals before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the annual career change and promotion signals line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
