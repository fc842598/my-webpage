How to Read the Spouse Palace: It Describes a Relationship, Not a Fairy Tale

The Spouse Palace describes how you behave in close relationships and the type you tend to attract, not only who the other person is. Read it with the triple-direction, the opposite Career Palace and romance or malefic stars.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

The Spouse Palace is not a spec sheet for a future partner; it first describes how you show up in a relationship, then the kind of person you tend to meet.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People study the Spouse Palace to ask how good their partner will be, without seeing that they are half of the relationship; a bond is never written by one side alone.

The Underlying Logic

The Spouse Palace holds the relationship script, intimacy pattern and the type you tend to attract.

Relationship quality needs the triple-direction: the home palace is the core, while the opposite and trine palaces show conditions and friction.

It sits opposite the Career Palace, so work and intimacy compete for time and energy and must be read together.

Romance stars, malefics and transformations in this palace shape how you relate, not a simple good or bad verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the Spouse Palace and read its star for the relating pattern and type attracted
  2. Open its triple-direction to see whether the relationship has support or clashes
  3. Read the opposite Career Palace to assess how work occupies the relationship
  4. Count romance and malefic stars to separate charm, friction and disputes
  5. Read transformations here: Lu is giving and Ji is attachment or entanglement
  6. Layer the decade and year to see whether the relationship is smooth, testing or at a threshold now

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the Spouse Palace only as who the other person is and missing your own half

Mistake 2: Seeing auspicious stars and waiting for a good marriage without effort or adjustment

Mistake 3: Judging divorce or lifelong singlehood from one malefic and frightening yourself

Back to Your Own Chart

The Spouse Palace shows how you relate. Read it within the triple-direction, career and transformations, and you learn how to build a bond that feels right for both of you.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Spouse Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Spouse Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
