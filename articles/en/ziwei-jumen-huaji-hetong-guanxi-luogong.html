The Same Ju Men Hua Ji: Why One Question Is About Contracts and Another About Relationships

How the palace where the annual transformation lands decides the topic, separating work-palace contract checks from relationship-palace communication.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-17 10:00

In 2027, Ju Men Hua Ji is a transformation everyone meets, but on different charts it lands in different palaces and asks different things. With the same Ju Men Hua Ji, one person should check contracts and terms while another handles communication and relationships. Without looking at the landing palace and staring only at the words, the question is read as one and the same.

The landing palace decides the topic

Ju Men already relates to speech, expression, disputes, and hidden matters, and with Hua Ji the communication blockage becomes clearer. But whichever palace it lands in, the topic follows that palace: in Wealth or Career it often relates to contracts, terms, and client communication; in Spouse or Sibling it relates to relationships, interaction, and arguments. Locating the landing palace first shows what is being asked.

Step-by-step landing-palace check

  1. Confirm the 2027 Ju Men Hua Ji, then find which palace it lands in on your chart.
  2. Read the palace's function to judge whether the topic is work, money, or relationship.
  3. In a work palace, focus on contracts, terms, and written communication.
  4. In a relationship palace, focus on expression, misunderstanding, and interaction.
  5. Combine the palace stars and transformations to judge the concrete block rather than vague "disputes."

The same transformation, different readings

The annual transformation is a shared background, while the landing palace and personal structure decide the concrete effect. Treating Ju Men Hua Ji as a fixed verdict easily creates needless worry. Locate first, then read separately, for a conclusion that fits the person.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and mark where the 2027 transformations land in each palace.
  2. Locate the Ju Men Hua Ji palace first, then read its function.
  3. Work palaces check contracts, relationship palaces check relationships.
  4. Find the concrete block through stars and transformations.
  5. Keep the conclusion conditional rather than reading the shared transformation as one result.
