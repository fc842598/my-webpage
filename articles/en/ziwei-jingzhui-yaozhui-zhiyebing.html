How to Handle Neck and Back Pain From Sedentary Work: Habits First, Then Rehabilitation

For neck, shoulder and back discomfort from prolonged sitting, first adjust your posture, screen height and sitting time, stand and move every forty minutes, and add regular stretching and suitable core and back exercise. If pain persists, radiates to an arm or leg, or comes with numbness or weakness, seek a proper orthopedic or rehabilitation clinic promptly; do not blindly massage or self-medicate, and let the doctor s diagnosis prevail.
