Da Hao in the Blessings Palace: Guarding Mental Energy

Da Hao in the Blessings Palace suggests mental energy drained by trifles, stray thoughts and over-worry, feeling heart-tired despite doing little. Subtract, let go and leave the mind blank, reserving limited heart for what and who matter.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-11

Mental capacity is limited; filling it with everything leaves nothing deep; clear it regularly to hold what truly matters.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some worry over everything and let nothing go, long drained; stars are tendency, while subtracting and leaving blank are practical ways to nourish the spirit.

The Underlying Logic

Da Hao in Blessings means spirit drained by trifles and stray thoughts.

Over-worry and heart-tiredness are causes.

Subtract, let go, leave the mind blank.

This is tendency, not an absolute verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Identify what drains the mind
  2. Learn to refuse and let go
  3. Reduce over-worry
  4. Set aside quiet time alone
  5. Reserve heart for important people
  6. Rest on a calm heart

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Worrying over everything, not subtracting

Mistake 2: Stray thoughts, long inner friction

Mistake 3: Dismissing heart-tiredness

Back to Your Own Chart

Da Hao in the Blessings Palace guards against a spirit emptied by trifles. Subtract, let go and leave blank, keeping the heart untired and the person blessed.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Da Hao in the Blessings Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Da Hao in the Blessings Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
