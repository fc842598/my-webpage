How the Natal Four Transformations Set the Baseline of a Zi Wei Chart

The natal Four Transformations come from your birth-year stem. Where Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji land shows where you repeatedly experience gain, control, reputation and blockage across life.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

Find the natal Four Transformations first; they are four permanent markers showing innate resources, where effort goes, and what keeps snagging you.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many start with the Life Palace major star and skip the natal transformations, producing vivid personality talk but missing the recurring themes of a life.

The Underlying Logic

The natal transformations follow the birth-year stem; everyone born that year shares the same transformed stars, but different landing palaces change the theme.

Lu is resources and gain, Quan is control and responsibility, Ke is reputation and smoothing, Ji is fixation and blockage.

They form a causal chain rather than four isolated stars: Lu supplies, Quan executes, Ke reputes, Ji gives the lesson to work on.

The natal transformations are the baseline; decade and annual transformations add timing on top.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. From the birth-year stem, identify which four stars become Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji
  2. Locate each transformed star in the twelve palaces
  3. Read Lu first: where innate resources and opportunities come from
  4. Then Quan and Ke: how you take control and gain recognition
  5. Focus on Ji: which kind of matter repeatedly blocks you and needs letting go
  6. Connect the four palaces into a natal storyline before moving to cycles

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fearing Ji alone while ignoring the resources and ability Lu, Quan and Ke provide

Mistake 2: Treating transformations as ordinary stars instead of a dynamic causal chain

Mistake 3: Judging a whole life from natal transformations and forgetting the layered cycles

Back to Your Own Chart

The natal transformations are a chart's factory-set theme. Understanding them explains why certain years suddenly magnify specific matters.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the natal Four Transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the natal Four Transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
