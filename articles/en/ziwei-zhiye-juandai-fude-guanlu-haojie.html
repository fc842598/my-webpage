Work Strain: Reading the Career and Inner Life Palaces Separately

The Career and Inner Life palaces offer different questions about work and satisfaction. A Zi Wei Dou Shu chart cannot diagnose burnout or establish its cause.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24Updated 2026-11-10 14:36 (UTC+8)

Feeling depleted at work is a real experience, but a chart is not a diagnostic test for burnout. The Career Palace can frame discussion of responsibilities and working conditions. The Inner Life Palace adds questions about satisfaction and the experience of time away from work. Keep those roles distinct.

Start With the Actual Workload

Ask what tasks have changed, which expectations are unclear, and what support is available. A short period of urgent work and an ongoing mismatch in responsibilities are different situations. Do not infer either from a malefic star or describe a person as unable to recover because a palace lacks favorable stars.

Keep Chart Layers Separate

Read the natal Career Palace with its related structure, then add the ten-year and annual context. State which star carries Hua Ji rather than calling every difficulty a work lesson. Inner Life Palace is the preferred English name for Fu De Gong; Fortune or Karma can obscure the subject being discussed.

Compare Possible Changes With the Problem

Unclear priorities call for a different conversation from missing resources or incompatible expectations. Describe what would need to change and how the person would know it helped. A chart cannot establish a health condition or prescribe treatment, and a favorable cycle is not a reason to dismiss someone's reported difficulties.
