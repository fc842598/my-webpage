How Zi Wei Dou Shu Reads Disputes and Litigation: Combinations of Ju Men, Tian Xing, Qing Yang and Hua Ji, with the Focus on Preventing Contract Gaps

Litigation tends to involve Ju Men (speech disputes), Tian Xing (legal liability), Qing Yang (hard collision) and Hua Ji (debt/block), rising when in Life/Career/Karma/Travel or activated by decade/year. It predicts no specific lawsuit but flags when to tighten contracts, evidence and conduct.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

No chart guarantees a lawsuit; it shows a structure prone to speech and rule conflicts. What truly helps is tightening contracts, process and evidence in the risk window.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Ju Men/Tian Xing alone sparks lawsuit fears. Ask which palace, whether Ji/Qing Yang aggravate, and when decade/year activate it—Ju Men alone often just means "living by words," not litigation.

The Underlying Logic

Ju Men rules speech/disputes/expression, Tian Xing law/contracts/liability, Qing Yang head-on collision and severance, Hua Ji debt/block/entanglement; litigation usually stacks these in a specific palace.

Palace sets the field: Career for work/contract disputes, Wealth for debt and money, Travel for out-of-town/traffic/cross-region, Friends for partnership/loans, Life/Karma for offending by words and holding grudges.

Decade/year sets timing: natal structure is only a baseline; when decade/year Ji/Qing Yang/Tian Xing activates those palaces—especially annual Ji clashing natal Career/Travel—it is the high-dispute stage to be careful.

The practical value is prevention: in risk windows, review contracts clause by clause, keep important exchanges in writing, turn verbal promises into documents, avoid gray operations, and mediate first—converting a conflict-prone tendency into disciplined habits dissolves most disputes early.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find Ju Men/Tian Xing/Qing Yang/Hua Ji and whether they share or trine a palace
  2. Use the palace to set the risk field: contract, debt, travel, partnership or speech
  3. Cast decade/year for stages where Ji/malefics activate these stars
  4. Before the window, harden compliance: contracts, authorizations, receipts and chat records all documented
  5. When disputes begin, prefer written communication and mediation, avoiding emotional escalation
  6. The chart shows tendency only; real legal matters rely on professional lawyers and formal channels

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Predicting lawsuits from Ju Men/Tian Xing while they also mean living by words and rules

Mistake 2: Ignoring decade activation and treating long-term tendency as imminent

Mistake 3: Worrying without preventing, missing the chance to kill risk at the source with contracts and evidence

Back to Your Own Chart

A chart is not a court summons; it marks the stages and fields where rule and speech conflicts are likelier. Treat these windows as compliance reminders—detailed contracts, full evidence, measured words—and disputes tend to pass you by.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to disputes, litigation and contract risk before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the disputes, litigation and contract risk line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
