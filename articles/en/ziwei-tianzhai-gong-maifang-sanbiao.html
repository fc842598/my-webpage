How to Read the Property Palace Before Buying a Home

A practical Zi Wei Dou Shu framework for reading the Property Palace alongside budget, living needs, and family responsibilities before a home purchase.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:12

Home and Security

The Property Palace covers housing, family assets, and the feeling of being settled. It is not a direct property-price forecast.

Three Tables

Translate the chart into a budget table, a living-needs table, and a family-responsibility table. Those tables make a vague home question concrete.

Read Four Palaces

Property shows the home, Wealth shows cash flow, Parents shows family support, and Outside shows the city or market beyond the familiar base.

Example

Property Hua Lu with Wealth Hua Quan can support a structured purchase. Property Hua Ji with Outside Hua Lu may favor renting or relocating first.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
