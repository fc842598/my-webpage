What This Question Is Really Asking

The Underlying Logic

The Spouse Palace describes how intimacy is conducted, and Lian Zhen Hua Ji tightens the rules and sense of safety, breeding suspicion and cold silence.

It tests honesty: naming unease, needs and bottom lines keeps the relationship light, while probing and holding it in makes misunderstanding heavy.

As a secondary romance star, with gathered malefics guard against unhealthy romance and complicated triangles rather than entangling with unclear people.

An annual Hua Ji sets only one year of relationship pluses and minuses; long-term marriage still rests on the natal Spouse Palace and decade.

Three Common Mistakes
