Can Zi Wei Dou Shu Predict Lifespan? Health Palace and Decade Boundaries

Many people who consult fortune tellers, one of the most concerned questions is "how long can I live." But this question itself is problematic—Zi Wei Dou Shu can indicate constitutional tendencies and health risks, but cannot precisely predict lifespan. Using the chart as health reference is fine, treating it as a lifespan verdict is superstition.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24

What the chart can do in health: first, "indicate constitutional tendencies"—stars in Health Palace reflect which body systems are weaker (e.g., Tian Tong/Ju Men governs digestion, Tai Yang/Lian Zhen governs cardiovascular), helping you pay attention and prevent early. Second, "indicate risk time windows"—when Decade/Annual activates Health Palace or Jealousy, health risk increases, reminding you to pay special attention to checkups and rest during this period. Third, "indicate relationship between personality and health"—stars in Life and Fortune palaces reflect personality, and personality affects health (e.g., impatient people have higher cardiovascular risk, anxious people have poor digestion). What the chart cannot do in health: first, "cannot precisely predict lifespan"—lifespan is affected by genetics, environment, medical care, lifestyle, accidents and many factors, the chart is only one reference dimension. Second, "cannot diagnose disease"—chart saying "weak spleen-stomach" does not mean you have stomach disease, specific diagnosis needs medical examination. Third, "cannot predict specific time of death"—any fortune teller who says "you have a major disaster in a certain year" "you will not live past a certain age" is either scaring you or scamming money. Fourth, "cannot replace medical care"—when sick see a doctor, do not delay treatment because the chart says "nothing wrong this year." Correct health concepts: first, "the chart is a reminder not a verdict"—seeing health risk signals in the chart, what you should do is "prevent early, get regular checkups," not "anxiety and panic." Second, "lifestyle matters more than the chart"—even if the chart shows good constitution, long-term staying up late, overeating, no exercise will cause problems; even if the chart shows weak constitution, healthy lifestyle can greatly reduce risk. Third, "regular checkups are the most reliable"—annual checkups after 30, add targeted examinations after 40 (gastrointestinal endoscopy, cardiac ultrasound, tumor markers, etc.), more accurate than any chart. Fourth, "mental health is equally important"—Fortune Palace state reflects mental health, anxiety, depression and other emotional problems need professional help, do not rely on "fortune telling" to solve. Fifth, "if there are health problems, follow正规 hospital examinations and doctor diagnosis, do not rely on the chart for medical decisions."

What This Question Is Really Asking

The Underlying Logic

Absolutizing the "good" and "bad" of Decade/Annual, ignoring that every stage has opportunities and challenges.

Only looking at Decade without Annual, or only Annual without Decade, lacking overall perspective.

Doing nothing during a "bad" Decade, wasting time for accumulation and preparation.

Treating chart fortune predictions as "fate," ignoring the role of personal choice and effort.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Chart which palace the current Decade moves through, determine the ten-year theme.
  2. See the Decade palaces major stars and transformations, understand the decades strengths and challenges.
  3. See which palace the Annual moves through, judge the specific event direction for the year.
  4. Take initiative in the Decades "advantage areas," hold steady and build in "challenge areas."
  5. Combine long-term goals with annual plans, progressing step by step.
  6. Review at year-end, adjust next years plan based on actual conditions.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Decade/Annual only indicates trends; specific results depend on personal choices and actions.

Mistake 2: A "good" Decade can be wasted by wrong decisions; a "bad" Decade can accumulate through caution.

Mistake 3: Do not do nothing because the chart says "a certain year is bad"; preparation and accumulation always have value.
