Zhi Bei in the Life Palace: Above Backbiting

Zhi Bei governs backbiting; in the Life Palace it suggests being talked about behind your back. Act upright, do not pass gossip or drain yourself, for clarity shows in time.

You cannot control behind-back words, but stay upright, do not pass or heed them.
