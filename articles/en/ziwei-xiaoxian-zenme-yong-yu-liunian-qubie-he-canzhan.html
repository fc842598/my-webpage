The Minor Limit in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How It Differs From the Year and How They Work Together

The minor limit starts from the birth-year branch and advances one palace yearly, an inner one-age-one-palace trend focused on mental state, body and personal feeling; the year is set by that year's branch and focuses on external environment and actual events. San He often reads signs in the minor limit and facts in the year—inner and outer together are fuller than the year alone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

The minor limit is inner/body weather, the year what happens outside; aligned fortune is certain, misalignment often means smooth events with restless mind or vice versa.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People conflate the two and read one, or dismiss the minor limit; when year events and inner state mismatch, it explains why good news arrives yet you feel anxious—the inner-outer gap.

The Underlying Logic

Start: the birth-year branch sets age-one minor-limit palace, then advances one palace yearly for both genders, cycling the twelve.

Nature: from the inner self, governing mindset, emotion, body and personal signs—internal.

Year: that year's Tai Sui branch sets the year Life palace, governing external environment, people and actual events—external.

Together: minor limit for signs/internal, year for facts/external; aligned fortune is clear, contradiction is an inner-outer gap to explain separately.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Set age-one minor-limit palace by birth branch, advance to current age
  2. Separately cast the year Life and year twelve
  3. Read inner signs: mindset, emotion, body in the minor limit
  4. Read external environment and events in the year
  5. Aligned fortune gives a firmer conclusion
  6. When contradictory, separate event problem from mind problem

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating minor limit and year as the same

Mistake 2: Reading only the year, unable to explain inner gaps

Mistake 3: Getting the minor-limit direction wrong

Back to Your Own Chart

The minor limit supplies the inner side the year misses. The year shows what the world gives, the minor whether you can receive it and how you feel; together a yearly reading sees both event and person.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to minor limit usage before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the minor limit usage line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
