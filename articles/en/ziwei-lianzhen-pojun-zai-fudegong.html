Lian Zhen and Po Jun in the Virtue Palace: A Driven, Change-Seeking Mind, Turning Discipline and Breaking-Rebuilding Into a Spirit With a Buffer

The Virtue Palace reads the inner state, spiritual enjoyment and blessings. Lian Zhen governs rules, discipline and public relations while Po Jun governs change, depletion and rebuilding, so together in Mao or You the mind is driven, restless with the status quo and change-seeking, bold in thought and action, often taking goals and self-renewal seriously. The risk is mental tension, change, inner friction and always wanting to start over, so the key is making change planned and leaving a buffer and outlet for the mind.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-03

People with Lian Zhen and Po Jun in the Virtue Palace have an inner fire, dislike settling and always want to renew themselves and life; yet when drive is fierce and no buffer is kept, they strain themselves in inner friction and repeated overturning.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the inner state with Lian Zhen and Po Jun in the Virtue Palace. Understand the palace reads the mind, then see how discipline and change combine so one keeps drive without straining.

The Underlying Logic

Lian Zhen in the Virtue Palace makes the mind rule-minded, disciplined and goal-driven, yet can be held back by rules and self-requirements.

Po Jun there gives change in the mind, daring to break and rebuild and succeeding after breaking, yet can bring inner friction, change and wanting to start over.

Together they mean discipline plus change: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars under planning drive becomes growth; with malefics and Ji, pressure from tension, repetition or worry follows.

The lesson is breaking with a buffer and rebuilding with rhythm: advance change in nodes and leave an outlet for rest and emotion.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Lian Zhen and Po Jun share the Virtue Palace and check their brightness
  2. Advance change and self-renewal in nodes rather than overturning all at once
  3. Leave an outlet for rest and emotion to avoid mental tension
  4. Practice accepting the present, turning inner friction into concrete action
  5. Cultivate interests unrelated to goals to give the mind a buffer
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of mood change and activation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Lian Zhen-Po Jun in the Virtue Palace only as driven and change-seeking while ignoring tension and inner friction

Mistake 2: Always wanting to start over with no buffer, straining oneself

Mistake 3: Much inner friction and not accepting the present, leaving the mind no outlet
