How to Trace a Flying-Transformation Route: From One Palace's Stem to "Who Causes What for Whom"

Palace-stem flying reads: palace A's stem transforms a star sitting in palace B into Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji, meaning A's matter acts on B in that mode. Follow the route hop by hop to reveal the causal chain—do not skip steps or lose the thread.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

Natal transformations are a static baseline; palace-stem flying is dynamic cause between palaces. Tracing one complete route answers who drives a matter, where it flows and where it jams.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some memorize transformation tables yet cannot connect lines on a chart. The hard part is not looking up a stem but fixing which palace takes off, which star transforms, where that star lands, and whether to trace another hop.

The Underlying Logic

Hop one sets the start: whoever acts is the launching palace—Life for how I treat love, Career/Wealth stems for how work uses money; a wrong start derails everything.

Hop two looks up the transformation: the launch palace's stem transforms some star into Lu (give), Quan (control), Ke (name/ease) or Ji (debt/block).

Hop three finds the landing: the transformed star's actual palace gives a launch→land relation, e.g. Life stem Hua Lu into Wealth means I direct effort and gain toward money.

Hop four chains: when needed launch again from the landing for a second/third hop to form a causal chain; note flying-in (energy received) vs flying-out (energy invested) and self-transformation; a route that loops or clashes completes the verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Translate the question into "who does what to whom" and choose the launching palace
  2. Write the launch stem and look up which stars it makes Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji
  3. Locate those stars' actual palaces to get the action lines
  4. Interpret each mode: what is given, controlled, eased or owed
  5. For cause, launch again from the landing only until the loop closes; never fly endlessly
  6. Verify against natal structure—flying is the dynamic line and cannot leave the static frame

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Choosing the wrong launch palace, using another stem for a question about oneself

Mistake 2: Looking up transformations without locating the star's landing, leaving the route half-drawn

Mistake 3: Flying endlessly from every palace into a tangle that loses the main line

Back to Your Own Chart

Route tracing trains step-by-step causal reading. On your chart pick one concrete matter and hop honestly from launch to landing; after three tries, many vague "whys" turn out to be written plainly along the route.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to tracing flying-transformation routes before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the tracing flying-transformation routes line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
