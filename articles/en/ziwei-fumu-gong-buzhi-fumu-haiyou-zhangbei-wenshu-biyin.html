The Parents Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Beyond Parents—Seniors, Documents and Protection

The Parents Palace (also appearance palace) governs bonds with parents and elders, the degree of elder protection, and also academic credentials, documents, inherited looks and relations with superiors. Auspicious transformations bring elder and institutional support; malefics/Ji mean more friction in kinship or paperwork.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Parents answers how much lift you receive from above—parents, elders, mentors, bosses, institutions—a key reference for early resources and upward channels.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Narrowing it to getting-along-with-parents misses that it also governs credentials, documents and boss relations—half the information.

The Underlying Logic

Parents governs parental bonds, upbringing and early protection, plus how elders and mentors—those above you—treat you.

It doubles as the documents palace: degrees, certificates, contracts, approvals and official papers; Hua Ke aids exams, Hua Ji stalls paperwork.

Also called the appearance palace, it covers inherited looks and first impressions.

At work it corresponds to the direct supervisor; read with Career to see whether bosses lift you or require磨合.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Parents major star and transformations for the bond tone with parents and elders
  2. Find Hua Ke/Lu to locate elder protection and credentials/paperwork support
  3. With malefics/Ji, anticipate friction with parents or bosses and communicate proactively
  4. Use it as the documents palace: double-check process and keep backups before signings and approvals
  5. Combine Career to read the relationship with your direct supervisor at work
  6. When the decade Parents activates, expect moves, property, elder matters or paperwork changes

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing only parent relations, missing documents and bosses

Mistake 2: Declaring bad parents from malefics, ignoring后天 relating

Mistake 3: Ignoring the document line in approvals and signings, stuck in process
