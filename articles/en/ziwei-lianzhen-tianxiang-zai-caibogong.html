Lian Zhen and Tian Xiang in the Wealth Palace: Earning Through Relations and Rules, Turning Skill and the Seal Into Steady Wealth

The Wealth Palace reads the earning style and cash state. Lian Zhen transforms into confinement, governing firmness and social skill, while Tian Xiang is the seal star governing rules, assistance and coordination, so together in Zi or Wu one is trustworthy and tactful in earning, making steady money within systems through skill and contacts, suiting finance, management, business-government and brokerage work. The risk is overcautious earning, disputes and pride of face, so the key is treating the seal as responsibility and acting within rules. This is only a metaphysical reference and not investment advice.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the wealth-seeking pattern with Lian Zhen and Tian Xiang in the Wealth Palace. Understand the palace reads cash, then see how social skill and the seal combine so one stays steady while daring to act.

The Underlying Logic

Lian Zhen in the Wealth Palace makes earning rely on social skill, tact and contacts, active and competitive, yet also lets disputes, face or emotion affect finances.

Tian Xiang there makes earning rule-minded, trust-focused and coordinating, steady, yet also cautious and passively waiting for authorization.

Together they mean confinement star plus the seal: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars social skill and rules become steady wealth; with malefics and Ji, disputes, loss and pressure from procedures or authority follow.

The lesson is smooth with rules and the seal used for oneself: treat trust and credentials as a pass, seek actively within rules, clarify contracts and procedures early and decide within one’s means.
