Sunlight at the Thunder Gate: Pattern Conditions and Modern Career Use

How to verify the Sun at the Mao palace pattern and translate its public, visible energy into modern career terms.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Sunlight at the Thunder Gate traditionally places Tai Yang in the Mao palace, like the morning sun rising in the east. It points to openness and development, but it still requires the right position and supporting stars.

The core conditions

The key is Tai Yang in the Mao palace, with helpful stars in the same or supporting positions and no severe disruption from difficult stars or Ji. Calling Tai Yang in other palaces by this pattern is a position error.

Verification steps

  1. Confirm that Tai Yang is truly in the Mao palace rather than only checking the star.
  2. Read the same and supporting palaces for helpful or disruptive stars.
  3. Check transformations for resources, responsibility, and blockage.
  4. Connect Career, Travel, and the host star to concrete work fields.
  5. Separate pattern tendency from real opportunity before concluding.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then confirm the palace.
  2. Verify the pattern conditions, then check supporting palaces and transformations.
  3. Translate the image into modern career direction rather than old status labels.
  4. Add the current cycle to see whether a window is open.
  5. Write the answer as position conditions plus career tendency, not a fame guarantee.
