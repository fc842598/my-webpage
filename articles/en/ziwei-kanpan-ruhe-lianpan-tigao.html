How to Practice Zi Wei to Improve: Build a Feedback Loop by Judging First, Verifying Later

Understanding theory is not the same as reading a chart, and real improvement comes from deliberate practice: take your own or a familiar chart, write judgments by method before knowing the outcome, then compare with what later happens and score where you were wrong or missed something. The key is reading widely, verifying promptly and reviewing repeatedly, linking every judgment back to stars, palaces and transformations rather than judging by feeling.

Many people memorize star natures yet cannot read a chart; what is missing is not knowledge but the volume of judging, verifying and reviewing, and like learning to ride, it must be done hands-on.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what practice method improves you fastest. Understand that ability comes from a feedback loop, then set a repeatable routine.

The Underlying Logic

Reading theory without practice leaves knowledge scattered; only judging on a real chart and being verified by reality makes star natures and combinations truly yours.

Judging first and verifying later matters: explaining after the result is hindsight, so in practice always write the prediction and mark your basis first.

Review what you got wrong, distinguishing a casting error, a missed trine or transformation, or a weight misjudgment, and adjust your reading accordingly.

Start with your own and close people’s charts, where information is full and verification easy, then move to unfamiliar charts, building judgment over time through volume and feedback.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First choose a chart with complete information and easy verification, your own or a familiar person’s
  2. Without seeing the result, write concrete judgments and their basis using palaces, stars and transformations
  3. Set a verifiable time and clear standard for each judgment to avoid ambiguity
  4. At that time, compare with facts item by item and note what was right, wrong or uncertain
  5. Review the wrong items to find whether you missed something, misjudged weight or cast wrongly
  6. Repeat regularly, gradually adding unfamiliar charts and complex topics to build your own case library

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Memorizing without practice, still unable to judge a real chart

Mistake 2: Explaining after knowing the result and feeling capable without truly practicing prediction

Mistake 3: Judging by feeling without marking the basis, unable to review and improve when wrong

Back to Your Own Chart

Practice is the only shortcut from theory to ability, and the method is judge, verify, then review. Keeping at it, you will clearly feel more grounded reading charts.

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to how to practice reading charts to improve before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone

  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
