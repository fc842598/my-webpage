A practical question like "适不适合看长期暧昧要不要及时止损" often looks simple on the surface, but it usually mixes two lines: 关系兑现 and 时间成本. If you do not separate them first, even a smart answer can flatten a structural decision into a short emotional suggestion.

The steadier use is to see which layer the chart is actually activating, then judge whether the immediate move enlarges the cost line or serves the main line.

Separate the real layer of the decision first

The key is not a flat yes-or-no. It is whether 关系兑现 is solving a short-term block while 时间成本 is deciding the longer position.

Once the main line and the cost line are separated, you can tell whether the move is aligned or just covering a deeper mismatch.

Look at two concrete situations

If the chemistry stays high but the relationship never receives a clear place, the real test is whether that heat turns into practical movement.

Some ambiguous ties are not impossible. They are just expensive to keep waiting on. When the delay keeps growing, time cost becomes part of the answer.

Do not flatten every cost into one yes-or-no

Do not assume 关系兑现 is right just because it feels lighter in the short term.

And do not assume 时间成本 is right just because it sounds bigger or more ambitious.

A steadier judgment order

  1. Decide whether you are solving a short-term block or changing a longer position.
  2. Check whether the chart is leaning harder toward 关系兑现 or 时间成本.
  3. Only then decide whether to move.
