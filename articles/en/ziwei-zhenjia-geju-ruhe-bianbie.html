How to Tell True Patterns from False in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Three Conditions Must Hold

Before naming a pattern, check whether the major star is well placed, auspicious stars truly meet, and malefics, Ji or Empty break it. All three make a true pattern; a name with incomplete conditions is false, so do not be led by the label.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

Pattern names sound attractive, noble or wealthy, but the name is not the formation. Beginners claim them without checking the conditions item by item.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Use three steps: is the major star strong, do auspicious stars truly meet, and do malefics or Ji break it. Pass all three to accept it; missing any step means an ordinary structure even if it resembles a pattern.

The Underlying Logic

First, the major star must be well placed and exalted; a weak star leaves the pattern without a head.

Second, the required auspicious stars must truly meet in the triple-direction rather than being forced.

Third, malefics, Ji or Empty must not break it; any breaking factor lowers or voids it.

All three conditions make a true pattern, while a name resting on only one or two is false.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. List all the pattern's formation conditions
  2. Check whether the major star is exalted and strong
  3. Verify one by one that auspicious stars truly meet in the trine
  4. Check whether malefics, Ji or Empty break it
  5. Accept a true pattern only when all three hold, otherwise read an ordinary structure
  6. Do not be swayed by the name; use the actual net effect

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Naming a pattern at an auspicious combination without checking conditions

Mistake 2: Taking one or two conditions as a noble pattern and treating false as true

Mistake 3: Being led by an attractive name while ignoring breaking factors

Back to Your Own Chart

A pattern is a summary of structure, not a wishful label. Check the three conditions and true versus false is clear, so the reading does not deceive itself.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to telling true patterns from false ones before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the telling true patterns from false ones line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
