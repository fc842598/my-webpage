When Jiang Jun Appears: Authority and Competition Rise; Lead, Do Not Just Win

Jiang Jun is a Bo Shi sign governing authority, competition, execution and the bearing to lead, so personal drive is strong and responsibility tends to find you. Aim that edge at the goal and guard against rigidity, one-upmanship and refusing advice.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Jiang Jun is like a sword given to you for the year, able to break a deadlock or wound others; wielded well it makes a leader, wielded poorly it makes a lone contender.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some work hard and take responsibility in a Jiang Jun year but stiffen relationships through one-upmanship and refusal to yield; the issue is not low drive but using the bearing to win rather than to deliver.

The Underlying Logic

Jiang Jun governs authority and competition, showing strong drive, fast progress and willingness to decide, often bringing leadership and hard-task roles.

It rises within the Bo Shi system, so direction must be right: toward a task it is confidence, toward people it breaks and isolates when rigid.

With auspicious stars and Hua Quan the authority helps and matches the name; with malefics it turns combative and dispute-prone.

It sets only one year of bearing and execution; long-term patterns still rest on the natal chart and decade.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate which palace Jiang Jun falls in to see which affairs need leadership and hard pushes this year
  2. Confirm the goal before acting and aim the edge at the task, not the person
  3. Rank tasks and put drive first into the key hard problems
  4. When leading, listen and share credit rather than taking it all
  5. In disagreement, reason around the goal instead of winning for face
  6. After each stage review whether the bearing was overused and soften the edge

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Using drive to win everywhere, hurting relationships and the task too

Mistake 2: Staying rigid and refusing advice until isolated

Mistake 3: Taking one year of strong authority as long-term status without the natal chart and decade

Back to Your Own Chart

A Jiang Jun year teaches authority without force. Put drive into hard tasks and bearing into leadership, and the year energy accomplishes things rather than wearing people down.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the year when Jiang Jun (the General) appears before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the year when Jiang Jun (the General) appears line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
