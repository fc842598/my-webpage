Zi Wei Dou Shu on Separation: Settle Practical Matters, Then Decide on Repair

When a couple lives apart for a time, the Spouse Palace shows the relationship state. Separation is not divorce; first settle housing, property and childcare and keep necessary records, then calmly decide whether to repair the relationship or move toward divorce, avoiding emotional decisions.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30

The key is first settling practical matters and keeping records, then deciding calmly, rather than using separation as a weapon.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some move out after a fight without settling property and children or keeping separation evidence, and later disagree on the timing and finances in divorce.

The Underlying Logic

Separation is often triggered when the Spouse Palace shows the relationship state, marking a marriage that still exists while cohabitation pauses.

First settle housing, property use and childcare, in writing if needed, and keep records of the timing and expenses.

Separation itself does not end the marriage, and divorce requires agreement and registration or litigation, with reassessment possible during the cooling period.

When the Spouse Palace stars are steady and practical matters are clear, there is room to buffer; with Hua Ji and malefics, avoid impulsive decisions, with professional legal advice as the basis.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First communicate calmly and frame separation as temporary, not divorce
  2. Settle housing, property use and childcare, in writing if needed
  3. Keep records of the start date, rent and expenses
  4. Read the Spouse Palace to judge the state and chance of repair
  5. Set a cooling period and consider marriage counseling
  6. If choosing divorce, proceed by agreement or litigation, with professional legal advice as the basis

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Moving out in anger without settling property and children

Mistake 2: Not keeping separation evidence, leaving timing and expenses unclear

Mistake 3: Treating separation as divorce and ignoring that the marriage continues
