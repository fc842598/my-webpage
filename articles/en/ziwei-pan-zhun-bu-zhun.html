Is a Zi Wei Chart Accurate? Tendencies, Not a Script

A chart is accurate about temperament tendencies and probability ranges, not a precise script of every event. Accuracy also depends on reading skill, correct birth information and how you use it; read right and used well, the chart feels accurate.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

A chart is like a weather forecast of trends and probabilities, not a guarantee of rain at a precise minute; demanding full accuracy is using the wrong tool.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some call it inaccurate when a few things do not happen, but the chart gives ranges; others take it as a full script and lose initiative; both extremes err.

The Underlying Logic

The chart is accurate on innate temperament, relating patterns and timing tendencies.

It is not accurate on precise events, exact numbers and personal endings.

Accuracy factors: birth information, chart building and reading skill.

Right use: reference tendencies, decide yourself, know fate without fatalism.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First verify birth information and chart building
  2. Validate the tendency layer with temperament and relating
  3. Do not demand precise prediction of every event
  4. Take both matches and misses as feedback
  5. When unclear, improve method rather than denying
  6. Use the chart to aid decisions, holding initiative

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Demanding full accuracy, using the wrong tool

Mistake 2: Negating all at a few misses

Mistake 3: Taking the chart as script, losing initiative
