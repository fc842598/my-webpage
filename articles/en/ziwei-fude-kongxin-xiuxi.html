How to Read an Empty Inner-Life Palace in Zi Wei

An empty Inner-Life Palace does not mean no inner world. Borrow the opposite Wealth Palace, then observe the real way the person rests and recovers.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 12:20

An empty Inner-Life Palace does not mean an empty mind or permanent overthinking. Borrow the opposite palace first, then observe how the person actually recovers and handles solitude.

The word ‘empty’ is only the entry point.

Borrow the opposite palace

The opposite of Inner-Life is Wealth. Its stars and transformations can show what the mind keeps calculating during rest. A busy Wealth line may need a planned shutdown before relaxation works.

Use the triad to confirm the borrowed meaning.

Read recovery behavior

Some people restore through solitude, others through movement, travel, or friends. Travel, Friends, and Health show whether recovery moves outward, connects socially, or starts with the body.

Timing can change which method works.

Example

An empty Inner-Life borrowing Wu Qu from Wealth may need the budget and task list settled before sleep. Tian Ma in the Travel triad can make a change of place restorative.

Practical reading order

Confirm the empty palace, borrow Wealth, add Travel, Friends, and Health, then use annual timing to see whether the recovery method shifts.
