Tian Kui and Tian Yue: The Benefactor Stars

Tian Kui and Tian Yue are benefactor stars, ruling crucial help from others. In the Life Palace or triple combination, they bring rescue at the key moment.

Helper Stars2026-08-16 10:15

Tian Kui and Tian Yue are the benefactor stars. People with them in the Life Palace have a kind of luck — someone appears at the hardest moment. It isn’t deliberate networking; you naturally encounter good people.

Tian Kui is Yang Fire, the daytime benefactor (public, male). Tian Yue is Yin Fire, the nighttime benefactor (private, female). They always meet in triple combination. Unlike Zuo Fu/You Bi (daily help), Kui/Yue rescue at key moments.

Core Meaning

They rule benefactor sponsorship — not someone who runs errands for you, but a guide who appears at life’s key nodes: job hunting, crisis, big decisions.

Tian Kui is yang benefactor — male, public, daytime. Tian Yue is yin — female, private, behind-the-scenes. Both is best.

People with Kui/Yue usually have good presence and are liked by elders and bosses. You may not notice, but people want to help you.

Across the Twelve Palaces

Life: lifelong benefactors, disaster turned to fortune.

Career: bosses sponsor you, promotion help.

Wealth: someone lends when you’re short, or brings earning opportunities.

Travel: benefactors away from home; developing outside your hometown may be better.

Parents: capable parents or a benefactor boss.

Kui/Yue vs Fu/Bi

Zuo Fu/You Bi are assistants — daily cooperation. Kui/Yue are talent-spotters — not always present, but pulling you up or giving opportunity at the key moment.

Fu/Bi are a line (continuous); Kui/Yue are points (nodes). Fu/Bi are colleagues; Kui/Yue are mentors.

Best setup: both. Help daily and rescue at key moments. Only Kui/Yue without Fu/Bi: opportunity arrives but no one executes. Only Fu/Bi without Kui/Yue: help but no big break.

No Transformations

They never transform and are purely auspicious. But combinations shape the benefactor type:

With Chang/Qu: cultured benefactors — teachers, scholars.

With Lu Cun/Hua Lu: benefactors bring money.

With Hua Quan: benefactors are leaders who give power.

With malefics: benefactors exist but help is bumpy, or the helper has their own troubles.

Reading Order After You Cast the Chart

When you see Kui/Yue:

  1. Which palace — where benefactors appear.
  2. Tian Kui or Tian Yue — yang or yin, male or female.
  3. Which stars share the palace — the type and manner of help.
  4. Check Fu/Bi — together, strongest benefactor luck.
  5. Check cycles — years when Kui/Yue activate bring benefactors.
  6. Do you actively maintain relationships with those who helped you?
