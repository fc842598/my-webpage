The Decade Moving Through the Property Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Ten Years of Home, Property and Security

When the decade Life reaches the natal Property position, the ten-year theme is family, real estate, housing and inner security. That palace's stars and transformations decide whether the decade settles a home or brings change and restructuring.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

When a decade brings a strong urge to buy, renovate and settle a home, the decade often moves through Property; for these ten years home and security become the main thread.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Property governs not only housing but family relations, inner stability and family resources. Lu, Quan and auspicious stars favor settling and buying, while Empty or malefics bring moves; major asset decisions still rest on budget, title and proper procedures.

The Underlying Logic

When the decade Life reaches natal Property, the axis turns to family, real estate and housing.

The Property star sets whether the decade settles, buys or changes.

Transformation activation decides property purchase and family upgrades, or a housing lesson.

Empty or malefics bring moves, repairs and change, and major decisions follow proper procedures.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the decade Life reaches natal Property
  2. Read the Property star to set settling or change
  3. Check transformations: purchase, family upgrade or housing lesson
  4. Look for Empty or malefics to gauge moves and repairs
  5. Separate property, family relations and inner security
  6. With budget, title and proper procedures, give a conditional reading

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking the Property decade as only buying and ignoring family and security

Mistake 2: Declaring the decade turbulent at Empty without reading transformations

Mistake 3: Encouraging a large purchase with incomplete information
