Is Zuo You Shou Yuan a Real Pattern or Forced? Its Conditions Are Too Broad, So Examine It

Rhyme calls it a pattern when Zuo Fu and You Bi guard the Life Palace, but the two arrange by year stem with broad conditions that many charts can meet, so naming it a pattern on that alone is forced. See whether the core star, triple-direction and transformations support it before counting it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Judging a pattern is like inspecting goods; a famous name is not enough, and you must check whether the conditions are strict or many charts can meet them, which usually means it is not a true pattern.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some grant themselves an elite pattern at the sight of the two stars and rejoice without substance; the issue is not a bad star but failing to separate real from forced, taking broad conditions as a hard threshold.

The Underlying Logic

Zuo Fu and You Bi arrange by birth-year stem with limited position pairs and high frequency, so guarding the Life Palace alone is not rare.

A true pattern needs an exalted core star, a good triple-direction net and transformation activation; the two are only one support and cannot carry the name alone.

Patterns with broad conditions anyone can meet are usually forced, so stay cautious without self-doubt or empty delight.

Schools differ and such differences are presented side by side; the baseline here is core star plus triple-direction plus transformations.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First see whether the two stars guard life, then check how rare that is given the year stem
  2. See whether the Life Palace core star is exalted and self-standing
  3. Count the triple-direction net and transformation activation for real foundation
  4. If only the two stars guard life and the rest is ordinary, do not name a pattern lightly
  5. Understand school differences rather than being led by rhyme names
  6. Fix the ruler for judging patterns before concluding

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Granting an elite pattern at a famous name without checking strict conditions

Mistake 2: Taking a frequent combination as rare and rejoicing emptily

Mistake 3: Judging pattern level from the two stars alone without core, triple-direction and transformations
