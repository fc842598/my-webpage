Learn Zi Wei/Major Stars

How the Fourteen Major Stars Learn Best: Follow Your Star Nature

Tian Ji suits deep reasoning, Tan Lang suits broad exposure, Wu Qu suits hands-on practice, Tian Tong suits visuals and atmosphere, Zi Wei suits systematic frameworks. Choosing a method that fits your star nature is far more efficient than forcing one method on everyone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

Learning is not about who studies harder, but whether the method fits; with the right method, the same time yields several times more.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some copy a method that worked for others and end up more exhausted; it is not that you lack effort, but that the method does not match your star nature.

The Underlying Logic

Deep-study type (Tian Ji, Tai Yin, Tian Liang): suits reasoning, close reading and note-taking; dislikes being too scattered.

Broad-exposure type (Tan Lang, Lian Zhen, Po Jun): suits parallel subjects and cross-connection; dislikes grinding one book.

Hands-on type (Wu Qu, Qi Sha, Tian Xiang): suits learning by doing and trial and error; dislikes only listening without practice.

Systematic type (Zi Wei, Tian Fu): suits building a framework first then filling details; dislikes diving into details before seeing the whole.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read your Life Palace major star to know whether you lean deep, broad, hands-on or systematic
  2. Pick a current topic and choose a method by your type
  3. Deep-study: list questions, read closely and take reasoning notes
  4. Broad-exposure: scan three subjects first then converge, draw connection maps
  5. Hands-on: find a small project to do directly, look things up when wrong
  6. Systematic: build an outline framework first, then fill sections and periodically review the whole

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Copying others methods without considering your own star nature

Mistake 2: Relying only on hours studied without adjusting the method

Mistake 3: Blaming yourself for being slow when the method simply does not fit
