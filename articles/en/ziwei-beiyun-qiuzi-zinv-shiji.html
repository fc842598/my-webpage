Trying to Conceive in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Children Palace, Decade and Year for Child Affinity and Timing (Tendency, Not Destiny)

When to have a child, how strong the child affinity and how smooth the process traditionally read the Children palace (the child's state and affinity), Property (household capacity) and Spouse (whether the couple is aligned), then decade/year for activated periods. But the chart reflects only tendency and timing; whether and when conception is possible and whether medical help is needed is entirely decided by formal reproductive-health exams and doctors—never replace care with the chart or limit yourself by it.

Many trying to conceive are under huge pressure and match every monthly result to the chart, growing anxious; the Children palace can pace reference, but the body belongs to medicine.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking which years favor children, how the child affinity looks or what to do with no result, the chart can organize pace while stressing that formal exams and care come first.

The Underlying Logic

Children palace sets affinity/state tendency: steady, grounded with Lu/Ke/Kui/Yue traditionally means close parent-child affinity, harmony and a smoother process; malefics/Ji/Kong/Jie is read as later arrival, a winding process or a relationship needing more work—a tendency, never a verdict on ability, and a fallen stressed palace never means infertility.

Decade/year set activation: a decade walking Children/Property, or a year whose Hong Luan/Tian Xi or Lu/Ke activates Children, is traditionally a window when pregnancy and parenting topics stir—a good phase to schedule trying, nourish the body and relax, but it only means the matter is touched, never guaranteeing or denying an outcome.

Spouse/Property set capacity: a child concerns a couple and household; steady Spouse with aligned partners and steady Property with housing/economic capacity make the process calmer; forcing timing amid relationship tension or unstable Property adds pressure, and the psychological state of trying itself affects the body—align the relationship and life rhythm first.

Medical priority is paramount: after a year of regular trying (six months over 35), both partners should seek a formal reproductive-medicine workup; age, ovulation, sperm quality, tubes and hormones have clear medical markers, and follow doctors promptly if assisted reproduction is needed. No "childless by fate / time not yet" claim replaces exams, and negative chart signals must not cause anxiety or surrender; tendency is reference only, health follows doctors.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Children only as a tendency for affinity and process smoothness
  2. See when decade/year activate Children/Property and schedule trying in a fitting phase
  3. Read Spouse/Property to confirm alignment and household capacity before proceeding calmly
  4. Try regularly, nourish the body, manage stress, and never self-limit from chart signals
  5. After the due time with no pregnancy, both partners seek a formal reproductive workup
  6. Follow doctors for treatment or assisted reproduction; the chart never replaces medical judgment

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Equating a stressed Children palace with infertility, self-limiting and skipping care

Mistake 2: Trying only in "good years" while ignoring exams, age and both partners' health

Mistake 3: Replacing reproductive workups with fate claims, delaying needed intervention

Back to Your Own Chart

The Children palace and decade/year help you understand child-affinity tendency and arrange life calmly, but fertility is medical: test when needed, treat when needed, never let a chart conclude for you or add to the anxiety of trying.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to trying to conceive, child affinity and timing before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the trying to conceive, child affinity and timing line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
