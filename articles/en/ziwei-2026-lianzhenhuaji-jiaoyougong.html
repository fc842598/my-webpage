2026 Lian Zhen Hua Ji in the Friends Palace: Friends and Colleagues May Let You Down; Settle Terms First

With Lian Zhen Hua Ji in the Friends Palace, friends and colleagues may let you down or fall out over money or feelings, and verbal cooperation can turn later. Put rules and accounts first rather than sparing face, and relationships last longer.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Lian Zhen Hua Ji in the Friends Palace is like a checkup on friendships for the year; those with clear accounts and boundaries stay, while those relying only on vague loyalty tend to part.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some guarantee for friends, partner without writing or take the blame for others and then fall out; the issue is not depth of friendship but sparing face when rules should have been named.

The Underlying Logic

The Friends Palace describes friends, colleagues and peers, and Lian Zhen Hua Ji tightens measure, accounts and boundaries, breeding misunderstanding and falling out.

It tests clarity before conflict: naming money, roles, exit and responsibility in writing protects the friendship later.

With gathered malefics it brings dragging feet, shifted blame and loyalty lost over money; with auspicious stars and Hua Ke someone mediates.

For partnerships, loans and guarantees, rely on a qualified lawyer, proper channels and a written contract.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the annual Friends Palace and see which relationships tend to trouble this year
  2. Before partnerships, loans and guarantees, clarify terms and accounts in writing
  3. Separate goodwill from responsibility and do not agree to vague matters to spare face
  4. Before helping, assess what you can bear rather than taking unlimited blame
  5. In misunderstanding, reconcile accounts and issues first without emotional reopening
  6. At year-end identify which relationships survived clear accounting and deserve depth

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Sparing face over rules, then falling out harder afterward

Mistake 2: Taking a friend affairs as unlimited responsibility and bearing the blame

Mistake 3: Blaming the other side without examining whether terms were ever clear
