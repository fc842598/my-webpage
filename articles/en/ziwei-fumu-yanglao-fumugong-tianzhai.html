Parental Elderly Care in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Parents and Tian Zhai Palace Analysis

As parents age, elderly care becomes an issue many middle-aged people must face. From the chart perspective, the combination of Parents Palace (relations with parents) and Tian Zhai Palace (family/residence) reflects the role you play in parental care and potential problems. Understanding in advance helps with planning.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24

Reading relations with parents from Parents Palace: Parents Palace has auspicious stars—good relations with parents, parents can support you, communication during care is smooth. Parents Palace has malefics—tension in relations with parents, may have conflicts since childhood, easily argue over care methods during old age. Parents Palace Jealousy—relations with parents are a long-term lesson, heavy responsibility during care but also easily have complaints. Parents Palace empty—unstable relations with parents, sometimes close sometimes distant, need to look at opposite palace (Health Palace). Reading elderly care living arrangements from Tian Zhai Palace: Tian Zhai Prosperity—sufficient family assets, able to provide good living conditions for parents (buy house, hire caregiver, good nursing home). Tian Zhai Jealousy—lessons in residence and family assets, may have conflicts over parents housing. Tian Zhai has auspicious stars—good family atmosphere, suitable for living with parents or nearby. Tian Zhai has malefics—friction in family, living with parents may cause much conflict, suitable for nearby but not cohabiting. Common problems in elderly care: first, "care responsibility distribution"—among siblings who cares, who pays, easily causes conflict. This is more prominent for those with many malefics in Siblings Palace. Second, "medical decisions"—when parents are sick, treatment plans, cost sharing, whether to do surgery, need siblings to decide together. Third, "living arrangements"—living with parents, nearby, or nursing home, each has pros and cons, need to decide based on family situation. Fourth, "emotional companionship"—many elderly do not lack money, they lack companionship. Those with good Fortune Palace can better give parents emotional support. Elderly care planning advice: first, "communicate early"—do not wait until parents are unhealthy to discuss care, understand their wishes while they are still healthy. Second, "financial preparation"—parents pension, medical insurance, critical illness insurance should be understood and configured in advance. What This Question Is Really Asking

Practical case: Someone with Jealousy in Siblings Palace easily has long-term lessons with siblings—possibly parental favoritism, property distribution, or personality clashes. But Jealousy also means "deep bond"; if they can proactively communicate and resolve, siblings can become the longest-lasting supporters.

The Underlying Logic

Only looking at the fortune of relative palaces, ignoring that relationships can improve through cultivation.

Equating Jealousy in relative palaces with "relationship will definitely be bad," giving up proactive communication.

Not watching Decade/Annual changes, using static info to judge lifelong family relationships.

Treating chart relative tendencies as "fate," making no relationship effort.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Chart the relevant relative palaces (Siblings, Parents, Children) major stars and transformations.
  2. Understand your patterns and needs in this relationship.
  3. See Decade/Annual changes, judge the relationship theme of the current stage.
  4. Identify relationship "lessons" (Jealousy, malefics) and consciously face them.
  5. Proactively communicate—many conflicts stem from misunderstanding, resolved once spoken.
  6. Respect boundaries; everyone is an independent individual, do not try to change them.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: The chart only indicates relationship tendencies; actual quality depends on both parties cultivation.

Mistake 2: There is no "perfect family relationship"; every combination has strengths and challenges.

Mistake 3: If there are serious problems in family relationships (abuse, abandonment, etc.), seek professional help.
