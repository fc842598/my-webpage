Medical Records and the Health Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Use the chart as a question guide while keeping clinical evidence first.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-29 09:20

Record when symptoms started, how often they occur, medicines taken, and test results. Seek medical care for new or persistent problems. The Health Palace can frame questions for reflection, and annual or monthly layers can help organize a timeline. They cannot diagnose a condition or explain its cause. A chart pattern appearing at the same time as a symptom does not establish causation.

A generic annual health forecast cannot diagnose one person. Compare the individual's chart layers with symptoms, sleep, work, and clinical findings. Seek care for persistent symptoms; an auspicious star never cancels a medical result.
