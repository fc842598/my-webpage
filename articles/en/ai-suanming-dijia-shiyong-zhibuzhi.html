A low-cost trial feels easy to justify, but the real question is whether it tests the part of the product you actually care about next.

Price matters less than whether the trial shows real continuity, not just a cheaper one-off answer.

What matters first

The best trial checks the future experience, not only the current discount.

The current public boundary can be checked as 3 guest uses a day, 8 logged-in free uses a day, 80 member uses a day, and a current member price of 19.90 RMB.

The answers worth keeping usually explain basis, scene, and next verification order rather than only a verdict.

A practical scenario

If you care about follow-up continuity, the trial should prove context carry-over, not just show one short reply.

When one reading lands on chart structure, a real-life scene, and a next verification step at the same time, it becomes much easier to judge whether it is actually useful.

The boundary to remember

Cheap is not the same thing as worthwhile.

Whether the layer is free or paid, safer products let you verify in a smaller scope before asking for deeper commitment.

A safer order

  1. Write down the one experience you want to verify.
  2. Score only that after the trial.
  3. Do not let the low price pull you into the next layer automatically.
