Tian Wu in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Four-Horse Auspicious Star of Promotion, Inheritance and Metaphysical Affinity

Like Tian Ma, Tian Wu moves only through the four horse palaces, governing promotion, inheritance, ancestral blessing and metaphysical affinity—a star of being pushed forward or gifted. It cannot resist malefics itself and must attach to a strong major star and auspicious transformations to work, emptying with malefics—a classic assistant.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

Tian Wu is an invisible baton: it does not create, but makes it easier to receive promotion, inheritance and mentor gifts handed down—provided you can stand on your own.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People wait for pie in the sky at its sight, ignoring that it works only with auspicious stars and fails with malefics; assistants add to what exists—with real strength and position, you catch the promotion or inheritance.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Wu is auspicious, only in the four horse palaces, governing promotion/transfer, inheritance and ancestral gifts, plus religious and metaphysical affinity.

An assistant with no own anti-malefic force: it works with a strong major star and Lu/Quan/Ke, weakening greatly with malefics/emptiness.

In Career: promotion and transfer; in Property/Wealth: inheritance, gifts and resource reception; in Life/Happiness: intuition and metaphysical insight.

Decade/year activation often brings promotion, transfer, inheritance, gifts, or interest in mind-body-spirit fields.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate Tian Wu to see where promotion or inheritance lands
  2. Check whether its major star is strong and auspiciously transformed
  3. As assistant, strengthen yourself first; stabilize work and position
  4. In Career: actively seek promotion and transfer
  5. In Property/Wealth: watch for inheritance, gifts and resource reception
  6. With malefics, don't wait—build strength before activation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Waiting passively for good fortune at its sight

Mistake 2: Ignoring that it fails with malefics and needs a host star

Mistake 3: Hoping only for inheritance while making no effort

Back to Your Own Chart

Tian Wu is a star of reception: promotion and inheritance favor those ready to catch. Stand firm first, await its activation, and the opportunity handed down won't pass you by.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Tian Wu the wizard star before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Tian Wu the wizard star line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
