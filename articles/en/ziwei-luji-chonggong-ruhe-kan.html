How to Read Lu and Ji Clashing Across Opposite Palaces

When Lu and Ji guard opposite palaces and clash, the investment direction and sticking-point direction pull along one straight line. Lu gives resources and Ji gives attachment; both are wanted yet restrain each other, and the stronger side leads.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Lu-Ji clash is like a rope pulled at both ends, one the desired benefit, one the attachment not let go; the tighter the pull, the greater the inner friction.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some call a Lu-Ji clash bad, but it is two forces coexisting; the key is which side leads and whether the sticking point can transform.

The Underlying Logic

Lu in the home palace and Ji opposite (or vice versa), clashing along a line.

Lu governs investment and resources at one end, Ji attachment and sticking points at the other.

When the two are close in strength the pull is heaviest; a gap lets the strong end lead.

Read star brightness and trines to judge which end is stronger.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate whether Lu and Ji guard opposite palaces
  2. Read the Lu end resource-investment direction
  3. Read the Ji end attachment-sticking-point direction
  4. Compare star brightness and trine strength at both ends
  5. Strong end leads: follow the resource end, resolve the sticking-point end
  6. In action avoid wanting both, take one end first

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Calling a Lu-Ji clash bad and frightening yourself

Mistake 2: Wanting both ends without choosing, friction to the end

Mistake 3: Not comparing strength, concluding vaguely

Back to Your Own Chart

Lu-Ji clash reads how investment and sticking points pull along a line. Know which end is strong, take the resource end first and resolve the sticking point, so the energy is not lost to friction.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Lu and Ji clashing across opposite palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Lu and Ji clashing across opposite palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
