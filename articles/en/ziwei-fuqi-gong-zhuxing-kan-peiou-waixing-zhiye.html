Reading Your Spouse From the Spouse-Palace Major Star in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Looks, Temperament and Career Lean

The Spouse-palace major star sketches the type you are drawn to and tend to meet: Zi Wei mature and assertive, Sun outgoing and sunny, Tai Yin soft and meticulous, Tan Lang versatile and charming, Wu Qu capable and pragmatic. It gives a type template, not a fixed person—read with brilliance, malefics and opposite Career, avoiding rigid matching.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

The Spouse palace is your mate-selection radar's factory setting: not a specific person, but the temperament, looks and profession you most readily attract.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Used as a wanted poster, any deviation means incompatibility; it is only tendency, and real bonds need both Life palaces, synastry and reality—template as reference, not cage.

The Underlying Logic

The major star sets the spouse type: Zi Wei/Tian Fu mature and assertive, Sun outgoing responsible, Tai Yin gentle meticulous and home-oriented.

Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang types are clever, easygoing or older-steady; Wu Qu/Tian Xiang capable, rational, professional; Sha-Po-Lang/Tan strong, driven, charming.

Looks follow star nature: strong stars appear open and positive, fallen ones less so; Chang/Qu add refinement, Kui/Yue bearing, peach stars standout looks.

Career lean combines star nature with opposite Career: Sun-Liang-Chang-Lu public/education, Wu Qu/Tian Fu finance-management, Tan/Lian sales-art-entertainment—always synthesize, never fix by one star.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Spouse major star for the type tone
  2. Check brilliance for positive or reduced expression
  3. Add Chang/Qu, Kui/Yue and peach stars for looks and bearing
  4. Read opposite Career for profession and interaction style
  5. Remember this is a tendency template, not a fixed person
  6. Combine both Life palaces and real interaction for the final call

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Using the star as a wanted poster, rejecting near-misses

Mistake 2: Fixing looks and career by one star

Mistake 3: Reading Spouse alone, ignoring opposite and synastry

Back to Your Own Chart

The Spouse star is a mate-tendency map: who stirs you and enters your life most easily. Understand the tendency without being trapped by the template, and you neither miss the right person nor mistake type for fate.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to spouse traits from Spouse palace star before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the spouse traits from Spouse palace star line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
