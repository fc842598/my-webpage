Career Hua Lu into the Wealth Palace: Why It Means Income Reinvested, Not Payment Collected

Why the flying transformation points to resources and reinvestment rather than collected payment, and how to separate income from cash collection.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-17 09:36

When Hua Lu from the Career Palace flies into the Wealth Palace, it looks like "work brings money," but the meaning of that money can differ. Hua Lu describes resources and opportunities flowing in through work, pointing more to income and reinvestment than to completed payment collection. Reading it as "money arriving now" causes cash-flow mistakes.

Hua Lu and payment collection are not the same

Hua Lu represents resources, opportunities, and a smooth source. Career Hua Lu into Wealth shows that the business can bring income and resources. Yet after income arrives, it often goes back into production, stock, and turnover, rather than being profit in hand or a collected final payment. Collection is another step, seen through Hua Ji, the contract, and actual arrival.

Step-by-step flying-star check

  1. Confirm the source palace and landing palace of the transformation, seeing Career Hua Lu into Wealth.
  2. Understand that Hua Lu means resources and opportunities, not cash directly.
  3. Follow the work-to-income path to judge whether the money is new income or reinvestment.
  4. Find collection-related Hua Ji, contract terms, and payment periods separately.
  5. Record "income ability" and "collection progress" apart before judging cash flow.

The flying star needs the whole chain

Looking at one Hua Lu easily equates "can earn" with "money is in hand." Real cash flow follows Career, Wealth, contract, and payment period together. Placing the flying star back in the whole business chain shows where resources stop and where they flow after entering.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and mark the palace-stem transformations of Career and Wealth.
  2. Confirm the direction of Hua Lu and understand its meaning first.
  3. Along the work-to-income path, judge reinvestment versus collection.
  4. Then check Hua Ji and contract payment periods for actual arrival.
  5. Keep the conclusion conditional rather than reading Hua Lu as instant cash.
