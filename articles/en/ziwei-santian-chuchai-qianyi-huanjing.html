A Three-Day Business Trip and the Travel Palace

A short trip calls for task-specific questions, not a fixed travel prediction.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-07 09:08

A three-day trip does not require a grand relocation reading. The Travel Palace can help frame how someone handles an unfamiliar setting, but flight delays and safety come from real conditions rather than one palace.

Different trip purposes

A buyer checking goods needs acceptance criteria and payment terms. A researcher attending a conference needs contacts and follow-up. Compare the Life and Travel axis, then add Career, Friends or Wealth according to the task.

Reading order

Start with the natal axis, name the actual work question, and add annual timing last. Prepare documents and backup plans regardless of whether a chart symbol looks favorable.
