How to Plan Finances With Irregular Income: A Thicker Safety Net and Smoother Spending

For freelancers and small-business owners with irregular income, first raise the emergency fund to about a year of basic expenses, spread the annual income across slow and busy seasons into a monthly salary, and cover fixed costs and savings before flexible spending. Arrange insurance and retirement early, do not treat a big payment as normal, and avoid expanding blindly when income is high.
