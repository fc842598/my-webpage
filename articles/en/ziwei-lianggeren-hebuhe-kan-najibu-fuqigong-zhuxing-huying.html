Are You Two Compatible in Zi Wei Dou Shu? Skip Star Sign Matching and Follow These Steps

Synastry is not which star pairs with which. It is whether each person's Spouse star echoes the other's Life star, whether relating modes complement, and whether birth-year Hua Ji clash—three steps and compatibility becomes clear.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Synastry checks whether two systems mesh long-term: the partner type you need (Spouse) matching who the other actually is (Life) makes the relationship effortless.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People compare only major stars and zodiacs, ignoring that Spouse is the partner you want; personalities look matched but daily relating grates.

The Underlying Logic

First read each person's Spouse major star: the ideal partner type and core relationship need.

Then see whether the other's Life star fits the type your Spouse wants, checking both directions—mutual echo is best.

Then read relating modes: whether the two Life stars' rhythms (fast/slow, inner/outer, rational/feeling) complement rather than clash.

Finally check whether birth-year Hua Ji and malefics strike each other's key palaces, avoiding structures where your need is repeatedly triggered by their innate weak spot.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Lay out both charts and read each Spouse star and core need
  2. Match your Spouse Palace against their Life star for your wanted type
  3. Reverse it to confirm mutual echo rather than one-sided fit
  4. Compare Life-star rhythms and mark complements and likely conflicts
  5. Check whether each side's birth-year Hua Ji and malefics strike Life/Spouse/Happiness
  6. Synthesize: matched needs, complementary rhythms and no hard clash mean fit

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Comparing only major stars or zodiacs, not the Spouse Palace

Mistake 2: Matching one direction without checking mutual echo

Mistake 3: Ignoring mutual Hua Ji clash and treating chronic risk as small friction

Back to Your Own Chart

Synastry is not romantic destiny but relationship due diligence: matching needs, compatible rhythms and no hard clash make a bond worth long-term investment.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to synastry compatibility steps before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the synastry compatibility steps line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
