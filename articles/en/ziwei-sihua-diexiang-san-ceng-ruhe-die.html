Layering the Four Transformations in Zi Wei Dou Shu Across Natal, Decade and Year

Transformations have three layers: the natal sets the baseline, the decade the ten-year activation and the year the current trigger. The same kind stacked in one palace strengthens, while different kinds meeting, such as Lu meeting Ji, require the net effect. Stacking the three shows whether a matter is repeatedly strengthened or mutually restrained.

Some read only the natal or only the year and find the same matter reading now good now bad; stacking the three makes the weight clear.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to count multiple transformations in one palace. Locate by layer, then see whether they are the same or different kinds, then take the net effect; the same kind stacks and different kinds offset or transform.

The Underlying Logic

The natal layer is fixed at birth, the lifelong baseline.

The decade layer changes every ten years and activates the domains lit in this stage.

The year layer changes annually and sets the specific trigger.

The same kind stacked strengthens and makes opportunity real; different kinds meeting restrain, and you check whether auspicious stars or Ke can resolve it.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First mark the natal, decade and year transformations by layer
  2. See whether the layers land in the same palace or line
  3. Treat the same kind stacked as strengthening and different kinds as restraint
  4. Count the auspicious and harsh stars in the triple-direction for the net effect
  5. Then use monthly luck to time the strengthened matter
  6. Conclude on the net tendency, not judging from one layer

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Concluding from one transformation layer

Mistake 2: Judging different kinds meeting as all bad without the net effect and resolution

Mistake 3: Marking layers but never stacking them
