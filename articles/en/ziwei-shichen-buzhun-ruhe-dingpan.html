What to Do When the Birth Hour Is Uncertain in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Reverse-Confirming the Chart

Zi Wei Dou Shu is sensitive to the birth hour; one slot off and the Life Palace and stars can change entirely. When the hour is uncertain, do not guess; set a range, then use the person's character, appearance, career and past major events such as schooling, marriage, moves, illness and losses to verify year by year, reverse-deriving the hour that best fits, which is fixing the chart.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some pick an hour at random or give up when uncertain, leaving a wrong chart on which all later judgment is built.

The Underlying Logic

First ask the rough range of birth time, whether morning or afternoon or only the date, and list the possible hours.

Hour boundaries, such as exactly nine or eleven, need care; first set charts for the adjacent two or three hours.

Use the person's character, appearance and career tendency to verify the Life Palace main star and first rule out clearly mismatched hours.

Then use past major events such as schooling, marriage, property, moves, illness, losses and promotion to verify year by year, fixing the hour that matches most accurately.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Set the rough range of birth time
  2. List the possible hours, and for boundaries set adjacent charts separately
  3. Use character, appearance and career to first rule out clearly mismatched ones
  4. Use past major events to verify year by year and find the best match
  5. Fix the most fitting hour and then set the formal chart
  6. If still uncertain, say so honestly rather than forcing a conclusion

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Picking an hour at random when it is uncertain

Mistake 2: Fixing the hour from one or two small matters

Mistake 3: Forcing a conclusion when it cannot be fixed

Back to Your Own Chart
