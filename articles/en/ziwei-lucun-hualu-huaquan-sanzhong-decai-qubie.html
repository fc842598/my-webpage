Lu Cun, Hua Lu and Hua Quan in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Three Kinds of Financial Gain

Lu Cun is stable stock and retained wealth (yin earth, fixed income, assets); Hua Lu is flowing opportunity and new income sources (encounter, chance, smooth gain); Hua Quan is seizing resources through control, action and status. All bring gain but respectively keep, attract and contest—only combined do they reveal one's earning pattern.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Can you keep it—Lu Cun; where chances come from—Hua Lu; can you actively scale and hold sway—Hua Quan. Three questions, three wealth signals.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Hua Lu alone is read as big wealth, yet without Lu Cun to keep or Quan to scale, money passes through; Lu Cun without Lu or Quan only guards. Read the division to avoid misjudging wealth.

The Underlying Logic

Lu Cun: yin earth from the year stem, governing stock, fixed income, savings and retained wealth, flanked by Yang/Tuo—static, passive, cumulative.

Hua Lu: a transformation governing encounter and smooth increment—opportunity money, network money, new sources—dynamic and flowing, needing a vault to receive it.

Hua Quan: control, action, authority and expansion, contesting and amplifying resources through ability, position and decision—the force that actively scales.

Best structure combines Lu/Quan with Lu Cun: Hua Lu opens the source, Hua Quan scales, Lu Cun locks results; Lu without a vault is money in-and-out, guarding without transformation stagnates.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find Lu Cun to judge stock and retention
  2. Find Hua Lu (birth-year + decade) for income sources and chance direction
  3. Find Hua Quan for active expansion and resource control
  4. Compare the three to classify you as conserver, opportunity or pioneer type
  5. Lu without vault: prioritize savings and assets to receive it
  6. Decades where Lu and Quan meet are windows to enlarge wealth

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Blending the three kinds of gain

Mistake 2: Predicting wealth from Hua Lu without a vault to keep it

Mistake 3: Settling for Lu Cun without pursuing Lu/Quan growth

Back to Your Own Chart

The three answer different questions—keep, attract, contest. See which cards you hold and which you lack, and you know whether your wealth needs a source, scale, or a vault.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Lu Cun vs Hua Lu vs Hua Quan wealth before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Lu Cun vs Hua Lu vs Hua Quan wealth line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
