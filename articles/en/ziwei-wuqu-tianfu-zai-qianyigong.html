Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Travel Palace: Pragmatic and Steady Away From Home, Building Thick Development Elsewhere

The Travel Palace reads the external environment, development elsewhere and the state away from home. Wu Qu governs pragmatic execution while Tian Fu governs solid storage, so together, when away or developing elsewhere, one can both work steadily and hold resources, suiting working out of town, postings, long-term travel and buying property elsewhere. The risk is conservatism and seeking stability away from home to the point of missing chances, so the key is leaving room for active pioneering alongside steadiness.

People with Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Travel Palace seem reliable, able to carry matters and hold steady away from home, and easily gain a foothold elsewhere; when seeking too much stability, they can lag in a new environment.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the away-from-home style with Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Travel Palace. Understand it reads the external environment, then see how pragmatism and storage combine for development elsewhere.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Travel Palace gives pragmatic, results-oriented, executable work away from home, suiting building a foothold elsewhere through expertise and ability.

Tian Fu there gives steady work away from home and skill at holding resources, able to slowly build the situation and reserves in a different place.

Together they mean able to do and able to hold away from home: suiting development elsewhere, postings and long-term travel; with Lu Cun, Hua Lu and auspicious stars, wealth away from home is steady and property can be bought.

With malefics and Ji, one becomes conservative away from home and afraid of change, easily only holding without attacking elsewhere and missing expansion windows.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Tian Fu share the Travel Palace and check their brightness
  2. Evaluate the opportunities and resources of developing elsewhere, separating steady holding from expansion
  3. Use the strength of pragmatic execution to gain a foothold in the new environment
  4. Actively set a budget and attempts for expansion, avoiding only holding without attacking
  5. Build stable networks and daily routines elsewhere to put down roots
  6. With the decade and year, see when going away is activated before deciding to stay or leave

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Tian Fu in the Travel Palace only as able to earn and hold money away from home while ignoring expansion elsewhere

Mistake 2: Seeking too much stability elsewhere and not daring to invest in new chances

Mistake 3: Turning holding money into pettiness, affecting cooperation and networks elsewhere
