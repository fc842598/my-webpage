Tai Yang in Travel Palace: Radiating Outside, Benefactors Far Away

Tai Yang in Travel makes you warm and popular outside, suited to developing away. Benefactors are far away, but don't overwork yourself out there.

Main Stars2026-08-21 10:15

Tai Yang in Travel means you "light up" outside — more cheerful, confident, and popular away from home. You're suited to leaving home to develop; benefactors and opportunities come easily outside. But one issue: you try too hard out there, wanting to do everything well and help everyone, exhausting yourself.

Travel palace shows travel luck and your state outside. Tai Yang here gives natural magnetism in the outer world — others immediately find you warm, reliable, approachable. Temple/prosperous Tai Yang means smooth sailing away; fallen means much toil with little return. Tai Yang with Kui/Yue brings extremely strong benefactor luck away; with Hua Ji, watch disputes and health outside.

Traits of Tai Yang in Travel

Popular outside — warm and cheerful, strangers quickly become friends.

Benefactors far away — key opportunities and mentors aren't local.

Suited to developing away — luck noticeably improves after leaving home.

Good external image — first impressions are usually "sunny, reliable."

Watch overwork — you push too hard outside, easily neglecting rest and health.

Travel Luck by Combination

Tai Yang with Tian Liang — elder benefactors outside; steady rise.

Tai Yang with Kui/Yue — extremely strong benefactor luck; help always arrives.

Tai Yang with Tian Ma — frequent travel/relocation; fame and opportunity through movement.

Tai Yang with Hua Lu — easy to earn away; strong social monetization.

Tai Yang with Hua Ji — disputes or health issues away; don't overdo it.

Advice for Tai Yang in Travel

Be brave and go out — your luck is far away; don't stay stuck.

Leverage social strengths — you're naturally suited to people-facing work.

Mind rest — no matter how hard you push outside, eat and sleep on time; health is capital.

Don't force it for face — you don't need to appear "fine" to everyone.

Visit home often — you shine outside, but family needs your warmth too.

Reading Order

For Tai Yang in Travel:

  1. Check brightness — temple/prosperous: smooth away; fallen: toil away.
  2. Check co-stars — Tian Liang: benefactors; Tian Ma: movement; Lu: earning.
  3. Check Life — are you two different people at home vs outside.
  4. Check cycles — when a cycle reaches Travel, external development is key.
  5. Check annuals — years when annual reaches Travel suit trips and moves.
  6. Does your light outside also shine on yourself?
