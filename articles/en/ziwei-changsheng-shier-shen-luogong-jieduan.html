How to Use the Life-Cycle Spirits by Palace: Birth/Emperor vs Death/Extinction in the Life Palace

The same major star on rising stages (Birth, Official, Emperor) versus falling ones (Decline, Illness, Death, Extinction) shows visibly different drive and pace. The spirits label each palace's matter with a life-cycle position—a second ruler alongside brightness.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09

Reading the spirits by palace is not labeling luck but whether a matter's qi rises or falls: Life on Emperor is fiercely driven, Life on Grave/Nurture is slower to warm and more patient.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why you or a matter always lag, or surge and fade fast, adds a dimension when you check the Life-Cycle stage of the Life or relevant palace.

The Underlying Logic

Life on Birth/Capping/Official/Emperor: progressive, quick to learn, rising through setbacks; Emperor especially pioneers but risks breaking from being too forceful—suited to opening new ground.

Life on Decline/Illness/Death: slower rhythm that sees difficulty first; Death is not bad but slow, steady and accumulation-based, better for maintaining, research and patience, not high-frequency competition.

Life on Grave/Extinction/Embryo/Nurture: Grave is reserved and good at settling; Extinction breaks then rebuilds with strong transformation; Embryo/Nurture are late bloomers needing gestation—often stronger in later stages.

It works elsewhere: Wealth on Emperor pursues money aggressively, on Grave/Extinction relies on settled sources; Career on Birth stays fresh, on Decline/Illness/Death hits bottlenecks; combined with the decade you see when a phase ends.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the Life palace stage to gauge innate tempo—progressive or slow to warm
  2. Read the asked palace (Wealth, Career, Spouse) for that matter's qi stage
  3. Push rising-stage palaces and seize windows; guard, nurture and accumulate in falling ones
  4. Do not call Grave/Extinction/Embryo/Nurture bad; read them as settling or breaking-then-rebuilding
  5. Combine with the decade stage to see whether the current ten years rise or fall overall
  6. Keep major stars and transformations primary; use the stage only to adjust pace and expectation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Despairing at Life on Death/Extinction when it is a tempo and stage, not doomed failure

Mistake 2: Using the spirits only in Life and not labeling Wealth, Career and other palaces

Mistake 3: Pitting life-cycle against brightness when power and cycle are two complementary rulers

Back to Your Own Chart

The spirits by palace mark where each matter stands in its life now. Reading your Life and key palaces' stages keeps you from forcing when you should nurture and from underrating yourself when it is time to rise.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to reading life stages from where the Life-Cycle spirits land before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the reading life stages from where the Life-Cycle spirits land line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
