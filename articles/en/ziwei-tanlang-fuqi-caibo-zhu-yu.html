Tan Lang in Spouse Versus Wealth in Zi Wei Dou Shu

The same star can raise different questions in the Spouse and Wealth Palaces: relationship boundaries or conversion into income.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-27 09:06

Tan Lang is often reduced to charm, networking, or appetite for novelty. That shorthand becomes misleading when the palace is ignored. In the Spouse Palace, first ask how two people negotiate time, social life, and boundaries. In the Wealth Palace, ask how contacts turn into paid work and collected cash.

Give each palace its own evidence

A partner who attends many industry events may need a conversation about shared time. The chart cannot label that person faithful or unfaithful. A consultant with many introductions may still have weak pricing or slow collection. The number of contacts is not revenue.

Read the star with its neighboring and opposite structure, note any transformation and its time layer, and then choose a matching real check. For a relationship, discuss expectations directly. For money, trace inquiries, contracts, delivery, costs, and receipts. The star may help frame a question, while the palace decides which question belongs there.
