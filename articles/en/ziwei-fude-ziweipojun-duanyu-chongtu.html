Contradictory Zi Wei Dou Shu Sayings About Zi Wei and Po Jun

Why opposing traditional sayings require chart conditions, not a quick vote.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-07 09:08

Two traditional sayings can describe the same Zi Wei and Po Jun combination in opposite ways. A short saying often leaves out where the stars sit, what supports them and which time period is under discussion. Treat it as a question about conditions.

Test the combination

Confirm whether both stars are in the Fortune Palace or merely connected to it. Then inspect supporting stars, transformations and the opposite Wealth Palace. A founder who chooses to rebuild a team and a caregiver whose schedule changes without consent may both feel busy, yet their situations differ sharply.

Reading order

Locate the stars, examine the full palace relationship, and only then add the current cycle. Keep whichever interpretation matches both chart conditions and observable life details. A phrase from a book cannot diagnose health or finances.
