Career Choice and Change in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Career Shows How You Work and Travel Shows Outer Chance

For career choice or change, first read the Career Palace, which shows how you best work and the role type; then Travel for outer opportunity, platform and far places; Wealth shows the income form, fixed or variable. The three set direction, and the decade and year set timing, steadier than liking alone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some change only by wanting to, then find the work style unfit and income unstable; Career, Travel and Wealth already show the fitting form.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is which direction to take: Career sets the work style, Travel finds the outer platform and Wealth the income preference, and the decade confirms timing and support.

The Underlying Logic

Career stars set how you work, with roles fitting initiative, planning, coordination or execution.

Travel sets the outer stage: platform size and far or outward opportunity.

Wealth sets the income form: steady salary or project-based variation.

When the decade activates Career or Travel with Lu-Quan meeting, change or promotion tends to have a smoother window.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read Career stars for work style and role
  2. Then read Travel for platform and far opportunity
  3. Read Wealth to confirm income-form preference
  4. Combine the three to set direction
  5. Use the decade and year to find change or promotion windows
  6. Change with your accumulated base, not on a passing impulse

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Changing only on liking or passing emotion

Mistake 2: Reading only Career without Travel and Wealth

Mistake 3: Ignoring decade timing and forcing a change against the trend
