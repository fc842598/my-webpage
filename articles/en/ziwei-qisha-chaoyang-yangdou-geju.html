Qi Sha Facing and Aspiring Toward the Dipper: Why One Seven Killings Chart Leads and Another Just Struggles

When Seven Killings sits in Yin/Shen/Zi/Wu with Zi Wei and Tian Fu across, plus assistants and good transformations, it forms the Facing/Aspiring pattern; malefics and void stars breaking it reduce the chart to mere struggle.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

This pattern is never just "Seven Killings in the Life Palace is good." It asks whether an pioneering general-star has the emperor-star across and a full supporting cast.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many people slap the "Facing the Dipper" label on any Seven Killings Life Palace. The real questions are: is it exalted, who sits across, does the trine offer support, and are malefics undermining it?

The Underlying Logic

Seven Killings in Shen or Wu is "facing," in Yin or Zi "aspiring"; Yin/Shen are exalted and Zi/Wu strong, so position sets the baseline.

The opposite Travel Palace must hold Zi Wei and Tian Fu, so the drive has steering and resources; otherwise it is solo brute force.

The trine needs Zuo Fu/You Bi, Kui/Yue, Wen Chang/Wen Qu, Lu Cun and Hua Quan/Hua Ke to form a real team; the cleaner it is, the bigger the responsibility it can carry.

When Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Huo Xing, Ling Xing, void stars or Hua Ji break through, pioneering turns into repeated churn—the classical rule "malefics break the pattern."

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Seven Killings sits in Yin/Shen/Zi/Wu; otherwise do not apply this pattern
  2. Check whether Zi Wei and Tian Fu occupy the opposite Travel Palace; without them, drop a grade
  3. Count assistants and Lu/Quan/Ke in the trine—the fuller the team, the purer the pattern
  4. Then count the four malefics, voids and Hua Ji to see whether and where the pattern breaks
  5. Use exaltation to tell whether the force drives real building or just reckless collision
  6. Finally use the decade limit to see which ten years activate it; a pattern is not an instant payout

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Claiming the pattern from Seven Killings alone while ignoring position and support

Mistake 2: Reading one star without counting assistants and malefics in the trine

Mistake 3: Treating a pattern as a guarantee and forgetting that an inactive decade leaves even a general dormant

Back to Your Own Chart

The essence is a tough pioneering star, a stable rear and a complete team. Count these three on your own chart and you will know whether your Seven Killings is a capable general or unsupported lone courage.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Qi Sha Facing/Aspiring Toward the Dipper pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Qi Sha Facing/Aspiring Toward the Dipper pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
