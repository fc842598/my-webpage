What This Question Is Really Asking

Some claim infringement when another sign looks a bit like theirs, without checking their registered classes or proving confusion, only to find the other side uses it on different goods and the claim fails.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm whether your mark is registered and its approved classes
  2. Check whether the other sign is identical or similar to yours
  3. Judge whether the goods or services are the same or similar and confusion is likely
  4. Read the Career Palace to judge brand operations and the impact
  5. Collect evidence of the other party’s use and your own brand-use records
  6. Send a letter or sue, with the registered scope and professional legal advice as the basis

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Looking only at whether signs resemble each other without checking classes

Mistake 2: Not proving likelihood of confusion, making the claim weak

Mistake 3: Not keeping use evidence, leaving brand influence unsupported
