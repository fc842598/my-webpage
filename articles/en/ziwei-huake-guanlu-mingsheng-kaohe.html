Ke in the Career Palace: When Reputation and Performance Reviews Do Not Match

How to separate the reputation brought by Ke from actual career output and performance evidence.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Ke in the Career Palace often brings a professional image, good reputation, and visibility. But reputation is not the same as a performance review. Some people are well regarded with average results, while others produce strong work without being promoted.

What Ke actually covers

Ke leans toward reputation, clarity, recognition, and a polished way of presenting. In the Career Palace, it shows how others see the work, not how much is actually produced. Output still depends on the host star, Lu, Quan, and the real review.

Verification steps

  1. Confirm which stem layer produces Ke and that it truly lands in the Career Palace.
  2. Read the host star and check for Lu or Quan to judge real output and authority.
  3. List evidence of reputation, image, and external recognition in one column.
  4. List tasks, metrics, and review results in another column.
  5. Compare the two to see whether the gap is weak visibility or weak output.

Reading order

  1. Fix the stem layer and Ke's palace, then check the Career host star.
  2. Record reputation evidence and performance facts separately.
  3. Add Lu, Quan, and supporting stars to judge resources and authority.
  4. Check whether the current cycle activates Ke as a new opportunity.
  5. Write the answer as image plus actual output, not reputation alone.
