Learn Zi Wei/Four Transformations

The Route Formed by the Four Transformations

Lu Quan Ke Ji each govern a segment, together forming one complete route: Lu gives resources to start, Quan gives control to execute, Ke gives reputation to finish and Ji gives the lesson to work on. They are not four isolated buttons but one energy-flow line.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

The transformations are like four stages of a matter: having resources, control, being seen and a lesson; missing one stage is incomplete.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some take the transformations as four independent labels, missing the route; reading along the route shows how a matter proceeds.

The Underlying Logic

Lu: resources, opportunity, start.

Quan: control, execution, advance.

Ke: reputation, smoothing, finish.

Ji: lesson, sticking point, work area; four segments linked.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First recognize each transformation stage
  2. See which palace Lu is in, where resources start
  3. See how Quan Ke advance and finish
  4. See where Ji lands, the lesson
  5. Link transformations along the route
  6. Judge whether the route is smooth, where it stalls

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking transformations as isolated labels, not linking

Mistake 2: Seeing one transformation without the four segments

Mistake 3: Taking the route as fixed good or bad

Back to Your Own Chart

The transformation route asks how energy flows from resources to lesson. Read four segments linked, and you understand a matter complete direction.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the route formed by the Four Transformations before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the route formed by the Four Transformations line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
