An Empty Wealth Palace Borrowing the Opposite Stars: Why Label the Source When Reading Cash Flow

Why borrowed stars are a reference rather than an equal, and how to label their source before returning to the cash-flow reading.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-17 09:54

If the Wealth Palace is empty with no major star, reading cash flow borrows the stars of the opposite Blessing Palace for reference. But borrowing is not equating. The borrowed stars describe another palace's nature, and their source must be labeled when applied to Wealth. Treating the opposite palace directly as the Wealth Palace easily misaligns the cash-flow judgment.

Why an empty palace borrows the opposite

An empty palace means it has no major star of its own, and its energy is shown more through the opposite palace and the three directions. An empty Wealth Palace borrowing the opposite Blessing often means spending is closely tied to inner thoughts, enjoyment, and values. Yet this relationship is a reference, and the reading still returns to the actual cash in and out of Wealth rather than treating Blessing's meaning directly as earning ability.

Step-by-step borrowed-star check

  1. Confirm whether the Wealth Palace is empty, then see which palace is opposite and what stars it holds.
  2. Clearly label this as a borrowed opposite reference, not the palace's own major star.
  3. Understand the opposite stars' nature, then see how they affect spending.
  4. Combine the three directions and transformations to return to Wealth for cash flow.
  5. Label the source throughout rather than treating borrowed stars as the palace's own conclusion.

Borrowed stars are not a verdict

An empty palace does not mean no money or bad fortune, only a different reading that relies more on the opposite palace and the whole structure. Labeling the source clearly and pairing it with the real bill keeps the cash-flow judgment stable. Making a verdict at the sight of an empty palace is the easiest misreading.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and confirm the empty Wealth Palace and opposite stars.
  2. Label the borrowed-star source, then understand its nature.
  3. Return to Wealth for cash through the three directions and transformations.
  4. Check with a real bill rather than judging from an empty palace.
  5. Keep the conclusion conditional, separating the own palace from borrowed stars.
