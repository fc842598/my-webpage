Partnership Expenses in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Roles and Reimbursement

A profitable project can still strain the partner who advances the cash.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-07 09:08

A partnership can have a good relationship and still fail over unpaid expenses. The Friends Palace concerns cooperation, the Career Palace delivery duties, and the Wealth Palace who pays and when money returns. Revenue on a contract is not cash in the bank.

Cash timing matters

An event planner may advance venue fees while the client pays months later. A second partner may receive a share without carrying that cost. Compare the invoice, payment date and advance before interpreting a favorable Wealth Palace symbol.

What to do

Read the three palaces, then the relevant transformations and cycle. Set approval limits, receipts and reimbursement dates in writing. Use bank records and the contract to settle an actual dispute.
