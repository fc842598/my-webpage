How to Judge Lu and Quan Sharing One Palace: Resources Meet Control

Lu and Quan in one palace layer resources over drive, often showing many opportunities and strong control, but also a tendency to take on everything and overextend. First see whether the major star can bear it and what matter the palace represents.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Lu and Quan together are a dramatic combination: resources and drive in one place, making things happen. But whether it succeeds is one question; whether you exhaust yourself and carry everything alone is another.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Many declare Lu and Quan together excellent, but doubled energy still needs structure to carry it. With a weak major star and broken trines, resources and power instead become pressure and contest.

The Underlying Logic

Lu gives resources and Quan gives drive; in one palace the two energies stack onto the same matter.

The positive side shows opportunities in hand plus execution, quickly building momentum in that palace's area.

The negative side is strong control, reluctance to share power, taking everything on, overextension, or fighting for the lead.

The verdict depends on whether the major star and triple-direction can carry the doubled force.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Lu and Quan truly share one palace
  2. See what the palace's major star is and whether it can bear doubled energy
  3. Open the triple-direction for assistants and malefics
  4. Use the positive: pair resources with execution to build momentum fast
  5. Guard the negative: deliberately share power to avoid taking everything on and overextending
  6. Layer the decade to see when this energy peaks before choosing the pace of expansion

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring Lu and Quan together absolutely good without checking carrying conditions

Mistake 2: Ignoring the negative of doubled energy and skipping power-sharing and pacing

Mistake 3: Looking only at the combination without the star's strength; a weak star bearing heavy power becomes pressure

Back to Your Own Chart

Lu and Quan together are a strong hand, but a strong hand must be played well. With resources and control at once, learning to share power and leave margin preserves momentum better than relentless expansion.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to judging Lu and Quan sharing one palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the judging Lu and Quan sharing one palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
