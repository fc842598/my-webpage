Tian Fu Guarding Life: The Treasury Star's Steadiness, Conservatism and Hidden Edge

Tian Fu is lord of the southern dipper, yang earth, qi of command, master of treasury and property. Guarding Life it makes one steady, reliable, good at accumulation and coordination, valuing security and dignity, with treasury-like包容 and leadership potential, but conservative, face-conscious and risk-averse. With Lu it enriches; with empty/drain stars the vault empties—the key is measured enterprise within steadiness.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Tian Fu is a steward guarding a vault: its gift is storing and steadying, arranging resources, people and money, but fear of loss can make it hoard and refuse to invest in the future.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some mistake conservatism for steadiness and miss expansion windows; others predict great wealth from Tian Fu, yet a vault fills only with Lu—with Di Kong/Di Jie/Da Hao even a big vault leaks.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Fu is southern dipper lord, yang earth, qi of command, governing treasury, property, salary, storage and coordination—a vault, not a source; wealth fills it via Lu.

Life personality: steady, generous, trustworthy, dignity-conscious with leadership and planning drive, skilled at resource scheduling and logistics, conveying reliability.

It loves Lu Cun/Hua Lu (filled vault, wealth) and Zi Wei/Tian Xiang (effective ruler-minister); it fears emptiness (exposed empty vault) and malefics (conservatism hardening into anxious stubbornness).

Growth lesson: overcome excessive caution and face-saving, try measured enterprise and delegation, upgrading conservation into active management.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Tian Fu's palace and brilliance
  2. Check Lu Cun/Hua Lu to see whether the vault is filled
  3. Check empty/drain/malefic stars for leakage and conservatism
  4. Follow it into finance, administration, operations, management or real estate
  5. Practice investment, expansion and delegation within controlled risk
  6. In Lu-decades manage actively; in empty/malefic decades guard the vault

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Predicting wealth from Tian Fu without Lu in the vault

Mistake 2: Mistaking conservatism for steadiness and missing chances

Mistake 3: Assuming the vault is secure amid empty/drain stars

Back to Your Own Chart

Tian Fu Life naturally holds and steadies. Do not let fear of loss trap you: fill the vault, then advance measuredly when you see clearly—and your steadiness becomes trusted confidence, not conservatism.

  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
