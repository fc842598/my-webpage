Zi Wei Dou Shu on Probation Dismissal: Hiring Conditions and Proof

Dismissal during probation shows the Career Palace being activated and then blocked at the start. The employer must prove you do not meet the hiring conditions, which must be clear in advance and backed by assessment; otherwise it may be unlawful termination.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-29

Probation dismissal is not at-will; the employer must produce the hiring conditions and evidence that you do not meet them.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some are dismissed with a simple “not a fit,” without checking the hiring conditions and assessment or confirming they are still within probation, and give up compensation easily.

The Underlying Logic

Probation dismissal often appears when the Career Palace is activated and then meets Hua Ji and malefics, marking a blocked start.

To dismiss for not meeting hiring conditions, the employer must have set those conditions in advance, act within probation and hold assessment evidence.

Probation length is tied to the contract term; after it expires, probation-based dismissal is no longer allowed.

If the employer cannot prove it, you may claim unlawful termination and seek reinstatement or damages.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm whether you are still within probation and whether its length is lawful
  2. Ask the employer to state the specific hiring conditions not met
  3. Check whether those conditions were given in advance and backed by assessment
  4. Keep the contract, job description, assessments and communication records
  5. Read the Career Palace stars to judge a blocked start or an environment problem
  6. If the employer cannot prove it, claim unlawful termination, subject to arbitration

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Giving up when dismissed as “not a fit”

Mistake 2: Not checking hiring conditions and assessment evidence

Mistake 3: Being dismissed on probation grounds after the period has expired
