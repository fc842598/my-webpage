What to Do About Constant Fights and Bringing Up the Past: Patterns and Communication

Repeated fights and bringing up the past often mean core needs are not truly answered and emotions are not processed. Calmly find recurring themes and triggers, focus on the issue, replace blame with I-feel statements, and agree on pause and review mechanisms to express the need behind the old grievances. If communication is impossible long term or there is abuse or ongoing harm, take it seriously and seek help.
