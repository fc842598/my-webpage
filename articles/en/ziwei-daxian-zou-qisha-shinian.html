The Qi Sha Decade: Taking On Hard Problems Independently and Seeing Them Through

Qi Sha governs courage, execution, independence and tackling hard problems. In its decade, the rhythm leans to tough battles, opening new ground and relying on oneself, often bringing situations that demand resolve and accountability. The strength is daring and dependability; the risk is solitude, risk-taking and physical cost, so the key is choosing the right battles and keeping a fallback.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-01

A Qi Sha decade often feels like ten years with few easy fights: things arrive hard and require one’s own call; pushing through opens real ground, while overreaching easily costs.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to use a Qi Sha decade and guard against isolation. Understand its general-star energy, then see whether auspicious stars aid the hard work or malefics raise the risk.

The Underlying Logic

Qi Sha is the general star, governing resolve, execution and independence, giving strong drive and the courage for hard tasks this decade, suiting pioneering and high-difficulty roles.

It also carries solitude: relying on oneself means less support and less room to maneuver, so health and safety need extra care.

With Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars, daring becomes career; with malefics and Ji, risk, head-on clashes and impulsive decisions bring setbacks.

The lesson is courage with a plan: choose battles worth fighting, assess risk and keep a fallback, then commit fully.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Qi Sha hosts the decade Life Palace and check its brightness
  2. List the hard battles and decisions these years bring
  3. Separate what deserves full commitment from what should be let go
  4. Before acting, weigh risk, cost and fallback rather than charging on impulse
  5. Protect fixed time for health, safety and rest to avoid long-term overdraft
  6. With the annual cycles, see which years suit pioneering and which favor steadiness

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking the Qi Sha decade only as pushing and pioneering while ignoring risk and fallback

Mistake 2: Meeting every hard fight head-on and mistaking brute force for daring

Mistake 3: Focusing only on the hard tasks while neglecting health, safety and relationships
