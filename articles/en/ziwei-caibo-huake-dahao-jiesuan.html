Hua Ke and Da Hao in the Wealth Palace: What Actually Pays?

Separate professional reputation, contracted revenue, and cash received.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-31 09:15

Good reviews can attract a project without producing retained income. Hua Ke may frame reputation, while Da Hao prompts a cost question; neither provides a loss percentage. Compare contract value, subcontracting cost, tax, and money received. A large slow-paying project and a smaller repeatable one can leave very different cash balances.

A reputable consultant can have large invoices and little cash if clients pay late. Follow the contract from delivery through payment before treating a project as retained income.
