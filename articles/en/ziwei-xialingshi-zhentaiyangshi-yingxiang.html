Do Daylight Saving and True Solar Time Affect the Chart? Check Longitude, DST and Leap Months

Clocks follow a uniform time zone, while the birth hour is set by local solar time at the birthplace, so longitude matters. Mainland China observed daylight saving from 1986 to 1991, so records from those years must first be shifted back one hour. Leap months, lunar phases and solar-term boundaries must not be mixed. For births near a boundary, comparing two charts is safest.
