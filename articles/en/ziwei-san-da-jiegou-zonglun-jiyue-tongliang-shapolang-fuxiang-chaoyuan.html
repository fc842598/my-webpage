Three Core Zi Wei Dou Shu Frameworks: Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang, Sha-Po-Lang and Fu-Xiang—Which One Are You

The major stars across your Life trine and Travel necessarily fall into one of three frameworks: Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang (stable builder), Sha-Po-Lang (pioneering changer), or Fu-Xiang (manager-coordinator). None is superior; only fitting tracks differ. Setting the framework before details is the first reading step.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

The frameworks are life operating systems: Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang accumulates steadily within systems, Sha-Po-Lang opens new ground amid change, Fu-Xiang coordinates resources and conserves. Choosing the wrong track is more tiring than low effort.

What This Question Is Really Asking

A Sha-Po-Lang chart chasing a stable civil-service job, or a Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang chart in high-risk startup, fights its framework. The framework does not decide success but where success costs less.

The Underlying Logic

The major stars across Life, Wealth, Career and Travel fall into exactly one of three base frameworks.

Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang: Ji/Tai Yin/Tong/Liang meet—steady, good at planning and coordination, suited to public service, large firms, expertise and advisory routes.

Sha-Po-Lang: Qi Sha/Po Jun/Tan Lang always pull in trine—bold, pioneering and volatile, suited to startups, sales, competition and transformation work.

Fu-Xiang: Tian Fu/Tian Xiang trine—steady, managerial, security-and-resource oriented, suited to finance, administration, operations and leadership.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Lay out the major stars of Life/Wealth/Career/Travel
  2. Classify the star group into one base framework
  3. Pick the broad direction by framework: conserve, pioneer or coordinate
  4. Read strength and auspicious/malefic stars to judge how well the framework expresses
  5. Choose career and pace with the framework, not against it
  6. In activated decades, observe whether the framework is amplified or suppressed

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Believing the frameworks rank high versus low

Mistake 2: Choosing against the framework then blaming effort

Mistake 3: Reading only the Life star, not the whole trine

Back to Your Own Chart

Knowing your operating system matters more than fixating on one star. Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang seeks stability, Sha-Po-Lang change, Fu-Xiang order; work with your framework and life gets far easier.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to three core chart frameworks overview before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the three core chart frameworks overview line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
