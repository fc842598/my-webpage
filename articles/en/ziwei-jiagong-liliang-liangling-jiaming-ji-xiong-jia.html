Flanking Palaces in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How the Two Neighbors Shape a Palace, Auspicious vs Hostile Flank

Every palace is flanked by its two neighbors, whose stars support or suppress it: Zuo-You flank, Sun-Moon flank and wealth-shelter flank are auspicious and strengthen it; Yang-Tuo, Fire-Bell, Empty and punishment-Ji flanks are hostile and quietly drain it. Beyond the trine, flanking is the second force beginners most often miss.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

The trine is visible meeting; flanking is the influence pressed against both sides. A palace's strength depends not only on itself and its trine but whether its neighbors lift or drag it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

A good-looking palace is judged well yet quietly flanked by Yang-Tuo or Empty stars, so benefits are discounted and effort feels blocked; adding flanking completes the read.

The Underlying Logic

Flanking: a palace's two neighbors flank it; less direct than trine meeting but long-term, close and subtle.

Common auspicious flanks: Zuo Fu/You Bi (more help), Sun/Moon (bright harmony), Tian Fu/Tian Xiang wealth-shelter seal (steady nobility), and double-Lu flank (thick wealth qi).

Common hostile flanks: Yang/Tuo (always around Lu Cun, grinding), Fire/Bell (torment), Di Kong/Di Jie (loss and void), Tian Xing/Hua Ji punishment-Ji (pressure and dispute).

Order: read the palace's main star, then its trine, finally its two flanks; combine all three, adding for auspicious and subtracting for hostile flanks with responses.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the target palace
  2. Read its own stars and transformations for the base
  3. Read its trine for external support
  4. Find both neighbors and judge auspicious versus hostile flank
  5. With auspicious flank press the advantage; with hostile, prevent drain early
  6. Conclude from all three layers, never a single palace or star

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the palace, ignoring both flanks

Mistake 2: Treating every flank as helpful, unable to tell them apart

Mistake 3: Reading Lu Cun's benefit but not its Yang/Tuo grind

Back to Your Own Chart

Flanking is the power at your side. The trine shows the big environment, neighbors the close influence; check the palace, trine and both flanks and you will not miss the hidden push or drag on its strength.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to flanking palaces power before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the flanking palaces power line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
