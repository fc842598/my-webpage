How to Read True Solar Time in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Clock time is unified zone time; true solar time corrects by birth-place longitude, especially at shichen boundaries. If born near a boundary, without correction the Life Palace may be wrong; this is the first verification.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

True solar time is like adjusting unified clock time back to the sun actual position at your birth place; across a large east-west span the difference can reach dozens of minutes.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some ignore true solar time and boundary charts may be wholly wrong; others obsess over minutes, but most are not on boundaries and the effect is small.

The Underlying Logic

China uses unified UTC+8 Beijing time, but spans large longitude.

True solar time = clock time + longitude correction + equation of time; boundaries require it.

If born on an odd shichen boundary, the Life Palace may shift.

For charts not on a boundary, true solar time usually does not change palaces.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm birth clock time and birth place (longitude)
  2. Judge whether within about half an hour of a shichen boundary
  3. If on a boundary, correct to true solar time by longitude
  4. Reset Life and Body Palaces with corrected time
  5. If not on a boundary, build normally
  6. When uncertain, verify the Life Palace against temperament and history

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Ignoring true solar time at boundaries, Life Palace wrong

Mistake 2: Obsessing over minutes when not on a boundary

Mistake 3: Not verifying, reading a wrong chart as is

Back to Your Own Chart

True solar time asks whether boundary charts are wrong. Calculate at boundaries, do not obsess otherwise; with the Life Palace correct, later reading is right.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to true solar time before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the true solar time line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
