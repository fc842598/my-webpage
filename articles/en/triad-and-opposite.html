Triad and Opposite in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Read Four Areas Together

A palace is the center of a question, the opposite palace shows its counterpart, and the two other palaces in the triad show the wider support structure.

Reading Method2026-06-30 15:31

Zi WeiSelfMoneyWorkWorldTime

A palace is the center of a question, the opposite palace shows its counterpart, and the two other palaces in the triad show the wider support structure. These relationships prevent one-box interpretations.

For a Life Palace reading, the connected structure includes Life, Wealth, Career, and Travel. The four areas describe the person, income path, working role, and outside platform.

Separate the Opposite From the Triad

The opposite palace directly faces the central palace and often reveals tension or complementarity. The other two linked palaces complete the four-palace structure. Do not use “triad” as a loose name for every connected area.

Test Whether the Topic Has Support

A strong Life Palace with weak Travel may describe talent without a supportive platform. A strong Career Palace with weak Wealth may bring responsibility before income. Each mismatch identifies a different practical problem.

Use One Complete Example

For a career question, begin with Career, compare its opposite palace, and then read the other two palaces in the connected structure. Add the main stars and Four Transformations to see whether authority, income, and external opportunity reinforce one another.

Combination one: if the Life Palace is empty and borrows Tian Fu from Travel, read the person together with the outside platform, then check Career and Wealth for a usable role and income path. The borrowed star does not mean the environment controls everything; it shows that external context is especially important to how the baseline develops.

Combination two: Zi Wei in Career, Hua Ji in Wealth, and Hua Lu in Travel can describe a visible role supported by external demand but strained by collection or operating costs. Career looks strong, Travel opens the market, and Wealth identifies the bottleneck. Reading all four areas prevents a promotion story from hiding a cash-flow problem. Write a one-sentence finding for each connected palace before combining them; this prevents the strongest symbol from swallowing the rest of the structure.

Chart-Reading Order

Choose the question palace, identify its exact opposite, and complete the four-palace structure. Only then add decade and annual timing. This keeps palace relationships clear and avoids repeating the same point under different labels.
