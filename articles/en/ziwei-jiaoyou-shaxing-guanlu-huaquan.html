Friendship Palace Friction and Hua Quan in Career

Check decision rights before labeling a team conflict.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-31 09:22

Challenging stars in the Friendship Palace do not identify a villain. Hua Quan in the Career Palace raises a question about authority. A lead may own delivery without permission to change deadlines; another may change deadlines without informing the team. Compare the chart layers with written roles, meeting notes, and actual decisions.

Two teams can argue for different reasons: one lacks authority, the other lacks information. Meeting notes and permission records can identify which problem to fix.
