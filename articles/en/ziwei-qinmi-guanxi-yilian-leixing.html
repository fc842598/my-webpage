Anxious, Avoidant and Secure Attachment in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Intimacy Patterns in the Chart

Whether you are anxious-clingy, avoidant-distant or relatively secure in intimacy reads Life (self baseline), Spouse (how you are in intimacy) and Karma (sense of security when alone). Star combinations map to attachment tendencies; knowing your and your partner's type explains why closeness brings fights and distance brings panic, and lets you build safer relating.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-10

Many recurring relationship conflicts are not right or wrong but different attachment systems: one pursues, one flees, one fears abandonment, one fears engulfment.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why love makes you anxious, why the partner always wants space, whether you fit, Life/Spouse/Karma reveal the attachment pattern.

The Underlying Logic

Anxious tendency: Life or Spouse with Tian Ji/Tai Yin/Tan Lang/Ji, or unstable Karma needing the partner's response for security, fears abandonment in relationships, catastrophizes a slow reply, needs frequent reassurance and reads normal space as not-loving; the tighter the chase the more the other hides, rooted in insufficient inner security, with the lesson of reclaiming reassurance from others back to yourself.

Avoidant tendency: Life/Spouse with Qi Sha/Po Jun/Wu Qu/Tian Xiang or Gu Chen/Gua Su/Kong/Jie, with fiercely independent Karma, feels bound when intimacy nears and self-protects through reason and distance, withdrawing at conflict and avoiding feelings, often maddening an anxious partner; rooted in fear of engulfment and equating dependence with danger, with the lesson of staying connected in intimacy instead of fleeing closeness.

Secure tendency: stable Life/Spouse/Karma with grounded stars and harmonized transformations can be close and also alone, trust the relationship and keep the self, addressing conflict by the issue and willing to repair—the relationship stabilizer. Secure is not innate perfection but high self-acceptance and emotion regulation, attainable through adult practice.

Pairing and repair: the most tiring is anxious-with-avoidant pursue-withdraw; breaking it needs both to see the pattern—the anxious self-soothes and gives space, the avoidant proactively expresses and responds on schedule, replacing "you must prove love constantly" and "I flee when close" with a predictable communication rhythm. Attachment is shaped by early relations (Parents) but can be noticed and adjusted in adulthood; the chart gives tendency, real security comes from stable self-worth rather than demanding the partner never leave.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Life/Spouse/Karma to place yourself as anxious, avoidant or secure
  2. Read the partner to spot a pursue-withdraw pair
  3. Anxious types practice self-soothing, reclaiming security from the partner's response
  4. Avoidant types practice proactive expression and scheduled response, not fleeing closeness
  5. Agree on a predictable communication rhythm to replace pursue/flee instincts
  6. Understand attachment is shaped early but adjustable in adulthood, not destiny

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking anxiety as devotion and avoidance as not-loving, missing the attachment difference

Mistake 2: Only demanding the partner change without doing your own type's work

Mistake 3: Taking attachment tendency as destiny, ignoring adult awareness and security rebuilding

Back to Your Own Chart

Anxious, avoidant and secure are not labels but visible, adjustable relationship habits. Life/Spouse/Karma reveal your pattern; understand pursue-withdraw and each do the work, and intimacy moves from recurring drain to stability.
