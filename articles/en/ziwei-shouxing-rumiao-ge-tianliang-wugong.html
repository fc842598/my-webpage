The Longevity Star Exalted Pattern in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Tian Liang Guarding the Life Palace

Tian Liang is the longevity and shelter star; when exalted in the Life Palace and free of malefic, Ji, punishment and drain breaks, with auspicious meetings, it forms the Longevity Star Exalted pattern, indicating steadiness, maturity, turning misfortune into good and a sheltered, long life. In practice it often ties to care, education, medicine, religion or mature lines of work.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some treat Tian Liang vaguely as an old-person star, but exalted in the Life Palace it is a steady turning-aside of trouble with shelter; the key is whether malefics break it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what an exalted Tian Liang offers. It governs shelter: trouble finds a way out, help appears and the temperament is steady, but malefics discount the shelter and it can lean to worry and meddling.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Liang is the Southern Dipper longevity star whose nature is shelter, turning trouble into good and gaining protection from elders or patrons.

Longevity exalted: Tian Liang in an exalted position guarding life, meeting Lu, Ke, Zuo You and Kui Yue, with no malefic, Ji, punishment or drain, qualifies.

Those who form it are steady, mature and principled, drawn to care-giving fields such as education, medicine, public service, religion or legal work.

With malefics the shelter weakens and worry rises; then the resolution comes from acquired steadiness and professionalism rather than waiting.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm an exalted Tian Liang guards the Life Palace
  2. Count whether malefics, Ji, punishment or drain break it
  3. Then see whether Lu, Ke, Zuo You or Kui Yue add shelter
  4. If formed, read the steadiness and trouble-turning tendency
  5. With malefics, note worry and discounted shelter
  6. Conclude with the cycles, not taking longevity as a guarantee of no illness or trouble

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking the pattern as a guarantee of long life without illness

Mistake 2: Reading Tian Liang without malefics and ignoring conditions

Mistake 3: Understanding shelter as needing no action
