Tian Ku and Tian Xu in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Where Hidden Emotional Drain and Emptiness Come From and How to Fill Them

Tian Ku governs low mood, sorrow and emotional drain; Tian Xu emptiness, unreality and weak confidence. Neither is strong alone, but each amplifies uncertainty in its palace, most visible with malefics/Ji or in Happiness; the fix is stable inner support and real results.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

They are not disaster stars but emotional leak points, marking where you feel inexplicably low or ungrounded; filling it with real accumulation resolves them.

What This Question Is Really Asking

With these stars people feel not-good-enough and expect failure; emotion amplifies uncertainty, and shifting from worry to producing results dissolves the hollow feeling.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Ku is yang metal of sorrow; Tian Xu yin earth of void and drain; often paired, they are small emotion-level stars.

The palace sets where you feel ungrounded: Happiness for low spirits, Life for a melancholic temperament, Wealth/Career for insecurity about money and work.

Mostly auxiliary, they show mainly with malefics/Hua Ji or sensitive major stars; alone their effect is limited.

The counter is not suppressing emotion but turning void into substance with verifiable small wins, stable routine and real-world connection.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the stars to find the domain that feels ungrounded
  2. Recognize amplified emotion, not fact; stop catastrophizing
  3. Rebuild control there with verifiable small wins
  4. In Happiness, prioritize routine, exercise, sleep and real connection
  5. In Hua Ji/malefic phases lower expectations and advance stepwise, not white-knuckling
  6. After the decade leaves their activation, the drain eases; do not over-read it

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking emotional drain as objective fact

Mistake 2: Suppressing anxiety without acting, deepening the void

Mistake 3: Ignoring the palace and labeling it generic pessimism

Back to Your Own Chart

These stars mark where your confidence leaks. Do not fight the emotion; build solid small things in that domain, for grounding is filled one real result at a time.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Tian Ku and Tian Xu before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Tian Ku and Tian Xu line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
