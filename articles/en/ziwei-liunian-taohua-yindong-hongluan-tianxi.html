Annual Romance Stars: Hong Luan, Tian Xi, and Xian Chi

Read annual romance-star themes with the natal chart and actual relationship context. A marker cannot identify the right partner or confirm a marriage or affair.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24Updated 2026-11-06 15:10 (UTC+8)

Hong Luan, Tian Xi, and Xian Chi are often discussed through relationship and social themes. Their presence is not proof that someone will meet a partner, marry, reconcile, or become involved in a love triangle. Explain the star and calculation layer being used.

Distinguish the Traditional Themes

Hong Luan is commonly associated with relationships and celebration, Tian Xi with joy and social occasions, and Xian Chi with attraction and social appeal. Avoid turning those distinctions into labels such as 'legitimate partner' and 'bad romance.' A star does not establish anyone's intentions.

Keep Natal and Annual Positions Separate

Identify whether the marker belongs to the natal, ten-year, or annual chart. Read the relevant Life and Spouse palaces and the transformations actually present. Overlapping themes provide context for the interpretation, not a measured probability or a confirmed event.

Compare With the Current Situation

A single person expanding their social circle and an established couple discussing commitment have different questions. Use the reading to organize those questions, then assess the actual relationship through communication, consent, and consistent behavior.
