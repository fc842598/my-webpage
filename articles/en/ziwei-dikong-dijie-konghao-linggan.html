Di Kong and Di Jie in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Void-Drain and Inspiration

Di Kong and Di Jie are the most misunderstood malefics in Zi Wei Dou Shu. Many people see them and immediately conclude "wealth loss" or "effort in vain," but their essence is "unconventionality"—they break常规 paths and make people play outside the rules. Used well, they bring inspiration and breakthrough; used poorly, they bring dissipation and futility.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24

Di Kong governs "void"—many ideas, strong inspiration, impracticality, indifference to material things. Di Jie governs "calamity"—sudden changes, unexpected losses, breaking established plans. The two often appear together, called "Kong Jie." Kong Jie in Life Palace: jumping thinking, unconventional, suitable for creative, artistic, R&D, independent planning, but not for work requiring strict process execution. Kong Jie in Wealth Palace: not necessarily wealth loss, but "unconventional earning methods"—may earn through inspiration, creativity, sudden opportunities, but also easily loses through impractical investments. Kong Jie in Career Palace: work tends to change, dislikes stable institutional jobs, suitable for freelancing, entrepreneurship, project-based work. Kong Jie in Spouse Palace: relationships prone to "unexpected" changes—may be flash marriage and divorce, or meeting a partner completely different from expectations. The key with Kong Jie is "going with the flow." Its energy should not be suppressed; the more suppressed, the more likely it erupts as "sudden misfortune." The suitable approach is to give Kong Jie a legitimate outlet: creative work, learning new things, regularly changing life rhythm. At the same time, need a "grounding mechanism"—write down inspirations, collaborate with pragmatic people, set execution deadlines, avoid "thinking much, doing little."

What This Question Is Really Asking

Practical case: Someone with Di Kong/Di Jie in Life Palace has jumping thinking, does not play by the rules, may be seen as "weird" in traditional workplaces, but in creative, R&D, entrepreneurship and other fields that require breaking conventions, they can stand out. The key is finding an environment that can accommodate this trait.

The Underlying Logic

Treating auxiliary and malefic stars as "absolutely good" or "absolutely bad," ignoring their duality.

Only looking at major stars without auxiliary/malefic stars, ignoring their important modifying effect on major stars.

Equating malefic stars with "disaster," not seeing their positive role in certain fields.

Not looking at combination effects of auxiliary/malefic stars—a single star and a multi-star combination have completely different meanings.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Chart the distribution of auxiliary and malefic stars in Life Palace and all palaces.
  2. Understand the basic meaning and duality of each auxiliary/malefic star.
  3. See combinations of auxiliary/malefic stars with major stars, judge overall effect.
  4. See which auxiliary/malefic stars are activated by Decade/Annual, judge current stage theme.
  5. Leverage positive traits of auxiliary/malefic stars, consciously manage negative traits.
  6. Excel in suitable fields—malefic stars can be advantages in certain professions.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Auxiliary/malefic stars are only part of the chart; they cannot alone judge fortune.

Mistake 2: Malefic stars are not necessarily "bad"; in fields requiring impact and decisiveness, they can be advantages.

Mistake 3: The influence of auxiliary/malefic stars varies greatly depending on major stars and Decade/Annual.
