How to Approach Weight Loss: Sustainability Over Extreme Dieting

Healthy weight management depends on a sustainable calorie deficit, balanced diet, regular exercise and enough sleep, not extreme dieting or blindly taking weight-loss pills. First understand your height, weight and health, set a reasonable goal and proceed gradually; for markedly abnormal weight, accompanying illness or repeated dieting and bingeing, seek a proper nutrition or endocrinology clinic, following professional guidance.
