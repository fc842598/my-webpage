Changing Jobs vs. Internal Transfer: Reading Career and Travel Palaces

How to separate the external move of changing companies from an internal role change using the Career and Travel palaces.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Changing work has two paths. A job change leaves the current company for a new environment, while an internal transfer keeps the same company but changes the role. These moves point to different palaces.

What the two palaces cover

The Career Palace covers the work itself: role, duties, and professional direction. The Travel Palace covers the external environment and outward development. Leaving a company more clearly activates Travel, while an internal transfer mainly changes Career.

Judgment steps

  1. Confirm the real intention: leave the company or only change the role.
  2. Read the Career Palace to see whether the work content needs to change.
  3. Read the Travel Palace to judge the strength of external signals.
  4. Check opposite and supporting palaces to separate external from internal change.
  5. Add the current cycle to judge whether this is a time to act.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then check Career and Travel.
  2. Record work-content changes and environment changes separately.
  3. Read opposite palaces, supporting palaces, and transformations for the type of move.
  4. Add the current cycle to see whether it is an action or preparation period.
  5. Write the answer as role plus environment, not one blended change.
