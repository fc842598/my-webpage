How to Handle Inheritance: Clarify Property and Heirs

For inheritance, first clarify the estate, whether there is a will, and the order and shares of legal heirs, and prepare the death certificate and kinship documents. Heirs who agree can use notarization or transfer directly at property and banking institutions; disputes can use mediation or litigation, and a lawyer should be consulted when needed.
