Zi Wei Dou Shu Empty Career Palace: Reviewing a Customer Support Rotation

Use the opposite palace, Career, and Wealth to review authority, handoffs, and pay during a customer support team rotation.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-25 10:02

Lin manages customer support for one product. She is now rotating among after-sales, renewals, and complaints. Her job title stays the same, but the right to promise a refund and the duty to answer a dispute move between teams. If her Career Palace has no main star in a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart, that does not mean she has no career. The opposite palace can help frame how she works with people, while the real decision still depends on written job rules.

Identify the person on the other side of the handoff

When borrowing stars from the opposite palace, keep them in their original position. Do not count the same stars twice. A Sun and Ju Men combination may prompt questions about public explanations and one-to-one conversations. For example, can after-sales promise a refund while renewals offers only an extension? A Tian Ji and Tian Liang combination may prompt a review of changing rules and senior colleagues' sign-off. Neither combination labels someone a good or bad support manager. Check supporting stars, transformations, and the related palaces as well.

Put authority on one page

In the first week, a customer requests a refund for three months of service. Lin can log the case but cannot approve the refund. Her former manager thinks the new team owns it, while the new manager has not seen a special term in the old contract. The useful questions are concrete: who receives the request, who approves it, who replies, and when does it move to another team? A chart can prompt a question about communication; a case record shows where the actual gap lies.

Compare responsibility with money received

The Wealth Palace helps separate fixed pay, renewal commission, and deductions linked to refunds. If the new role offers an extra 1,000 yuan in monthly performance pay, delayed case closure might push that money into another month. Lin should confirm the payment schedule. The Life Palace and current decade cycle add context about her willingness to manage several teams for the long term. Even if a transformation suggests more responsibility, verify that authority comes with it.

Read the chart, then review the job terms

Confirm the birth details and that Career truly has no main star. Mark the stars in the opposite palace without moving their original location. Then review Career's related palaces, Wealth, Life, and the decade cycle. Compare those prompts with the roster, approval matrix, and pay policy. A short rotation might need a two-week review; a permanent management role needs explicit authority.
