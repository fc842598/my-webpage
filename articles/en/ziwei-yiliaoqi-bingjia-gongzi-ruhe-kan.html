Zi Wei Dou Shu on the Medical Period and Sick Pay: Health and Career Together

When illness requires stopping work, read the Health and Career Palaces together; the Health Palace shows health tendencies and the Career Palace the employment relationship during this time. You cannot be freely dismissed during the medical period, and sick pay has a minimum standard.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-29

For health issues, seek formal medical care and rest as advised; the medical period and sick pay are protections, not favors.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some push through illness or do not know their medical-period rights when taking sick leave, and are dismissed without knowing they are protected.

The Underlying Logic

The Health Palace stars show health tendencies and areas to watch, but these are tendencies, not a diagnosis; seek formal care when unwell.

The medical period is set by total and on-the-job tenure; during it the employer cannot terminate, and sick pay cannot fall below a set share of the local minimum wage.

If the Career Palace meets Hua Ji and malefics, work tends to be burdened by illness, so use the medical period fully rather than rushing back.

The Karma Palace shows mindset and stress; prolonged anxiety worsens the body, so balance treatment and rest.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Seek formal care first and take sick leave as advised, keeping records and the diagnosis
  2. Confirm the medical-period length by tenure and know you cannot be dismissed during it
  3. Check that sick pay is not below the local minimum standard
  4. Read the Health and Career Palaces to arrange treatment and work
  5. Do not push through or rush back; restore your health first
  6. Keep leave and communication records and pursue claims by procedure if harmed

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking the stars as diagnosis and delaying formal care

Mistake 2: Not knowing medical-period rights and being dismissed without recourse

Mistake 3: Not checking when sick pay is pushed below the minimum
