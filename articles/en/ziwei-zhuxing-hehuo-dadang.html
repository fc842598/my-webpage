How the Fourteen Major Stars Partner: Complement vs. Collision

Partnership is not about finding friends but about dividing roles: resource types pair with execution types, principle types with coordinating types. Two control-heavy stars (Zi Wei, Wu Qu, Qi Sha) collide and easily split; write out authority and responsibility before discussing feelings.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

The first principle of partnership is divide roles first, then discuss feelings; unclear roles strain even the best of friends.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some partner because they are close, while others avoid cooperation because star natures clash; what really decides success is clear division and clear rules.

The Underlying Logic

Resource type (Tian Fu, Tai Yin, Tian Xiang): steady and good at guarding wealth, suits back office and finance.

Execution type (Wu Qu, Qi Sha, Po Jun, Lian Zhen): driven and practical, suits business and expansion.

Coordinating type (Tian Tong, Tian Liang, Tian Ji): detailed and diplomatic, suits personnel and communication.

Control type (Zi Wei, Tan Lang): wants to lead; two control types must clarify who decides or they collide.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read both Life Palace major stars to know each role tendency
  2. Classify as resource/execution/coordinating/control to see complement or overlap
  3. For overlapping control types: first agree in writing who decides and who executes
  4. Put division, investment, profit share and exit terms into a written contract
  5. Reconcile accounts regularly, address issues not personalities, keep business friction out of friendship
  6. When disputes arise, return to the contract and division rather than dredging up the past

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Partnering because of friendship without writing roles and contracts

Mistake 2: Two control types both wanting to decide with unclear authority

Mistake 3: Bringing business friction into personal feelings and dredging up the past

Back to Your Own Chart
