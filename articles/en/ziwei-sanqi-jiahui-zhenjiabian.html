The Three Wonders Pattern: When Lu, Quan and Ke Meet, Still Distinguish True From False

The Three Wonders pattern is when Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke meet on the Life Palace or its triple-direction, traditionally suggesting both rapport and dignified execution. But three wonders together do not automatically make a true pattern; misplaced palaces or clash and Ji may leave it looking only similar. Tell true from false before grading.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

Many see Lu, Quan and Ke on their chart and claim the Three Wonders. But which palace they meet on and whether they are struck decides whether it is real or merely a gathering.

What This Question Is Really Asking

What the question really asks: three things must be settled for the Three Wonders—whether Lu, Quan and Ke truly meet on the Life or its triple-direction, whether the palaces they land in are strong, and whether malefic or Ji scatters that dignity. All three hold for a true pattern.

The Underlying Logic

The three wonders are Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke; when they meet on the Life or its triple-direction, resources, initiative and reputation are said to align.

A true pattern requires the three to land in strong palaces and echo each other, not to sit scattered and isolated.

If they land weakly, are struck by Qing Yang, Tuo Luo or Hua Ji, or fail to meet the Life palace, it is mostly similar rather than true.

Decade or yearly limits can temporarily assemble the three wonders, a good stretch, not a natal true pattern.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First mark the natal Lu, Quan and Ke and their palaces
  2. See whether the three meet on the Life or its triple-direction rather than sitting apart
  3. Check whether the palaces they land in are bright or fallen
  4. Inspect whether Qing Yang, Tuo Luo or Hua Ji strike the meeting
  5. Distinguish a natal three wonders from a temporary decade-yearly smooth stretch
  6. Grade by true-or-false and quality; do not declare great success on sight

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring the Three Wonders as soon as Lu, Quan and Ke appear

Mistake 2: Counting the three without checking palace strength or actual meeting

Mistake 3: Treating a decade-yearly assembled three wonders as a lifelong natal pattern

Back to Your Own Chart

The Three Wonders sound impressive, but true or false is checked line by line: do they meet the Life palace, are their palaces strong, are they struck? A true pattern aligns resources, initiative and reputation; if it only looks similar, do not overstate it.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to distinguishing true and false Three Wonders pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the distinguishing true and false Three Wonders pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
