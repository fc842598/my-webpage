What to Do About Grandparent Child-Rearing Conflict: Gratitude, Communication and Priorities

When grandparents help with a child, recognize their effort first, then gently communicate on principles (safety, health, habits) and accommodate non-essential matters. Couples should align first, affirm the grandparents and guide them with sound methods, and take on more themselves when needed without hurting feelings or giving up principles.
