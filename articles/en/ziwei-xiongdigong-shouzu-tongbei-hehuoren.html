The Siblings Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Siblings, Peers and Business Partners

The Siblings palace reads sibling bonds and, more broadly, all peer relations: close friends, classmates, colleagues, partners and industry peers. Auspicious stars/transformations mean helpful peers and smooth partnership; malefics/Ji mean thin bonds, profit disputes or partnership conflict—key to judging whether to partner and rely on peers.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

It is the window to your peer ecosystem: whether peers build you up or drain you, and whether partnership is safe, depends on its star combinations, not on affection alone.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some read it only for siblings and miss its guidance on partnership, team and network; others partner blindly from affection, yet heavy malefics here often turn friendship into profit conflict.

The Underlying Logic

On the family axis it reads siblings; on the social axis it extends to peers, close friends, classmates, colleagues, partners and industry circles.

Strong major stars with auspicious transformations and assistants mean loyal, helpful siblings, friends and partners.

With malefics/Ji/empty stars, bonds are thin or distant and disputes arise over money or stance; partnership especially needs written terms and clear roles.

It also sits opposite Property and is one of wealth's storage positions, indirectly affecting cash scheduling and turnover.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read its major star and strength to set the peer-relation tone
  2. Check auspicious versus malefic stars to see whether peers help or drain
  3. For partnership: with auspicious stars partner but still contract; with heavy malefics stay independent or loosely bound
  4. Combine the Friends palace to judge whether the whole team and network align
  5. Read its flying transformations to see whether peer resources flow to you or away
  6. In years it activates, expect partnership, split or team changes

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only blood siblings, ignoring partners and peers

Mistake 2: Partnering from affection without checking malefics

Mistake 3: Treating it in isolation, unlinked to Wealth and Property

Back to Your Own Chart

The Siblings palace really asks whether you can rely on peers. Read it and you know when to partner for leverage, when to keep distance, and when to set rules first.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Siblings palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Siblings palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
