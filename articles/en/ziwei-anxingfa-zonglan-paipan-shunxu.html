Zi Wei Dou Shu Star Placement Overview: The Sequence That Builds a Chart Step by Step

A chart is not an app black box but a fixed layering: set the twelve palaces, place Zi Wei and the fourteen major stars, then assistants and malefics, then the Four Transformations, then minor and annual stars. Understanding this sequence lets you read a chart and catch app errors.

Beginners think stars appear all at once, but they follow a strict order: the palace skeleton first, then Zi Wei as the northern-leader, the fourteen majors around it, and assistants, malefics and transformations filled last.

What This Question Is Really Asking

To move from reading app output to understanding why a chart is cast that way, the thing to learn is the overall placement sequence, not more isolated star meanings.

The Underlying Logic

Step one places Life/Body and the twelve palaces: birth month and hour fix Life/Body, the other eleven are placed in reverse, and the Life Palace stem-branch sets the Five-Element phase, which determines Zi Wei's position.

Step two places the fourteen major stars: the phase and birth-day number fix Zi Wei, then fixed pairings place the Zi Wei and Tian Fu series—fourteen majors forming the chart's trunk.

Step three places assistants and malefics: Zuo Fu/You Bi by month, Wen Chang/Wen Qu by hour, Tian Kui/Tian Yue by year stem, Lu Cun/Qing Yang/Tuo Luo by year stem, Huo Xing/Ling Xing by year branch and hour, Di Kong/Di Jie by hour.

Step four places the Four Transformations (the year stem decides which star and palace gets Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji) and minor stars such as Hong Luan, Tian Xi and Tian Ma; only after all placement do triple-direction and decade/year readings begin.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Use lunar birth month and hour to place Life and Body, then the full twelve palaces in reverse
  2. Derive the Five-Element phase from the Life stem-branch (Water-2, Wood-3, Metal-4, Earth-5, Fire-6)
  3. Use the phase and birth-day number to locate Zi Wei, then place the fourteen major stars
  4. Place assistants and the six malefics by year stem, year branch, month and hour respectively
  5. Place the Four Transformations from the birth-year stem, then add minor and annual stars like Hong Luan, Tian Xi and Tian Ma
  6. Check every palace after placement, then begin the substantive triple-direction and decade/year reading

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Skipping placement to memorize verdicts, unable to notice a wrongly cast chart

Mistake 2: Confusing assistants, transformations and majors without knowing what is fixed first and what depends on what

Mistake 3: Staying dependent on the app black box with no judgment for leap months, Zi-hour or solar-time edge cases

Back to Your Own Chart

Star placement is the underlying grammar of Zi Wei Dou Shu. You need not hand-cast every chart, but once you understand the full sequence from Life/Body to majors to assistants and transformations, you read any chart with confidence and will not be led astray by a buggy app.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the full star-placement sequence before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the full star-placement sequence line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
