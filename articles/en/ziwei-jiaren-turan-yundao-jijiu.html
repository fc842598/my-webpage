What to Do When a Family Member Faints: Assess, Call for Help and Act

If someone faints, gently shake and call to check consciousness and breathing, and dial 120 immediately. If there is no breathing or only gasping, start CPR and ask someone to get an AED; if breathing but unconscious, place them on their side, keep the airway clear, and do not move them or give water. Protect the neck if injury is suspected, and keep watching while waiting for help.
