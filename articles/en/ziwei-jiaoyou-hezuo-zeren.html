How Zi Wei Reads Responsibility in Team Partnerships

Friends Palace patterns are about networks and collaboration. Define who brings resources, makes decisions, and carries outcomes before calling someone a benefactor.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-23 11:20

Partnerships usually fail when resources, decisions, and outcomes belong to no clear person. The Friends Palace describes the network and should be read with Career.

Break the duties down before asking whether someone is a ‘benefactor.’

Friends shows the collaboration structure

The main star sets the team tone; transformations point to resources, authority, expertise, or friction. Hua Ji especially asks for written promises and profit rules.

Siblings describe peer support more than project networks, so do not merge the two palaces.

Career defines the interface

A busy Friends Palace with a weak Career process can stay at discussion and favors. A strong Career with an ordinary Friends Palace often works better after roles are written.

Travel shows whether outside clients will change the allocation.

Examples

Hua Lu in Friends with Hua Quan in Career suits a project-lead structure with a named budget signer. Hua Ji in Friends with Tian Ma in Travel needs exit and compensation clauses for shifting clients.

Practical reading order

Read Friends for the network, Career for roles, Travel for outside change, Wealth for the split, and timing for the contract.

Practical Reading Order

Start with the question palace, compare its opposite and trine partners, then add timing and transformations before making a practical judgment.
