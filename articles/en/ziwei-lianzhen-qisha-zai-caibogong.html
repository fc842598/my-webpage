Lian Zhen and Qi Sha in the Wealth Palace: Bold, Storming Earning, Turning Discipline and the General Into Grounded Wealth

The Wealth Palace reads the earning style and cash state. Lian Zhen governs rules, discipline and public relations while Qi Sha governs decisiveness, independence and pioneering, so together in Chou or Wei earning is bold and storming, doing hard work through skill and discipline, suiting pioneering, storming and military-police or technical posts. The risk is firmness, fluctuation, risk and loss of money, so the key is using boldness on calculable hard battles and keeping a bottom line for risk. This is only a metaphysical reference and not investment advice.

People with Lian Zhen and Qi Sha in the Wealth Palace earn with drive, daring to take work others avoid and making money through skill and discipline; yet when too firm and risk-loving, income fluctuates and money is easily lost in forcing through.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the wealth-seeking pattern with Lian Zhen and Qi Sha in the Wealth Palace. Understand the palace reads cash, then see how discipline and the general combine so one dares to storm while keeping the bottom line.

The Underlying Logic

Lian Zhen in the Wealth Palace gives rule-minded, disciplined, skill-based earning able to act under constraints, yet can be held back by rules.

Qi Sha there gives decisive, independent, pioneering, storming earning, yet can be risky, fluctuating and leave no room.

Together they mean discipline plus the general: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars under a bottom line boldness becomes real results; with malefics and Ji, loss of money from risk, forcing through or disputes follows.

The lesson is firm with calculation and driven with a base: use boldness on calculable hard battles and set stop-loss and cash flow for risk.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Lian Zhen and Qi Sha share the Wealth Palace and check their brightness
  2. Use boldness on calculable hard battles rather than forcing through on luck
  3. Set stop-loss and cash flow for risk to avoid losing money
  4. When income fluctuates, steady the base before expansion
  5. Build through skill and discipline, reducing impulse decisions
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of earning activation and harvest

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Lian Zhen-Qi Sha in the Wealth Palace only as bold and storming while ignoring fluctuation and loss

Mistake 2: Risk-loving with no bottom line, losing money in forcing through

Mistake 3: Income fluctuates with no stop-loss, losing hard-earned money
