What This Question Is Really Asking

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate the annual Karma Palace and see the source of mental pressure this year

  5. Schedule things that relax and focus you, giving thoughts an outlet
  6. If low mood persists and affects life, seek formal medical care and professional help promptly

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Forcing through and treating help as weakness until the drain deepens

Mistake 2: Holding excessive self-demand and blaming any imperfection

Mistake 3: Taking annual low mood as a natal verdict without the decade limit
