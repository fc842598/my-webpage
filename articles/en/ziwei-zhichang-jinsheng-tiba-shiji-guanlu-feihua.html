What This Question Is Really Asking

"When do I get promoted?" cannot be year-stamped from natal Career alone. Trace where natal Career flies Lu/Quan, and when decade and year reach it; three layers aligned make a real window.

The Underlying Logic

First set natal Career: the major star defines your workplace role (pioneer, coordinator, specialist, steward)—the base of whether and how you rise.

Then trace Career-stem flying: where its Lu/Quan lands points opportunity's field—Lu into Wealth rewards revenue performance, Quan into Life puts real authority in your hands.

Use the decade for the ten-year window: when decade Life or Career reaches natal Career's Lu/Quan landing, official fortune flows; decade Career transforming auspiciously raises promotion odds.

Use the year to pin timing: within a good decade, the year Life/Career reaches the Lu/Quan landing with annual Lu/Quan stacking is the year to compete and negotiate title; conversely a decade Ji clashing natal Career plus a clashing year is layoff/demotion risk—defend, do not attack.
