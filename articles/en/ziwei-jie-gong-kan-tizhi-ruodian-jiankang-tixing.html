The Health palace is a maintenance-priority map: where to take innate care and what lifestyle to avoid, helping prevention rather than issuing medical conclusions.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Self-diagnosing from the Health palace causes fear or self-medication, a misuse; treat it as a checkup/lifestyle reminder—star tendencies never replace doctors and tests.

The Underlying Logic

Health governs innate constitution and weak spots, read with Life/Happiness/Travel; no conclusion from one palace.

Star tendencies: strong Zi-Wei/Tian Fu/Tian Tong/Tian Liang without malefics are sturdy; Fire/Yang lean acute/inflammation/injury, Bell/Tuo chronic/dragging/recurrent.

Organ imagery (cultural only, not medical mapping): Lian Zhen blood/urinary-reproductive, Ju Men digestion/oral, Tian Ji nerves/liver-gallbladder, Tai Yin fluid metabolism/gynecology.

Correct use: schedule rest, diet, exercise and targeted checkups by tendency; at symptoms seek formal care and follow doctors—never self-diagnose or stop medication from a chart.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Health stars and malefics for weak tendencies
  2. Combine Life/Happiness/Travel, no single-palace call
  3. Turn tendencies into rest/diet/exercise changes
  4. Schedule targeted checkups accordingly
  5. Let formal medical examination rule on symptoms
  6. No self-diagnosis, stopping medication or supplements from the chart

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Self-diagnosing from the Health palace

Mistake 2: Buying drugs or stopping medication by stars

Mistake 3: Treating cultural imagery as medical mapping

Back to Your Own Chart
