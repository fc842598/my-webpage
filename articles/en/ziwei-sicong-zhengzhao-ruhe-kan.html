How the Four-Direction Palaces Work in a Zi Wei Chart

The four-direction means the home palace, the opposite palace and two trine palaces forming a complete view. The home palace is the core, the opposite supplements along a straight line, and the two trines supply from the side; all four together read fully.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

The four-direction palaces are like four legs of a table; the home palace is the tabletop, the opposite and trines are legs, and missing any one makes it unsteady.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some read only the home palace without the opposite and things always lack the other half; the opposite often describes external conditions and hidden supplements.

The Underlying Logic

Four-direction = home palace + opposite + two trines, the complete view of one palace.

The home palace is the core, the opposite faces it along a straight line, the trines supply from the side.

For example, Life Palace opposite Travel: inner self and outside encounters are two sides.

Read all four together; the home palace alone is only a quarter.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate the home palace, the core area to read
  2. Find the opposite palace for the direct supplement along the line
  3. Find the two trine palaces for side supply
  4. Count major, auspicious and malefic stars in all four
  5. Read how transformations distribute across the four to set energy flow
  6. Read all four together to judge the complete state of the area

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the home palace without opposite and trines, missing the full view

Mistake 2: Treating the opposite as opposition when it is a supplement

Mistake 3: Reading the four separately without connecting them

Back to Your Own Chart

The four-direction asks for the complete view of one area. Connect the home palace, opposite and two trines, and you read the whole matter.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the four-direction palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the four-direction palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
