Zi Wei Dou Shu: Sun in the Wealth Palace and Livestream Sales Costs

A busy livestream can produce many orders without much cash. Read the Wealth Palace alongside refunds, platform fees, work and audience reach.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-12 20:00

A livestream shows one hundred orders, yet the bank balance barely rises. If the Sun sits in the Wealth Palace of a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart, public visibility may be part of how income is generated. Visibility does not tell you how much money stays. Separate orders, refunds, platform fees, advertising spend, and settled cash before interpreting the result.

Follow the money after the stream

Imagine a teacher selling a course. Some customers cancel, the platform keeps a share, and payment settles a week later. The sales dashboard is not the same as usable cash. A second stream may double orders after paid promotion while leaving less cash than the first. This is a testable business question, not a promise made by a star.

Read work and audience reach together

The Career Palace helps frame the labor behind preparation, customer service, and delivery. The Travel Palace can frame contact with unfamiliar audiences and platforms. If Hua Quan is relevant, ask who controls the spending and refund policy. If Hua Ke is relevant, check whether professional trust leads to repeat purchases. Read the natal pattern before longer and annual cycles. Then compare a month of settled cash and costs before changing the livestream budget.
