Buy Your Own Work Equipment: Is a Stipend Income or Cost Recovery?

Use Career and Wealth Palaces to frame duties and real cash flow for an equipment stipend.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:16

A design company asks employees to buy a computer first and receive a monthly stipend later. The Career Palace frames the tool required for the job; the Wealth Palace frames when cash leaves and returns. A promised stipend is not money already received.

Read the policy and the numbers

Check who chooses the model, owns the device, pays for repairs, and receives it after employment ends. One worker may spend a large amount now and recover it slowly. Another may get a company-owned device with no personal cash outlay. Record the purchase date, stipend dates, and any stop condition.

Review the choice

If cash is tight, ask about a company device, advance, or reimbursement. Use real receipts, payroll, and data-security rules. Read Career requirements and Wealth cash flow before the active cycle; a favorable transformation cannot replace the calculation.
