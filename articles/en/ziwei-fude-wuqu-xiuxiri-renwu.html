Wu Qu in the Fortune Palace: Can Tasks Feel Restful?

Wu Qu may link satisfaction with finishing tasks, but rest still needs a stopping point.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-07 09:08

Some people feel calmer after completing a small practical task on a day off. Wu Qu in the Fortune Palace can describe that kind of satisfaction. It does not mean that working all weekend is healthy or required.

Compare outcomes

An accountant who sorts receipts and then enjoys the afternoon has a different recovery pattern from a manager who checks messages until midnight. Examine whether Wu Qu is in the palace itself or linked through the chart, then compare the Wealth and Career Palaces. The location changes the question.

Use the chart carefully

Read the natal Fortune Palace first, then its opposite and the Career Palace. Add the current cycle last. Record how you feel after each activity and set a clear stopping time. Real fatigue and sleep problems deserve ordinary attention.
