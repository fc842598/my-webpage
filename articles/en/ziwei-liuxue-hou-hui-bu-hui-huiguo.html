Will You Come Back After Studying Abroad, or Stay Out Long-Term?

A Zi Wei Dou Shu reading for telling the difference between overseas study that feeds back home and a life path that truly moves abroad.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-30 04:58

Going abroad to study and building your later life abroad are not the same chart movement. The Travel Palace may open the door, but the Body Palace often tells you whether the center of life actually stays outside.

What This Means

The travel palace also means platforms, markets, and life outside the familiar setting. That is why some charts grow only after movement or relocation.

How To Read It

Do not read movement as travel only. Compare the outside-world palace with the Life Palace and Career Palace to see whether a bigger stage strengthens the chart or simply adds stress.

Simple Examples

  • When the travel palace is involved, outside platforms, clients, or new environments matter more.
  • A move, a bigger platform, or a new market can activate the chart more strongly than staying in the original environment.
  • A chart may stay average at home but become far more visible after a move or platform change.

Practical Order

For a topic like "Will You Come Back After Studying Abroad, or Stay Out Long-Term?," start with the main palace named in the topic, then compare the opposite palace and the supporting structure. Finish by testing how the structure turns into a real-life result so the reading stays concrete instead of drifting into a fixed label.
