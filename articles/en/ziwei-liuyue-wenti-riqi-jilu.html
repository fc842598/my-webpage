Zi Wei Dou Shu Monthly Career Reading: Date the Event Before Reading the Chart

Tie a monthly chart to an interview, offer, or start date instead of asking whether an entire month is good for work.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 09:00

“Can I change jobs this month?” may refer to an application, interview, offer, or start date. These events can fall in different monthly periods. A Zi Wei Dou Shu monthly chart becomes easier to check when the question names the action and its date.

Build an event timeline

An application in late October, an interview in early November, and a start date in December are not one event. The Career Palace frames duties, the Travel Palace can frame a new company or setting, and the Wealth Palace frames compensation. Record the dates and the tool's month boundary before interpreting any palace.

Keep the time layers separate

The natal chart describes a more stable work pattern. A ten-year period supplies the current background, the annual cycle the year's emphasis, and the monthly cycle the nearer window. A monthly signal does not guarantee an offer. Compare it with job requirements, pay terms, and relocation costs.

Write down the original question, birth inputs, chart version, date rule, and eventual result. Leave the outcome blank until it occurs so the later review does not rewrite the earlier question.
