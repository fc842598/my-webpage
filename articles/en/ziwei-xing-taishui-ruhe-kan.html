How to Read Punishing the Tai Sui in 2026

In 2026 Bing Wu, the Wu horse self-punishes (Wu-Wu punishment), bringing self-torment, inner friction and overthinking. Punishing Tai Sui is mostly fighting oneself; learning to address issues not oneself, keeping regular routines and asking for help works better than seeking external solutions.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

Punishing Tai Sui is like competing with yourself; there are not that many enemies outside, it is the knot in your mind that traps you; the key to untying it is in your own hands.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some fear misfortune when punishing Tai Sui, while others blame every emotion on it; what is needed is attention to your own mental state and routines, not seeking charms everywhere.

The Underlying Logic

In 2026 Bing Wu, the Wu horse self-punishes, bringing self-entanglement, inner friction and overthinking.

Common signs: looping on the same issue, insomnia, self-blame, demanding too much of oneself.

Remedies: address issues not oneself, keep regular routines, exercise, ask for help and do not tough it out alone.

Punishing Tai Sui only adjusts one year; the long term depends on the natal chart and decade, not destined disaster.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm whether your zodiac punishes Tai Sui in 2026 (Wu horse self-punishment)
  2. Understand that punishment is mostly self-entanglement, not external disaster
  3. Watch for signals in sleep, emotion and looping thoughts
  4. Establish regular routines and exercise, using the body to lift the mind
  5. When stuck, write it down or talk to someone, do not brood alone
  6. Address issues not oneself, replace I cannot with how can this be done

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating punishing Tai Sui as destined disaster and becoming overly anxious

Mistake 2: Blaming every emotion on Tai Sui without attention to one own state

Mistake 3: Toughing it out alone and refusing help, letting inner friction grow heavier
