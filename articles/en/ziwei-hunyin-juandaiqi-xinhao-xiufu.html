Marriage Burnout in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Which Charts and Decades Slip Into Staleness, and How to Repair

Marriage burnout is not the end of a bond; it is usually activated in a specific decade when Spouse or Karma is touched by Ji or malefics, bringing less communication and cooled feeling. Distinguish phase fatigue, structural problems and outside冲击 to know whether to repair, adjust or let go.

Every long bond has low-pressure phases; the question is not whether burnout exists but whether the chart shows which kind it is, how long it lasts and whether it is worth repairing.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether a flat, silent marriage is ending means first reading the Spouse and Karma foundation, then what the current decade and year activate.

The Underlying Logic

First read the natal Spouse base: steady with Lu/Ke and gentle stars means a sound foundation where burnout is mostly phased; a natal Spouse heavy with malefics/Ji and clashed already holds structural conflict, and burnout only surfaces it.

Karma shows inner feeling: marital happiness is largely written in Karma; when the decade activates its Ji, one side often feels lonely and misunderstood—an unmet emotional need, not necessarily lost love.

Decade/year shows the trigger: a decade activating Spouse or a year whose Ji clashes Spouse/Karma is a high-risk burnout phase; if Friends or Travel is simultaneously touched by romance stars, distinguish an outside challenge rather than blaming simple staleness.

Match the response: phase fatigue (sound natal, luck-activated) warms through rebuilding shared rhythm, regular deep conversation and new joint experiences, easing as the phase passes; structural problems (natal Spouse long-stressed) require facing real divides in communication, values and division of labor; outside challenges first need boundaries. Where personal safety or ongoing harm is involved, protect yourself and seek professional help first.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the natal Spouse base to judge the relationship's foundation
  2. Read Karma to tell unmet emotional need from a truly ended bond
  3. See what the current decade/year activates to locate the burnout trigger
  4. Check Friends/Travel for romance activation to rule out or confirm an outside factor
  5. Phase fatigue rebuilds rhythm and communication; structural problems face divides; outside factors first set boundaries
  6. Set an action-based observation period rather than deciding impulsively at the lowest point

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking burnout as lost love and divorcing impulsively at the luck low point

Mistake 2: Reading Spouse without Karma, missing the inner-feeling thermometer of marriage

Mistake 3: Not separating phase fatigue, structural problems and outside factors, applying one fix to every cool-down

Back to Your Own Chart

Burnout is a normal fluctuation in long bonds, not the finale. Read the Spouse/Karma foundation and what the current luck activates, then repair accordingly or choose rationally—it comes closer to what you truly want than deciding at an emotional peak.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to signs of marriage burnout and repair before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the signs of marriage burnout and repair line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
