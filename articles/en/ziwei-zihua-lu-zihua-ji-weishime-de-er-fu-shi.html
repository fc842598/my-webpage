Self-Lu and Self-Ji in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Why Gains Slip Away

Self-transformation keeps energy from flowing out and digests it inside the palace, so self-Lu often means gaining then willingly letting go—easy come, easy go—while self-Ji means losing after repeated brooding. The fix is building external anchors to retain results.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Transforming-to-nothing is not never getting, but failing to hold a gain due to an inner pattern; self-Lu loses by careless release, self-Ji by draining brooding.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People repeatedly watch chances, relationships and savings arrive then fly away in the same domain—often a self-transforming palace whose energy no external structure locks in.

The Underlying Logic

Self-Lu grants its own comfort: easily content and open-minded there, with the side effect of not clinging and not actively defending results.

Self-Ji grants its own knot: over-caring and repeated pulling there, draining the relationship or chance instead.

Both show gain-then-loss by opposite mechanisms—one releases loosely, the other grips too hard—so responses differ.

The fix imports external structure: self-Lu is locked by commitments, contracts and shared goals; self-Ji breaks the loop with stop-loss lines, outside feedback and action closure.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm which palace has self-Lu or self-Ji, locking the cannot-keep domain
  2. Self-Lu: admit you release easily; use written commitments and external agreements to lock gains
  3. Self-Ji: admit you drain internally; set stop-loss lines and decision deadlines
  4. Build checkable external anchors there (deadlines, partners, separated accounts)
  5. Be extra careful in years the decade activates the self-transformation; no emotional quitting or grasping
  6. Review afterward: was the loss from loose release or tight grip? Adjust accordingly

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Blaming gain-then-loss on luck without checking self-transformation

Mistake 2: Using one fix for opposite mechanisms of self-Lu and self-Ji

Mistake 3: Only talking oneself around without building external anchors

Back to Your Own Chart

The cannot-keep of self-transformation is not fate but a reminder: such matters cannot be held by inner feeling alone—external structure must anchor the result for you.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to why self-Lu and self-Ji lose what was gained before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the why self-Lu and self-Ji lose what was gained line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
