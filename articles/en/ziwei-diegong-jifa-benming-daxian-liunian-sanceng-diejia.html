Palace Stacking in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Reading Life, Decade and Year Layers Together

Palace stacking overlays Life, decade and year charts: Life is the base, the decade the ten-year environment, the year the trigger. When a palace is stacked across layers, that domain becomes the life focus then. Stacking sets the theme; flying/self-transformations set how it plays—the core technique from static chart to dynamic reading.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Reading only the natal chart is half the skill; stacking tells you which matter is pushed front-stage in a decade or year. Natal sets the base tone, stacking the current focus.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Reading a year by its Life star alone, ignoring which decade and natal palace it stacks on, yields vague predictions; aligning three layers clarifies the year's focus and the domain it manifests in.

The Underlying Logic

The natal chart is the unchanging body; the decade is the ten-year environment; the year the trigger—palaces overlap by branch position, which is stacking.

Method: the year Life palace landing on a decade palace and a natal palace makes that domain the year's focus, e.g. stacking decade Wealth and natal Career puts work and income center-stage.

Same-direction multi-stacking (decade Spouse plus year Spouse) is the strongest theme, raising relationship-event odds; stacking Life/Travel returns focus to self or outward change.

Stacking only sets the likely theme frame; concrete fortune still needs that palace's stars, flying and self-transformations—body and use together.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Know the natal twelve palaces and lifelong structure
  2. Lay out the current decade twelve palaces and mark the ten-year environment
  3. Lay out the year twelve and find the year Life palace branch
  4. Stack layer by layer: which decade and natal palace the year Life sits on
  5. Lock the year's theme domain, then read that palace's stars
  6. Use flying/self-transformations to see how the theme unfolds and in which months

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the year Life star without three-layer stacking

Mistake 2: Taking the stacked theme as fixed result, ignoring transformations

Mistake 3: Stacking before knowing the natal chart, on a faulty base

Back to Your Own Chart

Stacking makes the chart move. Natal fixes who you are, decade your environment, year which matter surfaces—align the three and predictions gain both direction and substance.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to palace stacking technique before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the palace stacking technique line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
