How to Understand Stars Divided by Yin and Yang

Stars have yin and yang, which determines their expression of cold-heat, movement-stillness and inner-outer: yang stars lean outward, active and warm, yin stars lean inward, passive and cool. Yin-yang is the underlying key to understanding star nature; the same star with different yin-yang expresses differently by palace.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

Yin-yang is like the gender and temperature of a star, not a good-bad distinction but a difference in expression; yang stars are like daytime, yin stars like nighttime, each with its own use.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some memorize star natures but cannot read the differences because they miss the yin-yang underlying layer; even among major stars, different yin-yang means different ways of handling matters.

The Underlying Logic

The fourteen major stars divide into yin and yang: Zi Wei, Tian Ji, Sun, Wu Qu, Tian Tong, Lian Zhen are yang; Tai Yin, Tan Lang, Ju Men, Tian Xiang, Tian Liang, Qi Sha, Po Jun, Tian Fu are yin (schools vary slightly).

Yang stars lean outward, active, warm and firm; yin stars lean inward, passive, cool and gentle.

Yin-yang is not good or bad but expression; after landing in a palace, still read brightness and transformations.

Yin-yang pairing: a yang star in a yin palace or yin star in a yang palace has its expression tempered or activated.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First learn the yin-yang attribute of each major star
  2. Translate yin-yang into expression: yang = outward and active, yin = inward and passive
  3. Read the yin-yang of the palace the star lands in, judging whether tempered or activated
  4. Combine brightness to see whether the yin-yang energy can express itself
  5. Read transformation activation: yang star with Hua Quan is firmer, yin star with Hua Ke is gentler
  6. Finally land in reality: whether this person handles matters by proactive attack or passive response

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating yin-yang as good-or-bad, yang is good and yin is bad

Mistake 2: Reading only star nature without yin-yang, missing expression differences

Mistake 3: Ignoring how the palace yin-yang tempers the star nature
