How to Read Social Anxiety: Normal Nerves vs When You Need Help

Nervousness and fear of saying the wrong thing in social settings are common. First distinguish occasional nerves from strong fear, avoidance and impaired study, work or life whenever you socialize. You can improve gradually through preparation, practicing in small groups and focusing on the task rather than your performance. If it is long-term, intense and clearly affects functioning, seek an evaluation at a proper mental-health clinic or a licensed counselor, with professional help prevailing.
