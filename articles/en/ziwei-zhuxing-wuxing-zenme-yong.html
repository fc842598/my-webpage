How to Use the Five Elements of Major Stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Each major star carries an element, for example Zi Wei is earth, Tian Ji is wood, Tai Yang is fire, Wu Qu is metal and Tian Tong is water. Elements show whether stars and palaces generate and support or control and restrain one another, but they are only a reference; the combination still decides.

Some memorize many star traits but cannot use them; elements are the hidden thread linking the stars, and once you know generation and control you can judge whether a pairing fits.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how elements actually enter a reading. Use them in two places: the star versus its palace, and the generation-control between stars that share or meet, then add the transformations for the net effect.

The Underlying Logic

Major-star elements: Zi Wei, Tian Fu and Tian Liang are earth; Tian Ji and Tan Lang are wood; Tai Yang and Lian Zhen are fire; Wu Qu and Qi Sha are metal; Tian Tong, Tai Yin, Ju Men, Tian Xiang and Po Jun are water.

Star versus palace: when the star element and the palace branch element generate or match, the star expresses; when they control, it must work harder.

Star versus star: co-located stars that generate complement each other, while those that control restrain, yet restraint can curb an over-strong star and is not all bad.

Elements only explain whether force flows smoothly, not good or bad; that depends on what matter the force lands on.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm the major star element
  2. Check the palace branch element to see generation or control
  3. Then read the elements of co-located, opposite and trine stars, counting generation and control
  4. An auspicious star that is generated is steady; a harsh star that is controlled does less harm
  5. Add the transformations to see whether the relationship finally helps or restrains
  6. Treat elements as one reference layer, never judging apart from the combination

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Equating element generation-control with good or bad, as if control is always harmful

Mistake 2: Reading one star element without its palace and other stars

Mistake 3: Applying elements rigidly and ignoring the transformations and brightness

Back to Your Own Chart

Elements are the grammar of star relationships, not a verdict. Generation or control only shows whether force flows; put it back into the combination and transformations for a sound judgment.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to major-star elements and generation-control between stars and palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the major-star elements and generation-control between stars and palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
