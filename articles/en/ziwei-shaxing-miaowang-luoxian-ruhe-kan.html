How Malefics Differ in Exaltation Versus Fall: Sharp Force Versus Awkward Drain

The same malefic in exaltation carries sharp, concentrated force that may strike or drive a breakthrough, while in fall it turns awkward and becomes inner drain and repetition. Telling the two apart keeps the remedy pointed the right way.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Malefics are not uniformly bad; an exalted one is like a sharp blade that can wound or cut wood, while a fallen one is a dull blade that will not cut yet chafes the hand.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some fear and suppress every malefic alike, missing the drive of an exalted one and failing to fix the drain of a fallen one; the issue is not the star but not distinguishing its state.

The Underlying Logic

Malefics such as Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Huo Xing and Ling Xing change with brightness: exalted force is concentrated and abrupt, fallen force is awkward and repetitive.

When an exalted malefic points the right way it often becomes drive and breakthrough, best channeled to a concrete goal rather than suppressed.

A fallen malefic tends to delay and drain with effort that will not land, so lower expectations, add process and go slowly.

Still read the home major stars, triple-direction and transformations rather than judging by brightness alone.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Identify which malefic it is and whether its nature is abrupt or entangling
  2. Check its brightness in the current palace to separate exaltation from fall
  3. Exalted malefic: find a proper goal and channel the force into it
  4. Fallen malefic: lower expectations, add process and use small steps instead of forcing
  5. Combine the home major stars and triple-direction transformations for the net effect
  6. Only then set the remedy rather than treating all malefics alike

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating all malefics as one and suppressing or fearing them uniformly

Mistake 2: Suppressing an exalted malefic and wasting its drive

Mistake 3: Forcing a fallen malefic and turning drain into repeated trouble

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading malefic brightness shows that sharp force is best channeled and entangling force best slowed. Separate power and state, and malefics stop being mere scary labels and can be put to proper use.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to how malefics break things differently in exaltation versus fall before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the how malefics break things differently in exaltation versus fall line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
