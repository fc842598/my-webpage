The Major Star in the Yearly Travel Palace: How Travel, Trips and Changing Environments Go in a Given Year

The Travel Palace governs going out, trips, outer social reach and adapting to environments. The major star in the yearly Travel Palace sets the tone for that year's outward activity. It only suggests whether travel, business trips or changing environments should be pushed or held that year; it cannot be read for fortune apart from the natal chart and the decade limit.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-28

Someone asks whether this year suits business trips or a city move. Do not answer fortune like an almanac; first see which major star sits in this year's Travel Palace, then layer in the natal Travel Palace and the current decade's baseline. Only the three together point to a direction.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The Travel Palace asks how the outer year goes: whether trips meet helpful people, whether a new environment fits, whether being out costs or saves effort. It is a short-term tone; the natal Travel Palace is the long-term base, and the two must not be conflated.

The Underlying Logic

The natal Travel Palace sets the long-term outer reach and travel style, your lifelong outward base.

The decade Travel Palace sets the ten-year direction for going out and changing environments, the mid-term riverbed.

The major star in the yearly Travel Palace is that year's trigger of outward energy, short-lived.

Read the layers: without momentum in the natal and decade palaces, even a lively yearly Travel Palace is just occasional trips; only when they align does the year truly favor moving.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First find the natal Travel Palace and read the long-term outer base
  2. Then read the decade Travel Palace for the ten-year outward direction
  3. Locate this year's Travel Palace and see which major star sits there
  4. Read that star's tone: favor moving, holding, or meeting helpful people
  5. Layer in yearly transformations to see whether they activate the Travel Palace for a concrete trigger
  6. Only when the three layers align do you advise travel or a move; otherwise give a conditional note

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging guaranteed long trips or losses from one star in the yearly Travel Palace alone

Mistake 2: Treating the yearly Travel tone as a long-term conclusion on outer reach

Mistake 3: Reading the year's fortune in isolation without the natal and decade layers

Back to Your Own Chart

The major star in the yearly Travel Palace is only that year's outward wind. The natal and decade palaces are the ship, the yearly Travel Palace the wind; the wind matters only if the ship is fit to sail. When the three align, a year of travel or change is worth moving for.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the major star in the yearly Travel Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the major star in the yearly Travel Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
