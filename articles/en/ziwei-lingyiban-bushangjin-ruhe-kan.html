How to Read an Unmotivated Partner: Temporary Low vs Long-Term Attitude

When a partner seems unmotivated, separate temporary exhaustion or confusion from a long-term avoidance of responsibility and a mismatch in money habits. See whether they will act for shared goals, clarify expectations and division of labor, and give a concrete timeframe for change; if they refuse to take responsibility long term, evaluate the relationship seriously.
