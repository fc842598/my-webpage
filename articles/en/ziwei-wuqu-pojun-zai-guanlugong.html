Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Career Palace: A Career Built on Change and Storming, Turning Execution and Breaking-Rebuilding Into Grounded Success

The Career Palace reads the work state and style. Wu Qu is the wealth star governing firm execution and skill while Po Jun is the depletion star governing change and breaking-rebuilding, so together in Si or Hai one dares to open new fields, fight hard battles and succeed after breaking, with larger work swings and turns than most, suiting technical storming, reform and pioneering posts. The risk is frequent change, depletion and stubbornness, so the key is making change planned with a way back and cash flow.

People with Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Career Palace are bold at work, daring to take hard problems others avoid and often winning credit in transformation; yet when change is fierce and no base is kept, they repeatedly place themselves in start-over situations.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the work pattern with Wu Qu and Po Jun in the Career Palace. Understand the palace reads work, then see how execution and change combine so one dares to storm while keeping the bottom line.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Career Palace makes the career rely on skill, execution and hard ability, willing to work hard, yet also blunt and slow to compromise.

Po Jun there makes work meet change, daring to break and rebuild and succeed after breaking with strong pioneering force, yet also change, depletion and starting over.

Together they mean wealth star plus change: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars under planning storming and transformation become great capacity; with malefics and Ji and no restraint, frequent job changes, loss and setbacks from forcing through follow.

The lesson is breaking with planning and building with a way back: advance change in nodes, keep cash flow and a way back, hear professional advice before deciding and use boldness on calculable hard battles.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Po Jun share the Career Palace and check their brightness
  2. Advance change and transformation in nodes rather than overturning all at once
  3. Keep cash flow and a way back to avoid runaway depletion
  4. Storm through skill and use boldness on calculable hard battles
  5. Hear professional advice before deciding to avoid stubborn forcing
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of work change and pioneering

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Po Jun in the Career Palace only as bold and storming while ignoring change risk

Mistake 2: Frequently starting over with no way back or cash flow

Mistake 3: Stubborn forcing, using boldness on things that cannot be calculated
