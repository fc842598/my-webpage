Qi Sha Facing the Pole Pattern

When Qi Sha guards life at Yin or Shen with Zi Wei opposite, like Qi Sha facing the North Pole, it brings authority, decisiveness and martial distinction, favoring police-military, pioneering and management. Guard isolation and injury; auspicious stars must temper it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

This pattern is a malefic with the emperor star directly opposite, fierceness becoming authority; but authority needs assistants, guard isolation.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see this pattern and assume high martial rank, but auspicious tempering matters even more, or fierceness brings injury.

The Underlying Logic

Qi Sha guards life at Yin Shen, Zi Wei opposite.

It brings authority, decisiveness and martial distinction.

Auspicious stars temper, Zuo You assist.

Guard isolation and injury; malefics break it.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Qi Sha at Yin Shen with Zi Wei opposite
  2. Read Qi Sha brightness
  3. Count auspicious stars for tempering and assistance
  4. Count malefics to guard injury
  5. Read trines for distinction direction
  6. Judge valid or isolation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing the pattern and assuming rank without tempering

Mistake 2: Ignoring isolation and injury

Mistake 3: Seeing only Qi Sha without Zi Wei and trines
