Hua Lu Isn't Always Good: Too Much Lu Can Overwhelm

Hua Lu rules resources and connections, but too much or in the wrong palace can mean scattered opportunities or losing drive from unearned gains.

Methods2026-08-20 10:15

Seeing Hua Lu and feeling happy is every beginner's instinct. Lu means money, opportunity, popularity — who wouldn't? But is more Lu always better? Imagine someone with five earning opportunities at once: which to choose? Trying to grab all, none done well. Hua Lu works the same way — Lu is resource, but too much that you can't catch becomes a burden.

Hua Lu's essence is "connection" and "resource," not "guaranteed good." Which palace Hua Lu falls in shows what you have "affinity" with — affinity doesn't mean ability to catch it. Hua Lu in Wealth means affinity with money, but whether you save depends on Hua Quan or Lu Cun guarding. Hua Lu in Spouse means affinity with romance, but whether it lasts depends on malefics and Hua Ji.

Side Effects of Hua Lu

Too many opportunities scatter — people with much Lu have many choices, but choices ≠ results.

Unearned gains kill drive — some Lu falls from the sky (inheritance, lottery), but doesn't last.

Popular but can't say no — Lu rules affinity; people with much Lu can't refuse others, trapped by obligations.

Lu hit by Ji — if opposite palace has Hua Ji, Lu's benefits get swept away: "looks like it's there but can't get it."

When Hua Lu Is Bad

Hua Lu plus Kong Jie — money comes and goes, like drawing water with a sieve.

Hua Lu plus Hua Ji same palace — sweet first, bitter later; given then taken away.

Hua Lu in Health palace — affinity with illness, prone to weight gain and chronic conditions.

Hua Lu in Parents with malefics — parents give resources but also control you.

Multiple Hua Lu in one palace — too much Lu means "biting off more than you can chew."

How to Use Hua Lu Well

Hua Lu plus Hua Quan — Lu gives resources, Quan gives execution; turns opportunity into result.

Hua Lu plus Hua Ke — Lu gives popularity, Ke gives reputation; earn through name.

Hua Lu plus Lu Cun — Lu Cun is the "savings star," keeps Hua Lu's money.

Hua Lu in the right palace — Wealth suits business, Career suits building, Travel suits developing away.

Reading Order

When you see Hua Lu:

  1. Which palace is it in — where is the "affinity."
  2. Check Hua Quan/Hua Ke — with them, Lu can land.
  3. Check malefics/Kong Jie — they discount Lu.
  4. Check opposite for Hua Ji — Ji opposing Lu sweeps benefits away.
  5. Don't rejoice at Lu — Lu is "opportunity," not "result."
  6. Is your recent "opportunity" real or a distraction?
