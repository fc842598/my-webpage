Zi Wei and Tian Fu Together: Dividing Leadership and Resource Stewardship

How to separate the leadership of Zi Wei from the resource stewardship of Tian Fu in a career reading.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Zi Wei and Tian Fu in the same palace form a steady combination. Zi Wei leans toward direction and authority, while Tian Fu leans toward storage and resource management. Reading them only as a leadership pattern misses the division of work.

Two distinct roles

Zi Wei is the leading star, focused on direction and decisions. Tian Fu is the treasury star, focused on stability, reserves, and risk control. One answers where to go and who decides; the other answers whether resources are enough and how to keep them.

Judgment steps

  1. Confirm the palace where Zi Wei and Tian Fu sit and whether the question is career.
  2. Mark Zi Wei's direction and decisions separately from Tian Fu's reserves and management.
  3. Check transformations to see whether authority or resources are strengthened.
  4. Read helpful and difficult stars to judge steady balance, caution, or dominance.
  5. Connect the Career, Wealth, and opposite palaces to real duties before concluding.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then confirm the shared palace.
  2. Record the two roles separately rather than using one label.
  3. Check transformations, helpers, difficult stars, and supporting palaces.
  4. Add the current cycle to see which part is activated now.
  5. Write the answer as direction plus resource stewardship, not one pattern name.
