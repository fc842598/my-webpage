Zi Wei Dou Shu Birth Chart at 11 p.m.: Comparing Two Candidate Charts

A practical way to compare charts when birth time falls near the late Zi hour boundary.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:14

A birth certificate says 10:55 p.m., but a relative remembers 11:05 p.m. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, that small gap may cross a chart boundary. Keep the original time, birthplace, and date before looking at either interpretation.

Check the inputs first

Confirm whether the recorded clock time included daylight saving time. Use the birthplace rather than the city where the person now lives. Then enter both candidate times with the same software version and calendar settings. Write down which palaces change and whether the program treats the late Zi hour as the next day.

Read only what the evidence supports

A different Life Palace is a reason to inspect the birth record, not to choose the chart that sounds more flattering. Compare the Life Palace, its opposing and supporting palaces, and the longer cycle only after the input rules are clear. If the time cannot be settled, keep both candidate charts and mark conclusions that depend on the disputed minutes.
