How to Memorize the Fourteen Major Stars Fast: Groups and Pairs Beat Rote Order

Group the fourteen major stars by leading, pioneering, managing, strategizing and serving, then link them through pairs such as Sun-Moon and Treasury-Minister; remembering functional roles is far faster than reciting order.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

Memorizing stars is not reciting a roster but first sorting what each mainly governs and then linking them into groups and pairs.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some recite from Zi Wei and get mixed up later; the issue is not memory but treating the fourteen stars as unrelated terms to force in.

The Underlying Logic

Group by function: Zi Wei and Tian Fu lead and preserve, Qi Sha, Po Jun and Tan Lang pioneer and change, Wu Qu manages resources, Tian Ji and Tian Liang handle strategy and principle, Sun and Moon cover light and shade, Tian Tong and Tian Xiang serve and coordinate, and Ju Men analyzes.

Pairs aid memory: Sun and Moon, Tian Fu and Tian Xiang, and Tian Tong and Tian Liang are often understood together.

First remember one-line functions, then learn brightness and palace changes.

Use your own Life Palace major star as an anchor; it sticks better than abstract recitation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First split the fourteen stars into five or six functional groups to shrink the load
  2. Summarize each group shared theme in one line
  3. Then learn three or four pairs and deepen them through contrast
  4. Write one line for each star on what it mainly governs
  5. Match the stars on your chart and familiar charts one by one
  6. Finally learn how they change with transformations and palaces

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reciting in order, which mixes easily and fades fast

Mistake 2: Memorizing names without functions and being unable to use them

Mistake 3: Learning brightness and palaces at once before the basics hold

Back to Your Own Chart

Memorizing major stars teaches grouping, pairing and grasping function. With the frame set, fill in brightness, transformations and palaces and learning no longer tangles.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to memorizing the fourteen major stars before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the memorizing the fourteen major stars line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
