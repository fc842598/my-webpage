How to Map the Four Transformations to Real Life Situations

The Four Transformations are changes in how a star expresses itself, not fixed good or bad scores. Lu is opportunity and resources, Quan control and responsibility, Ke reputation and documents, Ji attachment and debt. Combine the star and palace to translate the terms into real situations.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

If transformation terms are not grounded in real affairs they merely spin; understand the meaning first, then ask which palace and star they attach to, and the meaning becomes concrete.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some treat the transformations as fixed plus or minus scores, while others memorize terms without translating them into plain language, and nothing fits their own life.

The Underlying Logic

The transformations describe changes in how a star expresses; the palace sets the area and the star sets the concrete form.

Lu is opportunity, resources and smoothness; Quan control, responsibility and competition; Ke reputation, smoothing and documents; Ji attachment, debt and sticking points.

The same transformation maps to very different affairs across palaces and stars, so translate each separately.

Turn the meanings into real scenes of work, money, relationships and health to know how to respond.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First learn the basic meanings of Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji without judging good or bad
  2. Locate which palace each lands in to set the area
  3. Read which major star it attaches to for the concrete form
  4. Translate the meaning into a specific scene in real life
  5. Combine the triple-direction and decade to see whether it is activated or discounted
  6. Finally settle how to act rather than stopping at terms

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the transformations as fixed good or bad scores and labeling them on sight

Mistake 2: Memorizing terms without plain-language translation, turning hollow in real life

Mistake 3: Reading only the transformed star without the host star, palace and trines

Back to Your Own Chart

Mapping transformation meanings to real affairs moves the practice from memorizing terms to reading life. Put each transformed star back into its palace, star nature and real situation, and you understand what it is saying.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to mapping the Four Transformations to real human affairs before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the mapping the Four Transformations to real human affairs line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
