Changing Jobs in a Difficult Cycle: Check the Offer First

Use Zi Wei Dou Shu to organize job-change questions, then verify the written offer.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28 09:11

A difficult annual or decade cycle is not an automatic ban on changing jobs. Once an offer arrives, compare the actual role, pay, probation, benefits, location, and exit cost. The Career Palace frames the work and authority; the Travel Palace frames a new environment; the Wealth Palace frames net income.

A management title may come with responsibility but little approval power. A higher salary may be offset by a long commute. Keep these as separate rows in a comparison table. Read the natal and annual charts after confirming birth data and transformation rules. Make the decision from the written contract and your capacity to absorb uncertainty, not from a single unfavorable symbol.
