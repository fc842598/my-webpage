Loss Signals and Wealth Defense in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How to Prepare for Wealth-Palace Ji and Empty Stars

Loss risk reads Wealth-Palace Ji, Di Kong/Di Jie/Da Hao flanking or entering wealth, and an unstable Property vault, plus the decade/year activation timing. These signals are not destined loss but flag a phase of fragile cash flow, impulsive investment or being dragged down; lowering leverage early, keeping reserves and avoiding guarantees/high risk converts loss tendency into a close call.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

The chart gives loss tendency and its window, not fixed loss; knowing which years wealth is fragile and cutting leverage, guarantees and speculation early keeps tendency from becoming reality.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some freeze at Wealth Ji; others leverage up and guarantee loans in an empty-star decade, fulfilling the signal. The right move is calibrated defense, neither lying flat nor charging.

The Underlying Logic

Wealth Ji: tight cash flow, money worry, investment misjudgment or unpaid dues; Wu Qu/Tai Yin Ji is heavier—focus on cash-chain safety.

Empty/Da Hao: Di Kong/Di Jie are void and drain; in Wealth/Property or flanking wealth they mean sudden loss, failed investment and inability to save, clearest when a decade activates.

Property is the vault: stable Property stores wealth, malefic/Ji Property leaks the vault, so even strong earners see money in and out.

Activation and timing: natal shows tendency, decade which ten years are fragile, year which to defend; cut leverage early, keep 6–12 months reserves, no guarantees or speculation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Check Wealth for Ji and Empty/Da Hao stars
  2. Check the Property vault for whether money can be saved
  3. Use the decade to find financially fragile ten years
  4. Use the year to lock key defense years
  5. In defense phases cut leverage, keep reserves, no guarantees or speculation
  6. Resume normal investment after the signal phase, not permanently idle

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Lying flat at any loss signal

Mistake 2: Leveraging, guaranteeing and speculating during empty-star activation

Mistake 3: Reading natal tendency without decade/year timing

Back to Your Own Chart

Loss signals are valuable because they warn early. See where Wealth Ji and empty stars fall and which years activate them, then cut leverage, keep reserves and avoid guarantees and speculation—and loss tendency becomes close-call financial discipline.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to loss signals and wealth defense before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the loss signals and wealth defense line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
