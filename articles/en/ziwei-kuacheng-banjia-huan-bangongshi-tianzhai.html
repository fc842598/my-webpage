Zi Wei Dou Shu Property Palace: Moving Cities Versus Changing Offices

The Property Palace covers both home and workplace, but a cross-city move and an office change need separate checks.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-06 11:32

In the same month, someone receives a transfer to another city while the company also changes offices locally. Both are loosely described as the Property Palace moving, yet they affect different spaces, costs and time spans. The Property Palace does cover both home and workplace, but the first step is to identify which space is changing and who bears the cost.

Identify which space is changing

The Property Palace includes the home, office, factory and inherited property, along with one's sense of stability. A cross-city move changes the long-term residence and often involves a lease, family relocation, household registration and daily-life radius. A local office change affects the workplace, where the company usually holds the lease and the person faces mainly commuting and desk changes. They may be triggered in the same month, but they should be labeled separately.

Read the natal chart first, then the timing

Read the natal Property Palace first. Steady stars such as Lu Cun and Tuo Luo suggest stability and slow change, while moving stars such as Tian Ma, Kong Jie and Huo Xing suggest more fluctuation. That is the base. Then check whether decade and annual transformations activate it: without timing, a moving star is only a tendency, and an annual trigger points to a specific move. Separating the natal structure from the timing prevents a hasty prediction.

Use different checklists

An engineer moving to another city checks the new lease and deposit, family arrangements, moving costs and the reporting date. A worker whose company moves to another building in the same city checks the new commute, transport allowance and office conditions, with no need to change housing. Both charts show movement, but one lands on living and the other on work. Read the natal tendency, then the timing, then the real contracts, leases and costs.
