Qi Sha and Po Jun in an Annual Cycle

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-01 08:43

An annual cycle is the one-year layer of a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart. Qi Sha emphasizes decisions under pressure, while Po Jun represents dismantling an old arrangement so something different can replace it. Together they can mark a demanding year of change, but the palace they activate shows what is changing and whether the cost is worthwhile.

Qi Sha Brings Decisions and Responsibility

Annual Qi Sha often coincides with situations that cannot be postponed. If Qi Sha enters the Career Palace while Hua Quan, the transformation of authority, also activates that palace, a person may inherit a troubled project, compete for a position, or take responsibility for a team. This can build status, although it rarely feels effortless.

Po Jun Rebuilds Through Disruption

Po Jun does not simply mean loss. It points to replacement: changing jobs, restructuring a business, moving home, ending an outdated contract, or replacing equipment. Annual Po Jun in the Wealth Palace may bring renovation costs, investment in new systems, or project overruns. If Hua Ji also reaches the Wealth Palace, protect liquidity and require a clear return before spending.

Judge Whether Change Creates a Better Structure

When Qi Sha activates the Career Palace and Po Jun activates the Travel Palace, a company reorganization, relocation, or new market may alter both role and working environment. If Hua Lu supports the Wealth Palace, the transition may open a workable income channel. Without financial support, a busy year can produce movement without durable gain.

The useful question is not whether the year looks calm. Ask whether the disruption produces a clearer role, stronger operating system, better market, or sustainable income. If no replacement structure is visible, reduce commitments and preserve options.

Chart-Reading Order

Begin with the annual palace containing Qi Sha or Po Jun and define the affected life area. Read its opposite palace and supporting triad, then inspect the Career, Wealth, and Travel Palaces for position, funding, and external conditions. Add Hua Lu, Hua Quan, and Hua Ji to identify resources, responsibility, and leakage. Finally compare the annual layer with the active ten-year cycle before deciding what to change, fund, or postpone.
