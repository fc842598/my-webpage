The Six Auspicious Assistant Stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Real Help or Just Noise

The six auspicious assistants form three pairs—Zuo Fu/You Bi for team support, Wenchang/Wenqu for talent and documents, Tian Kui/Tian Yue for helpful people. Read them by whether the major star can receive them, not by their lucky name.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

The six assistants are not six lucky tickets but three ways of being supported; they fire only when attached to a major star with a clear main line.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People see all six around the Life Palace and predict constant help, yet in reality no one steps up—the error is counting stars without checking reception.

The Underlying Logic

Zuo Fu and You Bi run the team-network line: visible open help versus quiet backstage cover.

Wenchang and Wenqu run the talent-document line—learning, exams, expression and turning thought into visible output.

Tian Kui and Tian Yue run the helpful-people line, often a decisive push from a senior, boss, institution or key moment.

One tradition treats helper stars as other-power and skill stars as self-power: patronage waits on timing, skill must be trained.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Sort the six into team, talent or helpful-people lines
  2. Locate which palace they land in, i.e. where help appears
  3. Check whether that palace's major star has a main line to receive it
  4. Read Zuo Fu/You Bi for teams, Chang/Qu for output, Kui/Yue for key people
  5. Use decades and years to see when the help actually arrives
  6. Without reception, build the major star and ability instead of waiting for a patron

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging a good fate from the six without the major star's reception

Mistake 2: Blending three different kinds of help into a vague someone-will-help

Mistake 3: Assuming patronage arrives with no effort on your part

Back to Your Own Chart

The six assistants really answer who receives your effort and how. Build a main line and real skill first, or the auspicious stars spin as empty reputation.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the six auspicious assistant stars before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the six auspicious assistant stars line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
