How to Find the Focal Palaces of a Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart

A chart does not spread force evenly across twelve palaces; the focus sits where stars cluster, transformations repeatedly activate and malefics meet. Count the stars and transformations in each triple-direction to find the heaviest palaces, which form the main axis of the life.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Many read every palace with equal effort and understand none deeply. A chart has a focus; find the busiest, most repeatedly activated places and the main axis becomes clear.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is which palaces to enter through. Three clues set the focus: where stars cluster, where the transformations land, and which palace the current decade is moving through.

The Underlying Logic

Star focus: a palace whose triple-direction clearly holds more auspicious or malefic stars than others is heavy.

Transformation focus: the palaces of natal Lu and Ji, and those the decade transformations repeatedly activate, are the main channels of energy.

Time focus: the current decade Life Palace and its triple-direction are the domains lit in these ten years.

The focus usually lies on one or two lines rather than one isolated palace; finding the line beats finding the point.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First list the triple-direction stars of each palace and count auspicious and harsh
  2. Mark where the natal transformations land to set energy channels
  3. Then see which line the current decade palace and transformations activate
  4. Circle the palaces where clustered stars, transformations and the decade overlap
  5. Connect palaces into lines to find one or two main axes
  6. Read the focal points deeply first, then fill the rest

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Spreading effort evenly across twelve palaces with no focus

Mistake 2: Reading only the number of stars without transformations and the decade

Mistake 3: Fixating on one palace instead of connecting palaces into lines

Back to Your Own Chart

A chart is read deeply through its focus: where clustered stars, transformations and the decade overlap is the main axis. Grasp that line first, then add detail, and the whole chart gains priorities.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to finding the focal palaces of a chart before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the finding the focal palaces of a chart line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
