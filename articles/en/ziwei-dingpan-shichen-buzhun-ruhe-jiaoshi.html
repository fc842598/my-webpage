How to Verify a Zi Wei Chart When the Birth Hour Is Uncertain

When the hour is uncertain, do not force a verdict; cast the two adjacent hours and compare personality, family and major past turning points against each—the chart that fits the facts is the right one.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

Verification means fitting the chart to the person, not bending the person to a possibly wrong chart; facts are the only test of the hour.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Knowing only morning or afternoon, people pick an hour loosely and find the reading never fits; known life events can reverse-engineer the correct block.

The Underlying Logic

One block spans two hours, and crossing it can move the whole chart, so hour is the first gate.

List the two or three adjacent candidate blocks and cast a candidate chart for each.

Match personality via the Life major star, family via its palaces, and past event years via decades and annual palaces.

The candidate that explains personality and several events together is the verified chart; a single match does not count.

Leap months, late Zi hour and distant birthplaces also need calendar and true-solar-time correction.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Gather birth facts: time range, birthplace and leap-month status
  2. List every plausible adjacent block and cast candidate charts
  3. Use personality to quickly rule out obvious mismatches
  4. Narrow with parents, siblings and partner circumstances
  5. Compare years of past events such as schooling, moving, marriage and job changes
  6. When several stay close, use the most recent one or two annual events as the final arbiter

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading in detail before the hour is fixed and trusting conclusions on a wrong chart

Mistake 2: Verifying from one coincidental event instead of cross-checking multiple points

Mistake 3: Ignoring true solar time and early/late Zi hour, sending correction the wrong way

Back to Your Own Chart

Verification precedes every interpretation. Better to spend time correcting the hour than let an uncalibrated chart lead you astray.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to chart verification and hour correction before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the chart verification and hour correction line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
