Yue De in the Blessings Palace: A Broad, Self-Healing Mind

Yue De in the Blessings Palace suggests a broad mind able to bear matters, working through mental passes and easing them slowly. Stay content and grateful, give emotion an outlet, for a broad heart is blessing.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-12

Broad-minded people are not free of trouble but can hold and release it; a wider inner scale feels lighter.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some fixate on matters and turn on themselves; stars are tendency, while tolerance and letting go are practiced.

The Underlying Logic

Yue De in Blessings means a broad, bearing mind.

Mental passes can self-resolve.

Stay content, give emotion an outlet.

This is tendency, not an absolute verdict.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Think broadly in trouble
  2. Cultivate contentment and gratitude
  3. Give emotion an outlet
  4. Fixate less
  5. Nourish the mind with interests and exercise
  6. Rest on a calm heart

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fixating, turning on self

Mistake 2: Unable to let matters go

Mistake 3: Ignoring emotional outlets

Back to Your Own Chart

Yue De in the Blessings Palace is a fine sign of a broad, self-healing mind. Stay content, let go and keep an outlet for steady ease.
