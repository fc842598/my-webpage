An annual cycle shows which parts of a birth chart are emphasized for one year. When Tan Lang becomes prominent, opportunities, social activity, ambition, pleasure, and temptation may all increase together. The palace it occupies tells you where the activity is likely to appear.

Start With the Annual Palace

Annual Tan Lang in the Wealth Palace directs attention toward earning, transactions, customers, and spending. In the Spouse Palace, relationships, attraction, and negotiation become more important. In the Travel Palace, new networks, trips, outside markets, or a change of environment may create openings. Tan Lang does not mean the same event everywhere; it describes an appetite-driven way of engaging with that palace.

Money and Desire Often Arrive Together

Annual Tan Lang in the Wealth Palace together with Hua Lu can support sales growth, more clients, commissions, or revenue from entertainment and audience-facing work. Yet Tan Lang in the Wealth Palace while Hua Ji affects the Inner Life Palace can describe earning more while also spending more for comfort, status, or emotional relief. The year may look prosperous in gross income but feel disappointing in retained cash.

Use Career Structure and Relationship Boundaries

A strong Career Palace can turn Tan Lang's social energy into campaigns, partnerships, and measurable business development. A weak or obstructed Career Palace may leave a trail of exciting conversations without dependable results. Annual Tan Lang in the Spouse Palace with an unsettled Inner Life Palace can also bring attraction or relationship ambiguity that interferes with judgment. Written terms, spending limits, and a clear distinction between personal and commercial ties are especially valuable during such a year.

Chart-Reading Order

First locate annual Tan Lang and define the real-life topic of that palace. Next check Hua Lu, Hua Quan, Hua Ke, and Hua Ji for resources, authority, recognition, or blockage. Compare the Wealth, Career, Spouse, and Inner Life Palaces for support and leakage. Finally connect the annual pattern to the ten-year cycle and natal chart before deciding whether to expand, negotiate, save, or step back.
