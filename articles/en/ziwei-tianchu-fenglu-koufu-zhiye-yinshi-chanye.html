Tian Chu in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Salary-and-Palate Star of Food Blessing and the Food Industry

Tian Chu governs salary and food blessing, palate and cuisine-related talent. In Life or Property it brings institutional benefits and no worry over food, plus discernment, enjoyment and cooking or tasting gift, clearer with Tan Lang or Ju Men Hua Lu; in Career it favors catering, food and nutrition industries.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

It is the rice-bowl star: one layer stable salary benefits, another palate and food talent—not great wealth but eating well and building a living around food.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Dismissed as a gluttony star, when activated in Career/Wealth it often points to catering, food, supply chain and nutrition—a genuine career clue.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Chu is a blessing assistant governing salary, allowance, palate, cuisine, banquets and food craft, leaning enjoyment and steady supply.

In Life/Property/Happiness: good palate, life enjoyment, institutional benefits, no lack of basic provision.

With Tan Lang: loves and cooks food; with Ju Men Hua Lu: earning through the mouth—catering, food, tasting streams, teaching.

Activated in Career/Wealth suits catering, food, agriculture, nutrition, hotels and supply chain, still needing major-star and transformation support.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate Tian Chu to see where food blessing shows
  2. In Career/Wealth: assess fit with food industries
  3. With Tan Lang/Ju Men, develop tasting, cooking and verbal expression
  4. Upgrade loving food into professional skill, not just indulgence
  5. With auspicious transformations, seek stable salary and benefit roles
  6. Mind moderation—even the palate star suffers excess

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Only seeing gluttony, missing the career clue

Mistake 2: Indulging without professionalizing food talent

Mistake 3: Unbounded palate becoming a burden

Back to Your Own Chart

Tian Chu governs food blessing: both the security of enough to eat and a talent to build on. Turn palate into profession and salary into accumulation, and this gentle star nurtures a genuinely good living.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Tian Chu the kitchen star before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Tian Chu the kitchen star line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
