Named Patterns Must Be Bright Enough to Count

A guide to judging famous Zi Wei Dou Shu patterns by brightness and working strength first, instead of by name alone.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-26 10:39

A pattern name sounds impressive, but it only becomes real when the stars are bright enough and the structure is strong enough to work.

What This Means

This topic is about chart discipline. A famous pattern should not be granted just because the pieces are present. Brightness, support, and correct placement decide whether the pattern is fully alive or only loosely similar.

How To Read It

Confirm the structure, then test brightness, support, and palace position. Only after those conditions pass should you decide whether the pattern deserves the name and the larger interpretation.

Simple Examples

  • Read the star through the palace and the real-life role it points to, rather than using a vague fixed prediction.
  • A chart can contain the right stars and still fail to produce the real pattern because brightness is too weak.
  • Another chart may not use the famous label often, yet it works strongly because the structure is complete.

Practical Order

First define the question. Then read the palace, its opposite palace, the supporting palaces, and the ten-year or annual trigger. This keeps the reading useful for career, money, relationships, and real choices.
