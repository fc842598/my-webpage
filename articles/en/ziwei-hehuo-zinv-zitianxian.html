Why Partnerships Are Read in the Children Palace: Career's Friend, the Children-Property Axis, and Flying Prosperity

Partnerships are read through the Children Palace because it is the Career Palace's friendship position, with the Children-Property axis and transformations showing whether cooperation can produce results.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 10:35

In Zi Wei Dou Shu, partnerships are mainly read through the Children Palace because it is the friendship position of the Career Palace and represents cooperation partners; many partnerships grow out of friendships. Whether a partnership should be entered depends on the Children Palace, the Children-Property axis, and transformation paths rather than one lucky star.

Why the Children Palace

The Children Palace is the career's friend, meaning the cooperation position in work, and it also represents branches, customers, and paying partners. A stable palace with prosperity gives a better chance of gaining through cooperation, but financial control often sits with the partner while you take a share. A challenge or empty-type star warns that the partnership may come to nothing.

The axis and prosperity path

If the Children-Property axis in the major limit or annual cycle carries tense or challenging stars, partnership is not advisable. To see whether cooperation can produce results, look for prosperity, power, or reputation flying from the Children Palace to the Parents Palace, which is the career position of the Children topic; only that trigger suggests the partnership may bear fruit.

Step-by-step reading

  1. Read the birth-chart Children Palace's stars and helper stars to judge the partnership base.
  2. Confirm the palace as the career's friend and separate partners from ordinary friends.
  3. Look at the Children-Property axis in the major limit and annual cycle; pause if it carries tense stars.
  4. Check whether transformations fly to the Parents Palace to judge whether results can be made.
  5. Then review Wealth and documents, checking profit sharing and contract risk before deciding.

Reading limits

The palace describes partnership tendencies; it does not guarantee success or mean a challenged star makes cooperation impossible. In real life, writing clear equity, division of work, and exit rules prevents most risks. The chart flags risk, while contract and execution decide the result.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart and locate the Children Palace and the Children-Property axis.
  2. Read the birth-chart base first, then the limit triggers.
  3. Check the transformation path to the Parents Palace to see whether results are possible.
  4. Combine Wealth and Parents documents to review profit sharing and contracts.
  5. Match the conclusion to real cooperation conditions without absolute claims.
