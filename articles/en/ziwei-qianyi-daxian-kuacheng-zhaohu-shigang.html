Zi Wei Dou Shu Relocation: Trial Work Before Moving for a Care Job

Compare the Travel Palace, decade cycle, and annual timing before deciding whether a two-week care job trial justifies a full move.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-26 09:00

A care home in another city offers a two-week trial before a one-year contract. The recruiter mentions staff housing, but the written offer says only that the employer will “help with accommodation.” You also support your father at home. A Zi Wei Dou Shu chart can organize the questions around travel and work. It cannot turn a trial into a secure long-term move.

Start with the natal Travel Palace

Read the stars and supporting factors in the Travel Palace alongside the opposite Life Palace, Career, and Wealth. A favorable travel pattern does not establish the number of night shifts or the price of a room. If Zi Wei and Tan Lang sit opposite a Life Palace with Qing Yang, keep their original chart positions clear. The opposite palace offers context; its stars do not physically move into Life. If the birth hour is uncertain, check the chart before using a palace placement to guide a contract decision.

Separate the decade trend from this year's offer

A decade cycle connected with Career and Travel may make a change of workplace relevant over several years. It does not endorse this employer. The annual layer can narrow the questions. If Wealth and Property are also involved, ask when trial pay arrives, whether housing is charged, and what happens after the trial. If the annual Travel Palace is active while family care is under pressure, a short trial with a replacement care plan may be workable even when an immediate full move is not.

Make the trial reversible

Request written details for trial length, shifts, rest days, housing address and charges, and return travel. Put transport, temporary lodging, replacement care for your father, and any gap in earnings into one budget. Treat an oral housing promise as unconfirmed until it appears in writing. Keep the current home arrangement until the trial produces an actual roster, net pay, and a sustainable family care plan.

Finish with evidence outside the chart

Confirm birth details and the convention for the annual cycle. Locate natal Travel, Life, Career, Wealth, and Parents, then compare the decade and annual placements. Match each question to a document or observed result: contract, accommodation terms, trial costs, and family coverage. The chart structures the review; the decision to move depends on what the trial proves.
