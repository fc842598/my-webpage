The Xiong Su Gan Yuan Pattern: Lian Zhen and Qi Sha

When Lian Zhen and Qi Sha share the Wei palace guarding life, yin fire tempers yin metal, bringing professional standing, martial distinction and resolute strategy. The pattern forms with Lu Quan Ke and no malefic Ji; it most fears Huo Xing conjunct breaking it (misregulated furnace).

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

This pattern is like forging: Lian Zhen is fire and Qi Sha metal; only the right heat makes a vessel, too fierce a fire burns it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Lian Zhen and Qi Sha and call it this pattern, but it needs the Wei palace, auspicious transformations and no Huo Xing break; conditions are many.

The Underlying Logic

Lian Zhen yin fire and Qi Sha yin metal together in Wei, fire making a vessel.

It governs professional or martial work, technical standing, resolve.

It likes Lu Quan Ke and Wen Chang Wen Qu for name and substance.

It avoids Huo Xing conjunct (too much fire) and malefic Ji clashes, which break it.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Life Palace in Wei with Lian Zhen and Qi Sha
  2. Check the trine for Lu Quan Ke
  3. Focus on whether Huo Xing is conjunct (key break)
  4. Count other malefics and Ji for furnace balance
  5. Read Chang Qu for professional name
  6. Judge vessel formed or pattern broken

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing Lian Zhen and Qi Sha and calling it this pattern regardless of palace

Mistake 2: Ignoring the Huo Xing break

Mistake 3: Seeing only resolve, not guarding rigidity causing loss

Back to Your Own Chart

This pattern asks whether fire tempers metal into a vessel. Right palace, auspicious transformations and no excess fire, and resolve becomes professional distinction rather than recklessness.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Xiong Su Gan Yuan pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Xiong Su Gan Yuan pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
