Wealth Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Income Is Not the Same as Cash Flow

A practical guide to reading the Wealth Palace for income sources, payment timing, spending pressure, and the ability to keep money.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:42

Money Has a Flow

The Wealth Palace covers income source, payment rhythm, spending pressure, and whether money can stay available.

Read Career and Property

Career shows the skill or role that creates income. Property shows long-term housing or family assets. Life shows whether the person can keep a rule.

Two Examples

Wealth Hua Lu with Career Hua Quan can bring orders and heavier delivery. Wealth Hua Ji with Property Hua Ke may call for a budget before investment.

The Practical Question

Do not ask only how much money appears. Ask when it arrives, what claims it, and which structure keeps the cash flow healthy.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
