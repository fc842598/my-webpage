Partnership Wealth in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Siblings and Wealth Palace Analysis

Partnership entrepreneurship is a path many choose, but success rates are not high. From the chart perspective, whether you suit partnerships, who to partner with, and how to distribute power can be seen from the combination of Siblings Palace (peers/partners) and Wealth Palace (money distribution).

Chart structures suitable for partnerships: first, Siblings Palace has auspicious stars (Zuo Fu, You Bi, Tian Kui, Tian Yue, Prosperity, Recognition)—good peer relations, reliable partners, easily meets good co-founders. Second, Life Palace has cooperative stars like Tai Yin, Tian Xiang, Tian Fu—good at compromise and coordination, suitable for team roles. Third, Wealth Palace has Prosperity or Authority—financial support after partnership, can earn money through collaboration. Fourth, Friends Palace is in good condition—wide network, can expand business through partners resources. Key distributions in partnerships: first, "equity distribution"—do not split 50-50 (most likely to cause decision deadlock), one person should lead (51%+). Those with Siblings Palace malefics should especially avoid equal equity. Second, "power distribution"—clarify who is responsible for what, financial power and operational power are best separated. Fourth, "financial transparency"—those with Wealth Palace Jealousy most easily develop suspicion due to opaque finances, must establish standard financial systems. Methods to avoid partnership fallout: first, "be tough before, gentle after"—put all rules (equity, roles, exit, dividends) in the agreement before partnering, do not rely on verbal agreements. What This Question Is Really Asking

Practical case: Someone with Prosperity in Wealth but Jealousy in Tian Zhai has good income but cannot save—earns much but spends much, or loses money through property or family expenses. The key is "income looks at Wealth, preservation looks at Tian Zhai"—both palaces must be read.

The Underlying Logic

Only looking at Wealth Palace without Tian Zhai, earning much but not preserving.

Equating Prosperity with "will definitely get rich," ignoring that Prosperity can also mean "more money more problems."

Not watching Decade/Annual wealth changes, making wrong investments at wrong times.

Treating chart financial tendencies as investment advice, without own research and risk assessment.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Chart the Wealth, Tian Zhai, and Fortune palaces major stars and transformations.
  2. See the Decade Wealth Palace state to judge the ten-year income trend.
  3. Distinguish between "regular income" (salary) and "windfall" (investment, side business) sources.
  4. Check Tian Zhai wealth-preservation ability, set savings and asset allocation plans.
  5. Take initiative in good wealth years, hold steady in bad wealth years.
  6. Regularly review financial status and adjust strategy based on actual conditions.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: The chart only indicates financial tendencies; it does not constitute investment advice.

Mistake 2: Good wealth years can still lose money from wrong decisions; bad wealth years can still preserve capital through caution.

Mistake 3: Financial content is based on licensed institutions and official product descriptions; investments carry risk.

Back to Your Own Chart

The chart helps you understand your financial personality and risk points; real wealth accumulation comes from consistent saving habits, reasonable asset allocation, and continuously improving earning ability.
