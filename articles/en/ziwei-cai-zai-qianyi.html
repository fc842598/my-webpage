Wealth in the Travel Palace

Wealth in the Travel Palace links income to external environments: other cities, remote clients, online platforms, cross-regional networks, or markets.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-01 08:43

Wealth in the Travel Palace links income to external environments: other cities, remote clients, online platforms, cross-regional networks, or markets beyond the familiar circle. It does not mean that travel automatically creates wealth. The outside opportunity still needs a workable role, collection path, and cost structure.

Define What “Outside” Means

Travel can describe physical relocation, but it can also mean a larger company, a digital platform, a foreign-facing role, or customers outside the local network. Wu Qu or Hua Lu in Travel may support commercially measurable work in those settings. Tan Lang may favor client development, products, or networking. Tian Ma adds movement and speed, which can widen reach but also increase turnover and operating costs.

Check Whether Outside Income Can Be Kept

The natal Wealth Palace shows whether revenue can be collected and retained. A strong Travel Palace with weak Wealth may produce visible sales but thin profit after platform fees, logistics, travel, refunds, or delayed payment. Hua Ji on the Travel-Wealth axis can make contracts, credit terms, and collection more important. Di Kong or Di Jie calls for testing promised demand before committing fixed costs.

Use Career and Timing as Conditions

For example, Travel with Tian Ma and Hua Ke may favor a launch, presentation, certification, or move that makes expertise visible, especially when Career supplies a clear role. Travel with Hua Lu can open customer flow, but expansion is safer when Wealth confirms margin and liquidity. If only the annual cycle activates Travel, the year may bring a market test or temporary movement rather than a permanent relocation. A matching ten-year cycle makes the external theme more persistent.

Chart-Reading Order

Start with the stars and strength of the Travel Palace. Then inspect Wealth for collection, margin, and retention, followed by Career for operational support. Read the opposite Life Palace to see whether the person can adapt to the outside environment. Add the Four Transformations, Tian Ma, and difficult stars as conditions, then compare natal, ten-year, and annual layers. Finish with a practical decision about testing, moving, pricing, or limiting expansion.
