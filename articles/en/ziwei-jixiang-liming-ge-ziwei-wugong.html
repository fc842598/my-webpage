The Ji Xiang Li Ming Pattern in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Zi Wei in the Wu Palace

Zi Wei alone in the Wu palace, with Tan Lang in Travel, Lian Zhen Tian Fu in Career and Wu Qu Tian Xiang in Wealth, forms the Ji Xiang Li Ming pattern when no malefic or Ji breaks it and Zuo You, Kui Yue and the transformations meet, indicating high standing. With no auspicious meeting it falls to a lone ruler in the wild, and heavy malefic clashes break it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some assume Zi Wei in the Life Palace means wealth and rank, but even the emperor needs ministers; whether Zuo You and Kui Yue attend and whether malefics clash set the pattern level.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is why Zi Wei in Wu leads a great pattern for some and an ordinary life for others. It lies in the triple-direction: auspicious meetings without malefic breaks form the pattern, while a lone ruler without support drops sharply.

The Underlying Logic

Ji refers to Zi Wei the polar emperor and Li Ming to the Wu palace, the bright position; Zi Wei in Wu is always exalted.

To form it, the triple-direction holds no Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Huo Xing, Ling Xing, Kong, Jie or heavy Ji, and meets Zuo You, Kui Yue and the transformations.

It breaks when heavy malefics clash into a lone ruler without a way, while no auspicious meeting makes a lone ruler in the wild, lowering the pattern.

It indicates leadership, command and noble bearing, but real achievement still depends on support and the time cycles; Zi Wei alone is not enough.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Zi Wei alone sits in the Wu Life Palace
  2. List the trine: Tan Lang in Travel, Lian Fu in Career and Wu Xiang in Wealth
  3. Count whether malefics, emptiness or Ji break it
  4. Then see whether Zuo You, Kui Yue and the transformations meet
  5. Auspicious meetings without breaks form it; no meeting is a lone ruler and heavy malefics break it
  6. Conclude with the time cycles, never judging rank from Zi Wei alone

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring great wealth and rank at the sight of Zi Wei

Mistake 2: Reading only the emperor without support and malefics, ignoring the conditions

Mistake 3: Treating a lone ruler without auspicious meetings as a top pattern
