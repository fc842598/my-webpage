Read the Decade or the Year First: Natal as the Base, Decade Before Year

The right order is natal as the base, then the decade to set the ten-year tone, then the year to set that year trigger; skipping the decade to read the year makes short-term fluctuation look like long-term direction.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

The decade is the background to the year; knowing where the ten years head first gives a coordinate for any single year.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some ask at once about this year and conclude from the year alone, then find good and bad years read loosely; the issue is the missing decade background.

The Underlying Logic

The natal chart is the base, describing lifelong structure and tone and the premise of every judgment.

The decade is the first application, a ten-year segment setting that stage main line and resources before the year.

The year is then applied within the decade background to set that year specific trigger and rhythm.

Reversing the order overgeneralizes: a good year in a weak decade is limited, and a bad year in a strong decade still has support.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First cast the natal chart to set lifelong structure and trines
  2. Then find the current decade and read its ten-year main line, transformations and resources
  3. Within the decade background locate this year and see its activation
  4. Check the year conclusion against the decade to gauge strength and bounds
  5. When acting, refer to monthly and daily stars for fine adjustment
  6. Build the fixed natal, decade, year order without skipping

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Skipping the decade and concluding from the year alone

Mistake 2: Taking annual fluctuation as long-term direction

Mistake 3: Mixing the order so layer conclusions conflict

Back to Your Own Chart

Decade-before-year teaches keeping the base-and-application order. With the ten-year tone clear, any single year reads accurately and sits in its proper place.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to whether to read the decade or the year first before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the whether to read the decade or the year first line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
