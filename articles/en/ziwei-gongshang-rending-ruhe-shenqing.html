Zi Wei Dou Shu on Work-Injury Determination: Seek Care and File in Time

How an injury at work is determined often maps to the Career Palace for work and the Health Palace for injury. Within the framework of injury arising at work time, place and cause, seek care and keep evidence first; the employer generally applies within 30 days and the individual within one year, submitting evidence promptly.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-30

The key is seeking care and keeping evidence first and filing within the period, with proof of both the employment relationship and how the injury happened.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some take a small private settlement after injury without applying for determination or keeping medical and accident evidence, only to find the period has passed when the condition recurs.

The Underlying Logic

Work injury is often triggered when the Career Palace covers work and the Health Palace injury, marking physical harm arising from work.

Determination usually looks at work time, place and cause, requiring an employment relationship and evidence.

The employer generally applies within 30 days; if it does not, the individual may apply within one year.

The Health Palace shows health tendencies rather than diagnosis, so seek formal care after injury; with Hua Ji and malefics, keep medical records, attendance and accident evidence, with the determination and professional legal advice as the basis.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. After injury, seek formal care and keep medical records, diagnosis and payment proof
  2. Confirm the employment relationship and gather the contract, attendance and pay records
  3. Fix evidence of how the accident happened, such as photos, witnesses or footage
  4. Read the Career and Health Palaces to judge the work-injury link
  5. Apply for determination within the period, individually if the employer does not
  6. If you disagree with the determination or compensation, seek remedy by procedure, with professional legal advice as the basis

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking a private settlement without applying for determination

Mistake 2: Missing the application period and unable to claim later

Mistake 3: Not keeping medical and accident evidence, making proof difficult
