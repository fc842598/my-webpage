Tai Yang in Friends Palace: Many Benefactor Friends, but Beware "Fair-Weather Friends"

Tai Yang in Friends brings many friends and benefactors with strong social luck. But being good to everyone makes you exploitable; learn to filter.

Main Stars2026-08-21 10:15

Tai Yang in Friends means many friends — warm, generous, helpful, others naturally want to be your friend. Your circle does contain benefactors who help at key moments. But the problem: you're good to everyone and can't tell who's genuine from who's using you. The lesson: kindness needs an edge.

Friends palace shows friends, colleagues, and subordinates. Tai Yang here means strong social luck with many friends of good caliber. Tai Yang's universal love earns trust and affection but also attracts "fair-weather friends" who drain you. Temple/prosperous means many true benefactors; fallen means many friends but few genuine ones, or worry and loss through friends.

Social Traits of Tai Yang in Friends

Many friends — natural social charm, makes friends everywhere.

Many benefactors — friends willing to help and mentor you.

Generous to friends — treating, helping, connecting resources; never stingy.

Easily exploited — because you're good to everyone, some take your giving for granted.

Good subordinate luck — if you manage, subordinates tend to be loyal.

Relationship Patterns by Combination

Tai Yang with Tian Liang — elder-type benefactors among friends; long, reliable bonds.

Tai Yang with Kui/Yue — extremely strong benefactor luck; help always arrives.

Tai Yang with Hua Lu — friends bring resources and opportunities; strong social monetization.

Tai Yang with Qing Yang — quick-tempered friends; conflicts but possibly the most loyal.

Tai Yang with Hua Ji — dragged down or deceived by friends; choose carefully.

Advice for Tai Yang in Friends

Learn to filter — not everyone deserves your all; observe how someone treats those weaker.

Don't be a "yes person" — refuse when needed; real friends won't leave over a refusal.

Clear accounts even with brothers — money matters must be explicit; don't be vague for face.

Cultivate a few true friends — quality beats quantity; invest in deep relationships.

Reserve kindness for those who deserve it — your light and warmth are precious; don't waste them on users.

Reading Order

For Tai Yang in Friends:

  1. Check brightness — temple/prosperous: true benefactors; fallen: mixed crowd.
  2. Check transformations — Lu: friends bring resources; Ji: dragged down.
  3. Check co-stars — Tian Liang: reliable; Kui/Yue: benefactors; Qing Yang: impatient.
  4. Check Life — does your kindness have boundaries.
  5. Check cycles — when a cycle reaches Friends, relationships are key.
  6. You're good to everyone, but who is good to you?
