Zi Wei and Tian Xiang in the Wealth Palace: Earning With Authority and Rules, Turning Leadership and the Seal Into Steady Wealth

The Wealth Palace reads the earning style and cash state. Zi Wei governs dignity, dominance and leadership while Tian Xiang governs the seal, rules, assistance and coordination, so together in Chen or Xu one holds initiative, keeps trust and minds tact in earning, making steady money through management, authorization and positions within systems, suiting management, institutions, finance and posts requiring authorization. The risk is pride of face, dependence on authorization and overcaution, so the key is treating the seal as responsibility and acting within rules. The Underlying Logic

Zi Wei in the Wealth Palace gives initiative, pride of face and a wish to grow, daring to take big projects, yet also conceit and disdain for small money.

Tian Xiang there gives rule-minded, trust-focused, coordinating earning that is steady and reliable, yet also caution and passively waiting for authorization.

Together they mean leadership plus the seal: authority and responsibility match, and with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars one earns steadily within systems; with malefics and Ji, pressure from unclear authority, face or procedures follows.

The lesson is leading with rules and the seal used for oneself: treat trust and credentials as a pass, clarify contracts and procedures early, seek actively within rules and decide within one’s means.
