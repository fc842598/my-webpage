Free quota is easiest to waste on huge vague questions or on questions you cannot verify at all.

The better use is one chart-linked issue that matters to a live choice right now.

What matters first

Spend the free layer on questions you can verify or use to sort action order.

A small quota still has value if every question stays on the main line.

The current public boundary can be checked as 3 guest uses a day, 8 free logged-in uses a day, 80 member uses a day, and a current member price of 19.90 RMB.

The more useful answer usually explains why it is reading the chart that way, what to verify next, and where not to over-claim.

A practical scenario

A current job-move question is usually worth more than a total-life question.

Asking which chart line matters first is also a stronger free test than asking for a verdict.

When a reading lands on chart structure, a real-life scenario, and a next verification step at the same time, it becomes much easier to judge whether it is actually useful.

The boundary to remember

Free quota is not for collecting comforting lines.

Scattered questions empty the quota faster than the platform does.

Whether the layer is free or paid, a safer product lets you verify in a small scope before asking for deeper commitment.

A safer order

  1. Pick one main line.
  2. Use the quota on verification or action-order questions.
  3. Leave broad summary questions for later.
