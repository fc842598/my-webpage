Lu and Ji in the Same Palace: When Opportunity and Blockage Appear Together

How to read a palace that receives both a Lu and a Ji, and how to decide which side to handle first.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

When Lu and Ji land in the same palace, opportunity and blockage are both real. Lu points to resources and willingness, while Ji points to a concentrated point that keeps getting stuck. The two are two sides of one matter, not a simple good-then-bad sequence.

Check the host star first

The meaning depends on the palace's main star and the stars around it. With a steady host star, the blockage may be a process problem that can be reorganized. With difficult stars added, the obstacle may be structural. The stars alone, without the palace context, cannot show how serious it is.

Judgment steps

  1. Confirm which stem layer produces the Lu and the Ji, birth-year or palace-stem.
  2. Read the host star and surrounding stars to judge whether the foundation is stable.
  3. List the Lu resources and the Ji blockage in two separate columns.
  4. Locate the exact step where the blockage keeps appearing.
  5. Decide whether the blockage can be resolved before committing resources.

Reading order

  1. Fix the stem layer and confirm whether Lu and Ji come from birth-year or palace-stem.
  2. Check the host star, difficult stars, and supporting palaces.
  3. List resources and blockages separately and locate the stuck point.
  4. Verify against real conditions whether the blockage can be resolved.
  5. Write the answer as an opportunity plus its precondition, not one fortune label.
