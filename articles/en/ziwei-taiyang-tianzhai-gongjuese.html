Tai Yang in the Property Palace: Light, Family Duty, and Visibility

A focused Zi Wei Dou Shu reading of Tai Yang in the Property Palace, with separate checks for home atmosphere, elder duty, and public use of space.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:15

Do Not Stop at Brightness

Tai Yang in the Property Palace can describe visibility, responsibility, and the use of the home, not simply a bright house.

Separate the Lines

Check home atmosphere, elder duty, and whether the space also serves work or hosting. Different supporting palaces can emphasize different lines.

Hua Quan and Hua Ke

Hua Quan may concentrate management and decisions in the family. Hua Ke can support a visible, educational, or professional home setting.

Use the Full Chart

Check dignity, transformations, meeting stars, and actual housing history before turning a symbol into a claim about a building.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
