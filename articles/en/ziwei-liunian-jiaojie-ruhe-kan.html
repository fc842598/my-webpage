How to Read the Transition Between Years

The year transition reads how old and new year transformations and palaces switch: the old theme concludes, the new begins; around the boundary matters pile and minds unsettle; see where the energy turns for an orderly transition.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

The year transition is like a shift change; old duties close and new ones begin; the busiest when both overlap.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see only the new year without closing the old, and unfinished matters pile; the transition needs both.

The Underlying Logic

Read the old year transformation theme concluding.

Read the new year transformation theme starting.

Around the boundary matters pile and mind unsettles.

Separate old and new matters, not mixing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the old year theme
  2. Inventory unfinished old matters
  3. Read new year transformation landings
  4. Set the new year theme direction
  5. Steady rhythm around the boundary
  6. Close the old, then take the new

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing only the new year, not closing the old

Mistake 2: Mixing old and new, increasingly chaotic

Mistake 3: Panicking at the boundary, not seeing energy turn

Back to Your Own Chart

Year transition asks the old theme to close and new to start. Mind both and separate matters, and the transition is smooth and mind settled.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the transition between years before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the transition between years line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
