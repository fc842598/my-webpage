How to Read Hong Luan and Tian Xi in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Pinpointing Marriage Timing

Hong Luan and Tian Xi are set by the birth-year branch and always face each other, governing marriage and joy. Timing needs three layers: the natal Spouse Palace for the base, the decade for the stage, and a yearly Hong Luan/Tian Xi entering a key palace as the trigger.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

Saying marriage is certain from a yearly Hong Luan alone fails most often; they are joy stars, not guarantees, and answer only on the right decade and a stable Spouse Palace.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People see Hong Luan move every year yet never marry, chasing the yearly joy star without confirming they are in a marriage-suited decade.

The Underlying Logic

Hong Luan drives active joy and relationship launch; Tian Xi adds celebration and matchmaking; they face each other, so one moves as the other shines.

First read the natal Spouse major star and transformations for the innate relationship pattern and ideal partner type.

Then read the decade Spouse Palace and transformations to see whether these ten years carry conditions to commit or marry.

Only then use the yearly Hong Luan/Tian Xi entering Life, Spouse, Happiness or Career as the concrete timing, narrowing with months if needed.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Lay out the natal Spouse Palace for the relationship base and partner profile
  2. Read the decade Spouse and transformations for marriage conditions in these ten years
  3. Find which palace the yearly Hong Luan/Tian Xi enters to lock the trigger year
  4. Entering Life/Spouse/Happiness is most direct; Career can also settle a relationship through work
  5. Layer the yearly Spouse transformations to confirm commitment versus mere excitement
  6. Use months within the trigger year to narrow the season for engagement or marriage

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Declaring certain marriage from the yearly Hong Luan alone

Mistake 2: Skipping the decade conditions so the joy star comes to nothing

Mistake 3: Taking Tian Xi matchmaking celebration for relationship stability itself

Back to Your Own Chart

Hong Luan and Tian Xi announce joy but do not decide for you. Natal base, decade stage and yearly trigger aligned—that is when marriage timing reads reliably.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to timing marriage with Hong Luan and Tian Xi before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the timing marriage with Hong Luan and Tian Xi line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
