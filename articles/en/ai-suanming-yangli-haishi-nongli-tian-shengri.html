Once the birthday layer is off, later timing and structure can still look detailed while resting on the wrong chart.

Calibrate the input first

Your family memory and the system input are not always the same thing.

The right first move is conversion, not rushing ahead to interpretation.

For questions like "For AI Fortune Telling, Should You Enter the Solar Birthday or the Lunar Birthday," the real danger is not imperfect wording. It is making detailed judgments on top of uncorrected base input.

Where a small difference actually changes things

A family lunar memory entered into a solar-date field can shift the whole chart.

Trying to verify a chart with a different calendar story than the one used for input creates confusion later.

The steadier order is still to calibrate the time window, calendar, or condition first, then ask about timing or choice.

Do not rush past the setup

Do not start asking while the birthday layer is still a guess.

At the same time, a mixed-up start can still be fixed if you stop and align the calendar first.

Pausing to calibrate is usually not wasted effort here. It is what prevents a whole round of precise-looking error later.

A steadier asking order

  1. Check whether the form expects solar or lunar input.
  2. Convert your remembered date into that system.
  3. Only then move into interpretation.
