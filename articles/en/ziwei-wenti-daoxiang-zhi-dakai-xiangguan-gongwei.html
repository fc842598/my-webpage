Problem-Driven Reading in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Open Only the Palaces Your Question Needs

The twelve palaces are a toolbox opened by question: wealth opens the wealth line, career the career line. Open only the shared palaces your question needs rather than stacking every opposite relationship.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

Beginners spread out all twelve palaces and every opposite at once, drowning in noise. First clarify what you want to solve, then open palaces as needed.

What This Question Is Really Asking

It is like seeing a doctor: a headache does not trigger a scan of every organ. Once the question is set, the relevant palaces surface; forcing in unrelated ones dilutes the answer.

The Underlying Logic

The twelve palaces are not all read every time; shared systems are called by the type of question.

Wealth opens Wealth-Karma-Property, career opens Career-Spouse-Wealth, and relationships open Spouse-Karma-Travel.

The home palace, its opposite and trines form one topic unit, and different questions call different units.

Read one line fully before opening the next so multiple lines do not fight each other.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Classify the question: wealth, career, relationship, health or family
  2. Set the core palace the question needs
  3. Open the related palaces along its opposite and trine line
  4. List the major stars, assistants, malefics and transformations on that line
  5. Read only this line for its net effect without stacking unrelated palaces
  6. Once one line is clear, open another line only if another question remains

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Spreading out all twelve palaces regardless of the question

Mistake 2: Forcing unrelated opposite relationships into the main line

Mistake 3: Opening several lines before reading one through, producing contradictions

Back to Your Own Chart

Problem-driven reading makes the chart serve your question instead of burying you. One line at a time means reading less but reading accurately.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to problem-driven palace selection before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the problem-driven palace selection line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
