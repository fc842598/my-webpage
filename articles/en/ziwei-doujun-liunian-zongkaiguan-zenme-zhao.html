Dou-Jun in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Finding the Master Switch of the Yearly Chart

Dou-Jun is the first-month origin and overall commander of the yearly chart, derived from birth month and the year branch; yearly palaces and months unfold from it. Finding Dou-Jun gives the master key to the year's rhythm and to arranging months.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-05

If the yearly Life Palace is the year's protagonist, Dou-Jun is its first-month gate and dispatcher; how months run and where they begin are set by it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Starting months at the yearly Life Palace and skipping Dou-Jun shifts every month, so timing is off; this step cannot be skipped.

The Underlying Logic

Dou-Jun splits into yearly (for the year) and monthly commanders, the origin from which the moving chart unfolds.

Yearly Dou-Jun: count the birth month backward from the year-branch palace, then count the birth hour forward from zi; the landing palace is Dou-Jun.

The stars and transformations in the Dou-Jun palace show the year's overall state and the first-month starting quality.

Twelve months proceed from Dou-Jun as month one, so an error there cascades through every month of the year.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Lay out the yearly chart and fix the year-branch palace
  2. Count the birth month backward from that palace to the intermediate landing
  3. From there count the birth hour forward from zi to fix the Dou-Jun palace
  4. Read its major star and transformations for the year's overall tone
  5. Start month one at Dou-Jun and arrange the twelve months in order
  6. Arrange days below months, descending level by level without skipping

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Skipping Dou-Jun and starting months at the yearly Life Palace

Mistake 2: Counting month/hour in the wrong direction, misplacing Dou-Jun

Mistake 3: Confusing Dou-Jun with the yearly Life Palace—dispatcher versus protagonist

Back to Your Own Chart

Dou-Jun is the first button of the yearly chart. Buttoned right, every month and day falls into place; learning to find it takes your reading from roughly-which-year down to exactly-which-month.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the yearly Dou-Jun before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the yearly Dou-Jun line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
