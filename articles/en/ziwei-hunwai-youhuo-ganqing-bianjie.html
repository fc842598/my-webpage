Outside Temptation in Marriage in Zi Wei Dou Shu: High-Risk Years and Boundaries

Whether outside temptation appears in marriage reads Spouse stability, whether Karma is fulfilled in the current relationship, the external social scenes of Travel, and when the year activates romance stars Tan Lang/Lian Zhen and Tian Yao/Xian Chi. A year activating Spouse/Travel with romance stars is a high window for outside temptation, but the chart gives tendency, not fate; what actually works is the boundary you set in advance and the relationship's own fulfillment.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-15

Outside temptation rarely appears from nowhere; it surfaces when the relationship has a gap and the year happens to activate romance stars. Reading this timing lets you firm up the fence in advance.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why you still fancy others after marriage, which years need caution, or how to hold boundaries, first see the structure and timing of temptation, then return to fill the relationship gap.

The Underlying Logic

Relationship stability reads Spouse: steady Spouse with Lu/Ke and stable stars gives the bond cohesion, leaving temptation little crack; an already stressed Spouse pulled by its opposite动摇 more easily when the year activates. Temptation is the result; relationship tightness is the soil.

Inner fulfillment reads Karma: when Karma is nourished and not empty in marriage, outside emotional value can hardly slip in; long-empty, Ji, misunderstood Karma most easily mistakes being understood by another for love. Much so-called crush fills a part missing inside the marriage.

External scenes read Travel and romance stars: Travel is the channel to outside people; Tan Lang governs desire, Lian Zhen spiritual attraction, Tian Yao/Xian Chi/Mu Yu ambiguity and sensory pull. In years when annual Lu activates Travel and meets these romance stars, social settings, collaborations and reunions can become temptation entrances—proactively keep boundary and transparency.

Holding boundaries reads transformation choices: Lu into Spouse draws energy back into the relationship and invests in the partner; Ji tangling Spouse/Travel signals pull and crossing. The chart does not choose for you; it only marks the windy years. In those, reduce one-on-one scenes, be honest with your partner and put unmet marital needs on the table—that is the real resolution.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Spouse stability to judge whether the relationship leaves temptation a crack
  2. Read Karma to tell whether an outside crush is true love or compensation for a marital lack
  3. Read Travel and romance stars to identify high-temptation years
  4. In high-risk years proactively hold boundaries: reduce ambiguous one-on-ones and stay transparent
  5. Talk honestly about unmet marital needs to fix the soil
  6. The chart only flags tendency; keeping the relationship ultimately rests on clear choice, not fate

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating every post-marital crush as true love while missing that it often compensates a marital gap

Mistake 2: Ignoring annual windows and setting no boundary until entangled and finding exit hard

Mistake 3: Treating chart romance signals as fate and giving up active effort and choice
