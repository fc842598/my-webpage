How to Read Health Stages and Critical Windows: Tendencies and Timing, With Early Checkups and Formal Care

Reading health in the chart means seeing constitutional tendencies, the systems to mind and which stages look tighter, reminding you to do early checkups and keep regular habits rather than diagnosing or frightening yourself. Any discomfort relies on formal hospital exams and diagnosis.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Reading health is like a reminder sheet for habits and checkups; the chart marks where to pay attention, while the real answer lies in the hospital and daily habits, not a metaphysical line.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see malefics in the Health Palace and assume serious illness and worry all day; the issue is taking a tendency reminder as a diagnosis and possibly delaying formal checks.

The Underlying Logic

First read the natal Health Palace triple-direction to understand constitutional tendencies and systems to mind (tendency, not diagnosis).

Then read decade and annual transformations and malefics to see which stages look tighter and call for early checkups.

Seek formal medical care for discomfort and follow the doctor rather than delaying through the chart.

Regular rest, diet and exercise are the long-term foundation, and the chart only reminds of timing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the Health Palace to clarify constitutional tendencies and systems to mind
  2. Then read the decade and year to mark tighter health stages
  3. Schedule formal checkups early in those stages
  4. Seek medical care promptly for signals rather than enduring or delaying
  5. Solidify the things you control: rest, diet and exercise
  6. Do not take metaphysical tendencies as diagnosis and stay calm

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking tendencies as diagnosis and worrying or self-matching

Mistake 2: Delaying formal care because of the chart and missing check timing

Mistake 3: Reading only annual malefics without the natal Health Palace and habits

Back to Your Own Chart

Health windows teach trusting the reminder and trusting medicine more. Do checkups and habits early and seek formal care for problems, and chart tendencies only help you care for yourself sooner.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to health stages and critical windows before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the health stages and critical windows line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
