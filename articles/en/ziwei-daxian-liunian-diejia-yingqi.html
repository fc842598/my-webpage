How to Read the Decade-Year Overlap in Zi Wei Dou Shu: A Ten-Year Theme Meets a One-Year Trigger

To judge when something happens, the key is the decade-year overlap: the decade limit governs the ten-year theme and focus, while the year governs the concrete trigger. When the same line, such as spouse, property or career, is activated in both the limit and the year, the timing is strongest and the matter most easily lands in that year.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28

The decade is what the ten years are playing, the year which scene comes up; when the two align, the matter has a clear timing.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some judge a year from the annual layer alone, others a decade from the limit alone, and both time poorly, because the key is whether they overlap on the same line.

The Underlying Logic

The decade limit is a ten-year unit that sets the stage's life theme and focus; the year is a one-year trigger that sets the concrete rhythm and landing.

Strongest timing occurs when the same palace is activated in both the limit and the year, for example both spouse palaces moving, when marriage easily forms or changes.

When the property line shows Hua Lu in both limit and year, the timing for buying or moving is strong; when the career line is activated in both, promotion or change concentrates.

If only one layer is activated and the other is quiet, the matter has signs but lands with difficulty, and you should look to the monthly layer for the concrete trigger.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read the current decade limit first and set the ten-year theme and focus
  2. Then read this year's annual layer to find the trigger and palace
  3. Compare the same palaces in the limit and year to see which lines move on both
  4. Treat lines moving on both as the strongest timing focus this year
  5. For lines moving only on one layer, look to the monthly layer for the concrete trigger
  6. Combine the transformations to judge forming, changing or guarding

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging the year from the annual layer alone and ignoring the decade theme

Mistake 2: Judging the decade from the limit alone without the year trigger

Mistake 3: Not comparing the same palace on two layers and timing poorly

Back to Your Own Chart

Timing is not read from one layer but from the decade-year overlap. Set the ten-year theme, find the one-year trigger, and the lines moving on both are this year's focus to seize or guard, then refine with the monthly layer, and the rhythm becomes clear.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the decade-year overlap and timing before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the decade-year overlap and timing line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
