Po Jun in the Health Palace: Recurring ills, Teeth and Bones

Po Jun belongs to water and governs depletion and change; in the Health Palace it often points to teeth, bones and recurring ills, especially during big life changes. Keep routines steady in transitions, recheck old ills diligently, and follow professional advice when formal treatment including surgery is needed.
