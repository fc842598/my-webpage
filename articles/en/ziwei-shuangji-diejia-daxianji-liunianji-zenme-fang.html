How to Handle Double Ji Stacking in Zi Wei Dou Shu When Decade Ji and Annual Ji Hit the Same Place

When decade Ji and annual Ji land in the same palace or clash across, that field faces concentrated pressure that year—a year to defend proactively by spotting it early, lowering exposure and holding the line rather than charging.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

Double Ji on one spot is not fate but a warning that this area is most fragile this year; shrinking exposure early is exactly how you pass it steadily.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Most people look for causes only after double Ji hits, yet the decade Ji is a known ten-year lesson and the annual Ji can be cast ahead of time, leaving room to prepare.

The Underlying Logic

Ji is fixation and blockage; decade Ji is the field ground across ten years, annual Ji presses it again this year.

Same palace or clash means a long-term lesson is concentrated this year—the line requiring most caution.

Gauge strength by whether natal Ji is also triggered; three-layer convergence calls for defending, not attacking, that field.

A double-Ji year is not hopeless: Hua Ke smooths, Lu Cun adds resources, and lowering expectations and exposure is the most concrete resolution.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find where decade Ji lands to know the ten-year lesson area
  2. Cast the year's annual Ji and see whether it shares or clashes with decade Ji
  3. If they stack, lower that field's investment, commitments and leverage to a bearable range early
  4. Find Hua Ke, Lu Cun or auspicious stars to build a buffer and recovery plan
  5. Set that field's yearly goal as hold, close loops and avoid worsening—not expansion
  6. Review triggers through the year and treat it as concentrated cleanup of an old lesson

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Expanding heavily against a double-Ji year, turning defense into a charge

Mistake 2: Panicking at Ji while ignoring the buffer of Hua Ke and Lu Cun

Mistake 3: Noticing the stack only afterward and missing the early defense window

Back to Your Own Chart

  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
