How to Read the Spouse Palace Differently for Singles and Couples

The same Zi Wei Dou Shu Spouse Palace pattern can answer different questions for a single person and for someone already in a relationship.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:39

Start With the User's Situation

A single person may ask about meeting and screening. A couple may need help with communication, division of labor, or commitment.

Same Star, Different Question

Tan Lang Hua Lu can describe social opportunity for a single person and boundary work for a couple. Ju Men Hua Ji can show guessing before a relationship and repetitive arguments inside one.

Add the Supporting Palaces

Outside shows meeting context, Fu De emotional processing, Career time pressure, and Property shared living.

Practical Reading

Ask the right question first, then translate the palace into behavior that can be observed and discussed.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
