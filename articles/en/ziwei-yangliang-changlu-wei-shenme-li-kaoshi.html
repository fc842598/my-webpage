Why the Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu Pattern Favors Exams in Zi Wei Dou Shu

When Tai Yang, Tian Liang, Wen Chang and Lu Cun meet across the triple-direction, the pattern favors scholarly distinction and exams—academia and public examinations—provided all four meet without heavy malefics breaking it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

The strength of Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu is not wealth but an unusually smooth path in study, exams, professional credentials and scholarly reputation.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Expecting big money from Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu points the wrong way; its real talent is converting knowledge and credentials into a stable social position.

The Underlying Logic

Tai Yang is bright, noble and outward; Tian Liang is principled and protective; Wen Chang governs writing and exams; Lu Cun supplies steady resources—together they form advancement through scholarship.

The pattern needs all four to meet across the Life triple-direction; a missing star or heavy malefic clash discounts it.

It best favors exams, degrees, titles, public office and research—fields advanced through expertise and reputation.

Chang-Lu supplies learning and accumulation; Yang-Liang supplies reputation and principle—the combination wins scholarly standing through long-term expertise, not a windfall.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Tai Yang, Tian Liang, Wen Chang and Lu Cun meet across the Life triple-direction
  2. Check for heavy breakers such as Yang, Tuo or Hua Ji
  3. Decide whether the structure sits in the Life Palace or a related palace—personal trait versus field advantage
  4. Combine Wenchang/Wenqu and Hua Ke to gauge exam and document strength
  5. Use decades and years to find the best windows for degrees, certificates and titles
  6. Advise a professional-credential and scholarly route rather than chasing short-term money

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu as a wealth pattern and expecting quick riches

Mistake 2: Claiming the pattern when the four stars do not all meet or malefics break it

Mistake 3: Reading the pattern without the decade and missing the best exam years

Back to Your Own Chart

Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu is the scholar's pattern. Aiming at its real track—exams and professional accumulation—makes the path of respected expertise steadily wider.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Yang-Liang-Chang-Lu pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
