The Hero Star in the Temple Pattern: Po Jun at Zi

When Po Jun guards life in the Zi palace at temple, the transformation star is well-placed, bringing pioneering, breaking the old to establish the new and opening new situations. The pattern favors expansion; guard isolation, drifting and thin family bonds, needing auspicious tempering.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

This pattern gives Po Jun pioneering force a stage to break old structures; but the transformation star inherently carries isolation, needing auspicious stars for relationships.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Po Jun at temple and assume great pioneering success without isolation and drifting; the pattern also needs auspicious stars and real conditions.

The Underlying Logic

Po Jun governs transformation, pioneering and rebuilding after depletion, at temple in Zi.

It favors expansion, entrepreneurship, new situations and technical innovation.

With Lu Quan Ke and assistants, pioneering has resources and support.

Without auspicious stars, guard isolation, drifting and thin family bonds.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Po Jun guards life in Zi at temple
  2. See whether pioneering force is well-placed
  3. Count trine Lu Quan Ke and assistants for resource support
  4. Count malefics for isolation and drifting
  5. Read family palaces for bond strength
  6. Judge pioneering pattern or isolation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing Po Jun at temple and assuming success without isolation

Mistake 2: Ignoring drifting and thin family bonds

Mistake 3: Seeing only pioneering without resource support

Back to Your Own Chart

This pattern asks whether the transformation star well-placed can steady pioneering. With auspicious stars for relationships and guarding isolation, breaking old to establish new becomes a great cause.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Hero Star in the Temple pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Hero Star in the Temple pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
