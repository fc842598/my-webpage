Hua Quan in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Behind Power Lies Responsibility

Hua Quan makes its palace's matters heavier and more active, granting control while loading responsibility; wherever it lands, you must take charge there.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-04

Quan is not a guarantee of promotion or wealth; it means you call the shots and carry the load—check whether you can carry it before claiming the power.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People welcome Quan as coming power, only to find responsibility, pressure and conflict landing on them—reading power without the load-bearing side.

The Underlying Logic

Quan transforms into mastery, drive and weight, making a star more dominant and execution-oriented.

In Career you must lead at work; in Wealth you actively control money; in relationship palaces it can become dominance.

Quan resolves some of Ji's delay and makes Lu's resources land—it is the execution link among transformations.

Power must match position and ability; lone Quan without support means you carry responsibility without resources.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Locate Quan to confirm which field you must lead
  2. Check its triple-direction for Lu, Ke or assistants to gauge whether you can carry the load
  3. See which major star joins Quan to judge a steady or aggressive style
  4. If Quan clashes with Ji, resolve conflict before expanding
  5. Distinguish being empowered from being burdened, using the decade to check name versus reality
  6. Turn control into concrete division of labor and boundaries instead of carrying everything

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Quan as unconditionally good while ignoring responsibility and pressure

Mistake 2: Seeing power without resources or assistants, leading to lone burden

Mistake 3: Misreading dominance in a relationship palace as a partner's high status

Back to Your Own Chart
