A Po Jun Year: Breaking the Old to Build the New, Turning Change Into Opportunity

When Po Jun hosts the annual Life Palace, the year leans to change, breaking the old, restructuring and starting anew, often bringing job changes, moving, structural adjustment and ending old ties, and also favors thorough transformation. Yet the annual cycle sets only one year’s rhythm; the key is building after breaking, rather than only tearing down and draining resources in the change.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-02

A Po Jun year often feels like a year that cannot and does not want to stay steady: old things want replacing and new ground wants opening. The change is real, and whether the new gets built is the real test.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to use a Po Jun year and guard against dissipation. Understand the annual cycle as the year’s tone, then see whether Po Jun’s old-breaking energy is aided in building the new by Lu and auspicious stars.

The Underlying Logic

A Po Jun year brings big change, suiting breaking the old to build the new, transitions, moving, changing tracks and clearing old baggage, and often brings large spending.

Po Jun also governs dissipation: only breaking without building and changing frequently easily drains resources, networks and health together.

With Lu Cun, Hua Lu and auspicious stars, breaking the old that year leads to a new setup and new income sources; with malefics and Ji, impulsive change, financial loss and setbacks from upheaval follow.

A Po Jun year is annual practice in breaking first and building after: know what you want to build before tearing down, keep a budget and transition period for change, and do not treat one year of upheaval as a long-term lifestyle.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Po Jun hosts that year’s annual Life Palace
  2. List the old things to break and the new directions to build
  3. Know what to build before tearing down and changing
  4. Keep a budget, transition period and backup plan for change
  5. Avoid changing too many things at once; focus on breaking one and building one
  6. Review at year end to see whether the year’s change actually built a new setup

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking a Po Jun year as a year that simply must change while ignoring building the new

Mistake 2: Only breaking without building and upheaval frequently until resources are drained

Mistake 3: Changing too many things at once until the situation spins out of control

Back to Your Own Chart

A Po Jun year offers a year of breaking the old, changing and transforming. Knowing what to build first and leaving room for change turns the year’s breaking into real new opportunity rather than empty upheaval.
