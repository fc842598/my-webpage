Tian Chu in the Wealth and Career Palaces in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Provision and Salary

Tian Chu governs provision, salary and food-based support. In Wealth it means steady sustaining income tied to food, provisions or support; in Career it means a stable salary and benefits with ties to food industries or logistics. It leans to steady provision rather than speculative riches, and finances still defer to licensed institutions and official product terms.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some with Tian Chu have modest but stable income and real benefits; it is the provision star of steady support, not risky riches.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to read it: Wealth shows steady sustaining income and food-related earnings, while Career shows salary, benefits and ties to food or logistics, both centered on stability rather than high risk.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Chu turns toward nourishing, governing provision, salary, food and being supported.

In Wealth income is steady and fixed, tied to dining, food, provisions or everyday needs.

In Career the salary is reliable and benefits real, suiting food, logistics or institutional roles.

It does not indicate speculative riches; finances defer to licensed institutions and official terms, and this is not investment advice.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate Tian Chu in Wealth or Career
  2. In Wealth, read steady sustaining income and food-related earnings
  3. In Career, read salary, benefits and food-logistics ties
  4. Note the steady provision and avoid high-risk bets
  5. Use licensed institutions and official products for finances
  6. Use the decade to see when the salary and livelihood are steadiest

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking it as a riches star for speculation

Mistake 2: Missing the stability and misreading the income nature

Mistake 3: Ignoring licensed institutions and product terms in finances
