The Underlying Logic

The nourishing rear reads Property, which governs the home food environment; whether you can cook and eat regularly at home decides whether nourishment lands. Steady Property builds healthy rhythm easily; stressed Property with constant eating out struggles despite knowledge, and can start from fixed healthy takeout choices and meal prep.

Regulation rhythm reads decade/year and seasons: phases activating Health make the corresponding system more vulnerable, calling for proactive diet adjustment; traditional nourishment raises yang in spring-summer and yin in autumn-winter, essentially following season and body rhythm rather than one year-round menu. Eating is long-term; gentle sustainability beats short extreme diets.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  2. Read Life energy pattern: brain types stabilize sugar, high-burn types fix rhythm, steady types control amount
  3. Read Property to improve the home food environment so nourishment actually lands
  4. Recognize stress eating and give emotion a non-food outlet
  5. Adjust proactively when decade/year activate Health and eat with seasonal rhythm
  6. Reject extreme diets for gentle sustainable eating, consulting a physician/nutritionist when needed

Three Common Mistakes
