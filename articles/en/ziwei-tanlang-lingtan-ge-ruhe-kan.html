The Ling Xing and Tan Lang Pattern: Quiet Hidden Gain

When Tan Lang and Ling Xing share a palace, Ling Xing is yin fire governing patient planning, bringing quiet gain and low-key accumulation into wealth others do not notice. The pattern fears malefic breaks, and hidden gain must also guard being unkeepable.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

This pattern is a dark fire simmering; Tan Lang desire is ground by Ling Xing into patient planning, quietly accumulating; unlike Huo Tan open gain, Ling Tan is hidden.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some confuse Ling Tan with Huo Tan, but one is open and one hidden, one urgent one slow; conditions are similar, but Ling Tan emphasizes long planning.

The Underlying Logic

Ling Xing yin fire governs patience and planning, Tan Lang opportunity and desire.

The two together bring quiet low-key gain.

It likes Lu Cun, Hua Lu and assistants for rooted supported gain.

It avoids malefics conjunct breaking it, guard being unkeepable.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Tan Lang and Ling Xing share a palace
  2. Distinguish Ling Tan (hidden) from Huo Tan (open)
  3. Check malefic breaks
  4. Read Lu Cun and Hua Lu for rooted hidden gain
  5. Read the palace for the accumulation area
  6. After hidden gain, likewise arrange preservation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Confusing Ling Tan and Huo Tan

Mistake 2: Seeing Ling Tan and assuming certain wealth without malefic checks

Mistake 3: Not arranging preservation after hidden gain
