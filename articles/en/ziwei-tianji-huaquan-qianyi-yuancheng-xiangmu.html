Tian Ji Hua Quan in the Travel Palace for Remote Projects

Cross-team coordination is different from authority to approve decisions.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-07 09:08

A remote project can make someone visible to many teams without giving them final authority. Tian Ji Hua Quan in the Travel Palace may frame adaptation and coordination, while the Career Palace describes the actual work role. Check which time layer supplies the transformation.

Two remote roles

A design lead proposes changes but sales signs the contract. An engineering lead has written release authority. Both face outside teams, yet their decisions carry different consequences. Review collaborators, budgets and approval records.

Reading order

Locate Tian Ji and Hua Quan, compare Travel with Life and Career, then add Friends and Wealth. Ask for a written decision map before treating increased meetings as a promotion.
