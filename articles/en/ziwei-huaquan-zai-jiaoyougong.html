Hua Quan in the Friends Palace: Leading Among Friends and Teams While Carrying the Group’s Responsibility

Hua Quan in the Friends Palace makes one the decision-maker among friends, teams and subordinates, with strong organizing ability, but it is also easy to carry the group’s affairs personally; guard against unclear authority and the urge to win.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-30

Some naturally become the lead as soon as they join a group, arranging activities and setting direction; this is the tone of Hua Quan in the Friends Palace: the urge to lead among peers and subordinates, backed by real organizing ability.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is what Hua Quan in the Friends Palace means. See both layers—leading within the group and taking responsibility for it; it gives organizing power and also a lesson in authority boundaries.

The Underlying Logic

The Friends Palace governs friends, peers, teams and subordinates; with Hua Quan here, one likes to lead within the group, can command respect and is good at coordinating.

The positive side is strong organizing ability: leading teams, setting divisions of work and driving cooperation, often serving as the de facto organizer in projects and communities.

The other side is responsibility settling on the shoulders: it is easy to treat group matters as one’s own, carrying and worrying for others when authority is unclear, and competing to lead out of pride.

Read the triple-direction: with Lu and assistant stars, the team becomes your support; with malefics and Ji, you meet forceful members, resistant subordinates or drains from organizing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Hua Quan is in the Friends Palace and see which star carries it
  2. Separate leading and organizing from taking responsibility for others
  3. Check the triple-direction for Lu and assistant stars to judge whether the team becomes support
  4. Count malefics and Ji to weigh forceful members, resistant subordinates and contests for leadership
  5. In the team, write out the division of work and authority rather than carrying it on loyalty
  6. Use the urge to lead on building mechanisms and developing key people rather than deciding everything personally

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking leading the team as everyone should obey me

Mistake 2: Carrying group matters out of loyalty and growing tired when authority is unclear

Mistake 3: Seeing only organizing power and ignoring member friction from malefics and Ji

Back to Your Own Chart
