How to Read the Clear Water Reflecting Laurel Pattern

When Tian Tong and Tai Yin guard the Life Palace in the Zi palace, it is like clear water reflecting the moon, bringing gentleness with conviction and a blend of blessing and wealth. It is steadier for those born at night; guard drifting and laziness, and auspicious transformations are still needed.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

This pattern is moonlight on still water: gentle outside, resolute inside; blessing and wealth come together, but water too still can become inertia.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some assume Tian Tong and Tai Yin guarantee a life of ease, while others read gentleness as lack of conviction; the real test is whether there is resolve and whether drifting is guarded.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Tong governs blessing and gentleness, Tai Yin governs wealth and inwardness; in Zi they are the moon rising over the sea at brightness.

The pattern brings gentleness with conviction and a blend of blessing and wealth, suiting service, education, the arts and patient work.

Those born at night are more in season and steadier; day births dim the moon and discount the blessing and wealth.

With malefics or Hua Ji, guard drifting, laziness, moodiness and spending through emotion.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm the Life Palace is in Zi with Tian Tong and Tai Yin, the basis of the pattern
  2. Check brightness to confirm the moon over the sea is well-placed
  3. Read the triple-direction for auspicious support so blessing and wealth materialize
  4. Count malefics and Hua Ji to guard drifting, laziness and moodiness
  5. Factor birth time (night births are steadier) to gauge the strength
  6. Read decade transformation activation to see how blessing and wealth express now

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming Tian Tong and Tai Yin guarantee an easy life and waiting

Mistake 2: Reading gentleness as lack of conviction and underestimating inner resolve

Mistake 3: Ignoring birth time and malefics or Ji, treating all cases the same

Back to Your Own Chart

This pattern asks how resolve within gentleness becomes blessing and wealth. Pair the moonlight softness with determination and action, so still water does not become stagnant.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the Clear Water Reflecting Laurel pattern before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the Clear Water Reflecting Laurel pattern line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
