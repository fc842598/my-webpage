How Neighboring Palaces Affect the Home Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Flanking and Hidden Harmony

Besides its triple-direction, a palace is shaped by its two neighbors: when both flank it they form a flanking position, double auspicious helping and double harsh pressing, while the palace in six-harmony forms a hidden echo. Adding the flank and hidden harmony completes the reading of a palace strength.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Beginners reading a palace often see only that cell, but the palaces beside it and the one hidden-harmonized with it quietly add or subtract force.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is which relationships beyond the home palace act on it: the two neighbors flanking it, the six-harmony hidden match, and the stars and transformations those neighbors carry.

The Underlying Logic

Flanking: when both neighbors hold auspicious stars, such as double Lu, Kui Yue or Zuo You, they help; when both carry malefics, they add pressure.

Hidden harmony: use the six branch harmonies to find the palace secretly matched with the home, governing private, indirect ties and pulls.

The stars and transformations of neighbors spill into the home, so open them together when judging.

Flanking and hidden harmony are supporting relationships that strengthen or weaken but do not change the home main theme.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First lock the home palace to read
  2. Find the two neighbors and see whether auspicious or harsh stars flank it
  3. Then use the six branch harmonies to find the hidden-harmony palace
  4. List the stars and transformations of the neighbors and hidden match
  5. Judge whether the flank and hidden harmony strengthen or weaken the home
  6. Finally keep the home theme central, with neighbor relations only adding or subtracting

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the home and ignoring flank and hidden harmony

Mistake 2: Judging a double-harsh flank as bad without seeing whether the home can bear it

Mistake 3: Treating hidden harmony as sharing and exaggerating the private pull

Back to Your Own Chart

A home palace is not an isolated cell: neighbors flank and the six-harmony hides a match, adding and subtracting force. Read these outer relations, then return to the home theme for a complete sense of strength.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to reading neighboring palaces, flanking positions and hidden harmonies before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the reading neighboring palaces, flanking positions and hidden harmonies line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
