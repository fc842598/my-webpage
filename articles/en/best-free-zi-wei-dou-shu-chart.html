How to Choose a Free Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart Website

A useful free chart website should make the chart easy to inspect before it asks you to trust an interpretation or pay for more detail.

Start with practical criteria: chart completeness, readable labels, transparent data handling, clear pricing boundaries, and an explanation of how to move from one palace to the wider chart.

Check the Chart Itself

The result should show all twelve palaces, the main stars, the Four Transformations, and the current timing layers. Life, Wealth, Career, and Travel should be easy to locate, and the opposite palace and triad should remain visible rather than being hidden behind a single-score result.

Check Privacy and Pricing

A birth chart uses birth date, time, and often location. Look for a plain-language privacy notice, a way to delete or avoid saving data, and a clear distinction between free output and paid interpretation. A site that hides these terms is difficult to evaluate, even if its design looks polished.

Avoid Fear-Based Interpretation

Good explanations use conditions: a pressured Wealth Palace may call for tighter cash-flow management, while a strong Career Palace may emphasize responsibility. Claims that promise certainty, predict disaster, or present payment as the only remedy are warning signs.

Combination one: if the Life Palace is empty, a reliable free chart should clearly show the main stars borrowed from the opposite palace and preserve the connected Career, Wealth, and Travel positions. A site that hides those relationships but produces a confident personality label gives you no way to check whether the interpretation follows the chart.

Combination two: if an annual cycle places Hua Ji in the Wealth Palace while the natal Career Palace remains stable, the reading should discuss collection delays, expenses, or tighter cash flow rather than predict bankruptcy. The decade palace and supporting Hua Lu conditions show whether the pressure is temporary and whether income still has a usable source.

Chart-Reading Order

After generating the chart, the site should help you read the Life Palace first, then the palace related to your question, its opposite palace and triad, and finally the ten-year and annual cycles. This sequence gives you more value than a list of disconnected predictions.
