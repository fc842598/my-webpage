Authority and Lu in the Wealth Palace: Income That Wants You to Hold the Wheel

A plain-English guide to Authority and Lu in the Wealth Palace: Income That Wants You to Hold the Wheel, with a practical reading order, simple examples, and clear boundaries for Zi Wei Dou Shu learners.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-24 18:32

Money and career topics become clearer once you separate ownership, authority, cash flow, and platform from one another.

What This Means

These charts often show who controls resources, who carries responsibility, and where income enters before they show personal wealth in a simple way.

How To Read It

Compare the Life, Wealth, Career, and Travel palaces so you can see whether the result comes from title, business route, outside markets, or institutional responsibility.

Simple Examples

  • When the wealth palace is involved, check cash flow, income source, and whether money can be retained.
  • Read the star through the palace and the real-life role it points to, rather than using a vague fixed prediction.
  • Read the palace first, then decide whether the pattern is about money, role, relationships, health, or the outside world.

Practical Order

First define the question. Then read the palace, its opposite palace, the supporting palaces, and the ten-year or annual trigger. This keeps the reading useful for career, money, relationships, and real choices.
