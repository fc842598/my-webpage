What Is "One-Six Shared Root" in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Why Life and Health Palaces Are Read as One

Count Life as one and Health six steps counterclockwise: One-Six share a root and rise/fall together. Likewise Two-Seven, Three-Eight, Four-Nine, Five-Ten. These numeric palace pairs reveal the surface, cause and hidden line of any matter.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

The twelve palaces are not flat cells; palaces a fixed count apart are two sides of one thing. One-Six shared root is the key to reading the visible self and its hidden carrier.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Reading only the Life Palace cannot explain why a strong surface collapses first in body or mind. Ask which palace sits six from Life, how the two mirror each other, and how pairs shift when the focal point changes.

The Underlying Logic

Set Life as one and count counterclockwise: Siblings two, Spouse three, Children four, Wealth five, Health six. Life and Health are one body—temperament and physical carrier mirror each other.

The rest follow: Siblings-Travel (2-7), Spouse-Friends (3-8), Children-Career (4-9), Wealth-Property (5-10); each pair is two ends of one matter.

Changing the focal point (counting from any palace as one) yields twelve shared-root pairs to trace a hidden line—e.g. reading Career also reads its six-step (Children) for results and subordinate follow-through.

In practice the home palace is the visible expression and its shared-root the hidden cost/support: strong Life with weak Health means forcing it; good Wealth with weak Property means earning without retaining—assess both.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Set Life as one and count six counterclockwise on your chart to find the shared-root Health palace
  2. Derive the 2-7, 3-8, 4-9 and 5-10 pairs and connect the twelve palaces in pairs
  3. Read each pair's stars: one shows surface, the shared-root shows carrier and cost
  4. Practice changing focus: count six from Wealth or Career to see that matter's hidden line
  5. Judge a matter with both palaces, balancing strength to avoid reading half
  6. Add decade/year: when both are activated by the same transformations, surface and hidden shift together most clearly

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the home palace and missing its shared-root half

Mistake 2: Memorizing the pair as a fixed rhyme but unable to change focus for other matters

Mistake 3: Thinking One-Six belongs only to Life when any palace can serve as "one"

Back to Your Own Chart

One-Six shared root trains the instinct to see both sides of one thing. Draw these five pairs on your chart and you begin to read what stays invisible on the surface yet quietly supports or drains you.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the One-Six shared-root palace relationship before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the One-Six shared-root palace relationship line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
