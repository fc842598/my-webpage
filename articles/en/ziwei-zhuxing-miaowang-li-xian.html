What Temple, Thriving, Favorable and Fallen Mean for Major Stars

Temple, Thriving, Favorable and Fallen describe how strongly and steadily a star expresses in a palace. Temple and Thriving express the star well, while Fallen is less steady and needs support, but Fallen is not automatically bad, and Temple still depends on transformations and harsh stars.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-27

These are energy and stability states, not labels; Fallen needs support, and Temple still depends on transformations.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some fear Fallen and celebrate Temple on sight; brightness alone is partial, so read the star combination together.

The Underlying Logic

Brightness is the strength of a star.

Temple expresses steadily; Fallen fluctuates and needs support.

Fallen is not necessarily bad, nor Temple automatically good.

Still combine transformations, helpful stars and harsh stars.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find the major star palace
  2. Check the brightness state
  3. If Fallen, read opposite and three-ways
  4. Check transformation activation
  5. Check harsh and helpful stars
  6. Rest on the combination

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fallen means bad

Mistake 2: Temple means good

Mistake 3: Read brightness alone

Back to Your Own Chart

Brightness is energy, not a verdict; for Fallen look for support, for Temple look at transformations, and always read the whole combination.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Major Stars Temple, Thriving, Favorable and Fallen before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Major Stars Temple, Thriving, Favorable and Fallen line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
