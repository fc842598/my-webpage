Side Income vs. Main Salary: Which Palaces Show Each One

How to separate stable career income from side or freelance income across the Wealth, Career, and related palaces.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

Many people have both a main salary and side income, and these do not appear in one Wealth Palace alone. Split the income sources, map them to different palaces, and then read the Wealth Palace as the overall summary.

Two income lines

The main salary links the Career Palace, which shows the work, with the Wealth Palace, which shows the income. Side or freelance work is less fixed and also depends on the nature of the work, such as expression, skill, cooperation, or resources.

Judgment steps

  1. List real income sources and separate stable salary from side work.
  2. Read the Wealth Palace for overall cash and money-management structure.
  3. Read the Career Palace for job stability and salary growth.
  4. Map the side work to its relevant palace and read its stars and transformations.
  5. Combine both lines in the Wealth Palace to judge which is worth more effort.

Reading order

  1. Fix the inputs and software version, then check Wealth, Career, and related palaces.
  2. Record main and side income separately rather than as one number.
  3. Judge each line by its host star, transformations, and difficult stars.
  4. Add the current cycle to see which line is activated now.
  5. Write the answer as stable salary plus side opportunity, not one Wealth label.
