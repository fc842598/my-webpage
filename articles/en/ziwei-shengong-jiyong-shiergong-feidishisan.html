How to Use the Body Palace: Why It Lives Inside One of the Twelve Palaces

The Body Palace has no separate box; it resides in one of six palaces and shows where later effort is directed.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-16 10:35

Newcomers often look for a thirteenth palace called the Body Palace. In fact, it has no separate box. It resides inside one of six palaces, the Life, Spouse, Wealth, Travel, Career, or Wellbeing Palace, and shares that palace's stars. It points to where effort is directed later in life, so finding its host palace matters more than treating it as a new box.

Which palaces host the Body Palace

The Body Palace only resides in six palaces: Life, Spouse, Wealth, Travel, Career, and Wellbeing. Wherever it lands, it is read together with that palace rather than occupying a separate cell. The first step is to see which palace the marker points to and treat that area as a later focus of energy.

How Life and Body Palaces divide the work

The Life Palace leans toward innate traits and the starting point, while the Body Palace leans toward where energy is actively invested later. Its weight often becomes more noticeable with age. A Body Palace in a career-related palace suggests continued investment in work, while one in Wellbeing or Wealth points toward inner satisfaction or resource management. They are two sides of one chart rather than separate lives.

Step-by-step Body Palace reading

  1. Find the Body Palace marker and confirm which of the six palaces it points to.
  2. Do not treat it as a separate box; read it with the host palace's major and helper stars.
  3. Read the Life Palace's innate traits first, then the Body Palace's later direction.
  4. Consider the life stage to judge whether the Body Palace weight is growing.
  5. Place the two readings side by side to see whether they reinforce or need balance.

Reading limits

The Body Palace does not change the host palace's basic meaning or add a new topic; it only highlights the later focus. It should not magnify one palace into an absolute verdict. Real choices and long-term effort decide whether that direction becomes concrete.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the chart, then locate the Body Palace marker and its host palace.
  2. Read it together with the host palace rather than inventing a thirteenth box.
  3. Start with the Life Palace, then move to the Body Palace to separate innate and later focus.
  4. Use the host palace's stars and transformations to identify the area of investment.
  5. Frame the result as a later tendency rather than a fixed fate.
