Tian Kong vs. Di Kong in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Two Emptiness Stars in Different Places

Di Kong, paired with Di Jie, leans to material emptiness: drain, money coming and going and empty hands; Tian Kong leans to mental emptiness: airy ideas and inspiration, but also plans falling through or losing touch with reality. Di Kong mostly concerns money and saving, Tian Kong the inner state and ideals; with auspicious stars the airiness becomes creativity and insight, and with malefics it spins and wastes.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-29

Some confuse the two and read any emptiness as loss; one empties the pocket and the other the ideas, with different readings and responses.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is why the emptiness differs so much. First identify the star, material drain versus mental airiness, then read the palace and company to see whether the emptiness leaks money, creates or becomes impractical.

The Underlying Logic

Di Kong pairs with Di Jie and governs material emptiness, drain and unkept money.

Tian Kong governs mental airiness, inspiration and detachment, with ideas that can fall through.

Di Kong in Wealth or Property shows in money and assets; Tian Kong in Fortune or Career shows in mood, ideals and creativity.

With auspicious stars the airiness becomes insight and creation; with malefics both empties waste, resolved through practicality and keeping reserves.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First tell Di Kong from Tian Kong
  2. Read Di Kong as material drain, focusing saving and restraint
  3. Read Tian Kong as mental airiness, focusing grounding and testing
  4. See the palace: money palaces show finances and inner palaces mood and ideals
  5. With auspicious stars turn airiness into creativity; with malefics practice practicality
  6. Use the decade to see when the emptiness activates

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Confusing the two and reading both as loss

Mistake 2: Seeing only the negative side and missing insight and creativity

Mistake 3: Not grounding Tian Kong so ideas stay half in the air

Back to Your Own Chart

Tian Kong and Di Kong are two emptinesses: one leaks materially and the other mentally. Distinguish them and read the palace, turning airiness to insight with auspicious stars and catching it with practicality under malefics, so emptiness is not only drain but room to turn and create.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the difference between Tian Kong and Di Kong and their placements before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the difference between Tian Kong and Di Kong and their placements line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
