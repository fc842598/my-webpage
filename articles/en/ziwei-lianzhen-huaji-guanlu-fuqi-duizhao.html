Lian Zhen Hua Ji in Career Versus Spouse Palace

The same annual transformation asks different questions in different palaces.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-07 09:08

An annual Lian Zhen Hua Ji does not give everyone the same bad event. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, its natal palace changes the subject. In the Career Palace, it may prompt a review of rules, approvals and responsibilities. In the Spouse Palace, it may prompt a conversation about boundaries and shared time.

Two ordinary cases

A project lead facing an audit may simply need clearer records. A couple with uneven household work needs an explicit agreement. Neither case follows automatically from one annual symbol. Check the opposite and connected palaces as well as the decade background.

Reading order

Locate the transformed star in the natal chart, identify its palace, then add the decade and annual layers. Compare chart prompts with actual documents and conversations.
