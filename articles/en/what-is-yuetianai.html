YuetianAI is a platform for generating and interpreting Zi Wei Dou Shu charts. It brings chart calculation, AI-assisted explanations, and learning articles into one place so users can move from a birth chart to a structured reading rather than a collection of isolated predictions.

What the Platform Focuses On

Zi Wei Dou Shu is a traditional Chinese chart system built from twelve palaces, star placements, the Four Transformations, and timing cycles. YuetianAI organizes those elements around practical questions about temperament, work, money, relationships, movement, and timing. The chart is used as a reflective framework, not as medical, legal, or financial certainty.

How Palace and Star Conditions Are Read

The Life Palace provides a starting point, while the Body Palace shows priorities that become more prominent over time. Career describes role and responsibility, Wealth describes how income is generated and retained, and Travel describes outside markets and environments. A star changes meaning with location: Wu Qu in Wealth is read differently from Wu Qu in Career. Hua Lu, Hua Quan, Hua Ke, and Hua Ji then modify resource flow, authority, recognition, and friction.

What a Structured Interpretation Looks Like

For example, a strong Career Palace with Hua Quan may indicate increased responsibility, but it does not by itself promise higher personal income; the Wealth Palace must receive and hold that value. A Wealth Palace connected to Travel may favor outside clients or online platforms, while Hua Ji on the same axis asks for closer review of payment terms and cash flow. These conditional readings are more useful than a single good-or-bad label.

Chart-Reading Order on YuetianAI

Generate the chart with accurate birth information first. Read the Life and Body Palaces for the person and long-term focus. Then compare Wealth, Career, and Travel as one working structure. Check the opposite palace, triad, main stars, and Four Transformations before adding the ten-year and annual cycles. Use the AI interpretation and articles to clarify terms, then turn the reading into questions and decisions that can be checked against real life.
