Tan-Lang in the Companions Palace: Versatile Friends and Wide Circles; Sort True Friends from Fair-Weather Ties

With Tan-Lang in Companions, friends are versatile and from all walks of life in wide, lively circles, but guard against banquet and desire-based ties; sort true friends from fair-weather ones and avoid draining yourself on empty socializing.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-26

You make friends easily and liven up any gathering with no shortage of events, but as circles mix, fair-weather and reliable people blend and you must know which is which.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Wide circles demand careful choosing: enjoy rich relationships yet actively select, giving time to sincere people who grow with you and staying clear of ties warm only over food and benefit.

The Underlying Logic

Tan-Lang governs versatility, desire and sociability; in Companions, friends come from all walks in wide circles.

Life is lively, information flows and opportunities are many.

The risk is banquet socializing, desire-based ties and empty networking.

Widely yet carefully connect, sort true from false and keep sincere people.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm Tan-Lang in Companions and its stars
  2. See whether friends are versatile and circles wide and mixed
  3. Use the information and opportunities wide networks bring
  4. Watch for banquet socializing and desire-based ties
  5. Sort true friends from fair-weather ones
  6. Give time to sincere people who grow with you

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Praising strong sociability without noting fair-weather friends

Mistake 2: Taking surface warmth as true friendship and misspending time

Mistake 3: Draining yourself on empty socializing and ignoring real close friends

Back to Your Own Chart

Tan-Lang in Companions gives wide networks and lively life, a real skill, but discernment sets people apart. Keep the sincere and take fair-weather ties lightly so your circles stay wide but not chaotic.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Tan-Lang in the Companions Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Tan-Lang in the Companions Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
