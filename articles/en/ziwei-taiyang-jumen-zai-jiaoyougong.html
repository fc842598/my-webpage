Tai Yang and Ju Men in the Friend Palace: Many Articulate Friends and Turning Warmth and Eloquence Into a Network Free of Disputes

The Friend Palace reads friends, subordinates, networks and cooperative bonds. Tai Yang is the noble star governing brightness, warmth and generosity while Ju Men is the dark star governing eloquence and speech, so together in Yin or Shen one is warm, sociable and expressive with a wide circle and easily becomes a connector in the crowd. The risk is friends of mixed character, disputes and too much entertaining, so the key is turning warmth into selection and using eloquence to help rather than relay gossip.

People with Tai Yang and Ju Men in the Friend Palace are warm and ready in speech, able to talk with all kinds of people and popular; yet once friends grow without filtering, disputes and trouble easily come to the door.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the network pattern with Tai Yang and Ju Men in the Friend Palace. Understand the palace reads friends, then see how warmth and eloquence combine so one socializes while keeping quality.

The Underlying Logic

Tai Yang in the Friend Palace gives many friends, warmth, helpfulness and noble contacts, yet also generosity, much giving and accepting everyone.

Ju Men there brings expressive friends who gather through speech, yet also disputes, gossip and relaying words.

Together they mean noble plus dark: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars warmth and expression become good resources; with malefics and Ji, trouble, friends who drag one down and pressure from speech or entertaining follow.

The lesson is warm with selection and speech with trust: filter friends actively, use eloquence to help things happen, do not relay gossip or join disputes and keep tact in dealings.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Tai Yang and Ju Men share the Friend Palace and check their brightness
  2. Filter friends actively rather than deepening ties with everyone
  3. Use eloquence to help things happen rather than relaying gossip
  4. Stay out of disputes and reduce empty entertaining
  5. Keep tact in dealings and rely on written terms for cooperation
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of networks and cooperation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Tai Yang-Ju Men in the Friend Palace only as popular with many friends while ignoring disputes

Mistake 2: Not filtering friends and being dragged down by the network

Mistake 3: Relaying gossip and joining disputes, harming a good reputation
