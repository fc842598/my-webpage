Qi Sha Chao Dou: How Qi Sha Is Directed by a Zi Wei-Tian Fu Center

Qi Sha Chao Dou, translated as Qi Sha Facing the Dipper, describes a precise palace opposition. Zi Wei and Tian Fu must sit together in one palace, forming a governing center, while Qi Sha occupies the palace directly opposite them. In plain English, force and risk face an organized command structure. A generic connection to Zi Wei, Tian Fu, Hua Quan, or assistant stars is not enough to create the named formation.

Formation Comes Before Metaphor

Zi Wei provides coordination and formal direction; Tian Fu provides storage, continuity, and resource management. Qi Sha contributes urgency and a willingness to take on difficult assignments. The formation works because the central pair and Qi Sha are on one exact axis. Hua Quan, the transformation of authority, can strengthen a mandate, while Zuo Fu and You Bi can add capable deputies. They refine a formed structure rather than substitute for it.

Two Conditional Comparisons

If Zi Wei and Tian Fu share Life and Qi Sha sits in the opposite Travel Palace, a strong Travel structure and supportive Career Palace may channel pressure into external assignments, relocation, or difficult markets. If the same axis lacks assistants and Hua Ji burdens Wealth, responsibility may grow faster than staffing or cash support. By contrast, Qi Sha in Life with only Hua Quan in Career but no opposing Zi Wei-Tian Fu pair may still describe decisive leadership; it should not be labeled Qi Sha Chao Dou.

Palace Placement Changes the Job of the Pattern

On the Life-Travel axis, the contrast is personal center versus external frontier. On the Career-Spouse axis, hard responsibility may compete with partnership time. On the Wealth-Fortune axis, large resource decisions may carry psychological or risk-management pressure. These interpretations remain conditional on star strength, supporting palaces, and actual opportunities. The formation does not guarantee military rank, executive status, or success in conflict.

Chart-Reading Order

First locate Zi Wei and Tian Fu and confirm that they share one palace. Second, verify Qi Sha in the exact opposite palace. Third, name both topics on that axis and judge which side is the governing center. Fourth, inspect the two trinal palaces, assistants, Hua Quan, Hua Lu, Hua Ke, Hua Ji, and malefic stars. Add the ten-year and annual cycles last, then describe the mission, authority, resources, and practical limits in ordinary language.
