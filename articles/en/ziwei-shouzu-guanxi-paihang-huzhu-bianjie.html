Sibling Relationships in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Mutual Help and Its Boundaries

Sibling relations are read mainly through Siblings, together with Property's distribution of original-family resources and Parents' attitude. Siblings stars set whether siblings help or compete; Lu/Lu Cun mostly means mutual backing, Ji/malefics bring rifts over eldercare, inheritance and favoritism. Birth order reinforces roles, but relationship quality is really set by Siblings structure and the boundaries you actively draw as an adult.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-15

Some siblings are lifelong backing, others fall out over eldercare and inheritance, some stay trapped by being the eldest who must yield; these scripts have their baseline in Siblings.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why siblings are not close, how to help without being dragged down, or how to divide eldercare and inheritance, first see the sibling structure, then draw clear boundaries.

The Underlying Logic

Help vs competition reads Siblings: Tian Tong/Tian Xiang/Tai Yin bring soft, mutually supportive bonds; Zi Wei/Wu Qu/Qi Sha make each strong-willed and prone to comparing achievements; Siblings with Lu/Lu Cun/Zuo Fu/You Bi is real help in a crisis; with Qing Yang/Tuo Luo/Ji it brings hidden rivalry and old grudges, so set rules upfront wherever interests are involved.

Resource distribution reads Property and Parents: original-family property, resources and parental favoritism sit there. Steady Property and balanced Parents rarely turn siblings against each other over resources; stressed Property and uneven Parents carry childhood unfairness into eldercare and inheritance, precisely where written agreements and fair procedures matter more than assumed goodwill.

Birth-order roles read the decade and Life: roles like the responsible eldest, cared-for youngest, overlooked middle are amplified or softened by Life nature. Hua-Quan/Tian-Liang Life naturally over-carries and risks becoming the perpetual rescuer; seeing this separates loving help from bottomless backing.

Adult boundaries read the rules you set: healthy sibling help rests on clear boundaries—help emergencies not chronic dependency, aid hardship not laziness, document large sums, share eldercare. Those with stressed Siblings must learn gentle-but-firm refusal of overreach; clear rules actually lengthen the relationship, while principle-less giving often ends with money spent and affection wounded.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Siblings stars to judge mutual-help versus competition type
  2. Read Property/Parents to assess hidden risks in resource distribution and favoritism
  3. Notice the over-carrying role in Life and refuse to be trapped by birth order
  4. Set help boundaries: emergencies not dependency, documented large sums, shared eldercare
  5. Use fair procedure and written agreements for inheritance and eldercare
  6. With stressed Siblings, refuse overreach gently but firmly—the relationship lasts longer

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Mistaking bottomless backing for family affection, losing both money and bond

Mistake 2: Relying on assumed goodwill for inheritance and eldercare without written rules, sowing conflict

Mistake 3: Staying trapped by the eldest-must-yield role without realizing it
