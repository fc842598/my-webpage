Every Zi Wei Dou Shu chart has six auspicious stars. What makes a meeting?

Finding all six auspicious stars somewhere in a chart differs from having them meet the Life Palace. Learn which positions actually count.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09 17:08

A full natal chart normally includes Zuo Fu, You Bi, Wen Chang, Wen Qu, Tian Kui and Tian Yue. Finding all six somewhere on the screen does not make your chart an unusual six-star configuration.

Location is the missing condition

When the Life Palace is the reference, its principal network consists of the Life, Wealth, Career and Travel Palaces. A traditional statement about auspicious stars meeting the Life Palace concerns their placement in that network.

For a hypothetical comparison, Wen Chang in Life and Wen Qu in Career belong to that network. Placing both outside it is a different arrangement, even though both charts contain the same named stars.

Stars in the two neighboring palaces may instead form a flanking arrangement. Record that separately. Do not rename flanking or distant placements as co-location to reach a desired count.

Once the geometry is clear, interpretation can examine the main stars and the life area involved. Six symbols are not evidence that six helpful people will appear. This is a distinction within traditional chart reading, not a validated prediction.
