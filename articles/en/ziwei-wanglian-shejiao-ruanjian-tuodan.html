What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether online dating suits you, why apps keep bringing the wrong people, or whether an online relationship can work, string Travel, Friends and Spouse together.

The Underlying Logic

Travel sets online external affinity: exalted Travel with Lu/Ke and social stars like Tan Lang/Lian Zhen/Sun is attractive in public and stranger domains, easily meeting people online, cross-region or in interest communities—the internet is your advantage field; fallen Travel with malefics/Ji struggles to judge packaged information and is better served by warm introductions or quickly moving online contacts into real-world understanding.

Friends sets screening judgment: apps offer many choices with packageable profiles; steady Friends with Ke/Tian Xiang/Tian Fu reads people accurately and keeps boundaries and pace; Friends with Kong/Jie/malefics/Tan Lang-Ji is led by scripts, personas and short emotion—stay highly alert to rushed pace, borrowing money, refusing video, dodging real information; that is a screening problem, not a fate problem.

Spouse sets the final want: online is only a channel, and whether it lands still depends on the type Spouse wants—steady Spouse wanting long-term stability earnestly moves even an online match to meeting, family and joint planning; a floating Spouse with heavy Tan Lang/Lian Zhen romance turns apps into a novelty loop, so first decide whether you want stability or short companionship.

Safe online-to-offline pace: after Travel introduces, Friends screens and Spouse sets the goal, land gradually—first thorough video and consistency checks, first meeting in public with friends/family informed, no rush to money or private exposure; treat any transfer, investment or "let's manage money together" as risk, judge relationships by real相处, and handle money/personal-safety matters through formal channels; the chart only helps you understand your pattern.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read Travel to judge whether public domains like the internet/distance are your advantage field
  2. Read Friends to assess screening judgment under information asymmetry
  3. Read Spouse to fix long-term stability versus short companionship instead of switching goals mid-way
  4. After meeting online, verify consistency through video and real information promptly
  5. Meet first in public and inform friends/family, with no rush to money or intimacy

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating the channel as the relationship itself, ignoring that Spouse's goal decides landing

Mistake 2: Weak Friends judgment without screening or boundaries, led by personas and scripts

Mistake 3: Keeping an online relationship from real-world verification, or lowering guard on money and intimacy
