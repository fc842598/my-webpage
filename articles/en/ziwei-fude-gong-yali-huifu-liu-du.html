How to Read the Fu De Palace for Stress and Recovery in Zi Wei Dou Shu

A practical Zi Wei Dou Shu guide to reading the Fu De Palace with the Life and Health Palaces when stress, sleep, and recovery become the real question.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:00

The Inner Rhythm

The Fu De Palace describes inner pace, satisfaction, and the way a person restores attention. It is more useful than a label such as overthinking.

Read the Three Areas Together

The Life Palace shows the default response, the Health Palace shows where strain is carried, and the Fu De Palace shows how the mind processes it.

Two Practical Examples

A Ji Men Hua Ji pattern can describe replaying a conversation, while Tai Yin Hua Ke may restore through quiet routines and a controlled environment.

Reading Order

Name the pressure, locate its source, then check the current cycle. Use the chart to design a recovery routine, not to diagnose a condition.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
