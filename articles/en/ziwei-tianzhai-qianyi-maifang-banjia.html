Reading the Property and Travel Palaces Together: Buying, Moving and the Sense of Settling

The Property Palace covers living and asset reception, not directly the number of properties; Travel adds moving motives, and buying also needs Wealth and cash flow. Read renting, buying and relocating separately.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Should I buy or keep renting, should I change cities cannot be read from Property alone. Property writes your bond with home and Travel the urge to go out; their tension is the truth of housing choices.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Stars in Property do not guarantee a purchase, and active Travel does not force a move. Real judgment adds budget, commute, cash flow and family responsibility; fortune reading only suggests tendencies, not prices or timing.

The Underlying Logic

The Property Palace first covers living and asset reception, not the number of properties.

The Travel Palace supplements the external environment and moving motives.

Buying requires the Wealth Palace, cash flow and family responsibility.

It cannot predict house prices or timing; renting, buying and relocating are read separately.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First set the Property Palace star for living and asset tendencies
  2. Then set the Travel Palace star for moving and outside motives
  3. Separate the question into renting, buying or relocating
  4. For buying, add budget, down payment and cash-flow calculations
  5. For renting or relocating, look at commute and work mobility
  6. Decide with family responsibility without treating fortune reading as price prediction

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming stars in Property guarantee a purchase

Mistake 2: Looking only at palaces without budget and cash flow

Mistake 3: Using fortune reading to predict house prices and timing

Back to Your Own Chart

Housing choices balance desired stability against real conditions. Read Property and Travel for tendencies, then ground them with budget and cash flow so where you live truly suits you.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Property and Travel palaces for buying and moving before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Property and Travel palaces for buying and moving line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
