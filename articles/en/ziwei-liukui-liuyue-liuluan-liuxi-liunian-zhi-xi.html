Branch-Based Flowing Stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Flowing Kui-Yue, Luan-Xi and Horse for Romance and Movement

A set placed by the year branch—Flowing Kui, Yue, Hong Luan, Tian Xi and Tian Ma (plus the Pre-Year spirits)—shows where this year's helpful people, romance/celebrations and movement land. It complements the stem set: one for resources/tests, the other for patrons, joy and travel.

Stem flowing stars use the year stem and branch flowing stars the year branch; overlaid together they form the complete temporary chart. For this year's romance, patrons or relocation/travel, look mainly to the branch set.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether romance stirs this year, where patrons are, or whether to move means placing flowing Luan/Xi, Kui/Yue and Ma by the year branch and seeing which natal palaces they enter.

The Underlying Logic

Placement uses the asked year's branch: flowing Luan and Xi by the branch (always in opposite palaces), Flowing Ma by the branch in the four horse grounds Yin-Si-Shen-Hai, and flowing Kui/Yue by the branch's patron directions.

Flowing Luan/Xi govern this year's romance and celebration: in Life, Spouse or Karma, or meeting natal romance stars/Hua Lu, they signal a lively love life, easy commitment, engagement, marriage or a new child—yet only stable if the natal Spouse base is sound.

Flowing Kui/Yue govern this year's patrons: whichever palace they hit finds help this year from elders, superiors or strangers; Flowing Ma governs movement—in Travel/Career/Wealth it means business trips, assignment, relocation or more running around.

The branch set is also temporary: Luan/Xi on a strong natal Spouse兑现 a happy event, on a stressed romance spot it may be only a fleeting or poor match; Flowing Ma meeting natal Ma and Hua Lu is "Lu-Ma galloping" income through movement, meeting malefics/Ji is fruitless rushing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find the asked year's branch and place flowing Hong Luan and Tian Xi
  2. Place Flowing Ma in the four horse grounds by the same branch, then locate flowing Kui/Yue
  3. Overlay the branch set on the natal chart and mark the palaces hit
  4. Use Luan/Xi for romance and celebration, judging stability against the natal Spouse base
  5. Use Kui/Yue for this year's patron direction and Ma for travel/assignment/relocation pace
  6. Read with the stem set to separate patron/joy lines from resource/test lines, both as this-year-only layers

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Promising marriage from Luan in Life without checking the natal Spouse base

Mistake 2: Taking Flowing Ma as bad rushing when Ma meeting Lu is income through movement

Mistake 3: Not separating stem-based from branch-based flowing stars and confusing their anchors

Back to Your Own Chart

Branch-based flowing stars govern a year's patrons, celebrations and movement: Luan/Xi for romance, Kui/Yue for helpers, Ma for travel. Overlay them with the stem set on the natal chart and you see clearly where this year brings people and events.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to branch-based annual stars (flowing Kui-Yue, Luan-Xi and the annual horse) before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the branch-based annual stars (flowing Kui-Yue, Luan-Xi and the annual horse) line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
