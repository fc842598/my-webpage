Wu Qu and Qi Sha in the Health Palace: A Body That Endures but Fears Hard Injury, Turning Firmness and the General Into Careful Health

The Health Palace reads the bodily foundation and health tendencies. Wu Qu is metal, governing firmness and the metal-related breathing, teeth, bones and injuries, while Qi Sha transforms into authority, governing decisiveness and also leaning metal, corresponding to injuries, bones and surgery, so together in Mao or You the bodily foundation is often strong and enduring, yet vulnerable to hard injuries, metal wounds, bone strain and forcing through. The stars show tendencies rather than a diagnosis, so the key is regular routine, safety and protection, regular checkups and prompt care at a proper hospital when unwell.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Health Palace, metal governs breathing, teeth, bones and metal injuries, and a firm character can endure, yet also tends to force through and bump into things.

Qi Sha there governs injuries, bones, surgery and acute damage, with decent recovery, yet can be risky and leave no room.

Together they mean metal plus the general: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars and good protection the body is strong; with malefics and Ji, injury, bone or breathing problems can follow.

The lesson is enduring while protecting and moving with safeguards: use protection in sports and work, do not force through or self-diagnose, take regular checkups and seek prompt care.
