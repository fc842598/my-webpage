The Zi Wei Star Overview: The Emperor Star

Zi Wei is the North Pole ruler and emperor star, governing distinction, leadership, face and high standards. Zi Wei alone needs the hundred officials (Zuo You Kui Yue Chang Qu Fu Xiang) around to form a great pattern; without assistants it is aloof, and malefics make it proud and toilsome.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-08

Zi Wei is like an emperor needing officials to sit steady; the emperor star alone feels lonely at the top.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see Zi Wei guard life and assume great rank, but pattern height depends on officials; the emperor star gives leadership tone.

The Underlying Logic

Zi Wei belongs to earth, governing distinction, leadership and face.

Hundred officials (Zuo You Kui Yue Chang Qu Fu Xiang) around form the ruler-minister meeting.

Without assistants it is aloof; malefics make it proud and toilsome.

It favors leadership, management and public office.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the Zi Wei palace and brightness
  2. See whether officials surround
  3. Count Zuo You Kui Yue Chang Qu Fu Xiang
  4. No assistants: guard aloofness
  5. With malefics: guard pride and toil
  6. Judge pattern height

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing Zi Wei guard life and assuming rank without officials

Mistake 2: Proud without assistants

Mistake 3: Not checking malefics, lonely at the top
