Lian Zhen and Tan Lang in the Virtue Palace: A Rich Mind With Many Desires, Turning Romance and Talent Into a Spirit Free of Inner Friction

The Virtue Palace reads the inner state, spiritual enjoyment and blessings. Lian Zhen is the secondary romance governing firmness, competitiveness and feeling while Tan Lang is the primary romance governing desire, versatility and socializing, so together in Si or Hai the mind is delicate and sensitive with a colorful spiritual world, enjoying life and carrying taste and talent. The risk is too many desires, overthinking and inner friction, so the key is ranking desires and turning talent into focus and an outlet.

People with Lian Zhen and Tan Lang in the Virtue Palace have lively minds and deep feelings and find their own pleasures, with never a dull spiritual life; yet when desires and thoughts are too many and all wanted, they fight inside and cannot settle.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the inner state with Lian Zhen and Tan Lang in the Virtue Palace. Understand the palace reads the mind, then see how the two romances get along so one stays rich without inner friction.

The Underlying Logic

Lian Zhen in the Virtue Palace makes the mind value feeling, competitiveness and strong sensitivity, yet also confinement in emotion and tangles.

Tan Lang there makes the mind carry many desires, talent, enjoyment and good taste, yet also overthinking and always looking at a higher hill.

Together they mean double romance: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars under restraint talent and feeling become creation and taste; with malefics and Ji and no restraint, inner friction, indulgence and pressure from desire and unease follow.

The lesson is desires with order and talent with focus: rank desires, land talent in one long-term outlet, practice calming the mind and balance enjoyment with restraint.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Lian Zhen and Tan Lang share the Virtue Palace and check their brightness
  2. Rank desires and thoughts rather than wanting everything
  3. Land talent in one long-term outlet and focus on it
  4. Practice calming the mind to reduce tangles and inner friction
  5. Balance enjoyment with restraint to avoid indulgence
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of spiritual activation and mood

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Lian Zhen-Tan Lang in the Virtue Palace only as enjoyment and talent while ignoring inner friction

Mistake 2: Too many desires and wanting everything, unable to settle

Mistake 3: Talent without a focused outlet, energy lost in tangles
