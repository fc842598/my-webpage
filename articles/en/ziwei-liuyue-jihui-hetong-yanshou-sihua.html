Zi Wei Dou Shu Monthly Transformations: Check the Contract Before Delivery

Use monthly transformations as prompts to review pricing, change requests, and acceptance terms for new projects.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-22 09:04

Three new project offers in one week can feel like a clear financial gain. The harder question is who decides when the work is finished. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, a monthly transformation can point attention toward resources or written communication. It cannot promise that every offer will become paid revenue.

Identify the layer and calculation rule

Natal, ten-year, annual, and monthly transformations may appear together. Record which marker belongs to the current month, its star, and its palace. If two chart tools disagree, check calendar conversion and the transformation table before choosing a reading. Do not turn a software setting into a personal forecast.

Match palace questions to contract facts

When the Wealth Palace is involved, review deposits, payment dates, and collection. When the Career Palace is involved, review delivery ownership and hours. A design client may say “two rounds” in a call while the agreement says “revise until satisfied.” The second wording can consume the margin even if the quoted price looks attractive.

Keep one written version of the work

A Wen Qu Hua Ji marker can prompt a review of specifications and records; it does not mean fraud. Put milestones, client inputs, change fees, and acceptance criteria in one document. Read the monthly layer against natal and longer cycles, then decide from the budget, capacity, and signed terms. A favorable chart label cannot repair an undefined obligation.
