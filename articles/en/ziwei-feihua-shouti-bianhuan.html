Changing the Reference Person in Zi Wei Dou Shu

Name the chart owner and reference person when changing a palace frame.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-01 10:09

Derived palaces depend on whose relationship is being examined. Write down the reference person before changing the palace frame, then recompute the transformation route. A relative's concern cannot be assigned to the chart owner as behavior.

A derived palace depends on the reference person. Write the chart owner and the relationship being examined before recomputing any transformation route. A concern about an adult child's relationship cannot simply be assigned to the chart owner's own marriage. Ask only about facts people choose to share.
