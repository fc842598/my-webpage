How to Judge Annual Wealth: Combine the Year Wealth Palace, Lu/Ji and the Decade Spending Cycle

Whether the year earns or loses depends on the annual Wealth stars and where annual Lu/Ji fly, layered over whether the decade Wealth accumulates or spends; Lu into Wealth/Property raises income, Ji clashing Wealth or meeting voids/Tian Ma calls for controlling spending and investment impulse.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-08

Annual wealth is not "certain riches this year" but cash-flow direction and tempo: where money comes from, where it goes, whether to expand or defend—clear only across three layers.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People invest heavily at annual Lu and freeze at Ji. Ask where Lu lands (steady vs. speculative), which palace Ji clashes, and whether the decade accumulates or spends heavily—or you easily move backward.

The Underlying Logic

Natal Wealth sets the earning model—salary, expertise, business or investment—and the ability to keep money; annual fortune happens inside this model.

Decade Wealth sets the ten-year cycle: Lu/Quan with auspicious stars is accumulation, suitable to add assets gradually; Ji/voids is spending or restructuring (buying a home, starting a business, repaying debt can be intentional big spending)—distinguish active investment from passive loss.

Annual Wealth reads current cash flow: annual Lu into Wealth/Property/Career brings raises, project payments, asset appreciation; annual Ji in/clashing Wealth with voids/Tian Ma brings surprise expenses, investment give-back, contract/loan losses.

Combine strategy: accumulating decade + annual Lu = expand modestly and in tranches; any decade with annual Ji clashing Wealth = cut leverage, hold cash, no guarantees, no chasing highs; even a great wealth year stays within the natal earning model, with take-profit/stop-loss especially for speculation-prone charts.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read natal Wealth: how you earn and how well you keep money
  2. Cast decade Wealth to classify accumulation vs. big-spending years
  3. Cast annual Wealth and transformations for where Lu enters and Ji clashes
  4. Separate steady income from speculative gain; different Lu sources, different strategies
  5. Expand in tranches and lock gains in good years; cut leverage and keep cash in risk years
  6. Judge every big decision against the decade background, not single-year emotion

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Heavy speculation at annual Lu without decade/natal earning model

Mistake 2: Calling every Ji a loss while missing intentional big spending vs. passive drain

Mistake 3: Skipping take-profit/stop-loss and treating annual luck as sustainable skill

Back to Your Own Chart

Annual wealth is best read as direction, tempo and risk control. Layer natal model, decade cycle and current cash flow; you dare to receive in earning years and can defend in loss years—then the year's money actually stays.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to judging annual wealth fortune before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the judging annual wealth fortune line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
