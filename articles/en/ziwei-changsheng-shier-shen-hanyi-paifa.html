The Twelve Life-Cycle Spirits in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Twelve Life Stages Starting From the Five-Element Phase

The twelve Life-Cycle spirits start from the Five-Element phase (Metal at Si, Wood at Hai, Water at Shen, Fire/Earth at Yin), proceeding clockwise for yang-male/yin-female and counterclockwise otherwise: Birth, Bath, Capping, Official, Emperor, Decline, Illness, Death, Grave, Extinction, Embryo, Nurture. They describe how a matter's qi rises, peaks and recedes.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09

The Life-Cycle spirits are not lucky or unlucky spirits but stage markers showing whether a palace's matter is in a rising, mature or receding phase of qi.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking why the same star shows different drive across charts means checking, beyond brightness, whether it sits on Birth/Emperor or Death/Extinction to see its qi stage.

The Underlying Logic

Placement: the phase sets the Birth start (Water-2 at Shen, Wood-3 at Hai, Metal-4 at Si, Earth-5/Fire-6 at Yin), then clockwise for yang-male/yin-female and counterclockwise otherwise through twelve stages.

The rising half governs growth: Birth starts and vitalizes (favored with Tian Ji), Bath governs attraction and adjustment, Capping shaping and small success, Official establishing with help, Emperor peak drive and maximum power.

The falling half governs contraction: Decline weakens, Illness blocks, Death stalls, Grave stores and settles, Extinction empties and turns, Embryo gestates unseen, Nurture develops before release—a complete qi cycle.

It complements brightness: brightness is whether a star has power, the Life-Cycle is which stage the matter occupies; an exalted star on Death/Extinction often has ability stuck in phase, while a strong star on Emperor carries maximum momentum.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Set the Five-Element phase from the Life stem-branch, then where Birth starts
  2. Place the twelve stages along the palaces, clockwise for yang-male/yin-female and counterclockwise otherwise
  3. Mark which Life-Cycle stage each palace sits on
  4. Split the stages into rising and falling halves and understand each qi level
  5. Read with the major star: a strong star peaks on Birth/Emperor and struggles on Death/Extinction
  6. Use the stage to explain why something is stalled or timely now, rather than judging luck in isolation

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating Birth/Emperor as lucky and Death/Extinction as unlucky when they are stages, not verdicts

Mistake 2: Not knowing Birth starts from the phase and misplacing the whole cycle

Mistake 3: Using brightness alone and unable to explain why a strong star can still stall

Back to Your Own Chart

The Life-Cycle spirits give stars a timeline: even the strongest star rises and recedes. See which stage your key palace sits on, and you learn better what to push now and what to patiently nurture.

  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
