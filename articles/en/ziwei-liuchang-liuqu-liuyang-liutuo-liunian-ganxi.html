Stem-Based Flowing Stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How to Place and Use Flowing Lu, Yang, Tuo, Chang and Qu

A set of flowing stars placed by the year (also decade or month) stem—Flowing Lu, Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Kui, Yue, Wen Chang, Wen Qu and the annual transformations—is a temporary annual layer over natal assistants, showing where the year's resources, tests and document chances land.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-09

Natal Lu Cun, Yang/Tuo and Chang/Qu are fixed; each year adds a flowing version by its stem, and where the two layers overlap is often where this year strengthens, tests or opens a document opportunity.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking how this year's Lu Cun and Wen Chang overlap the natal chart and where the key points are means placing the stem-based flowing stars and reading their intersections with natal stars.

The Underlying Logic

Placement mirrors the natal stars but uses the asked year's stem: place Flowing Lu (annual Lu Cun), with Flowing Qing Yang and Tuo Luo one palace on each side, plus flowing Kui/Yue, Chang/Qu and the annual Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji.

Flowing Lu is the year's temporary resource: where it lands or meets natal Lu Cun/Hua Lu, this year's cash, chance and network supply strengthen; flowing Chang/Qu govern documents, exams, contracts, expression and works this year.

Flowing Yang/Tuo are the year's temporary tests: Yang is open clash, competition and surgery-type imagery; Tuo is hidden delay, repetition and grinding; whichever palace they hit faces an extra test this year, sharper atop a natal malefic.

The core is layering: stem-based flowing stars never change the natal structure, only mark temporary plus/minus; Flowing Lu/Chang/Qu on a strong natal palace兑现 a bonus, Flowing Yang/Tuo on a natively stressed palace is where to be extra careful this year.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find the asked year's stem and place its Flowing Lu (annual Lu Cun)
  2. Place Flowing Qing Yang and Tuo Luo one palace on either side of it
  3. Place flowing Kui/Yue, Chang/Qu and the annual transformations by the same stem
  4. Overlay this stem set on the natal chart to find palaces intersecting natal stars
  5. Use Flowing Lu/Chang/Qu for resources and document chances, Flowing Yang/Tuo for tests
  6. Treat it as a temporary annual layer that does not change the natal verdict,细化 to months if needed

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Not knowing flowing stars use the year stem and mistaking natal Lu Cun for this year's Flowing Lu

Mistake 2: Treating Flowing Yang/Tuo as disaster instead of this year's temporary test on a specific base

Mistake 3: Reading flowing stars without the natal chart, reversing the "natal primary, flowing temporary" order

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to stem-based annual stars (flowing Chang-Qu, Yang-Tuo and Lu) before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the stem-based annual stars (flowing Chang-Qu, Yang-Tuo and Lu) line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
