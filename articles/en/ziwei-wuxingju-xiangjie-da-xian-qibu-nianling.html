Five-Element Phases in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Water-2 to Fire-6 and How They Set the Decade Start Age

The five-element phase is derived from the Life palace branch and birth-year stem (na-yin), into water-2, wood-3, metal-4, earth-5, fire-6; it sets Zi Wei's placement and the decade start age (2/3/4/5/6, ten years per palace), with a slight temperament tint. It is the key parameter for casting and decades; without it, decade direction and start age go wrong.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

Its most practical role is the first-decade start age: water at two, fire at six—at the same age, people of different phases walk entirely different decades.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People apply a fixed start age for decades ignoring the phase, misaligning every ten-year stage; a few years' offset shifts the whole reading—fix the phase before decades.

The Underlying Logic

The phase comes from the Life branch and birth-year stem na-yin: water-2, wood-3, metal-4, earth-5, fire-6.

It sets two things: Zi Wei's position (core of casting) and the decade start age (2/3/4/5/6).

Decades start at Life, clockwise for yang-male/yin-female and counterclockwise otherwise, ten years per palace, start age from the phase.

Temperament associations (water agile, fire late) are reference only; the hard role is casting and decade start—use software-verified phase.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Cast from birth date/time, fix Life branch and birth-year stem
  2. Look up the phase (water-2 to fire-6) by na-yin table
  3. Set the decade start age (2–6) from the phase
  4. Set clockwise/counterclockwise by gender yin-yang
  5. Lay each ten-year decade palace by palace from Life
  6. Verify with software-confirmed phase to avoid manual error

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Starting decades at a fixed age without the phase

Mistake 2: Treating phase temperament as its main use

Mistake 3: Mismatching direction and start age, shifting all stages

Back to Your Own Chart

The phase is a one-move-affects-all parameter: it sets both Zi Wei's position and your first-decade age. Fix it accurately and later decades and years get a correct time coordinate.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to five-element phases explained before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the five-element phases explained line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
