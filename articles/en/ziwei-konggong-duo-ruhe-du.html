How to Read a Chart With Many Empty Palaces: Not Empty Everywhere, but Borrowing and Confirming One by One

Several palaces without major stars do not mean no support everywhere; each empty palace borrows from its opposite, reads its trine and the transformations that trigger it, and after confirming the chart the reading actually clarifies.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

A chart with many empty palaces looks sparse, yet the information is all there; the answers sit in the opposite palaces and transformations and must be borrowed and matched one by one.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some panic at four or five empty palaces and feel the chart has nothing; the issue is not many empty palaces but trying to read every palace with the same home-major-star method.

The Underlying Logic

An empty palace has no major star but is not starless; assistant, malefic and natal transformation stars still land there.

The basic reading borrows stars from the opposite palace and then combines the trine to see where resources and resistance come from.

Multiple empty palaces are handled separately, each checked for which layer, natal, decade or year, activates it rather than dismissed as all empty.

A chart with many empty palaces especially needs the chart confirmed, verifying the birth time before conclusions.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Mark all empty palaces and count how many there are and which affairs they sit on
  2. For each, first borrow the opposite major star and write its basic tendency
  3. Then combine that palace trine, count auspicious, malefic and transformation stars for the net effect
  4. Check whether natal transformations land in the empty palace to see if the baseline adds support
  5. Read how the decade and year pass through these palaces to see when they activate
  6. Confirm the chart and verify before concluding, preferring to be slow early

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Equating empty palaces with nothing and limiting yourself

Mistake 2: Treating all empty palaces alike without borrowing opposites and checking activation

Mistake 3: Concluding from empty palaces without confirming the chart, so a wrong time shifts everything

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading many empty palaces teaches solid borrowing and confirming. Borrow each opposite star, match the transformations and verify the time, and the chart is no thinner than one full of stars.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to charts with many empty palaces before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the charts with many empty palaces line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
