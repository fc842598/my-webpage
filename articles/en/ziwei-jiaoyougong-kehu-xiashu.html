How to Read Clients and Subordinates in the Friends Palace: It Is Not Only Friendship

The Friends Palace, historically the Servants Palace, writes how you relate to subordinates, clients and peer collaborators. The major star shows whether you gain support or spend energy; heavy malefics make leading and selling harder.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-25

Many think the Friends Palace only covers friends, but it covers everyone you deal with yet cannot fully command: subordinates, clients and suppliers, the relationships that test coordination most.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Why do some find leading teams exhausting and lose out with clients? The answer is often in Friends. Read it to know whether you gain help or get drained in these relationships, then adjust how you manage and cooperate.

The Underlying Logic

The Friends Palace, anciently the Servants Palace, governs subordinates, clients and peer collaborators.

The major star decides whether you gain support or spend energy.

Heavy malefics and Ji make leading teams and dealing with clients more friction-prone.

Read with the Career Palace to judge the overall state of management and business cooperation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First set the Friends Palace major star and brightness
  2. Translate it into how you relate to subordinates and clients
  3. Read auspicious stars to see whether help comes easily
  4. Read malefics and Ji to identify friction and draining risk
  5. Combine with the Career Palace for team and business cooperation
  6. Adjust management style and client cooperation boundaries accordingly

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Narrowing the Friends Palace to only friends

Mistake 2: Looking only at the major star and not team friction from malefics

Mistake 3: Being exhausted leading yet not adjusting management and sighing over poor relations

Back to Your Own Chart

The Friends Palace mirrors your ability to collaborate. See the support and friction with subordinates and clients, then adjust management and boundaries, and the most tiring part of work often eases.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to clients and subordinates in the Friends Palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the clients and subordinates in the Friends Palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
