Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Virtue Palace: An Inner Peace Built on Accumulation, Turning Practicality and Storage Into a Mind That Is Not Tense

The Virtue Palace reads the inner state, spiritual enjoyment and blessings. Wu Qu is the wealth star governing firm execution and practicality while Tian Fu is the treasury star governing steady holding and tolerance, so together in Zi or Wu the mind gains security through real accumulation, working steadily and enjoying material and stable things. The risk is being too practical, mentally tense and living only to take in and never let out, so the key is leaving room for relaxation and sharing some of the treasury for enjoyment.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-31

People with Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Virtue Palace keep an inner ledger, feeling calmer as savings and resources grow and disliking empty things; yet when nerves are too tight and everything must be held, they tire easily and miss the sweetness of life.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the inner state with Wu Qu and Tian Fu in the Virtue Palace. Understand the palace reads the mind, then see how practicality and holding combine so one stays grounded without straining.

The Underlying Logic

Wu Qu in the Virtue Palace makes the mind practical, execution-minded and focused on real results, disliking empty talk, yet also tense and unable to rest.

Tian Fu there makes the mind seek stability, calmed by stored reserves and tolerant and content, yet also conservative and placing all security on material things.

Together they mean wealth star plus treasury: the mind is grounded and rich in blessings, and with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars accumulation brings ease; with malefics and Ji, anxiety, worry and pressure from holding money follow.

The lesson is practical without strain and holding while able to enjoy: treat rest and enjoyment as proper plans, leave an outlet for the mind, and keep money and resources while using them well.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Wu Qu and Tian Fu share the Virtue Palace and check their brightness
  2. Put rest and enjoyment into the schedule rather than enduring everything
  3. Leave an outlet for the mind and cultivate interests unrelated to money
  4. Plan resources and savings to avoid worry and anxiety
  5. Practice relaxing control rather than holding everything
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of inner tension and blessings

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Wu Qu-Tian Fu in the Virtue Palace only as grounded and able to save while ignoring the strain risk

Mistake 2: Placing all security on material things with no mental outlet

Mistake 3: Holding everything, growing more tired the more one keeps
