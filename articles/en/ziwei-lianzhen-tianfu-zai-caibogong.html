Lian Zhen and Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace: Steady, Rule-Minded Earning and Turning the Secondary Peach Blossom and Storage Into Steady Wealth Without Reserve

The Wealth Palace reads the earning style and cash state. Lian Zhen governs firmness, the secondary peach blossom and rules while Tian Fu is the treasury star governing steadiness and holding, so together in Chen or Xu one earns steadily and rule-mindedly, valuing trust and making steady money through skill and positions within systems, suiting management, institutions, finance and authorized posts. The risk is concern about appearances, conservatism and losses from socializing, so the key is treating rules as an inner measure and seeking actively within them. This is only a metaphysical reference and not investment advice.

People with Lian Zhen and Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace earn steadily, keep trust and carry matters, and positions and systems give them chances; yet when overly concerned about appearances and conservative, they easily lose money in socializing and let go of achievable wealth.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is the wealth-seeking pattern with Lian Zhen and Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace. Understand the palace reads cash, then see how firmness and holding combine so one keeps rules while daring to act.

The Underlying Logic

Lian Zhen in the Wealth Palace gives rule-minded, principled earning that carries matters, yet also rigidity and concern about appearances.

Tian Fu there gives steady earning with stored resources, yet also conservatism and reserve.

Together they mean rules plus storage: with Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious stars one earns steadily within systems; with malefics and Ji, pressure from face, connections or procedures follows.

The lesson is steady with progress and rules used for oneself: treat trust as a pass, clarify contracts early, seek actively within rules and budget socializing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Lian Zhen and Tian Fu share the Wealth Palace and check their brightness
  2. Clarify trust and procedures and handle contracts early
  3. Seek actively within rules and decide what can be decided
  4. Budget socializing and connections to reduce surprise losses
  5. Build through reputation and skill, avoiding overreach
  6. With the decade and year, see the stages of earning activation and harvest

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking Lian Zhen-Tian Fu in the Wealth Palace only as steady and rule-minded while ignoring initiative

Mistake 2: Proud of face and conservative, letting go of achievable wealth

Mistake 3: No budget for socializing, losing money over face and procedures
