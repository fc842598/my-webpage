How to Read the Three-Ways-Four-Corners: Life, Wealth, Career and Travel

From any palace, the three-ways-four-corners joins its two trine palaces and the opposite palace. For the Life palace, these are Wealth, Career and Travel together with Life. Judge a domain by the stars and transformations across this whole group, not a single palace.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-27

For Life, the three-ways-four-corners are Life, Wealth, Career plus the opposite Travel; read a group, not one palace.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some judge from one star in Life; a single palace misses support and pulls, so read the full group.

The Underlying Logic

The three-ways are the two trine palaces; the four-corners add the opposite.

For Life these are Life, Wealth, Career and Travel.

Stars and transformations meet and activate across this group.

Do not conclude from one palace; the group shows support and clashes.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Set the base palace
  2. Find the two trine palaces
  3. Find the opposite palace
  4. List the four major stars
  5. Check transformation meetings
  6. Judge the group together

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Read one palace

Mistake 2: Miss the opposite

Mistake 3: Mistake it for four stars

Back to Your Own Chart

Read any domain through the Life, Wealth, Career and Travel group; only when stars and transformations meet can you judge it accurately.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to How to Read the Three-Ways-Four-Corners before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the How to Read the Three-Ways-Four-Corners line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
