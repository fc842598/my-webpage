Ten-Year Cycle vs Annual Trigger: Background First, Yearly Movement Second

How to separate the ten-year cycle from the yearly trigger so you can tell long background from short-term rises and drops.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-07-26 07:46

A chart becomes much easier to read when you stop asking the ten-year cycle and the annual trigger to do the same job.

What This Means

The major cycle sets the environment, pressure, and long trend. The annual trigger tells you what part of that environment moves this year. Mixing them together creates confusion about what is temporary and what is structural.

How To Read It

Read the natal chart first, then the ten-year cycle, then the annual trigger. Ask what the long background allows, and only then decide what the current year is switching on inside that background.

Simple Examples

  • Read the star through the palace and the real-life role it points to, rather than using a vague fixed prediction.
  • A strong year cannot fully rescue a weak decade, but it can create one good opening inside it.
  • A rough year inside a good decade may be a dip, not a total reversal.

Practical Order

First define the question. Then read the palace, its opposite palace, the supporting palaces, and the ten-year or annual trigger. This keeps the reading useful for career, money, relationships, and real choices.
