Reading Timing in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Decade Sets the Baseline, the Year Triggers It

The decade limit sets the ten-year theme and capacity, while the yearly limit sets that year's trigger. Timing is not luck appearing from nowhere; it is the decade's topic activated in a given year. Read the two layers together to see why an event happened in that specific year.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-27

Someone asks why they fell in a particular year despite a good decade. The answer is that the decade sets the ten-year base, but which moment gets lit depends on that year's limit activating a certain palace.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Split the timing chart in two: the decade is the riverbed, setting the direction and volume for ten years, while the year is the rain, deciding which year the water rises. Timing is when the riverbed and that year's rain line up.

The Underlying Logic

The decade spans about ten years and sets the life theme, capacity and overall tone for that stretch, a long-term baseline.

The year spans one year and layers that year's palaces and transformations over the decade, a short-term trigger.

Timing is the moment the decade's topic is activated by the year: without the decade theme, even a harsh year has nothing to act on.

Read which area the decade lays as its base, then see which palace the year activates; only when both align is it a true timing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Identify the active decade and its theme palace for the ten years
  2. Read the decade's stars and transformations and note which area it lays
  3. Pinpoint the specific year in question
  4. Layer the year's palaces and transformations to see which decade palace it activates
  5. Judge whether the decade topic and the year trigger align in direction and area
  6. Only when aligned call it a timing, and read conditionally rather than absolutely

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Reading only the yearly fortune while ignoring the decade's baseline

Mistake 2: Declaring every year smooth because the decade is good, ignoring the yearly trigger

Mistake 3: Treating timing as luck from nowhere without asking whether the decade topic exists

Back to Your Own Chart

The decade is the base color and the year the brushstroke. The ten-year theme is set there, and only when a year touches it does an event land in that year; read both layers and timing is not hollow.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to decade baseline and yearly trigger for timing before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the decade baseline and yearly trigger for timing line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
