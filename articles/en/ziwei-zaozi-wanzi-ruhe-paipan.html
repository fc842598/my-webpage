Born After 11 PM: Early and Late Rat Hour and Comparing Two Charts

The Rat hour runs from 23:00 to 01:00. One school advances the lunar day at 23:00 when the Rat hour begins, while another keeps the part before midnight as the late Rat hour of the current day. For 23:00–24:00 births the two rules yield different lunar days, so in practice chart both and reverse-check against lived events rather than committing to one school in advance.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some insist one school is absolute; the schools genuinely differ, and comparing charts is more useful than arguing.

The Underlying Logic

The Rat hour crosses midnight, from 23:00 to 01:00.

One school advances the day at 23:00; the other keeps pre-midnight as the current day.

For 23:00–24:00, the two rules yield different lunar days.

Chart both and reverse-check with lived events.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm a 23:00–24:00 birth
  2. Chart by the early-Rat rule
  3. Chart by the late-Rat rule
  4. Compare the lunar-day difference
  5. Verify with key events
  6. Take the closer fit

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Accept only one school

Mistake 2: Keep the same day after 23:00

Mistake 3: Skip comparing charts
