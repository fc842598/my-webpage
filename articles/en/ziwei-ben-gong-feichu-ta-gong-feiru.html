Flying Out vs Flying In: A Two-Way Reading

Flying out of the home palace is what you actively do outward, the cause; flying in from other palaces is external influence on you, the effect. Read both directions to know how much is your initiative and how much is external push.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

Flying out is like you actively calling others, flying in is like others calling you; in a relationship, who initiates and who responds is read by two-way flights.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some only see flying out and take all as their initiative; others only see flying in and take all as external; the real way reads both directions and separates cause and effect.

The Underlying Logic

Flying out = the home palace is cause and landing is effect, active outward.

Flying out Lu: actively connect and seek gain; Quan: actively compete and lead; Ji: actively turn inward.

Flying in = other palaces are cause and home palace is effect, external toward you.

Flying in Lu: external resources to you; Quan: external pressure to you; Ji: external sticking points to you.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First set Tai Ji, determining the home palace
  2. Find the home palace stem transformations, see where they fly out to
  3. Read flying out: judge what you actively do outward
  4. Find which palace stems fly into the home palace
  5. Read flying in: judge what external forces do to you
  6. Read both directions together, separating cause-effect and active-passive

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Seeing only flying out and taking all as your initiative

Mistake 2: Seeing only flying in and taking all as external

Mistake 3: Not setting Tai Ji, losing direction in both ways

Back to Your Own Chart

Flying out and flying in ask who initiates and who responds in a matter. Read both directions and separate cause and effect, and you know how much you can control.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to flying out of the home palace and flying in from others before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the flying out of the home palace and flying in from others line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
