How Each Major Star's Hua Lu Differs in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Same Gain, Different Path

Hua Lu governs smoothness, gain and chance, but which major star it transforms decides what and how one gains: Wu Qu Lu is cash, Tai Yin Lu assets and hidden income, Tian Ji Lu ideas, Tian Liang Lu shelter-based gain, Lian Zhen Lu people and charm. Seeing Hua Lu is far from enough—star nature sets the earning path.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

Hua Lu is the action of gaining; the major star is the object and path. The same Lu on Wu Qu versus Tan Lang means hard cash versus opportunity-and-network money.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People say wealth generically at Hua Lu; a Tian Ji Lu person hoarding property or Wu Qu person monetizing connections gets the path wrong. Read star nature to know how to receive your Lu.

The Underlying Logic

Hua Lu governs encounter, gain and smoothness, but what is gained is set by the transformed star's nature, never one-size-fits-all.

Wealth-star Lu is most concrete: Wu Qu Lu for earned cash and finance skill; Tai Yin Lu for assets, real estate, savings and passive income.

Ingenuity-star Lu earns through intellect: Tian Ji Lu planning/ideas, Tan Lang Lu opportunity/network/versatility, Lian Zhen Lu social charm and business.

Shelter/repute-star Lu is non-cash: Tian Liang Lu shelter and reputation benefits, Tian Tong Lu enjoyment and blessing; different stars mean wholly different paths and pacing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Find which major star Hua Lu lands on
  2. By star nature classify the gain: cash, assets, ideas or network
  3. Wu Qu/Tai Yin Lu: finance, assets, professional monetization
  4. Tian Ji/Tan Lang/Lian Zhen Lu: creativity, opportunity, network routes
  5. Tian Liang/Tian Tong Lu: use shelter, reputation and blessing, not quick cash
  6. Combine the palace to fix the domain where this Lu pays out

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Predicting wealth generically from Hua Lu

Mistake 2: Ignoring star nature, using the wrong earning path

Mistake 3: Mistaking shelter/blessing for cash income

Back to Your Own Chart

Anyone can spot Hua Lu; seeing how one gains is skill. Wu Qu's Lu is on the ledger, Tai Yin's in assets, Tian Ji's in ideas—receive it along your transformed star's nature and the smoothness truly lands.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to major stars Hua Lu differences before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the major stars Hua Lu differences line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
