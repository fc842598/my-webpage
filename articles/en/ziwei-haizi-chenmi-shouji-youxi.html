What to Do About Phone Addiction: Find the Cause, Set Rules and Be a Model

Phone addiction often comes from boredom, lack of company, stress or escape. Increase parent-child time and real activities, agree on time, content and rules with the child, and set an example by reducing your own phone use. Guide rather than simply confiscating, watch the impact on learning and emotions, and persist gently when needed.
