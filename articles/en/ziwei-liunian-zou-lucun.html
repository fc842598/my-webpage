How to Read the Annual Chart Passing Through Lu Cun

When the annual Life Palace passes through Lu Cun, the wealth star is on duty, favoring steady income, saving and preservation. But it is not the same for everyone; still read the natal Wealth Palace and triple-direction, as Lu Cun with emptiness or robbery can mean seeing it but not holding it.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

The annual chart passing Lu Cun is the wealth star knocking at the door, not opening the vault; whether the door opens depends on your natal foundation and trine support.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some assume passing Lu Cun guarantees a windfall, while others with emptiness or robbery say it is useless; the real test is whether the natal Wealth Palace can receive this wealth energy.

The Underlying Logic

Lu Cun governs wealth, stability and preservation; the annual chart passing it means the wealth star is on duty this year.

It favors saving, preservation and steady finance, not aggressive speculation.

With Hua Lu or Hua Quan in the trine, the wealth is more real and opportunities more plentiful.

With Di Kong, Di Jie or Da Hao, guard seeing it but not holding it; stay steady.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate whether the annual Life Palace passes through Lu Cun
  2. Read the natal Wealth Palace foundation to assess whether you can receive the wealth
  3. Check the annual triple-direction for Lu Quan Ke support
  4. Count emptiness, robbery and waste stars to guard money coming and going
  5. Set strategy: prioritize steady income and saving, not speculation
  6. Layer the decade to see where this year sits in the ten-year wealth cycle

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming passing Lu Cun guarantees wealth and investing blindly

Mistake 2: Negating everything with emptiness or robbery without reading the natal foundation

Mistake 3: Reading only the annual chart without the decade and natal layers

Back to Your Own Chart

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the annual chart passing through Lu Cun before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the annual chart passing through Lu Cun line is activated in this stage
