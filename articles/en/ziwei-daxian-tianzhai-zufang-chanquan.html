A Property Decade Can Mean a Move Without Ownership

Separate where someone lives from what property they legally own.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-31 09:18

A ten-year cycle through the Property Palace can make housing a recurring question, but it does not promise a purchase. One person changes rentals to shorten a commute; another gains an ownership share without moving. Track residence, legal title, and monthly cost separately. Documents and payments determine the practical decision.

A rental move changes residence but not ownership; an inherited share changes ownership without requiring a move. Check leases, title documents, and monthly payments separately.
