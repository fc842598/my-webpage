Zi Wei Dou Shu on Stock Options and Equity: Vesting, Exercise and Exit

Company options and equity correspond to the Career, Wealth and Friends Palaces, governing career, long-term gains and partnership. They carry vesting, an exercise price and exit conditions, and their value depends on company growth and the agreement, not just the number of shares.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-29

Options are possible future gains; the key is vesting, exercise and exit, and paper shares are not cash.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some treat options as certain wealth and leave without exercising or understanding vesting, forfeiting equity; others do not read the terms and overvalue company shares.

The Underlying Logic

Options and equity correspond to the Career, Wealth and Friends Palaces, governing long-term career gains and partnership.

Options usually vest over time; leaving before vesting forfeits the unvested portion.

Exercise requires buying at the strike price and considering tax and whether the company can go public or exit.

Read the grant agreement for amount, price, vesting and buyback terms; this is not investment advice.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Confirm whether it is options or equity and the amount
  2. Check the vesting, strike price and exercise window
  3. Understand treatment and buyback terms on leaving, listing or exit
  4. Read the Career, Wealth and Friends Palaces to judge long-term gains and risk
  5. Assess company growth and tax without overvaluing paper shares
  6. Follow the grant agreement and professional legal advice; not investment advice

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Treating options as certain wealth without checking vesting

Mistake 2: Leaving without exercising and forfeiting equity

Mistake 3: Not reading buyback and exit terms and overvaluing shares
