How to Read Buying a Home: Property Palace as the Wealth Treasury

The Property palace governs real estate, the home environment and the wealth treasury, and it is linked to the Wealth palace as one root. For buying a home, first read the Property palace major stars and brightness and whether Hua Lu or Lu Cun is present, then read the Wealth palace and where Hua Ji falls to see whether cash flow holds, and use the decade and year for timing. This is a tendency reference, not investment advice.
