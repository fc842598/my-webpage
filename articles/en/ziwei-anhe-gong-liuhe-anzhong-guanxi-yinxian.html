Hidden-Harmony Palace (Liu-He) in Zi Wei Dou Shu: The Hidden Relationship Line Behind the Visible Chart

The hidden-harmony palace uses branch six-harmony pairs (Zi-Chou, Yin-Hai, Mao-Xu, Chen-You, Si-Shen, Wu-Wei) to find the palace secretly corresponding to a given one—hidden resources, ties or mental activity influencing behind the scenes. The visible palace shows the surface, hidden-harmony the concealed tie, used for quiet help, hidden romance and true inner thoughts.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-07

The visible chart is spoken and seen; hidden-harmony is unspoken and working behind—reading this dark line explains otherwise puzzling behavior and ties.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Reading only visible palaces cannot explain hidden help or saying-one-thing-feeling-another; hidden-harmony supplies the layer beneath the surface.

The Underlying Logic

Six-harmony pairs: Zi-Chou, Yin-Hai, Mao-Xu, Chen-You, Si-Shen, Wu-Wei; every palace has one hidden-harmony palace.

Meaning: visible palace governs the explicit; hidden-harmony the concealed ties, quiet help, secret romance or inner motive.

Common use: hidden career mentors/resources, implicit affection or crushes, and what the Life palace's hidden-harmony says one truly cares about.

Role: a San He supplementary detail; predictions still center on visible palaces, trine and transformations, with hidden-harmony as corroboration.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read visible palaces and trine
  2. Find the target's hidden-harmony palace by the six-harmony table
  3. Read its stars for the concealed tie
  4. Career: hidden resources/mentors; romance: implicit ties
  5. Life hidden-harmony: what one truly cares about
  6. Treat it as corroboration, not the main reading

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Only visible palaces, unable to explain quiet help

Mistake 2: Making hidden-harmony the main line, ignoring the visible chart

Mistake 3: Misremembering six-harmony pairs, finding the wrong palace

Back to Your Own Chart

Hidden-harmony is the chart's subtext. The visible chart plays the onstage scene while it hides the backstage line; seeing concealed resources, bonds and motives deepens your reading beyond the surface.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to hidden-harmony palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the hidden-harmony palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
