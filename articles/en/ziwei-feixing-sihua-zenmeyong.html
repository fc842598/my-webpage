How to Use Flying Star Transformations: See Where Energy Comes and Goes

Flying star uses each palace's stem to fly transformations, tracking energy flow between palaces. Where Lu comes from and where Ji flies reveals cause-effect and interpersonal dynamics.

Methods2026-08-20 15:30

Natal transformations are "given by heaven"; palace stem flying is "made by people." The flying star approach: each palace has its own stem, and each stem can fly Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji. Through flying you see "energy flow between palaces" — Life stem flying Lu into Wealth means "you direct energy to earning"; Wealth stem flying Ji into Spouse means "money issues affect the relationship." Flying turns the chart from a still photo into a video.

Basic logic: each palace's stem flies four transformations per the table. Lu flying into a palace means "affinity, resources, I like it"; Ji flying in means "I'm fixated on it, it worries me." Flying has "in" and "out" directions — Lu in is "I receive," Lu out is "I give"; Ji in is "I get stuck," Ji out is "I bring trouble to others."

How to Fly

Each palace has a stem.

The stem flies Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji per the transformation table.

E.g., Life stem is Jia: Lian Zhen Lu, Po Jun Quan, Wu Qu Ke, Tai Yang Ji.

See which palaces these four stars land in — that's Life's flight direction.

All twelve palaces can fly; their transformations show that palace's "energy flow."

How to Read Flying Stars

Lu flying in — "affinity, resources, I like it," good relationship with that palace.

Quan flying in — "I want to control that palace," it drives me to act.

Ke flying in — "that palace gives me reputation," respectable relationship.

Ji flying in — "I'm fixated on that palace," it worries and blocks me.

Palaces with Lu in and Ji in are the two domains you care about most in life.

Flying Stars for Relationships

Life flying Lu into Spouse — I give to the relationship, I like them.

Spouse flying Lu into Life — they treat me well, love is reciprocated.

Life flying Ji into Spouse — I'm too fixated on the relationship, pressuring them.

Spouse flying Ji into Life — they worry me, relationship feels burdensome.

Mutual Lu in = "mutual love"; mutual Ji in = "mutual debt"; one Lu one Ji = "love-hate."

Reading Order

For flying star reading:

  1. Determine which palace "flies" — Life flies for yourself, Career for work.
  2. Find transformations from that palace's stem.
  3. See which palaces they land in.
  4. Read the incoming meaning — what Lu enters, what Ji enters.
  5. Check bidirectional flights — is it Lu-to-Lu or Ji-to-Ji between palaces.
  6. Flying stars are advanced; build foundations first.
