How to Read the Overall Layout of a Zi Wei Chart

For the overall layout of a chart, first read the skeleton of the Life Palace triple-direction, then fill in the details of stars and transformations, and finally layer the decade and year. The layout reads where the power flows, not the good or bad of one palace.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-07

The layout is like reading a floor plan: first look at load-bearing walls and orientation, then where the furniture goes. Fixating on one star at the start is like looking only at one chair.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some go palace by palace asking good or bad and miss the forest for the trees; the real way is to first read the power structure of the whole chart, then narrow to specific palaces.

The Underlying Logic

The first step of layout reads the Life Palace and triple-direction to set the skeleton and dominant tone.

Then read star distribution: where the major stars gather, power flows toward that area.

Transformations are energy switches; read where Lu Quan Ke Ji land to set the distribution of resources, control, reputation and sticking points.

Finally layer the decade and year to see how the innate layout is activated now.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate the Life Palace and read its star for the dominant tone
  2. Open the Life Palace triple-direction to see the skeleton
  3. Count auspicious and malefic stars across the chart to gauge power
  4. Read where natal transformations land to set the energy switches
  5. Find the area where stars and transformations concentrate, the main life axis
  6. Layer the decade and year to see which part of the layout is activated now

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Going palace by palace at the start without seeing the structure

Mistake 2: Reading only the Life Palace without the trines, missing power flow

Mistake 3: Ignoring transformations and decades, treating static layout as the whole

Back to Your Own Chart

The overall layout asks where the power flows. Read the skeleton first, fill in the details, then layer time, and you will not spin on the good or bad of one palace.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the overall layout of a chart before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the overall layout of a chart line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
