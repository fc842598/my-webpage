The Twelve Pre-General Stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Another Set of Natal Helpers for Support and Hidden Drain

The twelve Pre-General stars are placed by the birth-year branch: General, Saddle, Year-Stage, Rest, Canopy, Robbery, Disaster, Heaven-Sha, Back-Point,咸池 (Bath/Pool), Month-Sha and Perishing-Spirit. The Pre-General and Doctor sets come from different sources: Doctors start at Lu Cun and lean to annual timing; Pre-Generals start at the birth-year branch and lean to lifelong support and hidden drain.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking whether General, Robbery or Perishing matter and how they differ from the Doctors means learning how they are placed, which support and which drain, then using them within the main structure.

The Underlying Logic

Placement uses the birth-year branch to fix General, Saddle, Year-Stage, Rest, Canopy, Robbery, Disaster, Heaven-Sha, Back-Point, Pool, Month-Sha and Perishing across twelve palaces; once born, this set is fixed and does not change yearly.

Supportive ones: General gives judgment, leadership and drive to finish; Saddle brings patronage and rescue; Year-Stage brings movement, travel and chance; Canopy gives intelligence, refined solitude and affinity for art/spirituality—a plus in a fitting palace.

Turbulent ones: Robbery and Disaster bring sudden loss and blockage; Heaven/Month-Sha carry restraint and emotional turns; Back-Point brings behind-the-back criticism; Perishing brings distraction, fallen-through plans and hidden loss—read by the matter of the palace they land in.

Their weight is light: they only confirm when co-resident with major stars/transformations that already trend the same way; an auspicious base with General/Saddle strengthens help, a stressed base with Robbery/Perishing amplifies turbulence—never judge from one star.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Use the birth-year branch to find the Pre-General placements on the natal chart
  2. Separate supportive General/Saddle/Stage/Canopy from the draining ones
  3. Read each palace's co-resident stars and transformations, judging the main structure first
  4. An auspicious structure with a support star is a plus and signals patronage
  5. A stressed structure with Robbery/Perishing calls for a contingency in that matter
  6. Remember this set is lifelong-fixed and light-weight—detail only, never leading

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Mixing Pre-General and Doctor sets without knowing each origin

Mistake 2: Predicting disaster from Robbery/Perishing without checking the main structure

Mistake 3: Thinking this lifelong-fixed set changes annually like the flowing stars

Back to Your Own Chart

The Pre-General stars are a light natal annotation: support stars show where help comes, drain stars warn where hidden loss is likely. Keep their place and weight right and your reading gets finer without the tail wagging the dog.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the twelve Pre-General stars from the birth-year branch before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the twelve Pre-General stars from the birth-year branch line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
