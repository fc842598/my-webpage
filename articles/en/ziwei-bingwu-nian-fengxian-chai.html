Reading Risk in a Bing Wu Year: Work, Cash Flow, and Relationships

A grounded Zi Wei Dou Shu framework for reading a Bing Wu year by separating work changes, cash-flow risk, and relationship pressure.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:45

Change Is Not Automatically Bad

A fast year becomes risky when several life lines accelerate at once. Separate work, cash flow, and relationships before reacting.

Three Palaces

Career covers position and duty, Wealth covers inflow and exposure, and Spouse covers commitment and communication.

Two Patterns

Career Hua Quan with Wealth Hua Ji calls for scope and payment control. Spouse Hua Ke with Outside Hua Lu can bring a relationship forward through travel or public cooperation.

Use It as a Priority List

The annual chart is most useful when it tells you what to budget, schedule, and discuss first.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
