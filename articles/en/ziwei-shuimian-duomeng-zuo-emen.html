What to Do About Dreams, Nightmares and Poor Sleep: Sleep Hygiene and Stress

Vivid dreams and nightmares often relate to stress, anxiety, irregular routine, screens before bed or stimulants. First fix a steady schedule, reduce screens and caffeine near bedtime, create a quiet environment and exercise in the daytime, and note ongoing stress or traumatic events. If it seriously affects daytime function for long or comes with marked emotional problems, seek a proper sleep or mental-health clinic, with the doctor prevailing.
