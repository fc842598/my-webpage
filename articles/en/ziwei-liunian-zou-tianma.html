How to Read the Annual Chart Passing Through Tian Ma

When the annual Life Palace passes through Tian Ma, it brings busyness, movement and favors travel and change. Whether the running becomes opportunity depends on the natal Travel Palace and trine; with Lu it generates wealth through movement, so guard against empty busyness, exhaustion and running back and forth.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-06

Passing Tian Ma is a year of constant motion; movement itself is neither good nor bad, the key is whether the direction is right and whether Lu travels with it.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some fear busyness when seeing Tian Ma, while others assume movement always brings wealth; the real test is the direction of movement and whether Lu Quan Ke travel along.

The Underlying Logic

Tian Ma governs movement, busyness and travel change; the annual chart passing it means obvious movement this year.

It favors business trips, relocation, expansion and changing environment, turning change into opportunity.

With Hua Lu or Lu Cun in the trine, it generates wealth through movement, the more you move the more you gain.

With malefics or emptiness, guard empty busyness, exhaustion and running back and forth for nothing.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First locate whether the annual Life Palace passes through Tian Ma
  2. Read the natal Travel Palace to assess your foundation and opportunity away from home
  3. Check the annual trine for Lu, Quan, Ke supporting the movement
  4. Count malefics and emptiness to guard empty busyness and exhaustion
  5. Set strategy: choose direction actively rather than being pushed passively
  6. Keep travel and change within your energy capacity to guard against overdraw

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Fearing busyness at the sight of Tian Ma and limiting yourself

Mistake 2: Assuming movement always brings wealth without checking whether Lu travels along

Mistake 3: Being pushed passively, not choosing direction and failing to guard empty busyness

Back to Your Own Chart

Passing Tian Ma asks how to run toward opportunity in a busy year. Choose the right direction, travel with Lu and guard against empty busyness, so the running is not wasted but a path to wealth through movement.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to the annual chart passing through Tian Ma before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the the annual chart passing through Tian Ma line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
