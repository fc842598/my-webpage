Tan Lang and Wen Qu in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Course Interest Versus Learner Retention

Use star placement to ask why a popular trial class fails to keep learners engaged.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 09:16

A lively trial lesson draws viewers, but few learners complete the full course. Tan Lang can frame attraction and Wen Qu can frame expression, yet neither proves teaching quality. Check whether the stars share a palace, meet in the triad, or occupy separate roles.

Separate audience and delivery

If Tan Lang connects to outside exposure while Wen Qu connects to the Career Palace, compare marketing with the actual lesson structure. A course with many trial signups but low second-lesson attendance needs an examination of promises, difficulty, and feedback. A smaller course with completed learner projects may have a different problem.

Read the chart in order

Locate the star combination, then the Career, Children, and Wealth Palaces. Compare completion, refunds, and learner work. Do not convert a star nickname into a judgment about a teacher's character.
