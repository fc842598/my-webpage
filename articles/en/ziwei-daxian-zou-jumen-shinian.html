The Ju Men Decade: Expression, Analysis and Words, Turning Speech Into Capital

Ju Men governs expression, analysis, research and words. In its decade the theme leans to earning a place through speaking, writing, research and reasoning, suiting teaching, communication, law, consulting, research and content. The strength is strong logic, depth and penetrating expression; the risk is disputes and saying too much, so the key is grounding expression in expertise and setting tact for speech.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-02

A Ju Men decade often feels like ten years of talking more and insisting more: able to reason thoroughly and dig deep, yet probing too deep and speaking too bluntly can cost in disputes.

What This Question Is Really Asking

The question is how to use a Ju Men decade and guard against verbal disputes. Understand its expression and analysis, then see whether auspicious stars aid its expertise or malefics draw friction.

The Underlying Logic

Ju Men is the dark and verbal star, governing expression, analysis, research and skepticism, and this decade suits earning a place through content, eloquence, law and research, while also bringing matters that need arguing through.

It also governs disputes: too much speech loses, probing too deep or speaking too bluntly draws friction, offends and gets one misunderstood.

With Lu, Quan, Ke and auspicious transformations such as Ju Men Hua Lu or Hua Quan, expression becomes professional influence, income and standing; with malefics and Ji, disputes, legal trouble and harm through words follow.

The lesson is speech with expertise and tact: turn expression into work and plans, pause before speaking, and distinguish clarifying from winning.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First confirm Ju Men hosts the decade Life Palace and check its brightness and transformations
  2. List the content, research and expression directions for these years
  3. Write views into articles, plans and courses so expression accumulates
  4. Practice pausing before speaking, avoiding bluntness and winning arguments
  5. Keep written records of important communication to reduce verbal misunderstanding
  6. With the annual cycles, see which years bring strong expression chances and which favor speaking less and cautiously

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Taking the Ju Men decade only as eloquence and suited to speech while ignoring substance

Mistake 2: Arguing to win and speaking bluntly, turning expression into disputes

Mistake 3: Keeping no written records, letting unrecorded words become conflict
