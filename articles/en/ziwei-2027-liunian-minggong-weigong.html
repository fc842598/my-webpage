Locating the 2027 Annual Life Palace from the Wei Position: How to Order the Steps

How to fix the annual Life Palace at Wei for the Ding Wei year, then arrange the annual palaces and transformations in the correct order.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-17 10:06

To read your 2027 annual chart, the first step is not rushing to the transformations but locating the annual Life Palace. 2027 is a Ding Wei year, and the annual Life Palace is fixed at the Wei position, from which the twelve palaces are arranged. If this location is wrong, every later landing-palace judgment is wrong with it.

Why it starts from Wei

The annual Life Palace is decided by the year's earthly branch, and in a Wei year it lands on the Wei position of the chart. It overlaps with the natal chart: the same Wei position may be a certain palace in your natal chart and then take the role of the annual Life Palace in 2027. Clarifying this overlap first lets you arrange the other annual palaces.

Step-by-step annual location check

  1. Confirm 2027 is a Ding Wei year, with the annual Life Palace fixed at Wei.
  2. Find the Wei position on your natal chart and see which palace it is and what stars it holds.
  3. Using Wei as the annual Life Palace, arrange the twelve annual palaces in fixed order.
  4. Then place the annual transformations one by one into the matching annual palaces.
  5. Read the natal and annual layers as overlaps rather than mixing them.

Only after locating do the transformations matter

Without the annual Life Palace and twelve palaces arranged, the transformations have no landing point. Set the skeleton first, then read how the transformations and stars act, for a stable conclusion. This basic step is where annual reading most often goes wrong.

Chart reading order

  1. Fix the natal chart and confirm the earthly-branch positions of the twelve palaces.
  2. Set the annual Life Palace at Wei for the Ding Wei year.
  3. Arrange the twelve annual palaces from Wei.
  4. Then place the annual transformations over the natal structure.
  5. Keep the conclusion conditional and label the two layers separately.
