The Friends/Subordinates Palace: Friends, Staff and the Network Around You

The Friends/Subordinates palace reads friends, staff, coworkers and all self-to-crowd relations. Its stem flying Lu into Life means loyal friends and capable staff; flying Ji means bad company or being dragged down. Zi Wei here means capable but proud staff; with malefics, help often turns to opposition later—key for hiring and choosing friends.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-06

Siblings reads equals; Friends/Subordinates reads the crowd and those below: whether you can lead a team, what friends you draw, and whether your network drags you—this palace is the most direct.

What This Question Is Really Asking

People think more friends are better, yet with Ji or heavy malefics broad networking drains; others miss the helps-then-turns signal and lose over someone who seemed strong at first.

The Underlying Logic

It governs friends, subordinates, employees and broad network beyond partnership, plus your leadership and popularity.

Its stem transformations are key: Lu/Quan/Ke flying into Life/Wealth/Career mean helpful friends and staff; Ji into Life means being dragged down, betrayed or losing money for friends.

Major stars set the crowd's quality: Zi Wei/Tian Fu high-level but needing control; Ji-Yue-Tong-Liang gentle and stable; Sha-Po-Lang/Tan capable but volatile.

With Yang/Tuo/Fire-Bell/Empty stars, bonds turn easily and staff help then oppose; hire with assessment and bounded delegation.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read its major star for the overall quality of people around you
  2. Focus on stem transformations: Lu/Quan/Ke into Life help; Ji into Life drains
  3. Assess leadership: whether you can lead and keep staff stable
  4. With heavy malefics/Ji, prune social life to quality over quantity and delegate with bounds
  5. Combine Siblings to separate equal-partner from subordinate/friend relations
  6. In years it activates, expect team changes, new networks or social friction

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming more friends are better, ignoring Ji's drain

Mistake 2: Trusting early competence without guarding against later opposition

Mistake 3: Blending friends and subordinates without separating the two

Back to Your Own Chart

This palace teaches choosing and using people. See who truly helps and who drags you down; value a refined network over a big one, delegate with bounds, and your connections become assets, not burdens.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to Friends and subordinates palace before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the Friends and subordinates palace line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
