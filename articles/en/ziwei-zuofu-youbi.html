Zuo Fu and You Bi: The Emperor’s Right and Left Hands

Zuo Fu and You Bi are assistance stars — help and support. In the Life Palace or triple combination, they mean people lend a hand and success isn’t lonely.

Helper Stars2026-08-16 10:15

Zuo Fu and You Bi are the warmest stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu. They rule neither wealth nor power — only one thing: help. People with either in the Life Palace always find a hand when stuck; not because of luck, but because others naturally want to help them.

Zuo Fu is Yang Earth, You Bi is Yin Water (some say Yin Fire), and they always meet in triple combination. They are the Zi Wei emperor’s hands: Zuo Fu is visible help (open support), You Bi is hidden help (someone quietly covering for you). They are key to reading whether you have support.

Core Meaning

They rule assistance and tolerance. People with these stars are usually warm, easy to work with, and team-oriented. You’re not a lone wolf; you function best in groups.

Zuo Fu is open help — public support, resources, advocacy. You Bi is hidden help — quiet backup, filling gaps, paving roads you don’t see. Having both is best.

They differ from Kui/Yue: those are benefactor stars for key moments with important people; Fu/Bi are daily assistance and cooperation. Kui/Yue are points; Fu/Bi are lines.

Across the Twelve Palaces

Life: personal charisma, people follow you. Rarely needing to beg for help.

Siblings: brothers and sisters help; good peer relations.

Spouse: partner supports you, a teammate feeling. (You Bi here can also hint at hidden romantic competition — check other stars.)

Wealth: earning through partners, teams, platforms — not solo money.

Career: lieutenants and teams at work; suited to management.

Fu Bi and Zi Wei

They matter most for Zi Wei. The emperor without Fu/Bi is a lonely ruler; with them, a true emperor.

Zi Wei in Life with Fu/Bi in triple combination greatly boosts leadership and followership. In the same palace it’s most direct; in opposition or triple it still counts.

Zi Wei in Life with no Fu/Bi or Kui/Yue is a lonely ruler — ambition without help; you must actively seek partners.

Neither Good Nor Bad on Their Own

They are neutral-to-auspicious and never transform to Lu/Quan/Ke/Ji. They amplify the stars they share a palace with — auspicious stars become more auspicious; malefics become more harmful.

With Zi Wei, leadership amplifies. With Qing Yang, the destructive power amplifies too (someone helps you fight).

Don’t just check whether they’re present — check whom they’re with. With the right star they’re wings; with the wrong one they aid trouble.

Reading Order After You Cast the Chart

When you see Zuo Fu/You Bi:

  1. Which palace — that determines where help comes.
  2. Which main star shares the palace — auspicious means real help; malefic means help that hurts.
  3. Zuo Fu or You Bi — open or hidden help.
  4. Check triple combination for other auspicious stars — Fu/Bi plus Kui/Yue is strongest benefactor luck.
  5. Check malefics sharing the palace — help is reduced or comes with traps.
  6. Do you appreciate the people who help you?
