How to Find Focus When Many Transformations Appear: Layer First, Then Lu and Ji

Natal, decade and year transformations can stack into a dozen or more; the way to focus is to layer by time first and then prioritize Lu and Ji, settling the main line before placing Quan, Ke and details.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-04

Many transformations dazzle, but they are not all equal; after layering, the ones that truly need attention are usually just two or three.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Seeing dense transformations, some feel every point is central and worth worrying over; the issue is not many transformations but viewing them all at once without layers or ranking.

The Underlying Logic

Transformations belong to three layers: natal set the innate baseline, decade set the ten-year main line and year set the annual trigger.

Within a layer, Hua Lu and Hua Ji move gain, loss and attachment and are read first; Hua Quan and Hua Ke govern control and reputation as support.

Transformations from several layers landing in one palace is overlap; that palace is repeatedly activated and is the top focus.

Transformations are changes in how stars express, not isolated good-bad scores, and are read with the palace major star.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First sort all transformations into natal, decade and year layers and mark them separately
  2. In each layer, circle where Hua Lu and Hua Ji land to locate gain, loss and attachment
  3. Then read Hua Quan and Hua Ke to add where control and reputation sit
  4. Find palaces where several layers land together to set the stage focus
  5. Read the focus palaces with their major star and trine rather than viewing transformations alone
  6. Finally summarize the main line in a sentence or two and stop giving every detail equal weight

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Viewing all transformations equally and ending with no focus

Mistake 2: Reading only the year layer without setting the natal and decade baseline

Mistake 3: Taking every Hua Ji as bad and Hua Lu as good without the star and palace

Back to Your Own Chart

Many transformations teach layering, then catching Lu and Ji, then reducing to a sentence. Once the main line is set, no amount of information scatters you.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to charts with many transformations in play before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the charts with many transformations in play line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
