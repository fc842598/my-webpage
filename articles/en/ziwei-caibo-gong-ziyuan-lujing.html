Wealth Paths in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Salary, Expertise, and Platform Income

How to distinguish salary, professional fees, business revenue, and platform income by reading the Wealth, Career, Life, and Outside Palaces together.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-16 18:48

Different Income Containers

Salary, professional fees, business revenue, and platform share each need a different relationship between Wealth, Career, Life, and Outside.

Four Palace Map

Wealth names the money form, Career names the work container, Life shows consistency, and Outside shows clients or platforms.

Examples

Career Hua Ke with Outside Hua Lu can support expert content and packaged services. Life Hua Quan with Wealth Hua Ji needs deposits and a risk reserve.

A Better Conclusion

Instead of saying someone is simply suited to business, describe the income mix, the acquisition path, and the cash-flow rule that supports it.

A Practical Reading Order

Start with the concrete question, identify the main palace, add the opposite and the three supporting palaces, and then check the major and annual timing. End with an observable next step instead of a fixed prediction.
