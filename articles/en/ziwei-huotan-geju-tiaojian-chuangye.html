The Fire-Tan Lang Pattern: Conditions and Modern Startup Boundaries

How to verify the strict conditions of the Fire-Tan Lang pattern and avoid treating a sudden burst as lasting success.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-13 11:04

The Fire-Tan Lang pattern is often reduced to sudden wealth, but it has strict conditions. Seeing Tan Lang near Huo Xing is not enough. In a modern startup, misusing the name can turn a one-time burst into a false belief in stable ability.

The conditions

Traditionally, Tan Lang must meet Huo Xing or Ling Xing in a specific same-palace or close-aspect relationship, with the required position and star state. Same palace, three-way aspect, and distant support are different positions and cannot be mixed. The version of the rules must be fixed.

Verification steps

  1. Confirm the real positions of Tan Lang and Huo Xing or Ling Xing.
  2. Check each condition under one fixed school rather than assuming the pattern exists.
  3. Look for difficult stars or Ji that may break the structure.
  4. Check steady forces such as Lu Cun or Tian Fu to see whether gains can be kept.
  5. Use Wealth, Career, and the real plan to separate a one-time chance from ability.

Reading order

  1. Fix the software version and rule convention, then confirm the positions.
  2. Verify conditions, then check difficult and steady stars.
  3. Separate burst power from the ability to keep results.
  4. Add the current cycle to see whether a window is open.
  5. Write the answer as burst conditions plus a keeping precondition, not a wealth guarantee.
