Wealth Inheritance in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Tian Zhai and Parents Palace Intergenerational Transfer

Wealth inheritance is an issue many middle-class and above families face. From the chart perspective, the combination of Tian Zhai Palace (real estate/family assets) and Parents Palace (elders/inheritance) reflects whether a person has inheritance luck and potential problems in the process. But wealth inheritance is complex legal and financial planning; the chart is only reference.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-24

Chart signals for inheritance luck: first, Parents Palace has Prosperity or Recognition—good elder relations, easily receives asset support from parents or elders. Second, Tian Zhai Palace has Prosperity—good family asset luck, real estate easily appreciates and is passed on. Third, Decade Prosperity enters Parents or Tian Zhai Palace—this decade has opportunity to receive inheritance or elder gifts. Fourth, Siblings Palace is in good condition—harmonious sibling relations, inheritance does not easily cause disputes. Signals of problems in inheritance: first, Parents Palace Jealousy—financial relations with parents have lessons, inheritance process may not go smoothly (will disputes, frozen assets). Second, Siblings Palace has malefics—siblings easily fall out over inheritance distribution. Third, Tian Zhai Palace has Qing Yang or Tuo Lo—real estate inheritance easily has disputes (unclear ownership, multiple heirs disagreeing). Fourth, Children Palace has malefics—next generation may not be good at managing inherited wealth, "wealth does not pass three generations." Wealth inheritance planning methods: first, "plan ahead"—do not wait until poor health to consider inheritance, start planning during asset stability. Second, "wills and legal tools"—arrange inheritance through wills, trusts, insurance, avoid disputes from statutory inheritance. Legal matters follow professional lawyers and正规 channels. Third, "asset diversification"—do not put all assets in one persons name or one form, diversify to reduce risk. Fourth, "financial literacy education"—Children Palace condition determines whether next generation can preserve wealth, cultivate next generations financial management ability before inheritance. Fifth, "communication"—many inheritance disputes arise because parents did not communicate distribution intentions clearly with children before death, early communication reduces much conflict. Sixth, financial content is based on licensed institutions and正规 product descriptions, not investment advice.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Practical case: Someone with Prosperity in Wealth but Jealousy in Tian Zhai has good income but cannot save—earns much but spends much, or loses money through property or family expenses. The key is "income looks at Wealth, preservation looks at Tian Zhai"—both palaces must be read.

The Underlying Logic

Only looking at Wealth Palace without Tian Zhai, earning much but not preserving.

Equating Prosperity with "will definitely get rich," ignoring that Prosperity can also mean "more money more problems."

Not watching Decade/Annual wealth changes, making wrong investments at wrong times.

Treating chart financial tendencies as investment advice, without own research and risk assessment.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Chart the Wealth, Tian Zhai, and Fortune palaces major stars and transformations.
  2. See the Decade Wealth Palace state to judge the ten-year income trend.
  3. Distinguish between "regular income" (salary) and "windfall" (investment, side business) sources.
  4. Check Tian Zhai wealth-preservation ability, set savings and asset allocation plans.
  5. Take initiative in good wealth years, hold steady in bad wealth years.
  6. Regularly review financial status and adjust strategy based on actual conditions.

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: The chart only indicates financial tendencies; it does not constitute investment advice.

Mistake 2: Good wealth years can still lose money from wrong decisions; bad wealth years can still preserve capital through caution.

Mistake 3: Financial content is based on licensed institutions and official product descriptions; investments carry risk.

Back to Your Own Chart

The core of wealth management is not "whether you have money in your destiny" but "whether you can preserve and grow what you earn." The chart helps you understand your financial personality and risk points; real wealth accumulation comes from consistent saving habits, reasonable asset allocation, and continuously improving earning ability.
