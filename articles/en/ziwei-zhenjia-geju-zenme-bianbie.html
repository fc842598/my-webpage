How to Tell Real from False Patterns in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Four Conditions Must Hold

Whether a pattern truly forms cannot be judged by its name alone; check whether the main stars are strong, assistant stars meet, malefics break it, and transformations activate it. All four must hold with pure energy for a real pattern; fallen stars with heavy malefics and no auspicious stars or transformations is usually a false pattern in name only.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-28

A pattern is like a recipe; main stars, assistant stars, malefics and transformations must all be right, and missing one discounts even the finest name.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Beginners most often see a group of stars resembling a pattern and judge by its good or bad name without checking the conditions, the most common source of misreading.

The Underlying Logic

First the main stars: whether the stars guarding and meeting the life are strong and grounded; fallen stars make the energy impure and discount the effect.

Second the assistant stars: whether Zuo Fu, You Bi, Tian Kui, Tian Yue, Wen Chang and Wen Qu meet; with their support the pattern has execution and helpful people behind it.

Third the malefics: whether Qing Yang, Tuo Luo, Huo Xing, Ling Xing and the emptiness stars clash or merely meet; a malefic break without restraint is a broken pattern, the common source of false ones.

Fourth the transformations: whether Hua Lu, Quan, Ke and Ji activate the line; without them the pattern is unpowered, and only all four together decide real vs. false and high vs. low.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. List the star combination the pattern requires and confirm the structure is right
  2. Read main-star strength to decide whether energy is pure
  3. Check whether assistant and auspicious stars meet to decide support
  4. Count malefics and emptiness to see whether they clash and are restrained, deciding breakage
  5. See whether transformations activate the line, deciding whether the pattern is powered
  6. Combine all four before concluding real or false rather than judging by name

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Judging good or bad by the pattern name when the stars merely resemble it

Mistake 2: Seeing only the main stars without assistants, malefics and transformations

Mistake 3: Taking a broken false pattern as real and seeing expectations fail

Back to Your Own Chart

Telling real from false patterns relies on item-by-item verification, not memorized names. Check main stars, assistants, malefics and transformations; only then speak strongly for a real pattern, and read a false one as an ordinary combination, so a chart is not led astray by a fine name.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to telling real from false patterns before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the telling real from false patterns line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
