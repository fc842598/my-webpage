Year-End Bonus and Profit Sharing in Zi Wei Dou Shu: How Your Reward Settles

A year-end bonus is how a year of value is settled. Career sets annual performance and the boss's evaluation, Wealth the actual incoming state, Karma your expectation of return, and the annual transformations directly trigger it. Annual Lu into Wealth/Career tends to beat expectations, Ji to shrink or delay. Reading this lets you anticipate rhythm and manage expectations rather than treating bonus size as the final verdict on your worth.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-09-15

Some beat expectations yearly; others are anxious before and disappointed after. Bonus involves the reality of company and performance, but also the chart's reward rhythm and your expectation management.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Asking about this year's bonus, whether profit sharing will materialize, or why effort and reward mismatch, first see how the year settles your value, then decide whether to push or adjust expectations.

The Underlying Logic

Performance evaluation reads Career: Career carrying annual Lu/Ke with steady stars gets smooth reviews and recognition; annual Quan activation may add reward for carrying the load, while Ji into Career invites fault-finding and credit being shared, so document contributions and keep them visible that year.

Actual inflow reads Wealth, where the bonus lands. Annual Lu or Lu Cun into Wealth brings pleasant surprises; Ji into Wealth commonly means budget cuts, delayed payment or tax and deductions eroding it. In a Wealth-stressed year do not spend the expected bonus in advance.

Expectation gap reads Karma, your inner baseline. Karma with Quan/Zi Wei expects much and feels let down by slightly less; steady Karma views fluctuation objectively. Bonus anxiety is often not low pay but a gap between Karma expectation and Wealth reality.

Settlement rhythm reads year and decade: months activating Career/Wealth are usually when bonuses are set and paid; a decade of auspicious transformations through Wealth/Career lifts the whole reward curve. When Ji enters Career/Wealth for several years running, it is often the industry or platform's distribution, not your failure—consider switching tracks rather than self-denial.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. Read annual Career to judge performance evaluation and contribution visibility
  2. Read annual Wealth to anticipate whether the bonus beats expectations or shrinks/delays
  3. Read Karma's expectation baseline and align expectations with reality
  4. In years with Ji into Career, document performance to keep credit from being diluted
  5. Remember bonuses are set and paid in months activating Wealth/Career; plan cash flow ahead
  6. If Ji enters Career/Wealth for years, assess whether it is platform distribution rather than personal ability

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Equating bonus size with self-worth and denying yourself over one fluctuation

Mistake 2: Spending the expected bonus ahead of a Ji-into-Wealth year, ending cash-flow passive

Mistake 3: Working heads-down without making contribution visible, losing at review and blaming luck
