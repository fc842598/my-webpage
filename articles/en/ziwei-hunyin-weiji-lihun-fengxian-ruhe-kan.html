How to Read Signs of Marriage Crisis and Divorce Risk: Early Recognition and Timely Repair

Reading marriage risk is not a verdict on divorce but recognizing prolonged communication breakdown, drifting values, a third party or overload of real-world pressure, then communicating, repairing or seeking professional marriage counseling while the issue is small.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-10-05

Marriage crisis rarely arrives suddenly; it is usually small matters never talked through that gather into chill, and while the chart shows activation, real repair should start early.

What This Question Is Really Asking

Some see malefics in the Spouse Palace and conclude divorce, frightening themselves; the issue is not a fierce star but taking a risk signal as the ending and ignoring room to communicate and repair.

The Underlying Logic

First read the natal Spouse Palace triple-direction: the lasting communication pattern, value fit and attitude to the relationship.

When the decade relationship line meets malefics, that stage carries more pressure with cold silence or outside interference.

Risk signals such as prolonged chill, avoided talks, a third party or real pressure remind you to repair, not to sentence.

Judge from real conduct and choices, seeking professional marriage counseling when needed rather than predicting separation from one star.

A Step-by-Step Way to Read It

  1. First read the natal Spouse Palace to set the lasting relationship tone
  2. Then read the current decade relationship line to see where pressure comes from
  3. Compare risk signals to separate temporary friction from prolonged chill
  4. Face problems with active, issue-focused communication rather than avoidance
  5. Seek professional marriage counseling when you cannot move things alone
  6. Treat signals as reminders and put energy into repair rather than fearing the ending

Three Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Concluding divorce at the sight of malefics and frightening themselves

Mistake 2: Taking risk signals as endings without communication or repair

Mistake 3: Reading the chart over real conduct and ignoring human choice

Back to Your Own Chart

The value of crisis signals is reminding you to repair while small. Communicate early, keep outside forces out and be willing to seek help, and most relationships have room to turn.

Reading Order

  1. Cast the natal chart first and locate the palaces and major stars tied to signs of marriage crisis and divorce risk before judging anything
  2. Open the triple-direction of those palaces and count the auspicious, malefic and assistant stars separately
  3. Check where the natal Four Transformations land to set the chart's baseline tone
  4. Read the decade-limit transformations to see whether the signs of marriage crisis and divorce risk line is activated in this stage
  5. Then read the annual transformations and the year's palace to pin down this year's trigger and timing
  6. Layer natal, decade and year before concluding, then decide how to act rather than labeling good or bad
