Zi Wei Dou Shu Health Palace and a Practical Sleep Log

Use the Health Palace to ask better questions while tracking observable symptoms.

Zi Wei Dou Shu2026-11-09 09:13

A difficult Health Palace symbol cannot diagnose a headache. In Zi Wei Dou Shu, it can prompt questions about strain and routine, but a usable record begins with sleep, workload and symptoms that can be checked.

Build a short log

Note bedtime, wake time, work end time, caffeine and the duration of any discomfort. “Tired again” is vague; “three late nights followed by afternoon headaches” gives a clinician a timeline. Career or Parents Palace themes may point to work or care demands, not to a medical cause.

Review and seek care

Try one small schedule change for two weeks and observe what changes. Bring persistent or worsening symptoms to a healthcare professional. Read the natal chart before timing layers, and never use a calm-looking chart to delay care.
